### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Introductory Speech (15 points): In an introductory speech you introduce yourself to the audience in a manner that will encourage them to perceive you the same way that you would like to be perceived. This assignment will be graded based on completion so long as the performance meets the minimum guidelines which are outlined in the assignment sheet.

Informative Speech (30 points): For this assignment you will perform a speech that provides your audience with new and interesting information about a topic that they are unfamiliar with. This speech will be no shorter than 5 minutes, and no longer than 7 minutes. This assignment will be graded based on the rubric outlined in the assignment sheet on blackboard.

Persuasive Speech (30 points): For this assignment you will deliver a speech that attempts to prompt your audience to take a particular course of action. This speech will be no shorter than 5 minutes, and no longer than 7 minutes. This assignment will be graded based on the rubric outlined in the assignment sheet on blackboard.

Proposal Speech (30 points): For this assignment you will deliver a speech proposing a new idea to your audience. There are many different types of proposals, but two common examples are research proposals, and business proposals. This assignment will be graded based on the rubric outlined in the assignment sheet on blackboard.

Celebratory Speech (30 points): For this assignment students will perform a speech celebrating a special event. The event will be of the student’s choice, but some common examples might include: a wedding toast, a graduation speech, or a thank you speech upon receiving an award. This speech will be no less than three minutes, but many should be substantially longer. This assignment will be graded based on the rubric outlined in the assignment sheet on blackboard.

Self Criticism Assignment (15 points): Students will be asked to analyze a speech which they have delivered, both from a performative and content perspective and identify what strengths and shortcomings they have demonstrated in their speech, and what changes they need to make in order to improve their performance.

Reading Quizzes (50 points): There will occasionally be reading quizzes over material that is considered to be especially important. Students will not necessarily know when these quizzes will be administered-part of their function is to be an accountability mechanism to insure even and thorough participation for learning. Each quiz will be worth 10 points.

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Academic Misconduct:**

Academic misconduct undermines the integrity of RPI, the course, and the person who commits misconduct. The instructor takes such concerns extremely seriously and any violations will be handled according to the process outlined in the RPI student handbook. Any assignment which has been plagiarized or has in any other way been completed in a manner that does not reflect the academic conduct expected of RPI students will receive a 0.

**AI Policy:**

Recent advances in artificial intelligence have created a number of tools which could theoretically be used to produce a script for a speech. The aim of the course is to teach the skills which this use of artificial intelligence circumvents. For the purpose of this course the use of artificial intelligence except in specific circumstances discussed and approved by the instructor ahead of time will result in a zero being awarded for the assignment.
