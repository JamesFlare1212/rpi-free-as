## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Jon Kabat-Zinn said that “Mindfulness means paying attention in a particular way: on purpose, in the present moment, and nonjudgmentally.” Throughout the day we tend to spend much of our time thinking about the past and the future. Mindfulness involves giving particular attention to our moment-to-moment experiences in a way that emphasizes observing, rather than judging or evaluating. Everyday activities such as driving a car or eating a meal provide us the opportunity to practice this focused, moment-to-moment attention. In addition, we can practice mindfulness through formal meditation. There is empirical evidence of the many health benefits of mindfulness meditation, including the potential to produce fundamental changes in brain structure and epigenetic change. In this class, we will discuss the historical and psychological foundations of mindfulness and empirical findings supporting its positive effects. Students will participate in structured exercises. This course is a very hands-on approach to mindfulness…you will be required to practice mindfulness and meditation and develop your mindful skills. It is also more of a discussion course.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

1. **Maya Angelou’s *Letter to My Daughter* (20 pts)**

- Prepare an oral overview of at 3-5 essays. You will present on one of them in class. We will then engage in class discussion
- Write a 3-4 page paper) highlighting how her book affected you and how it changed (or reinforced, or didn’t change) how you think about life. Submit on LMS

2. **Review of Holly Roger’s *The Mindful Twenty Something* book**

**20 points** Participate in class discussion (and write 1-2 page reflection of the 5 sections as specified below) highlighting how her book affected you and how it changed (or reinforced, or didn’t change) how you think about life. Reflection is due before class begins. Clarification: You will have a total of 4 papers, each worth 5 points. Submit on LMS. Note: this is **NOT** a summary of the readings, but a self-reflection.

3. **Koru App Daily Log**

**20 points** There are daily log opportunities in the app. Make sure you complete 20 days of reflections for 4 weeks during the time we will be discussing this book. Complete 2-3 things you are grateful for each day, and then reflect on the meditation you did that day. Questions you could answer: Which meditation did you do? How did you feel before and after the meditation? How did the meditation impact you?

This assignment is completed in the Koru app.

4. **Big Panda and Tiny Dragon**

**10 pts for each book reflection (20 pts total)**

You will read 2 books this semester: *Big Panda and Tiny Dragon* and *The Journey: Big Panda and Tiny Dragon.*

For each book, you will write a 1-page single spaced paper.

Choose a few passages that resonate with you in some way. Explain how the passages impacted you and how they made you think about your own life journey.

For example: on p. 62 “*I’m worried,”* said Tiny Dragon. “*I don’t know what to do next.” “For just a moment*,” said Big Panda, “*stop, breathe, and listen to the wind in the bamboo.”* This is such an inspiring way to remind me to be in the moment, especially when things are so uncertain. Yesterday I was asked a challenging question and I wasn’t really sure how to answer at first, so I stopped and took a breath to collect my thoughts, and then when I had my answer, I spoke. Listening to the bamboo is a way to clear our minds of the thing we are worried about so we can think of a way to move forward.

Papers are due before class on the days we will be discussing the books; the papers will be used in our class discussions (group/partner discussions).

5. **Attendance**

**20 Points** This is a communication intensive course. You are encouraged to contribute to class discussions. Attendance will be taken each class. You can miss 2 class meetings without an Institute accepted reason. Beyond 2 missed classed meetings, you must provide documented absence excuse to the Institute. Even on lecture days, we will often break off into group meetings so you do have to be physically present and attending the lecture.

If you are sick, please do not come to class. Let me know you will be absent and I will set up a WebEx meeting so you can still be “present” for class.

6. **Presentation and Paper**

**50 points** (10 points presentation, 40 points paper) Choose a topic related to mindfulness and write a minimum 4 page paper, with 12pt Times New Roman font, double spaced. Submit all parts of this assignment on LMS.

**Note: Only the final paper is graded. If drafts are omitted, then 5 point deduction for each missing draft will occur on final paper grade. And you CANNOT submit drafts at the same time.**

Submit all assignments on LMS

- Plagiarizing will result in a grade of 0 on the paper. If you copy material from a source, you must use quotations and a citation.
- Use PsycInfo
- Use APA style citations
- Minimum of 8 citations from empirical research studies, and 3-4 more citations from other sources (e.g., online sources that are not empirical)
- Writing a paper that is summarizing one research paper than another will result in highest grade of 20/40.
- You should strive to write a paper organized topic by topic with 2-3 research papers per topic.
- The Writing Center https://rpi.mywconline.com/
- Presentation is 8-10 min summary of your paper (highlight what you learned)
