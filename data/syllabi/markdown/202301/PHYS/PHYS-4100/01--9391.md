## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Quantum mechanics beyond Schr ̈odinger wave mechanics. The postulates of quantum mechanics. Second quantization, Dirac notation, Hilbert spaces, perturbation theory, and applications to simple systems.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

John Townsend, “Modern Approach to Quantum Mechanics”, 2nd edition ISBN: 9781891389788

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Calculate probability and probability amplitudes
- Understand meaning of Pauli matrices, and being able to calculate matrix representation of spin 1 systems
- Establish Hamiltonian as the generator of time evolution operator
- To be able to combine two spin-1/2 particles
- To be able to calculate the probability density in simple 1D wells
- Understand the impact of commutation relationships on the solution of the bound states of a spherically symmetric system
- To understand the solutions of the 1D Harmonic Oscillator in quantum mechanics
- To understand time-independent perturbation theory up to second order for degenerate and non-degenerate cases
- Understand the implication of indistinguishably on the possible symmetry of wave-function under the effect of the exchange operator
- To be able to solve the problem of the hydrogen atom

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Homework:

The homework assignments and respective due dates will be posted on the class calendar. The homework assignment will be due at the beginning of class meeting on the due date. In general, late homework will not be accepted. The homework will account for 15% of the final grade.

Quizzes:

There will be quizzes given randomly throughout the semester at the beginning of the class meeting. The lowest two quiz scores will be dropped in calculating the average score. The quiz average will account for 15% of the final grade. No makeup quizzes will be given.

Exams:

There will be three exams during the semester to test your progress and mastering of the material. The exams will be given during class time. Please see the class calendar for dates. There will also be a final during finals week TBA. The final counts as two exams. Your exam average will be calculated from the 4 (out of 5) best exam scores. The exams account for 70% of your final grade.

No makeup exams will be given. If a student misses an exam, that will be the dropped exam score. The final is mandatory and cannot be missed.
