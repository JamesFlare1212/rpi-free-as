## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**General Course Description:**  
A graphic portfolio of architectural design work is a living document representing the diversity, competence, and growth of abilities throughout an architect’s career. It is an essential and required document for securing employment and advancement in any architectural field from professional practice to academics. This course introduces students into the design and development of their portfolios that include work from every level of their education and professional experience. The immediate concern of this course is for students who are preparing for their ARCH AWAY experience to be able to demonstrate to potential employers the full spectrum of their architectural design capabilities with graphic clarity and compositional proficiency.  
This is a one credit, in-person course covering professional development topics to assist students in preparing and developing a semester away plan and search strategy.  
**SOA Internal Application for the AWAY Semester:**  
As part of the AWAY experience the school of architecture will maintain agreements with top tier AEC & Interiors firms, in the US and abroad. Select offices will offer internships and co-ops to qualified 4th year BArch candidates during their away semesters. In preparation of this program each student must complete ARCH 4200 and submit their portfolio application to be considered for placement.  
As with our study abroad programs your GPA will be considered. However, your portfolio is the most important document in demonstrating your skills to the committee and potential employers. In some cases you may be competing for positions against applicants from other schools and it is essential that you prepare the best portfolio possible. A list of potential offices will be made available in the spring of 2022. It is important to be familiar with the pool of firms and to rank your top choices on the application form. The committee will take these into consideration, but cannot guarantee that you will receive an interview from any of the offices that you have pre-selected.  
The SOA does not guarantee any positions. Once the shortlists are announced making contact with the potential employers on your shortlist and interviewing is the responsibility of each student. The ILE (individualized learning program) is an option for students that wish to develop an independent project. Proposals must be approved by the department and your advisor. A proposal, portfolio and application are also required. The institute requires that any students that do not have an offer letter by the designated fall or spring deadline must submit an ILE application.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

All students who successfully complete this course will be able to:  
1\. Demonstrate the ability to work at a level of high competence with various graphic design softwares such as InDesign.  
2\. Demonstrate the ability to construct image flow to support both graphic and verbal narratives of their design projects.  
3\. Demonstrate the ability to integrate graphically the diversity of their case studies, construction, and environmental  
coursework with the representation of their studio work.  
4\. Demonstrate the ability conceive, design, and construct a portfolio, a curriculum vitae, and a letter of interest specifically  
related to architecture and its allied fields of design and building.  
**Assessment**  
Assessment will be based on completion of the following modules. Sloppy or incomplete portfolios will receive an unsatisfactory  
grade. Students are expected to conduct their own self-motivated research to complement the material presented throughout the  
course. The design modules will be provided via LMS. You are responsible for viewing each module and incoprorating their  
content, concepts and methods into your portfolio.  
The grading structure for the studio is as follows:  
MODULE 1 5%  
MODULE 2 15%  
MODULE 3 15%  
MODULE 4 15%  
MODULE 5 15%  
FINAL PORTFOLIO + CV Drafts 35%

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

**The grading structure for the course is as follows:**

- 5% MODULE 1 Introduction: Portfolio Overview
- 15% MODULE 2 Initial Content Assembly and Template Development
- 15% MODULE 3 First Draft - Basic Layouts & Project Descriptions
- 15% MODULE 4 Second Draft - Portfolio Refinement
- 15% MODULE 5 Final Draft - Portfolio Refinement + CV Development
- 35% FINAL PORTFOLIO + CV Drafts

Grading schedule  
A = excellent  
A-= excellent  
B+ = good  
B = good  
B-= good  
C+ = average  
C = average  
C-= average  
D+/D = fail  
Grades will be established according to the following policy:  
\- A (EXCELLENT) Exceptional performance strongly exceeding the requirements of the course, showing strong academic  
initiative and independent resourcefulness.  
\- B (GOOD) Performance above the norm; accurate and complete; beyond the minimum requirements of the course; work  
demonstrates marked progress and initiative.  
\- C (AVERAGE) Satisfactory work that adequately meets minimum requirements and demonstrates satisfactory  
comprehension, communication skills, and effort; demonstrates little initiative to investigate the problem without substantial  
input from the instructor; work shows little improvement.  
\- D (INFERIOR) Unsatisfactorily meets minimum requirements; demonstrates minimum comprehension, communication  
skills, and effort at an inferior level; initiative lacking; improvement not noticeable.  
\- F (FAIL) Does not meet minimum requirements; fails to adequately demonstrate comprehension, communication skills,  
and effort.  
All grading will be done by the instructor. Your grade will ultimately depend on the completion of all five modules in relation to  
all work done through the semester. Each student will receive a mid-semester performance evaluation based on attendance/  
participation and completed assignments.
