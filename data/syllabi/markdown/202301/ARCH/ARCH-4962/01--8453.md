## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course will celebrate the past, present and future of structural design as an art form. Students will be presented with background on the classification and layout of structural systems and inseparable they are in the creation of a work of architecture. The nature of collaboration and how to foster a successful collaboration between architect and structural engineer will be a primary focus of the class, developed through a conceptual project. This class will look at historical and contemporary precedents of structural art, from wrought iron to reinforced concrete to mass timber and explore what makes a work of architecture successful in this present time.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

No Course Learning Outcomes are available for this course.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

- Demonstrate basic principles of structural systems: gravity and lateral forces, superstructure and foundation systems. etc.
- Demonstrate basic understanding of load paths within building structural systems.
- Demonstrate key principles of material design within building structures.
- Demonstrate selection and application of appropriate structural systems and dimensions with rule of thumb span-to-depth ratios.
- Demonstrate design of key structural elements and components beam, columns, frames, foundations in multiple materials (wood, steel, and concrete) by hand or with analytical software (IE: SAP2000).
- Develop a complex understanding of strength and serviceability in the design of a successful structure using intuition and first principles.
- Tools for collaboration between architects and structural engineers.
