## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Develop an in depth understanding of the systems that provides human comfort in buildings for specific climatic zones.
- • Analyze the spatial requirements necessary for the building systems to support the various and unique programs that is encountered in modern buildings.
- • Examine the requirements of a studio conceptual design project and develop a human comfort system appropriate to the program and climatic conditions while ensuring that the architecture and engineering design are integrated and developed with sustainable principles.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Attendance and participation**

Attendance for lectures and pinups will be taken for each class automatically and accounts for 20% of your total grade. If a course cannot be attended live due to time zone difference or health-related reasons, it is the student’s responsibility to alert the instructor. All lecture slides and recordings will be posted to LMS.

**Group Design Charette**

Throughout the semester, students in groups of 3-4 will work together to complete the schematic design of a building. By the end of the semester, students will calculate, design and specify the building envelope and design the mechanical, electrical and plumbing systems. Lab time will be used to apply the concepts and calculations represented in the lecture in an iterative schematic design process. All members of the group are expected to participate equally and the work should demonstrate that.

**Midterm Exam & Final Exam**

Both Midterm and Final exam will be administered through LMS on the date noted on the syllabus. The exams will be based on content from the lecture presentations as well as calculations and methods exercised in the labs. They will consist of true/false, multiple choice and multiple answer questions as well as some calculations where scrap paper may be required.
