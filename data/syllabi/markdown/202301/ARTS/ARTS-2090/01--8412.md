### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Students will create digital matrices that translate to analog printmaking forms and interdisciplinary print media.

Students will learn analog printmaking technical skills and apply these skills for the purpose of communication and expression.

Students will conceptualize analog print media in a context of modern and contemporary art and design.

Students will place printmaking movements in a context of social movements.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

**Grading:**

Studio projects will be graded on a combination of technical execution, strength of concept, and overall effort.

Technical Execution:

-Did you utilize proper printmaking skills in creating your work?

-Did you properly use studio tools? Did you clean up your work area appropriately?

Strength of Concept:

-Is your work expressive, clever, novel, original, personal, political, weird?

Overall Effort:

-Did you participate in critique?

-Did you clean up and properly manage lab materials?

Late submissions will not be excepted, and will be given a failing grade for that project.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Academic Dishonesty

*The Rensselaer Handbook* defines various forms of academic dishonesty and procedures for responding to them. Students are responsible for familiarizing themselves with these policies. Students should note that the penalty for plagiarism and other forms of cheating is a grade of “F” for the course.

In this class “cheating” consists of plagiarism or representing the work of others as your own. This can include the deliberate or unintentional omission of acknowledgments or references. Cheating also consists of copying graphics, created by others and submitting that work as your own.
