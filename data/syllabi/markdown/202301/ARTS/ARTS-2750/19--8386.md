## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

1. **Course Description**

Private applied music lessons are offered to provide a fine arts experience for credit.

1. **Course Expectations**

\- Regular attendance at all scheduled lessons (50 min. x 15/semester).

\- 30 min. daily practice.

\- Satisfactory progress on repertoire studied, as determined by private instructor.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

**Required Course Materials**

\- Student-owned instrument except in the case of piano, percussion and some other large instruments.

\- Texts and repertoire determined by instructor.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students in the private lesson program will improve their ability to read, discuss, interpret, and perform a plethora of music composed for their individual instrument.
- Students will be able to expressively and musically interpret a variety of musical works/styles/genres.
- Students will further mastery of their chosen instrument.
- Students will improve listening skills, as well as mature in their ability to work at once independently.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Assessment of student progress is made based on effort and improvement over the course of the semester.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Work at home and during lessons: 100%

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these.
