## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**Course Description:** This course will center on applications of basic data analysis techniques such as data visualization, classification, clustering, and ridge regression. A case study approach will be used to provide immediate immersion into the problem of understanding high-dimensional data. The course will develop the necessary mathematics to understand and execute the techniques. Each student will execute a mini-data analysis project on a real-world data problem such as from engineering, medicine, and business. (From the Rensselaer Catalog 2022-2023.)

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Extract structure from large scale data using techniques such as principal component analysis and linear discriminant analysis,
- Derive information from data by interpreting and visualizing this extracted structure,
- Present the derived information in a professional way understandable by others,
- Demonstrate knowledge of the mathematical scaffolding needed for expressing and analyzing data problems,
- Demonstrate knowledge of basic linear algebra, and
- Pursue interests in high-dimensional mathematics and data analysis techniques applied to problems in science, engineering, and business using cyberinfrastructure.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Homework:** About every two weeks, there will be written homework assigned. Each of these assignments will be of equal weight. Homework will be submitted via Gradescope and will be due on Fridays at 11:59 PM. Late submissions will be subject to penalties in accordance with the late policy described below. (See Absences & Late Policies).

**Labs:** On most weeks, there will be computer labs assigned. The labs have a Pre-Lab with an associated quiz to be done by Wednesdays at 9:00 AM. The lab may have both in-class and out-of-class components. The in-class components will be done in the Wednesday sessions. The labs will be submitted online through Gradescope and are usually due on Tuesday by 10:00 AM. Later in the semester, students will work on group lab projects which will have both individual and group components contributing to the grade. The group projects will require group presentations.

**Exams:** Exams will be given in person, during lecture, as long as the Institute allows it. Details about each exam will be announced on LMS when the date of the exam is near. The dates for Exams 1 and 2 are listed below.

**Make-up exams will only be given to students with an** **excused absence documented by the** **Student Success office** **or, if the absence is due to an athletic competition, documentation from a coach.** Thus, if a student misses an exam because they overslept, didn’t know where the exam was, or didn’t know there was an exam, to give a few examples, the student may not take the make-up exam and will receive a score of 0 on the exam.

**Missing an exam should not be taken lightly.** Thus, students who miss an exam due to an excused absence must make every effort to notify the instructor before the exam begins to avoid grade penalties. If a student misses an exam due to an excused absence and notifies the instructor before the exam begins, then the student may take a make-up exam without penalty.

- If a student misses an exam due to an excused absence and notifies the instructor after the exam begins but before 11:59 PM the day after their scheduled exam, then the student may take a make-up exam and a 30% penalty will be applied to the make-up exam score.
- If a student misses an exam due to an excused absence and fails to notify the instructor before 11:59 PM on the day after their scheduled exam, then the student may not take the make-up exam and receives a score of 0 on the exam.
- If a student misses an exam and does not have an excused absence, then the student may not take the make-up exam and receives a score of 0 on the exam.

It is the sole responsibility of the student to notify the instructor to make arrangements for a make-up exam when an exam is missed due to an excused absence.

**Final Project:** The last few weeks of class will be devoted to a final project. There will be both individual and group components contributing to the grade. During the final exam slot, there will be a mini-conference where we will plan a video from each group, and then the group will answer questions.
