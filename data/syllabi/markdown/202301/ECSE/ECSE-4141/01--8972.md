## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

A cross-listed senior/graduate course that introduces students to electric power generation from renewable sources and its integration into the power grid. Topics include fundamentals of photovoltaic and wind energy; principles and control of solar and wind power plants; as well as operation and control of power systems with renewables. Renewable energy is essential for a decarbonized power sector. With the commitment by many governments to achieving carbon-neutrality by 2050, renewables will replace most thermal power plants in the next 20 years. Developing such a new power system is a grand challenge for electrical engineers. This course teaches the fundamentals that students need in order to tackle this challenge.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

**Textbook:** R. Teodorescu et al., *Grid Converters for Photovoltaic and Wind Power Systems*, IEEE-Wiley, 2011.

**Supplemental Materials:** B. Wu et al., *Power Conversion and Control of Wind Energy Systems*, IEEE-Wiley, 2011.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Power generation and carbon-free electricity; fundamentals of photovoltaic and wind energy; power converters and their control for renewable energy conversion and grid integration; photovoltaic power conversion requirements, efficiency and maximal power point tracking, solar inverters, and solar power plants; wind turbines and aerodynamic control, wind turbines based on synchronous generators, wind turbines based on doubly-fed induction generators (DFIG), wind power plants, and offshore wind; grid interconnection requirements for renewables, grid support functions, operation and control of power systems with renewables.
