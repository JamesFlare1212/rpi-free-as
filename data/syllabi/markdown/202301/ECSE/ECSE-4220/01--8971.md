### Additional Information for Course Information Section:

ECSE 4220, VLSI Design, is designed as an introductory course to modern VLSI circuit design. The MOS transistor model, circuit, and system aspects of VLSI design are covered in an integrated fashion. Emphasis is placed on CMOS technology. Laboratory experiments focus on layout analysis, computer-aided layout, and logic and timing simulation. Topics include: MOS fabrication, static and dynamic circuit design and layout, circuit performance estimation, Cadence tool usage, sequential circuits and timing issues, low-power circuit design techniques

## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**Catalog Description:**

ECSE 4220 - VLSI Design

Introduction to VLSI design. The fabrication, device, circuit, and system aspects of VLSI design are covered in an integrated fashion. Emphasis is placed on NMOS and CMOS technology. Laboratory experiments focus on layout analysis, computer-aided layout, and logic and timing simulation. Project on digital design with standard cells.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

- Required: Neil H.E. Weste and David Money Harris, *CMOS VLSI Design: A Circuits and Systems Perspective*, 4th Edition, Addison Wesley (2011).
- Optional: Erik Brunvand, *Digital VLSI Chip Design With Cadence & Synopsys Cad Tools* (2009).

**Recommended Reference Books:**

- Jan M. Rabaey et al., *Digital Integrated Circuits, A Design Perspective*, Second Edition, Prentice Hall (2002).
- Sung-Mo Kang and Yusuf Leblebici, *CMOS Digital Integrated Circuits : Analysis and Design*, Third Edition, McGraw-Hill (2002).

## Course Goals

### Goals:

To introduce the basic concepts and techniques in CMOS circuit design.

To present the critical VLSI design issues in the very deep sub-micrometer era.

To provide experience designing integrated circuits using commercial CAD Tools (CADENCE)

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- An ability to design the static complimentary and dynamic combinational circuits structure and determine the optimum transistor sizing
- An ability to draw the compact layout for CMOS circuits
- an ability to use commercial CAD tool Cadence for simulation and parasitic extraction for accurate modeling
- An ability to analyze and compare different circuit structures for latches and flip-flops in both static and dynamic logic

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:
