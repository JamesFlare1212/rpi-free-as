## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Semiconductor manufacturing is a highly complex endeavor, drawing on many engineering disciplines including Chemical, Mechanical, Electrical, Material, Industrial, Physics, and Computer Science. This course, taught by leading industry experts, will provide insight into key concepts driving the microelectronics industry. It is targeted towards advanced undergraduates and early graduate students. There will be a tour of GlobalFoundries Fab8 in Malta New York where students can see how all of these elements come together in one of the world most advanced manufacturing facility.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Student Learning Outcomes (for ECSE 4961): Students who successfully complete this course will be able to**

1. Grasp the fabrication processes and technologies for state-of-the-art computer chips, as well as the issues and solutions related to the fabrication failures and the reliability of the chips.
2. Demonstrate an ability to understand several aspects of the computer chip design, including those related to design automation, memory, radio frequency and silicon photonics.
3. Demonstrate an ability to carry out independent research on selected topics, and gain experience on technical presentations.

**Course Assessment Measures (Subject to change)**

- (i) Pre-lecture questionnaire (PLQ) for students to get prepared for the lecture.
- (ii) Homework assignments in the form of multiple choices and short answers.
- (iii) Mid-term papers/presentations or final papers/presentations; one submission per group. Students will present their projects during the class hours, so they will learn from peers.
  1. Undergraduate term paper may be based on lecture topics and/or related literature to summarize the key learning, provide feedback or questions, identify the critical innovations, and figure out the future challenges, as well as the potential solutions.
  2. Extra requirement for Graduate term paper is that it should be at a level of research report with in-depth analysis on chosen research papers published in top conferences/journals.
