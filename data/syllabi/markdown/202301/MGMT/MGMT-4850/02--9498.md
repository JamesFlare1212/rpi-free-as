## GRADING CRITERIA

**Exams (60%)**

Exams will be required for most of the textbook chapters. Each exam will be equally weighted.

# Group Projects (10%)

Each group will be responsible for writing a paper about AI/ML in organizational behavior and making a class presentation. Information on both will be provided early in the semester. I will judge the quality of the group presentations and the class will provide input. Late submissions will be penalized by 10 points.

Relevant criteria for judging the presentations will include:

- (1) Scope - did the presentation cover the relevant material in sufficient breadth and depth?
- (2) Clarity - was the explanation of ideas and concepts clear?
- (3) Quality of analysis - how well did the group analyze the main issues?
- (4) Quality of application – how well did the group connect conceptual material to the topic of the presentation?
- (5) Contribution – to what extent did the group increase our understanding of the topic? and
- (6) Interest – how well did the group hold our interest?

**Individual Assignments (10%)**

You will submit one individual assignment about communication in the organizational context. The assignment should be analytical and reflective rather than simply descriptive. Description tells what happened. Analysis assesses how and why it happened. Any description should serve the analysis. That is, it should consider the whys? and hows? rather than simply reporting the whats? Carefully think through the issues and present a logically reasoned argument to support your point of view. Try to incorporate material from relevant classwork, chapters, readings, and/or lectures. The assignment should be submitted by the dates they are due. Late submissions will be penalized by 10 points.

# Attendance and Contributions to the Class (20%)

Class participation is an integral aspect of this course. Please read and be prepared to discuss the assigned readings and cases listed on the syllabus for each session. Participation includes active contributions to your group, both during class activities and for the group paper and presentation. Quantity and quality will be considered in evaluating participation. If you are interested in participating but have difficulty initiating it, please let me know so that we can try to ease the process.
