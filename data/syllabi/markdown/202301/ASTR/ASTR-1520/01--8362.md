## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course serves as an introduction to astronomy, focusing on understanding the physical nature of stars and galaxies, the vastness of the cosmos, and how astronomers have built up a comprehensive picture of the Universe in which we live through meticulous study. (The material covered in this course is complementary to that covered in the Earth & Sky lecture course.)

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will learn: the basic properties of stars, such as size, mass, temperature, and what makes them emit light; how stars and their solar systems form; how stars evolve and die; properties of exotic objects such as white dwarfs, neutron stars and black holes; the cosmic distance scale; the formation, structure and evolution of galaxies (including our own Milky Way) and galaxy clusters over cosmic time; the (accelerating) expansion of the Universe; and the possibilities for life elsewhere in the Universe.
- Students will apply their knowledge of learned physical processes towards interpreting observations and inferring the nature of entities/phenomena in the Universe and the Universe itself. These skills will be exercised through exams, online homeworks, and discussion/activity sessions.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Exams (30%): There will be a total of two 2-hour exams, each worth 15% of your final grade, held in the usual classroom during normal class hours (see the attached schedule for dates). A review for each exam will be held during the preceding class. You may take these exams at a different time ONLY with a prior notice of absence or an official excuse. The exams will consist of multiple-choice questions based on the material presented in lectures and activities. If you have any accommodation, such as for extra time on exams, please email the professor with the appropriate documentation ***at least* 1 week** before the first exam.

Homework (25%): Homework assignments are accessed through the homework folder on the LMS homepage for this course. At the beginning of the semester each student must register for Smartwork5 (Digital Resources for 21st Century Astronomy (wwnorton.com)) which is the digital resource associated with the text. Clicking the homework hyperlink assignment in LMS will bring you to the Smartwork5 page. The questions are well worded and follow the text/lectures closely. Each assignment consists of roughly 13 conceptual questions to test your knowledge of the material. For each question you will have 7 chances to answer. For each incorrect attempt your score for that question will be reduced by 15%. Assignments will be made available as we cover those respective chapters of the book. It is important to stay on top of these assignments and to regularly check the Smartwork website from the homework hyperlink.

Activities (25%): Part of class each Thursday (when not having an exam) will be dedicated to a collaborative worksheet activity which will be available on LMS. The activity will be worked on during class time and will be submitted using LMS. While it is the intention that you should be able to complete the activities during class time, you will have until 11:59 pm the same night to submit your assignment. Collaboration and discussion with others during this time is not only allowed, but encouraged.  
Worksheets are graded for correctness and count toward the overall grade. Access to these activities will be given during the lecture, meaning attendance to these sessions is required to complete the activity. If you are unable to attend the lecture you will be able to make up the activity *only if you provide notice of an excused absence*. Unexcused absences from these assignments will be reflected in the student’s grade. There will be 11 activities, and your *lowest activity score will be dropped.*

Comprehensive Final Exam (20%): This exam is *mandatory*, and can only be taken at a different time under extreme circumstances. It will cover all material covered in the course. The final exam will be designed to take about 2 hours to complete, but 3 hours will be allotted. Date, time, and location to be announced during the semester.

All exams will be **closed-book** and **closed-note**. You will be allowed a **calculator** on all exams. For each exam you will be allowed one side of an **8.5” x 11” sheet of paper** for a crib sheet (you will be allowed both sides of a full sheet for the final).

Any interaction with a wireless device (cell phone, iPad, laptop, etc.) during an exam (except when explicitly indicated otherwise by the instructor) will be considered an illicit data exchange and result in a zero for the entire exam.

**Final Grade**
