## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Directional derivatives, maxima and minima, double integrals, line integrals, div and curl, and Green’s Theorem; matrix algebra and systems of linear equations, vectors and linear transformations in R^n, eigenvectors and eigenvalues, applications in engineering and science.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Calculus: Early Transcendentals, 4th Edition (Rogawski, et al)  
Introduction toLinear Algebra, 5th Edition (Johnson, et al)

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will learn derivatives and integrals of functions of multiple variables
- Students will learn vector analysis and Green's Theorem
- Students will learn Gaussian elimination
- Students will learn to compute eigenvalues and eigenvectors of matrices

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Weekly quizzes, 3 midterm exams, 1 final exam

## Policies

### Other Course Policies:

**There will be no make ups for quizzes.** You can only be excused from a quiz if an [excused absence notice from the Student Success office](https://success.studentlife.rpi.edu/current-students/academic-and-personal-goals/requesting-excused-absence) is received by the instructor or, if the absence is due to an athletic competition, documentation from a coach is received by the instructor.

**Homework:** Problems will be assigned from the textbook and will be posted on [LMS](http://lms.rpi.edu/). Though these problems will not be collected nor graded, they should not be viewed as optional. Success in the course depends on successful completion of all homework. Quiz problems are expected to be very similar to homework problems or examples from lecture.

**Missing an exam should not be taken lightly.** Thus, students who miss an exam must make every effort to notify the four instructors **as soon as possible** to avoid grade penalties.

- If a student misses an exam and fails to notify the instructors before noon on the day after the exam, then the student may not take the make-up exam and receives a score of 0 on the exam.
- If a student misses an exam and notifies the instructor before noon on the day after the exam, then the student may take a make-up exam and a 30% penalty will be applied to the make-up exam score. The 30% penalty will be removed if an [excused absence notice from the Student Success office](https://success.studentlife.rpi.edu/current-students/academic-and-personal-goals/requesting-excused-absence) is received by the instructor or, if the absence is due to an athletic competition, documentation from a coach is received by the instructor before noon on the day after the exam.

The make-up exam policy above applies to Exams 1–3. It is the student’s responsibility to communicate with the instructor in a timely manner in the case of missing an exam so arrangements can be made for a make up.

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).
