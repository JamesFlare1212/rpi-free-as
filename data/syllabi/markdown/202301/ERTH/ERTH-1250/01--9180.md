### Criteria Details:

Each lab is worth 20 points. Labs will be completed and turned in and graded on completion, effort and correctness. Each lab will be due at the start of the next lab period.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

**Attendance:** Geology is an observational science, and as such, laboratory sessions provide an excellent opportunity to learn geologic concepts and methods though applied activities. As such, attendance is required! There are **NO**excused absences for lab. ***The lowest lab grade will be dropped***, in order to allow every student the opportunity to miss one lab due to illness, a schedule conflict with a game, etc., without penalty. Make-up labs are very difficult to arrange and are impossible in the case of field outings, so if you know you will be missing more than one lab, you need to work with Dr. Cadieux in advance. If you have a conflict but are able to attend a different lab section, please let Dr. Cadieux know in advance.

### Other Course Policies:

**Tardiness:** Being on time for lab is critical in order to have enough time to complete the assignment!

**Late Policy**: Labs will generally be due at the end of the lab period. If you need additional time, you can turn in at the beginning of the next lab session. Labs turned in after this will receive 10% deduction for each day late. Late labs will not be accepted after 1 week.

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. ### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Working together and plagiarism:** Science is a collaborative subject! You are strongly encouraged to discuss course material with your classmates. Working together allows students to ask each other questions and brainstorm ideas together, but the resulting work should reflect each student’s own understanding of the topic. On labs where you are allowed to share results with your peers, specific instructions will be provided. However, unless specifically told otherwise, you should not be sharing your written or drawn work for submission with classmates. ***Submission of any work that is not your own will result in a failing grade for the course.***
