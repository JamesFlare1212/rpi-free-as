## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course will focus on learning about geological environment of humankind: the atmosphere, oceans, groundwater, rivers, glaciers, deserts, and coasts. We will explore the processes by which these and other features develop and change, the opportunities or hazards they present, and the ways in which humans can modify their development.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understand how wind, water, ice and biology can change the surface of the Earth
- Understand Earth’s climate system
- Apply principles of logic and reasoning to analyze geologic problems
- Understand how geology relates to your everyday life, and how you interact with the Earth
- Travel to new and exciting places and explain to friends and family how those landscapes formed and may change in the future

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Engagement:** Research has shown that regular engagement with material through the semester increases retention and also leads to better grades. There will be a variety of engagement strategies employed throughout the semester (before class, in class, after class) in order to improve your learning and retention.

- I will often request that you complete a brief exercise prior to lecture, on your own, as a warm-up for class. This may mean reading an article, watching a video or looking at a dataset. For most pre-class engagement, you will complete a short assignment on BlackBoard. These will be due at the beginning of class and available on LMS at least a week in advance.
- In class, you will work independently and in small groups on activities, taking quizzes to gage overall classroom understanding, and generating discussion. Occasionally, if an assignment is not completed in class, you will be asked to complete it as homework and turn in the next class period.
- After class, you may be given a short quiz on BlackBoard OR be given a writing prompt to respond to on BlackBoard in the form of a minute paper in order to gage comprehension.
- If you miss engagement work due to an emergency or other unexpected circumstance that arises, please talk with me as soon as possible so we can make arrangements to get you caught up. There are no make-ups for missed engagement assignments. However, the lowest 20% will be dropped at the end of the semester (The dropped scores act as your missing class freebies and can be used for any reason--illness, studying, travel, wild monkeys breaking into your dorm room, binge watching Netflix, etc.)

**Journal Entries:** The geology journal is designed to help you be reflective about what you are learning in class and connect course material to your everyday life. During the semester, you will submit **three** journal entries. Additional information on journals is available on LMS.

**Quizzes:** There will be 8 quizzes throughout the semester to check for content understanding and to ensure you are engaging in distributive studying. Each quiz will be administered at the start of class on Mondays. Late arrivals will not be given additional time. One make-up will be allowed for quizzes but must be made-up within 48 hours and arranged prior to the quiz. Additionally, the lowest quiz grade will be dropped at the end of the semester. The dates of the quizzes are on the schedule and will not change.

**Exams:** There will be 3 exams throughout the semester. Each exam will cover the topics preceding that exam (**not cumulative**-however, keep in mind that we will be building upon prior knowledge). There will be **no** make-up of exams without a legitimate excuse discussed **prior** to the exam (I reserve the right to determine what is “legitimate”).

**Optional Final Exam**: During finals week, there will be an optional cumulative final exam. If you are happy with all of your exam scores, you do not need to take this. However, if you are unsatisfied with one of your exam scores, taking the final will replace the exam grade for your lowest exam. If you score lower on the Final than on all of your exam scores, the Final Exam will not be used in your final grade calculation. This is your opportunity to go back, revisit material and try again!

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

**Attendance:** I assuming you are coming to learn. Attendance will not be taken, as I also assume you are responsible for your own education and are paying for this course. Nevertheless, you should know that poor attendance at lectures is generally associated with a poor final grade. The lectures will contain material not covered in readings. Class notes will be posted on LMS. However, do not assume that looking at the lectures without coming to class will afford you the knowledge you need to pass the class.

### Other Course Policies:

**Late Policy:** If an assignment is due in class, it will be due at the start of class. If it is turned in at the end of class, it is considered late. Assignments will be accepted up to 1 week past the due date. Scores on late assignments will be penalized 10% each day they are late. So, if an assignment is due in class on a Tuesday at 2:00 pm and you turn it in between Tuesday at 2:01 pm and Wednesday at 2:00 pm, you will lose 10%. *After 1 week, the grade becomes a zero.*

**Accommodations:** If you need any special accommodations (learning challenges, religious holiday conflicts, planned classes you will be missing), please set up a meeting with Dr. Cadieux during the first 2 weeks of classes.

4. Make sketches as notes!
5. All powerpoint notes will be posted after class. Instead of focusing on getting down all the words on the slide, focus on key phases, questions, and concepts. Your notes should be in our own words, not mine!
6. Don’t forget to take notes on class discussions, activities and videos
7. Allow yourself to have fun!
