## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- (1) Identify the common minerals found in igneous and metamorphic rocks using hand sample and thin section identification
- (2) Use the petrographic microscope for the identification of minerals in thin section
- (3) Use the petrographic microscope for the recognition of important textural relationships in thin section
- (4) Assess the conditions of metamorphism through identification and analysis of mineral assemblages
- (5) Interpret the sequence of mineral growth, reaction relationships, and the significance of common textural relationships
- (6) Perform basic thermodynamic calculations that reveal the relative stability of common phases assemblages in igneous and metamorphic rocks
- (7) Interpret and analyze the phase relations of igneous and metamorphic rocks using a variety of composition and free energy diagrams
- (8) Discuss the current theories regarding the origin, evolution, and emplacement of common igneous and metamorphic rocks.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Rensselaer does not tolerate cheating. In my courses, collaboration in the form of discussion and sharing of ideas is not only permitted but is encouraged between students working on problem sets, laboratory exercises, field projects, and when studying for examinations. However, the final write-up of any problem set, field report, laboratory exercise or paper must be a student’s individual work. That is, under no circumstances shall a student turn in a problem set or a laboratory exercise that is a copy or paraphrase of another student's work. Collaboration of any sort (oral, written or otherwise) during an examination period is strictly prohibited.
