## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is an introductory course to simulation, a numerical approach that uses computers to model complex stochastic systems that are difficult to understand via analytical methods. We will explore simulation types, the foundations of random variable generation for both discrete and continuous cases, queuing modeling, and input/output analysis. General principles of simulation modeling will be covered using FlexSim (https://www.flexsim.com/) software, Microsoft Excel, and Python. The second part of the course focuses on statistical analysis and validation techniques. Upon completion of the course, students will be able to develop complex simulation models, design the simulation experiment to be run on the model, and analyze and interpret the results. Along with it, the management of simulation projects is also addressed.

**Tests:** The students will have two mini-exams. The exam #2 covers all concepts throughout the semester and has two parts: Theory and Practical.

**Homework Assignments:**

- Four homework sets will be assigned.

- Students must illustrate their understanding of the material, which means that copying solutions from previous semesters or book solutions verbatim is not allowed and is considered a form of academic dishonesty. Homework is meant to be a learning tool.
- *If you are having difficulty, find help right away – don’t wait until you fall even further behind!*
