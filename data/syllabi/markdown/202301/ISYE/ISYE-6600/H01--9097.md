### Instructor Note & Getting Started

Welcome to ISYE 6600: Design of Manufacturing Systems and Supply Chains! My role will be to assist and guide you in completing the project-based learning activities for this course. This course is designed to provide students with practice applying analytical and numerical methods to solving supply chain design and execution problems.

Before you get started, review this document to familiarize yourself with the course, the course structure, expectations, outcomes, and the flow of work.

## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

### Course Description

In this class you will learn a mix practical tools and use them to explore a variety of supply chain activities, both analytically and numerically. BOM management, Modeling, data analysis, network creation, forecasting and other mathematical techniques are used to examine data to understand both micro and macro levels of supply chain execution, design, and outputs for managerial recommendations.  
**Credits Allocated:** 3 semester hours

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understand and apply the Supply Chain SCOR model
- Create and manage a bill of materials and look for improvement opportunities.
- Develop and apply inventory models with focus on key levels of service
- Develop a working knowledge of Enterprise Resource Planning Systems.
- Understand the role and advantages of various modes of transportation.
- Design a supply chain network for regional and/or global requirements.
- Recognize the role of sourcing for either new suppliers or applying vendor management for existing suppliers.
- Document and present a representative Sales & Operations Planning process deliverable.
