## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**COURSE DESCRIPTION**

This course is a discussion-based investigation of the history of chemistry, from its earliest beginnings until the middle of the 20th Century. Emphasis on the thought processes that transformed the art into the science, the transition from alchemy to chemistry, the insights that gave rise to what might be called “MODERN CHEMISTRY”, and an in-depth analysis of the personalities of the main players involved in the development of the field will be covered.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**STUDENT LEARNING OUTCOMES**

- ) Students will learn to read historical scientific texts, understand their contribution to our understanding of Nature, identify their limitations, and be able to discern the quality of the information recorded in the chemical literature.

- ) Students will learn the contributions of key figures in the development of chemistry and of the scientific method.

- ) Students will learn how to access and use online resources to locate relevant information regarding the topics of interest.

- ) Students will present their work as a paper AND a final presentation, which will focus on a particular chemist and his or her contribution to our understanding of Nature.

## Other Course-Specific Information

### Additional Course Information:

**SUCCEEDING IN THIS COURSE**

The purpose of this course is to read ahead of time and be prepared to discuss the topic and make contributions to the dialogue.

To be successful, you must:

- **READ CAREFULLY** the chapter in your textbook **before** it is introduced in lecture. This way you will already know which chemical concepts you do not fully understand, and you can pay extra attention to those points during the lecture. Take notes and highlight those areas that are confusing or hard to understand.

- Use online resources to further increase your understanding of the material.

- Bring the textbook and notes you may have taken to each and every class.

- Don’t miss any classes.

- Check the LMS daily for new information, homework assignments, useful links, etc.

- Don’t hesitate to ask questions and come to see me during office hours if you need the extra help
