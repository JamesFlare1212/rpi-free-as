## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An introductory course focused on analyzing and creating art and graphics designed for screen-based environments, with a bias toward asset production and workflows related to 2D and 3D game development

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Rapid Viz worksheets

## Course Goals

### Goals:

To explore the creation of art for interactive media using basic visualization (drawing), basic design principles, an overview of color theory, and exposure to 2d and 3d tools.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Introduction to Drawing, Visualizing, and Conceptualizing

Perspective

Basic Shape and Construction

Shading and Value

Elements and Principles of Design

Brief History/overview of Art

Brief History/overview of Games

Intro to 2d graphics tools

Basic Color Theory

Intro to 3d graphics tools

Breakdown of Games Elements:

Considering Characters and Animation

Objects and Hazards

Environments and Scenes

Special Effects

Interface
