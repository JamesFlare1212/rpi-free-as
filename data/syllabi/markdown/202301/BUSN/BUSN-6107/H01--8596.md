## Course Description

Change and innovation occurs through the people of an organization. Students in this course use mentoring and coaching tools to invoke the highest performance from employees, and to focus on the achievement of organizational goals and strategy. Students in this course are mentored by a Master Mentor, and in turn use that methodology with three mentees to build their own mentorship capacity. Students help their mentees set SMART goals, make recommendations for performance improvement, and evaluate the achievement of goals. By using a mentorship approach, students ensure that each individual is directly aligned with, and is contributing to the organization’s change and innovation.

***Credits Allocated: 3 semester hours***

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Determine the degree of change or innovation needed in order for the unit or organization to achieve its objectives.
- Communicate change or innovation as a purposeful activity to improve performance.
- Articulate change or innovation as a set of performance metrics to be achieved.
- Deploy the Rensselaer Development Model as a mentoring framework to engage employees and invoke the highest individual and unit performance.
- Use expertise, reflective listening, SMART goal setting, and planning to align people with organizational and unit direction.
- Facilitate meaningful and purposeful meetings with employees to achieve results.
- Deploy the individual problem-solving technique to help employees diagnose and solve problems.
