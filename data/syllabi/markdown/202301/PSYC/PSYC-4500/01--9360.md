## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

There are two textbooks: Martha S. Rosenthal's DRUGS, mind, body and society and Larry Reid & Metal Reid's Big Booze is Slowly, Softly, Killing Women.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course provides critical information on commonly used drugs. Such information is often critical in as much as drugs can be fatally toxic.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

The content closely follows the chapters in the Rosenthal textbook and the other textbook provides a thorough look at alcoholism and its effects on women.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

No Course Learning Outcomes are available for this course.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

The goal of this course is to have students thoroughly understand the content of the two textbooks, which provides critical information on the effects of drugs on the person, and the society. Students demonstrate their learning by multiple tests. Data show that learning is best achieved by reading books and frequent testing. The Rosenthal book provides testing post a chapter of reading and the course's tests are used to verify that the goal of learning the course's content embodied in the two textbooks.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

100% of the grade is based on performance of multiple tests.  
There is a built-in ability to enhance performance on a test, because all tests are given twice to ensure adequate understanding of key concepts and facts.  
Any student can appeal a grade by sending me an e-mail and I will closely examine the basis for the assigned grade.  
The students test-scores are immediately available via LMS, hence they have a running sense of their pending grade.

Each student is advised to set aside time each week for reading and studying the textbooks. Attentive reading is known to be the best way to master complex material. If that reading is accompanied by frequent testing, such has been proven to be the most efficient way of learning complex content.
