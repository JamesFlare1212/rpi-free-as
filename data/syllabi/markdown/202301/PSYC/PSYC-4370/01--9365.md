## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Cognitive psychology is the scientific discipline that attempts to answer questions about how the human mind works through behavioral, computational, and biological experimentation and investigation. Cognitive psychology seeks to uncover the processes and mechanisms that underlie how sensory information and the knowledge we gain through learning and experience are used to guide our actions and overcome problems and obstacles.

## Policies

### Other Course Policies:

Assignments will be infrequent and (therefore) significant undertakings. These will take the form of a recorded presentation or a written paper. There will be three of these throughout the semester (roughly evenly-spaced).

Quizzes will be non-cumulative, open note, and given in class. They will be organized by topic. You are required to bring a laptop to class to complete the quiz on quiz days. If you prefer to complete quizzes on paper, let me know.

Presentations will be short and sweet, concise and thorough. These will be given live to a small group of classmates. You will be graded on both your presentation itself and feedback provided on others’ presentations.

Participation refers to in-class participation in discussions, both at the overall level and within small groups when appropriate. Attendance is not explicitly taken, but it’ll be impossible to get participation credit without actually being present in class.

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these.
