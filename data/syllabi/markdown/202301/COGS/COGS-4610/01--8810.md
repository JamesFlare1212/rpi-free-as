### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Participation and attendance (20%):** Attendance and active engagement in class is mandatory. Students will receive an average grade for class participation and attendance. I will be posting participation challenges on LMS that can be completed for participation points as well.

**Assignments**

**Journal Article Critiques (10%):** Students will be responsible for reading and writing a critique of 5 journal articles. Each article will be discussed in class (see schedule). These critiques should be brief (1 page, single-spaced), include student’s thoughts about the work, and submitted on LMS.

**Group presentation (20%):** Working in groups, students will complete a research project that they will present as a poster at the end of the semester. Groups will be based upon similar topical interests and scheduled early in the semester.

**Reflection paper assignment (10%):** Students will be asked to complete one reflection paper. We will discuss details later this semester. Reflection papers should be 2-3 pages, double-spaced, with 12 point font.

**\*\* Any assignment that is worked on outside of class needs to be submitted via the LMS link by 11:59 pm on its due date to be considered on time. See lateness policy below for more details.\*\***

**Quizzes & Exams**

**Quizzes (10%):** Quizzes will be given most weeks on the dates indicated in schedule (most Mondays). These quizzes will be multiple-choice, true/false or fill-in-the-blank questions. Students will be expected to answer question associated with assigned readings and the lecture materials from the previous lecture(s). No make-up quizzes will be permitted. I will drop the lowest quiz grade for each student when calculating the average quiz grade.

**Exams (30%; 10% each):** There will be three exams during the semester consisting of multiple-choice, short answer, and true/false questions. Questions will be related to *all* materials (e.g. lectures, readings from text, journal articles, films watched) covered in the class up until when the exam is given.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Attendance of our in-person class is mandatory. Attendance will be taken during each class meeting. Any unexcused absences will be reflected by a lower attendance grade. Certain situations do arise that preclude coming to class that are allowable, which would be excused absences. For example, if you are not feeling well or cannot come to class because of other health related issues, it is expected that you contact me and do not come to the classroom; in this case, you can attend via WebEx. If you need to obtain an official excuse for an absence, please contact the Office of Student Success success@rpi.edu. Make-up exams may be given with documentation of these valid excuses; however, the make-up exam may be different than that given in class and may take a different form. There are no make-up quizzes. Please note: If you are late, not fully participating, or leave early (without prior approval), points will be deducted from your attendance/participation grade.

### Other Course Policies:

***Lateness policy*** Late assignments will be accepted, but for each day an assignment is late, 5 points will be deducted from the grade and assignments will only be accepted up to one week late. Please email me in the case of extenuating circumstances and difficulties completing assignments BEFORE they are due.

***Incomplete Grades*** In the case of an extended excused absence from class, and inability to complete coursework by the end of the semester, an Incomplete may be given. The student needs to contact me to work out the logistics and deadlines for the Incomplete before the last day of class. This is worked out on a case-by-case basis with me.

***Extra-credit opportunity*** I encourage students to learn about the material outside of class. Students can earn extra credit by attending an event or doing an activity (even additional article critiques) related to the class material and submitting a concise one-page summary of what you did what you learned from this experience. Students can earn one point to be added to their final averages for each assignment completed well. Students can turn in a maximum of 3 assignments. All extra credit assignments are due by the last day of class, but can be turned in at any point during the semester.

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).
