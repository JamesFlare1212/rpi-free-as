COURSE DESCRIPTION

This course introduces students to the practice of graphic design with a variety of hands-on projects. Studies include the principles and theories of two-dimensional design and the integration of form, color, type, image and content. Students will develop a heightened visual awareness and an under- standing of how design influences meaning. The course emphasizes strategic and critical thinking in the solving of graphic design problems for the purposes of professional communication across a range of media platforms. In addition students will study the basic functions and integral properties in the following applications–Adobe Illustrator, InDesign, Photoshop and After Effects to create solu- tions for various graphic design projects.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- LEARNING OUTCOMES Upon completion of this course, students will demonstrate an ability to: 1. understand and explain visual communication and graphic design concepts and terms. 2. use design thinking, techniques and theories through guided projects and readings to visually solve communication problems and increase visual literacy. 3. understand how design thinking and the utilization of graphic design principles can be applied to solving marketing,engineering and other design application problems. 4. learn the basics of the following design software while creating documents for all class projects: Adobe Illustrator, Adobe InDesign, Adobe PhotoShop, Adobe After Effects 5. develop and demonstrate team working skills through group interaction, critique, problem solving and verbal expression

CLASS CRITIQUES

Critiques will be provided at comp stages and upon completion. A critique is part of the studio course environment. In the critique process each student will pin-up their work in the lab (inperson classes only) and/or on the class BOX and each student will have an opportunity to present and speak about their work. The class is invited to comment and give constructive feedback. The critique by the instruc- tor will be given before the entire class and is also instructive. Students are encouraged to speak about everyone’s work using the terminology and design language we are developing in class. If you have any questions about the evaluation of your work see me at your earliest convenience. If a student feels un- justly criticized or unfairly singled out and feels emotionally unsettled by the forum of the critique the student should inform me of this fact privately and quickly.

Note: Class critiques for comp work, final projects or formal focus start on time. If you are not ready by the start of class you will not get a critique. (in person classes - you will not be allowed to print during the critique ).

- Line Compositions (5%) A series of design studies using line to express emotion.

- Motion Graphics (12%) Introduces students to the fundamental concepts of motion graphics and
