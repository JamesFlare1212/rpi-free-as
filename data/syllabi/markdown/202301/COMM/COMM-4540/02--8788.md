## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course will give you tools to critically examine the barrage of images we encounter daily and understand how these images affect us emotionally as individuals, how they influence the way we think and understand our reality, and in turn, how we conduct our lives together in the world. We’ll examine a broad range of visual media from photographs, art, television, and film to social media platforms and new media technologies (e.g., YouTube, TikTok and VR). We’ll also spend some time examining how “visuality” is experienced and expressed for those who are blind or visually disabled, and how visual media plays a role in witnessing traumatic events and their political and cultural implications.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will be able to analyze visual images in terms of their cultural meaning and affective impact.
- Students will be able to discuss and to write about how we create and are created by the visual media environment that we inhabit.
- Students will learn key concepts in the study of visual culture and be able to produce their own visually based project engaging these concepts.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

- Class Participation
- Midterm Exam on key concepts
- One Short Response Paper (1.5-2 pages)
- One Longer Paper written outside of class (4-6 pages)
- One Group Project including presentation
- Final Exam on key concepts

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process.
