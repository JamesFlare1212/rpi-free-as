## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

# **Course Overview**

The purpose of this course is to introduce the basics of strategic communication in digital and social media platforms, with a specific interest in the applications of digital and social media for communication professionals. In this course, students will learn key principles and techniques for digital content creation and promotion. Students will brainstorm, conceptualize, and develop an online presence and apply writing, research, and critical thinking to the practice of strategic communication. **Note: Your continued enrollment beyond the first day of class affirms your willingness to abide by the conditions set forth in this document.**
