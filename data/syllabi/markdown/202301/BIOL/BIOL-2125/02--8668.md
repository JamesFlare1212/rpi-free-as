## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The goal of this course is to gain practical experience with cellular and molecular biology through hands-on experimental techniques. The laboratory exercises are designed to illustrate current concepts in cellular and molecular biology. This is the laboratory course that accompanies the lecture BIOL-2120.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

None – laboratory manuals will be proved as PDFs on BIOL-2125 Lab LMS. A physical lab notebook will **not** be needed, but you **MUST bring your laptop to lab** to record that day's work in your digital notebook.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

1. The student will learn to successfully perform common techniques currently used in prokaryotic and eukaryotic cell biology research.
2. The student will generate, analyze, and interpret data from experimental techniques.
3. The student will demonstrate proper documentation of laboratory work by keeping a laboratory notebook.
4. The student will demonstrate effective communication through the oral presentation of their work.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Pre-lab Questions**

- ­ Pre-lab questions can be found on BIOL-2125 Lab LMS. These questions will also appear in your lab manual, but they **must be submitted to LMS for a grade**. These assignments must be submitted **before you meet for that week's lab**.

**Quizzes**

- ­ There will be 3 lab quizzes held during the semester. All quizzes are conducted through the BIOL-2125 Lab LMS; each quiz will be open for a period from 6AM to midnight on Wednesdays of the weeks specified in the *Lab Schedule*. Quizzes are timed**. Group work is NOT allowed** on quizzes; plagiarism and other forms of cheating will result in at minimum the **loss of all credit for the assessment** on the first offence and potentially an **F for the course.** A second offence will always result in an **F for the course**.

**Notebook Checks**

- ­ All students will keep a lab notebook. Your lab notebook will be proved to you as a Google Docs file that you can edit; physical notebooks will not be used. You **MUST bring your laptop to all in-person labs** for the purposes of recording that day's work. Notebook checks will be conducted on the dates listed in the *Lab Seclude*; on these dates you are expected to export your Google Docs notebook as a PDF file and submit it to BIOL-2125 Lab LMS **before the start of lab**. More detailed instructions are given on LMS.

**Student Presentation**

- ­ Students will work as pairs during the semester; you will give a presentation at the end of the semester with your lab partner. The presentation will be based on the data that you collected during one of our lab sessions. A PDF version of your presentation slides must be submitted on LMS, and you will give your presentation in lab or on WebEx depending on COVID-19 restrictions at the end of the semester.

**Lab Citizenship**

- ­ Good lab citizenship includes being on time, not eating or drinking in lab, proper sharing of instruments, maintaining a clean workspace, following instructions, and social distancing. Violation of these rules or any rule listed under the *COVID-19 Precautions & Restrictions* and *Lab Safety* sections will result in loss of points.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

**ATTENDANCE POLICIES**

- Attendance is **REQUIRED** for all labs. Unexcused absences are not allowed; missing a lab will result in a **deduction of 10****%** from your lab grade.
- If you miss a lab, you must obtain a Dean’s excuse. Students who need to report an illness should contact the Student Health Center via email or call 518-276-6287. Then, you must **submit this documentation to BIOL-2125 Lab LMS under the "Excused Absences" section**. For example, if you receive a Dean's excuse or doctor’s note via email, "print" this email as a PDF and submit the PDF to LMS; if you receive physical documentation, you must scan this document and then submit the scanned image to LMS. Your lab TA/instructor tracks attendance, and this is the only way in which they will know about the excused absence.
- For any excused absence from lab, your lab work must still be made up. If you foresee yourself missing a lab, contact your lab TA and instructor well ahead of time. For any absence, it is your responsibility to coordinate a make-up time. Failure to make up lab work will result in the same penalty as missing the lab. When a make-up lab is not feasible (e.g., when quarantining), an online make-up lab will be offered.

### Other Course Policies:

**COVID-19 PRECAUTIONS & RESTRICTIONS**

- **Masks**
  - ­ **N95** **or KN95 masks are to be worn by all students and faculty in BIOL-2125 labs**. Masks must be worn properly, covering both the mouth and nose.
  - ­ Students who violate this policy by refusing to wear an N95 or KN95 mask or refusing to wear one correctly will not be allowed to participate in lab and will be recorded as absent. As a reminder, absences will result in a **deduction of 10%** from your lab grade per absence.
- **Cleaning of Spaces**
  - ­ Students are required to disinfect their work surfaces in the lab before they begin work and after they have finished for the day. 70% ethanol will be provided for disinfection. Disinfecting lab chairs and stools is also encouraged. This is standard procedure for this lab irrespective of COVID precautions.
- **Student Health**
  - ­ Students who are ill, under quarantine for COVID-19, or suspect they are ill must report this to Student Life. Once faculty notification is made, your instructors will make every reasonable effort to accommodate the student’s absence and will communicate that accommodation directly to the student. However, to maintain accurate attendance tracking, **your instructor/TA must be informed through BIOL-2125 lab LMS** (see *Attendance Policies*).
- Refusal to comply with these rules will be treated as a classroom disruption and disciplinary actions and sanctions will be taken through the judicial process in the Student Handbook.

**LAB SAFETY**

- Follow all rules that your lab TA or instructor issue.
- Do not engage in any activities that will endanger yourself or those around you.
- **Wear gloves** when interacting with any laboratory equipment and **change gloves often**. You will be provided gloves.
- **You must wear safety glasses or goggles** at all times during lab except when using microscopes - disinfect eye pieces of microscopes with ethanol before and after use.
- Wear clothing that entirely covers the legs and torso. Lab coats are optional but encouraged; lab coats are not provided.
- You must wear shoes that **enclose the entire foot**; footwear like sandals and crocs are not allowed.
- Report any spills or accidents to your TA or instructor **immediately**.
- When finished, you must clean your work area and return it to the condition you found it in; **disinfect your work areas with 70% ethanol**.
- Follow traffic flow guides in lab; do not crowd other students while they are working at a shared location or on shared equipment.
- Always **wear your N95 or KN95 mask correctly** while in lab.
- **Use of cell phones is not allowed in lab except when indicated by the TA, instructor, or lab manual**.
- Book bags, backpacks, and other personal items must be stored under your lab bench, away from other student's belongings. Be sure to avoid making your personal belongings a tripping hazard.
- Violation of any rules stated here, in your lab manual, or verbally by your lab TA or instructor will impact your **Lab Citizenship grade**, and major violations can have you removed from the lab.
