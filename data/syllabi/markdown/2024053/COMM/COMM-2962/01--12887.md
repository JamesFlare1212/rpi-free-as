## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Language serves a medium through which we express not only ideas, but also our identities, which establish our positions in the social world. This course examines the role that language plays in structuring the social world—from identity formation in day-to-day human interactions to reproducing ideologies at the national and global levels. Exploring the discipline of sociolinguistics, a subfield of linguistics focusing on the interplay of language and society, this class will give students an opportunity to use the tools of the discipline to examine language use in their own social worlds.

We will examine different topics in sociolinguistics such as language and identity, language variation, language ideology, language and power, language attitudes, language policy, multilingualism, globalization, and language education. We will then use the information learned to examine and better understand the role of language in our lives.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- understand basic concepts in the field of sociolinguistics including the role of language in communities, variation in language, language in human interaction, and language and power
- understand basic methods of language analysis in the field of sociolinguistics
- apply understanding of basic concepts in the field of sociolinguistics and methods of language analysis learned in the class to language use in their own lives
- critically reflect on studies in the field of sociolinguistics

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Students are expected to attend on time and actively participate in all classes. As students cannot participate well in class without being present, students will be expected to be physically in class and participate actively to receive full points for participation.

5 participation points are given for each 60 minutes of class (10 points for each class). Students can miss a total of 25 points and still receive a perfect attendance score. These classes can be missed for any reason without any documentation.

If a student has a prolonged illness or other circumstance that would cause them to miss more classes, please see the instructor and special arrangements may be made.

Students arriving after attendance has been called will receive 9 points for the day (if they fully participate while in the class). Students arriving more than 10 minutes late but less than 1 hour late will receive 5 points for the day (if they fully participate while in the class), and students arriving more than 1 hour 10 minutes late will not receive any points that day.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

For plagiarism that is deemed unintentional (poor/lack of citation), the first violation will receive a reduced score on the assignment. Further violations will result in a 0 grade for that assignment.

For plagiarism that is deemed intentional (copying someone else’s work), the first violation will result in a 0 grade for that assignment. The second violation results in failure of the course. If you have any questions concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification.

Use of AI

AI programs such as Chat GPT may be used as an aid to your writing in this class for things such as but not limited to the following: help with choosing a topic, creating outlines, finding sources, making reference lists, and editing papers. If you use an AI tool, you should cite that tool and state at the end of your paper how you have used that tool. AI should never be used to write your entire paper or large portions of your paper for you. If an AI tool such as Chat GPT or a translator is used without citation and explanation of its use or if it is used in a way that is not appropriate, the paper will be considered to be intentionally plagiarized and will receive a zero. If this happens more than once in the class, you will fail the class.
