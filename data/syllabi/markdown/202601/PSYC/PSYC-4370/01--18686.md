## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Define Cognition and Cognitive Psychology
- • Identify techniques and methods used to study cognition
- Demonstrate an informed understanding of the basic mechanisms of cognition
- • Combine cognitive aspects of every-day life with laboratory findings
- • Demonstrate an informed understanding of higher-order processes of cognition

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

***Quizzes & Exams***

LMS Quizzes (30%): There will be six LMS quizzes consisting of multiple-choice or true/false questions from assigned readings and the lecture materials that were covered in the previous weeks (not cumulative). They will be available on LMS for 48 hours and you will have unlimited attempts. The attempt with the highest grade will be kept in the gradebook. ***There are no make-ups or extensions for LMS quizzes.*** The instructor will drop the lowest quiz grade for each student at the end of the semester, so the LMS quiz portion of the course grade will be based on five quizzes (%6 each).

In-class short quizzes (10%): There will be recurring in-class short quizzes consisting of three to five questions. There will be a total of around 10 in-class short quizzes and taking 5 of these will be sufficient, regardless of the correctness of your answers, to earn the full assigned credit of 10% of the course grade.

Two Exams (60%; 30% each): There will be two non-cumulative exams during the semester consisting of multiple-choice and open-ended short answer questions. Questions will be on materials (e.g. lectures, readings from text, exercises) covered in class up to the exam date. *The exams will be closed books, but you will be allowed to use one two-sided sheet with notes, which will be collected with the exams. No calculation or communication devices will be allowed during the exam.*

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Lateness policy:**

In the case of extenuating circumstances and difficulties completing an exam, please email me BEFORE the exam date.

**Policy on Collaboration and Cheating/plagiarism:**

In this class, all exams and assignments (except for group assignments) that are turned in for a grade must be the student’s own work. In cases where help was received (applies to graded assignments only, and not exams), a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration (e.g. if you went to COMM+D for help). Submission of any assignment or exam that is in violation of this policy will result in penalties. A single offense of cheating on an assignment, or exam will result in a zero grade for that assignment. In instances of such cheating on multiple occasions by the same student, this can result in getting an F for the course. If you have any questions or require clarification of these policies, please email me before turning in the material.
