## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course is intended for undergraduate students in their second year (Sophomore students) in the Computer Science Department. In this class, students will delve into the essential building blocks of machine learning, focusing on topics in linear algebra, vector and matrix analysis, vector calculus, and probability and stochastic. These mathematical foundations are the bedrock upon which machine learning is constructed. By mastering these fundamental concepts, students will gain a powerful understanding of machine learning algorithms and predictive modeling. This course has become a prerequisite for all the machine learning theory classes in the Computer Science department.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

- Provide students with a solid foundation in essential mathematical concepts relevant to machine learning, including linear algebra, vector calculus, and probability and stochastic.
- Establish a clear connection between mathematical principles and their applications in basic machine learning models, emphasizing how these mathematical foundations form the basis of various algorithms and predictive modeling techniques.
- Ensure students comprehend the core components of machine learning - data, models, and learning algorithms - and understand their interactions and significance in designing and evaluating predictive models.
- Foster critical thinking skills by encouraging students to evaluate and compare different machine learning algorithms based on their mathematical underpinnings.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

- Chapter 1: Introduction
- Chapter 2: Linear Algebra
  - Systems of Linear Equations
  - Matrices
  - Solving Systems of Linear Equations
  - Vector Spaces
  - Linear Independence
  - Basis and Rank
  - Linear Mappings
- Chapter 3: Vector Analysis
  - Norms
  - Inner Products
  - Lengths and Distances
  - Angles and Orthogonality
  - Orthonormal Basis
  - Orthonormal Component
  - Inner Product of Functions
  - Orthogonal Projections
- Chapter 4: Matrix Decompositions
  - Determinant and Trace
  - Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors
  - Cholesky Decomposition
  - Eigendecomposition and Diagonalization
  - Singular Value Decomposition
  - Matrix Approximation
- Chapter 5: Vector Calculus
  - Differentiation of Univariate Functions
  - Partial Differentiation and Gradients
  - Gradients of Vector-Valued Functions
  - Gradients of Matrices
  - Useful Identities for Computing Gradients
  - Backpropagation
  - Higher-Order Derivatives
  - Linearization and Multivariate Taylor Series
- Chapter 6: Probability and Stochastic
  - Construction of a Probability Space
  - Discrete and Continuous Probabilities
  - Sum Rule, Product Rule, and Bayes’ Theorem
  - Summary Statistics and Independence
  - Distributions
  - Change of Variables/Inverse Transform

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understand and apply fundamental linear algebra concepts, including vectors, matrices, matrix operations and decomposition, eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and their relevance to machine learning algorithms.
- Introduction of vector calculus concepts, including gradients of functions of vectors and matrices, highlighting their relevance in optimization techniques used in basic machine learning models.
- Understand basic concepts of probability theory with a focus on continuous probabilities and apply them to elementary machine learning models.
- Connect foundational mathematical concepts to the components of machine learning - data, models, and learning algorithms - at an introductory level, emphasizing their basic functionalities and relationships.
