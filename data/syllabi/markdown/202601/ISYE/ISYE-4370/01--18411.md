## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Transforming data into useful information and actionable insights for decision-making is an important skill that is becoming indispensable for scientists and engineers. The objective of this course is to introduce data analytics tools to engineers through hands-on computational applications. Topics include probability and statistics review, data visualization, linear regression, time-series analysis, forecasting, optimization, simulation, and spreadsheet models. Applications will be in Python, MATLAB, and Excel.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Apply data cleaning methods (exploring missing values, duplicates, outliers, etc.)
- Compute descriptive statistics of a given data set
- Analyze and model data using predictive and prescriptive techniques such as linear regression, time series analysis, forecasting, optimization, and simulation
- Use computational tools for implementing data analytics methods (in Python, MATLAB, and Excel)
- Communicate inferences from data to guide decision-making effectively through text and visualizations considering various audiences including researchers and industry practitioners

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:  
1\. Apply data cleaning methods (exploring missing values, duplicates, outliers, etc.)  
2\. Compute descriptive statistics of a given data set  
3\. Analyze and model data using predictive and prescriptive techniques such as linear regression, timeseries  
analysis, forecasting, optimization, and simulation  
4\. Use computational tools for implementing data analytics methods (in Python, MATLAB, and Excel)  
5\. Communicate inferences from data to guide decision-making effectively through text and visualizations  
considering various audiences including researchers and industry practitioners  
In addition to the above learning outcomes, students enrolled in ISYE 6370 will be able to:  
6\. Independently conduct a literature review on the applications of selected predictive and prescriptive  
techniques discussed  
7\. Discuss the implications, challenges, and limitations of the frequently used data analytics methods  
8\. Present the literature review and data analysis results in professional written and oral formats  
The final grade in the course is based on the following items:  
**Exams:** Students will have two exams within the semester. Note that, in the exams, there will be additional questions that must be answered by students enrolled in ISYE 6370.  
**Labs (4 Lab Assignments):** Lab work includes solutions to assigned problems to be uploaded in required file formats in LMS (using the Gradescope tool) before the specified deadlines. Note that, in the lab assignments, there will be additional questions that must be answered by students enrolled in ISYE 6370. After the first submissions are reviewed, if complete responses are submitted for each question in the assignment, students will have the option to revise and resubmit  
their work and the grade of the final submission will be used in evaluation.  
**Class Participation Exercises (CPE) (10 CPEs):** Each student should provide their response to CPEs through LMS. At the end of the semester, three of the lowest CPE grades will be  
dropped, including missing CPEs with or without an excuse. If, for some extraordinary reason, you have more than three absences, you will be graded over the remaining CPEs.  
**Final Project:** To be developed towards the end of the course, including presentations the last week of class. The project can be completed by a team of at most two students enrolled in ISYE 4370. The project must be completed individually for students enrolled in ISYE 6370. Students enrolled in ISYE 6370 must complete additional project requirements such as a literature review on the selected data analytics methods, application of additional analysis and discussion of implications, challenges, and limitations of the applications.
