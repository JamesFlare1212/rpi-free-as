## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course covers the basic methods of planning, organizing, and controlling the production of goods and services. Students will be exposed to the mathematical and graphical models which have been developed and applied to operations. Students will apply these models to problem instances supported by computer software and interpret the results in terms of actions or policies as they relate to the operations problem presented.

Overview of methods used in the design and operation of production and service systems. Topics include forecasting, capacity planning, line balancing, production scheduling, staff scheduling, inventory control, just in time, time study, and project planning. The goal of the course is to educate students in basic operations management principles and models.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Define key principles of planning, organizing, and controlling the production of goods and services
- Apply and interpret the mathematical models of operations management
- Design a production system or process to meet desired economic and volume goals
- Identify, formulate, and solve engineering problems using tools of operations management
- Use techniques, skills, and modern tools in designing, organizing, and managing operations

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Student's Learning Outcomes will be measured through Exams, Homework assignments and a Presentation.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

The final grade in the course is based on a **100-point** scale:

- **Exams (72 points):**
  - o There will be **6 midterms**.
  - o Each midterm is worth **12 points**.
  - o The grade obtained in your **best midterm** will replace the grade of your **worst midterm**.
- **Homework assignments (21 points):**
  - o There will be **7 homework assignments**. Each homework is worth 3 points.
  - o The purpose is to work on the development of a **POM project** throughout the semester.
  - o In groups of at most 5 people.
  - o First homework (coming soon):
    - **Task 1:** Provide the name of your group and list of all the members of the group.
    - **Task 2:** Describe the product/service your group will work on.
  - o **Homework 2-7:** One assignment for each midterm. You will need to apply/use some of the tools of that midterm in the development of your project.
- **Final Presentation (7 points):**
  - o Each group will need to give presentations at the end of the semester.
  - o **Goal:** a summary of your POM project
  - o Important to describe the product/service
  - o Tell the class how your project benefited from POM techniques

The total points earned on the course will be the weighted sum of points on the assessment requirements.

The final grade will be **rounded up** for assigning the final grade. The final course grade will be assigned using the ranges below. Final grades will not be changed unless a numerical error is made in computing your final grade. Please do not ask for extra credit or extra work after final exams have been administered.

## Policies

### Other Course Policies:

**Teaching modes:**

**In-Person:** The professor meets with the students in-person in the assigned classroom.

**Online (synchronous):** Lectures can be delivered via WebEx when necessary. All students should stay connected during the WebEx sessions.

**Course Materials on LMS:** The course webpage is maintained through the LMS (https://lms.rpi.edu). All necessary materials will be progressively made available on the LMS. Students are responsible for periodically checking the webpage for updates, downloads and announcements.

**Missing Exams or Assignments:** Missing exams are only allowed if there is an emergency verified by the Dean of Student’s office. There is no makeup for exams. Missing assessments without official excuses will receive a zero.

**Excusal from Course Assignments:** If you know ahead of time that you will miss an exam or an assignment deadline, then you must make arrangements before the assessment is due. Failure to do so will result in 25% off for each 24-hour period the assessment is late.

**Exam Protocol:** You are allowed to use one two-sided letter size reference sheet in your own handwriting, and the use of a calculator. No computers, tablets, phones, textbooks, notes, or slides are allowed. All answers must be recorded on the exam answer sheet and full credit is awarded for correct numerical answers as well as direct answers to the problem presented in the question.

**Exam Regrading Request:** Exams submitted for regrading must not be altered from the original work submitted for grading. Altering work for regrading is academic dishonesty and will be treated as such. All requests must be made in writing by attaching a well-phrased note to the original work explaining in full the reason for the request and the points you think you deserve for your error. Requests for a regrade must be submitted no later than the next class meeting after the grade is announced or the material is handed back to the class for the request to be considered.

**Special Testing Accommodations:** If you have a special accommodation letter, make sure to email it within the first three weeks of the semester so that necessary arrangements can be made.
