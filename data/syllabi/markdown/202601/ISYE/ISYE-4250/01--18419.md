## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

*ISYE-4250 – Facilities Design and Industrial Logistics*, An in-depth study of the major design issues in location and physical configuration of production and service facilities. The course emphasizes the use of mathematical models, computer modeling and quantitative analysis as aids to the design process. Topics include plant layout and location, materials handling, material flow analysis, and distribution systems. Major course concepts are developed through problems and projects.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

**Course** **Learning Outcomes:** It is anticipated that this course will contribute to varying degrees to the following learning outcomes:

- Speak knowledgeably about facility logistics systems, system components, and component operations.
- Construct models for the analysis and design of facility logistics systems.
- Illustrate the techniques and approaches used to design facility logistics systems.
- Apply theory and concepts to the design of facilities; and
- Show proficiency in techniques for the design and operation of a distribution center.
- ABET 1.) An ability to design a system, component or process to meet desired needs within realistic constraints such as economic, environmental, social, political, ethical, health and safety, manufacturability, and sustainability,
- 2.) An ability to identify, formulate and solve engineering problems.

**Exams/Quizzes:** There will be one exam and one cumulative course concept quiz. The format for the exams will be discussed before the exam. Exams are an individual effort.

## **Homework Assignments:** Throughout the semester exploratory assessment activities will be assigned to reinforce or expand on material that is covered in the lecture. Homework sets are to be worked outside of class. Students may collaborate on the assignments, meaning students can discuss the assignments, but *every student must complete and turn in his/her own written solutions in his/her own words (unless otherwise stated in the assignment instructions)*. Students must illustrate their own understanding of the material, which means that copying solutions from previous semesters or book solutions verbatim is not allowed and is considered a form of academic dishonesty. While students are allowed to use additional sources, they should not be copying directly from these sources. This also means you should not copy directly from resources, like ChaptGPT.

## Homework is meant to be a learning tool. If you are having difficulty, find help right away – don’t wait until you fall even further behind!

**Excusal from Course Homework Assignments and Examinations:** If you know ahead of time that you will miss an exam/homework assignment deadline, then you must make arrangements before the assessment is due.

**Opportunity to Shift Assignment Weights:** Each student can move the weight of homework 1 to exam 1. That is, if a student is not able to submit the homework assignment on time, then they can elect to have the homework weight transferred to the exam that the homework material is on. This option cannot be done for homework 2. Homework 2 must be turned in and in general late assignments will not be allowed unless arrangements have been made ahead of time.

**Emergency Situations:** If an emergency arises and a student cannot submit assigned homework on or before the scheduled due date or cannot take an exam on the scheduled date, the student (or the Student Experience Office) MUST give notification to the instructor NO MORE THAN 48 HOURS AFTER the scheduled date.

**Re-Grading Policy:** Document in writing which part you want re-graded and why you are advocating for a regrade. Turn in this document with your original assignment. You will be given one chance (per assignment) to request a re-grade. No re-grade will be accepted more than one week after an exam or an assignment has been returned to the student. Re-grades made after this period have elapsed will NOT be considered.

**End-of-semester grades:** End-of-semester grades are not given. Instead, end-of-semester grades are calculated according to a student’s performance on the individual assessment requirements and the weights in this syllabus. Final grades are, final. Therefore, final grades will not be changed unless a numerical error is made in computing your final grade. After final grades have been calculated, I will not respond to emails received requesting a grade change unless it is in response to a numerical calculation error. Please do not ask for extra credit or extra work after final exams have been administered.
