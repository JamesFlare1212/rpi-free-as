## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course develops an understanding of stochastic processes that evolve over time, and the ability to model, analyze, and solve optimization problems for systems that involve sequential and interrelated decisions. The application areas include but are not limited to production and inventory systems, customer service, revenue management, and health care.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

**Course Texts (Suggested/Optional)**

- Ross, Sheldon M. Introduction to Probability Models. Academic Press, 2014.
- Solberg, James J. Modeling Random Processes for Engineers and Managers. Wiley, 2009.
- Winston, Wayne L. Introduction to Mathematical Programming: Applications, 2002.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

**Course Content**

- Review of Probability
- Markov Chains
- Deterministic Dynamic Programming
- Stochastic Dynamic Programming
- Queueing Theory

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- understand processes that evolve over time in a random manner,
- model Markovian systems,
- solve optimization problems that involve sequential and interrelated decisions in deterministic and stochastic settings,
- analyze birth-and-death processes and queueing models and assess their applicability in practice.

### Other Course Policies:

Grading will be based on assignments and in-class exams.

The purpose of the assignments is to support learning and to practice the material continuously. Students are allowed to discuss the assignments, but everyone should develop their own solutions and write them on their own. Students will have one week to complete an assignment (e.g., when an assignment is released at 5pm on Monday, it will be due 5pm on the following Monday). All submissions must be made through the course LMS page.

Late submissions of assignments will not be graded.

Exams will be held in class, and they are closed-notes and closed-books. No make-ups will be given unless the student has a University-approved excuse.

Students with disabilities should let me know as early as possible so that arrangements can be made in a timely manner.
