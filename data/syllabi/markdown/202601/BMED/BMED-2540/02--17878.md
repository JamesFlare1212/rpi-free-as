## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Application of mechanics to the study of normal, diseased, and traumatized biological system. Areas covered include fundamental concepts in statics and deformable body mechanics, as well as its application in biomedical engineering such as determination of joint and muscle forces, mechanical properties of biological tissues, and structural analysis of bone-implant systems.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

- Students will be able to:
  - Understand and define quantitatively the role of biomechanics in human physiology
  - Understand the fundamental definition and manipulation of stress and strain components
  - Understand the mathematical equations, and underlying assumptions, that describe uniaxial, torsion, bending, and inflation biomechanics, and combinations of these behaviors.
  - Understand mechanical testing of biological material and identify structure-function material property relationships in skeletal tissues
  - Construct constitutive models of common physiological tissues
  - Use the biomechanical theories described above to obtain quantitative solutions to biomechanics problems in physiology
  - Use computational tools to set up and solve biomechanics problems in physiology

## Course Content

### Content Details:

- Equilibrium problems related to human bodies
- Stress and strain; stress/strain relationship; stress/strain transformation
- Extension and torsion, with application to the musculoskeletal system
- Bending, with application to bone
- Shear force and bending moment diagrams
- Statically indeterminate systems
- Use SolidWorks to simulate the changes in stress and strain inside a bone under various loads

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Identify quantitatively the role of biomechanics in human physiology.
- • Determine stress and strains in objects under various loads.
- • Set up mathematical equations and underlying assumptions that describe uniaxial/multiaxial, torsion, bending, and inflation biomechanics, and combinations of these behaviors.
- • Use computational tools to set up and solve biomechanics problems in physiology.
- • Build and simulate a physiological model in SolidWorks under various loads.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Contact your instructor for excuses if you have to miss any assignments. No makeup for the two midterms or quizzes. The final exam grade will be used to replace the excused missing assignment. Missing assignments without excuses will receive zero.

### Other Course Policies:

Exams/Quizzes: There will be two midterm exams and one cumulative final exam. Closed notes/book, and in person. No personalized crib sheet allowed. Relevant formulas are provided for all your quizzes and tests. Upon receiving your grade on any assignment, there is one week of grade challenge period.

Homework: Homework questions and solution are available on LMS. It is your responsibility to solve the homework questions as the course progresses. They will not be collected or graded. Warning: A tried and failed approach of handling the homework is to look through the questions and solution together without solving them yourself! Attend office hours if you get stuck with homework.

Simulation Project: You will learn how to use SolidWorks to perform simple finite element simulations to predict stresses and strains inside elastic bodies. Late submission receives 25% per day reduction from earned points.
