## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Introduction to multivariate statistical and machine learning methods to model and analyze recorded data from physiological systems. The related analysis includes linear and nonlinear multivariate regression, classification, clustering, and latent variable analysis. Associated methods include principal component and factor analysis, Fisher discriminant analysis, support vector machines, ordinary and partial least squares, k-means and hierarchical clustering and nonlinear kernel-based methods. The course discusses both traditional linear and nonlinear techniques which constitute the first solid steps in machine learning. The course strongly emphasizes biomedical applications and presents associated results from studies related to autism, burn severity and tissue engineering problems.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

## Course Assessment Measures

**Quizzes:**

There will be a total of 10 short quizzes during Weeks 2 and 14 (not on the date for the midterm exam). Each quiz starts at the beginning of class, 4:00 pm sharp, and has a duration of 5 minutes. ***The two lowest quiz grades will be dropped. There will be no makeup for any missed quiz. Arriving after 4:00 pm to a lecture is not a valid excuse for missing a quiz.***

**Homework Assignments:**

There will be a total of 2 homework assignments, which contain understanding-type problems based on the methods covered in class. Homework 1 covers material discussed during the first five weeks and homework 2 is based on the material presented during the first ten weeks. In addition to that, the homework assignments are also designed to apply these methods to experimental data sets of your choice. Each homework submission is to be *submitted via LM*S on the due date (02/27 for homework 1 and 04/10 for homework 2, up until 23:59). ***L******ate submissions will not be accepted and will receive a grade of zero, as there are at least two weeks to work on each assign***ment.

**Midterm Examinations:**

There is one midterm examination (03/20). The Midterm examination covers linear regression, and linear classification methods, as well as clustering and includes the material up to and including 03/17. Midterm exams contain understanding-type and multiple-choice questions about the methods we cover and plots of modeling results that are to be interpreted.

**Final Examination:**

There is one final examination that comprehensively assesses the course material. The exam will include understanding-type and multiple-choice questions about the methods covered in this course on linear and nonlinear multivariate statistical and machine learning methods, and also present plots of modeling results that are to be interpreted. ***The final exam is during finals week (05/04 - 05/08).***

**Project (for Graduate Section Only):**

The course includes one project to apply the methods discussed in class with a particular focus on nonlinear kernel-based methods. The aim is to conduct in-depth studies on the data for classification and regression purposes. The models identified are then utilized to draw meaningful conclusions from the data and the biomedical problem under study. The project report is to be submitted via LMS by 04/29 (up until 23:59). ***Late submissions will not be accepted and will receive a grade of zero.***
