## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course serves as an introduction to biomedical instrumentation and imaging with emphasis on current medical imaging modalities. Basic principles for the selection and appropriate use of instruments for solving bioengineering and medical problems such as microscopy, magnetic resonance imaging, and ultrasounds, among others, are addressed.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

1\. Bioinstrumentation. John G. Webster (Ed.). John Wiley & Sons, 2004.  
2\. Introduction to Biomedical Imaging. Andrew Webb. IEEE Press series in Biomedical Engineering, 2003.  
Additional materials are posted on RPI/LMS related to specific lecture topics.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Provide students with an understanding of bioinstrumentation and bioimaging principles. Student should learn the basic concepts of the different methods to acquire biosignals for clinical diagnostic. Other specific associated objectives include ability to perform a spectral analysis, knowledge of the characteristics and performances of clinical imaging modalities and diagnostic accuracy.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Fundamentals: Measurements system; Linear System Theory, Convolution, Fourier Transform; Properties of FT.  
BioInstrumentation: Basic concepts of electronics; Digital Signal Processing; Circuits.  
BioImaging: Def.Imag./Diagnostic accuracy; X-ray principles and image formation; CT principles and image formation; Physics of ultrasound; Ultrasound imaging and Doppler US; Principles of MRI; Instrumentation for MRI and imaging; Nuclear Medicine; Optical imaging

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Perform a Fourier transform and a convolution using Matlab
- Estimate the diagnostic accuracy of bioinstrumentation
- Demonstrate the operating principles and describe the characteristics of the main clinical imaging modalities (X-Ray, MRI, US and Nuclear imaging).

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Class Participation and Attendance: 10%  
Exams: 3 exams that are made of numerical problems, short answer, and multiple choices questions testing student's understanding of the basic concepts and techniques taught in class.  
Homework are made of numerical problems, short answer, or multiple choices questions and MATLAB homework to implement and testing student's understanding or the main concepts discussed in class.
