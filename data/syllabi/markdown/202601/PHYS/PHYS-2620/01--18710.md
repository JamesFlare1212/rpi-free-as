## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

PHYS 2620 Optics is a course building on students’ learning outcomes defined for PHYS 1200 Physics II and PHYS 1250 Introduction to Electromagnetic Theory. An introduction to the properties of light and its interactions with matter. Topics include wave and ray treatments of light; geometrical optics and imaging systems; interference and diffraction; polarization; coherence, laser, nonlinear optics. A laboratory is included. This is an undergraduate course.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

***Class hours:*** Students are required to attend class hours as scheduled (see attached course calendar). Students who miss scheduled class hours cannot make-up the class.

Formal excuses are not required for medical absences for up to a week of missed classes. Students must contact the instructor to discuss completing missed assignments and exams.

Students missing more than a week of classes due to illness will be required to provide a note of excused absence issued by the Dean of Students Office.

Students who present to the course instructor a note of excused absence for a scheduled class will be excused from class. Notes of excused absences because of non-medical/health but other sufficient reasons are issued by the Dean of Students Office.

***Exams:*** Every student must take all exams at the scheduled time unless excused because of illness or other sufficient reason by the Dean of Students Office.

A make-up exam is offered to students who present to the course instructor a note of excused absence (issued by the Dean of Students Office) for the day and time of the missed exam.

***Notes of excused absences:*** Documentation for excused absences is processed by the Dean of Students Office. Students who need an official excuse should contact the Dean of Students Office. The link to the form is here.
