## Course Goals

### Goals:

The goal of Built Ecologies 2 is to provide foundational knowledge and practical simulation tools for daylighting analysis and embodied carbon assessment as essential building blocks for designing healthy, high-performance and low carbon buildings.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate a working understanding of daylighting by designing for acceptable Spatial Daylight Autonomy (sDA) and Annual Sunlight Exposure (ASE) criteria.
- Explore various methods for reducing embodied carbon during the building design process.
- Calculate the impact of building materials’ global warming potentials via environmental product declarations (EPDs).
- Explore how daylight, embodied carbon, and operational carbon interact as both competing and complementary objectives by balancing their trade-offs.
- Analyze total (whole life) carbon of building designs using future projections.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Attendance is mandatory and will be recorded during each day of class, unless explicit permission is given based on unique individual circumstances. Two unexcused absences will result in the reduction of a letter grade. Further absences may result in failing the class.

### Other Course Policies:

Late arrivals to class impact the entire session and are not tolerated unless prior explanation is provided by the student.  
Missed assignments due to an excused absence can be made up the following week, uploaded prior to the lecture.  
Final project submission and presentation dates are not flexible. Only in extraordinary circumstances (such as severe illness), it can be made up via an online session.  
AEFIS student assessment is required. Incomplete assessments will result in no grade for the course.
