## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Entrepreneurship & Architecture provides students with a broad overview of career and business opportunities in the building industries, both within and outside traditional architectural design services. The course places specific emphasis on understanding the organizational, financial, and legal dimensions of designing, starting and running building industry businesses, including market dynamics, opportunities and risks of different business models. The course is broadly structured in two parts. First students research and assess various business typologies and firms, and are introduced to the broad spectrum of possible firm typologies through lectures by firm leaders and startup founders. This part of the course culminates in the identification of a business typology students will pursue for the remainder of the term. The second part of the course guides students through the components of designing and planning a business, using established startup design, ideation, and testing exercises.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

- Understand the types of organizations and careers involved in the design, development, and management of buildings
- Learn how to think about career opportunities in a world of changing professional dynamics
- Go through the process to design, plan, market test, launch, capitalize and monetize building industry businesses
- Explore and strategize how AI is changing work processes, careers, firm practices, operations, and valuations

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate ability to develop assessment criteria for the evaluation of business model typologies and apply these to evaluate the characteristics of specific firms
- Demonstrate the ability to use AI to formulate business assessment questions, search firm typologies and characteristics, and develop comparative assessment matrices
- Demonstrate ability to design and plan a business using key lean startup processes
- Demonstrate the ability to effectively work in teams to develop strategies, perform research, develop work products and deliver presentations

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

The course includes weekly assignments and a final project.  
The weekly assignments are used both to reinforce the learning conducted during class, and also build up to develop the overall class project.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Percentage of grade determined by each part of the course sequence:

- Participation[1] 20%
- Assignments 40%
- Final Project 40%

At the eighth week of the semester, students will receive notification if their grade for the semester is in danger of being below a C or below. Written appeal may be made to the instructor for each assignment, mid term notification, and final grade. If this appeal to the instructor doesn't satisfactorily resolve the matter, the appeal will be referred to the Associate Dean for Undergraduate Studies in the School of Architecture.

Attendance:

Attendance is required for all course meetings during the semester. Three unexcused absences will result in the lowering of your grade by one letter. Six unexcused absences from studio during the term result in an automatic F.
