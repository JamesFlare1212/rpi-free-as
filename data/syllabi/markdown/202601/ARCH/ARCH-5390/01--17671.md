## Course Goals

### Goals:

1. Provide an overview of the depth of Professional Practice
2. Introduce students to Project Management
3. Provide a summary of architecture firm organization and management
4. Introduce students to options in delivery methods, the Architect’s contractual obligations and the role of architects during construction
5. Expose Students to legal issues of the profession
6. Expose students to the methods used for determining costs associated with projects and the delivery of architectural services
7. Familiarize students with ethical obligations and conduct requirements

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Professional Practice [2020 NAAB SC.2] – Student will be able to demonstrate through in-class discussions, assignments and testing the ability to properly identify the relevant ethics, regulatory requirements and fundamental business practices of architecture. a. Professional Ethics and Conduct – Student will have the ability to identify the ethical issues involved in the exercise of professional judgment in architectural design and practice and to locate where to find the ethical requirements of the profession. b. Regulations – Student will have the ability to analyze and identify the legal roles and responsibilities of the architect as determined by regulations including legal practice structures in New York State. Student will be able to identify the legal considerations in the practice of architecture and professional service contracts. c. Business Practices - Student will have the ability to identify the proper basic principles of a firm’s business practices, including finances, project management, risk, business planning, marketing, business development, organization and project delivery.
- 2\. Career Paths [2020 NAAB PC.1] – Student will be able to demonstrate through assignments, in-class discussions and testing the ability to identify licensure paths and career opportunities. a. Student will be able to identify the requirements of the AXP and ARE. b. Student will be able to organize the typical career path chronologically. c. Students will be able to discuss the different types of career paths.
- 3\. Leadership & Collaboration [2020 NAAB PC.6] – Student will be able to demonstrate through a group project, meetings with professor, in-class discussions, assignments and testing the ability to identify, organize and discuss leadership and collaboration in diverse multidisciplinary teams and how to solve complex problems in dynamic contexts. a. Student will demonstrate comprehension by meeting with the professor and submitting a response to a request for proposal utilizing Project Management skills in a group to collaborate to choose a proper legal theoretical architecture firm, choose real world consultants, prepare an organization chart, describe a viable project delivery method, calculate a basic service fee, distribute the fee by phase both internal and external, calculate the architectural staff hours that can be assigned to the typical basic services phases and prepare a milestone and bar chart schedule based on all project parameters. b. Student will have the ability to identify the relationships and responsibilities among key stakeholders in the design process.
