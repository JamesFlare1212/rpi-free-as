## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is a graduate-level course on the foundations of detection and estimation theory, with applications to communication, sensing and imaging, pattern recognition and other signal processing systems; decision-theory concepts; detection of signals in noise; parameter estimation, linear and nonlinear estimation, filtering; and other advanced topics.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Fundamentals of Statistical Signal Processing, Volume 1: Estimation Theory, Steven Kay, 1993. ISBN 0-13-345711-7

Fundamentals of Statistical Signal Processing, Volume 2: Detection Theory, Steven Kay, 1998. ISBN 0-13-504135-X

## Course Goals

### Goals:

To cover the theoretical foundations of detection and estimation theory and its applications in digital communication, sensing and imaging, and pattern recognition systems.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Introduction and Preliminaries

Estimation Theory

1. Sufficient statistics
2. Minimum variance unbiased estimation
3. Cramer-Rao lower bound
4. Maximum likelihood estimation
5. Bayesian estimation
6. Bayesian estimation: Gaussian linear model
7. Wiener filtering
8. Kalman filtering
9. Applications

Detection Theory

1. Bayesian risk theory
2. Neyman-Pearson detection
3. Signal detection in Gaussian noise
4. Bayes factors and GLRTs
5. CFAR detection
6. Applications
