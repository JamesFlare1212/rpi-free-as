## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This advanced studio course focuses on acrylic painting techniques and concepts, aiming to enhance students’ hands-on skills, aesthetic sensibilities, and image-making abilities with acrylic mediums. It emphasizes building technical proficiency as a painter, sharpening critical observation skills as an artist, and cultivating a personal artistic perspective. Special attention will be given to the process of image-making, exploring how composition, paint handling, color theory, and image construction contribute to compelling visual narratives. Through assignments that incorporate both figuration and abstraction, students will work toward achieving individual expression in acrylic mediums. The course is conducted through a combination of lectures, discussions, readings, critiques, and strong painting practice.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

This is a studio-based course in which a significant amount of class time will be spent in the physical act of painting. You will also be presented with slide lectures and be expected to participate in discussions and critiques. This course is designed to encourage stylistic development as it relates to the medium of painting. We will do this through 4 main assignments that are supported by smaller, related projects. Risk-taking and stepping out of your comfort zone at times is expected. Assignments will explore mostly representational images, including the human figure, but certain assignments will include abstract elements. Expect a significant amount of time spent outside of class, about 3+ hours a week.

Expect periodic in-class critiques, necessitating preparation to discuss your work and engage in  
dialogues about your peers’ work. Light reading materials are part of the course, and will be provided electronically.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Deepen knowledge of acrylic techniques and mediums
- Build a confident and versatile skill set as a painter
- Develop an intellectual approach to painting and cultivate a unique personal perspective
- Expand understanding of the histories and traditions of painting
- Strengthen approach to color theory and composition
- Foster creativity and confidence in invention and experimentation
- Enhance skills in image-making and constructing compelling visual narratives

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Each assignment will be graded on both your effort (how much time and care given to project) and process (following instruction, demonstrating understanding of concepts, showing investigation)

Each assignment will be graded with the following criteria: (\_\_\_/100)

Composition and Design (care/intention in the arrangement of compositional elements ): 25 pts

Technique (care/intention toward the application of materials in relation to desired outcome): 25 pts

Color: (demonstration of color theory concepts and experimentation): 25 pts

Invention/Creativity: (pushing investigation of ideas and concepts to find personal expression): 25 pts

If your project is not hanging at the beginning of class on Crit day then it will be considered late. Unfinished work will not be critiqued. You will only receive a grade for finished work. Late work will suffer in grade by 10 points. A no show/no word on crit days will result in an additional 10 points off projects. (20 points off total)

**Class Preparedness** means you are prepared and ready to work as soon as class begins with the assigned materials at hand. It also means that you have all assignments/homework hung at the beginning of crit with your name clearly written on the front of each drawing.

**Class Participation** means you are actively contributing to discussions and class critiques by showing interest in what others make, asking questions about the assignment goals and outcomes, and using learned vocabulary. It also applies for the explanation and presentation of your own work. Critiques begin by describing observations and interpretations grow from there. Do your best to speak up at each critique.
