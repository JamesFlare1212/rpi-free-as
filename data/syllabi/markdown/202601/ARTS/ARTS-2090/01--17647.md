## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Radical Graphics seeks to expand on the definition of print graphics from one that traditionally creates multiple images on paper to a practice that includes art-making activities as varied as t-shirt printing, “fine art” prints, sculpture, and punk rock and political protest graphics. Students will focus on the concept of the art “matrix”; a template for image-making that can work across media. Students will create matrices that can be applied across multiple art-making platforms. Students will focus on learning the basics of the screenprinting process and its ability to communicate visual ideas.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Studio projects will be graded on a combination of technical execution, strength of concept, and overall effort.

Technical Execution:

-Did you utilize proper printmaking skills in creating your work?

-Did you properly use studio tools? Did you clean up your work area appropriately?

Strength of Concept:

-Is your work expressive, clever, novel, original, personal, political, weird?

Overall Effort:

-Did you participate in critique?

-Did you clean up and properly manage lab materials?

Late submissions will not be excepted, and will be given a failing grade for that project.

### Attendance Policy:

After teaching at the college level for more than a decade, I can attest that attendance is the most definitive indicator of whether a student will succeed or fail at a course. I urge you to attend each and every class session whenever possible, realizing that sometimes illness or emergencies may prevent your attendance. Lectures will not be archived, although I encourage students to share notes. Inevitably, missing class will mean missing discussions, questions, important demonstrations and the intellectual community of the classroom. It is your responsibility to familiarize yourself of the content of any class period you miss.

While in class, I ask you be engaged in the classroom community. A student who is excessively texting or scrolling social media, arrives more than 15 minutes late, or sleeps in class will be marked absent.

You receive 100 points for your attendance over the semester. If you miss a class because of illness, please email Nathan **the day of your absence** to possibly receive credit for the missed class. More than two consecutive absences OR three absences over the course of two consecutive weeks will require an official excuse from health services or your class dean. Retroactive excuses are only allowed from health services or your class dean.

On certain workdays, it is allowable to not come in and work asynchronously IF you check with the instructor ahead of time, and send an email to the instructor on the day of class making note of the work you have done or plan on doing in lieu of attending.
