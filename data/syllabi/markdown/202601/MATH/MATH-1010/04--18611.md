### Additional Course Description :

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to demonstrate basic and advanced symbol manipulation skills.
- Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to demonstrate the ability to convert between calculus concepts and their graphical, numerical, and symbolic representations.
- Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to demonstrate the ability to apply calculus concepts to selected problems in science and engineering.
- Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to demonstrate the ability to apply certain fundamental theorems, basic concepts, and rules from calculus to solve symbolic and graphical problems.
- Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to demonstrate the ability to present problems related to the theory of calculus and its applications to science and engineering disciplines.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Students are responsible for all material covered in lectures and recitations whether they are present or not. Attendance is expected, but will not be checked. Attendance is required for recitation sections when quizzes are administered and test blocks when in-semester exams are administered.

### Other Course Policies:

**Exams:** Exams will be given during test block with the exception of DSS students and excused absences as outline below. Attendance will be taken at all exams using sign in sheets. Details about each exam, including the material to be covered on the exam and time limit, will be announced on [LMS](http://lms.rpi.edu/) when the date of the exam is near. The dates for Exams 1–4 are listed above in the section **Grading.**

DSS students will have the option to take their exams at the testing center provided they make appointments earlier enough to secure a testing slot. DSS students are responsible for booking their own testing appointments with the testing center following the guidelines that the instructor provides you. Note that these appointments **must be made well in advance** of scheduled exam dates. If a DSS student fails to schedule an testing appointment with the testing center more that 96 hours prior to the scheduled exam date, it is possible that their accommodation can only be met by taking the exam as scheduled with their classmates.

A make-up exam is scheduled for each in-semester exam as noted above in the **Grading** section. Any student who misses an exam, must take the make-up exam at the time scheduled. If a student misses both the scheduled exam and the make-up exam dates, the student will receive a score of 0 on the exam. The only exception to this policy is a student who has an excused absence (e.g. official excused absence documented by Student Success to attend a conference, athletic competition) on the day of the exam AND on the day of the make-up exam. In such a case, the student must have notified the instructor of said absences as soon as they learn of it and no later than one class period prior to the regularly scheduled exam.

Make-up exams will only be given to the following students

- A student with documentation excusing the student from class at the time of the regularly scheduled exam ([**excused absence documented by the Student Success office**](https://success.studentlife.rpi.edu/current-students/academic-and-personal-support/requesting-excused-absence)**,** *similar documentation provided directly to instructor or documentation from a coach with game departure time noted)***.** A student must inform the instructor before the exam and provided documentation as outlined above ***before the exam begins.*** *(Make up given without penalty)*
- A student who misses the exam due to an unexcused absence (e.g. did not know there was an exam, feeling ill, overslept, etc) **and informs the instructor via email no later than 5 PM** on the day of the exam of their absence and request to take a make up exam. *(Make up given without penalty)*
- A student who misses the exam due to an unforeseen reason and fails to email the instructor by 5 PM on the day of the exam but **does email the instructor within 24 hours of the exam** and requests to take the make up exam, may take a make up exam ***with penalty.***

**Missing an exam should not be taken lightly.** Thus, if a student fails to inform (via email) the instructor within 24 hours of the exam of their absence (excused or unexcused), it is at the discretion of the instructor to give the student a 0 on the exam or allow the student to take a make-up exam with application of a penalty.

Note that the policy outlined here is for in-semester exams only. The policy for the final exam is outlined below.

**Final Exam:** The Final Exam will be cumulative and will be administered during the finals period May 4-8. **All students must take the final exam at the time scheduled by the Office of the Registrar. Exceptions will only be made if there is a scheduling conflict as defined** [**here**](https://registrar.rpi.edu/services/class-scheduling/final-exam-schedule)**, or if a student is granted a NE grade by the Student Success office.** Please note that the Student Success office will only issue an NE for final exams for extreme circumstances, such as hospitalization (Read more here.) All students **must** take their exam at the assigned time for MATH 1010 Sections 1 - 4. ***Do not make arrangements for travel at the end of the semester until the Final Exam Schedule from the Registrar’s Office is published or schedule your travel after the last day of final exams - May 8.***

**Homework:** Homework will be assigned on Tuesdays and Fridays coinciding with the content covered in the lecture that day. To view homework, go to the **Homework: Achieve &** Textbook tab on LMS. Achieve homework assignments will be due weekly on Tuesdays and Fridays at 11:59 PM on the evening of the next lecture after it was assigned and will be graded automatically by the system. Students may submit these assignments late up to two days after the original deadline, but a penalty will be applied to the score. A 10% penalty will apply to any problems in an assignment submitted the day after the deadline and a 20% penalty will apply any problems in an assignments submitted two days after the deadline. **No other due date extensions will be granted for any reason.** To further compensate for this, several homework assignment scores will be dropped at the end of the semester.

Additional problems will be assigned from the textbook and will be posted on [LMS](http://lms.rpi.edu/) in the **Homework: Achieve & Textbook** content area found in the left toolbar. Though these problems will not be collected nor graded, they should not be viewed as optional. Success in the course depends on successful completion of all homework, both Achieve homework and textbook homework. Exam problems are frequently chosen from homework assignments and lecture examples. Full solutions to odd-numbered textbook problems can be found on Achieve. When logged in to our Achieve course, select **Viewing by: Resource Type** to see the student solutions manual for chapters 1-6. (Tip: you can download and save these files pdf on your computer.)

**Quizzes:** Quizzes will be given in recitations based on your section of calculus 1. Quizzes will be short fill-in questions, multiple choice or multiple select questions and will be 10 minutes. Sections 1 & 2 have recitations on Monday and sections 3 & 4 have recitations on Thursday as indicated in the **Meeting Times** section above. A detailed schedule will be made available on LMS in the **Quiz and Exam Schedule** content area. Students must take quizzes during the recitation time that is listed on their schedule on SIS. There are two schedule make up dates for quizzes listed on the quiz schedule for the semester. On a make-up quiz date, a student may make up **1 (one)** quiz that they did not take during recitation out of the 4 quizzes given prior to the make up date. A student is not allowed to *retake* a quiz. Students who miss a quiz due to pre-scheduled absence (e.g. official excused absence documented by Student Success to attend a conference, athletic competition) should contact the instructor for details on how to make up a quiz due to an excused absence. The quiz average will be calculated from the top 6 quiz scores that a student has over the course of the semester.

**Disability Services for Students (DSS):** Students with a memorandum from the [Disability Services for Students (DSS)](http://studenthealth.rpi.edu/disabilityservices) office identifying accommodations for exams and quizzes must present the memorandum to the instructor, ideally by Tuesday, January 19. Providing the accommodations requires advance planning. Students will not be granted accommodations unless a memorandum from DSS has been submitted to the instructor **and** the student has followed emailed instructions and communicates promptly with the instructor.

**Academic Integrity:** Students should familiarize themselves with the section on academic integrity in the [Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities](https://rpi.app.box.com/s/d4ndpn3f7qdwxh9jaz5n1hkkr0mnd25e) (pp. 16–18). All work submitted on exams and quizzes must be the student’s own. In addition, no calculators, computers, tablets, open books, open notes, phones, headphones, or smartwatches may be used during exams or quizzes. Talking or communicating with other students in any way during exams or quizzes is also prohibited. Students may discuss homework with other students, CAs, TAs, and instructors, but the work submitted must be the student’s own.

The minimum academic sanction applied to a student found in violation of the above policies will be a grade of 0 on the assignment. A stronger sanction may be applied if warranted. A report regarding the violation will also be submitted to the Dean of Students Office and will remain on file until graduation. Repeat offenders will fail the course and will be subject to judicial action.

**Attendance:** Students are responsible for all material covered in lectures whether they are present or not. Attendance is expected in lecture and recitation, and attendance activities may be conducted as a way to achieve bonus points in the course.

**Calculator Policy:** Calculators will **not be allowed** on exams or quizzes. Problems will be designed so that this does not create an issue. Occasionally, a calculator may be required to complete a homework problem primarily on Achieve, but students should not rely on calculators for simple arithmetic or trigonometric calculations.

**Health and Safety:** Students are required to follow the health and safety protocols of RPI, which are subject to change throughout the semester.

**Visitors:** Visitors are prohibited during all class meetings. Only students who are registered for the class should be present in the classroom. If you have a visitor (for example, a student considering enrolling at RPI), please asks the instructor permission to have the visitor attend before bringing anyone to lecture or recitation. Students may only attend class meetings at the time and place listed on their schedule on SIS.

**Classroom Decorum:** Students must be on time for class and should refrain from leaving and reentering the classroom during class. If a student has a legitimate reason for arriving late to class or being excused early from class, then they should discuss this with the instructor before class. Phones may not be used during class and should be silent. Computers and tablets may not be used for anything other than taking notes. Students who are unable to courteously comply with these policies may be asked to leave the room.
