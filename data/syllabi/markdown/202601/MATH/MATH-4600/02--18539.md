## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Advanced calculus this semester will use a differential forms approach in addition to the standard multivar integration, differentiation, Inverse and Implicit function theorems.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Edwards, Advanced Calculus from a differential forms approach, 1997.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course aims to introduce students to a powerful approach based on differential forms to advanced calculus

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Differentions, Integration, vectorial methods, implicit and inverse function theorems, Stokes and Green theorems, based on line and surface and volume integrals.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Outcome one: understand and use differential forms Outcome: Calculations of line and surface integrals
- Understand and use Stokes theorem
- Understand and use the Implicit Function Theorem

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Assessment are based on three one and half hours tests, weekly graded homework and class attendance and participation
