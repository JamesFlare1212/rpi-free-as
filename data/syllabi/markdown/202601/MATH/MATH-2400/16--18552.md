## Course Goals

### Goals:

Mathematical models of practical problems in science and engineering often involve differential equations. The equations may be written as a single equation of a certain order or may be written as a system of first-order equations, and they may be linear or nonlinear. The goal of this course is to teach analytical techniques for the solution of elementary differential equations that arise often in areas of application.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Successful completion of this course should enable you to demonstrate an understanding of fundamental techniques for the solution of first-order and second-order ordinary differential equations.
- Successful completion of this course should enable you to demonstrate an understanding of Fourier series and separation of variables for certain partial differential equations.
- Successful completion of this course should enable you to demonstrate an understanding of solution techniques and the behavior of solutions of systems of first-order differential equations.
- Successful completion of this course should enable you to present written solutions to problems in a clear, concise and coherent fashion.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

In the case of homework assignments, collaboration between students is encouraged. However, each student must write his/her own solutions and submit his/her own problem sets. Copying someone else's solution or using a problem-solving site (such as Chegg) is not allowed.

In the case of exams, there is no collaboration of any kind permitted, and each student will be required to write out and sign an Honor Pledge on each exam confirming this. If cheating is suspected, then an explanation will be requested. If the explanation is not satisfactory, then an exam grade of zero will be given and a report will be made to the Dean of Students.

During this time when assignments and exams are handled remotely, we are expecting a high level of maturity, responsibility and integrity from students. We are comfortable with this as these qualities will be expected throughout your careers, and in life generally.
