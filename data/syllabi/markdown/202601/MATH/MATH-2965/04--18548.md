## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The course is aimed at providing science majors, in particular those from Mathematics, Physics and Computer Science, with comprehensive coverage of the subject early in their education. Topics include: vectors and matrices, systems of linear equations, affine spaces, vector spaces and subspaces, independence, bases, dimension, linear transformations, orthogonality, projections, determinants, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, decomposition and diagonalization of matrices, linear transformations.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

**Recommended Textbook:** Introduction to Linear Algebra, Fifth ed., by Gilbert Strang, 2016.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Apply results and definitions in Linear Algebra.
- Work examples in Linear Algebra.
- Explain topics in Linear Algebra.
- Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the basic ideas of Linear Algebra, including concepts of linear systems, independence, theory of matrices, linear transformations, determinants, vector spaces and subspaces, bases and dimension, eigenvalues, eigenvectors and diagonalization, inner products, etc.
- Apply Linear Algebra concepts of this course to solve problems arising in applied sciences.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Exams:** Exams will be given during our test block. Attendance will be taken at all exams using sign in sheets. Details about each exam, including the material to be covered on the exam and time limit, will be announced on [LMS](http://lms.rpi.edu/) when the date of the exam is near. The dates for Exams 1 & 2 are listed above in the section **Grading.**

A make-up exam is scheduled for each in-semester exam, as noted above in the **Grading** section. Any student who misses an exam, must take the make-up exam at the time scheduled. Students missing an exam should notify the instructor by the end of the day of the regularly scheduled exam that they will be taking the makeup. If a student misses both the scheduled exam and the corresponding make-up exam, the student will receive a score of 0 on said exam. The only exception to this policy is a student who has an excused absence (e.g. official excused absence to attend a conference, athletic competition, or due to an extended illness) on the day of the exam and on the day of the make-up exam. In such a case, the student must have notified the instructor no later than one class period prior to the regularly scheduled exam. Documentation excusing the student from class at the time of the regularly scheduled exam and make-up exam ([**excused absence documented by the Dean of Students office**](https://success.studentlife.rpi.edu/current-students/academic-and-personal-support/requesting-excused-absence)**, similar documentation directly to instructor or documentation from a coach with game departure time noted)** must be received by the instructor before the day of the regularly scheduled exam. Note that if you know ahead of time that you are going to miss an exam, for any reason, you must discuss this with the instructor no later than one class period prior to the exam. Note that the policy outlined here is for in-semester exams only. The policy for the final exam is outlined below.

**Final Exam:** The Optional Final Exam will be cumulative and will be administered during the finals period May 4-8. **All students who opt to take the final exam must do so at the time scheduled by the Office of the Registrar. Exceptions will only be made if there is a scheduling conflict as defined** [**here**](https://registrar.rpi.edu/services/class-scheduling/final-exam-schedule-conflicts)**, or if a student is granted a NE grade by the Student Success office.** Please note that the Dean of Students office will only issue an NE for final exams in extreme circumstances, such as hospitalization (Read more [**here**](https://doso.rpi.edu/student-support-deans/missing-final-exam).)

**Homework:** Written Homework assignments will be due weekly. Students will submit assignments on Gradescope. Assignments may be submitted late up to one day after the original deadline, but a 10% penalty will be applied to the score. **No other due date extensions will be granted for any reason.** To further compensate for this, two (2) homework assignment scores will be dropped at the end of the semester.

Additional problems will be assigned and will be posted on [LMS](http://lms.rpi.edu/). Though these textbook problems will not be collected nor graded, they should not be viewed as optional. Success in the course depends on successful completion of all homework. Exam problems are frequently similar to homework assignments and lecture examples.

**In-class activity:** Quizzes or other in-class activities will be given in-person most weeks of the semester. A detailed schedule will be made available on LMS. Students must complete quizzes during the lecture time that is listed on their schedule on SIS. No make-up quizzes will be given for any reason. Any missed quiz will be automatically replaced with the score on the corresponding exam containing the material of the missed quiz. Additionally, two (2) in-person quiz scores will be dropped at the end of the semester.

**Academic Integrity:** Students should familiarize themselves with the section on academic integrity in the [Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities](https://rpi.app.box.com/s/d4ndpn3f7qdwxh9jaz5n1hkkr0mnd25e) (pp. 16–18). A report regarding the violation will also be submitted to the Dean of Students Office and will remain on file until graduation. Regrade requests will only be accepted during the grade appeal window specified on LMS once the grades are released. Regrade requests made outside of Gradescope will be ignored. Please note that regrade requests will not be available for the final exam.

Occasionally, a calculator may be required to complete a homework problem, but students should not rely on calculators for simple arithmetic or trigonometric calculations.

**Visitors:** Visitors are prohibited during all class meetings. Only students who are registered for the class should be present in the classroom. Students may only attend class meetings at the time and place listed on their schedule on SIS.

**Classroom Decorum:** Students must be on time for class and should refrain from leaving and reentering the classroom during class. If a student has a legitimate reason for arriving late to class or being excused early from class, then they should discuss this with the instructor before class. Phones may not be used during class and should be silent. Computers and tablets may not be used for anything other than taking notes. Students who are unable to courteously comply with these policies may be asked to leave the room.

If you are struggling, please know that asking for help is a smart and courageous step. It is a skill that will serve you well far beyond your time here. RPI has partnered with [**Mantra Health**](https://studenthealth.rpi.edu/list-services/mental-health-counseling-services/mantra-health) to extend the health and wellness services offered to students. ### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).
