## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course introduces you various advanced econometric methods and techniques for analyzing econometric data. It emphasizes both the theoretical and the practical aspects of econometric analysis, focusing on techniques for estimating econometric models of various kinds and for conducting tests of hypotheses of interest to economists. Topics covered this semester include Least square, resample methods, instrumental variables, GMM, panel data, nonparametric regression, series regression, quantile regression, and machine learning.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Bruce E. Hansen “ Econometrics” (BH), Princeton University Press, 2022

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The goal of this course is to provide students with theoretical and practical knowledge to conduct empirical research that involves inference and prediction, to evaluate economic/business policies, and to critically read quantitative analyses by other researchers.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Upon successful completion, students will have the knowledge and skills to: define system linear regression, IV, GMM, bootstrap, nonparametric regression, and quantile regression estimators mathematically; derive and examine finite sample and asymptotic properties of these estimators analytically; demonstrate an understanding of the strengths and limitations of the different estimators; employ linear algebra in key econometric derivations; apply econometric theory to concrete examples in economics;
