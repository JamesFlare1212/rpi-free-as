## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An interdisciplinary course focusing on the fundamentals and applications of semiconductor electrochemistry, and will serve as a bridge between classical electrochemistry and solid state physics. Topics include fundamentals of semiconductor physics, principles of electrochemistry, nature of semiconductor/electrolyte interfaces, current flow, and the applications of above principles to environment remediation and renewable energy devices such as solar cells, photocatalysis, and battery technologies. This course is co-listed with CHME 4011.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate proficiency in modern semiconductor terminology and concepts and apply them to the workings of the semiconductor photovoltaic devices.
- Understand fundamental electrochemical principles and apply them to determine performance, efficiency, and limitations of electrochemical conversion devices.
- Describe and evaluate journal publications and media reports describing solar energy conversion and storage devices, and present these findings, analysis, and recommendations in a technical presentation to non-specialists.
- In addition to the above, develop the ability to apply fundamental electrochemical principles to address the challenges in the current electrochemical device technologies, and help engineer more efficient devices through intelligent design, and rational analysis of the thermodynamic and material limitations.
