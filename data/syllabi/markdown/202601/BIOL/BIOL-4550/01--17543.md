## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will demonstrate a thorough understanding of energy calculations for molecular modeling, analysis, and simulation.
- Students will be able to communicate in on the subject of protein structure and dynamics and their relevance to protein function.
- Students will be able to visualize and understand protein interactions with other biomolecules and drugs.
- Students will be able to understand and carry out basic tasks in structure-based drug discovery.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

tests, homework, exams and term projects.
