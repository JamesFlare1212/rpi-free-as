## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An introduction to the concepts and techniques of modern statistics. Computer-based, in-class sessions will emphasize hands-on application of statistical techniques using data and examples drawn from the biological and medical sciences. Pre-class, online exercises will prepare students for in-depth classroom explorations. Topics covered will include sampling, regression, analysis of variance, and factor analysis, MANOVA, maximum likelihood methods, discriminant analysis, time series analysis, and forecasting. The final project requires analysis of a major research-related data set and write-up of results in the format of a scientific paper.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Class attendance is crucial for this type of course. So much happens in each class that you will quickly find yourself dazed and confused if you start missing classes. In-class assignments will be assigned every class meeting. Excused Absences: There will be times during the semester when you may need to miss a class because of other commitments, unexpected events, or illness. If you are unable to attend class, please complete the absence notification submission on LMS with your excuse for requesting an absence that day. You are responsible for making up the missed material. I suggest you first reach out to another student in the class to obtain any code that you may have missed. Then review the lecture and the code, and submit the in-class assignment. Please email me with any specific questions after you review the material - we can then find a time to meet if necessary. Missed In-class assignments must be turned in to LMS within one week of returning to class to be eligible for credit.

### Other Course Policies:

**On-line Content & Homework**:  
To prepare for the hands-on classes, it is essential that you complete all of the on-line, pre-class exercises. The BlackBoard site for Biostatistics is managed by RPILMS. Course-related LMS problems should be referred to the TA and your instructor. General LMS problems should be referred to the Help Desk in the VCC. Homework assignments will be a mix of Data Camp assignments and prompts with Large Language Learning Models (LLM's) such as ChatGPT. Sometimes you will have a choice between Data Camp and LLM's. Other times you will need to use either Data Camp or LLM's. Please note that a sign up link for Data Camp will be provided for free access to Data Camp. Thorough instructions and templates will be provided for the LLM assignments. Homework Late Policy: Due dates will be announced ahead of time (check LMS assignment due dates). Late homework may be submitted up to 1 week late for 50% credit.  
**In-Class Sessions**:  
Most sessions will involve introductory discussion of the topic at hand followed by exercises and hands-on work with the computer. As you are well aware, computers can be a serious distraction in the classroom. In order to create an environment where we can all learn and receive the maximum advantage from the course's computer intensive learning environment, please refrain from texting and surfing the web during class.  
**Quizzes**:  
There will be 7 quizzes including a a mix of in-class and take home quizzes. The lowest quiz will automatically be dropped from your final score. Quizzes will assess statistical theory (in class) and the application of the R programming language (take home). Quiz 1: The use of GPT or AI tools are not permitted for Quiz 1. Quizzes 3, 5, and 7: Any use of GPT or AI tools or online resources must be properly cited at the end of the quiz! No direct copying or pasting of any online resource including GPT or AI tools is permitted.
