## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course provides an introduction to research in the groups affiliated with the BCBP program, covering key topics in Biophysics and Biochemistry.  
Students will participate in three rotations, each lasting six weeks. After completing each rotation, they will present their research findings to the Department in a formal talk.  
Following the three rotations, students will consult with faculty and the Graduate Program Director (GPD) to select the laboratory they will join.
