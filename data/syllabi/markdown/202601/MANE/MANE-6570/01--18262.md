## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is a graduate level course. It is aimed to provide the students with familiarity of traditional and modern flow control techniques, and to introduce the students to the subject of laminar-to-turbulent transition via hydrodynamic stability analysis, which is a crucial component in designing and implementation of intelligent flow control strategies.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Lecture notes will be provided. Optional text books:

## Course Goals

### Goals:

To provide the students with familiarity of traditional and modern flow control techniques. To introduce the students the subject of laminar-to-turbulent transition via hydrodynamic stability analysis, which is a crucial component in designing and implementation of intelligent flow control strategies.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

**Part 1: hydrodynamic stability**

1.1 Balance between opposing forces

1.2 Kelvin-Helmholtz Instability

1.3 Method of normal modes – review of Fourier transform

1.4 Temporal vs. spatial wave propagation

1.5 Rayligh’s Equation – inviscid flows

1.6 Orr-Sommerfeld’s Equation – viscous flows

1.7 3-D boundary layers

1.8 Non-parallel flows

1.9 Slightly non-linear stability theory

**Part 2: Aerodynamic Flow Control**

2.1 Flow control devices

2.2 Flow control strategies

2.2.1 passive vs. active

2.2.2 open vs. close loop control

2.3 Separation control and control of separation

2.4 Control of laminar to turbulent transition

2.5 External flows

2.6 Internal flows

2.7 Drag reduction

2.8 Mixing enhancement
