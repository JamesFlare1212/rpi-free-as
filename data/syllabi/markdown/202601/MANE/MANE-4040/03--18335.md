## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Laboratory experience to complement Elements of Mechanical Design (MANE-4030). Includes aspects of dimensional measuring and tolerancing; Stress and strain measurement in tension and bending; Contact mechanics; Sliding friction and wear characterization; Fasteners; Shafts and gears; Reverse engineering of a mechanical assembly. This course is 2 credit-hours.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

No new technical text required specifically for this class. It is assumed that each student already has access to current EMD text (J. A. Collins, H. R. Busby & G. H. Staab, Mechanical Design of Machine Elements and Machines, 2nd edition, Wiley (2010) ISBN - 13 978-0-470-4130306. Laboratory instructions will be made available with each new lab.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Develop hands-on abilities to conduct mechanical engineering system testing
- Predict behavior prior to experimentation so that hypotheses may be tested and identify sources of discrepancy
- Critically evaluate multiple means of measurement and their discrepancies considering sources of error
- Consider the design of experiments and how to modify the experimental design to overcome limitations
- Learn about the role of experimentation and analysis in the design process

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Learning outcomes will be evaluated through a series of assessments divided into:

- Lab reports (6): Describe experiences, data and results collected throughout each of the experiments realized during the course.
- Homework assignments (6): Preparatory exercises to ensure students are familiar with the concepts to be verified through experimentation.
- Exam (1): Individual component of the grade, designed to test knowledge retention of each student.
