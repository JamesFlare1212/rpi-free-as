## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Comprehensive treatment of conduction and convection heat transfer. Emphasis is on physical and mathematical modeling of heat transfer phenomena with applications in engineering systems. Analytical and computational solution methods are discussed. The class is centered around a semester long project.

**Credits Allocated:** 3 semester hours

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understand and solve relevant engineering application problems through use of physical and mathematical modeling.
- Be able to select series solutions methodologies to address and evaluate various sets of engineering problems
- Be able to apply heat transfer models to solve problems in several engineering disciplines
- • Analyze simple systems subjected to conduction heat transfer using analytical and numerical methods • Demonstrate an understanding of boundary layer theory for forced and natural convection • Analyze simple systems subjected to convection heat transfer using analytical and numerical methods • Select simplifying assumptions and analyze, numerically and/or analytically, complex thermal transport systems, subjected to combined heat transfer modes. • Demonstrate professional conduct and adequate project contribution when part of an engineering team • Perform advanced analysis such as system level, optimization studies or combined steady-state/transient heat transfer for the design of thermal systems. • Present the analysis, results and recommendations in a technical report.
