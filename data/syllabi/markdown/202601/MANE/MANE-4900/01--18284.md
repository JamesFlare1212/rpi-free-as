## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

- Vibration of structures with single to multiple degrees of freedom.
- Modeling mass, stiffness and damping of structural elements.
- Damping treatments including viscous and coulomb damping.
- Free and forced response of flexible structures including axial, bending and torsional vibrations.
- Natural frequencies and mode shapes, vibration testing and methods to quantify damping.
- Introduction to numerical methods in structural dynamics including finite element (FEM), Rayleigh-Ritz and Galerkin methods.
- Energy methods in structural dynamics - Hamilton’s principle and Lagrange equations.
- Introduction to static aeroelastic instabilities including the phenomena of divergence and control reversal.
- Introduction to dynamic aeroelastic instabilities including flutter and buffeting.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The students who finish this course in a satisfactory manner will be able to demonstrate: i) An ability to identify, formulate and solve aerospace engineering problems; ii) A knowledge of structural dynamics and vibrations; iii) A knowledge of static aeroelastic effects and instabilities; iv) And a basic knowledge of dynamic aeroelastic effects and instabilities.
