## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is a cross-disciplinary, instructor-guided seminar in which students will actively pursue research, engineering and creative development projects related to the theory, application, and impact of quantum and related technologies. Open to all majors and grade levels.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Integrated perspectives in quantum technologies: - Analyze how quantum elements impact system design and behavior, and vice-versa. - Develop new applications that support or integrate quantum technologies. - Understand downstream and external impacts of quantum technologies.
- Design & innovation: - Develop and execute multidisciplinary, open-ended projects with real-world constraints. - Become proficient in rapid exploration and iteration in creative research and engineering projects. - Apply foundational knowledge and technical judgement in making tradeoff decisions.
- Professional development: - Collaborate with peers and colleagues across multiple technical and non-technical disciplines. - Effectively communicate complex subject matter to peers, colleagues, and general audiences. - Demonstrate novel applications and insights of quantum technologies to wide-ranging audiences.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Students will be assessed based on one or more of the following, subject to development pathways, resource budgets, technical pacing, mutual agreement with the instructor, and other variabilities inherent in research and creative work.

Delivered Artifacts

- Written reports
- Verbal presentations
- Software and computer code
- Hardware design, development, and fabrication
- Experimental data and analysis
- Individual and/or team self-assessments
- Feedback from peers and external collaborators
- Other deliverables as mutually agreed upon with the instructor

Methodology

- Depth and rigor of inquiry into existing research and designs.
- Demonstrated passion, drive, and courage to push boundaries in an unstructured research setting
- Professional candor, questioning attitude, and fact-based assessments of technical dissent and negative results
- Technical integrity and dedication to quality
- Effective management of project scope and budget
- Adherence to high ethical standards, policies, and codes of conduct

Participation and engagement

- Individual and/or team meetings with the instructor
- Independent team meetings
- Leadership of team members
- Leadership and management of technical work
- Participation in public presentations
- Active communication and engagement with collaborators

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Collaboration Policy

Students are encouraged to work in teams and seek collaboration opportunities both within and external to the Rensselaer community. Students are expected to properly acknowledge the contributions of all external collaborators, and adhere to any applicable intellectual property and confidentiality policies.

Plagiarism Policy

The Rensselaer Student Handbook defines plagiarism as “representing the work or words of another as one's own through the omission of acknowledgment or reference." It is your responsibility to ensure that all material is cited and attributed properly. The Rensselaer Library has resources available on proper citation and attribution at https://library.rpi.edu/citation-and-copyright/plagiarism.

Assignments found to have intentionally included plagiarized material (including plagiarized material or false citations produced by generative AI tools) will result in an F for the course and disqualification from future project opportunities.

Generative AI Policy

The use of generative AI tools (e.g. ChatGPT or other large language models) is discouraged unless communicated otherwise by the instructor. The use of such tools must be properly acknowledged in all submissions, and students are solely responsible for the correctness and quality of all material submitted. Failure to disclose the use of generative AI tools will be treated as intentional plagiarism.
