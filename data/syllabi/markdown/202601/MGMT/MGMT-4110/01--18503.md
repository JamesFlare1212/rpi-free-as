## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Operations is concerned with the design and management of the systems and processes that turn input into higher-valued output in an organization - - i.e. how companies turn good ideas and designs into actual products and services that their customers want to buy. Operations is responsible for producing and delivering the product or service to the customer. It is also how strategy becomes action. This course is designed to address key operations issues in both service and manufacturing firms. Operations can also be an effective competitive weapon and provide opportunities to win in markets worldwide.
