## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Digitization is transforming industries, changing business models and fundamentally altering how firms compete and the drivers of firm performance and success. In today’s digital economy, effective use of information and knowledge are critical necessities for firm success and profitability. Ability to develop innovative business models and use IT to drive value creation often differentiates successful firms form unsuccessful ones. The implications are that information systems are radically changing the world in which we live and work. Understanding information systems, how it is managed, and how it impacts individuals and organizations is an important part of understanding the world you will work in.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Written Case Analysis  
Each student is expected to prepare a written brief for five cases. For each case, a two-page (max, singlespaced) brief should be submitted on or before the day in which the case is scheduled for discussion  
in the class - late submissions cannot be graded. This is an individual task and students are expected to  
submit original analysis of each case. Each student will be assigned the five cases he/she is expected to  
submit written briefs at the beginning of the semester.  
Cases briefs will be graded for both form and content. Errors in spelling, grammar, or punctuation can  
detract from or distort the message you are trying to communicate! As a result, such errors will lower your  
grade. The grading of the briefs will be based upon the quality of your analysis. Merely restating case facts  
will not help your grade and, in fact, will use up valuable space in your brief. In writing your briefs, assume  
that you are a consultant to the company who is being paid to analyze the company's situation and make a  
set of recommendations.  
Briefs are due at the beginning of class. Before starting to write your brief, read the following framework  
for case analysis carefully so that you may formulate a targeted response.  
Approach each case analysis as a problem solving and decision making exercise. For each case analysis,  
you should:  
• Use the questions provided as the broad guidelines to focus on the key issues that needs to be addressed  
in your analysis.  
• Identify the core problem(s) being currently faced by the organization or the core opportunities that are  
potentially exploitable,  
• Design alternative solutions that are candidates to solve the problem(s) or capitalize on the  
opportunities,  
• Compare and contrast the alternative solutions using appropriate criteria  
Based on your analysis, make a recommendation on course of action that should be pursued by the  
organization.  
Participation in Class Discussions  
Students should read the assigned materials, analyze the cases and come prepared to discuss their findings  
in the class. Students will be assigned to breakout groups in each class meeting and are expected to engage  
in discussions among your peers in these groups and report out to the class the analysis and opinions. It is  
not sufficient to make simple comments in the class. Students are expected to take ownership of class  
discussions and help the class to develop a shared understanding of issues related to a topic or a case. So  
come prepared to express your views, defend your ideas, raise questions, probe other students’ viewpoints  
and engage in rigorous discussions.  
In assigning the participation grades both the quantity of comments (i.e. how many times a student speaks)  
and, more importantly, the quality of the comments will be taken into account. The quality of your  
comments will be evaluated using the following criteria:  
• Does the comment represent a solid analysis of a case or just a reiteration of case facts?  
• Does the comment address the question currently on the floor, or is it way off the mark?  
• Does the comment demonstrate an ability to listen to and build from what others have said?  
• Is the point made concisely, or is it buried in a long, rambling, diatribe?  
• Does the comment move the discussion to an important area or does it just rephrase what has already  
been said?  
• If "cold called," was the individual prepared?  
Leading case discussion: It is expected that students will lead the case discussions in the class. They are  
expected to come prepared to present their analysis in the class, when called to do so. This is a an important  
step in the learning process and active participation in leading case discussions will count towards the class  
participation grade.  
Technology Updates  
A key responsibility of a business professional irrespective of the functional role they are in is to monitor  
emerging technologies and management practices and evaluate how organizations could use the technology  
for performance improvement. You are expected to do something similar here. This is a group task, each  
group should choose one technology. You are required to let the Professor know the specific technology  
you have picked and get approval before proceeding with your analysis. You are required to 1) clearly  
describe the technology, 2) describe its use in organizations and 3) discuss the potential impact of and the  
business rationale for using the technology. A crisp presentation of your findings should be made as per  
schedule.  
You are also required to produce a report that can serve as a resource for understanding the business  
implications of the specific technology you are assigned. The report should contain an in-depth analysis of  
the technology and provide hyperlinks to appropriate and useful information about the technology. Keep in  
mind that there is a mountain of information available on any technology. You report should cut through  
the clutter and present the information in a concise and useful fashion. The value of your report also depends  
on the quality of your analysis.  
Pipes to Platforms Analysis  
This group task focuses at the organizational level. You are expected to pick an organization that is currently  
employing a “pipe” business model that you feel is ripe for transition to a “platform business model”. You  
are expected to describe the business model, the company’s market position and considerations for making  
such a transition. As part of you analysis, you should articulate how the business might proceed with a  
transition to a platform strategy, possible variants of such a strategy, important considerations, pricing and  
revenue, etc. You should also identify and discuss potential issues the company might face in such a  
transition. A written report and a presentation are the deliverables for this assignment.  
Final Project  
This group project is designed to provide you an opportunity to analyze how digitization is transforming  
industries. Industries are in various stages in being transformed with new business models, industry  
structure and players evolving in each industry. The extent to which emerging digital technologies has  
transformed or might transform the business practices might vary across industries. Some industries such  
as those dealing with information products such as financial service might be radically transformed by  
digitization while others such as mining might see more subtle impacts. However, in any industry, there are  
clear differences between innovative users of technology and others.  
The goal of this project is to help you analyze and discover how industries are likely to be impacted by  
the current and emerging information technologies. Each team is expected to select a specific industry and  
develop an analysis of the digital transformations occurring or likely to occur in that industry. You are  
expected to present a historical analysis leading up to the present time as to how IT has been used in the  
industry and the nature and extent of transformation in business practices that has occurred because of IT.  
You are expected to identify the innovators, fast followers and laggards in the industry with respect to  
using IT and compare and contrast their strategies and tactics and areas where they have effectively  
deployed IT. You are expected to critically evaluate the business models of the key firms in the industry,  
identify industry key success factors if any, and examine if and how firms are using IT to excel in areas  
critical for success in this industry.  
The information needed for your analysis can be collected from secondary sources such as reports in the  
business press (WSJ, Business Week, Lexus Nexus Dow Jones database, Computerworld, Information  
Week, CIO) as well as from industry contacts you might have. You may want to choose an industry you  
are familiar with or where you might have contacts you can tap for information. Your analysis must be  
fairly comprehensive and must cover business models and strategies, IT initiatives and innovations,  
revenues, cost structures, profitability, critical organizational capabilities etc. You are expected to present  
your findings in the form of a report and also make a formal presentation to the class.
