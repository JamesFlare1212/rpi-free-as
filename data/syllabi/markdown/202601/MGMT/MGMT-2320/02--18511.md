## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course studies the major financial decisions made by corporate managers. Essential in many of these decisions is the process of valuation. Topics include valuation of corporate securities, the cost of capital, capital structure choice, capital budgeting principles, corporate governance, and mergers and acquisitions.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- You will be able to make each of the following statements by the end of the semester:  I understand the conflicts of interest between different stakeholders of the firm and the tools used to solve these governance conflicts.  I can implement techniques to decide which projects a firm should undertake, and I can address many of the common issues in capital budgeting.  I can calculate a firm’s cost of equity and debt capital and I know how to use WACC appropriately in capital budgeting decisions.  I can evaluate stocks and bonds issued by corporations.  I understand how a firm can finance itself and can evaluate the factors that go into the debt issuance versus equity issuance decision of the firm.  I know the steps involved and the costs and benefits of raising capital for a firm through an IPO.  I know why firms retain or pay out profits and understand the costs and benefits of dividend payments and share repurchases.  I understand the benefits and common mistakes associated with mergers and acquisitions.
