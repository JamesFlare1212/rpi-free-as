## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course introduces the core concepts and practical knowledge needed to succeed in modern digital marketing. Students develop the ability to understand, evaluate, and execute digital marketing decisions. Topics include digital marketing foundations, web design, analytics, on-site and off-site SEO, paid search, display advertising, email marketing, social media strategy, online reputation management, mobile marketing, digital strategy, and AI for digital marketing.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Stukent Digital Marketing Essentials and Mimic Pro Simulation bundle.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- \- Demonstrate mastery of core digital marketing concepts and terminology. - Apply course concepts to make informed digital marketing decisions. - Locate, evaluate, and synthesize digital marketing information through research. - Communicate clearly, logically, and insightfully in presentations. - Practice critical thinking by asking high-quality questions and reasoning from first principles.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Exams**

There will be two closed-book exams assessing your understanding of key course topics.

**Assignments**

Assignments include chapter quizzes and in-class activities. These are primarily completion-based and are designed to reinforce your understanding and help you check your progress throughout the course.

**Presentations**

Each student will present twice during the semester as part of a group of two to three students. Presentations develop and assess your ability to do research and communicate effectively.

**Simulations**

You will apply course concepts in simulations to make informed marketing decisions and improve your practical skills.

**Class Contributions**

You will practice critical thinking through class interaction.

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).
