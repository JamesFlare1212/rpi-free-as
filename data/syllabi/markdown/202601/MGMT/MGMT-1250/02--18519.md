## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The course is the second in a two-part series of Professional Development class sessions that teach practical skills to enhance business students’ readiness to enter the professional world and achieve workplace success and a high degree of professionalism. The course emphasizes the following themes: professional oral and written presentation, professional goal setting, feedback giving and receiving, conflict and business ethics, networking, as well as advanced employment search and application process skills.

## Course Text(s)

## Course Goals

### Goals:

- Demonstrate and apply oral and written communication skills through class discussions, case analyses, formal and informal presentations, and written assignments.
- Recognize and demonstrate professionalism and ethics.
- Understand and apply Feedback and Conflict resolution strategies.
- Identify, demonstrate and evaluate their core leadership competencies.
- Apply knowledge of career planning resources and tools to effectively create a plan for identifying, applying to, and obtaining full time, internship, or co-op opportunities, including career searches through various job search platforms, application materials and online presence, professional networking, and effective interviewing techniques.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

**Attendance:** Attendance is mandatory and worth 10% of the final grade. Students who attend every class, or miss one class, earn ten points. Those who miss two unexcused classes earn five points. Students who miss three or more unexcused classes earn no credit for attendance. Should a student have five or more unexcused absences, they will **automatically fail** the course.

It is the student’s responsibility to contact the instructor and obtain course material missed due to absence. Students are expected to complete all assignments according to their due date listed in the syllabus. Whether excused or not, missing class immediately prior to an assignment due date or test does not exempt the student from completing the work on its due date.

Make-up work must be completed within 1 week (7 calendar days) of the missed class.

Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process.
