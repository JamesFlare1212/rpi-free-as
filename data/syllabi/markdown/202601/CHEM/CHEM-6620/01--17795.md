## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course is designed to provide those understandings for graduate students in chemistry, chemical engineering, biomedical engineering, material science and engineering or related disciplines, who did not take introductory level polymer chemistry as undergraduate. This course will focus on the structure, synthesis, and reaction of chemistry associated with both natural and synthetic polymers. Associated topics such as polymer properties, characterization and processing will be discussed.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

1. Students will understand the basic principles that apply to the synthesis, structure, properties, characterization and applications of polymers.
2. Students will gain fundamental knowledge on polymer synthesis methods in conjunction with development of new properties of polymeric materials.
3. Students will show competence in the fundamental concepts in the structure-property-relationships of polymeric materials that are a key to the application of polymers.
4. Students will learn the elemental concepts in the molecular heterogeneity of polymers as materials.
5. Students will learn the analysis and characterization of polymers.
6. Students will gain knowledge on recent progress in polymer research through literature survey.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

**Lecture topics**

1. Introduction, Definitions and History & Polymer Classification and Structure (Ch 1)
2. Molecular Weight and Polymer Solutions (Ch 2)
3. Chemical Structure and Polymer Morphology (Ch 3)
4. Chemical Structure and Polymer Properties (Ch 4)
5. Evaluation, Characterization, and Analysis of Polymers (Ch 5)
6. Free Radical Polymerization and Transition Metal Coordination Polymerization of Vinyl Polymers (Ch 6 and 8)
7. Controlled Polymerization of Vinyl Polymers: Anionic, Cationic, and Radical, Ring-Opening Polymerizations (Ch 7, other references)
8. Reactions of Vinyl Polymers (Ch 9)
9. Step Growth Polymerization and Ring-Opening Polymerization (Ch 10)
10. Polyesters and Polyamides (Ch 12 and 13)
11. Other Nonvinyl Polymers: Polyethers, Polysulfides, and Related Polymers (Ch 11 and 14)
12. Miscellaneous Polymers: Heterocyclic Polymers, Inorganic Polymers, Natural Polymers (Ch 15, 16, 17, 18)

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Student will be able to apply organic chemistry knowledge to practical chemical problems particularly focusing polymer chemistry topics.
- 2\. Students will be able to analyze polymer synthesis methods in conjunction with development of new properties of polymeric materials.
- 3\. Students will be able to interpret the molecular heterogeneity of polymer materials.
- 4\. Students will be able to infer the fundamental concepts in the structure-property relationships of polymeric materials that are keys to applications of polymers.
- 5\. Students will identify and illustrate selected applications of polymer by reading chemistry literatures.
- 6\. Students will summarize a selected topic of cutting-edge polymer research from literature survey.
