## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The course objective is to add economic consideration to the engineering decision-making process. Mathematical models and economic analysis methods will be presented in this course. Financial functions will be used in MS Excel spreadsheets for analyzing complex problems. This course will provide students with an integrated systems perspective on the practice of their chosen engineering field. After completion, students will be able to use computing and related engineering tools when performing engineering economic analysis  
in the business and the public sector, will have the knowledge to pass the engineering economics portion of professional licensure exams, and will have the skills and tools to include economic considerations in design decision-making.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Comprehend, formulate, and solve engineering economics problems with financial transactions or cash flows.
- Evaluate alternatives based on equivalent worth, rate of return, cost-benefit analysis, etc.
- Implement concepts of replacement, depreciation, taxation, and inflation in engineering decision-making.
- Evaluate the probabilistic nature of engineering problems by quantifying sensitivity and risk involved.
- Analyze portfolios for optimal resource allocation scenarios under budget constraints.
