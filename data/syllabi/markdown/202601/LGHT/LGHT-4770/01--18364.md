## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Lighting Technologies & Applications is a 4-credit course intended to provide students with an in-depth understanding of the components of lighting systems and enable them to critically explore their use. Through lectures, lab sessions, readings, research assignments, and application projects, students will acquire principles and working knowledge of the relevant equipment and techniques for developing lighting solutions.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

In this course, students in LGHT-4770 will be able to:

1. Assess the characteristics of light sources, luminaires, and lighting controls as they apply to their integration into lighting systems for general illumination. Characteristics covered in class include, among others, photometric, electrical, thermal and mechanical properties, useful lifetime, installation and maintenance, and cost.
2. Describe the operation and key performance characteristics of lighting systems under ideal and realistic conditions.
3. Evaluate various applications of lighting systems and their components, and identify what makes them effective.
4. Evaluate and compare alternate lighting systems based on multiple criteria, such as photometric performance, economics, and energy efficiency.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

- Lighting terminology and introduction to photometry units
- Fundamentals of photometry
- Overview of legacy light sources
- Introduction to solid-state lighting and basics of LED technology
- Electrical, thermal and optical properties of LEDs
- LED system integration issues
- Application issues with LEDs
- Light distribution, basics of optics and photometric reports
- Principles of lighting calculations, lumen method
- Lighting controls
- Lighting economics
- Overview of lighting standards
- Advanced lighting topics
