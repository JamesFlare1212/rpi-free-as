## Course Goals

### Goals:

Introduction to heat transfer including conduction, external and internal convection, and radiation. Emphasis is on applications of heat transfer to a wide variety of engineering systems including HVAC, automotive design, materials processing etc.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- On successful completion of this course students will be able to: • to select simplifying assumptions in analyzing a complex thermal system. •
- • to analyze heat transfer in thermal systems by identifying heat transfer modes–convection (forced/natural, internal/external), conduction (transient and steady-state) and radiation using the appropriate equations to evaluate them.
- to analyze thermal systems by applying conservation of mass and energy.
- • to analyze the performance of heat exchangers and design heat exchangers given a set of specifications.

**Project**

The project consists of a finned heat sink able to decrease the temperature of a small size heater (40mmX40mm) from 70°C to 50°C under natural convection (no fans). Examples of finned heat sink are shown in the image below. A team of two students must be working on this project. The finned heat sink must be designed assuming the sink is made out of Ice9 Flex TPU from TCPoly. The room temperature is set at 25C and the heater with or without the heat sink is mounted vertically. Updated specifications and project requirements/grding rubrics will be provided/confirmed in class before the project starts.
