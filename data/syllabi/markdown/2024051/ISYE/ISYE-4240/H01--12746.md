## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course is meant to provide you with skills, tools, and perspectives for engineering project management. I want you to be able to directly apply what we learn to your work now and in the future. The focus of the course is two class projects (CP) that will provide you with tools that you might apply at work. One project will ask you to use traditional project management thinking and processes, while the second will ask you to use “agile” and “scrum” thinking and processes.  
**Credits Allocated:** 3 semester hours

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Utilize traditional project management processes, along with "product led planning" to develop a highly effective project plan (scope, WBS, and activities list) and schedule (Gantt chart).
- Employ traditional project management processes, together with their creativity, to effectively predict the deliverables and activities needed to reach a project goal.
- See the limitations and strengths of traditional project management through the study of Agile methodologies.
- Organized project planning focused on delivering value to the customer.
- Understand the Triple Constraint (scope, time, & cost) and how important it is in establishing the EVM (Earn-value management) monitoring during execution.
- Develop a vision statement that provides clear direction for a project.
- Iteratively create product road maps, release plans, and sprint plans from a product backlog.
