## Course Content

### Content Details:

Kinematics of Particles and Rigid Bodies; Kinetics of Particles and Rigid-Bodies using Newton’s Second Law, Work-energy for Particles and Rigid Bodies; Impulse-momentum for Particles and Rigid bodies, Introduction to Three-Dimensional Kinetics of Rigid Bodies

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Master the basic principles of particle and rigid body dynamics
- Systematically apply these principles to mechanical systems rising in diverse aspects of technology.
- Solve the mathematical equations that govern these systems, and physically interpret the results.
- Master vector algebra at a high level as a result of applying previous outcomes.

## Homework:

Ten Homework sets will be assigned, which will usually consist of approximately 4 problems. The schedule of the homework due dates is included in the schedule at the end of this document, and LMS will also inform students of the due dates of homework sets. Homework assignments are to be scanned to the portable document format (.pdf) and must be submitted by the **beginning of class** on the dates given in the syllabus. **No late homework assignments will be accepted**. For homework grading, the usual procedure will be such that only one out of the assigned homework problems will be randomly chosen for grading. 50% of the credit will be given to effort in attempting all homework problems, and the other 50% will be given to solving the selected homework problem, which will be announced on the day the homework is due. **The lowest homework grade will be dropped.** The last assigned homework, HW10, cannot be dropped. As an addendum to this policy, certain homework assignments may be graded out of 10 points total. Students who do not complete a particular portion a homework assignment which was graded may receive 0/10 points, even if other non graded work is submitted.

You are encouraged to collaborate with other students on homework assignments, however, each student is expected to submit their own homework.

## In Class Assignments

Some lectures, an “in-class assignment” will be collected and graded. When conducted in lecture, these assignments are due at the end of class. If it is an online quiz, these quizzes will have clearly labeled timing, which will vary pending the difficulty or scheduling of content in the course. Some of these assignments will be single problems, while some lectures will feature digital assignments assigned through LMS. You may use any available resource to help you complete problem statements, including direct help from the professor. Digital assignments given through LMS are expected to be solely the work of the individual student. The lowest grade of this category will be dropped at the end of the semester, in addition to any excused

absences. Note, these assignments will be created and distributed on an ad hoc basis, to allow flexibility in the delivery of content through the semester.

## Project:

There will be a project to complete during the semester. The project will be announced and discussed during class on Thursday July 18th. **The due date for this project is Thursday August 8****th** **at 5:00 PM RPI local time. This is a hard deadline, and will not be extended.** This project will be the creation of a PowerPoint Presentation demonstrating how to solve a Engineering Dynamics Problem. Detailed instructions on how to create this project will be provided on LMS after the delivery of the first examination. Note : No Oral presentations will be conducted. **Failure to submit an acceptable attempt of this project will result in, at a minimum, a reduction of one letter grade at the end of the term. You must attempt this project.**

## Exams:

Three exams are scheduled on the syllabus. **These exams will be in person examinations.** Exams must be completed. If you are in need of a makeup examination, you must contact the instructor for a makeup exam, and the policy for excused absence may vary. If for *any reason, including excused absences*, more than one exam must be missed, an incomplete will be given for this class, and you will need to retake the course.

The final exam (which is the third exam of three total exams) must be taken to receive a grade for this course. This final examination will be comprehensive, in that the material of this course will continually build upon itself during the semester. The final examination will be administered on Thursday, August 15th during class. If you are in need of a makeup final examination, you must contact the instructor for a makeup exam, and the policy for excused absence may vary.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

This statement is intended to cover most, but not all, forms of academic dishonesty. (You should refer to *The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities* for detailed information on Institute policy in this regard.)

## Homework:

Collaboration among the students in the class is permitted and, in fact, encouraged, as described above. Seeking help from other sources is also encouraged. However, the work you submit should be your own. Evidence of you merely copying another student’s homework (or from the homework solutions) will be considered academic dishonesty and will result in **a zero grade for homework for the semester for all parties involved.**

## Exams:

Collaboration during an exam (exchanging verbal or written information or copying) is, of course, prohibited. In addition, use of email or the internet during exams for any purpose is strictly prohibited. The use of Chegg, Yahoo! Answers, Quora, Discord, Twitter, Facebook, messaging programs of any kind, or other materials that are not assigned through the course are prohibited in the event of a pivot to online examination. You may be required to take and complete an integrity quiz on LMS prior to the start of exams, indicating that you accept the terms under which the exams are given.

## Unauthorized collaboration or compromising of any examination will result in investigations. All parties involved will receive an F in the course.

In the event that the course needs to pivot to online instruction, which is beyond the control of the instructor, examinations will be given online, and will be subject to these rules in addition to other rules which will be communicated electronically through LMS. In person examinations will be limited to pen, paper, a hand held calculator. Any additional materials permitted for the examination will be on an exam-by-exam basis (for instance, a table of moments of inertia)

## Other:

Outrageous offenses such as arranging for a substitute to take an examination, attempting to obtain examination questions or solutions in advance, or attempting to change a grade record will result in a failing grade for the course and a recommendation to the Dean of Students that the student(s) in question be suspended.
