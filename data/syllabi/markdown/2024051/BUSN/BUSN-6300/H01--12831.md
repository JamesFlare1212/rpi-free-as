## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

In this course, students develop their capacity as leaders in a dynamic world, planning and executing strategy to achieve organizational objectives. Students develop frameworks for making decisions that enhance the value creation of their organization while demonstrating full commitment to ethical decisions and outcomes. Students learn to plan and execute to achieve goals fully and effectively. Students develop their emotional intelligence as managers and leaders of the human elements of their organizations. *NOTE: Students must attend a Rensselaer Leadership Experience (RLE) as part of this course.*

### Synchronous Sessions

Please see the “Interactions” page in Canvas for more information about the synchronous sessions for this course.

### Rensselaer Leadership Experience

During this course, you will be required to attend the Rensselaer Leadership Experience (RLE).  
The RLE is a 2-day immersive experience in which you will explore leadership on a personal, professional, and actionable level. Here you will participate in high impact case studies, exercises, and discussions with your classmates around topics related to leadership effectiveness.  
Information about the RLE for this semester can be found in the semester-specific syllabus in the Rensselaer*Studio* —note that this will be updated in the month following the semester start date.

- **Pre-Work**: You will be completing an EQ-i assessment prior to attending the RLE, as there will be a session during the event that invites you to explore how your EQ-i relates to your leadership qualities. Details about this can be found on the “Projects” page of the Rensselaer*Studio* under “Pre-Work for RLE.”

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Develop and enhance personal leadership capabilities to influence and lead individuals and teams and realize organizational objectives.
- Explore ethics in leadership in a range of decision-making scenarios in various contexts and domains.
- Use the RPI Analytic Framework for decision-making with data, create sound responses to problems, make informed decisions, and develop analytical hypotheses.
- Understand and perform strategic analyses and how these are used to develop a sustainable competitive advantage.
- Diagnose, prescribe, plan, and execute organizational and transformational change.
- Communicate with purpose and clarity to present ideas in written and oral formats.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

This course uses a series of projects that serve as the context in which students learn and then apply this learning. Specifically, this course has five major projects:

1. **Project 1 – Value Creation**: In this project, you will learn value creation for a range of important stakeholders for an organization and assemble an executive presentation using your company or organization as an example.
2. **Project 2 –** **Strategic Analysis and Planning**: In this project, you will perform strategic analyses using multiple methods in order to contemplate and understand potential challenges facing your organization as it considers strategic planning, competitiveness and growth.
3. **Project 3 –** **You as a Leader:** As a follow-on project to your experience in the RLE, you will create a personal and strategic leadership development plan using a leading assessment tool. You will spend time evaluating your plans in real time, and with personal coaching. In addition, you will spend time taking an outcome-based view of ethics in leadership through study and scenario-based application.
4. **Project 4 –** **Leaders Making Decisions with Data:** In this project, you will analyze a predetermined dataset along with a preliminary investigation to determine how customers feel about a product in the market. Using data summary/analysis techniques with visualization, you will draw out your conclusions and present deductive reasoning to support possible actions.
5. **Project 5 –** **How the Leader Makes Things Happen:** In the culminating course project, you will be provided with a case study that will enable you to employ all elements of this course in a final project. You will be in the position of a CEO required to perform a business turnaround confronting financial, organizational, commercial and operational challenges. You will create short and longer-term plans using the data provided, strategic analysis performed, and your understanding of the organizational issues.
