### Prerequisites or Other Requirements:

CHME 2010 and CHME 2020.

To be eligible for the online safety courses, you will have to become a member of AIChE. Membership is free for undergraduate students, but you should also join RPI's Student Chapter as well. If you are not already a member, you can signup at https://www.aiche.org/membership/student

### Additional Information for Course Information Section:

This course is part of your Chemical Process Design sequence and is designed to supplement your lab safety courses and deals with safety protocols and practices associated with chemical process plants. It is designed to provide information necessary for your ABET accredited degree. The course is offered on-line by the American Institute of Chemical Engineers. It is part of the Safety and Chemical Engineering (SAChE) Certificate program offered by the society.
