## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course is meant to provide you with skills, tools, and perspectives for engineering project management. I want you to be able to apply what we learn directly to your work now and in the future. The focus of the course is two class projects (CP) that will provide you with tools that you might apply at work. One project will ask you to use a predictive project management approach, while the second will ask you to use an adaptive (agile) project management approach.  
**Credits Allocated:** 3 semester hours

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- RPI at Work Section Learning Outcome 1: Utilize predictive project development processes and tools (e.g., requirements traceability matrix, work breakdown structure, Gantt chart) to develop a project plan.
- RPI at Work Section Learning Outcome 2: Describe the limitations and strengths of predictive project management and adaptive (agile) project management.
- RPI at Work Section Learning Outcome 3: Utilize adaptive project development processes and tools (e.g., product backlog, product roadmap, release plans, sprint plans) to develop a project plan.
- RPI at Work Section Learning Outcome 4: Understand the “triple constraints” (scope, time, & cost) in planning and executing projects.
- Utilize traditional project management processes, along with "product led planning" to develop a highly effective project plan (scope, WBS, and activities list) and schedule (Gantt chart).
- Employ traditional project management processes, together with their creativity, to effectively predict the deliverables and activities needed to reach a project goal.
- See the limitations and strengths of traditional project management through the study of Agile methodologies.
- Organized project planning focused on delivering value to the customer.
- Understand the Triple Constraint (scope, time, & cost) and how important it is in establishing the EVM (Earn-value management) monitoring during execution.
- Develop a vision statement that provides clear direction for a project.
- Iteratively create product road maps, release plans, and sprint plans from a product backlog.
