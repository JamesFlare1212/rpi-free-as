## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course will require participating undergraduates to complete **three (3) written/survey assignments, three (3) assigned Percipio modules** on the NACE Career Readiness Competencies, as well as a series of **weekly** **critical reflection journals** throughout the semester. These assignments, modules, and reflections are to be directly connected to the three objectives you will set for yourself. At the end of the semester, you will receive an “S” or a “U” – complete all assignments thoroughly and thoughtfully and you will successfully meet the graduation requirement.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Students will be challenged to develop in at least three (3) of the areas outlined by the National Association of Colleges & Employers (NACE) Career Readiness Competencies. These competencies are:

- Career & Self-Development
- Communication
- Critical Thinking
- Equity & Inclusion
- Leadership
- Professionalism
- Teamwork
- Technology

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will identify their personal and professional goals for future career development
- Students will articulate and evaluate their professional growth and career readiness through a collection and presentation of relevant evidence (e.g., portfolio)
- Students will be able to explain the eight NACE Career Readiness Competencies, how each connects to the world of work, and how they’ve applied them to achieve their own self-set goals
- Students will produce and communicate evidence of their learning, skills, and accomplishments to professional audiences

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Assignment 1:** Objectives, Milestones, & Self-Evaluation

Describe three measurable deliverables that you would like to focus on during your Discovery Semester. In this assignment, you must describe your three objectives (using the SMART goal development model as a guide), provide a detailed description of milestones/deliverables that you will accomplish to ensure progress, and complete a survey to self-evaluate your confidence levels with each of NACE’s Career Readiness Competencies. You must also upload a recent resume. **You should plan to actively work toward your goals for 15-20 hours per week during the semester in addition to completing each assignment.**

**Assignment 2:** Industry Exploration

Write a two-page (minimum), single-spaced report on the industry you are interested in pursuing. This will include identifying the industry, reporting on three potential job titles or roles that fall under that field, and determining/reflecting on which skills you already have *and* still need to acquire or strengthen to prepare for a career in said industry.

**Assignment 3:** Final Survey & Reflection

Submit a final self-evaluation survey and a reflection on your Discovery Semester. This will take the form of a two-to-three page, single-spaced essay. While the prompt is open-ended and offers you the opportunity to creatively tell your professional development story, the assignment should ultimately address the top three (3) NACE Career Readiness Competencies you have gained or enhanced during your Discovery Semester. This includes specific actions you took or projects you completed to develop those skills and why those competencies matter to your field of study.

**Percipio Modules**

As a part of your development of the NACE Career Readiness Competencies, you will need to complete three (3) of the assigned Percipio modules. These modules will help you learn more about the competencies and how they relate to the world of work. **Additional details and instructions will be added to the LMS course after the start of the semester.**

**Critical Reflections**

Each week, you will be required to submit critical reflection entries (at least 100 words) via prompted LMS discussion boards to reflect on what you are learning during your experience.

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).
