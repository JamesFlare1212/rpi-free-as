### Additional Information for the Instructor Section:

Welcome to Agile Planning and Execution! My name is [David Stec](https://faculty.rpi.edu/david-stec) and, as your instructor, I would like to welcome you to BUSN 6105. My role will be to assist and guide you in completing the project-based learning activities for this course where you will experience change from a new perspective by integrating and applying contemporary change management tactics and adaptive project management practices to plan and execute a sustainable change.

You will leave the course with a better understanding of types of change, change models, and agile management practices to best implement these changes within your own organization. Typically, the study of project management focuses on the tasks needed to accomplish a project (e.g., what, when, who, etc.). It provides project structure and addresses the actions and deliverables. Change management, however, focuses on the people impacted by the change, ways to reduce resistance to change, and to elicit behaviors to support change. Although change management is critical to the success of any change initiative, it is often forgotten and not integrated into project management theory.  
Before you get started, review this document to familiarize yourself with the course, the course structure, expectations, outcomes, and the flow of work. I will be here throughout the course to help provide guidance and mentorship.

## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course focuses on the integration of adaptive (Agile) project management principles with change management models to plan and execute a change initiative within an organization. An array of change management models, processes and their respective applicability are presented facilitating the appropriate selection of a change model for the given change initiative. Integrating Agile and change management allows for a more robust understanding of the types of change and how to effectively plan and implement the change. Students will:

- Identify a proposed change by evaluating the organization’s current state, proposing specific improvements that better meet organizational objectives.
- Develop a fully integrated change and project management plan using the Agile methodology to plan and execute a change management initiative.
- Use leadership best practices to engage and involve stakeholders to ensure the project is fully vetted.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Critical Thinking Skills - Analyze and solve complex managerial and organizational problems.
- Plans for Change - Use project management techniques and the change management process to develop achievable plans that deliver change on time, on budget, and on specification.
- Implementation and Management of Change - Use change processes to put the change(s) in place
- Effective Communication - Communicate with purpose and clarity to present ideas in written and oral formats.
- Leadership Capacities - Use leadership tools to facilitate the inquiry and change processes within the organization.
