## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understand the principles required to make good material selections concerning drug and gene delivery.
- Be able to read a scientific paper and present the findings of the paper to a classroom of peers.
- Assess and re-engineer a current drug or gene delivery approach.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

1. Understand the principles required to make good material selections concerning drug and gene delivery. – **Evaluation: Exam.**
2. Present a research paper in the drug and gene delivery field. – **Evaluation: Paper Presentation.**
3. **Term project** – where students individually assess and re-engineer a drug or gene delivery approach currently in clinical trials or being sold commercially.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

- **Exams: 30%** Two Midterm Exams (15% per exam),
- **Class Attendance/Participation: 10%** Class Participation 5% - Student Must Actively Participate in Case Study/Class Discussion Class Attendance 5% - Attendance is Mandatory. The following penalty system will be assessed for missing class. For each unexcused absence, 1% will be deducted.
- **Paper Presentation: 20%** Students in teams must select one scientific manuscript from a journal with an impact factor of 4 or greater and present the paper to the rest of the class (10% report, 10% presentation).
- **Term Project 40%** Comprehensive Outline (5%) Final Report/Presentation (35%)

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Potential Places Where Academic Dishonesty May Take Place In This Course:

1. Signing an attendance sheet for a friend who has missed class.
2. Changing exam answers after the exam has been graded to receive additional points in a dishonest manner.
3. Lying to a professor or TA concerning reasons for missing an exam/assignment.
4. Since the course requires a group project presentation and report, any instance of plagiarism or copying other’s work word for word without proper citation is academic dishonesty.
5. Use of cell phone during an exam.

I have a zero tolerance policy concerning academic dishonesty. Any of the above or other instances where I believe academic dishonesty has occurred will result in the student receiving an F in this course. Those students who I believe are guilty of academic dishonesty will be reported to the Dean of Student’s Office.
