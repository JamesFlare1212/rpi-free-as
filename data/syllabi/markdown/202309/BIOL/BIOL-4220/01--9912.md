## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course is designed to create an applied learning environment to introduce students to large scale datasets in the environmental field and learn advanced techniques for analyzing them. Students will learn multivariate data exploration techniques, evaluate the quality of large datasets, and analyze the data using machine learning techniques. Specifically students will propose, develop, and finalize projects where they will apply machine learning approaches to datasets to understand complex environmental biology processes. Along with these topics, students will learn to critically read current scientific literature relevant to their projects. This course can be used to fulfill the Culminating Experience requirement in Biology.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate an understanding of the basic principles of multivariate data analysis with an emphasis on supervised and unsupervised learning by answering multiple choice, short answer and true/false questions on in-class and take home exams.
- Apply the techniques of machine learning to understand environmental issues using large datasets in in class project work.
- Design a mini proposal for research.
- Conduct a machine learning project that explores a biological process
- Present the findings of the research project to the class.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Exams**: The three exams will be a mixture of short answer and multiple choice questions (in-class / closed-notes) and a take home portion.  
**Homework:** will be a mixture of Datacamp exercises or example coding exercises, and development of a team presentation (class# 20-23) for in-class review.  
**Class Project:** will be a semester long effort that will culminate in a final presentation which will be included in their assessment. Checkpoints though the semester will include class update every other week, a ‘mini-proposal’ and a final presentation and analysis.  
**Class participation:** will be graded by attendance and level of participation in in class exercises and discussions.
