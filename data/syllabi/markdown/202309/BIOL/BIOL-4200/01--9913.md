## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An introduction to the concepts and techniques of modern statistics. Computer-based, in-class sessions will emphasize hands-on application of statistical techniques using data and examples drawn from the biological and medical sciences. Pre-class, online exercises will prepare students for in-depth classroom explorations. Topics covered will include sampling, regression, analysis of variance, and factor analysis, MANOVA, maximum likelihood methods, discriminant analysis, time series analysis, and forecasting. The final project requires analysis of a major research-related data set and write-up of results in the format of a scientific paper.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will be able to apply the concepts and techniques of modern, computer-based statistical analysis in R to analyze data from the biomedical literature.
- Students will develop both verbal and written fluency in using the language of statistics to summarize, discuss, make meaning of, and present the outcome of statistical tests and procedures.
- Students will understand, interpret, and critique the statistical procedures and results.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Class attendance is crucial for this type of course. So much happens in each class that you will quickly find yourself dazed and confused if you start missing classes. In-class assignments will be assigned every class meeting.

Excused Absences: There will be times during the semester when you may need to miss a class because of other commitments, unexpected events, or illness. If you are unable to attend class, please send me an email stetlj2@rpi.edu with your excuse for requesting an absence that day.

You are responsible for making up the missed material. I suggest you first reach out to another student in the class to obtain any code that you may have missed. Then review the lecture and the code, and submit the in-class assignment. Please email me with any specific questions after you review the material - we can then find a time to meet if necessary. Missed In-class assignments must be turned in to LMS within one week of returning to class to be eligible for credit.

## In-Class Sessions:

Most sessions will involve introductory discussion of the topic at hand followed by exercises and hands-on work with the computer. As you are well aware, computers can be a serious distraction in the classroom. In order to create an environment where we can all learn and receive the maximum advantage from the course’s computer intensive learning environment, please refrain from texting and surfing the web during class.

## Exams:

There will be 1 quiz and 3 exams, each covering ~1/3 of the course. Each exam will consist of a take-home component involving analysis of data sets with R using the techniques we have learned about, and an in-class part that will be oriented towards concepts and problems similar to the homework and the examples we go over in class. With a handful of exceptions that you will know about in advance, the formulas needed for the in-class exams will be included as part of the exam. We will discuss the date, the topics, and the format of each exam in-class. There will be no final exam. There will be a data analysis presentation that will be discussed during the course.
