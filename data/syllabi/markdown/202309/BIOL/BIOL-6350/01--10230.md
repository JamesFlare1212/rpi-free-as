## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Student Learning Outcomes (BIOL-4350/6350) At the end of the course a successful student will be able to: • Demonstrate the ability to distinguish the main properties of virus • Demonstrate the ability to explain virus entry to the host cells • Demonstrate the ability to summarize the mechanisms of viral replication assembly, and release • Demonstrate the ability to distinguish the genetic organization of virus • Demonstrate the ability to summarize the evolution of virus • Demonstrate the ability to summarize current topics on viral infections.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Basic principles:**

The basis of the relationship between the teacher and students is trust. The teacher has the obligation to prepare the lectures and work with the students. Students must work on the assignments and learn the material.

Students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process.

Students and teachers will act in honesty. The Rensselaer Handbook defines various forms of academic dishonesty and procedures for responding to them. All forms are violations of the trust between students and teachers. Students should familiarize themselves with this portion of the Rensselaer Handbook and should note that the penalties for plagiarism and other forms of cheating can be quite severe. Reading the syllabus is important.

-Plagiarism. Anyone cheating on an exam will be given a zero for that exam. **Two cheating episodes will result in an F in the course and may be reported for academic discipline.** NO BOOKS, notes, crib sheets or other written materials are allowed during exams.
