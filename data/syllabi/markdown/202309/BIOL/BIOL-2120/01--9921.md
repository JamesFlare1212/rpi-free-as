## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**Course Description****:** This is an introductory course in cell structure and function. It is intended for Biology and Biomedical Engineering majors, and covers such topics as chemical composition of cells, molecular structure and function of proteins, transport of solutes across the membrane, and the basic biochemistry underlying cellular behavior including cancer cells**. Having *Introductory college courses in biology and chemistry are highly encouraged.***

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

***Essential Cell Biology,6th edition***

## Course Goals

### Goals:

**Aims****:** To provide an understanding of the fundamental mechanisms of cellular function, to develop critical thinking skills, and gain experience in laboratory procedures in the context of modern cell biology.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Demonstrate understanding of prokaryotic and eukaryotic cell structure and function by answering multiple choice and short answer/drawing questions on in-class exams and homework problems.
- • Gain experience in writing about basic biological processes.
- • Interpret the results of online databases regarding protein alignments and analysis
