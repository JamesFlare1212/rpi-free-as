## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate the ability to distinguish the main properties of virus
- Demonstrate the ability to explain virus entry to the host cells
- Demonstrate the ability to summarize the mechanisms of viral replication assembly, and release
- Demonstrate the ability to distinguish the genetic organization of virus
- Demonstrate the ability to summarize the evolution of virus
- Demonstrate the ability to summarize current topics on viral infections.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

This course will provide a general overview on Viruses. The course will offer the essential and basic aspects of Virology.

The objectives of the course are to introduce: viral structure, classification, entry, and replication for each of the major classes of viruses.

We will also use case studies to understand host interactions, prevention of viral infections and therapies against viral infections.

Finally, we will review some current topics including viruses that infect viruses, the use of viruses as research tools and COVID.

This course can be used to fulfill the “Culminating Experience in Biology.”
