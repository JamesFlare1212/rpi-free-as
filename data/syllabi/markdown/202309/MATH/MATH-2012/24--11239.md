## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Directional derivatives, maxima and minima, double integrals, line integrals, div and curl, and Green’s Theorem; matrix algebra and systems of linear equations, vectors and linear transformations in R^n, eigenvectors and eigenvalues, applications in engineering and science.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

1. Calculus: Early Transcendentals, Fourth ed., (Rogawski, et al) Macmillan Publishers, 2019. We will cover material from chapters 14–17.
2. Introduction to Linear Algebra (Classic Version), Fifth ed., by Lee Johnson, Dean Riess, and Jimmy Arnold, Pearson, 2018. We will cover material from chapters 1, 3, and 4.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

**Attendance:** Students are responsible for all material covered in lectures and recitations whether they are present or not. Attendance is expected in lectures, but will only be checked on exam days. Attendance is required in recitations whenever there is a quiz.

### Other Course Policies:

**Exams:** The material covered on each exam will be announced on LMS when the date of the exam is near. Exams will be given during lecture (see **Meeting Times** above). Exam 1 covers the first half of the Multivariable Calculus content and Exam 2 covers all Multivariable Calculus content (including material on Exam 1). Exam 3 covers the first half of the Matrix Algebra content and Exam 4 covers all Matrix Algebra content (including material on Exam 3).

**Make-up exams will only be given to students with an** [excused absence documented by the Student Success office](https://success.studentlife.rpi.edu/current-students/academic-and-personal-support/requesting-excused-absence) **or, if the absence is due to an athletic competition, documentation from a coach.** Thus, if a student misses an exam because they overslept, didn’t know where the exam was, or didn’t know there was an exam, to give a few examples, the student may not take the make-up exam and will receive a score of 0 on the exam.

**Missing an exam should not be taken lightly.** Thus, students who miss an exam due to an excused absence must make every effort to notify the instructor **before the exam begins** to avoid grade penalties.

- If a student misses an exam due to an excused absence and notifies the instructor before the exam begins, then the student may take a make-up exam without penalty.
- If a student misses an exam due to an excused absence and notifies the instructor after the exam begins but before 11:59 PM the day after their scheduled exam, then the student may take a make-up exam and a 30% penalty will be applied to the make-up exam score.
- If a student misses an exam due to an excused absence and fails to notify the instructor before 11:59 PM on the day after their scheduled exam, then the student may not take the make-up exam and receives a score of 0 on the exam.
- If a student misses an exam and does not have an excused absence, then the student may not take the make-up exam and receives a score of 0 on the exam.

It is the sole responsibility of the student to notify the instructor to make arrangements for a make-up exam when an exam is missed due to an excused absence.

**Disability Services for Students (DSS):** Students with a memorandum from the [Disability Services for Students (DSS)](http://studenthealth.rpi.edu/disabilityservices) office identifying accommodations for exams must email the memorandum to the instructor by **Monday, September 11**. Students will not be granted accommodations unless a memorandum from DSS has been received. Providing the accommodations requires advance planning. Students may not be granted accommodations if a memorandum is received on the day of an exam.

**Homework:** Homework will be assigned regularly from the textbooks. Though these problems will not be collected nor graded, they should not be viewed as optional. Success in the course depends on successful completion of all homework. Exam and quiz problems are frequently chosen from homework assignments and lecture examples.

**Recitations:** The teaching assistant (TA) will conduct weekly recitations. During these 50-minute sessions, the TA will solve problems and answer questions about the material, including homework problems. Quizzes will be given most weeks during recitation. The problems will be taken from the suggested homework problems list. Material to be covered on each quiz will be posted on LMS. Students must take quizzes in the section for which they are registered to receive a grade on the quiz.

No make-up quizzes will be given for any reason. To compensate for this, the lowest quiz score for each student will be dropped at the end of the semester. Only students who miss a quiz due to an [excused absence documented by the Student Success office](http://studentlife.rpi.edu/student-experience/excused-absence) may be excused from a quiz.

**Calculator policy:** Calculators will not be allowed on exams or quizzes. Problems will be designed so that this does not create an issue. Occasionally, a calculator may be required to complete a homework problem, but students should not rely on calculators for simple arithmetic or trigonometric calculations.

**Classroom decorum:** Students must be on time for class and should refrain from leaving and reentering the classroom during class. If a student has a legitimate reason for arriving late to class or being excused early from class, then he or she should discuss this with the instructor before class. Phones may not be used during class and should be silent. Computers and tablets may not be used for anything other than taking notes. Students who are unable to courteously comply with these policies may be asked to leave the room.

**Visitors:** Visitors are prohibited during all class meetings. Only students who are registered for the class should be present in the classroom. Students may only attend class meetings at the time and place listed on their schedule on SIS.

**Extra credit:** No extra credit opportunities will be available during the course, so be sure to give your best effort on exams and quizzes throughout the semester. Any requests for extra credit will be ignored.

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Academic integrity:** Students should familiarize themselves with the section on academic integrity in the [Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities](https://info.rpi.edu/sites/default/files/Rensselaer%20Handbook%20of%20Student%20Rights%20&%20Responsibilities%20%C2%BB.pdf) (pp. 16–18). All work submitted on exams and quizzes must be the student’s own. A student may not change an answer on an exam or quiz before submitting it for an appeal. In addition, no calculators, computers, tablets, open books, open notes, phones, headphones, or smartwatches may be used during exams or quizzes. Talking or communicating with other students in any way during exams or quizzes is also prohibited. The minimum academic sanction applied to a student found in violation of the above policies will be a grade of 0 on the assignment. A stronger sanction may be applied if warranted. A report regarding the violation will also be submitted to the Dean of Students Office and will remain on file until graduation. Repeat offenders will fail the course and will be subject to judicial action.
