## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Techniques and applications of integration, polar coordinates, parametric equations, infinite sequences and series, vector functions and curves in space, functions of several variables, and partial derivatives. (from the [Rensselaer Catalog](http://catalog.rpi.edu/))

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Basic symbol manipulation skills pertaining to integration.
- Ability to state and explain basic Calculus definitions and theorems and their applications.
- Ability to describe curves parametrically and apply Calculus concepts and techniques to curves described in this way.
- Ability to express curves via polar equations and to solve Calculus problems in the polar coordinate system.
- Ability to apply Calculus to selected problems in Science, Engineering and Mathematics.
- Ability to use convergence tests to analyze the behavior of infinite series.
- Ability to represent functions using power series and solve problems using these representations.
- Ability to describe lines and planes in space in equation form and solve problems using these representations.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Exams:** The material covered on each exam will be announced on [LMS](https://lms.rpi.edu/) when the date of the exam is near. Exams will be given in person during lecture in SAGE 3303. **Students who receive testing accommodations from the** [**Disability Student Services (DSS)**](https://studenthealth.rpi.edu/list-services/disability-student-services) **office may need to take their exams at a different time due to student, proctor, and room availability.**

**Make-up exams will only be given to students with an** [**excused absence documented by the Student Success office**](https://success.studentlife.rpi.edu/current-students/academic-and-personal-support/requesting-excused-absence) **or, if the absence is due to an athletic competition, documentation from a coach. In either case, the documentation must be received by the instructor before 11:59 PM on the day after the exam.** If the documentation is not received by that time, then the student must provide proof that the documentation was requested before the deadline. If the documentation is not received and no proof is provided that it was requested, then the student may not take the make-up exam and receives a score of 0 on the exam. If a student misses an exam because they overslept, did not know where the exam was, or did not know there was an exam, to give a few examples, the student may not take the make-up exam and will receive a score of 0 on the exam.

**Missing an exam should not be taken lightly.** Thus, students who miss an exam due to an excused absence must make every effort to notify the instructor **before the exam begins** to avoid grade penalties.

- If a student misses an exam due to an excused absence (and documentation is received as described above) and notifies the instructor before the exam begins, then the student may take a make-up exam without penalty.
- If a student misses an exam due to an excused absence (and documentation is received as described above) and notifies the instructor after the exam begins but before 11:59 PM on the day after the exam, then the student may take a make-up exam and a 30% penalty will be applied to the make-up exam score.
- If a student misses an exam due to an excused absence and fails to notify the instructor before 11:59 PM on the day after the exam, then the student may not take the make-up exam and receives a score of 0 on the exam.
- If a student misses an exam and does not have an excused absence, then the student may not take the make-up exam and receives a score of 0 on the exam.

It is the sole responsibility of the student to notify the instructor to make arrangements for a make-up exam when an exam is missed due to an excused absence.

**Final exam:** The final exam will be cumulative and will be held on a date and time determined by the [Office of the Registrar](https://info.rpi.edu/registrar/grades/#FinalExamScheduleandConflicts). **All students must take the final exam at the time scheduled by the Office of the Registrar. Exceptions will only be made if there is a scheduling conflict as defined** [**here**](https://info.rpi.edu/registrar/grades/#FinalExamScheduleandConflicts)**, or if a student is excused by the Student Success office.** Please note that the Student Success office will only issue an excuse for final exams for extreme circumstances, such as hospitalization, and not for travel. (Read more [here](https://success.studentlife.rpi.edu/current-students/academic-and-personal-support/missing-final-exam).) Thus, students should not make end-of-semester travel arrangements until the final exam schedule is posted, or should only schedule travel for the time period after the last day of final exams, which is Wednesday, December 20.

**Homework:** Achieve homework assignments will be due once or twice weekly on Mondays and Thursdays at 11:59 PM and will be graded automatically by the system. Students may submit these assignments late up to two days after the original deadline, but a penalty will be applied to the score. A 10% penalty will apply to assignments submitted the day after the deadline and a 20% penalty will apply to assignments submitted two days after the deadline. These extensions are granted by Achieve automatically to all students and do not need to be requested. **No other due date extensions will be granted for any reason.** To further compensate for this, several homework assignment scores will be dropped at the end of the semester.

Additional problems will be assigned from the textbook and will be posted on [LMS](http://lms.rpi.edu/). Though these problems will not be collected nor graded, they should not be viewed as optional. Success in the course depends on successful completion of all homework, both Achieve homework and textbook homework. Exam and quiz problems are frequently chosen from homework assignments and lecture examples. Full solutions to odd-numbered textbook problems can be found on Achieve. When logged in to our Achieve course, view content by assignments and scroll to the bottom of the page. The student solutions manual for our textbook may be found below No Due Dates. Tip: download and save these files for use in future courses!

**Recitation Quizzes:** Quizzes will be given most weeks during recitations. Material to be covered on each quiz will be posted on [LMS](http://lms.rpi.edu/). Students must take quizzes in their assigned sections in order to receive credit for the quiz.

**No make-up quizzes will be given for any reason.** To compensate for this, the lowest two quiz scores for each student will be dropped at the end of the semester. Only students who miss a quiz due to an [excused absence documented by the Student Success office](https://success.studentlife.rpi.edu/current-students/academic-and-personal-goals/requesting-excused-absence) or, if the absence is due to an athletic competition, by a coach, may be excused from a quiz. The student does not need to contact the instructor or TA, but the documentation must be received before the last day of classes in order for the quiz grade to be excused.

### Other Course Policies:

**Disability Student Services (DSS):** Students with a memorandum from the [Disability Student Services (DSS)](http://studenthealth.rpi.edu/disabilityservices) office identifying accommodations for exams or quizzes must present the memorandum to the instructor **by Monday, September 11**. Providing the accommodations requires advance planning. Students will not be granted accommodations unless a memorandum from DSS has been received.

**Classroom Decorum:** Please adhere to the following during meetings for this course.

- Students must be on time for class and should refrain from leaving and reentering the classroom during class. If a student has a legitimate reason for arriving late to class or being excused early from class, then they should discuss this with the instructor before class.
- Phones may not be used during class and should be silent while the instructor is lecturing.
- Computers and tablets may not be used for anything other than taking notes while the instructor is lecturing.
- Visitors are prohibited during all class meetings. Only students who are registered for the class should be present in the classroom.

Students who are unable to courteously comply with these policies may be asked to leave the room.

**If you do not plan on following these rules, then please do not come to class. Note the attendance policy below.**

**Calculator Policy:** Calculators will not be allowed on exams or quizzes. Problems will be designed so that this does not create an issue. Occasionally, a calculator may be required to complete homework problems, but students should not rely on calculators for simple arithmetic or trigonometric calculations.

**Regrade Requests:** If upon reviewing the solutions for an exam a student feels that their work was graded incorrectly, then the student may submit a regrade request to the instructor via Gradescope. Specific dates will be given for regrade request periods for exams at the time that scores are released. Regrade requests for quizzes will also be available on Gradescope for one week after the quiz scores are released or until the last day of classes, whichever occurs first. Please note that regrade requests will not be available for the final exam.

**Extra Credit:** No extra credit opportunities will be available during the course, so be sure to give your best effort on exams, quizzes, and homework assignments throughout the semester.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Academic Integrity:** Students should familiarize themselves with the section on academic integrity in the [Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities](https://rpi.app.box.com/s/itdt9diqmjkfj2luqhe8irxf99f9pntu) (pp. 16–18). All work submitted on exams and quizzes must be the student’s own. In addition, no calculators, computers, tablets, open books, open notes, formula sheets, phones, headphones, or smartwatches may be worn or used during exams or quizzes. Talking or communicating with other students in any way during exams or quizzes is also prohibited. Students may discuss homework with other students, tutors, the TA, or instructor, but the work submitted must be the student’s own.

The minimum academic sanction applied to a student found in violation of the above policies will be a grade of zero on the assignment. This zero will not be able to be dropped at the end of the semester. A stronger sanction may be applied if warranted. A report regarding the violation will also be submitted to the Dean of Students Office and will remain on file until graduation. Repeat offenders will fail the course and will be subject to judicial action.
