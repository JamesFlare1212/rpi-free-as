## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**Professional Development: Group Dynamics MISSION STATEMENT**

The purpose of Professional Development: Group Dynamics is to provide students with an introduction to a simulated professional environment where they can be exposed to the body of knowledge relating to effective teams. Coursework consists primarily of skills-based learning designed to foster effective teamwork abilities. Coursework and assignments are designed for students to gain topical knowledge, analyze and apply basic concepts, and expand written and oral communication skills.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

**LEARNING OUTCOMES**

- At the end of the course, students will be able to demonstrate understanding of at least 1 team development model by explaining in presentations and how it applies to team experiences.
- At the end of the course, students will be able to identify different personality preferences and explain in consultations, discussions, written assignments and a semester-end presentation how they impacted their team interactions.
- At the end of the course, students will be able to utilize interpersonal skills such as conflict management, giving effective feedback, and negotiation in their interactions with others and in course assignments.
- Experience content and activities that promote diversity, equity, and inclusion awareness for career success.

**ABET CRITERIA 3 OUTCOMES**

3.3 – an ability to communicate effectively with a range of audiences

3.5 - An ability to function effectively on a team whose members together provide leadership, create collaborative and inclusive environment, establish goals, plan tasks, and meet objectives
