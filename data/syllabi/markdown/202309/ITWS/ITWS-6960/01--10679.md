## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course addresses the growing need for cybersecurity professionals who understand the Cloud and the specific needs and requirements for securing the Cloud. Students will learn the core theories behind Cloud security, including but not limited to those codified by the international cybersecurity industry through the ISC2 CCSP Common Body of Knowledge: 1. Cloud Concepts, Architecture, and Design; 2. Cloud Data Security; 3. Cloud Platform and Infrastructure Security; 4. Cloud Application Security; 5. Cloud Security Operations; 6. Legal, Risk, and Compliance.

Students will apply theory with hands-on access to a simulated enterprise cloud environment with industry-standard security tools, in which gamified challenges will test student knowledge and execution of real-world solutions to Cloud security challenges and incidents. Students will develop, as a class, Cloud Security awareness training for the RPI community. Students will also conduct independent research into a Cloud Security topic of their choice to demonstrate their individual emerging specialty within Cloud Security.

Some baseline cybersecurity knowledge, such as what would be gained from ITWS 4370 Information Systems Security, would be beneficial but is not required to take this course.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course covers foundational models and theories for information security specifically as it relates to the Cloud. It covers the primary categories of Cloud Security theories identified by security practitioners around the world as codified in the ISC2 CCSP CBK. Students will put those theories into practice through the use of industry-standard tools in a simulated enterprise cloud environment, using gamification challenges to hone their thinking and execution of real-world solutions to Cloud security incidents. Students will develop, as a class, Cloud Security awareness training for the RPI community. Students will engage in independent research to begin specializing in the subfield of their choice. Students will develop a solid foundation upon which to build a Cloud-aware information security career in the subfield of their choosing.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will demonstrate knowledge of the theoretical foundations of information security as it applies to the Cloud and Cloud computing.
- Students will synthesize knowledge of a broad range of issues that must be addressed to design and build secure systems in the Cloud.
- Students will demonstrate practical knowledge of industry-standard tools and techniques to secure Cloud computer resources and enterprise networks.
- Students will create and secure Cloud resources.
- Students will be able to investigate and present topics in Cloud-based information systems security at a level so that other students obtain an understanding of the key issues.
- (Students in 6960 only) Students will lecture and develop educational activities to train others on Cloud Security.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

(4960%/6960%)  
CTFs -- Students will play CTF challenges throughout the semester, culminating in participating in the National Cyber League (NCL), a national university-level CTF tournament (15%/15%)

Paper & Presentation -- There will be a presentation in which you (individually or in a small group of no more than 3 people) explore a Cloud security topic of your choosing (20%/15%). It will be accompanied by a research paper (individually, even if presenting with a group) that demonstrates emerging expertise in that topic above and beyond what is covered in class lectures (25%/20%)

Cloud Security Awareness Training -- The class will develop a Cloud Security awareness training program suitable for the RPI community (20%/15%)

Attendance/Participation – Students will engage with the global cloud security story through weekly current events readings. Students will engage with hands-on practical training on a simulated enterprise network and using industry-standard Cloud security tools (20%/15%)

Lecture – Students in 6960 will participate in a “flipped classroom” approach in which they are responsible for one class meeting-length lecture and associated learning activities (0%/20%)
