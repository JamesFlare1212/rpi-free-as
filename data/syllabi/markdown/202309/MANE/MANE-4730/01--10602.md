## Course Goals

### Goals:

Objectives of Course  
Introduction to heat transfer including conduction, external and internal convection, and radiation. Emphasis is on applications of heat transfer to a wide variety of engineering systems including HVAC, automotive design, materials processing etc.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- to select simplifying assumptions in analyzing a complex thermo-fluids system.
- to analyze thermo-fluids systems by applying conservation of energy.
- to analyze heat transfer in thermo-fluid systems by identifying heat transfer convection (forced/natural, internal/external), conduction (transient and steady-state) and radiation using the appropriate equations to evaluate them.
- to analyze the performance of heat exchangers and design heat exchangers given a set of specifications.

• In order to pass the class each student must turn in the class project. Not turning in the

• The project comes with both positive and negative grade modifiers as follows:  
The project consists of designing of a finned heat sink able to decrease the temperature of a small  
heater of 25 mmx 50 mm area from 45C to 35C under natural convection (no fans). The heat sink  
must de designed using Ice9™ Flex TPU from TCPoly (https://tcpoly.com/) so it can be 3D printed. A

overdesigned nor underdesigned) and the approach is entirely correct and the project is completed  
and turned in by November 2nd 2023.  
One grade modifier (i.e. the grade increases by one level, for example from C to C+) is applied if  
the original heat sink is incorrect/ does not meet specification, but the team  
addresses the weakness and returns a correct design that also meets specification (i.e. it is not  
overdesigned nor underdesigned) by November 27th, 2023. Should the revised project design continue  
not to meet design requirements and is riddled with flaws, up to two negative grade modifiers will  
be applied (i.e., from C to C-, or from C to D+).
