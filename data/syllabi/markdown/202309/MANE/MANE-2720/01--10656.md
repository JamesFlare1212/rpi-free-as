## Course Goals

### Goals:

- Develop a thorough understanding of the fundamentals of fluid mechanics of incompressible flows.
- Demonstrate knowledge of the solution of fundamental problems in fluid mechanics and the underlying physical interpretation.
- Understand and be able to use tools to solve simple problems in fluid mechanics.
- Develop basic knowledge of turbulence and turbulent flows.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

No Course Learning Outcomes are available for this course.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Student Learning Outcomes**

1. Students should understand basic conservation laws of Fluid Mechanics.
2. Students should be familiar with control volume analysis and flow applications.
3. Students should understand the kinematics of fluid motion.
4. Students should understand the concept of a Newtonian fluid and viscosity.
5. Students should be comfortable with the Navier-Stokes Equations.
6. Students should be able to formulate and solve simple incompressible viscous flow problems.
7. Students should be familiar with boundary layer flows.
8. Students should have an introduction to turbulent flows.
