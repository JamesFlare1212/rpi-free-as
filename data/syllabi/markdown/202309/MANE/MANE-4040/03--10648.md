## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Laboratory experience to complement Elements of Mechanical Design (MANE-4030). Includes aspects of dimensional measuring and tolerancing; Stress and strain measurement in tension and bending; Contact mechanics; Sliding friction and wear characterization; Fasteners; Shafts and gears; Reverse engineering of a mechanical assembly. This course is 2 credit-hours.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

No new technical text required specifically for this class. It is assumed that each student already has access to current EMD text (J. A. Collins, H. R. Busby & G. H. ## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Develop hands-on abilities to conduct mechanical engineering system testing
- Predict behavior prior to experimentation so that hypotheses may be tested and identify sources of discrepancy
- Critically evaluate multiple means of measurement and their discrepancies considering sources of error
- Consider the design of experiments and how to modify the experimental design to overcome limitations
- Learn about the role of experimentation and analysis in the design process

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Learning outcomes will be evaluated through a series of assessments divided into:

- Lab reports (7): Describe experiences, data and results collected throughout each of the experiments realized during the course.
- Homework assignments (7): Preparatory exercises to ensure students are familiar with the concepts to be verified through experimentation.
- Quizzes (3): Individual component of the grade, designed to test knowledge retention of each student.

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Student-instructor relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that instructors have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and instructors must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts, which violate this trust , undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities defines various forms or academic dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all homework assignments that are turned ion must represent the student’s own work, while all lab reports that are turned in must represent the team’s own group work. In cases where help was received, by the individual student on a homework assignment or the team on a lab report, a notation on the submission should detail this collaboration. Quizzes are purely individual efforts, and collaboration of any form will results in a zero grade on the quiz for all students involved. If any section of a lab report is found to be plagiarized from a prior semester, the entire lab group receives a zero grade on that lab report. If you have any questions concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification.
