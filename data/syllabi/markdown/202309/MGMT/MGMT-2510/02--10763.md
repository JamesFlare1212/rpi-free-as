## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An introduction to the data driven decision making. Through hands-on exercises students will develop competencies in working with data through spreadsheets applications and relational databases. Topics include foundational concepts in business process modeling, database design, and dashboard creation.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Define the role of data in an organization.
- Learn how to gather requirements.
- Analyze a business problem and create process model.
- Choose between competing requirements based on scope and budget.
- Create a data model from a process model.
- Create an entity-relationship model from a data model.
- Create a relational database from an entity-relationship diagram.
- Extract data and create reports from the relational database.
- Identify management reporting needs and build a Data Warehouse to satisfy those needs.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Exam 1: Business Process Model Fundamentals

Percent of course grade: 20% CLO 1-4

Students will be given a business process and will be required to build a Business Process diagram and an Entity-Relationship diagram.

Exam 2: Operational and Dimensional Model Fundamentals

Percent of course grade: 20% CLO 5-7

Students will use Access to write queries against a pre-built database.

Exam 3: Excel Fundamentals

Percent of course grade: 20% CLO 8 and 9

Students will be tested on their mastery of Excel fundamentals through a series of problems.

Participation in classroom activities

Percent of course grade: 20%

Contribute! Participate! Engage with group discussions!

Final paper project

Percent of grade: 20% CLO 1 - 9

Your choice: either construct a formal data management plan for a research project or construct a 5-page reflective essay, based on our experiences this semester on whether or not data can help us become more wise. (More details on both later in the semester.)
