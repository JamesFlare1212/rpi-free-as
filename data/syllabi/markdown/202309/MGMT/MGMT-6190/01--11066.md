## Course Goals

### Goals:

The primary course objectives are:

1. Introduce students to basic accounting techniques and methods to understand, interpret, and prepare basic financial data and accounting statements.
2. Provide students with the theoretical background and analytical tools necessary to make sound financial decisions.

Secondary course objectives are:

3. Prepare students for more advanced graduate work in finance and investments.
4. Introduce students to business ethics.
5. Provide students with data analysis tools to analyze and interpret real financial data.
