## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is an introductory course in business statistics. This course will provide participants with the quantitative foundations for statistical methods, which will be used extensively in future management courses. In this course, students will learn the basic concepts and tools in statistics, and then apply the techniques on phenomena of interest. Interactive lectures will be supplemented with videos, homework exercise, quizzes and exams, but the chief goal is competence in basic statistical techniques and an interest in pursuing more advanced business statistics.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate and understand the basic concepts and tools in business statistics.
- Distinguish between different statistical tests and identify the usage of different tests
- Conduct statistical tests of hypotheses and infer conclusions.
- Use software for statistical analysis and apply statistical methods in a real business environment.
- Discuss the ethical considerations of data analysis.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

- **Attendance, Preparation, and Participation****:** Students are expected to attend all lectures, come prepared by having done the assigned readings, and participate during lectures. To help with this, I am asking that you fill out a self-assessment at the end of every week, letting me know if you have been keeping up with the reading, the assignments, and preparation for the final project.

- **Assignments****:** There will be a number of self-assessment assignments posted online to help you with the material. Students are responsible for turning in completed assignments by the due date. Unless the student has made prior arrangements with the instructor, no late assignments will be accepted. Solutions will be provided immediately after the due dates for students to check their work.

- **Quizzes:** There will be a number of quizzes throughout the semester to evaluate how well you are keeping up with the material. They will be offered via Gradescope, with a link provided in the week folder on the LMS. You are encouraged to reconsider incorrect responses and resubmit.

- **Exams****:** There will be two exams. Each exam will cover material discussed in class up to the exam date. The second exam will focus mainly on material covered after the first exam.

- **Final Project**: Students will be divided into groups of 3-4 to work on final project. More details will be given after the first exam. The written report will include the following:

o 5 pages max of text and a max of 8 pages total of text, figures, tables, and references.

o Sections: an abstract, introduction, a short literature review, methodology, results and implications, conclusion, and list of reference.

- o Clean and well-documented data files (codes and data)
- o A poster presentation
