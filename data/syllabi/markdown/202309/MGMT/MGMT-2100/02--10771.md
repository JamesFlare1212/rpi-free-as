## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate and understand the basic concepts and tools in business statistics.
- Distinguish between different statistical tests and identify the usage of different tests.
- Conduct statistical tests of hypothesis and infer conclusions.
- Use software for statistical analysis and apply statistical methods in a real business environment.
- Discuss the ethical considerations of data analysis.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**1. Attendance, Preparation, and Participation****:** Students are expected to attend all lectures, come prepared by having done the assigned readings, and participate during lectures.

- Valid excused absences require “RPI Student Success” verification.

**One-minute memos:**

As part of the class participation grade and to track attendance, students will be asked to write a one-minute memo to be submitted online at the end of some of the lectures. The purpose of the one-minute memo is to establish a direct line of communication between the students and the instructor. The memo will help the instructor assess the class’ understanding of the main concepts discussed during the lectures, identify unclear points, and address any other concerns.  
I want students to feel that they can be open and honest in these memos, so your grade is not affected by the content of these memos. I will respond to the major questions raised in the memos during the following lecture. Missing a class or failing to submit a memo will result in an absence being recorded.

**2. Assignments****:** There will be a number of self-assessment assignments posted online to help you with the material. Each assignment will be graded based on general effort, and completion. Students are responsible for turning in completed assignments by the due date. Unless the student has made prior arrangements with the instructor, no late assignments will be accepted. Solutions will be provided immediately after the due dates  
**3. In-Class Quizzes****:** There will be surprise in-class quizzes in some of the lectures. The quizzes will take place at the beginning of the lecture and **will be completed on LMS (students should bring their laptops/tablets to class).** These quizzes will be unannounced and will cover material from the previous **two lectures**. The quizzes will be based on the concepts taught in the class. These quizzes are to ensure that you are not falling behind. **The lowest two quiz grades will be dropped**. **Make-up quizzes will NOT be offered.** If you miss a quiz, it will be counted as one of your lowest grades to be dropped.  
**4. Exams****:** There will be two exams. Each exam will cover material discussed in class up to the exam date. The second exam will focus mainly on material covered after the first exam. Tests are individual work; students cannot collaborate in any way. Copying, communicating, or using any materials during an exam is cheating unless it is an open-book exam that will be decided by the instructor.
