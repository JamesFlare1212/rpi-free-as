## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Develop theoretical knowledge and skills related to optimization and stochastic modeling for application to problems in the domain of quantitative finance and risk analysis.
- 2\. Apply variety of mathematical tools, including those from matrix linear algebra and differential and integral calculus, as well as probability and stochastic processes, for analysis of different financial models.
- 3\. Analyze various financial instruments under varying market conditions for valuation or hedging purposes, or when designing financial portfolios for risk management.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Homework: There will be homework assignments assigned in continuation of topics discussed and quantitative exercises done in lectures. These assignments will be a mix of descriptive, mathematical, and quantitative exercises. All homework assignments will be done in a **group of two** that the instructor will create. The homework assignments will be due at specified time on Learning Management System, only one copy for each group. No late homework or e-mail submissions will be accepted.

Discussions with the instructor are strongly encouraged in lecture and/or office hours.

Exams: Two exams will be held in the semester. First exam will be half way in the course and the second exam after two-thirds of the term. The second exam will be cumulative, with emphasis on the recent material.

Research Report: As a final deliverable of the course, a research report will be due on a topic related to applying financial modeling techniques. This report will be prepared by individual effort, along with making a presentation in class. More details will be provided later in the term.

Software: Microsoft Excel, R or Matlab.
