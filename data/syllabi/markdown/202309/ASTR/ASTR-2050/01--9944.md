## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Astronomy for undergraduate students with a background of college mathematics and physics. Topics include: astrophysical concepts, solar system basics, stellar astronomy and the interstellar medium, the Milky Way system, galaxies, quasars, and cosmology.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Foundations of Astrophysics, by Ryden and Peterson

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will be able to use Kepler’s Laws to calculate the properties of planetary and binary star orbits.
- Students will be able to describe the components of the Solar System.
- Students will be able to explain the electromagnetic spectrum, and its importance to the study of astronomy.
- Students will be able to describe and to calculate the relationships between magnitude, distance, color, temperature, and the Hertzprung-Russell Diagram.
- Students will be able to describe the physical processes that govern stellar evolution as a function of initial stellar mass.
- Students will be able to list the types of stars, galaxies, and compact objects (e.g. - pulsars, black holes, and AGN) that have been classified, and describe their characteristics.
- Students will be able to describe the formation and evolution of the Universe.
