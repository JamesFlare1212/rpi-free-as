## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Perform conceptual analyses and design development for lighting design projects.
- Enhance lighting design skills to include development, management and communication of lighting design projects.
- Participate in the analyses of lighting design case studies (field trip).
- Submit written summaries of required research and/or reading material.
- Engage in developing compelling design experiences with light (and color).
- Study various design philosophies and their relevance in our zeitgeist (reading assignments).

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Student assessment will be based upon a review of assignments, exams, and papers submitted; evaluation of the quality and preparation of any in-class presentations; and the degree to which the student participates in class discussions.
