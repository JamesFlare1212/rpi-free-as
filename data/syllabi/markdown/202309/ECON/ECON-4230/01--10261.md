## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The primary goal of this undergraduate course is to study how economics can be utilized to understand the source and solution to environmental problems. Generally speaking, economics provided a set of analytical tools that can be utilized to study any situation in which the scarcity of means requires the balancing of competing incentives. This includes, for instance, important questions in the behavior of non-profit organizations or profit-maximizing firms, government agencies, and consumers. Since most environmental issues are entailed by economic activity (biodiversity loss, overfishing, greenhouse gas emissions, ecosystem degradation), we will study several solution approaches capable of bringing such environmental issues more into balance with human and the needs of the ecosystem itself.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- By the end of this course, the typical student should be able to demonstrate a deep understanding of environmental problems and solution approaches and be able to communicate them effectively. Specifically, the typical student should be able to • Understand the link between the economics and the environment • Understand the causes of environmental issues and policy approaches • Understand how different types of policy interventions could affect environmental and economic conditions • Understand the efficiency, equity, sustainability, and international aspects of environmental issues and policy solutions

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Grading strategy**.

Exams (50% of your grade)

There will be two exams over the course of the semester. All the exams are open note and open book. The midterm exam (exam #1) and final exam (exam #2) are not cumulative and will be in-class. The midterm will be made of the material covered from the beginning of the term until October 19, 2023. The final exam will be made of material covered after October 19, 2023.

Homework assignments (20%)

For each homework assignment, you are required to utilize the tools developed in the course to address environmental related problems. Figuring out **the solution approach** is an important part of the course. You can work in a group of **up to three** people for each homework assignment. You must work with different groups across assignments. Your group must submit a single assignment with all group members’ names on it. Sharing solutions across groups is not allowed. Any non-compliance to this measure will be treated as plagiarism. Homework assignments will be posted on the course website. Late assignments will be accepted but will be penalized following the rule

Grade *=* Assignment Grade (1 - 0.2*D2*), where is the number of days late the assignment is handed in.

Participation (10%)

Individual students should actively participate in class. Specifically, each student must demonstrate that he/she is making efforts to understand the subject, come prepared before each class. The class participation grade will be based on individual contributions to the class discussion and dynamics. Individual students should ask at least one relevant and thoughtful question per week. Each student will receive a single grade for class participation, which will depend on his/her contributions over the entire semester.

Project: Analysis of a current event or policy issue (25% of grade)

Students will select a single event or policy related to environmental economics to explore in depth in a written paper. The paper will include a description of the chosen topic using the language of economics, a synthesis of existing discussion/debate, and your original critical evaluation of the topic. There are three components to this project:

1. A one paragraph summary of your selected topic (due about one third of the way through the semester)
2. A first draft or detailed outline of your project that includes a list of references (due about two thirds of the way through the semester)
3. Your final project (due by the end of the semester: December 08, 2023)
