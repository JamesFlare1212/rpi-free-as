## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course will teach students to apply their structural engineering skills to the analysis and design of bridges. Upon successful completion of this course, students will be prepared for further study or a position as an entry-level bridge engineer.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Design Documentation, History, Loads, Superstructures, Substructures, Maintenance, Special Topics

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Identify common bridge types and components using appropriate terminology
- Compute typical structural loads via design codes
- Analyze and/or design (by hand and via software) basic bridge components
- Explain the role of proper bridge maintenance and documentation

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Students will have the opportunity to demonstrate understanding of the course material through participation in lectures and in-class activities, homework assignments, a midterm exam, and a final project.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Mobile Devices**

Use of (or **ANY** interaction with) a mobile device during an exam without explicit permission of the instructor will be interpreted as the illicit transfer of exam data, will be considered an act of cheating and will be treated as such.

**Mid-Term Exam**

A missed midterm exam will result in a grade of zero *unless permission is granted before the scheduled exam time*. A makeup exam will only be given for serious illness or a family emergency. Such situations *must be documented* through the Office of Student Success (4600 Academy Hall, x 8022, Excused Absence Request Form).

**Collaboration**

Students are *strongly* encouraged to solve the homework problems through collaboration with classmates (after trying the problems themselves); however, each student is responsible for their own understanding and must turn in their own work as described under the homework policy. Collaboration on the exam is strictly prohibited and any student involved will be assigned a grade of zero.  
**Artificial Intelligence (AI) Tools**

Use of AI tools (chatGPT or similar) is permitted; however, submitting the output of such tools for a graded assignment or exam without proper attribution is considered an act of plagiarism, akin to using a published source (journal article, website, etc.) without citation. Such instances will be treated as violations of academic integrity. If these tools are used, students must properly document exactly what content was created by the program and must also be able to demonstrate that the material generated has been cross-checked from a different source/reference.
