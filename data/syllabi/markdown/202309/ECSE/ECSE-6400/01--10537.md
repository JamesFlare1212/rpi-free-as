## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course trains students in basic state-space theory for analysis of linear systems and design of linear state-feedback controllers. Broadly, the course covers (1) state-space models and representations of finite dimensional linear time-invariant systems, (2) controllability & observability, and (3) controller design through pole placement. The course also reviews linear algebra and matrix theoretic concepts that are relevant to the state-space theory, with applications. Some computation tasks will be done with MATLAB.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understand the basic concepts of vector spaces, linear operators, basis, and linear algebra fundamentals.
- Understand the concept of state variables and how to derive them from different linear system representations.
- Know how to solve a system of linear differential equations in state space form.
- Understand the geometric aspects of linear systems including eigenvalues decomposition and singular value decomposition.
- Understand the concepts of controllability and observability of linear systems.
- Demonstrate design of state-space controllers for stabilization and pole placement for linear systems.
- Demonstrate design state-space observers for state estimation of linear systems.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Students are expected to conduct themselves in a professional manner. Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities defines various forms of academic dishonesty, and you should make yourself familiar with these; all forms are violations of the trust between students and teachers. Collaborative discussion on homework assignments is encouraged, but copying is not permitted; all homework assignments must represent the student’s own work. Quizzes are to be done individually; collaboration is not permitted in any form. Submission of any quiz or homework assignment in violation of this policy will result in a grade of zero for that quiz or homework assignment. All re-grade requests must be submitted *within one week of the exam grading*. *No collaboration of any kind or use of unauthorized notes/materials is permitted on in-class exams*. Violation of this policy will result in a zero grade for the exam and may result in an academic malpractice report filed with the Dean. Violation of academic integrity may result in punitive action up to a fail grade in the course in addition to the filing of the malpractice report.
