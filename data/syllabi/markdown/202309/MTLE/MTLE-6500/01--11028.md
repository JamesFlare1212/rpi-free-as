## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This class covers commonly used theoretical and simulation methods at the electronic, atomistic, meso and continuum scales.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

There is no required textbook. All class materials will be in the form of handouts. Reading materials and lecture notes will be uploaded to: https://lms.rpi.edu. Several books are reserved at the library as additional references:

**Recommended but not required:**

1. A.R. Leach, "Molecular Modeling: Principles and Applications", 2nd Ed., Prentice Hall, 2001.

*A detailed description of the techniques employed in molecular modeling and computational chemistry. Assume the reader has only a basic knowledge of the relevant physical and chemical principles.*

2. Frenkel and Smit, "Understanding Molecular Simulation: From Algorithms to Applications", 2nd Ed., Academic Press, 2002.

*Very good background and theory on molecular dynamics (MD), Monte Carlo (MC) and Statistical Mechanics. Applications are mainly on molecular systems.*

3. Allen and Tildesley, "Computer Simulation of Liquids", Clarendon Press, Oxford, 2017. A general introduction to computer simulation.

*Excellent book on Molecular Dynamics Simulation (MD). Explains well the relevant*

*statistical mechanics. A “must” if you will be doing MD. Not much on solids.*

4. Landau and Binder, "A Guide to Monte Carlo Simulations in Statistical Physics", Cambridge University Press, 2014. *Focus on Monte Carlo simulations.*

5. Richard M. Martin, "Electronic Structure: Basic Theory and Practical Methods", Cambridge University Press, 2012. *Focus on first principles calculations.*

## Course Goals

### Goals:

This class provides students with the background and skills needed to:

- Appreciate and understand the use of theory and simulation in materials.
- Be able to read the simulation literature and evaluate it critically.
- Identify problems in materials amenable to simulation, and decide on appropriate theory/simulation strategies to study them.
