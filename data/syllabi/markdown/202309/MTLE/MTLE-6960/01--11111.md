## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Project-based course on the application of state-of-the-art machine learning (ML) methods in materials science and engineering. Discussion topics and project areas include neural-network interatomic potentials for molecular dynamics simulations, materials discovery using high-throughput ML property prediction, and recommendation systems for proposing new materials. Additional project areas can be explored based on the expertise and interests of the participants. This course will assume familiarity with ML methods, data science and Python programming.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

No textbook: journal articles and online software documentation, as appropriate for each project.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Select appropriate machine learning techniques for modeling and predictions in materials research problems.
- Apply state-of-the-art machine learning techniques to research projects in materials science and engineering.
- Communicate research utilizing machine-learning techniques to a scientific audience in the form of journal publications.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Final project report in the form of a manuscript draft suitable for submission to a peer-reviewed journal.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

This course will be graded S/U, with S requiring successful execution of a research plan that culminates in a publication that is suitable to submit to a peer-reviewed journal.

## Course Calendar

### Calendar Details:

Weeks 1-2: Research topic identification and teaming based on interests.  
Weeks 4-10: Carry out research plan  
Weeks 11-15: Draft associated publication
