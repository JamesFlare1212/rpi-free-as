## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course is a grad-level course on mechanical properties of materials. We will cover elastic, plastic, fracture behaviors, sometimes under high temperatures or cyclic loading, of various materials, including crystalline and non-crystalline materials, monolithic, composite, or cellular materials.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Upon successful completion of the course, students will have a detailed understanding of the mechanical properties of materials.
- Upon successful completion of the course, students will have a detailed understanding of the relationship between mechanical properties and nano/micro/macro scale structure.
- Upon successful completion of the course, students will have a detailed understanding of the relationship between mechanical properties and the underlying physical mechanisms.
- Upon successful completion of the course, students will have a detailed understanding of time and temperature dependent behavior in all materials with an emphasis on metals and polymers.
- Upon successful completion of the course, students will be able to use quantitative equations to describe observed mechanical behavior of materials.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Students are assessed through exams, homework, and term project.
