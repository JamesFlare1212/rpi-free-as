## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The design of enclosures engages broader discussions about architecture and its relationship to the social, historical, and technical aspects of contemporary practice. This course provides a foundation in enclosure system design principles, materials, and construction typologies, with the aim of giving students the tools and conﬁdence to design enclosure systems. The course is structured around three system typologies (monolithic, layered, openings), with an emphasis on how these systems respond to performance criteria such as structural loads, air and water inﬁltration, and thermal performance. Students explore applications via individual and group design exercises that address materials, assembly, and performance.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Outcome 1 Foundational Principles: Students will demonstrate a foundational knowledge in the design principles of enclosure systems including monolithic systems, cavity systems and openings through exams. Additional CLOs for Graduate students: In addition to the undergraduate outcomes noted above, graduate students will demonstrate a foundational knowledge in the design principles of emerging and conventional enclosure systems including biogenic systems, monolithic systems, cavity systems and openings.
- Outcome 2 Environmental Principles: Students will demonstrate the capacity to investigate and differentiate various enclosure systems as they relate to enviornmental site conditions through a semester long project. Additional CLOs for Graduate students: In addition to the undergraduate outcomes noted above, graduate students will use their environmental literacy to investigate and critically evaluate enclosure systems as they relate to environmental conditions
- Outcome 3 Documentation and Representation: Students will develop documentation and representational techniques appropriate to the communication of materials, assemblies and the performance of enclosure systems through a series of assignments. Additional CLOs for Graduate students: In addition to the undergraduate outcomes noted above, graduate students will develop and expand on documentation and representational techniques appropriate to the communication of the materials, assemblies and performance of the enclosure systems.

**Semester Assignments 1-5**  
Throughout the semester, students will work in groups to analyze the construction documents of a notable case study building. Individuals within the groups will take responsibility for a single transition detail and the result will be a comprehensive 2D and 3D representation of the full wall section from ground to roof. Students will apply concepts learned in the class to the case study projects and overlay diagrams to demonstrate comprehension of the course material.  
**Midterm and Final Exam**  
Both Midterm and Final exam will be administered through LMS on the date noted on the syllabus. The exams will be based on content from the lecture presentations as well as calculations and methods exercised in the labs. They will consist of true/false, multiple choice and multiple answers.
