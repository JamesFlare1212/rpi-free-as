## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The course provides an understanding of the breadth of the construction Industry and the roles that the various constituents play in large-scale projects. The seminar proceeds by forensic research into prominent building disasters and successes in order to delineate the importance of the interrelationships across the industry.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Texts are not provided as the seminar proceeds by research and reporting rather than reading and recitation.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The student will develop and ability to put together a comprehensive understanding of the building industry from a details and relationships found during the research.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- The student will demonstrate the ability to develop a comprehensive view of the Construction Industry built from a variety of sources over the course of the semester.
- The student will demonstrate the ability to keep organized and annotated records of research.
- The student will demonstrate the abilioty to engage in forensic case study research as a method.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

The seminar proceeds by weekly research and presentation by the student. Each class meetings presentation is worth 5%. The final compilation of the semesters work will be 25%.
