## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Describe, identify and distinguish between different psychological concepts
- Explain psychological concepts to a classmate and/or audience in a way that is understandable to a novice
- Use critical and creative thinking to apply psychological concepts to solve problems in their lives

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

1. **CLO1:** Describe, identify and distinguish between different psychological concepts

Assessed through exams, assignments, and a presentation

2. **CLO2:** Explain psychological concepts to a classmate and/or audience in a way that is understandable to a novice

Assessed through the Pair-Teach assignment and a group presentation

3. **CLO3**: Use critical and creative thinking to apply psychological concepts to solve problems in their lives

Assessed through the Self-Reference Plan and Deliberate Rest assignments

**Re-Grade Request Policy:**

This policy applies to assignments and papers only. If you would like to request a re-grade, you must wait 24 hours after receiving your grade to contact your professor and/or your TA. After the 24 hours, you will have 7 days to submit a request. In your request, you must include why you believe you should have received a different grade; be as specific as possible. Please note that if you request a re-grade and we determine that we are actually too *lenient* with the grade you initially received, we may adjust your grade *downward*. If you are unsatisfied with the TA’s response, you have 7 days to contact the professor and request a re-evaluation.

## Policies

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).
