### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

After successfully completing this course, students will be able to:

- Interpret, compare and critique a variety of forms of contemporary art
- Develop a daily artistic process
- Develop and articulate a personal philosophy toward contemporary art and art- making
- Learn and demonstrate practical skills of the working artist

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Students are expected to commit to the project idea and to develop their work progressively over the course of the semester, both in execution of production and writing. In this first semester students will:

- Develop the conceptual basis for the senior project through research, prototyping, writing and discussion
- Develop and accomplish one’s own disciplined, production process
- Produce and present a Proof of Concept prototype that provides a “snapshot” of the project, demonstrating its technical feasibility, conceptual integrity and progress.
- Write a Project Proposal and implementation plan
- Write an artist statement
- Develop a Research Question and preliminary outline and bibliography for the written thesis
- Develop a “family tree” of artistic influences
- Develop a daily studio practice

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. ### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Academic Dishonesty

*The Rensselaer Handbook* defines various forms of academic dishonesty and procedures for responding to them. Students are responsible for familiarizing themselves with these policies. Students should note that the penalty for plagiarism and other forms of cheating is a grade of “F” for the course.

In this class “cheating” consists of plagiarism or representing the work of others as your own. This can include the deliberate or unintentional omission of acknowledgments or references. Cheating also consists of copying images, audio, video, or any creative assets generated by others and submitting that work as your own.
