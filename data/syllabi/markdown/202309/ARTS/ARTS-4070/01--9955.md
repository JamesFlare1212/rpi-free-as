## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course explores the foundations of 3D animation. It will begin with basic animation hierarchies and controls systems for mechanical and non-living objects, building towards the animation of fully rigged bipedal characters. While most of the course will be spent on animation, we will also construct simple models and rigs to give us a more holistic sense of animation’s relationship to the model and rig.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Explore approaches to both mechanical and character animation.
- Introduce the 12 Principles of Animation.
- Build and set up basic group hierarchies and create user-friendly animation controls for objects and simple characters.
- Learn a variety of traditional and non-traditional workflows within Autodesk Maya.
- Animate idle/walk cycles using pre-rigged characters.
- Engage in group discussions/critiques of animations.
- Become proficient at keyframe animation using the graph editor.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

While deciding who the "author" is in artistic production can be a thorny process, in this class, all work must be made WHOLLY by you. If there are any exceptions to this rule, I will inform you in advance.  
This means:  
\* If a model is required for a project, **you and you alone** will be doing that modeling.  
\* If a rig needs to be constructed, **you and you alone** will be the one constructing it.  
\* If a script needs to be written, **you and you alone** will write that script.  
The first infraction against this policy will result in failure of the project.  
The second infraction will result in failure of the course and a report to the Office of the Dean.
