## Course Goals

### Goals:

Students will advance the foundational elements of drawing such as line, tone, and mark, as well as elements of design - composition and spatial relationships - and expression as it relates to the human form. You will gain a basic knowledge of human anatomy in an effort to understand and record the structure and movements of the human body. You will also gain some understanding of the history of the human body in art history and work to develop personal points of view.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Each time we meet, we will perform in-class exercises and assignments, often using live models as our subject. Their will be 5 main projects required throughout the semester, each of which will require out-of-class work. However, your final project will be worth significantly more that the other projects, the equivalent of 2 assignments due to it’s size being larger. In between assignments we will work on exercises and homework that help develop your understanding of the concepts presented. You will be required to study and identify basics of the human anatomy. There will be several quizzes that you will be required to complete to demonstrate this. Each assignment builds on the previous one, so please do not miss any classes as there is no real way to make up for missed classwork. Be prepared to work as soon as class begins (12:00PM). Students will be graded on their class participation, preparedness, and quality of work. Excessive absences will negatively effect your grade. Sign and date ALL of your work. Please keep all of your work throughout the semester for portfolio reviews at Midterm and Final points in the term.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Knowledge of basic anatomical structure (skeletal, muscular, surface) of the human body • Measuring techniques related to drawing the human body • Line, surface, and light quality • Proportional accuracy • Personal expression through drawing media • Expanding knowledge of Art History

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Assignments and homework will be graded on both your *effort* (how much time and care given to project) and *process* (following instruction, demonstrating understanding of concepts, showing investigation)

Each assignment will be graded with the following criteria: (\_\_\_/100)

Effort toward accuracy (proportion, measuring): 40 points

Effort toward craftsmanship (quality of marks (or) value/sense of light): 30 points

Effort toward composition and project concepts (balance/arrangement of subjects/investigation of ideas): 30 points

Homework will be graded on their timely completion and duration spent on execution. It is important to finish homework on time as it helps prepare and understand concepts and techniques for assignments.

You are required to attend each class. No more than 2 unexcused absences will be permitted. Three unexcused absences will result in a dropped full letter grade (Example: A to B). Tardiness will not be tolerated. Three tardies will count as 1 absence. Tardy returns from class breaks also count as a tardy. ### Other Course Policies:

**Class Preparedness** means you are prepared and ready to work as soon as class begins with the assigned materials at hand. It also means that you have all assignments hung at the beginning of crit with your name clearly written on the front of each drawing.

**Class Participation** means you are actively contributing to discussions and class critiques by showing interest in what others make, asking questions about the assignment goals and outcomes, and using learned vocabulary. It also applies for the explanation and presentation of your own work.
