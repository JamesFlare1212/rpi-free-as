## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Review of Solid State Lighting Systems and Applications. Includes operating principles of LEDs, LED drivers, thermal management, optical systems, color science, sensors and controls. Applications of SSL lighting systems and controls in healthcare, horticulture, emerging IoT technology are discussed with a focus on design, manufacturing and new applications of digitized color tunable lighting systems and new applications using germicidal UVC LEDs

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The students who finish this course in a satisfactory manner will be able to demonstrate:  
**(*i*)** an ability to describe the function of key components of a solid state lighting system;  
**(*ii*)** an ability to describe basic lighting measurements and properties of lighting systems;  
***(iii)*** describe the emerging applications enabled by the integration of lighting, sensors and controls in lighting systems for human use (lighting and displays), horticulture and communications.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

1. Basics of LEDs
   1. LED operation
   2. Manufacturing of LEDs (epitaxy, fab, packaging)
   3. Device level thermal and optical considerations
2. LED Driver Design
   1. AC/DC power supplies (including PFC)
   2. Buck/Boost architectures and operation
   3. Dimming
3. LED Fixture Design
   1. Retrofit LED products (use some current commercial designs
   2. Power LED versus mid-power LEDs: advantages and tradeoffs
   3. Basic fixture optics (reflective, diffusers, waveguides)
4. Use of sensors in lighting systems
   1. Design and use of ambient light sensors
   2. Design and use of motion detectors
   3. System level integration considerations
5. Emerging concepts in SSL systems
   1. Color tuning and control (analog and PWM)
   2. Visible Light Communications (basic concepts)
   3. MicroLED display systems
   4. Applications (UV disinfection, health, horticulture)
   5. Lighting, IoT and AI, Smart Systems Evolution

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Describe the basic physics of LED operation and manufacturing
- Describe thermal management and electronic driver systems for LED lighting system operation
- Understand the basics of illumination measurement, including the principles of color science

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Learning outcomes will be assessed in a series of four quizzes and a final examination. All tests, including the final exam will be open note and will include access to the course slide material. Quizzes and the final exam will focus on the evaluation of analytical thinking capability that demonstrates an understanding of the course material. Examples include:

- Comparing the advantages and disadvantages of two different types of LED designs or LED package designs
- Assessing differences between published LED datasheets from representative vendors
- Evaluating the advantages of different LED circuit design strategies for lighting applications
- Ability to explain the role of the human vision system in lighting system design
- Compare and contrast different lighting system control strategies
