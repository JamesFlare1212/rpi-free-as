## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

-Course is UNDERGRAD. only  
-Topics covered (Introduction to CMOS Design, MOS Amplifiers, Differential Amplifiers, Current Mirrors, Frequency Response, Feedback, MOS Operational Amplifier Configurations, Stability and frequency compensation of MOS Amplifiers)  
-Course include computer simulations using CADENCE  
\- There will be at least 4 assignments based on using circuit simulations (CADENCE). Each assignment will be weighted as a homework assignment.  
\- The software will be used for the project and has a learning curve (need to practice to become comfortable with using it for the project).  
\- If these simulations are not submitted, the student will NOT be allowed to participate in the project. Cadence support will be provided only by the assigned graduate student.  
Project: All students have to design an opamp as their project. (A team of 2 for undergrad. Students, graduate students can either work alone or team up with an undergrad.)

## Course Goals

### Goals:

•To learn design techniques of analog integrated circuits in standard CMOS technology.  
•Apply analog IC design techniques by performing the electrical design and simulation of an operational amplifier OPAMP or analog circuit of similar complexity in an industrial environment (Cadence).

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understand the operating regions of NMOS and PMOS field effect transistors
- Analyze and design single stage and differential CMOS amplifiers
- Analyze CMOS current mirrors
- Perform frequency analysis of an electronic circuit
- Design simple, telescopic and cascode integrated CMOS operational amplifiers
