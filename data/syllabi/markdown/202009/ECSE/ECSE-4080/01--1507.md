## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The application of power semiconductor devices to the efficient conversion of electrical energy. Circuit analysis, signal analysis, and energy concepts are integrated to develop steady-state and dynamic models of generic power converters. Specific topics include AC/DC conversion, DC/DC conversion, DC/AC conversion, and AC/AC conversion. These generic converters are applied as controlled rectifiers, switching power supplies, motor drives, HVDC transmission, induction heating, and others. Ancillary circuits needed for the proper operation and control of power semiconductor devices are also discussed.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Introduction and Review of Circuit Concepts and Analysis Techniques; Half-Wave Rectifiers, Line Commutation, and Distortion; Polyphase Rectifiers; Phase-Controlled Converters; Basic DC-DC Converter Circuits and Operation; Practical Aspects of DC-DC Converters; Discontinuous Conduction Mode; Transformer-Isolated DC-DC Converters; Dynamic Modeling and Control; Basic Principle and Circuits of DC-AC Inversion; Pulse-Width Modulation; Single-Phase PWM Inverters; Three-Phase PWM Inverters.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- An ability to determine the steady-state operation of circuits containing switches and energy stor-age elements (capacitors and inductors)
- An ability to determine and understand the steady-state operation and characteristics of single-phase rectifiers
- An ability to determine and understand the steady-state operation and characteristics of three-phase rectifiers
- An ability to analyze the operation of dc-dc (isolated and non-isolated) converters in continuous and discontinuous conduction mode
- An understanding of the practical issues related to dc-dc converters such as switching ripple, effi-ciency, device stresses, and control
- An understanding of the principles and applications of dc-ac inverters and their control
