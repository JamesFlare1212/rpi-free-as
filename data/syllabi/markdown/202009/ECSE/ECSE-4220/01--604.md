## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • To introduce the basic concepts and techniques in CMOS circuit design.
- • To present the critical VLSI design issues in the very deep sub-micrometer era (speed, power consumption, layout configuration, & clocking).
- • To provide experience designing integrated circuits using commercial CAD Tools (CADENCE).
- To provide the ability to analyze and compare different circuit structures for latches and flip-flops
- To provide the ability to draw the compact layout for CMOS circuits

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Assessment of student progress in the course objectives will be performed through a variety of mechanisms. Homework will be assigned for each major topic in the class. Hands-on projects will be assigned to asses student ability to use commercial IC layout tools. Finally, two comprehensive exams will be given during the semester; one midterm (on Thursday October 15) and one final (during finals week).

Any disabilities, including time zone difficulties, should be brought to the instructor’s attention, the sooner the better. Final grades will be curved so that around \~20% of the class will receive A, \~25% of the class will receive A-, and \~50% of the class will receive B+/B/B-/…. Class attendance and class participation are not graded. However, at the end of the semester, if a student is on a borderline between grades, these factors will be considered when deciding the final grade.
