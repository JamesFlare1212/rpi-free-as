## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Modeling of power system components including HVDC systems, flexible AC transmission systems, and wind turbines. Analysis and control techniques such as reactive power control, coherency, and model reduction. Synchrophasor technology – measurement, communication, and control.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

A student will be able to understand the characteristics and constraints of several power system components such as HVDC, FACTS controllers, and wind turbine generators, which are not covered in the Computer Methods for Electrical Power Engineering course. A student will learn about methods to analyze large scale power systems. In addition, the new technology of synchrophasor measurements and methods for analyzing and using the synchrophasor data to improve the reliable operation of large power systems will be presented. The course materials are useful for understanding the operation of the future power grid.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Load and generator models, subsynchronous resonance

HVDC systems: two-terminal and multi-terminal systems

FACTS controllers; SVC, TCSC, STATCOM, SSSC, UPFC, and IPFC

Damping control design

Wind turbine models – Types 1, 2, 3, and 4

Coherency and model reduction

Synchrophasor measurements – phasor calculation and phasor data applications

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- A student who finishes the course in a satisfactory manner will be able to: (1) calculate HVDC operation conditions and control modes,
- (2) control voltage and power flow using FACTS controllers,
- (3) develop wind turbine models suitable for stability analysis,
- (4) find areas of machines that are coherent with respect to Interarea modes,
- (5) understand the basics of synchrophasor measurements and extract appropriate information from the data.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Homework problems will be assigned after each topics to assist students to familiarize with the key concepts. These homework problems may consist of computation using calculators or MATLAB, and simulating and observing power system behavior. Control designs using specific apparatus for damping of power system swings will be assigned as computer projects as such design problems are more involved.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:
