### Additional Information for Course Information Section:

This course is designed to provide an overview of the art of effective story development. Students will come away with a heightened awareness of the structures and principles that master storytellers apply to their craft in games and other popular media. It is intended for all GSAS majors and other interested students, as well as the prerequisite for the GSAS concentration in Writing.

## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Stories and narrative elements continue to play an increasingly important role in the creative and financial success of games of nearly all types, from AAA next-gen blockbusters to innovative indie titles to ubiquitous mobile games. Nearly every member of a game development team ultimately contributes to the implementation of that game’s narrative, and so becomes – to one degree or another – a storyteller. But without a shared language of storytelling spoken by all team members, an unfocused narrative result is almost inevitable.

This course is designed to provide that common frame of reference, so that every game developer – whether a narrative expert or not – can do his or her part to drive the game story in a positive direction. Through a combination of lectures, readings, case studies, analytical exercises and storytelling problem-solving, students will gain a better understanding of what it can take to bring a game story to vibrant life.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

**Learning Objectives:**

- Learn a common language of storytelling that all game developers should know
- Become a better storyteller by understanding core principles of narrative development
- Provide a framework for developing, evaluating and improving game characters
- Improve analytic skills with regard to stories in games and other media
- Learn the practices and major challenges of writers and narrative designers who create characters and stories in the games industry
- Explore and analyze key elements of narrative in various types of games
- Develop critical thinking skills that can increase your abilities as a professional communicator
