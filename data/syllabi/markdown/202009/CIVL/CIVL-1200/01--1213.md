## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An introduction to the elements of computer aided design for Civil and Environmental Engineers using AutoCAD. Students will be introduced to basic AutoCAD drafting techniques.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

None required

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Students who successfully complete this course will:  
1\. Develop an understanding and knowledge of the use of AutoCAD in the engineering  
profession.  
2\. Understand basic drafting techniques and drafting terminology in the engineering  
profession.  
3\. Learn how to develop basic, two-dimensional engineering drawings using AutoCAD.  
4\. Apply appropriate industrial tools and practices to the preparation of drawings and detail.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

1\. Geometric constructions- with finding and retrieval of files  
2- Text Styles  
3- Orthographic projections with sketching as necessary  
4 - Shop drawings with dimensioning  
5- Intersection and development- with building models  
6- Building section with elevations, sections, AutoCAD blocks  
7- Site plan development  
8- Surveyor units and road development
