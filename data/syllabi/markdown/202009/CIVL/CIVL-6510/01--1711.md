## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The behavior of soil is extremely variable because it is affected by various factors such as compositions, stress state and environmental conditions. In other words, no soil behaves the same way. To account for such behavior, it is necessary to understand how soil behavior alters, how to determine its properties, how to model its behavior and how to provide reliable prediction. This course aims to achieve the aforementioned four points through acquisition of the fundamental knowledge of soil behavior and its application to geotechnical engineering problem.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course is designed to achieve the following skills:  
• Understanding fundamentals of soils that govern their behavior.  
• Identifying the conditions that alter soil behavior.  
• Determination of soil properties and model parameters.  
• Solving simple problems analytically.  
• Application of soil mechanics in the framework of Civil Engineering design.  
• Organizing and delivering effective written and graphical communications.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Chapter 1: Introduction  
Chapter 2: Soil state  
Chapter 3: Soil strength  
Chapter 4: Evaluation of soil properties  
Chapter 5: Elastoplastic constitutive models  
Chapter 6: Calculation of ground settlement caused by tunneling  
Chapter 7: Concluding remarks
