## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Catalog Description: *Subsurface investigation. The application of the principles of soil mechanics to the design of footings, retaining walls, pile foundations, bulkheads, cofferdams, bridge piers and abutments, and underpinnings.*

**Topics covered:** Subsurface investigations, interpret boring logs, shallow spread foundations, eccentric loads on shallow foundations, settlement, ground improvement, underpinning, axial pile capacity, lateral loading of piles, dynamic pile driving formulas, wave equation analysis of piles, pile load tests, drilled shafts, analysis of cantilever retaining walls, cantilever sheetpiling, construction vibrations.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

## Course Goals

### Goals:

**GOALS:**

1. Understand, apply, and communicate basic foundation engineering concepts
2. Understand, apply, and communicate basic design methods, with focus on practical applications

## Course Content

### Content Details:

- Design/Plan Subsurface Investigations
- Interpret Boring Logs
- Develop Idealized Subsurface Models
- Analyze & Design Shallow Foundations
- Analyze & Design Deep Foundations
- Understand Ground Improvement Concepts
- Analyze & Design Retaining Walls
