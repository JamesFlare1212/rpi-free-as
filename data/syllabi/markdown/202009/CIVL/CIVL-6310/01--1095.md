## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This graduate level class deals with the advanced design concepts of reinforced concrete structures including more fundamental understanding of the basics behind the undergraduate concrete design course CIVL 4080 - Concrete Design. Then, advanced design topics are taught to cover: Slender Columns, Biaxial Bending, Deflections, Torsion, Two Way Slabs, Stairs, Seismic Design, Analysis of concrete structures using SAP2000. The class includes both theoretical design with hand calculations and analysis as well as a design project of a medium rise building using SAP2000

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

“Structural Concrete Theory and Design”, 7th Edition, M. Nadim Hassoun, Akthem Al-Manaseer

## Course Goals

### Goals:

After finishing this course, the students will understand the following  
1 - Scientific principals behind the code design formula and how code design formula are developed  
2 - Limitations in codes and how to apply them to the design of structures  
3 - Design procedures for different elements in medium and high rise buildings with understanding of seismic design requirements for concrete structures  
4 - Modeling assumptions for concrete structures using computer analysis software

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- To learn about advanced concrete structures and understand the basic design principals of these structures
- To improve critical thinking and problem solving capabilities in regards to the optimization of time, cost and safety of designed structures
- To improve student time planning and management towards achieving desired outcomes
- To gain exposure to practical design procedures and software with understanding of code requirements, ethics and limitations

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

The following assessment methods are used, for each method, the CLOs assessed are listed in parentheses  
1 - Homework: Homework will test the students mastering of the procedural application of element design steps. Homework grading is meant to provide students with feedback regarding their mistakes (S582, S584)  
2 - In class problems: These problems are short, focused and ensure the understanding of critical concepts being delivered during class (S582)  
3 - Mid0term exams: Exams are to test the students broader understanding of the subject matter and practical design challenges (S582, S583, S584)  
4 - SAP2000 design project: The project is fundamental to practical life of a civil engineer working in design. Students will perform individual designs with full analysis of a real structure and will produce. simple drawings and design calculation reports (S582, S583, S584, S585)
