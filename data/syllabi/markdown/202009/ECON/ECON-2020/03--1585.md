## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Attention is directed primarily to variations in the aggregate volume of output, income, and employment. Cyclical fluctuations and long-term economic trends are examined and the interrelations of business and government policies are analyzed. The applicability of economic theory to the problems of business forecasting is discussed.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

“Macroeconomics” by R. Dornbusch, S. Fischer and R. Startz. Irwin-McGraw Hill, 13th Edition.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- demonstrate an understanding of major macroeconomic events and explain several macroeconomic facts using standard economic models.
- use social science understanding to evaluate various fiscal and monetary macroeconomic policies, putting similar emphasis on both policies that promote long-term economic growth and the ones related to the management of the economy in the short-term.
- demonstrate knowledge of basic historical facts about U.S. macroeconomic performance using the economic data that is commonly analyzed.

make-up quizzes (NO EXCEPTION). For the exams, in case you can’t make an exam as scheduled due to  
health or family issues (which could typically be considered as acceptable excuses and are at the discretion of the  
instructor to decide on a case-by-case basis), you should let me know in advance to get it approved. I will then  
make arrangements for you to take the exam early, depending upon the circumstances of your absence.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Academic Dishonesty  
As a future financial professional, your most valuable asset will be your personal integrity. Cases of academic  
dishonesty are viewed as a serious violation of the University’s student code of conduct. Examples of academic  
dishonesty include, but are not limited to:  
• copying or sharing answers on graded homework assignments  
• doing assignments for other students  
Academic dishonesty will not be tolerated and penalties can include canceling a student's enrollment without a  
grade or receiving an F for the course. While problems of academic dishonesty rarely come up, feel free to bring  
them to my attention, and I will work with you to address them appropriately.
