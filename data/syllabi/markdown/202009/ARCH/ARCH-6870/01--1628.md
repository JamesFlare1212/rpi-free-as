## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

***The Sonics Research Lab is completely research based***. The first part (the Fall semester) will focus on hands-on research tools and techniques. First, we will prepare basics of digital signal processing, develop understanding of measurement equipment and analysis methods for sound. We will examine the ISO standards of room-acoustic measurements, develop our research goals and gather a specific set of data for our research. We will also prepare the students with the fundamental knowledge for the geometrical acoustics modeling (on which CATT Acoustic and/or EASE are based). Then the students and professors will visit a number of performance venues (e.g. EMPAC Concert Hall, and/ or other venues) and perform the room-acoustic measurements. Students will then work on analyzing and interpreting the research results. Practice measurements will be scheduled in the gallery or somewhere on campus before trips to performance halls.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

After successful accomplishment of this course work the students are able to analyze / calculate basic acoustical indices obtained from room-acoustical measurements using both commercially available tools and the MATLAB-codes written by themselves. The students will demonstrate ability to conduct a number of fundamental acoustical measurements, such as sound-pressure level measurements, computer-aided physical-acoustics measurements, architectural-acoustics measurements, particularly binaural and spatial room-acoustics measurements. The students will demonstrate ability to conduct hall measurements on their own, to calculate/analyze most of acoustics/ room-acoustics parameters required in the ISO standards.
