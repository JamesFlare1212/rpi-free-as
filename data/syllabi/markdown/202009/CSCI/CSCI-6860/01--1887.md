## Course Content

### Content Details:

Contents: Formulation, analysis, and implementation of the following methods:

- ⋄ Finite element methods for elliptic, and possibly also for parabolic equations
- ⋄ Discontinuous Galerkin methods for elliptic equations, convection-diffusion equations, and advection/transport equations

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- To understand the mathematics behinds classical finite element methods and discontinuous Galerkin methods: formulations, matrices assembly for implementation, discrete spaces, approximation theory, numerical stability, error estimates
- To carry out standard mathematical/numerical analysis and derivations
- To implement and to evaluate numerical methods for one- and two-dimensional problems

## Policies

### Other Course Policies:

Collaboration: You are encouraged to discuss the problems with others and to seek assistance from online or library references. However, the work you turn in must be written by yourself and represents your own independent understanding on the subjects. If your work involves a teammate, or significant help from other people, reference books or papers, explicitly state this in your report to avoid penalty in grade.

Late policy: No late homework is accepted. Under very special circumstances, approval might be granted for extension. Any request for extension needs to be made at least 24 hours before the deadline. Regardless of the reason, any granted extension, as long as it is not for the entire class, will result in a 10% reduction of the grade per day.

Attendance Policy: Attendance is expected.
