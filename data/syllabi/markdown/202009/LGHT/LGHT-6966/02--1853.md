## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The Perspectives in BSE & ALZ course is lecture-based and designed to expose graduate students to overviews of the fundamental science and engineering principles inherent in research conducted by NIH training program faculty. Each training faculty member as well as invited individuals from industry partners describe their work as a case-study within the context of the broader field of biotechnology; however, they focus on the key fundamental underpinnings of their specific research and its impact on biotechnology. Furthermore, the lecturers describe the critical need for interdisciplinary research both in their own area and in related areas.

### Goals:

2\. Provide a strong foundation in experimental design with an emphasis on rigor and reproducibility through review of journal publication and training faculty research.
