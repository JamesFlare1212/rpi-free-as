## Course Goals

### Goals:

- Demonstrate how lighting design can be used to effectively create emotional impression
- Lecture and facilitate discussion about the lighting design process
- Lecture and facilitate discussion about importance of client in effective lighting design
- Facilitate mockups, physical models, computer models, and field trips to analyze lighting needs and techniques
- Facilitate development and discussion of student lighting designs

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will develop their own unique lighting designs.
- Students will establish lighting goals based upon client needs, intended emotional impressions.
- Students will present appropriate verbal, written, and visual presentations required to convey the lighting design concept

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Students will present their designs for critique by classmates, visiting lighting professionals, and the instructor.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Lighting Designs (70%), Homeworks (20%), Participation (10%)
