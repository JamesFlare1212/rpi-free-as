## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

LANG 1410 is the first half of a yearlong beginning level course in Modern Mandarin Chinese. This course is for students who have ***NO*** or ***LIMITED*** prior knowledge of the language.Insomecases,students who have had some Chinese courses, study abroad experience,or heritage background may take this course with the instructor’s diagnosis. Based on the pre-course assessment,students may be required to do extra assignments for evaluation.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

In the course of the semester, students are engaged in communicative tasks inside and outside of the classroom. We will begin by introducing the Chinese language in terms of sound/writing systems. More emphasis will be given to listening and speaking skills. Students will be exposed to innovative learning environments based on new technologies and language acquisition theories. A broad spectrum of Chinese culture will also be brought to students throughout the semester.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

*Participation & Performance*: Language learning involves using the vocabulary and grammar you learned to interact with others for all purposes of life in a target language society. You are required to participate and perform on different language learning tasks/activities in class. You receive full credit of participation for excused class absence and can schedule a make-up work for the absence. No make-ups for any un-excused absences and lateness. ***Your class participation will be checked by random QR code. If you do not respond to the QR code check in, you will lose the participation points of that period***.

*Homework*: Homework is important integral for successful Chinese learning. For the best effect of proficiency-based language learning, assignments of this course are ***spread out over the week*** so that you practice 20-30 minutes daily. Each new week’s homework is posted on Thursday on LMS (http://lms.rpi.edu) and there are THREE (3) due times for the routine weekly homework: (1) Monday before class; (2) Thurs before class, and (3) Sunday Midnight.

It is **your responsibility** to check the weekly homework and due time carefully when posted on the LMS “week by week” section. If homework is submitted late, 10% of the possible points will be deducted per 24-hour period after the due time. Log on to the LMS from this link with your RPI credentials: https://lms.rpi.edu/

*Unit Dictation*: Dictation is a form of testing the listening and writing of vocabulary and sentences. You write down what you hear. Unit Dictations usually take place after we finish each Unit (two chapters). You are not allowed to make up dictations for unexcused tardiness and/or absence.

*Quizlet*: Quizlet is an online flashcards website allowing you to study each chapter’s vocabulary with different modes of study. Much of the vocabulary learning and assessment will be through this Web 2.0 platform. An invite to join will be sent to you via course updates.

*Project*: There will be a couple projects in this course. Check out a folder called “Projects & Instructions” under “Course Documents” on LMS Blackboard.

*Exam*: There will be two exams in this course. Each exam consists of two parts: written and oral. The two exams test your knowledge of vocabulary, grammar, cultural knowledge as well as your competency in listening, speaking, reading, and writing of the Chinese language.

**\*\*\* Note**: If you need **extended time on exams**, or other forms of accommodation, please send me **a memo issued by the Disability Services for Students at the beginning of the semester**. See more about academic accommodations at the end of the syllabus.

Attendance is required since language learning relies heavily on interactions and practices. Documentation for excused absences is processed by the Student Experience Office. ### Other Course Policies:

*Tardy*: Tardy with more than 10 minutes counts as absence for the class. Leaving class early more than 10 minutes will also count as an absence for the class (unless otherwise excused).

*Preparation*: You are expected to be well prepared for each lesson before attending class. Preparation includes:

- Complete required homework that is due to the class time.
- Read the text and vocabulary list carefully until the meaning of each word is fully understood and the characters recognized
- Listen to text audio materials and read aloud to get familiar with the texts
- Prepare to do class activities/tasks by familiarizing yourself with sentence structures.

*Class Participation*:

- You are expected to actively participate in all activities and learning community.
- This is a BYOD class. You can bring in your portable electronic devices for class use. However, ***No Open Lid Electronic Devices*** are allowed unless directed by the instructor. You are *NOT* allowed to check Facebook, emails/ text messages in the classroom. If caught, your class participation points for that class period are ***ZERO***.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

It is VERY EASY to spot cheating in a foreign language course. Students are not permitted to turn in work done by someone else. Getting assistance from a native speaker on assignments is OK, but your final product must be your own work as it is easy to identify the excessive structures beyond your level of proficiency. In addition, use of a WEB TRANSLATE (e.g., Google Translate) for translation other than vocabulary or phrase is not permitted.

The first time I suspect cheating I warn the student and tell why I suspect (e.g. use of non-course grammar or vocabulary) and I take off 50% points of the grade. If it's very obvious I do not give a warning. I will just give a zero. If there is a subsequent infraction the student will receive a grade of F for the course or be reported to the Student's Office.
