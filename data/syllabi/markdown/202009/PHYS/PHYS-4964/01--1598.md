## Course Goals

### Goals:

Develop basic knowledge of optics that includes wave optics, wave coherence, Fourier optics, diffraction theory and nonlinear optics. Establish foundation in photonics and optoelectronics for advanced undergraduate and graduate students in science and engineering disciplines

## Course Content

### Content Details:

(A) Electromagnetic field theory of Light & Reflection/ Refraction & Dispersion;  
(B) Interference & Thin film & Coherence;  
(C) Optical Diffraction & Imaging Processing;  
(D) Polarization of Light & Optical Modulation

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

No Course Learning Outcomes are available for this course.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Students will be able to become familiar with basic concepts of physical optics that include physics of waves, dispersion relationship of wave propagation in dielectric and metal materials, various diffraction phenomena in the near-field and in the far-field, superposition of light waves; polarization of light and Fourier optics as well as non-linear optics and optical modulation based on non-linearity.
