## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

As a Physics Mentor, the student provides leadership for two groups (~10 each) of freshman students, and prepares content for sessions every week. The mentoring sessions will address topics such as problem solving skills for Physics, adjusting to life at Rensselaer, and time-management skills.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

No text. Materials are supplied via LMS as needed.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The student will gain confidence in presenting technical material and in working with diverse students. The student will work with faculty and staff and learn by example while getting to know staff personally. The student will solidify their knowledge of basic Physics while learning how to present it.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- The student will develop and practice techniques for small group facilitation.
- The student will master pedagogical principles for teaching basic technical material.
- The student will demonstrate an understanding of how to assist peers and staff.
- The student will learn how to better communicate with supervisors and peers in a team environment.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

The students will meet regularly with supervising faculty and staff. The student is expected to communicate openly with respect to any issues that may arise in acting as a mentor.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

The mentor is expected to:

1\. Prepare problems and methods of facilitation for two weekly mentoring sessions.

2\. Fully participate in mentor sessions, weekly staff meetings, and training sessions.

3\. Check-in with mentees, offer help if needed, and point students in need towards appropriate resources.

4\. Take attendance at mentoring sessions and promptly report absences and struggling students to professional staff.

5\. Hold one study hour per week.

6\. Be a role model and a resource for mentees.  
If faculty or staff do not feel that a student is performing in an exemplary manner, they will discuss with the student any issues and help the student to address them.

It is expected that the Mentor will diligently fulfill the expectations outlined above and will earn an A for his or her contributions. Minor issues will be discussed promptly with Mentors with explicit recommendations for improvement. If the student takes suitable action there will be no consequence to the grade. If a student fails to correct a serious issue, such as failing to meet his or her student group more than once without making arrangements for a substitute, the grade may be lowered. The student will be asked to resign or receive a lower grade for more serious issues.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Students are expected to run two group sessions per week as scheduled, participate in weekly staff and supervisory meetings, be available for mentoring office hours on the schedule agreed. Failure to do so, will result in rapid feedback from staff and a meeting to address any issues.
