## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- To help you acclimate to university life in a rigorous academic program.
- To help you to do well in PHYS 1100 by sharing productive approaches to understanding physics, problem solving, studying, and taking tests.
- To help you develop collaborative problem-solving skills to aid you in further courses both in and outside the major.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Mentoring will count for 5% of your course grade in PHYS 1100. Mentoring is a mandatory part of your class and it is not possible to get a 100% in the class without adequate effort, attendance, and participation in the sessions. In the event of an absence or conflict, email your mentor ahead of time to work on a make-up time.  
The weekly mentoring sessions will access each of the above listed Course Learning Outcomes.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Mentoring will count for 5% of your course grade in PHYS 1100. Mentoring is a mandatory part of your class and it is not possible to get a 100% in the class without adequate effort, attendance, and participation in the sessions. In the event of an absence or conflict, email your mentor ahead of time to work on a make-up time.

Grading per session is done on a trinary scale and is awarded as follows:

- 0 – The student is not present at the session (unexcused absence)
- 1 – The student is present but does not actively participate (e.g. the student is preoccupied with electronic devices, does not collaborate with other students during group activities.)
- 2 – The student is present and actively participates during the session (e.g. - The student is not distracted by electronics, collaborates with other students during group activities, answers questions or does work on the board when asked) ). The student unmutes video and audio devices when requested.

- **Note**: There may be in class assignments given out by your mentor during the semester. While these are not graded for credit, they do count towards your overall participation in that session.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Attendance to the weekly mentoring sessions is required. Failure to attend a session without an excused absence or contacting you mentor ahead of time will result in a 0 for that day:

- 0 – The student is not present at the session (unexcused absence)

### Other Course Policies:

**Expectations during sessions**

- **Be respectful** to the mentor and to your fellow mentees. Listen to others and do not speak when others have the floor.
- **Please do not use** technology for non-mentoring activities during the 50 minutes we spend in the classroom.
- **Be on time and prepared** so that we can make the best use of our sessions.
- **Be engaged** in our sessions and participate in activities so that you are an active part of the learning group (this is 5% of your class grade!)

**Be receptive to new ideas and processes**, because other mentees may have ideas that you hadn't thought of. This is one of the best ways to learn, especially with a difficult subject like physics.

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).
