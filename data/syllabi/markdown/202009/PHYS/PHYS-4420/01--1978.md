## Course Goals

### Goals:

By completing this course, students will be introduced to the principles and physical applications of classical thermodynamics are developed. In addition, basic concepts in classical and quantum statistical mechanics are introduced and their relations to thermodynamics are developed.

## Course Content

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will be able to provide definition and explanation of concepts such as: temperature, entropy, phase transitions, partition function, Gibbs energy, Helmholtz function.
- Students will learn all four laws of thermodynamics and explain their origin and applications
- Students will be able to calculate the partition functions of simple quantum mechanical systems (e.g., harmonic oscillator, hydrogen atom) and obtain thermodynamic potentials from them.
- Students will be able to explain basic statistical mechanics of quantum gas. Those concepts include Fermi-Dirac and Bose-Einstein distributions.
- Students will be able to apply their knowledge of thermodynamics to complex systems such as the Earth atmosphere and provide a critical description of the effect of global temperature increase.
