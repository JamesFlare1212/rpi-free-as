## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**Short Description:**  
This course covers the statistical design of experiments for systematically testing hypotheses about properties of a system. Topics covered will include: introduction to experiments, the scientific method and randomized controlled trials; tools for reproducible research; completely randomized, blocking, full factorial and fractional designs; response surface methods, and Taguchi designs.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Use the language of Experimental Design to describe the structure of an experiment.
- Design a system, component or process to meet desired needs within realistic constraints such as economic, environmental, social, political, ethical, health and safety, manufacturability and sustainability.
- Identify different types of factors and response variables.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

**Course Grading:**  
Students who take this course will be assessed by four projects, each worth 25% of the final grade. The grading will be based on rubrics that will be available on the LMS. All students are expected to contribute the experimental design and analysis of examples from the medRxiv or bioRxiv repositories.  
Project 1 - Randomized and Factorial Designs  
Project 2 - 2k Designs  
Project 3 - Fractional Factorial Designs  
Project 4 - Response Surface or Taguchi Designs  
The grading for undergraduates will be based on the mastery of the course topics; the grading for graduate students will be based on this and additional theoretical material.
