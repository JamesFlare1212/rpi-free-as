### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Students will be able to demonstrate: (1) an ability to design and analyze dynamic models of systems including mechanical and electrical systems, (2) an understanding of the basic principles of feedback, including disturbance rejection, robustness to parameters, and tracking accuracy, (3) an ability to apply feedback control design and analysis techniques, including root-locus, frequency response, and state-space, (4) an ability to build and analyze models and design feedback controls in MATLAB that achieve design requirements.

Copying: Copying is deﬁned as obtaining information pertaining to a graded exercise by deliberately observing the paper of another student; for example, noting which alternative a neighboring student has circled on a multiple-choice exam. Plagiarism: Plagiarism is deﬁned as representing the work or words of another as one’s own through the omission of acknowledgment or reference. Examples include using sentences verbatim from a published source in a term paper without appropriate referencing, or presenting as one’s own the detailed argument of a published source, or presenting as one’s own electronically or digitally enhanced graphic representations from any form of media.
