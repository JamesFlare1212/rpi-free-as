## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Analyze the performance of a helicopter in hover, vertical climb and descent, and forward flight
- 2\. Identify what aspects of a helicopter’s design affect which aspects of performance, and how
- 3\. Understand the principles behind the control of a helicopter, and the relationship between different control inputs and aircraft response
- 4\. Write computer code that applies models discussed in class
- 5\. Identify several different classes of helicopter and identify relative advantages and disadvantages of each
