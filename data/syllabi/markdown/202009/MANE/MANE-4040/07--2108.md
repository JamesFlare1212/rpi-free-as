## Course Content

### Content Details:

Dimensional measurement and tolerancing; Stress and strain measurement in tension and bending; Contact mechanics; Sliding friction and wear characterization; Fasteners; Shafts and gears; Reverse engineering of a mechanical assembly.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Develop hands-on abilities to conduct mechanical systems testing
- Predict behavior prior to experimentation so that hypotheses may be tested and identify sources of discrepancy
- Critically evaluate multiple means of measurement and their discrepancies considering sources of error
- Consider the design of the experiments and how to modify the experimental design to overcome limitations
- Learn about the role of experimentation and analysis in the design process

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Homework:** In preparation for each of the 7 labs, a small set of homework problems will be assigned.

**Lab Reports:** Every group (generally consisting of the 3-5 students at each lab bench) is expected to prepare a group lab report for each lab completed.

**Quizzes:** Three quizzes will be based upon introductory lab lectures and discussion, hands-on lab activities, the processing of data into results that are presented and discussed in the lab report, and homework.  
**Participation:** Every person must serve as the Group Leader at least once.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Attendance is required, at your regularly scheduled weekly time, so that your entire lab group may work together with continuity. You will be allowed one unexcused absence without penalty; however, you will be required to make up that week’s laboratory activity at another time. A second unexcused absence will cause a 2.5-point penalty on your overall course percent grade. The severity of the additional penalty for each subsequent absence will increase by 2.5 points (i.e. a third absence will have a 5 point penalty, on top of the 2.5 point penalty for the second absence). *All missed labs (excused or unexcused) must be made up to avoid an Incomplete (I) grade*.

### Other Course Policies:

Closed-toe shoes are required in the lab. Safety glasses must be worn when performing experiments on the Instron test frames.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

In this class, all homework assignments that are turned in must represent the student’s own individual work, while all lab reports that are turned in must represent the team’s own group work. In cases where help was received, by the individual student on a homework assignment or the team on a lab report, a notation on the submission should detail this collaboration. Quizzes are purely individual efforts, and collaboration of any form will result in a zero grade on the quiz for all students involved. If any section of a lab report is found to be plagiarized from a prior semester, the entire lab group receives a zero grade on that lab report. If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification.
