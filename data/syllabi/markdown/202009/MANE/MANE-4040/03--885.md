## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**:**

Laboratory experience to complement Elements of Mechanical Design (MANE-4030, EMD). EMD should be taken before or simultaneously with this course. Dimensional measurement and tolerancing; Stress and strain measurement in tension and bending; Contact mechanics; Sliding friction and wear characterization; Fasteners; Shafts and gears; Reverse engineering of a mechanical assembly.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Develop hands-on abilities to conduct mechanical systems testing; Provide first-hand laboratory experiences to complement theories and concepts introduced in the classroom during lecture-style courses; Predict behavior prior to experimentation so that hypotheses may be tested and identify sources of discrepancy; Critically evaluate multiple means of measurement and their discrepancies considering sources of error; Consider the design of the experiments and how to modify the experimental design to overcome limitations; Learn about the role of experimentation and analysis in the design process.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Lab 1: Geometric measurement and tolerance  
Lab 2: Stress-strain behavior of polymers and metals  
Lab 3: beam bending experiment and analysis  
Lab 4: Mechanics of Contact; tribology friction and wear  
Lab 5: Integral fastener design and experiment  
Lab 6: Gear assembly and analysis  
Lab 7: Mechanical component design and analysis

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts, which violate this trust, undermine the educational process. The

*Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities* defines various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all homework assignments that are turned in must represent the student’s own individual work, while all lab reports that are turned in must represent the team’s own group work. In cases where help was received, by the individual student on a homework assignment or the team on a lab report, a notation on the submission should detail this collaboration. Quizzes are purely individual efforts, and collaboration of any form will result in a zero grade on the quiz for all students involved. If any section of a lab report is found to be plagiarized from a prior semester, the entire lab group receives a zero grade on that lab report. If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification.
