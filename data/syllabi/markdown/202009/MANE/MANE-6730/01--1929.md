## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An introductory course overviewing the topics of wear, friction and lubrication. Earlier portions of the course will focus on fluid film hydrodynamic lubrication, with applications to hydrostatic, thrust and journal bearings. The balance of the course focuses on contact mechanics, conduction of frictional heat, surface topography, and resultant wear behavior of unlubricated contacts.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Upon completion of this course students will be able to: estimate temperature rises on bearing surfaces due to frictional heating at both the macro- and roughness asperity-scale; predict contact stresses on bearing surfaces at both the macro- and roughness asperity-scale; assess the predominance of various wear mechanisms at given frictional temperature and contact stress conditions; calculate fluid film thicknesses in hydrodynamically lubricated bearings and assess whether they are sufficient to maintain separation of rough bearing surfaces.
