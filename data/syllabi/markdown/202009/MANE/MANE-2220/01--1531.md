## Course Goals

### Goals:

This is a first course in a 3 course series which focuses on learning how to ideate, innovate and design technology products and services. It enhances critical thinking skills for leaders of technological innovation, emphasizing creativity, teamwork or collaboration, communication and work across engineering disciplines. Students learn how to build on personal and innate talents through critical thinking skills, human-centered-design (HCD) oriented mindsets, and an understanding of selecting appropriate tool and process. Students will utilize tools and processes for ideation and innovation, develop ideas from concepts to minimum viable products via the ideate - build - learn cycle, and recognize and understand prototyping methods. There would be 3 presentations and 3 reports due for different stages of your innovation viz. Concept, EIS (Engineering Innovation for Society), and Final. Templates are provided. Emphasis would be on engineering content, and analysis and synthesis of information collected. Throughout the course students would learn and practice a few “Ideation & Innovation” (I&I) tools, as well as select “tools” from each of the 6 phases of “ Lean Design for Six Sigma – Ideation, Rapid Prototyping & Pilot Manufacturing Innovation” (Lean DFSS-IRPPMI). Ideation Tools are listed as IT. Complete Exercises in Sketch Book mentioned in class. Complete Inventor’s notebook.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Highlights of Topics or Content Learned:

1)3 Cs of Inventor’s Studio – Critical Thinking, Communication, & Collaboration 2)Engineering Design Process 3)Lean Design for Six Sigma Process 4)Engineering Innovation for Society 5)Writing technical journal papers & technical reports. 6)Presenting Posters 7)Writing Patents. 8)Working as a Professional Engineer in an organization 9)Review & provide feedback on technical reports and technical journal papers.

Weeks 1-2: Introduction, Syllabus, Grading Criteria, Start to learn and practice 15 “Ideation & Innovation” tools, Essentials of teamwork or collaboration, Communication skills

5 I&I tools. Lean DFSS-IRPPMI process- Define phase – 10 questions, 100 questions. Start ideation for individual innovation. Overview of Additive Manufacturing. IT1: Day in the Life Of. IT2: I wish, IT3: Backward Imaging IT 4: Snowballing HW1: 1 problem definition and 1 solution

Overview of teamwork, conflict resolution, collaboration, providing feedback, communications skills.

Overview of MBTI assessment etc.

Weeks 3-6: EIS 4 week blitz + 2 weeks combined EIS and Individual Concept Project– Team project, Global Challenges, etc. EIS Rubric, Engineering Ethics, etc. EIS

5 I&I tools. Lean DFSS IRPPMI process - Gather & Analyze phases – Pugh Matrix & Quality Function Deployment (QFD). Continue developing individual innovation. Guest lectures. Define rapid prototyping steps for your innovation. Innovation Concept presentation and report submission. Overview of Team forming for Innovation Project teams - Approval, Resource, Members & Informed (ARMI) and Goals, Resources, Process & Interdependencies (GRPI) tools. SWOT analysis – Strength, Weakness, Opportunity, Threat analysis.

Weeks 7-8: Individual Innovation, Concept Presentation & Report,

6 I& I tools. Continue to build a MVP (Minimum Viable Product) of an innovation, either individually or in a group. Guest lectures. Lean DFSS IRPPMI process - Design, Optimize and Validate phases – Failure Modes & Effects Analysis (FMEA), Multi generation product design, Customer Critical to Quality, “Must Haves vs. Nice to Haves”. Finalize innovation design and rapid prototyping process. Overview of Dimensioning & Tolerancing.- Design for Manufacturing & Assembly (DFMA), Design for Assembly (DFA) and Design for Manufacturing (DFM). IT7: 5/7 Whys. Introduction to Lean. Introduction to Six Sigma.

Weeks 9-12: Final Project – Individual or Teams,

Final presentation and report completed. Iterate/Refine/Ideate on group innovation project. Continue working on Lean DFSS IRPPMI process. Continue completing rapid prototyping Innovation system or sub-system. Patent and/or paper submission. Introduction to Lean and Six Sigma

Week 12: Final Project Presentation & Report, Provide Feedback to team members, etc.

Final individual, or group innovation presentation and report.
