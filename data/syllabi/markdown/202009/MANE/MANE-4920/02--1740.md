## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Laboratory experiments with primary emphasis on light-weight materials and flexible structures that are used in aircraft, spacecraft and rotorcraft.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

None required

## Course Goals

### Goals:

To develop fundamental understanding of the static and dynamic response of flexible, light-weight structures used in aerospace applications.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

1. Materials Selection (Tensile Testing)
2. Structural Stability Investigation (Column Buckling)
3. Forced Vibration (Natural Frequencies and Mode Shapes)
4. Free Vibration (Damping Measurement)
5. Impact Testing (Energy Absorption and Crashworthiness)

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Students will perform experiments in groups of 5 to 7. Each student will submit an individual report after the completion of each experiment. Each lab report accounts for 20% of the grade. There is no final exam.
