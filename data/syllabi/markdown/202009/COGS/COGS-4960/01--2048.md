## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is an upper division course targeted towards PSYC or COGS or other Majors who have interested in continuing onto Graduate School. Hence, the class will be run as a hybrid upper division and graduate course. The particular focus will be on Human Motor Control with a high level view from physiological levels and a more detailed view from functional issues in cognitive psychology. Some of the class will involve lectures but most of the class will be based on discussions of the readings.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Human Motor Control (2nd Edition) by David A. Rosenbaum.  
Supplementary readings available through the instructor's website.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Students who complete this course will:

1\) Acquire a deeper understanding of the biological brain as it functions in movement initiation and control

2\) They will also acquire an understanding of the human mind as a computational system with a particular emphasis of modern theories of Predictive Processing in the control and initiation of motor actions

3\) Demonstrate the ability to engage in critical discussion related to scientific theories

4\) Develop skills for presenting complex topics in an accessible manner

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Introduction Core Problems in the domain of Motor Control.  
Introduction to the Physiological Foundations of Motor Control.  
Introduction to the Psychological Foundations of Motor Control.  
Detailed focus on several Human Activity Systems including:  
A. Walking  
B. Looking  
C. Reaching and Grasping  
D. Drawing and Writing  
E. Keyboarding  
F. Speaking and Singing  
G. Smiling  
Overview of PRINCIPLES AND PROSPECTS underlying the studies of Motor Control

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

• Students are expected to have read all assignments for each class before the class meets.

• [30%] During class, students are expected to contribute to class discussions at each class session and will be asked to lead discussions on different parts of the text.

• [40%] Students will be expected to prepare and present Power Point Presentations of research papers which are either  
(a) discussed in **Human Motor Control** (HMC),  
(b) are more recent updates of work discussed in HMC, or  
(c) other relevant research papers (where relevance is determined by the Professor).  
Each student should expect to make one presentation about once every three weeks of class.

• All such readings will be listed on the class DropBox site. These presentations are expected to be approximately 20-30 min in duration.

• [30%] Quizzes will be frequent with ``pop’’ quizzes on different parts of each textbook chapters and more extensive quizzes at the end of each chapter. Text book readings, topics which arose during classroom discussions, and material discussed during the APower Point Presentations may all be tested by the quizzes.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

The percentage of the total grade determined by each part of the course assessment sequence (e.g., in class discussions 30%, PPT presentations 50%, quizzes 20%).

• Within each series of 5 quizzes, your worst grade will be dropped.

• After each 5 week class period, you will meet with the Professor to discuss your progress in the course.
