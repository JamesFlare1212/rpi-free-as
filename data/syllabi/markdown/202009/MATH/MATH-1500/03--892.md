## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**Course Description:** Basic concepts in differential and integral calculus for functions of one variable. Topics will include functions, limits, continuity, derivatives, integration, exponential and logarithmic functions, and techniques of integration. Application areas will include topics in Management, Architecture, and Social Sciences with special emphasis on the role of calculus in introductory probability. (from the [Rensselaer Catalog](http://catalog.rpi.edu/))

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- basic and advanced symbol manipulation skills
- the ability to compute derivatives using the definition or basic rules
- the ability to compute basic integrals and those requiring substitution or integration by parts
- the ability to apply certain fundamental theorems, basic concepts, and rules from calculus to solve graphical problems
- the ability to apply calculus to selected problems in management, architecture, and social sciences

**Assessments:** The material covered on each assessment will be announced on [LMS](http://lms.rpi.edu/) in advance of each assessment. Assessments will be given on WebAssign on Mondays. No recitations will be held on Mondays when there is an assessment. There will be a window of availability for each assessment and each assessment will be timed. Students may take the assessment at any time during the window of availability, but once the assessment is opened, it must be completed within a specified amount of time. Details, including what material is covered on each assessment, will be posted on LMS before each assessment.

Make-up assessments will only be given to students with an [excused absence documented by the Student Success office](https://success.studentlife.rpi.edu/student-success/excused-absence). **Missing an assessment should not be taken lightly.** Thus, students missing an assessment due to an excused absence should notify the instructor to make arrangements for a make-up assessment before it begins. Any request for a make-up assessment must be received by the Tuesday following the assessment (i.e., the following day) at 11:59 PM. Requests received after that time will not be honored. If a student misses an assessment due to an unexcused absence (e.g., did not know there was an assessment, overslept, etc.), then the student will not be granted a make-up assessment since the lowest assessment will be dropped at the end of the semester.

**Homework:** WebAssign homework will be due usually once or twice weekly on Tuesdays and Fridays at 11:59 PM and will be graded automatically by the system. A two-day extension can be requested through WebAssign once per homework assignment. These requests will be automatically granted by WebAssign, but they must be requested within 2 days of the original due date. A 25% penalty will be applied to points earned after the original due date and a record of requests will be retained. No other extensions will be granted for any reason. To further compensate for this, several homework assignments will be dropped at the end of the semester.

Additional homework problems may be assigned as necessary from the textbook. These will not be collected nor graded. Success in the course depends on successful completion of all homework, both online and written.

**Grade Appeals:** If upon reviewing an assessment a student feels that his or her work was graded incorrectly, then the student may submit a grading appeal to the instructor. The student should submit, in writing, an explanation of the grading issue, including the number of the problem on the assessment. Do not submit screenshots unless asked. Students have one week after the grading of the assessment was complete to submit an appeal.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Academic Integrity:** Students should familiarize themselves with the section on academic integrity in the [Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities](https://rpi.app.box.com/s/3f1cl3w1lt1mq8gqla68v6uk3vh1c19f) (pp. 16–18). All work submitted on assessments and homework must be the student’s own. Students may use their textbook and notes during assessments, but the use of online resources outside of WebAssign and LMS is prohibited. Communicating with other people in any way during assessments about the content of the assessment is prohibited. The minimum academic sanction applied to a student found in violation of the above policies will be a grade of 0 on the assignment. This grade will not be dropped at the end of the semester. A stronger sanction may be applied if warranted. A report regarding the violation will also be submitted to the Dean of Students Office and will remain on file until graduation. Repeat offenders will fail the course and will be subject to judicial action.
