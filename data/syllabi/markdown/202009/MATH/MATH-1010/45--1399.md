## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Functions, limits, continuity, derivatives, implicit differentiation, related rates, maxima and minima, elementary transcendental functions, introduction to definite integral with applications to area and volumes of revolution.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to demonstrate basic and advanced symbol manipulation skills.
- Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to demonstrate the ability to convert between calculus concepts and their graphical, numerical, and symbolic representations.
- Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to demonstrate the ability to apply calculus concepts to selected problems in science and engineering.
- Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to demonstrate the ability to apply certain fundamental theorems, basic concepts, and rules from calculus to solve symbolic and graphical problems.
- Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to demonstrate the ability to present problems related to the theory of calculus and its applications to science and engineering disciplines.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Lecture videos are posted to LMS on Saturdays for Mon/Tues lectures and Wednesdays for Thurs/Fri lectures and supplemental lectures are scheduled as described above. Recitations are held as a mix of hybrid in-person and online webex meetings. ### Other Course Policies:

Make-up exams will only be given to students with an [excused absence documented by the Student Success office](https://success.studentlife.rpi.edu/student-success/excused-absence). **Missing an exam should not be taken lightly.** Thus, students missing an exam due to an excused absence should notify the instructors to make arrangements for a make-up exam before the exam begins. If a student misses an exam due to an unexcused absence (e.g., did not know there was an exam, overslept, etc.), then the student must inform the instructors no later than noon on the Friday following the exam. The student may elect to take a make-up exam on that Friday at 9:05 PM and a 25% penalty will be applied to the score.

**Exam #3:** Exam #3 will be given during finals week on a date and time determined by the [Office of the Registrar](https://info.rpi.edu/registrar/grades/%23FinalExamScheduleandConflicts). The exam will cover new material as well as some topics from Exams #1 and #2. **All students must take the final exam at the time scheduled by the Office of the Registrar. Exceptions will only be made if there is a scheduling conflict as defined** [**here**](https://info.rpi.edu/registrar/grades/%23FinalExamScheduleandConflicts) **or if there is a legitimate time zone conflict.** Please note that [the Student Success office does not issue excused absences during final exams](https://success.studentlife.rpi.edu/student-success/administrative-policies-and-procedures/missing-final-exams). Please contact your Class Dean immediately if you believe you might miss a final exam.

No make-up quizzes will be given for any reason. To compensate for this, the lowest quiz score for each student will be dropped at the end of the semester. Only students who miss a quiz due to an [excused absence documented by the Student Success office](https://success.studentlife.rpi.edu/student-success/excused-absence) may be excused from a quiz.

**Homework:** WebAssign homework will be due usually once or twice weekly on Tuesdays and Fridays at 11:59 PM and will be graded automatically by the system. A two-day extension can be requested through WebAssign once per homework assignment. These requests will be automatically granted by WebAssign, but they must be requested within 2 days of the original due date. A 25% penalty will be applied to points earned after the original due date and a record of requests will be retained. No other extensions will be granted for any reason. To further compensate for this, several homework assignments will be dropped at the end of the semester.

Additional problems will be assigned from the textbook. Students will be asked to submit a small number of these problems to be graded most weeks on Tuesdays at 11:59 PM. These written homework assignments will be available on LMS on Thursdays at 12:00 AM along with instructions for submission.

**Time Zone Conflicts:** Students with legitimate time zone conflicts with the test & quiz block on Wednesdays at 9:05–10:55 PM—or with the exam given during finals week that has not yet been scheduled—may opt to take quizzes and exams at an alternate time. Time zone information will be collected during the first week of the semester so arrangements can be made in advance.

**Webex Decorum:** Please adhere to the following during Webex meetings for this course.

- Join Webex meetings promptly at scheduled times.
- Turn your camera on, if at all possible. In case of connectivity issues, upload a photo in your Webex profile, which will be displayed when your camera is off.
- Use good microphone etiquette. Mute your microphone unless you are speaking. If you are having microphone issues, you may participate by using the chat feature.
- Whenever possible, set yourself up to attend meetings in a space where you are not distracted and your surroundings are not distracting to others in the meeting. This is a class and meetings require your attention.

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. ### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

All work submitted on exams and quizzes must be the student’s own. Students may use their textbook and notes during exams, but the use of online resources outside of WebAssign and LMS is prohibited. Communicating with other people in any way during exams about the content of the exam is prohibited.

The minimum academic sanction applied to a student found in violation of the above policies will be a grade of 0 on the assignment. A stronger sanction may be applied if warranted. A report regarding the violation will also be submitted to the Dean of Students Office and will remain on file until graduation. Repeat offenders will fail the course and will be subject to judicial action.
