## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is a project-based course. Students will work directly in a consultant role for a company. The major deliverable for the course will be a consultant report that analyzes the nature of the challenges the client organization is facing in the area of focus, an assessment of options along with the one recommended, and, if workable, an outline of an implementation plan.

We will begin the semester with sessions to cover skills that you will apply to your team project throughout the semester. Within the first two weeks of the term, we will engage in the process of finding an appropriate, real-world project from the business community to work on for the class. Project proposals with stated milestones and deliverables will be developed by the third week of class and must be approved by me. The written proposal describing the milestones and deliverables is key and will be used throughout the class. The proposal is a living document and we fully anticipate that plans may change as the project evolves and you tackle the realities of the challenges presented so that both you and the client become more knowledgeable about the direction to take. This process is characteristic of the types of consulting projects you will be exposed to in industry.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Additionally, students will experience the following dynamics associated with the practice of management consulting*:*

Concretely defining the scope of a consulting engagement

- Establishing an understanding with a client on the nature of the engagement and approaches and resources available to be deployed
- Breaking a complicated problem into logical pieces that can be individually addressed
- Gathering the data necessary to build a relevant fact base
- Extracting information from internal and external experts
- Developing and presenting analysis, options, and recommendations in a persuasive manner to gain buy-in
- Influencing others effectively using a range of ethical tactics and
- Assessing your own level of interest in consulting and where you stand in relation to the skills required to do it

## Course Content

### Content Details:

**Overall schedule. The specific dates will be arranged as the semester develops, in a way that will respond to the stages of the projects:**

We will spend the first couple of weeks introducing the course and identifying the projects and project groups. Your initial contact with the client should occur then or shortly after.

Once the projects and groups are identified, you will do all you can to learn about the client’s business and get a better feel for the project you will be working on.

The first deliverables are spelled out above. The project plan must be sure to incorporate your client’s needs and get their buy-in. This plan will include a written report to accompany the chart and an oral presentation to the class.

The middle of the term will see the first significant delivery point, consisting of the components specified above.

The second half of the semester will involve the groups doing much of the work on your own and checking in with me and the other groups regularly in sessions held during scheduled times for class meetings.

The final presentations and papers will be delivered during final exam week, December 15-18 and 21, with the final presentations to the client happening in the same time period.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Apply concepts and methods from MBA courses to a client organization’s objectives
- • Develop and execute a plan to manage the project’s work flow
- • Find and analyze relevant information from a wide variety of sources
- • Diagnose an organizational challenge and obstacles to its fulfillment
- • Develop strategic options related to courses of action for the client

- • Communicate effectively both in writing and verbally

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Students will work individually and in groups. Individually, you will be asked to work to hit certain milestones given in the project description. Group-wise, we will form project groups early in the semester and assign your group to work with a client on a project. Grading requirements focus on the project itself and include presentations, written reports, Gantt charts, project timeline updates, and meetings with clients and with me.

**Grading Schedule**

**This semester will include initial, mid-term and final oral in-class presentations and accompanying written reports.**

Presentation #1 – Kick off paper and presentation (5% each) = 10% of final grade

Presentation #2 – Mid-term paper and presentation (15% each) = 30% of final grade

Presentation #3 – Final paper and presentation (25% each) = 50% of final grade

Content of Presentations and Written Reports:

- 1\) A kick off Gantt chart, deliverables, and milestones, along with a one-page plan that includes your contract with the client and its scope of work to be performed.

- 2\) A mid-term stage with an updated and better informed Gantt chart, deliverables, and milestones based on what you have learned, along with a three-page report on progress and plans.

- 3\) An end-of-term stage with a final updated Gantt chart, update on milestones achieved and missed since the last report, and the final deliverables, along with a six-page report on recommendations and implementation. Included will be an update on how your initial contract and scope of work fared. It should be six pages long and submitted to me by the last day of class

Attendance, participation, and preparation for class sessions – 10% of final grade

Client Presentation - Schedule a time to present a final report to your client – it must

be scheduled before the last day of class and needs my approval

You can anticipate spending significant time and effort outside of class. The Practicum is essentially a capstone course which will require significantly more work than a regular class. In-class time is mostly used for project preparation and tracking.
