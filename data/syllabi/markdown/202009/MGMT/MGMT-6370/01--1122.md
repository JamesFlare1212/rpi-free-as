## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**Topics:**

1. Introduction to the institutional structure of the financial markets for derivatives.
2. Hedging and basis risk, interest rate, and stock-index derivatives.
3. Financial management applications of derivatives.
4. Introduction to options, rational option pricing restrictions.
5. Binomial option pricing model, and put and call option strategies.
