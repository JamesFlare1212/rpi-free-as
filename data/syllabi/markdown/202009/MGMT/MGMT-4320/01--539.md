### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

COURSE ASSESSMENT MEASURES  
Attendance and Class Participation (10% of Course Grade)  
Attendance for each class will be taken informally using digital imaging. A picture of the classroom (and/or WebEx videos) will be taken at the beginning of each class and following any mid-class breaks. Additionally, students will receive credit for participation via interaction with the class hashtag #rpiINV on Twitter.  
Quizzes (10% of Course Grade)  
Approximately every two chapters, a four question, multiple choice quiz will be given during class time. Completing this quiz during the class will earn full credit regardless of the number of correct/incorrect answers. The purpose of the quizzes is for students to self-evaluate their progress during the course and ask questions/request clarification regarding specific topics, concepts, and/or formulas.  
Assignments (4 Assignments, 10% Each)  
Students will complete four assignments throughout the semester. Each assignment is worth 10% of the course grade. Students will work independently on the multiple choice and Excel portions of these assignments and the instructor will provide review sessions and/or additional office hours before each assignment deadline (see course calendar for tentative assignment deadlines). The assignments will include a multiple choice (25 questions) within Blackboard/LMS and a Microsoft Excel component (3 questions) that is to be downloaded, completed, and uploaded back into Blackboard before the assignment deadline. The instructor will provide feedback through comments within the Excel assignment documents and upload the comments back to Blackboard/LMS. Students are strongly encouraged to review this feedback for Microsoft Excel and data exercises.  
Midterm and Final Exam (20% Each)  
The midterm and final exams will have a very similar format to the assignments, but the midterm (final) exam will be administered in/during (take home) class. Each student will be required to bring/use a laptop computer with network connectivity and the required software (Microsoft Excel, web browser to access LMS, etc.). For the midterm exam, no external resources (e.g. non-LMS websites, chat programs, electronic or paper documents, etc.) will be permitted for use during the final examination with the exception of a single page, double-sided paper document with formulas and/or notes of the student’s choosing. The final exam will be open-book.

## Policies

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).
