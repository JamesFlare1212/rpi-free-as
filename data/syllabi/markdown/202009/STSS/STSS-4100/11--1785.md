## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Participation is required for this course. As such, Packback Questions platform will be used for online discussion to reflect on weekly readings and presentations. Packback Questions is an online curiosity community where you can be fearlessly curious and ask BIG questions to elevate the course topics. Explore how the content of the course relates to life and the real world. Cite readings and explore the meaning and applications of the content provided in the class.

**The cost to register for this required course engagement tool is $25.**

Writing purposeful questions and answers on Packback will:

- Help you develop writing skills necessary for any career path.
- Reinforce the imperative skill of justifying thoughts and claims with credible evidence (while citing the evidence!).
- Enhance critical thinking sought by employers.
- Deepen your understanding of the course content by gaining diverse insights and perspectives from your peers.

**Your participation on Packback will count for 30% percent of your final grade**.

In order to receive your points per week, you must post **1 Question and 1 Answers per week during presentation weeks and answer all of the Pinned Question (by the professor) as noted on the class schedule.**

**Each question MUST be relevant to presentation topic being discussed. When a team is presenting members of the team are exempt from posting one question for their presentation but must answer questions relating to their presentation**.

Posts should aim to reflect on the readings and the material presented, but you are not limited to citing and incorporating course readings. You may add content, links, citations, and references that bolster you and your peers’ learning. Posts and questions should be substantive and include at least one citation or media link. Contextualize your posts.

Before you start posting, be sure to read the **Community Guidelines** found in the tutorial on Packback. If your post does not follow the Packback Community Guidelines, there is a chance it will be removed, and you won’t receive points for that post.

The deadline for submissions on your weekly schedule is every Sunday by 11:59PM regardless of your section in this course. **Note**: it takes 24 hours for the Packback team to moderate a post and send a coaching email. In order to get credit for a moderated post, you must edit and re-publish by the deadline. This is why it is important that you complete your Packback questions and responses far before the deadline in case your post is moderated.

**You will receive a welcome email from** **help@packback.co** **prompting you to finish registration and payment. Packback has already created an account for you with your school email, all you need to do is reset your password. This email may be directed to spam or filtered out, so make sure you do a thorough scan of your inbox if you can't find the email.**

**If you search your inbox and still can't find the welcome email, or if you are new to the course, you may manually register by following the instructions below:**

1. **Navigate to** **https://Packback.co/questions** **and click “Register as a new student”.** Note: If you already have an account on Packback you can login with your credentials.
2. **Make sure to register with your SCHOOL email address and real first name and last name.**
3. **Enter our class community’s access code into the “Join a new Community” module on your dashboard. Your course access code will be provided to you via email before the class officially enacts.**
4. **Follow the instructions on your screen to finish your registration.**

**If you have ANY questions or concerns regarding Packback throughout the semester, please contact the customer support team at** **help@packback.co** **(not “.com”).**

For a brief introduction to Packback Questions and why we are using it in class, watch this video: vimeo.com/packback/Welcome-to-Packback-Questions

This course is a series of thematic conversations about your values and responsibilities as an emerging/aspiring engineer. There is no traditional textbook, but there is much reading. *Various articles/case studies will be available on LMS and via email including select chapters from the following books.*

Casey, S.M. (1998). *Set phasers on stun: and other true tales of design, technology, and human error*. Aegean Publishing Company.

**WRITING ASSIGNMENTS**

**Weekly Reading RESPONSES to PINNED QUESTIONS by Professor** **(6 @ 5 points each)**

**30 POINTS – 10% of course grade**

You will write a brief (2-3 paragraph) response to key questions on the assigned readings and submit it on Packback. Reading responses are due each Sunday by 11:59 PM EST on Packback*. **All weekly reading responses on Packback from me are required.** During this time, you will also have an opportunity to ask practice questions, so you become comfortable with using Packback and get additional writing practice.*

**1 Question and 1 Answer starting with (6 Qs and 6 As @ 5 points each)**

**60 POINTS** – **20% of course grade**

Starting with case presentations you are responsible for choosing 6 cases and posting one questions and one answer for a total of 6 questions and 6 answer by the end of the semester. Your questions and answers must be in reaction to the case. You may answer one question or provide detailed counter points or supporting points to answers from other members of the class.

**Mid-term Assessment**

**30 POINTS – 10% of course grade**

For the mid-point assessment, students will write a 3-page paper relating key issues discussed in class to an assigned case. The paper will address key questions. The case and the questions will be provided ahead of time.

The paper should be double-spaced, Times New Roman, 12 point-font with 1-inch margins on all sides. Use headings as appropriate to guide the reader (the instructor). Any figures or tables included do not count against the page requirement and should be explained thoroughly in the body of the paper. Always \*cite your resources, parenthetically and in the reference page. Citations should follow \*\*APA format. Please don’t forget your name and page numbers. Due dates are listed on the Course Calendar.

**GROUP PRESENTATION**

**60 POINTS – 20% of course grade**

It has been noted that the best way to learn a topic is to teach it to someone else. To that end, I am organizing part of this class much like a graduate school seminar, with “groups of 5” assigned to one topic (from a list of topics to be provided in class). Groups will share information about the topic (reference the articles in their presentation) and encourage class discussion on the various issues (e.g., causes of the disaster, implications for the future).

Success as a discussant is not predicated on how much you say. In fact, the **best discussants** will *encourage everyone else to speak and create a lively debate*. Your presentation may be formal or informal (e.g., PowerPoint presentation and list of discussion questions, videos, skit, role-play, debate). Keep in mind that the purpose of a PowerPoint is to help you organize your thoughts. Groups will encourage to engage and entertain comments/questions from the class.

Team and Individual Team Member Criteria and Deliverables:

All team members will contribute to the presentation (creation and delivery of the presentation). Team members will meet outside of the classroom (virtually) prior to the presentation.

· A short summary of the case will be available on LMS or via email to the class. **Each team** will send the instructor their relevant additional information (for example - links to websites) for review and distribution to the class via email 2 days ahead of their presentation date. Although **ALL class members will read the articles** on the topic and be prepared to discuss the issues, **group members will be viewed as the experts** on that topic.

· Each team must have a comprehensive PowerPoint with annotations in speaker notes (presentation may include brief videos). Each speaker slide must include the name/initials of the individual member responsible for the creation/presentation of the slide.

· On the day of the presentation***, each member of the team*** must be available and present on live WebEx teams for at least 5 minutes and no more than 10 minutes.

· PowerPoint slides may be shared in the general chatroom in WebEx teams, at least one day ahead of the presentation. This will enable each audience member to review and formulate questions for their post on Packback.

· Following the live presentation, audience members may ask questions of the team aside from the questions that will post on Packback.

·

Each Team/team member will be evaluated on:

· Their contribution to the presentation (including content/design/cohesion of their presentation slides)

· Verbal communication and presentation skills including communication style and ability to respond to questions from the audience

· Perceived knowledge of case

Each team member will also be peer evaluated by other members of the team.

Grading rubrics for the above will be available on LMS.

PLEASE NOTE: If your presentation is going to have an interactive exercise/experiment component, it is essential that you run the details of your exercise by me a week in advance of your presentation. I would estimate you will need a minimum of seven hours preparation (additional to just reading) for your day as discussant. Finally, please feel free to reach out to me prior to your discussion day; I would be happy to guide your thought process.

**Professional Class Behavior**: It is important that you maintain a professional demeanor in class and on-line. RPI expects this from you, as do future employers. The classroom (virtual or in-person) is the perfect place to practice interacting in a manner appropriate to a professional setting.

**Email Etiquette**: ***Only Rensselaer email accounts may be used when conducting Institute business***, which includes, among other things, teaching and research***. Please type “[your section #]” in the subject line when you email me***. This ensures that your emails are separated from other emails. I may not respond to emails that do not have this subject line. Also, please refrain from using the “text messaging” format when emailing.

**Regular and Timely Attendance**: You are expected to attend each synchronous live session regularly and be prompt and fully prepared. Failure to attend and participate regularly decreases your grade, as I will be taking attendance and expect that you will be present and engage in team activities on WebEx:

You will lose points:

- If you miss more than 1 class without an excused absence
- If you attend regularly but do not engage
- If you provide minimal feedback to your peers

If you neither attend regularly nor participate, your participation grade will be 25 points or less

As in business, you will receive 1 personal days for unexcused absences. You may have a trip coming up or wish to study for another exam. If I do not hear from you, I will assume you are taking the class off as one of your personal days. **Please note: This day may not be on the day of your team presentation.**

**Changes to Schedule**: While I plan to stick to the class schedule, there might be reasons to modify the schedule. In these cases, all changes will be announced in class. It is your responsibility to become aware of any such changes.
