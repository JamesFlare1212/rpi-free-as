## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Listening to each other, ourselves, and our surroundings can be a transformative experience that enhances well-being. In this course, listening skills—as related to self-reflection, sensory awareness, interpersonal communication, and our environmental surroundings—will be developed through experiential exercises, creative projects, readings, lectures, and discussion. The course introduces “Deep Listening”—a practice developed by pioneer composer and humanitarian Pauline Oliveros to enhance and expand listening abilities and to encourage creative work. All abilities and backgrounds welcome! Gateway course for Well-being Pathway.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate listening skills through detailed observations of the acoustic environment, conducting interviews, making effective audio recordings, and applying audio editing a skills to produce a cohesive sonic work.
- Successfully complete a self-designed, listening-themed creative project.
- Write clearly, effectively, creatively.
- Reflect on personal creative work, and offer meaningful feedback to peers.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

- 10% Attendance in synchronous class meetings
- 15% Participation in in-person activities (or equivalent digital “make up" assignments)
- 5% Short reading responses
- 5% Listening Essay 1 (“Listening to your Listening”)
- 5% Listening Essay 2 (“Listening to Others”)
- 10% Listening Essay 3 (“Environmental Listening”)
- 10% Audio interview project (including peer review)
- 10% Field recording composition (including peer review)
- 30% Final Creative Research Project / Research Paper (including proposal & peer review)
