## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

*Music and Nature* explores the intersections between music and nature from a variety of disciplinary perspectives – artistic, scientific, spiritual/religious. Music will be approached as artistic expression, drawing on diverse world cultures, as well as a form of knowledge that can heighten our sensitivity and awareness of the world around us.

*Music and Nature* incorporates reading and writing assignments, and individual and group creative projects.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Identify and engage with key artists, movements, music traditions, and works by composers and sound artists across a range of genres and geographic locations that are in dialogue with environmental issues and nature
- Trace the relationships between music, developments in recording technology, the environmental movement, and environmental activism since the late 19th cen.
- Identify the ways that listening, sound, and music are used to translate and collaborate with scientific information and data
- Synthesize interdisciplinary perspectives and navigate through scholarly and non- scholarly forms of knowledge in the analysis of unique considerations of music, sound, and the environment.
- Express personal aesthetic responses to music and sound art that are grounded in objective knowledge of style, period, and social context

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration. Submission of any assignment that is in violation of this policy will result in a penalty. In the case of major acts of plagiarism (e.g., text copied verbatim from an unidentified source) the student will receive a grade of F for the course. In the case of minor acts of plagiarism (e.g., referencing the findings of others without appropriate citations) the student will fail the assignment and receive a reduction of final course grade by one letter grade. If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification.
