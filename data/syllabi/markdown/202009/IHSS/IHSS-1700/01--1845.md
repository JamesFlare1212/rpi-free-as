## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

From opera arias to indigenous folk tunes to hip-hop, the act of setting words to music has produced some of the world’s most popular and enduring art in the form of songwriting, with many of the world’s greatest songs brought into existence with little more than imagination and a guitar or a beat. This course examines the wide variety of elements present in the creation of a well-written song, while honing a higher sense of critique when analyzing the work of both successful and obscure songwriters. Song recordings, videos, and lively class discussion frame a closer look at multiple lyrical and creative techniques, and students are encouraged to experiment on a grand scale, without fear, when writing at least one song per week.

Students get positive and useful creative feedback along with opportunities for collaboration, while learning how to make more effective demonstration recordings and communicate well with musicians and recordists in a studio session. Though it focuses primarily on aspects of songwriting as a popular and commercial art form, this course can also be seen as an effective study in creative structure building through songwriting, which is in many ways analogous to design forms in architecture, mathematics, chemistry, and computer sciences. It is also a study of professional problem solving while working both solo and as a team. Or it can just help you finish that surefire hit you’ve been working on, like, forever.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Students will create a portfolio of best works while discovering a wealth of strategies for creativity in the study of great writers, lyricists, and songwriting teams, becoming more familiar with the enormous body of great (and not-so-great) works available through streaming and downloading. All students will participate in the songwriting process in whatever way they see fit, and quite possibly find their appreciation of the artform will be deepened and enriched by its closer examination, where they can potentially find their artistic voice through collaboration, creative promotion, and music production.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

No Course Learning Outcomes are available for this course.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

SONGWRITER PAPER- Students will select a songwriter, and produce 1000-word paper focusing on the individualized processes and methods employed by the songwriter, with music files and lyrical examples provided. Citations APA style.

SONG PRESENTATION- Students will provide a 20-minute analysis and presentation on a chosen “Popular Song” to the class online, using Webex. These will be spread out over the second half of the semester.  
SONGWRITER PORTFOLIO- Students will meet the song deadlines as presented, and turn in a professional portfolio of their four best songs, complete with demonstration recordings and lyrics, on the last day of class.  
“MORNING PAGES” or JOURNAL- as suggested by the author of The Artist’s Way, students are requested to maintain a journal throughout the semester for the sole purpose of “morning pages,” whereby students will write whatever they wish onto three (3) pages daily.

PRESENCE AND PARTICIPATION- The success of this class depends on the presence and participation of each student when discussing the reading and critiques. We will strive to make a community where quality creative feedback can be freely given and taken. Any student in danger of not receiving these points will be notified discreetly weeks in advance of assessment, and given every chance to reclaim them.
