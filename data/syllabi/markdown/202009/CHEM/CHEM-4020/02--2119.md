## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will be familiar with variety of instrumental and techniques employed in modern inorganic and physical chemistry.
- Students will be able to estimate experimental uncertainty in quantitative measurements.
- Students will be able to write professional style papers.
- Students will be able to make an effective oral presentation on a technical subject.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Plagiarism will result in a grade of 0 for the report and loss of a letter grade for the course. Plagiarism consists of copying other people’s writings. It is generally permissible to quote, with proper attribution, brief excerpts of perhaps a sentence or less, when there is reason to use the writer’s original words, but reproduction of longer passages, even when properly referenced, is not acceptable. Simply changing a few words is not adequate either. If you are using other sources as a basis for your writing, you must make your material different enough to show that you have made some intellectual contribution to the material. Avoid using a single source if at all possible; it is easier to be creative if you are integrating material from several sources. (Integrating does NOT mean cut-and-paste construction.) Copying material from a summary or formal report of another student will be considered plagiarism. Copying from the lab manual excessively or without attribution also is plagiarism.
