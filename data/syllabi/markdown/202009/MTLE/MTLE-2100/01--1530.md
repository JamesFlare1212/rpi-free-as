## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Structure of metals, ceramics, and polymers and method of structural determination will be discussed. The structure includes crystal structure, structural changes (transformation), defects, and micro-structure. The crystal structure determination, theoretical background and practice of x-ray diffraction will be covered. Local structure of non-crystalline materials, such as glasses and Polymer by FT-IR spectroscopy will also be covered. Effects of defects such as point defects, dislocation and surface on selected properties of materials will also discusssed.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The importance of materials structures will be emphasized since they affect various materials properties. The important structural determination tool, x-ray diffraction, will be covered extensively. Students will learn the background information such as x-ray generation and its absorbance by different materials, selected diffraction peaks for different structures using Structure Factor Calculation.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Crystal Structures, Point Defects, Dislocation and Surface. X-Ray diffraction, absorption, structure factors.
