## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course is a grad-level course on mechanical properties of materials. We will cover elastic, plastic, fracture behaviors, sometimes under high temperatures or cyclic loading, of various materials, including crystalline and non-crystalline materials, monolithic, composite or cellular materials.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understand mechanical properties of materials from the atomic structure and chemistry.
- Understand differences in mechanical properties among different classes of materials
- Understand how materials response under different conditions such as temperature, strain rate and chemical environments.
- Understand plasticity of metals and alloys from dislocation activities.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Homeworks, midterms, finals, term paper/presentations.
