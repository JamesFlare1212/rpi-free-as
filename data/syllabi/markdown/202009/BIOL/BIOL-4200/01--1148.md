## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course provides an introduction to the concepts and techniques of modern statistics. Computer-based, in-class sessions will emphasize hands-on applications of statistical techniques in R using data and examples drawn from the biological and medical sciences. Pre-class exercises and readings will prepare participants for in-depth classroom explorations. Topics covered for the last part of the course are given below.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

**Assessments:**

The activities that will measure your progress towards mastering these goals will include both formative and summative assessments. Formative assessments take place during the learning process. We will be assessing conceptual understanding and the ability to transfer your knowledge to problems and research questions via homework.

As the central part of a hands-on, interactive-learning approach, you can engage hands-on analyses as you move through course materials. The summative assessments will consist of 3 exams, one at the end of each major unit. Associated with the exams are take-home projects. The dates for the exams and the due dates for the take home projects will be posted. The take home projects will involve 3-4 research-based questions related to real-world data sets. Answering these questions will require the application of the statistical analyses and procedures that we have addressed in class. You will submit the output for each question/analysis, along with a brief statement of your results and conclusions.

**Grading:** There will be a total of ~500 points for the course, distributed as follows:

Homework: ~100 points
