### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Learning Outcomes**

4000-level Students:

- Demonstrate competence in reading and critically reviewing primary scientific literature in muscle and cardiac biology though group discussions, summarizing the key points within selected articles, and writing critiques.
- Demonstrate the ability to objectively evaluate selected articles based on facts in the selected articles and scientifically grounded perspectives.
- Lead class evaluation of peer-reviewed papers, and demonstrate understanding of the subject, including knowledge of the broader discipline.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

4000-level students will be assessed by participation in class discussion and on their written reviews of 2 of the chosen papers. A rubric for participation will be given to the students on the first day of class. For their reviews, students will read the chosen papers and write 2-3 pages about the findings and conclusions of the work. After each review, students receive feedback on their assessment of the work. Reviews will be graded by the instructor.

4000-level Student Grade Allotment:

Class Participation – 60%

Paper Reviews – 40% (20% each)

Total – 100%
