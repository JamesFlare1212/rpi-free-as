## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- identify structural difference between bacterial, viral, and eukaryotic genomes
- explain the difference in the regulatory mechanisms of gene expression in bacteria, viruses and eukaryotes
- analyze genes across organisms and species via BLAST search, multiple sequence alignments and phylogenetic analysis
- explain the relationship that exists between genome sequence composition, genome regulation and population diversity and disease
- explain how sequences and regulatory pathways influence the pathogenesis of bacteria, viruses and eukaryotic pathogens
- explain how genome wide analysis can be utilized to study disease etiology
- explain how synthetic genomes can be constructed for therapeutic as well as commercial benefit
- demonstrate the ability to communicate advanced molecular biology topics (discussed in this course) to different audiences (e. g. general public vs upper-level undergraduate biology majors) in oral form
