**COURSE DESCRIPTION AND OBJECTIVES:**

This course will provide a general overview on Viruses. The course will offer the essential and basic aspects of Virology.

The objectives of the course is to introduce: viral structure, classification, entry, and replication for each of the major classes of viruses.

Extra topics:

-Case studies will be used to understand host interactions, prevention of viral infections and therapies against viral infections.

-Virus of virus, and the use of virus as research tools.

This course can be used to fulfill the “Culminating Experience in Biology.”

**PREREQUISITES/COREQUISITES:** **PREREQUISITE: BIOL 4620 AND BIOL 4310 OR PERMISSION OF INSTRUCTOR.OFFER: FALL TERM ANNUALLY.**

**Student Learning Outcomes** (BIOL-4350/6350)

At the end of the course a successful student will be able to:

- Demonstrate the ability to distinguish the main properties of virus
- Demonstrate the ability to explain virus entry to the host cells
- Demonstrate the ability to summarize the mechanisms of viral replication
- Assembly, and release
- Demonstrate the ability to distinguish the genetic organization of virus
- Demonstrate the ability to summarize the evolution of virus
- Demonstrate the ability to summarize the current topics on viral infections.
