## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

## Course Description

Data summery and presentations; introduction to probability; discrete and continuous probability distributions; introduction to descriptive statistics; confidence intervals; hypothesis testing; regression analysis and introduction into machine learning; use of computer applications in statistical analysis. The examples and exercises strongly emphasize engineering applications and the use of R language.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

## Course Goals / Objectives

This course teaches students

- An appreciation for uncertainty in the application of engineering models;
- Defines the difference between probability and statistics;
- What are discrete and continuous distribution functions;
- Marginal and conditional distributions;
- Descriptive statistics;
- Sample distributions and point estimates;
- Confidence intervals;
- Hypothesis testing;
- Univariate and multivariate regression analysis; and
- An introduction into machine learning

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

## Student Learning Outcomes

Students will be able to:

- Understand the concept of probability and conditional probability;
- Obtain and graph information about random variables and variable sets;
- Understand and apply various discrete and continuous distribution functions;
- Understand one sample and two sample confidence intervals
- Formulate hypotheses tests and define confidence intervals;
- Setting up and solving univariate and multivariate regression problems;
- Appreciate the role of machine learning.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

## Grading Criteria

Grading is based on:

- **Quizzes (25%):** A total of 9 quizzes will be assigned.
- **Projects (25%):** There are two projects. One serves as an introduction to R **(10%)** and is designed to experimentally verify the central limit theorem, the other is more comprehensive **(15%)** and involves the use of R to determine regression models based on real data. Late submissions receive a reduction of 10% per day from points earned. Submission later than a week will not be accepted. An excellent introduction and source for R is available on https://moderndive.com/index.html.
- **Tests 1 and 2 (20% for Test 1, 30% for Test 2):** Test 2 is cumulative, i.e. it also includes material covered before Test 1.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

## Course Policies

**In-person or online attendance:**

For students attending in-person classes, attendance is highly recommended. Please follow the institute guidelines for in-person lectures. Lectures during the first week and after Thanksgiving are online. For students attending the online component, videos for all lectures are made available and can be viewed on LMS. For a successful completion of this course, it is essential to engage with the course material, either by attending in-person lectures or through watching the video-based material. It is also essential to review the material after each class and practice the homework problems that are available with worked solutions on LMS.

**Missing Quizzes or Test:**

Missing quizzes or tests are only allowed if there is an emergency verified by the Dean of Student’s office. No makeup for any quizzes or Test 1. Test 2 grade will be used to replace the excused missing assessments. Missing assessments without official excuses will receive zero.

**Webex Quiz/Test protocol:**

1. Close all other windows and tabs. You only need Webex and LMS during the quiz/test period.

2. Webex settings: Do not use WebEx background. Mute your mic but turn on the camera; Enable the Chat in Webex. Select your TA’s name in the dialogue box to avoid disturbing the whole class.

3. Your camera needs to be on during the entire quiz/test period. Adjust your camera to show your work area, not just the face. Cell phone, notebook, or any other unauthorized material should not be visible in your work area. Position your camera at an angle to protect your solution from other participants.

4. You may print out the quiz/test questions and write your solution on those papers if you prefer.

5. Once you are finished, step 1: notify the TA; step 2: scan or take photos of your solution and submit to LMS; step 3: check your submission on LMS; step 4: send a chat message to TA then sign off from the meeting. If you run into difficulties in accessing the submission site, you can email TA and me your solution. Allow yourself several minutes before the submission deadline to deal with the submission.

6. The TA will be recording your logoff time from Webex. This time should match your submission time on LMS. If a mismatch is found, your submission will be denied. Make sure to double check your submitted file to ensure you submitted the right one BEFORE logging out from Webex.

7. The answer keys for a quiz or test will be posted shortly after the submission deadline. The grades for each quiz and test are uploaded on LMS by the TA. To seek clarification for a graded quiz or test, compare your solution with the answer key first and then contact the TAs during their office hours. For a particular quiz or test, the period for seeking clarification is limited to one week after the grades are posted on LMS.
