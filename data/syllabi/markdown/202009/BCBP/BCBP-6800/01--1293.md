## Course Goals

### Goals:

Biophysics is the study of the physical principles of biological function, which requires the application of physical methods. The focus of this course is on methods used to study the interactions and dynamics of biomolecules *in vitro*, primarily proteins and nucleic acids. The course has two objectives: 1. Teach the underlying physical principles for major biophysical methods. 2. To identify the advantages and limitations of each method as applied to various biomelcular systems with specific constraints.

We will cover both the theoretical and methodological aspects of molecular biophysical methods. Of note, structural biophysics is covered in a separate class. This course is designed as an interdisciplinary introduction to the field and is open to students in Biology, Chemistry, Physics or Engineering.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Demonstrate the ability to distinguish between equilibrium and stoichiometric binding
- • Demonstrate the ability to distinguish between cooperative and simple interactions
- • Demonstrate the ability to analyze kinetic and diffusional dynamic processes
- • Demonstrate the ability to devise experiments and analyze results testing biomolecular interactions or dynamics using fluorescence
- • Demonstrate the ability to devise experiments and analyze results testing biomolecular interactions or dynamics using circular dichroism
- • Demonstrate the ability to devise experiments and analyze results testing biomolecular interactions or dynamics using infrared spectroscopy
- • Demonstrate the ability to devise experiments and analyze results testing biomolecular interactions or dynamics using ITC
- • Demonstrate the ability to devise experiments and analyze results testing biomolecular interactions or dynamics using DSC
- • Demonstrate the ability to devise experiments and analyze results testing biomolecular interactions or dynamics using AUC
- • Demonstrate the ability to devise experiments and analyze results testing biomolecular interactions or dynamics using SAXS
- • Demonstrate the ability to devise experiments and analyze results testing biomolecular interactions or dynamics using data analysis
- • Demonstrate the ability to choose the most appropriate method for particular biomolecular problems.
