## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This asynchronous, online course provides an introduction to the field of psychology. Topics covered include: a brief history of the field; psychological research methods; biology of behavior; consciousness; human development (through the life span); sex, gender, and sexuality; sensation and perception; learning; memory; thinking, language and intelligence; motivation and emotion; stress, health and human flourishing; social psychology; personality; and psychological disorders and therapy. Students will demonstrate their mastery of the course material via four timed, online exams administered via Rensselaer’s LMS. Additional required online activities were included to facilitate students’ understanding of the course material. Students will have an opportunity to gain first-hand experience with psychological research, and earn extra credit, by serving as participants in one or more IRB- approved research projects being conducted by Rensselaer’s cognitive science department.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Required:  
Myers, D.G. and DeWall, N.C. (2019). Exploring Psychology in Modules, 11th edition. Macmillan. ISBN: 9781319127756

Students may purchase 6-months access to LaunchPad, which gives them access to the digital textbook and related materials, either through RPI’s bookstore or directly from the publisher. For students who also wish to obtain a print copy of the book, I recommend purchasing it and LaunchPad, as a bundle from the publisher.

Exploring Psychology in Modules, 11th edition, is ideal for use in an online course. It contains all of the

topics traditionally included in introductory psychology textbooks. Moreover, the material in the 15

chapters (units) is arranged into 46 self-contained modules. Each module includes integrated features

designed to enhance mastery of the course material, including: (1) reading material*, (2) video

activities that highlight specific topics/issues, (3) practice quizzes, and (4) a component called

LearningCurve, a “game” that enables students to test their knowledge of the material in each module.

*Note: course readings can be viewed digitally, downloaded as a pdf file, and listened to (modules have been professionally audio-recorded).

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Final grades will be calculated using the average of four online multiple-choice exams (weighted at 90% of your final grade) and the percentage of assigned Video activities (weighted at 5% of your grade) and LearningCurve activities (5% of your grade) completed “on time”. Each exam is graded on a 0-100% scale. Participation in research will earn 1 percentage point per hour of documented research participation up to a limit of 5 percentage points, which will be used to adjust the final average.

## Policies

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).
