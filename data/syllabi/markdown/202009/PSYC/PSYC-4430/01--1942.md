## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Jon Kabat-Zinn said that “Mindfulness means paying attention in a particular way: on purpose, in the present moment, and nonjudgmentally.” Throughout the day we tend to spend much of our time thinking about the past and the future. Mindfulness involves giving particular attention to our moment-to-moment experiences in a way that emphasizes observing, rather than judging or evaluating. Everyday activities such as driving a car or eating a meal provide us the opportunity to practice this focused, moment-to-moment attention. In addition, we can practice mindfulness through formal meditation. There is empirical evidence of the many health benefits of mindfulness meditation, including the potential to produce fundamental changes in brain structure and epigenetic change. In this class, we will discuss the historical and psychological foundations of mindfulness and empirical findings supporting its positive effects. Students will participate in structured exercises. This course is a very hands-on approach to mindfulness…you will be required to practice mindfulness and meditation and develop your mindful skills. It is also more of a discussion course.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Students will learn what mindfulness is and will practice mindfulness daily.
- Students will apply mindfulness to activities such as eating, exercising, driving a car, and interacting with others.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

1. **Maya Angelou’s *Letter to My Daughter***

Discussion and paper due Nov 3rd

**10 points** Participate in class discussion (and write a 2 page paper) highlighting how her book affected you and how it changed (or reinforced, or didn’t change) how you think about life. Submit on LMS

2. **Review of Holly Roger’s *The Mindful Twenty Something* book**

**30 points** Participate in class discussion (and write a 1 page summary of each of the 5 sections) highlighting how her book affected you and how it changed (or reinforced, or didn’t change) how you think about life. Review is due before class begins. Submit on LMS. Each worth 6 pts.

**The summaries are due the same day as the discussions:**

- Parts 1 and 2 due September 15th
- Part 3 due September 22nd
- Part 4 due September 29th
- Part 5 due October 9th

3. **Koru App Daily Log**

**20 points** There are daily log opportunities in the app. Make sure you complete 5 of those each week. You only need to write a few sentences daily.

4. ***Leadership and Self-Deception*** **(Discussion Oct 23rd)**

**10 points** Participate in class discussion and share 1 demonstration of a situations where you were in the box and the outcomes of that, and 1 demonstration of a situation where you were out of the box and the outcomes of that. Be prepared to speak for about 5 minutes. Your grade is your class participation of your examples/demonstrations.

5. **Presentation and Paper**

**30 points** (15 points presentation, 15 points paper) Choose a topic related to mindfulness and write a 2-3 page paper, with 12pt Times New Roman font, double spaced. Submit all parts of this assignment on LMS.

Topic is due September 18th

1st Draft is due October 23rd

2nd Draft is due November 6th

Final paper is due November 20th

Submit all assignments on LMS
