## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students who successfully complete this course: - will be able to communicate effectively through visual design using design elements to create engaging and well conceived images. - will demonstrate the ability to discuss articulately their own and other students’ work through group discussions and critiques of student work. - will be able to discuss and critically analyze works of art from the history of art and contemporary art and to apply concepts and strategies of pictorial design that they have learned to their own work. will demonstrate their understanding of design elements by the production of a well-designed, high quality, coherent portfolio to be presented at the end of the semester

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Weekly hands-on homework projects, using either traditional or electronic media, will be complemented by in-class slide presentations, videos, discussions and critiques.

Homework assignments will be issued each Tuesday to be due and reviewed the following Friday. Weekly reading assignments and online lecture viewing will be due on Tuesdays in preparations for in class discussions. Students will also gather three found images relating to the weekly theme to present to the class each Tuesday. It is essential that you keep up with reading and homework assignments so that you can participate fully in reviews and discussions. Tuesday sessions will be used to discuss readings and to present additional images, texts and video to augment the readings.
