## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

As members of the Fusion Music Ensemble, students will be able to gain technical skills and acquire musical experience as part of a cutting-edge project for redefining musical creativity and social cohesion in an age of social distancing and remote instruction. The ensemble will have a flexible format that will allow students to participate in large-group performances and to pursue their own musical interests in smaller sub-groups. The rehearsal process will continue to adapt to accommodate the demands of remote instruction and social distancing, but will at least involve one-on-one meetings with the instructor so that repertoire and format can be responsive to students’ unique interests and learning situations, small-group real time coachings and instruction, and large-group meetings to discuss practice and rehearsal strategies and to critically assess past work. “Live performance” will be reimagined to include asynchronous individually-tracked recordings, remotely implemented real-time performances, and socially-distanced or multi-spatial live performances. In addition to the possibilities for musical growth and experimentation, the Fusion Music Ensemble will also provide the opportunity to have fun and create social bonds with fellow members of the Rensselaer community.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will develop their skills for playing music in a variety of ensemble configurations.
- Students will explore different modes of technologically mediated musical performance.
- Students will expand their technical and collaborative prowess as musicians, both individually and as participants in a group.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Rehearsal Participation:** The most important part of membership in this ensemble is focused, active participation in scheduled rehearsals. As the collective thriving of the group is the ultimate goal, students are encouraged to contribute their energies and talents in pursuit of that goal.

**Collegiality and Team Spirit:** It is my firm belief that ensembles are at their most vibrant when powered by a groundswell of energy. Students are encouraged to lift each other up and collaboratively support the group’s music-making.

**Preparation:** The music that we make in this ensemble will require at-home practice. Students will be expected to come to rehearsal prepared.

**Timely Submission of Recorded Materials**: As an ensemble that will be making music using a variety of remote and asynchronous collaborative methods, it is vital that all participants record and submit their parts on time.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

As a hybrid course, students will be participating both in person and remotely. For on-campus students, the assumption is that you will attend courses in person. Of course, given the extreme unpredictability of the world in the time of COVID, unexpected events are bound to occur. Unlike most academic courses, a musical ensemble like the Fusion Ensemble depends on the active participation of everyone involved. With that in mind, you are strongly encouraged to make all efforts to be present (in person or remotely) at rehearsals. If you cannot for some reason, please communicate with the instructor as early as possible.

## Other Course-Specific Information

### Additional Course Information:

**COVID Policies**

The following policies have been mandated by Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute for the Fall 2020 semester.

**Masks/Shields:** We know from existing data that wearing a mask in public can help prevent the spread of COVID-19 in the community. Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute has determined that everyone will be required to wear a face mask in all public spaces, including classrooms. You MUST wear a mask appropriately (i.e., covering both your mouth and nose) in the building if you are attending class in person. Masks have been provided for students, instructors, and staff, and everyone is expected to wear one. Students who choose not to wear a mask may not attend class in person. This is to protect their health and safety as well as the health and safety of their classmates, instructor, and the university community. Anyone attending class in person without a mask will be asked to put one on or leave.

Instructors will end class if anyone present refuses to appropriately wear a mask for the duration of class. Students who refuse to wear masks appropriately or adhere to other stated requirements may face disciplinary action for Code of Conduct violations. Student who violate not wearing a mask will be reported to the Dean of Students and will be requested to leave a classroom of building and return to their living quarters. The Dean of Students will provide the appropriate sanctions for the students per the code of conduct signed by the students.

**Traffic Flow and Social Distancing**: Students and faculty will respect the need for social distancing to the degree possible by the setting. Please maintain six feet of space while walking into and out of classes and enter and exiting the building.

Faculty and students will move in and out of the classroom as per the appropriate instructions of the faculty/administration. They are expected to follow printed traffic flow statements posted in all rooms and buildings.

**In-Class Seating**: Faculty are asked to assure that students sit in the appropriate designated seating in the classroom, using social distancing. Students are not allowed to move furniture or sit in seats not designated by the Institute.

**Cleaning of Spaces**: Students are encouraged to clean the surfaces of the chairs/tables/desks they occupy before they sit down and as they prepare to leave.

**Student Health:** On a case-by-case basis, students may consult with Student Disability Resources for accommodations if they cannot wear a mask. Students requiring such accommodations may be advised to take advantage of and participate in the course through remote learning.

Students who are experiencing COVID-19 related symptoms should not attend class in person and are encouraged to contact a health care provider. Students who are ill, under quarantine for COVID-19, or suspect they are ill will report that to Student Life. Student Life will verify and notify all faculty who have that student. Once notification is made, all faculty will make every reasonable effort to accommodate the student’s absence and will communicate that accommodation directly to the student. Failure to make an appropriate accommodation for a verified or reasonably suspected case of illness may be appealable under the student grade appeal process. Students who need to report an illness should contact Office of the Dean of Student. They may also call: 518-276-6266.

**Refusal:** Refusal to comply with any appropriate request will be treated as would any classroom disruption (request to change the behavior; request to leave the class; dismissal of the class and referral to Student Affairs.)
