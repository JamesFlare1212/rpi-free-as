## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Upon completion of this course, a student’s progress will be measured by their ability to: • Complete and fulfill required assignments. • Research and write a critical analysis of an existing graphic novel. • Come to class prepared: with all necessary materials and with assignments completed and, if required, duplicated for class or small group critiques. • Constructively contribute to discussions and workshops; demonstrate the ability to work independently, seek help when it’s needed, and exhibit self-motivation.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Several assignment modules will be given during the semester. These assignments will reflect work covered in class.

This course will encompass the following:

- Reading and viewing professional pieces, including comic book and graphic novels and animated films with careful attention to content and technique.
- Class discussions of student work.
- Exercises and explorations geared to strengthen students’ powers of imagination, inventiveness, basic illustration techniques, and comprehension of the choices available to the graphic storyteller.
- Note: Studio assignments are to be completed on a cross-media basis, depending on the skills of the student. Assignments may be completed as hand-drawings, computer illustrations, collage, photography, or any image-making technique a student may choose. For the purposes of critique and evaluation, projects must be turned in via a hardcopy/physical/paper format.
