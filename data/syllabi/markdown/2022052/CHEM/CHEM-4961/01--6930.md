## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will learn to watch the selected movies with an eye towards deciphering what is scientifically possible. Each movie has aspects of chemistry, so the student will be asked to go into detail regarding what is scientifically accurate and what is not.
- Students will be given worksheets designed to probe the validity of the science portrayed in each movie, and the student will provide free responses to these questions citing relevant scientific principles.
- Students will determine if the scientific method is employed in the movie, and if so, how.
- Students will be asked to find a movie which includes aspects of chemistry and construct a worksheet for the movie similar to the worksheets used for each of the 6 movies.
