## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Jon Kabat-Zinn said that “Mindfulness means paying attention in a particular way: on purpose, in the present moment, and nonjudgmentally.” Throughout the day we tend to spend much of our time thinking about the past and the future. Mindfulness involves giving particular attention to our moment-to-moment experiences in a way that emphasizes observing, rather than judging or evaluating. Everyday activities such as driving a car or eating a meal provide us the opportunity to practice this focused, moment-to-moment attention. In addition, we can practice mindfulness through formal meditation. There is empirical evidence of the many health benefits of mindfulness meditation, including the potential to produce fundamental changes in brain structure and epigenetic change. In this class, we will discuss the historical and psychological foundations of mindfulness and empirical findings supporting its positive effects. Students will participate in structured exercises. This course is a very hands-on approach to mindfulness…you will be required to practice mindfulness and meditation and develop your mindful skills. It is also more of a discussion course.

Discussion and paper due June 21st

**10 points** Participate in class discussion (and write a 3-4 page paper) highlighting how her book affected you and how it changed (or reinforced, or didn’t change) how you think about life. Submit on LMS

2. **Review of Holly Roger’s *The Mindful Twenty Something* book**

**30 points** Participate in class discussion (and write a 1-2 page summary of each of the 4 sections) highlighting how her book affected you and how it changed (or reinforced, or didn’t change) how you think about life. THIS IS NOT A SUMMARY. This is a self-reflection based on the reading. It is due before class begins. Submit on LMS. **The summaries are due the same day as the discussions:**

- Parts 1 and 2 due May 28th (9 pts)
- Part 3 due June 4th (7 pts)
- Part 4 due June 11th (7pts)
- Part 5 due June 18th (7pts)

3. **Koru App Daily Log**

**10 points** There are daily log opportunities in the app. Make sure you complete 20 days of reflections for 4 weeks during the time we will be discussing this book. Complete 2-3 things you are grateful for each day, and then reflect on the meditation you did that day. Questions you could answer: Which meditation did you do? How did you feel before and after the meditation? How did the meditation impact you?

This assignment is completed in the Koru app.

You can start May 24th and end June 25th.

4. ***Leadership and Self-Deception*** **(Discussion June 17th)**

**10 points** Participate in class discussion and share 1 demonstration of a situation where you were in the box and the outcomes of that, and 1 demonstration of a situation where you were out of the box and the outcomes of that. Be prepared to speak for about 5 minutes. Your grade is your class participation of your examples/demonstrations.

For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own.
