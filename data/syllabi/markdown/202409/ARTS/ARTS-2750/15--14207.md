## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**Course Description**

Private piano lessons are provided to students in a wide variety of skill/experience levels. This course aims to improve student’s musicality as well as technical command of the piano.

Students are expected to prepare their assigned repertoire and technical exercises to present and refine at the weekly scheduled lessons. Repertoire is drawn from the broad possible view of the canon of literature, including traditionally accepted masterworks as well as experimental, non-traditional, or recently composed pieces. The repertoire will be focused closely for the level and needs of each individual student.

There will be 15 lessons over the course of the semester, which can include a final jury at the end of the semester. Private lessons will have a duration of 50 minutes.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

**Required Course Materials**

Students are required to bring sheet music of repertoire they are working on to every lesson. Instructor will advise each student to obtain their own music outside the class, or provide a scanned material that the student can print out.

**Additional Course Materials**

YouTube & Naxos Digital Library. Find various interpretations of the repertoire you are learning/performing. Instructor will also recommend some recordings to the student over the course of semester. Consider *purchasing* a copy of CDs or audio files of your favorite performances. All of this will help your growth as a musician.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Students taking private piano lessons will improve their ability to read, discuss, interpret, and perform a plethora of music composed solo piano.

Students will be able to expressively and musically interpret written music in playing the piano.

Students will further mastery of the piano as their instrument.

Students will be able to articulate and discuss stylistic differences between repertoires of different eras, as well as discuss the historical contexts in which these differences came to exist.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

The private lesson will be tailored to individual student to maximize the student's musical and technical growth as a pianist and a musician.  
In addition to attending all scheduled lessons, engaging fully and creatively in your own practice, cultivating piano repertoire, as well as improving sight-reading skills and musicianship is expected.

Students are expected to learn at least three pieces in different styles during the semester. The typical practice obligation for this course is, but not limited to, 60-90 minutes per day outside of lessons. If your level of practice does not meet your instructor’s expectations, it is advised that you have a conference about practice techniques and expectations.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students in the private lesson program will improve their ability to read, discuss, interpret, and perform a plethora of music composed for their individual instrument.
- Students will be able to expressively and musically interpret a variety of musical works/styles/genres.
- Students will further mastery of their chosen instrument.
- Students will improve listening skills, as well as mature in their ability to work at once independently.

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own.
