## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An introductory course overviewing the topics of wear, friction and lubrication. Earlier portions of the course will focus on fluid film hydrodynamic lubrication, with applications to hydrostatic, thrust and journal bearings. The balance of the course focuses on contact mechanics, conduction of frictional heat, surface topography, and resultant wear behavior of unlubricated contacts.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Upon completion of this course students will be able to: estimate temperature rises on bearing surfaces due to frictional heating at both the macro- and roughness asperity-scale;
- predict contact stresses on bearing surfaces at both the macro- and roughness asperity-scale;
- assess the predominance of various wear mechanisms at given frictional temperature and contact stress conditions;
- calculate fluid film thicknesses in hydrodynamically lubricated bearings and assess whether they are sufficient to maintain separation of rough bearing surfaces.

Each student’s lowest HW grade will be dropped.

**Exams:** Three exams will be given during the semester, the first two being in class on October 17 and November 14 with the third exam to be scheduled by the Registrar during Finals Week anytime from December 13 to possibly as late as December 19. Each exam will cover only material from the preceding two homeworks. Exams will be of 110 minute duration and will be closed book, though two sheets (each front and back) of handwritten crib notes will be permitted. Each exam will offer the student three problems, from which they may select any two upon which to submit work. Each student’s lowest exam will be weighted to only 10% of their course grade, while the higher two exams will be weighted to 20%.

**Quizzes:** There will be four multiple-choice quizzes on readings assigned from the required textbook with dates on September 12 and 30, October 31 and November 11. Each quiz’s corresponding reading assignment will be announced during the preceding meeting of the class.
