## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An introductory course on wind energy: wind turbine subsystem design and underlying theory, power plant siting and economics, applications and potential for wind turbines.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Wind Energy Explained, 2nd ed., by J.F. Manwell, et al., John Wiley & Sons, 2009.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Topics:

1. Wind resources and assessment
2. Wind turbine aerodynamics
3. Wind turbine mechanics
4. Wind turbine components and material selection
5. Electricity generation, transmission, and storage
6. Design and validation
7. Wind turbine controls
8. Wind turbine siting and integration
9. Applications
10. Wind energy economics

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Gain an understanding of the breadth of disciplines needed for the design and application of wind turbines
- • Gain an understanding of wind energy terminology
- • Demonstrate an ability to apply previously-learned math, science, and engineering fundamentals to a new topic
- • Learn how to assess a proposed site for its energy potential, environmental considerations, and ability to meet local energy needs
- • Learn how to design and model wind turbines
- • Learn how to integrate wind energy plant components
- • Learn how to estimate the cost of electricity for a wind turbine power plant

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

A problem set (homework) will be assigned for each topic (module).  
There will be one mid-term exam, covering Modules 1-4 and 6.  
There will be a project assigned at the completion of Module 4 and due at the end of the semester.
