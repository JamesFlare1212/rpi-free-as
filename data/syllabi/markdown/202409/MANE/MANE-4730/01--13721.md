## Course Goals

### Goals:

**Course Objectives**

This course is an introduction to heat transfer. The course will discuss the three modes of heat transfer - Conduction, Convection, and Radiation. Apart from learning the fundamental aspects of heat transfer, the course also emphasizes on applying this knowledge to solve a wide variety of engineering problems.

**Project:**

Students will be assigned a course project involving a design problem, requiring utilizing heat transfer knowledge gained during the semester. The report for the project will be due on the last day of instruction for the semester. Each student must turn in the class project.

**Project Description: As an intern at a company deploying solar farms in a hot region, you have been tasked to design a heat sink intended for a photovoltaic (PV) panel. With an area of 1 m², this panel is subjected to intense solar illumination of 1000 W/m² applied perpendicular to the panel. Your mission is to ensure the heat sink can effectively reduce the panel's operating temperature to below 40°C in Jackson, Mississippi, during summer. The panel has an efficiency of 20%. Consequently, assuming no reflection, 80% of the incident solar energy is converted to heat. Consider the ambient temperature as the average temperature of July and August, approximately 34°C, and perform the calculations for the worst-case scenario, namely steady-state operation and a calm day.**

**A team of two students** can work on this project. The heat sink design should be appropriate to meet the specifications (i.e., it is not overdesigned nor under-designed), the design approach should be correct, and the project should be completed and turned in by the due date provided in the course calendar.

**Plagiarism**: Plagiarism is defined as representing the work or words of another as one's own through the omission of acknowledgment or reference.
