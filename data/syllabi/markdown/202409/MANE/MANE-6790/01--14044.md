## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Comprehensive treatment of conduction and convection heat transfer. Emphasis is on physical and mathematical modeling of heat transfer phenomena with applications in engineering systems. Analytical and computational solution methods are discussed. The class is centered around a semester long project.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

*Heat Transfer* by Gregory Nellis and Sanford Klein, Cambridge University Press

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Analyze simple systems subjected to conduction heat transfer using analytical and numerical methods
- Demonstrate an understanding of boundary layer theory for forced and natural convection
- Analyze simple systems subjected to convection heat transfer using analytical and numerical methods
- Select simplifying assumptions and analyze, numerically and/or analytically, complex thermal transport systems, subjected to combined heat transfer modes.
- Demonstrate professional conduct and adequate project contribution when part of an engineering team
- MANE 6790 only: Perform advanced analysis such as system level, optimization studies or combined steady-state/transient heat transfer for the design of thermal systems.
- MANE 6790 only Present the analysis, results and recommendations in a technical report.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

In class assignments, homework, semester long project
