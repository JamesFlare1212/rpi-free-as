## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Analyze the performance of a helicopter in hover, vertical climb and descent, and forward flight
- Identify what aspects of a helicopter’s design affect which aspects of performance, and how
- Understand the principles behind the control of a helicopter, and the relationship between different control inputs and aircraft response
- Write computer code that applies models discussed in class
- Identify several different classes of helicopter and identify relative advantages and disadvantages of each

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

There will be two in-class exams with take-home problems. There will also be an optional final exam.  
There will be 4-6 homework assignments throughout the semester, with additional problem(s) relative to the undergraduate section.
