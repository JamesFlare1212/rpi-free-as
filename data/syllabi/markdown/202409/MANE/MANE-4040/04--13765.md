## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Laboratory experience to complement Elements of Mechanical Design (MANE-4030). Includes aspects of dimensional measuring and tolerancing; Stress and strain measurement in tension and bending; Contact mechanics; Sliding friction and wear characterization; Shafts and gears; Reverse engineering of a mechanical assembly. This course is 2 credit-hours.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

No new technical text required specifically for this class. It is assumed that each student already has access to current EMD text (J. A. Collins, H. R. Busby & G. H. Staab, Mechanical Design of Machine Elements and Machines, 2nd edition, Wiley (2010) ISBN - 13 978-0-470-4130306. Laboratory instructions will be made available with each new lab.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Develop hands-on abilities to conduct mechanical engineering system testing
- Predict behavior prior to experimentation so that hypotheses may be tested and identify sources of discrepancy
- Critically evaluate multiple means of measurement and their discrepancies considering sources of error
- Consider the design of experiments and how to modify the experimental design to overcome limitations
- Learn about the role of experimentation and analysis in the design process

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Learning outcomes will be evaluated through a series of assessments divided into:

- Six lab reports: Describe experiences, data and results collected throughout each of the experiments conducted in the lab.
- Six homework assignments: Preparatory exercises to ensure students are familiar with the concepts to be verified through experimentation.
- One comprehensive quiz: Individual component of the grade, designed to test knowledge retention of each student.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Attendance is mandatory, missed labs must be made up. Failure to make up for missed labs will result in an automatic Incomplete final grade.

### Other Course Policies:

Attendance is required, at your regularly scheduled weekly time, so that your entire lab group may work together with continuity. You will be allowed one unexcused absence without penalty; however, you will be required to make-up that week’s lost laboratory activity at another time. A second unexcused absence will cause a 2.5 point penalty on your overall course percent grade. The severity of the additional penalty for each subsequent absence will increase by 2.5 points, i.e., a third absence will sum to a 7.5 points penalty in total for absences.  
Late arrivals to class by more than 15 minutes will be considered equivalent to an unexcused absence and will need to be made up by attending another lab section for the portion missed.
