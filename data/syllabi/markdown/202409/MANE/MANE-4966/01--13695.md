## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- A student who finishes this course in a satisfactory manner will be able to demonstrate: i) an ability to apply the engineering design process to produce solutions that meet multiple constraints, ii) an ability to recognize what steps are required to expedite translation from lab to market, iii) ethical and professional responsibilities of engineers in the context of design and innovation, iv) an ability to communication effectively, v) an ability to acquire and apply new knowledge.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

In-class lectures and examples of each step on the TRL from ideation - design thinking- TRL1, 2, and 3

In-class lectures on Business readiness as well, where I touch on sources of money, steps to create a company, and also talk about licensing a technology to another company

Two basic projects with homework at critical points along the development.

Project 1 - take a patent of interest from the RPI patent portfolio, read, understand, and talk to the inventor about the status. Outline the steps needed to mature the technology including the customer development part where they develop a value proposition matched to a customer type for this invention. Students are required to develop a plan describing how the technology will be matured and translated.

Project 2 - start with a need as expressed by a real outside customer (or it could be an RPI prof) with a need for something to solve a current problem. Interview the person, understand the need and use design thinking to develop ideas. Take these ideas and try to mature them along the TRL
