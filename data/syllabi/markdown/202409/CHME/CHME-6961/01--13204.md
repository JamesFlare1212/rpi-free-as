## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understanding conformations of linear polymers.
- Understanding thermodynamics of polymer solutions and blends.
- Understanding principles of scattering characterizations.
- Understanding viscoelastic properties of polymers.
- Understanding elasticity of gels.
- Understanding glass states of polymers.
- Understanding semi-crystalline states of polymers.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

The learning outcomes of this course will be measured by homework, exams, and class attendance/participation. Students showing active reasoning on the course subjects will receive favorable considerations for the final grades.  
EXAM Dates and Materials:

- Mid-term Exam 1 (September 30, Monday): Chapter 1, 6, and 7
- Mid-term Exam 2 (November 4, Monday): Chapter 1, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10.

All exams will be given during regular class hours. Closed book and notes. Only pencil or pen, eraser, and simple calculator are allowed. Graduate students registered at CHME-6961 may have extra a problem(s).
