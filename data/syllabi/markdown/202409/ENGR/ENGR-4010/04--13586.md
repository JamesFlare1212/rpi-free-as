## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The PD: Leadership Competencies course complements Professional Development 1 / Introduction to Engineering Design by providing a model of professional leadership which students may apply while determining their future after graduation and in their work as new engineering professionals. Through experiential learning, students are exposed to professional skills including ethical decision-making, critical thinking, and tools to succeed in a diverse organizational culture. The interactive learning approach, in addition to discussions, exams, and presentations, is designed to promote further development of students’ leadership abilities.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

**Learning Objectives:**

At the end of PD: Leadership Competencies, students will be able to:

- incorporate philosophical and professional ethical principles into their leadership practices in order to develop personal ethical guidelines
- utilize critical thinking skills to analyze professional scenarios and determine proper course of action
- identify their strengths and weaknesses regarding leadership development competencies and articulate a plan for growth
- recognize the impact of social identities and social inequalities in different professional environments
- apply leadership and professional development competencies in their professional/academic practice

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Classroom attendance is required for this course. Attendance will be taken at the beginning of class. Students are expected to be on time and remain for the full class period. Attendance is critical for success in PDLC.It is the student’s responsibility to contact the instructor and to obtain any course materials missed due to excused or unexcused absences. Beginning the first week of class, students with two (2) or more unexcused absences may fail PDLC. **Please note that an unexcused absence on a presentation/simulation day will result in an automatic 10-point deduction from your final course grade.** **Presentation days are defined as class meetings where ANY student and/or group of students in the class are scheduled to present to the whole/part of the class (e.g. ALL Cultural Competency presentation days, Leadership Simulation days, etc.).** There are four (4) designated Presentation Days during the semester (see schedule).

## Other Course-Specific Information

### Additional Course Information:

**Valuing Diversity & Inclusive Classroom Community**

This course is intended to reach students from diverse backgrounds and perspectives - all are welcome here. The diversity students bring to class, across gender, sexuality, age, disability, socio-economic status, ethnicity, race, religion, and learning needs is valued as an important asset and strength in the learning process.

We will have many opportunities in this class to explore challenging issues and increase our understanding of differing perspectives. Our conversations and structured dialogues may not always be easy, and we will need patience and compassion, and a foundation grounded in trust and respect for others. Therefore, an additional aim of this course is to increase our capacity for these kinds of conversations, as this is a valuable skill in both scholarship and in life.
