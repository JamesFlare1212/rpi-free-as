## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course focuses on conveying fundamental physiological processes and their mechanisms of action in human and higher mammalian organisms. It emphasizes the control and interaction of physiological systems such as the muscle, nervous, circulatory, renal, respiratory, digestive, reproductive and hormonal systems.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

```
Students will be able to recall, integrate, and write about the basic processes that regulate the major physiological systems of the body. How well they have learned the material will be evaluated by multi-format exams. Students will demonstrate a basic understanding of how physiology experiments are performed by collecting and analyzing simulated experimental data. Students will be able to interpret data and relate data to physiological concepts discussed in lecture which will be evaluated by the quality of their written studio reports. Students will be able to state/explain/describe the major anatomical features of the human body which will be evaluated by a test 1/3 of the way through the course.
```

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Studio class attendance is required for all students for the entire class period (you cannot leave early except when given permission by the instructor or TAs). The exceptions to this rule are: 1) the studio immediately prior to Thanksgiving break or 2) that have a legitimate conflict (e.g., participating in an RPI Athletic event) for which you have received permission from me in advance. Missing a studio class or leaving early may result in points being deducted from your grade for that studio. One reason for the required attendance is that there will be class discussions/presentations at the end of all the studio classes (except the anatomy studios). The studio group that presents and leads the discussion will receive an automatic 10/10 grade for that studio. More information about studio class is available on LMS and will be provided during our first studio class.

### Other Course Policies:

**Questions regarding exam grading will be considered only if your concerns are submitted to me in writing prior to the next exam.** You need an official excuse from your Class Dean to make up an exam if you miss an exam (e.g. illness). Notifying me in advance you will miss the exam is helpful but does not replace an official excused absence.
