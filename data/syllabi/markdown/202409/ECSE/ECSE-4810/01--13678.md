## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course introduces both the theories for learning and inference with both directed and undirected PGMs, including Bayesian Networks, Markov Random Fields, Hidden Markov Models, and Dynamic Bayesian Networks, as well as their applications in machine learning and causal discovery and inference.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Probability Calculus, Markov Condition, D-Separation, V-structure, Sum-Product Inference, Max-Product Inference, Belief Propagation, Mean Field Variational Inference, MCMC/Gibbs sampling, Score-based BN learning, Dynamic Bayesian Networks, HMM Learning (Baum-Welch) and Inference (Viterbi), Markov Random Field, Conditional Random Field, Gaussian Graphical Models, Restricted Boltzmann Machine, Latent Dirichlet Allocation, Structure Causal Models, Do-calculus, Interventional Inference, Counterfactual Inference, Confounder, Spurious Correlations, and Model Generalization.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Student understands the theories that underpin different types of probabilistic graphical models •
- Students can identify and explain the important methods for graphical model learning and inference
- Student can successfully apply graphical models to applications of interest.

The assessment measures include homework, class projects, midterm exam, and the final project.
