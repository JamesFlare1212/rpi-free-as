### Prerequisites or Other Requirements:

Analysis and simulation of linear electric circuits.

**By Topic:** Basic electrical engineering laws such as Kirchhoff’s current and voltage law.

Resistive and energy-storage elements.

Controlled voltage and current Sources.

Systematic analysis of operational amplifiers.

AC steady state, transients, frequency response.

Laplace transform and computer-aided methods.

**Prerequisite** ECSE 2010 (Electric Circuits) and (PHYS 1200, Physics II or PHYS 1250, Introductory Electromagnetic Theory)

## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Description in course catalog: The physics and operation of semiconductor diodes, bipolar junction transistors, and field-effect transistors in elementary analog circuits. Non-ideal operational amplifier characteristics. Amplifier biasing, small-signal analysis, and frequency response. Elementary bipolar and MOSFET digital circuits.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate circuit analysis techniques for ideal and non-ideal op amps.
- Analyze diode circuits using large and small signal analysis
- Perform DC analysis for MOS and BJT amplifiers
- Analyze MOS amplifiers (Common-source, common-gate and common-drain). Find gain, input and output impedances
- Analyze BJT amplifiers (Common-emitter, common-collector, and common-base). Find gain, input and output impedances
- Analyze Differential amplifiers to estimate differential and common-mode gain and impedances
- Explain the fundamentals of digital circuits

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Significant laboratory component is present where students work in small groups (2-3 members) to collect, analyze, and interpret data from experiments involving key electronic devices such as diodes, BJTs, and MOSFETs. Students are assigned to present a detailed analysis of at least one of these devices.  
Students will learn ways to design circuits to minimize the effect of the device variabilities. For example, students will identify better circuits that minimize the data variabilities on circuit performance. They will also perform sensitivity analysis of the output on the data like threshold and transconductance.

### Other Course Policies:

**To maintain accurate course records, follow the following guidelines:**

- Print your name clearly on all papers.
- To submit a paper for re-grading, attach a note and give it to the TA who will be responsible for the grading. Do not make any changes to the work, but describe the discrepancies in an attached note. Do this no later than *one week* after the paper was returned.
