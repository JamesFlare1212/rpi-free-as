## Course Goals

### Goals:

1. To learn design techniques of analog integrated circuits in standard CMOS technology.
2. Apply analog IC design techniques by performing the electrical design and simulation of an operational amplifier OPAMP or analog circuit of similar complexity in an industrial environment (Cadence).

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1.Understand the operating regions of NMOS and PMOS field effect transistors 2.Analyze and design single stage and differential CMOS amplifiers 3. Perform frequency analysis of an electronic circuit 4. Design a 2-stage, telescopic and cascode integrated CMOS operational amplifiers

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

1.Understand the operating regions of NMOS and PMOS field effect transistors (HW1, Test1)  
2.Analyze and design single stage and differential CMOS amplifiers (HW2, HW3, Test1, Project)  
3\. Perform frequency analysis of an electronic circuit ((HW4 and Test2)  
4\. Design simple, telescopic and cascode integrated CMOS operational amplifiers (Design project: 30% of the grade)
