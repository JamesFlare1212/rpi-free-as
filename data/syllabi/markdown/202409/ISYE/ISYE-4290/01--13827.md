## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course provides an introduction to discrete-event simulation (DES), a numerical approach that uses computer software to model complex, real-world systems, particularly those that are challenging to analyze through traditional analytical methods. We will begin with foundational topics such as random number generation, random variates, queuing theory, and input/output analysis for both discrete and continuous cases.

The course also emphasizes key aspects of simulation modeling, including effective techniques for ensuring accuracy through verification and validation, as well as robust statistical analysis to interpret simulation results. Hands-on practice will be provided through the use of FlexSim (www.flexsim.com), Excel, and Python, allowing students to apply theoretical concepts to practical scenarios. Throughout the course, we will explore applications of DES in healthcare and manufacturing systems, demonstrating how simulation can be used to optimize processes, improve efficiency, and support decision-making in these critical industries.

By the end of the course, students will be equipped to develop complex simulation models, design and execute simulation experiments, analyze and interpret results, and effectively manage simulation projects in both healthcare and manufacturing contexts.

All students need to actively participate in discussions to articulate their ideas and share their thoughts.
