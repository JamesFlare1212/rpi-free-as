## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Practicum in mentoring new students in Physics with focus on developing Mentor technical leadership skills.  
As a Physics Mentor, the undergraduate student provides leadership for two groups (~8 each) of first year students, and prepares content for sessions every week. The mentoring sessions will address topics such as problem solving skills for Physics, adjusting to life at Rensselaer, and time-management skills.

Returning Mentor act as a role model and providing support to new mentors and as a mentor to first year students. Returning Mentors provide leadership for two groups (~8) of first year students every week covering the same topics. Returning Mentors are expected to lend support to Senior mentors during the training of New Mentors.  
Senior Mentors are role models to all other mentors and to a group of first year students (~10 students). Senior Mentors are expected to train and supervise the New and Returning Mentors.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- All: Display and develop proper facilitation of the classroom and working groups.
- PHYS 2160: Demonstrate an ability to assist peers and faculty members.
- PHYS 2160: Communicate openly and honestly with faculty and staff with respect to student issues or issues with regard to performing duties.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

New mentors (PHYS 2160) are expected to:

1. Actively prepare problems and methods of facilitation for weekly mentoring sessions.
2. Fully participate in all mentor sessions, weekly meetings, training sessions, and mentoring functions.
3. Check-in with mentees, offer help if needed, and point students in need towards appropriate resources.
4. Discuss progress and state of mind of mentees. Offer help if appropriate. Point students in need toward resources. Apprise Student Life staff or Faculty of students in need.
5. Take attendance at weekly mentoring sessions and promptly report absences to senior mentors and faculty/staff.
6. Be a role model and a resource for mentees.

Returning mentors (PHYS 4160) are expected to:

1. Actively prepare problems and methods of facilitation for weekly mentoring sessions.
2. Fully participate in all mentor sessions, weekly meetings, training sessions, and mentoring functions.
3. Check-in with mentees, offer help if needed, and point students in need towards appropriate resources.
4. Discuss progress and state of mind of mentees. Offer help if appropriate. Point students in need toward resources. Apprise Student Life staff or Faculty of students in need.
5. Take attendance at weekly mentoring sessions and promptly report absences to senior mentors and faculty/staff.
6. Be a role model and a resource for mentees.
7. Be a role model and a resource for new mentors.

Senior mentors (PHYS 4161) are expected to:

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

**Grading**: It is expected that the Mentor will diligently fulfill the expectations outlined and will earn an A for their contributions. Minor issues will be discussed promptly with Mentors with explicit recommendations for improvement. If the student takes suitable action there will be no consequence to the grade. If a student fails to correct a serious issue, such as failing to meet his or her student group more than once without making arrangements for a substitute, the grade may be lowered. The student will be asked to resign or receive a lower grade for more serious issues.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Mentors are expected to fully participate in mentor sessions, weekly staff meetings, and training sessions. This means that attendance is required at all of the regular scheduled sessions during the course of the semester. If a student is unable to attend a session due to a planned conflict, they must coordinate with the instructor and find suitable substitutes for their sessions. In the event of an unplanned absence, for example due to illness, the students must reach out to the instructor as soon as possible so the instructor can find suitable substitutes. Unexcused absences on more than 1 occasion will result in a lowered grade or asking the mentor to step down from their role.
