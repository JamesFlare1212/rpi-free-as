## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An examination of the cultural impact of advertising in various media: TV, radio, print, the Internet. This course will address the following kinds of questions: How does advertising inform our experience and identity? How has it shaped our culture? Who pays for it and why?

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Nicholas Holm. Advertising and Consumer Society. 2nd edition.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The primary goal of this course is to deepen awareness of what is currently a huge and largely unexamined cultural phenomenon affecting all of our lives. Advertisements are approached as texts that reveal the cultural attitudes, ideologies, and conventions of their era.

By the end of this course, you should be able to:

- perform detailed textual analyses of specific ads
- create ads as a method of analysis
- demonstrate an understanding of the broader impact of advertising on consumer culture.

These outcomes will be assessed through performance in class discussion, projects, and formal essays.
