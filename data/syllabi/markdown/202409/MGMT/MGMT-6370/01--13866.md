## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The purpose of this course is to provide an introduction to second generation financial instruments including forward and futures contracts, options, futures options and swaps on a variety of underlying instruments including fixed income securities.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

REQUIRED TEXT: Options, Futures & Other Derivatives by John C Hull. Pearson Education Group; 11th edition

## Course Goals

### Goals:

1\. Forwards and futures.  
2\. Hedging and basis risk, interest rate, and stock-index derivatives.  
3\. Financial management applications of derivatives.  
4\. Introduction to options.  
5\. Pricing models and strategies.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Define the roles of different derivative markets, instruments and participants.
- Create models for pricing risk under different markets – interest rate, commodity, equity, credit.
- Apply option pricing methodologies.
- Develop hedging strategies using derivatives.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

COURSE ASSESSMENT MEASURES  
Homework: There will be homework assignments assigned. These assignments will be a mix of descriptive, mathematical, and quantitative exercises.  
Exams: Two exams will be held in the semester. First exam will be halfway in the course and the second exam at the end of the term. The second exam will be cumulative, with emphasis on the recent material.
