## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course gives students an in depth understanding of the strategic issues involved in managing technology and innovation within firms. Additionally this course also gives students exposure to the implications of a select set of emerging technologies that are tectonically impacting the business landscape. Through this course, students will:

1. Develop an understanding of how technologies and industries evolve over time, and the organizational, sociological and economic foundations that underpin technological change.
2. Be exposed to the constraints that arise in mature firms in adapting and reacting to disruptive technologies.
3. Gain an appreciation of how various elements such as markets, customer characteristics, and value creation/value capture interact to influence firm behavior in technologically intensive environments.
4. Understand the role of eco systems and platforms
5. Develop insight into the organizational and economic effects of emerging technologies impacting the business landscape, including Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and other developments such as Blockchain.

The course is relevant for students interested in managing organizational change and technology strategy in large firms. Additionally, it is relevant for entrepreneurs and managers interested in starting a new venture in a technologically driven industry. The course would also benefit those that are interested in pursuing careers in venture capital, or management consulting.

The course will utilize a combination of articles, cases, team projects, workshops, and presentations. A few of the articles assigned are academic in tenor, and the objective is to expose students to the basic concepts and thinking in the field of technology management. Students are nevertheless expected to understand the main principles of these articles, and how they build on the topics covered in class. Theoretically the course will draw on insights from several disciplines, including industrial organization, strategy, finance, entrepreneurship and organizational behavior.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Describe how technologies evolve over time, and the organizational, sociological and economic factors that affect evolution.
- Identify the constraints that arise in mature firms in adapting and reacting to disruptive technologies, and some potential ways to overcome them
- Explain various types of innovation and how they impact firms and strategy
- Describe the role of the eco system in technological evolution and commercialization, and the impact of various players such as customers, suppliers, complementors
- Frame how emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence and machine learning are reshaping the business world in practical and strategic terms

**Class Participation**

Class contribution is an essential and significant component of evaluation. In order to facilitate a constructive discussion, please go through all the readings and cases assigned for a particular session (see the class schedule below). You should be able to offer insights into the central themes being discussed in the session, and outline the main ideas/concepts in each reading and case. Depending upon how the class progresses, I may also assign additional articles from time to time. You are expected to go through these additional readings as well, and come prepared accordingly.

Strong class participation is grounded in the ideas and concepts we cover. General participation that is disconnected from the readings and class discussions will not be rewarded.

In terms of attendance, you are allowed a maximum of two absences throughout the semester, after which two percentage points will be deducted for every additional absence.

Note: Attendance alone will not count toward your class participation grade, and the two will be graded separately. Additionally, you will be required to complete certain assignments (e.g. the Kodak case) which are required for class discussion and will not be graded separately. Failure to complete these required assignments will result in a full letter lower participation grade.

Student athletes- you will be excused from class only with supporting documentation, and for travel etc. for games. Please arrange practice and other schedules so they do not interfere with class attendance.

**Case Analysis- Report and Presentation (Group)**

The case analysis report and presentation accounts for 15% of the final grade. You will be responsible for preparing a written report and making a formal presentation of your analysis and recommendations in class.

The enclosed schedule indicates the date each case is to be presented (apart from the Kodak case, which is a general discussion case for the whole class). Broadly speaking, your presentation will cover the background of the case, the issues identified (see the assignment questions for the cases below), recommendations for the future, and any other points that you think are relevant.

For this assignment you will be required to prepare a report that is no longer than 12 pages, excluding the cover page (please see the guidelines for written reports below). In addition, you will make a presentation in class for a maximum of 20 minutes (guidelines are also provided below for presentations). Note that the report is due the day the case is scheduled to be presented. Please also email me a copy of the slides you intend to use ahead of time. Late submissions will not be accepted.

The cases discussed in class are designed to illustrate specific concepts and decision scenarios. Please therefore do not look up any online material or ‘solutions’ for the case, including ChatGPT. Any attempts to do so will be treated as a serious ethical violation, and in the minimum equivalent to a first offense (see below section on Academic Integrity, and also see ChatGPT policy).

**Brief Report (Group)**

In this report (maximum of 3 pages, excluding the cover page), you are required to identify the central issues in the case, develop a brief analysis, and provide your main recommendations. The brief report is due the day the case is scheduled to be presented, and should be submitted to me ahead of class. Late submissions will not be accepted, and failure to turn in a report on the due date will lead to a lower grade.

**Workshop Assignment (Group)**

The workshop assignment requires you to develop an insightful presentation on an assigned topic (e.g. why are some company cultures good for innovation; what are some current practices to counter bias in AI systems). You will be given brief guidelines ahead of the assignment, and your task is to develop a presentation of about 20 mins as a group. The purpose is to not only get you thinking about the topic, but also to inform the rest of the class and enhance their understanding of the subject. It is essential that you develop an in depth analysis and come well prepared to present on the day that the workshop assignment is scheduled, since you will be playing the lead role in these discussions.

**Innovation Project**

The purpose of this project is threefold. To understand (1) how an emerging technology impacting today’s business landscape is taking shape, (2) what strategies firms in this space are adopting to compete and gain market share, and (3) how incumbent firms and technologies are responding to the threat and fighting back.

Further details on the assignment are provided below. This project has the following deliverables: (1) a choice of technology to be made latest by September 12th , (2) an interim presentation covering Phase I (October 21st, October 24th, October 28th), (3) an interim report due on October 28th covering Phase I, (4) a final presentation covering Phase I feedback and Phase II deliverables (December 5th/ December 8th), (5) a final report covering Phase I feedback and Phase II, due the last day of class (December 8th).

Note: All work related to the Innovation Project is expected to be original work undertaken in this class. Repurposing previous projects or material from another class constitutes a serious ethical violation.

**Quiz (4)**

Four quizzes worth 32.5% will be held on the dates indicated in the course schedule. The quizzes will cover the material discussed in class and the readings, including the cases (specific guidelines will be provided). The emphasis will be on testing your understanding of the material, rather than on how well you remember various concepts. **Please note the dates of the quizzes and ensure you are in class on those dates. Makeups will not be provided, barring very exceptional circumstances (supporting documentation will be needed).**

**Innovation Project Guidelines**

The purpose of this project, broadly speaking, is to understand (1) how an emerging technology impacting today’s business landscape is taking shape, (2) what strategies firms in this space are adopting to compete and gain market share, and (3) how incumbent firms and technologies are responding to the threat and fighting back.

While these are some broad guidelines, modifications may be made based on the specifics of your particular project. The project is divided into two phases. Below are a detailed set of guidelines for each phase.

**Phase I guidelines**

1. Give a brief history and overview of your technology, and how it has evolved up to this point in time. When did your technology first emerge? What were some of the basic principles/innovations/scientific advancements that spurred the technology? Were there any key inventors/firms that pioneered it?

2. Identify the key performance parameters for your technology. Why are they important, and why are they the focus? Can you trace the improvements in these parameters over time (along the lines of our S curve discussions)? You may identify purely technological parameters, and additional ‘market’ based economic parameters, such as costs. In doing so, address the following:
   1. The key innovations that were responsible for the evolution of the technology along these performance parameters at various stages.
   2. Were these innovations incremental, architectural, component, or radical? Were they product innovations or process innovations? Any other way to characterize the innovations taking place that have led to the technology evolving?
   3. Briefly describe how these innovations improved performance and why they were important.

3. What are the main firms that are associated with and pioneering the emerging technology? Are they entrepreneurial firms and startups? Are they established firms? Give some background and brief statistics about them to the extent possible- e.g. when they were founded, sales, their primary industry in terms of SIC/NAICS code. What are their strategic reasons for pursuing this technology? Are there external reasons- such as a large addressable market size and demand? Are there internal reasons- such as pre existing capabilities, competencies, synergies?

1. What are the strategies of these firms- what specific areas are they focusing on to gain an advantage? What R&D investments are they making to gain and advantage? Do you see any differences in approaches and emphases across the various firms (e.g. emphasizing certain parameters over others)? Why are they following these different approaches, and what are the strengths and weaknesses of each?

5. Taking an industry perspective, how would you characterize the stage of evolution of the industry constituting your technology? Is it still in ferment or has it gone through shake out? Is there a discernible dominant design that has emerged? Can you identify key periods of entry and exit based on the number of firms (i.e following the Abernathy/Utterback model)?

6. What supply side network economies and what demand side network economies are relevant to the technology? Are there increasing returns to scale on either side? Can a platform strategy be built around your technology? Are any of the firms pursuing such a platform strategy?

7. Do a detailed analysis of the eco system risks- Who are the key complementors, suppliers in the eco system, and is the eco system to commercialize and scale the technology well established? Are there significant barriers and switching costs to adopt the technology for suppliers, complementors? Are there any significant regulatory concerns, and is government a player? Are there any regulatory concerns that need to be addressed? Are there any standards to be met or established? What other eco system risks do you see?

8. Looking at the customer/marketing aspect, at what stage of evolution is your technology? Who are the main customers? Has it crossed the chasm, or it still early stage? What challenges may arise in crossing the chasm? More generally, what are customers’ switching costs and what factors prevent adoption of the technlogy?

9. Segueing into finance, how are startups in the industry being funded? Are they early stage, VC funded, or public firms? Are they funded by Corporate Venture Capital (CVC)? Or is the funding mainly internal sources in large corporations? Any data on deal sizes, investments made to date?

1. Based on these factors, develop a strengths/weaknesses assessment of the different firms. Who is likely to develop a sustained competitive advantage in your opinion and win in this particular technology? Is it likely to be a winner take all market where only one firm is likely to survive and thrive, or can multiple firms co exist at the same time?

**Phase II guidelines**

11. Conduct an initial patent search related to your technology. Identify a few key patents that underpin it (say about 5). Provide some details about the patents- Who holds them, when were they issued, the type of innovation they cover (e.g product/process; architectural etc.), why the patent is important in the evolution of the technology.

12. What other technology sourcing and commercialization strategies are firms using? For instance, what is the role of licensing, alliances, and acquisitions? Have there been any prominent partnership deals? Any significant and noteworthy acquisitions? Are some firms more vertically integrated than others?

13. How are emerging technological trends playing a role? E.g. Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning, Internet of Things, Blockchain, LLMs. For instance how does AI/ML affect various aspects of this industry? How is it being incorporated? What are some concerns that the deployment of AI/ML raises that are specific to the industry? Similarly, how is Blockchain/iOT likely to play a role?

14. Shifting focus to incumbents, what are the key industries/ markets that are affected by your emerging technology? Who are the key players and firms in these industries?

15. How much of an advantage does the existing technology have over your new, emerging technology? Can you plot the incumbents’ performance and the emerging technology performance alongside in the same space and provide insight into where each stands? On which parameters is your emerging technology ahead, on which parameters is it behind?

16. What technology strategies have incumbent firms been pursuing? What performance improvements have they been seeking? Are they pursuing incremental, architectural, component innovation? Are they focused on product improvements, efficiency/process improvements, both?

17. Are incumbents being threatened by any other emerging technologies besides your own (e.g. wind and solar both threaten existing incumbents in power)? If so give a brief overview of that substitute technology and its evolution, and how it is potentially posing a threat.

18. What are the barriers that are preventing incumbent firms from switching to the new technology? Consider these barriers comprehensively- e.g. sunk costs, inertia, culture, disincentives, managerial blind spots. Are incumbent firms taking any other actions such as investing in CVC, acquisitions, alliances etc to develop a presence in the emerging technology?

As you address these issues, the objective is stitch together an analysis that gives a clear conceptual overview of your technology and where it stands in competitive/business terms. In doing so please consider any other issues that you think are relevant and critical toward that objective. While the first phase of the project will be based on secondary data, in the second phase I would encourage you to speak to a few key stakeholders (e.g. industry experts, customers, or the firms themselves) to gain first hand views on the technology and its evolution.
