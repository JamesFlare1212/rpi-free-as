## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is a required course for management majors. It is designed to introduce students to the key operational characteristics of a real company. Students are expected to secure an internship and successfully complete it before registering for this class. Students will submit a written report documenting what they did, what they learned, etc. It is a 0-credit hour class used to document the fact that Lally students have completed an internship. This is a requirement for graduation for all Lally students.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

A set of short readings will be posted on the course LMS (Blackboard) and serve as required readings for the course.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Students will demonstrate an understanding of the core functional areas in business based on their experience during their internship.
- 2\. Students will demonstrate the ability to secure an internship and perform on the job.
- 3\. Students will demonstrate the ability to apply what they have learned in school to help with their internship experience.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

The course requirements resulting in the final grade are as follows:

**Written Report/Reflection (50%) – Due 10/11/24**

Students will be required to complete a survey form that provides information on your internship experience, what you learned from it, and how it relates to your coursework at RPI and your professional goals. Only complete responses to this online survey will be accepted to complete this portion of the course. The link to the survey will be available on the course LMS.

**Video Reflection (50%) – Due 11/22/24 (Prior to Thanksgiving Break)**

After the written assignment has been completed, students will also be required to participate in the creation of a brief (1-2 minute), video interview of their internship experience. To do this, each student will sign up to meet with the course instructor in person to complete the video. More will be provided about this assignment early in the semester and via our Blackboard space (LMS).

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Written Report - 50%  
Video Assignment - 50%

**Grading Scale**

Since this is a 0-credit course, but required for graduation, there are two possible grading outcomes:

S = Satisfactory in a Satisfactory/Unsatisfactory graded course

U = Unsatisfactory in a Satisfactory/Unsatisfactory graded course

*As such, failure to complete either of the assignments listed on the previous page will result in a grade of “U” and this graduation requirement will not be completed.*

## Course Calendar

### Calendar Details:

All of the work to complete the two assignments required to pass this course will be done outside of any formal class meeting time by the dates indicated above. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. ### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process.

The *Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities* and the Graduate Student Supplement (for 6000 level and above courses) define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these.
