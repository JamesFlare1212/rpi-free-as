### Additional Information for Course Information Section:

This course emphasizes the basic principles of managerial functions and processes using an interdisciplinary approach to goal-oriented situations of private and public organizations.

The course is designed to introduce students to the key decisions and processes inherent in business. We will gain an overview of decision making in the management field through the lenses of strategy, marketing, finance, HR, technology management, entrepreneurship, among other disciplines. Additionally the course will touch upon important phenomena relevant to management, such as disruption and the implications of AI for business.

For business majors, the class will serve as an introduction to these topics such that you can be familiar with them as you work through your curriculum at Lally. For non business majors, the course will give you familiarity with these concepts so that you can use them and have a basic understanding as you go to work in organizations as engineers, scientists, architects, etc.

We will use readings and lectures to provide content. Additionally we will use cases and/or exercises extensively in order to help you *apply* the ideas. In some deliverables you’ll apply concepts to specific questions, but in others you’ll be tasked with making decisions and recommendations that would impact an entire business. That is one of the main objectives of this class: to give you experience with the complex, multi-faceted decision making that is done by organizational leaders. Additionally you will also build team, interpersonal, and communication skills through various assignments and projects.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

You will be required to attend classes regularly, and to read the materials assigned for each class. Depending upon how the class progresses, additional articles may be assigned from time to time. Based on your reading, you are then expected to make thoughtful and constructive contributions to the discussions. Students may be called upon randomly to answer questions in class to ensure uniform participation.

An attendance sheet will be circulated at the beginning of every class. It is your responsibility to make sure that you record your attendance. You are allowed a maximum of **two absences** (unexcused without documentation). Any additional absences will require clear and supporting documentation (e.g. a doctor’s note) giving reasons for why you should be excused from class. In general, unless circumstances are exigent, I would expect your total absences to be minimal. **Unexcused absences and excessive excused absences will reduce your class participation grade.** Three unexcused absences will reduce your class grade one-half a full letter (ex. B to a B-), after which two percentage points will be deducted for every additional absence.

Note: Attendance alone will not count toward your class participation grade, and the two will be graded separately. Additionally, you will be required to complete certain assignments (e.g. the Crown Cork and Seal and Kodak case) which are required for class discussion and will not be graded separately. Failure to complete these required assignments will result in a full letter lower participation grade.

Student athletes- you will be excused from class only with supporting documentation, and for travel etc. for games. Please arrange practice and other schedules so they do not interfere with class attendance.

**CHAT GPT/ LLM POLICY**

You are encouraged to use AI tools such as ChatGPT and other LLMs to supplement your learning in class. LLMs can be a powerful tool for researching industries, firms, and technologies. However they can also be wrong and be subject to hallucinations, and in many instances they can be superficial and not in depth enough. Hence you should critically examine and question whatever information they produce.

If you are using ChatGPT or another LLM, it is your responsibility to cross check the information and ensure it is correct and sufficiently detailed to address the class assignment. LLMs may provide a useful starting point and a first draft, but you will need to conduct additional detailed research to meet class requirements.

If you use an LLM such as ChatGPT, please provide a short paragraph at the end of the assignment stating how you used it, and be transparent about usage. Not doing so is a violation of academic integrity policies, and would count in the minimum as one strike.

However note that you are *explicitly prohibited* from using Chat GPT or any other LLM to solve case questions or obtain information pertaining to the cases assigned in class. Doing so, again, constitutes a violation of academy integrity policies, and would count in the minimum as a strike.

### Additional Course Information:

**CASE DISCUSSION QUESTIONS**

Wal-Mart & Wal-Mart update 2019

1. Conduct a five forces analysis of the physical discount retailing industry. Is it structurally attractive?
2. How did Wal-Mart historically build a cost leadership position in physical retailing? What are the different steps it took in various parts of the value chain, and how did it tie them all together?
3. How sustainable are Wal-Mart’s advantages? How transferable are those cost leadership advantages as Wal-Mart moves into new physical retail formats, new international locations, and most prominently e-Commerce?
4. What steps should Wal-Mart take to enhance its domestic market position? What steps should it take to enhance its international presence? How should Wal-Mart respond in e-Commerce, especially to Amazon’s superior performance in recent years?
5. Conduct independent research and elaborate on Wal-Mart’s current omni channel strategy, including how it is using AI analytics to execute it.

HTC

1. Evaluate HTC’s performance up to the point in time of the case. What are its assets and liabilities that affect its competitive advantage in the long run?
2. Is HTC’s position sustainable? Systematically identify the main challenges it faces.
3. Chou and Wang successfully led HTC from a small player into becoming a top 5 player in the smartphone industry. What do they need to make HTC a top 3 player?
4. Specifically: how should HTC differentiate its products as more handset manufacturers enter the Android market? Should HTC abandon the tablet market or stay in that market? What should HTC’s OS strategy be? Should it focus on operators or end customers? What geographic markets should HTC focus on, and which ones should it abandon? Analyze each of these questions comprehensively.

Innocent Drinks

1. Why has Innocent Drinks succeeded to date? What makes the venture so attractive? What are some steps the founders took that were particularly noteworthy?
2. Should Innocent expand its geographic territory to Europe or the US? Should it extend its current product line?
3. How would you value Innocent Drinks at the time of the case? Should the company consider a purchase offer at this time? What terms do you think they should accept at a minimum?
4. What should Richard Reed, Jon Wright and Adam Balon do going forward? What steps would you recommend to them?

Siemens

1. How is iOT/Industry 4.0 likely to affect businesses? What is different about it? What new business models does it support?
2. How do you evaluate Siemens approach to digitalization? Is it likely to be more effective or less effective than GE’s centralized approach? What are Siemens’ strengths, and what are Siemen’s weaknesses in this area? How does Siemens stand with respect to competitors such as IBM?
3. What are likely to be the challenges going forward? Provide your evaluation and offer some more recommendations and steps for Siemens going forward to ensure they are successful in digitalization.
