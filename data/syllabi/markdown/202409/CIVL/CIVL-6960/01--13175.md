## Course Content

### Content Details:

This course will introduce the basic data ***acquisition, processing, visualization,*** and ***analysis*** techniques that are important to civil and environmental engineering (CEE) problems. Students will go through the entire life cycle of data analytics and learn to use these techniques to solve CEE problems. This course provides an opportunity to develop the skills of studying civil and environmental engineering issues through data analysis and to gain hands-on experience with python and other data analysis tools. The course will emphasize application, but the underlying theory and limitations will also be discussed to ensure that the methods are fully understood, properly applied and potentially enhanced.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Apply fundamental data acquisition, processing, visualization, and analysis techniques to solve civil and environmental engineering (CEE) problems.
- Use Python and essential data analysis tools proficiently to handle the entire data analytics lifecycle, from data collection and cleaning to model implementation and interpretation.
- Through hands-on experience, students will critically evaluate and apply machine learning methods, including classification, regression, clustering, and deep learning, while understanding their theoretical foundations and limitations.
- Complete an end-to-end project demonstrating their ability to integrate these techniques into practical CEE applications.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

**Attendance and Absences:**

You are expected to attend class promptly and regularly. If you are unable to attend class, it is your responsibility to obtain class notes from a classmate. If you arrive late to class, or must leave early, please be considerate with your classmates, and do so as quietly and unobtrusively as possible. If you are unable to submit a homework assignment or take and exam or quiz due to an absence, you will be granted a waiver of the late homework policy and be given the opportunity to make up for the missed exam or quiz only under special circumstances. These include 1) illness or injury; and 2) university-related extracurricular activities. Illnesses and injuries include you or your children or spouse, and extracurricular activities include athletics. A written statement from a responsible party is required to be granted these special considerations. In the case of illness or personal injury, a letter from a physician must indicate that you were incapacitated from attending class. In the case of athletics, a letter from your coach or trainer must explain why you had to miss class.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Students are reminded: "RPI expects the highest standards of honesty and integrity in the academic performance of its students. Any act of scholastic dishonesty is regarded as a serious offense, which may result in expulsion. RPI defines scholastic dishonesty as submission of false records of academic achievement; cheating on assignments or examinations; plagiarizing; altering, forging, or misusing an academic record; taking, acquiring, or using test materials without faculty permission; acting alone or in cooperation with another to obtain dishonestly grades, honors, awards, or professional endorsement. Aiding and abetting an act of scholastic dishonesty is also considered a serious offense." All course work, including tests and homework, will be assigned to students individually. Where collaboration among students in groups is permitted and encouraged, this will be made clear in the assignment. Plagiarism and other forms of cheating are serious violations of the trust between student and teacher and among students. Penalties for such violations are severe, and are detailed in the Rensselaer Handbook.

**AI Policies:**

You are encouraged to use AI tools like ChatGPT for debugging and Google Gemini for code generation throughout this course. These tools should be used to enhance your learning by providing assistance with tasks such as debugging, code structuring, and exploring different approaches. It’s crucial that you understand the material and can explain any AI-generated content.For every assignment where AI tools are used, you must include a brief statement specifying which tools were used and what steps they assisted with. Here is an example: *In this assignment, I used ChatGPT to debug the data processing function by identifying syntax errors and suggesting corrections. I also used Google Gemini to generate the initial structure of the data visualization code.*

Failure to provide this statement may be considered a violation of academic integrity.
