## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Quantitative analysis of transport phenomena in physiological systems, including fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, and mass transfer. Fluid statics, mass and energy conservation, laminar and turbulent flow, microscale and macroscale analytical methods, mass transport with biochemical reactions, applications to transport in tissue and organs.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Basic Transport Phenomena in Biomedical Engineering, 4th Ed. Ronald L. Fournier

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

## Course Calendar

### Calendar Details:

**Course Calendar:**

Week 1-2: Basic concepts and principles including mass conservation. First law of thermodynamics; Readings: Chapter 1.1-1.4; Chapter 2.1-2.2

Weeks 3-4: Second law of thermodynamics and fugacity; Chapter 2.3-2.4, 2.6

***Test 1**

Weeks 5-6: Viscosity of simple and complex fluids (e.g. blood) and laminar and turbulent flow; Chapter 4.1-4.9

Weeks 7-9: Flow of fluids through membranes, tubes, or other geometries; Chapter 3, 4.10

***Test 2**

Weeks 10-12: Mass transfer fundamentals in homogenous systems; Chapter 5

Weeks 12-14: Mass transfer in fundamentals in heterogenous systems; Chapter 6

***Test 3**
