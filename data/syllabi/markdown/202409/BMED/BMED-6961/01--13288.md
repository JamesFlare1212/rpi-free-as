## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course is designed to equip participants with foundational skills in identifying unmet needs, developing innovative solutions, and understanding market dynamics within the biomedical sector. Through a combination of theory and hands-on application, students will engage in activities ranging from need-finding and ideation to market analysis and intellectual property management. The course emphasizes real-world applications by integrating tools like the Value Proposition Canvas and the Lean LaunchPad methodology to prepare participants for successful biomedical venture creation. The course culminates in a pitch showcase, where students present their findings and proposed solutions, focusing on rigorous market analysis and problem-solution fit.  
The course leverages the impressive expertise in biomedical commercialization found within the Mount Sinai faculty, [Mount Sinai Innovation Partners](https://ip.mountsinai.org/) (MSIP), and external advisors to support the course via classroom lectures and extra office hour sessions to support project work. Additionally, we take advantage of Mount Sinai’s and NYC’s impressive biomedical ecosystem with visits from leading entrepreneurs.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Identify and articulate unmet needs within the biomedical field through observational and analytical techniques.
- • Develop innovative solutions through structured ideation sessions and concept development methodologies.
- • Conduct comprehensive market analysis, including competitive analysis and market sizing using tools like SWOT, Porter Five Forces, TAM, SAM, and SOM.
- • Utilize the Lean LaunchPad methodology to test hypotheses, conduct customer discovery, and develop Minimum Viable Products (MVPs).
- • Prepare and present a well-researched pitch focusing on market analysis, problem identification, and proposed solutions.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Student performance will be evaluated based on active participation, the quality of assignments and presentations, and the ability to apply course concepts to develop and pitch biomedical innovations.
