## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course covers the theory and practice of protein structure determination (PSD), and many useful techniques for the biophysical characterization of proteins, their interactions and their structure/dynamics activity relationship.

GRADUATE COURSE: *Students enrolled in BCBP 6870 will do the term project on their own.

\*\*Presentation order: in alphabetical order of the last name.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Amit Kessel, Nir Ben-Tal. “Introduction to Proteins: Structure, Function, and Motion”, Second Edition (Chapman & Hall/CRC Computational Biology Series), 2018 (required)

Steven M. Pascal. "NMR Primer: An HSQC-based approach." IM Publications LLP.

Gordon Rule and Kevin Hutchens. “Fundamentals of Protein NMR Spectroscopy (Focus on Structural Biology, 5) Springer, 2006.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will be able to understand the structure-function relationship of proteins and demonstrate this ability in an oral presentation.
- Students will be able to demonstrate a thorough understanding of biophysical methods for protein structure determination by answering questions on an exam.
- Students will be able to recall and explain the reasons behind steps in the experimental process of examining protein structure-function relationship by answering questions on an exam.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Test, projects, quizzes, attendance, oral presentation
