## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course integrates the knowledge and professional practice of ITWS core and applications courses. Topics in database systems, networking, software design, web science, management of technology, human computer interaction, and ethics are applied within a framework of global strategy. The course utilizes an Information Technology Team Project with a real organization to practice the major concepts of the ITWS Degrees. The Team Project involves strategic and business planning, systems design and development, and technology implementation.

### Attendance Policy:

**Attendance and Grading Policy**

The majority of your grade will be received through classroom participation and the Capstone Project. Therefore, it is expected that you attend every class. ### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. ### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Academic Integrity**

Integrity is an extremely important part of any professional’s character and behavior. This course expects the highest level of personal and academic integrity. You are encouraged to discuss homework and cases in groups, but each student should write the individual case and homework assignments separately. Any ideas and/or quotations from any source, including the Internet, should be properly referenced in homework and the Capstone Project. The Capstone Project is to be accomplished in collaboration with other team members. For any assignment associated with the Capstone Project, one individual from the team should submit one copy for the entire team on LMS. Any breach of the academic integrity code listed in the Rensselaer Handbook will be considered grounds for failure in the course.
