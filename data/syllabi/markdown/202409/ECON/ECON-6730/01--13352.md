## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course introduces probability and statistical inference at the calculus level, aiming to master concepts and techniques essential for subsequent coursework across the standard PhD economics sequence as well as research. Students will also develop skills using computational software.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

- Brief introduction to empirical research questions, design and identification
- Basic Probability
- Random Variables
- Sampling & Estimation
- Asymptotic Theory
- Maximum Likelihood, Method of Moments
- Hypothesis Testing
- Linear Regression Models

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Evaluate empirical research design at a basic level.
- Utilize the theory of random variables to rigorously develop probabilistic models.
- Interpret features of probability distributions such as the mean and variance.
- Understand the role of sampling and commonly-made assumptions regarding dependence.
- Apply the law of large numbers, central limit theorem and related results to, e.g., conduct valid hypothesis tests.
- Appraise the quality of estimators and implement popular procedures such as MLE.
- Employ the use of the multiple linear regression model as a tool for causal inference.
- Write code to execute basic routines in statistics along with conditional statements, for/while loops, etc.
