## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Appreciation and understanding of uncertainties and the conditions under which they occur, within the context of the engineering problem-solving pedagogy of measurements, models, validation, and analysis. Problems and concerns in obtaining measurements; tabular and graphical organization of data to minimize misinformation and maximize information; and development and evaluation of models. Concepts will be supported with computer demonstration. Applications to problems in engineering are emphasized.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course teaches students

- An appreciation for uncertainty in the application of engineering models;
- The difference between probability and statistics;
- Discrete and continuous distribution functions;
- Marginal and conditional distributions;
- Descriptive statistics;
- Sample distributions and point estimates;
- Confidence intervals;
- Hypothesis testing;
- Univariate and multivariate regression analysis;
- An introduction into machine learning

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Students will be able to:

- Understand the concept of probability and conditional probability;
- Obtain and graph information about random variables and variable sets;
- Understand and apply various discrete and continuous distribution functions;
- Understand one sample and two sample confidence intervals;
- Formulate hypotheses tests and define confidence intervals;
- Setting up and solving univariate and multivariate regression problems;
- Appreciate the role of machine learning.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

- **Tests (85%):** Each of the four tests on Wednesdays is 1.5 hours long and counts for 15%. All tests are cumulative but with emphasis on the material since the last test. The final exam includes the first nine handouts and counts for 25%. The lowest test grade can be replaced by the final exam grade if it is higher. None of the four tests can replace the final exam.
- **Projects (15%):** There are two projects based on R. Late submissions receive a reduction of 10% per day from points earned. Submission later than a week will not be accepted.
