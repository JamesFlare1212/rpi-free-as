## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Students use visualization and cluster analysis tools to gain deeper insights into production and logistic relationships. Students apply the data analytic process to real-world production problems and questions, including evaluating production throughput, factor isolation, and output risk analysis, modeling production changes, forecasting environmental control factor changes, and minimization of defects and shortages. Students tune models to represent the current state and adjust models as underlying assumptions change. Students cannot receive credit for both this course and ENGR 6210, or ENGR 6215.

# Assessment Measures

#### Assignments

This course is meant to provide you with useful skills, tools, and perspectives for designing, and implementing analytic investigations into Production and Manufacturing data. I want you to be able to directly apply what we learn to your work now and in the future. The focus of the course is four class projects (CP) that involve real data from a manufacturing organization's Enterprise Resource Planning system (‘ERP’).

1. In the first project you will research the custom laptop manufacturing industry, get acclimated to the landscape, and frame a series of hypotheses. You will document these findings and use them to inform the projects that follow. (25% of total grade) Due in Week 2.
2. In the second project, you will conduct an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA). The purpose of this second project, and the EDA process, is to familiarize you with the techniques that are used to get acclimated to a new dataset. You will use visualizations, and descriptive statistics to expedite becoming familiar with the data, and to inform a more formal analysis. (25% of total grade) Due in Week 6.
3. In the third project you will utilize different analytical skills to test the hypotheses that you framed earlier in the course and translate the test results into strategic business recommendations. You will then estimate the savings that can be obtained by implementing changes that enhance production efficiency. (25% of total grade) Due in Week 9.
4. In the fourth project, you will conduct business forecasting and simulation to estimate the cumulative cash flow of the company to justify the expenses on improving productivity and efficiency, and then using a dynamic chart to show the break-even point of the whole project. (25% of total grade) Due in Week 12.

**My objectives as the faculty mentor:** I hope to provide you with analytic tools and methodologies that you can immediately start using in your work. You will be working with real data, sourced from a real customer’s ERP system. My expectation is that each of you will be able to make data-driven recommendations based on your analysis of these data.

Assessment Rubrics Each project has an assessment rubric that shows the specific competencies upon which your learning will be evaluated.
