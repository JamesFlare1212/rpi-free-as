## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is an undergraduate introductory course covering basic concepts and skills in engineering communication. Topics include technical writing, project planning and proposal writing, data visualization, system modeling and simulation, engineering graphics and CAD, and effective uses of software tools.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

No Textbook Required

## Course Goals

### Goals:

1. Understanding 3-D space and parametric 2-D sketching.
2. General solid creation.
3. Standard documentation practices.
4. Data Integrity and responsibility.
5. Prepare the outline of a technical document effectively using MS Word™.
6. Generating effective graphs/charts from a set of data using MS Excel™.
7. Develop and organize a small project plan.
8. Generate effective formats for technical slides.
9. Organize the logistics of an effective meeting.
10. Describe and use NI LabView™ to visualize the behavior of a system.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

1. Attendance in sessions is mandatory to receive TA assistance and an in-class evaluation/grade for assignments.

2. Students **with** an **official excuse** will need to contact the coordinator for approval to make up the assignment and obtain a written excuse from an official entity (see “Excusal Policy”).
3. Grades will be posted on LMS Gradebook in a timely manner.

### Other Course Policies:

Early Warning System and Mid-Semester Evaluation:

1. If the student is in danger of failing the course by Week 7 of the semester, an Early Warning System (EWS) alert will be issued through the Institute Grade System. The student should receive an e-mail and is encouraged to contact the Lead TA or Course Coordinator immediately.
2. At any time, the student may request his/her current overall course grade from the course coordinator ONLY.
3. Specific assignment grades may be discussed with the LEAD TA.

Portfolio Grade:

The student *Portfolio* is defined as “the digital collection of **every** student-created file submitted for grade AND files obtained through LMS necessary to include in all graded assignments”.

New students to BOX must register for BOX first using: https://itssc.rpi.edu/hc/en-us/articles/360004829931-Requesting-a-Box-RPI-Account

Once registered, access your folder through https://box.rpi.edu/

1. Within 24 hours of your second class (Session 02), a portfolio folder titled “rcsid-1400” will appear (“rcsid” will be YOUR RCSID).
2. This folder is UPLOAD ONLY. Please save your work to your local machine FIRST, before uploading to BOX.
3. Students will NOT have privileges to delete files once uploaded. If an errant file is uploaded, students may e-mail the course coordinator for removal.

Students will upload **only their final version** of **each** computer files generated each week for grade. These submissions will eventually become your portfolio.

These computer models will **NOT** be **re-graded** for the purposes of improving a grade, forgiving special circumstances.

Each CAD file (8 total for Mod I) or Lab Group of Files (9 total for Mod II/III) present (must be at least half complete) is worth (1.176 pts per file) for a total of 20 points.

**DO NOT** submit **duplicate** **files** for portfolio submission. If a file already exists within the portfolio folder, it will be counted.

1. If a file is not complete enough to earn portfolio credit, you may upload a newer, more complete version.

**Students MUST use the given assignment names when creating and saving their CAD (Module I) files. Append drawing part names with “\_dwg1” or “\_dwg”. Incorrectly named files will not be graded.** Some examples of acceptable file naming formats for a sample model or drawing from an assignment named “Blade Hook”:

1. **Model File:** blade hook, blade_hook (ensure there is only a single file type extension “.prt”)
2. **Drawing File:** blade hook_dwg, blade-hook_dwg1
3. **Never use** the following **characters in file** or **folder name** on **BOX:** { **< , > , : , ; , “ , / , \ , | , ? , *, $** }. Doing so may result in a non-grade.

Please refrain from copying LMS PDF assignments, .ZIP/.RAR/.7z (compressed) folders, videos, or any LECTURE supplement files that you have downloaded.

**It is your responsibility to maintain this data throughout the course. Hard drive crashes, stolen laptops, re-images, computer repair slips, etc.** **WILL NOT BE VAILD EXCUSES!**

**DUE:** The complete portfolio is **due** by 11:59 PM Eastern time of the day of Session 14 for your section.

Student portfolio folders are **removed** from BOX when a) the student drops the course *OR* b) three weeks after classes end.

Due Date & Extension Policy:

1. Laboratory work is graded in-class by a Teaching Assistant at or before **class expiration time** on the day of the laboratory session unless an excuse is granted.

1. These assignments must ALSO be uploaded to the student BOX folder by class expiration time.
   1. Barring official excusal, any assignment uploaded to BOX late will be marked as late, IF a TA does NOT grade the assignment in-class.
   2. If you are NOT graded by a TA during class time, but uploaded the assignment to BOX before class expiration, the assignment is considered “on-time”.

- If you ARE graded during class time, but upload the assignment to BOX after class expiration, the assignment is still considered “on-time”.

2. Extensions are granted if VALID excuse is presented to the course coordinator via E-Mail.
3. **VALID EXCUSE:** Medical/Family/Athletics/Activities/Career/Job/Travel/Weather
   1. Issuance by the Dean’s Office, Athletic Staff, Health Center, ALAC, or Student Experience are automatically deemed VALID.
   2. Issuance by the student is subject to the coordinator’s discretion.
   3. Installation Trouble: **You must contact the course coordinator/visit WebEx office hours immediately.**
      1. Waiting until less than 48 hours before assignments are due will NOT be counted as excused for installation issues.
   4. Computer/Software Trouble: See FAQ #3 on the following page
   5. In-Lab Only Assignments (Modules II & III): Make-up during scheduled office hours ONLY (See Above for Office Hours)
   6. Students will have as many sessions as they have been excused to submit missed assignments
   7. Portfolio – see above section.
4. **INVALID/NO EXCUSE:**
   1. If a student is not in class to have that session’s work graded and does not upload their work to BOX within class time, then that submission will be marked as late.
   2. Class Assignments: deduct 50% per week late.
      1. An assignment uploaded after 10 minutes beyond class expiration time will be counted as 1-week late
      2. For example, an assignment that earned 5 points would become 1.25 points if it were 10 days late

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

1. While some **verbal** collaboration between students is expected and encouraged, all work submitted by an individual student MUST be his/her OWN work.
2. **EXCHANGING ANY FILES** associated with this course in any manner (e.g. E-MAIL, UPLOAD/DOWNLOAD, USB, etc.) is a VIOLATION of this policy.
3. Parts retrieved through any website other than LMS are illicit and not allowed.
4. **NO** student’s electronic data should reside (i.e. be saved or opened) on another student’s computer/USB drive/storage media.
5. If you experience computer trouble, see the course coordinator immediately. **There is no excuse to use another student’s computer.**
6. Direct copying of work is prohibited.

Failing to adhere to the above six policy statements will result in a FAILING course grade for all parties involved and further disciplinary action if needed. If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. Further Academic Dishonesty information for the course can be viewed on LMS.
