## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is a course designed to supplement your lab safety courses and deals with safety protocols and practices associated with chemical process plants. It is designed to provide information necessary for your ABET accredited degree and to assist you in finding an internship and a job. **It is not meant to be a Chemical Engineering Elective as it is only 1-credit.** The course is offered on-line by the American Institute of Chemical Engineers. It is part of the Safety and Chemical Engineering (SAChE) Certificate program offered by the society. The link is:

https://www.aiche.org/ccps/education/safety-and-chemical-engineering-education-sache-certificate-program
