## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The purpose of this course is to introduce the basic concepts involved in applying physics to understand the Earth.

We will also discuss fundamental techniques of geophysical data collection and analysis.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

There is no “required” textbook for this course and all of the course notes will be available to the class online via LMS and the RPI BOX.

However, I would suggest that you avail yourself of other published resources as not everyone learns the same way.

Strongly Suggested:

W. Lowrie and A. Fitchner, Fundamentals of Geophysics (3rd edition),Cambridge University Press, 2020

Other current texts of note:

Reynolds, J. M., An Introduction to Applied and Environmental Geophysics, Wiley.

Fowler, C.M.R., The Solid Earth: An Introduction to Global Geophysics, Cambridge University Press.

Lillie, R. J., Whole Earth Geophysics: An Introductory Textbook for Geologists and Geophysicists, Pearson

Burger, H. R, A. F. Sheehan, and C. H. Jones, Introduction to Applied Geophysics: Exploring the Shallow Subsurface, Norton.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

We will cover the fundamentals of Gravity, Seismology, Geochronology, Heat Flow, Electromagnetism, and Geodynamics as they apply to the Earth.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

- Review of basic Geological Concepts
- Gravity
- Seismics
- Geochronology
- Thermal properties and Heat Flow
- Electrical Methods (SP,IP,MT,Resistivity)
- Magnetics
- Radar
- GPS
- Geodynamics

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will be able to analyze Gravity observations
- Students will be able to interpret refraction Seismological data
- Students will be able to interpret fundamental Geochronolgical isotopic ratios
- Students will be able to solve basic problems in Geothermal Heat Flow
- Students will be able to analyze resistivity observations.
- Students will be able to apply basic applications in Geodynamics.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Students will be assessed through homework and exams that relate specifically to the outcomes listed above.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Grades will be determined according to the following weights

| Class Participation | 5% |
| --- | --- |
| Homework (Bascially everything but Exams) | 50% |
| Midterm and Final Exams | 45% |

Given the weights shown above, you can figure your standing at any point first by computing your current percentage. To do so, let

| SUMQ | be the total sum of all possible points on Homeworks given thus far |
| --- | --- |
| MYSUMQ | be the total sum of all points that you have accumulated on the Homeworks. |
| SUME | be the total sum of all possible points on Exams given thus far |
| MYSUME | be the total sum of all points that you have accumulated on the Exams. |
| SUML | be the total sum of all possible points on class partcipation. |
| MYSUML | be the total sum of all points that you have accumulated from class participation. |

You can roughly estimate MYSUML/SUML to be the fraction of lectures you have attended.

Then, your current percentage can be calculated from

MYPERC = 100\*[(MYSUMQ/SUMQ)\*0.50 + (MYSUME/SUME)\*0.45 + (MYSUML/SUML)\*0.05)

Your current grade is estimated from the following binning:

| MYPERC | Estimated Letter Grade |
| --- | --- |
| Greater than 95% | A |
| Between 90% and 95% | A- |
| Between 87% and 90% | B+ |

### Other Course Policies:

Late homeworks will be penalized by subtracting 20% of the total value per day (including holidays and weekends).

Thus, any homework more than 4 days late gets no credit. Note that a "day" is considered over at 5PM.

If your homework is not delivered to me or in my mailbox by that time it will be docked an extra day.

Missed exams may be retaken only if the instructor is satisfied with the reasons given by a student for being absent from the exam.

If a student has an unavoidable conflict of significance which he/she knows about in advance of an examination, he/she MUST notify the instructor prior to the day of the exam. In cases of emergency (e.g., health problems) the student MUST provide sufficient evidence of the emergency (e.g., a note from a doctor) to the instructor before any action will be considered.

Exams will be retaken only at the discretion of the instructor.
