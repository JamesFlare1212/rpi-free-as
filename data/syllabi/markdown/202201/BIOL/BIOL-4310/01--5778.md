## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- At the end of the course a successful student will be able to: • Demonstrate the ability to distinguish between prokaryotes and eukaryotes • Demonstrate the ability to distinguish different microbes • Demonstrate the ability to summarize the main properties of each group of microorganisms: fungi, protozoa, algae, bacteria and viruses • Demonstrate the ability to summarize current topics in Microbiology • Demonstrate the ability to summarize current problems with antibiotic resistance • Demonstrate the ability to summarize the current topics on viral infections, including: Ebola, H1N1, H1N5 and swine flue.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Basic principles:**

NO CHEATING! Every professor tells you this. I mean it! For complete details in academy honesty, review the Academic Dishonesty document attached.

The basis of the relationship between the teacher and students is trust. The teacher has the obligation to prepare the lectures and work with the students. Students must work on the assignments and learn the material.

Students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process.

Students and teachers will act in honesty. The Rensselaer Handbook defines various forms of academic dishonesty and procedures for responding to them. All forms are violations of the trust between students and teachers. Students should familiarize themselves with this portion of the Rensselaer Handbook and should note that the penalties for plagiarism and other forms of cheating can be quite harsh. Reading the syllabus is important.

-Cheating on an exam. Anyone cheating on an exam will be given a zero for that exam. **Two cheating episodes will result in an F in the course and may be reported for academic discipline.** NO BOOKS, notes, crib sheets or other written materials are allowed during exams. Looking at another student’s work is not allowed.

-Re-grading: Exams could be submitted for re-grading no longer the day the exam is solved in class. Exams will not be re-graded after that.

DO NOT WRITE ANYTHING ON AN EXAM PAPER returned to you after grading by the Instructor. If an exam paper has new markings, this will disqualify you from any consideration of re-grading. **Anyone submitting an altered exam for re-grading will be given an F in the corresponding exam. Two cheating episodes will result in an F in the course and may be reported for academic discipline.**
