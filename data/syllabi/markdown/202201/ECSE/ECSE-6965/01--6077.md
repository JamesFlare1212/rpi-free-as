## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This graduate-level course teaches the conversion and control of solar and wind energy and its integration into the power grid. Topics include fundamentals of photovoltaics and wind energy; power electronics for renewable energy; solar inverters and solar energy system architectures; wind turbines based on synchronous generators and doubly-fed induction generators (DFIG); grid interconnect requirements and grid support functions for renewables; high-voltage dc technology for renewable energy transmission; as well as renewable energy system modeling, stability and control.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

- Fundamentals of photovoltaic and wind energy
- Power electronics for renewable energy and high-voltage dc transmission
- PV inverters and photovoltaic energy conversion
- Full power conversion (type-IV) wind turbines
- DFIG-based (type-III) wind turbines
- Grid interconnect requirements and grid support functions of renewables
- High-voltage dc transmission of renewable energy and offshore wind
- Renewable energy system modeling, stability and control
