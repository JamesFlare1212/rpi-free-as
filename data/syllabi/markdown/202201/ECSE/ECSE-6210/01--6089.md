### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- In light of the stated objectives students should be able to understand the state-of-the-art of advanced semiconductor technology, including quantitative measures of current trends in Si CMOS and TFTs, develop knowledge of fundamentals of semiconductor physics required for understanding advanced device physics, and should be able to compare emerging semiconductor technology with the state-of-the-art technology and evaluate its possible impact on systems.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Copying from a neighbor in an examination or turning in someone else’s homework/reports/

term papers as one’s own constitute academic dishonesty.

The compilation, ad verbatim reproduction, or paraphrased reproduction of someone else’s work in a written or oral report without citation of original source constitutes academic dishonesty.

What is plagiarism? This is the use of someone else’s work without crediting the source.

What is an “ad verbatim” reproduction? This is an exact word-by-word reproduction.

What is paraphrased reproduction? This is a reproduction using very similar words and phrases.

What is a phrase? This is a sequence of two or more words.

What is the penalty for academic dishonesty? The penalty is reduced or no credit and may result in failing the course.

Note: Teamwork during class exercises and homework and use of other resources (calculators, laptops, iPad, tablets, books, etc.) are allowed.
