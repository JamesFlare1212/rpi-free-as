## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Basics of dynamic response of soil and soil-foundation systems, including applications to earthquake engineering and machine foundations. Systems studies include shallow and deep foundations, buried structures, earth structures, slopes, and earthquake site response.  
This is a graduate course addressed especially to geotechnical and structural engineering students.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Book: Whitman, R. Dobry and M. Vucetic, Soil Dynamics (in preparation and not commercially available).

Lecture notes from this book Will be posted on LMS (divided in 14 modules).

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The course covers:

- Cyclic Soil Properties
- Dynamic Response of One- and Two-Degree-of-Freedom Systems
- 1D, 2D and 3D Wave Propagation
- Dynamic Response of Shallow Foundations
- Dynamic Response of Deep Foundations

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Analyze and solve problems of the dynamic response of one- and multi-degree-of-freedom systems.
- Analyze and solve problems of wave propagation in soil media.
- Analyze and solve problems of the dynamic response of foundations.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Two mid-term Exams and a final exam will be used to assess the three course learning outcome.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:
