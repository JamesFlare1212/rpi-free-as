## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

OVERVIEW  
This series of digital workshops is meant to be an introduction to Building Information Modeling, primarily using  
Revit 2022, but also introducing Vectorworks and ARCHICAD. We will approach design using a continuous  
workflow process to model and produce working design development / construction document drawings. We will  
explore how to use the software most effectively without limiting creativity.  
FORMAT  
This course will begin approximately two weeks into the semester. The first four weeks will run as a "BIM  
Essentials" intensive workshop on Wednesdays 4:30pm - 6pm EST, unless otherwise noted. These workshops will  
cover all of the basics of BIM and will be recorded and shared with the students. A draft tech set and BIM model  
will be due at the end of this workshop intensive.  
For the second part of the semester, we will move to the "BIM Advanced" tutorials. There will be open sessions at  
the end prior to final reviews for BIM desk crits and help sessions. Advanced pre-recorded tutorials will also be  
available for students to watch asynchronously, with recommended weeks listed in the schedule at the end of this  
syllabus. Students will also be able to schedule BIM troubleshooting sessions and discuss project specific modeling  
needs throughout the semester.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understand the relevance and benefits of Building Information Modeling as a methodology used in civil engineering, building design, and construction.
- Utilize BIM software as a continuous workflow, with a shared Central Model in which multiple team members can model/draw simultaneously.
- Be able to assemble a Design Development level Construction Document drawing set using proper graphic techniques, title blocks, schedules, and annotations.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

EVALUATIONS AND ASSESSMENT MEASURES:  
Workshop Attendance: 50%  
Tech Set / Digital Model Submissions: 50%  
(See BIM Workshop Schedule in this syllabus for due dates)  
Students will be asked to upload their studio project digital model at the end of the semester to the RPI  
Server. (A guide for connecting via VPN to the Server is included at the end of this syllabus). Digital model and  
studio project tech sets will be evaluated on how well the student utilized the BIM workflow process, accuracy of  
digital information communicated, and overcoming of technical challenges.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities defines various forms of Academic Dishonesty  
and procedures for responding to them. Academic dishonesty violates the trust between students and teachers. Students must trust that teachers have  
made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses  
they teach. Teachers must trust that the assignments students turn in are their own performance. Violations of this trust undermines the educational process.  
Cheating, plagiarism (defined as the lack of attribution or citation of the work of others), willful damage or theft of  
another person’s work violate academic integrity. The first instance of the breach of this trust will merit a failing grade in this course and the violator(s) will be reported to the Dean of Students for disciplinary action.
