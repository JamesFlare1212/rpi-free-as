## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The second semester of the Sonics Research Lab includes advanced acoustical measurement techniques. Another important part of the semester curriculum concerns noise control and vibration measurements. State-of-the-art, commercial software and our own research-based software will be used for simulation / analysis / measurement of room acoustics in order to show the students how such technical tools assist in acoustics research and consulting practice for the design of performance and public venues. The course will also give students a deeper theoretical understanding of architectural acoustics in order to assist them in room acoustics research and practice. There will also be labs to reflect typical measurement procedures and (if time permits) site visits to acoustics testing labs and different types of acoustical spaces. The Sonics Research Lab II emphasizes more training on independent hands-on and problem-solving skills (than the SRL I).

## Course Goals

### Goals:

After successful accomplishment of this course work the students will demonstrate:

Ability to conduct some advanced acoustical experiments

Ability to analyze / calculate experimental results independently using the MATLAB-codes written by themselves

Ability to conduct a number of advanced acoustical measurements, such as frequency- dependent polar responses of sound sources, chamber absorption measurements, chamber scattering measurements, in-situ reflection coefficient measurements and so on

Ability to use the commercial available modeling tools and software developed by themselves to conduct real-life projects

**In addition, for graduate students (taking ARCH 6880)**

Ability to develop independent research project

Ability to carry out acoustics projects independently.
