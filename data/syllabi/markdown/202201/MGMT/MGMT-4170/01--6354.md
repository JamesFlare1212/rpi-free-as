## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The primary objective of this co-listed course is to introduce the role of data as a resource of the organization. In this respect, the course examines the role of data throughout the organizational hierarchical levels such as the operational, tactical, and strategic levels. Second, the course provides the student with knowledge and hands on training of technologies that manipulate data at the various organizational levels such as the structured query language (SQL), extraction transformation and loading tools (ETL), data warehousing (DW), and online analytical processing (OLAP). Finally, the third dimension of the course is to provide the student the theory and hands on training to understand the transformation of data from simple data points to advanced pieces of information.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

- Define the role of data in an organization and the life cycle of a data project.
- Define strategic objectives and determine how to measure progress.
- Analyze a business problem and create process model.
- Learn how to gather technical requirements.
- Choose between competing requirements based on scope and budget.
- Learn how to build an end-to-end solution from operational data to dashboard.
- Learn how to customize reporting tools and create dashboards.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Test 1: Business Process Model Fundamentals

Percent of course grade: 20%

Students will be given a business process and will be required to build a Business Process diagram and an Entity-Relationship diagram.

Test 2: SQL Fundamentals

Percent of course grade: 20%

Students will be given an Entity-Relationship diagram and will be required build an On-Line Transaction Processing (OLTP) database.

Test 3: Data Warehousing

Percent of course grade: 20%

Students will create an On-Line Analytical Processing (OLAP) cube from the OLTP Data Warehouse.

Test 4: Reporting

Percent of course grade: 20%

Students will write Data Analysis Expressions (DAX) in Excel and Power Bi to create custom reports. Student will also write Visual Basic programs to customize Excel.

Percent of course grade: 20%
