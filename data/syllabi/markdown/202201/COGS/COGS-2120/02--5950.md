## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This undergraduate course is an introduction to the new and quickly growing field of cognitive science. Cognitive Science is a highly interdisciplinary field of study of mind at the intersection of philosophy, psychology, computer science, neuroscience, linguistics, and anthropology. Since it is so highly interdisciplinary, we will spend a bit of time on a number of different topics; this broad approach will serve to introduce the students to the wide range of subfields within cognitive science.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Friedenberg, J., & Silverman, G. (2015). Cognitive science: An introduction to the study of mind, 3rd edition. Sage.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Differentiate the many subfields and approaches within cognitive science and describe the connections between them
- Assess and critique various models of cognition
- Demonstrate in-depth knowledge of a specific topic and present this material clearly and thoughtfully

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Regular quizzes to gauge understanding of core concepts and class discussions.  
One project (paper/presentation) to allow the student to more deeply investigate a topic within cognitive science of their choosing.  
Occasional assignments to probe deeper into certain elements of course content.
