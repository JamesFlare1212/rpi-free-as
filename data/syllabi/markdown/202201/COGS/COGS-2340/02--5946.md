## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course will provide an overview of the field of linguistics, the study of human language. As linguistics is a relatively diverse and interdisciplinary field (as we will see), we will cover many different topics and sub-fields in an attempt to discover and discuss the various avenues linguistics has to offer. This broad approach will introduce students to a range of language-related topics.

## Policies

### Other Course Policies:

Exams will be conducted through LMS. These exams will be open-note/open-book, so you may consult outside resources (e.g., textbook, lecture slides) during the exam. You still must put everything into your own words – copying directly from any source is plagiarism and you’ll get a 0 on the entire exam if you do this.

On exam day, the exam will be available on LMS for the entire day (12:00 midnight until 11:59 PM ET). You may decide to begin the exam whenever you want. Once you start the exam, you will have 2 hours to complete it. You must submit by 11:59PM ET.

On the exam days, we will not meet for lecture.

You may not discuss anything with other students during the exam day. Any communication with other students about the exam will be considered cheating. So again, consulting materials (textbook, lectures, etc.) = fine; consulting other people = cheating.

If you have technical difficulties during the exam, you must email me as soon as you possibly can and we will work together to find a solution.

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own.
