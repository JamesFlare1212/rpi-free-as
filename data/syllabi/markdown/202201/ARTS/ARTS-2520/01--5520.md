### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

In this class, students will:

- Have significant contact with diverse perspectives on and examples of global music cultures
- Become acquainted with major music culture areas of the world
- Develop an understanding of the ecology of music cultures and the factors that impact cultural sustainability
- Recognize and appreciate the music of diverse peoples and their instruments of music
- Survey and engage with scholarship and recordings of traditional and modern music from around the world
- Develop analytic and auditory skills across music genres, global music cultures, and audiovisual media
- Become sensitive and informed listeners and will be able to articulate their listening experiences.
- Gain an appreciation of the incredible diversity of contemporary musical practices and traditions and will be able to situate them within a broader historical and cultural context.
