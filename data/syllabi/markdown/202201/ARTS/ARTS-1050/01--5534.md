### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Students acquire a broad repertory or art historical knowledge. Students recognize and identify styles, important artists, and iconic works of art. Students understand how to contextualize works of art and other cultural artifacts. Students learn a specific vocabulary in order to articulate ideas about art. Students develop their communication skills and creative thinking.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

There are **3 exams, each worth 20 points of the final course grade.** **Exams are taken in class and on LMS, so students must bring their laptops with fully charged batteries on exam days**. All exam questions are based on artworks discussed in class and featured on the Gardner’s book. Exams questions are *multiple answer* problems with no partial credit. Students get 2 attempts on each exam. Students may take notes on paper during the exams. The use of other devices or apps is considered cheating. Cheating will result in a 0 for the exam grade. To practice the exam format, take the “Exam sample” quiz on LMS.

There is **1 paper assignment worth 20 points of the final course grade**. Students must turn in two versions of the paper on scheduled dates. The first version is returned with an initial grade plus comments and suggestions for improvements. Students must then implement the suggestions and turn in a second version for a new grade. Papers will be docked five points if they are not turned in on time, for both 1st and 2nd versions. No exceptions. Plagiarism will result in a 0 for the paper grade. Detailed guidelines for this assignment are available on the “Paper” document on LMS.

The presentation is **worth 20 points of the final course grade.** Each student is required to deliver one presentation, scheduled by the second week of class. Students who enroll later are responsible for requesting a presentation date. This is a group assignment but students are graded individually. Detailed guidelines for this assignment are available the “Panels” document on LMS.
