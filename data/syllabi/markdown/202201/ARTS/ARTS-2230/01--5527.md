## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An introductory course in digital 3D tools and concepts, focusing on polygonal modeling, texturing, and digital sculpting; some lighting and rendering; professional pipelines and best practices; and considerations for animations and real time digital environments.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Software

- Autodesk Maya
- Substance Painter
- ZBrush

Other Required Materials

- Sketchbook, pencils, pens and/or fine markers
- External storage: don’t rely on storing files on the lab computers. You'll need an external drive, at the very least to transfer files between the lab workstations and your computer
- Backup storage: you are responsible for maintaining backups of your projects.
- 3-button mouse with a scroll wheel.

Recommended Materials

- A graphics tablet, such as a Wacom Intuos. A basic model is fine. These can greatly speed up your work in 3D apps and make digital painting and sculpting intuitive and fluid. They are well worth the investment.
- A small digital camera, or smartphone with a good camera, for reference and texture photos

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- By successful completion of the course, students will be able to: utilize principles of modeling for games and animation create and edit polygonal geometry create, light, texture, and render models and scenes model organic and hard surface objects design and sculpt original characters

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

3D art assignments, quizzes
