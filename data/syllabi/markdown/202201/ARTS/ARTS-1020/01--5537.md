## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

##### Digital Imaging is an introductory studio seminar in the visual arts using raster and vector based digital imaging, photography, and interactive mixed media for creative expression. Inquiry and experimentation are encouraged as students develop basic skills through a series of short studies which lead to a final project and web portfolio. Projects reflect equal attention to concept, expression, and technique. Screenings, related readings, and discussions of contemporary themes help give background to the history and theory of contemporary visual arts practice and lead to the development of conceptual, aesthetic and critical thinking skills in the construction of meaning, personal expression, identity, social and environmental futures.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

##### Course goals include the development of creative and expressive abilities utilizing basic raster and vector based digital imaging, photography, and interactive mixed media. In addition to increasing conceptual, aesthetic, and critical thinking in digital literacy, the course enables the development of a series of short studies which lead to a final project and web portfolio. Provide an environment that nurtures creativity, inventiveness and experimentation, interexchange with other students and faculty enabling students to develop their creative capacities and ability for personal expression. Gain a broader understanding and appreciation of artistic research, intellectual endeavor, and cultural activity.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

##### 1. **Sustainable Utopia Montage** 10% Constructing a point of view about something you feel strongly about by envisioning a sustainable future using raster based imaging. Create a series of 5 effective and expressive images. (Photography, Photoshop: image resolution, ppi, dpi, cut, copy, paste, layers, masks, color) Your own original images vs appropriation: the weight of “found imagery”, where does processing another’s image become yours? Copyright, public domain, digital commons

##### 2. **Self Portrait in 4 parts**: 10% 1. as you are naturally (no makeup or retouching), 2. retouched to idealized beauty, 3. aged to 55 years of age, 4. create a morphed identity

##### 3. **Logo:** 10% your personal expressive logo created using vector based imagery

##### 4. **Map: personal geographies:** 10% Using either raster or vector based imaging, create a map of where you have been and where you are going. This can be literal, figurative or allegorical.

##### 5. **Toy:** 10% create a 3 dimensional toy or creature using 3D imaging

##### 6. **Animation:** 10% Bring to life! Animate a short motion using animation techniques such as stop action, morphing, in-betweening, layer animation, onion skinning

##### **Final Project** 20% Utilizing one study, develop it further to where it speaks more fully to convey your expressive message and content using interactive mixed media. Potentials can include the use of augmented reality, qr coding, marker images programmed through applications such as Augment, EyeJack, etc.

##### **Web portfolio** A final web portfolio of all perfected works, including reading and screening reactions, is handed in on a labeled non-returnable thumb drive, as well as uploaded to the student’s class Box folder on the last day of class for final grading purposes.,

##### **Class Participation** 15%

##### **Reading & Screening Reaction Paragraphs** 5%
