## Course Goals

### Goals:

The goal of this class is to develop the student’s musical ability relative to capacity and interest.  
Students will learn basic-advanced music for the particular music utilizing various texts/lesson etude books.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Students will be taught all major and minor scales as well as exploring various musical styles.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Students are expected to learn traditional, as well as, alternate fingerings on their woodwind instrument, including full range from chalumeau to altissimo register. Achieved by – Consulting fingering charts and weekly assigned chromatic scale.
- 2\. Students will gain expertise in performing all key signatures by weekly assignments of Major and minor scales. Special emphasis on reviewing the “Circle of Fifths”.
- 3\. Students should gain insight on various articulations, technical skills and dynamics, through the utilization of etude books, with the introduction of new notes and the “Circle of Fifths” concept of key signatures.
- 4\. Each student will be assigned 3-4 solos per semester. These pieces will be standard repertoire and will fairly match the performer’s ability. The literature will reflect varied styles ie. Baroque, Classical, Romantic, Contemporary, etc.
- 5\. The students will have a goal to obtain rhythmic independence. This is best achieved by assignment of duet materials where various styles will be presented. The player will be expected to learn to play his/her part independently “against” the accompaniment.
- 6\. Students will be assigned Contemporary styles as a vital part of their lesson. These will include Jazz studies of swing style, jazz waltz, funk, etc. Players will be asked to take note of legato style, accents, glissandos, etc.
- 7\. Students will have availability of help with any challenging parts assigned in addition to the lesson material. The instructor shall always be available for assistance with Band, Orchestra or Ensemble parts.
- 9\. Students, as well as the Course Co-ordinator, will receive a Progress Report at the conclusion of the semester lessons.
- 8\. The student will receive weekly written and verbal reactions, grades, and assignments. Each part of the lesson shall be evaluated.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Students will be assessed at each lesson session after performing the variety of music expressed by the instructor. There will be homework in the form of a Lesson Plan for each lesson.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Grades will be based on each aspect of the lesson in accordance with the Student Learning Outcomes. An Overall Grade will be assigned for each lesson session.
