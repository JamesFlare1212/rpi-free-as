### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

In this class, students will:

- Learn about the history and methods of video game music and its reception.
- Produce critical analyses of music in video games through processes of play, writing, and discussion.

- Synthesize interdisciplinary perspectives, as well as navigating through scholarly and non-scholarly forms of knowledge, bringing these to bear on the unique considerations of video game music.

- Explore their own relationships between music/sound and play through listening-gaming logs and guided projects of their own choosing.

- Engage with *Ludomusicology* as a new and developing field of study.
