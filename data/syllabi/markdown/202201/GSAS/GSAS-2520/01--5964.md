## Course Goals

### Goals:

- Learn core tenets of game storytelling techniques, principles and approaches. Including:
  - o How plots work
  - o Games and Stories
  - o Plot Models
  - o Environmental Storytelling
  - o Multilinear/branching narratives
  - o The ways in which game narrative structures and techniques are different from other media.
- Learn how to analyze and assess storytelling in games through exercises and by case study analysis.

- Present exercises on identifying plot models in use
- Practice creating short stories
- Practice game story analysis with acquired tools
- Present an in-depth analysis of a game story of your choosing

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Introduction to Game Storytelling comprises 26-48 pages of written formal student assignments outside of class. The second Fairy Tale assignment and the Game Narrative Review entail iterative feedback.

Written work outside of class accounts for 75% of the grade, with an additional 10% coming from a presentation that is worked on outside of class, then presented in class. (This 10% is for the presentation as a whole.)

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

A successful class isn’t measured solely by the high grades of its students. Understanding basic concepts, indeed, is critical, but so too is providing emotional support and basic respect to one’s fellow students. As such, beratement, overt disrespect, and poor attendance will not be tolerated. The work in this class will be difficult, maybe even emotional heart-wrenching. We must assess these works with the same grace and professionalism that we’d expect in return.

Lastly, while attendance is not an official grading criterion, lack of attendance will affect one’s grade detrimentally. Two unexcused absences are the equivalent of 5 points off one’s final grade.
