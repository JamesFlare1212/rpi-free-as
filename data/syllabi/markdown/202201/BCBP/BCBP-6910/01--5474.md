## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Introduction to research in the Royer group. Subjects include protein conformational landscapes, the G1/S transition in budding yeast and transcriptional adaptation to pressure in bacteria. Techniques used are in vitro biophysical approaches such as fluuorescence, NMR, SAXS, and protein production and purification, or in vivo advanced microscopy.
