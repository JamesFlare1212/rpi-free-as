## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understand history of typography and classification systems
- Develop an intelligent and discriminating eye for choosing typefaces in order to convey meaning and evoke a mood
- Learn to use type as image in order to represent a concept.
- Create meaningful relationships between the integration and/or juxtaposition of text with images.
- Understand the rules and conventions and expanding perception and analysis of typographic form and applications including anatomy of type, details of setting type, organizational hierarchy and using a grid system.
- Creatively explore and experiment with type as a visual language.
- Identify appropriate problems to which data communication can be applied through an understanding of typography.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Homework questions  
Reading analysis

**EXERCISES**

**Single Letterform Compositions (5%)** A series of design studies to get you familiar with different characteristics of typefaces and how those characteristics influence meaning.

**Single Word Compositions (5%)** A series of design studies using typography to express meaning of specific words.

**Descriptive Pair Compositions (5%)** Grouping two different words together to design a descriptive pair.

Projects

**Shaping Type (15%)** Shaping typography as image

**Word and Image (15%)** Explores the relationship in a design between a single word or words and an image.

**Protest Poster (15%)** Research and Design of various 20-21century art & design movements and the posters that represented the voices of their times.

**Magazine Design using Data (20%)** This piece approaches a current issue or debate, where students will use research and data to support their position, and communicate the problem and or solution using data, text and integration of type and image.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

HOMEWORK and READING ASSIGNMENTS: (20% - 8 assignments each worth 2.5%)

EXERCISE COMPOSITIONS: (15% - 3 exercises each worth 5%)

PROJECT ASSIGNMENTS (65% - 3 projects each worth15% and 1 worth 20%)

– Aesthetics 25% Does the project have strong aesthetic qualities?

– Conceptual Framework 25% - Are the concepts or ideas behind the project original, and thoughtful

– Technical Skill 25% Does the project use techniques and skills that are challenging and/or demonstrate a mastery of the skills and techniques addressed with quality production value?

– Comp Work 25% How far did you take the ideas. How many variations did you make? Did you push your explorations and make numerous variations on the theme or explore multiple ideas?

Class critiques are provided regularly during the multiple iteration process, giving students ample opportunity to rework their pieces before a final is handed in for a grade.
