## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is a graduate level course.  
Description: Topics include tensor calculus, kinematics accounting for large deformations, stress and strain measures, balance equations, thermodynamics of materials, constitutive equations - introductory concepts.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

M.E. Gurtin, E. Fried, L. Anand, The mechanics and thermodynamics of continua, Cambridge University Press, 2010

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Students will learn the generalized theory of deformation from which all formulations commonly used in engineering are derived, as well as the basics of thermodynamics of continua, including the restrictions imposed on the functional forms of constitutive equations.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Develop an understanding of the fundamental description of the mechanics of solids and fluids
- Develop an understanding of thermodynamics as applied to mechanics of materials.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Homework, Midterm exam, Final exam.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:
