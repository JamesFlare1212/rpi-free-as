## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

#### Course Description

Review of basic aspects of manufacturing engineering including driving forces, quality

attributes, tolerances, etc. Examination of basic principles of mechanics, engineering materials,

analysis of both bulk-forming (forging, extrusion, rolling, etc.) and sheet-forming processes, metal cutting, and other related manufacturing processes. Discussion of the role of computers in

manufacturing.

**The course contains the following key topics:**

1. Introduction
2. Solidification Processing
3. Deformation Processing
4. Machining Processing
5. Heat Treatment Processing
6. Powder Particle Processing
7. Manufacturing Logistics

**Credits Allocated:** 3 semester hours

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students successfully completing this course are able to: • Construct mathematical models of a manufacturing system and use them to estimate values of quantities or parameters of interest in engineering applications.
- • Develop a vision of manufacturing as an integral collection of engineering methods and techniques for the production of hardware components with desired characteristics.

***Icebreaker***

Students shall complete the “Icebreaker” and submit the requested deliverable no later than the first day of classes. The icebreaker assignment consists of the following FOUR items:

- A brief written bio-sketch (~ 100 words long in PDF format).
- A computer screen capture proving that you have downloaded and installed the required software for this class into a computer and that the software is ready for use.
- A signed statement indicating that you have carefully and thoroughly read through this course syllabus and are aware and in agreement with the information and instructions contained therein.
- The specific topic area selected for your term project.

**Milestone Reports**

Students shall demonstrate their grasp of the subject by creating three Milestone reports. Each Milestone will report on progress made on the analysis of their selected manufacturing process. Please note that reports are CUMULATIVE. Detailed constructive feedback will be provided after submission of Milestones 1 and 2 reports. Students will be expected to act on the provided feedback by reviewing and editing their reports as appropriate in preparation for the following report. Deadlines for these reports are as follows:

- Milestone 1: February 6
- Milestone 2: March 13
- Milestone 3: April 24

Milestone reports must demonstrate mastery of the course material through its application to the student’s chosen term project topic. Reports must be carefully edited, well-written, persuasive, typeset documents in order answer to earn full credit. All milestone reports MUST be submitted with a cover page containing your name and the milestone number and in PDF format (see Report Template provided).

While Milestones 1 and 2 shall describe partial progress, Milestone 3 will be a comprehensive document that, while focusing on the most complex version of the student’s chosen topic, must also incorporate material from the reports produced for Milestones 1 and 2.
