## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**Course Description**: Laboratory experiments with primary emphasis on light-weight materials and flexible structures that are used in aircraft, spacecraft and rotorcraft.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

None

## Course Goals

### Goals:

To study the static as well as dynamic (vibration, impact) response of aerospace structures.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

**Experiments:**

1. Materials Selection (Tensile Testing): 20% of grade
2. Structural Stability Investigation (Column Buckling): 20% of grade
3. Forced Vibration (Natural Frequencies and Mode Shapes): 20% of grade
4. Free Vibration (Damping Measurement): 20% of grade
5. Impact Testing (Energy Absorption and Crashworthiness): 20% or grade

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):
