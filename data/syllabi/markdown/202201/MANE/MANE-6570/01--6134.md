## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is a graduate level course. It is aimed to provide the students with familiarity of traditional and modern flow control techniques. To introduce the students the subject of laminar-to-turbulent transition via hydrodynamic stability analysis, which is a crucial component in design and implementation of intelligent flow control strategies.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

• Drazin, P.G. and Reid, W.H., Hydrodynamic Stability, Cambridge University Press, London, United Kingdom 1981 (recommended)

• Gad-el-Hak, M., Flow Control: Passive, Active and Reactive Flow Management, Cambridge University Press, London, UK 2000 (recommended)

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Part 1: Hydrodynamic Stability

1.1 Balance between opposing forces

1.2 Kelvin-Helmholtz Instability

1.3 Method of normal modes: review of Fourier transform

1.4 Temporal vs. spatial wave propagation

1.5 Rayligh Equation – inviscid flows

1.6 Orr-Sommerfeld Equation – viscous flows

1.7 3-D boundary layers

1.8 Non-parallel flows

1.9 Slightly non-linear assumption

Part 2: Flow Control

2.1 Flow control devices

2.2 Flow control strategies

2.2.1 passive vs. active

2.2.2 open vs. close loop control

2.3 Separation control and control of separation

2.4 Control of laminar to turbulent transition

2.5 External flows

2.6 Internal flows

2.7 Drag reduction

2.8 Mixing enhancement
