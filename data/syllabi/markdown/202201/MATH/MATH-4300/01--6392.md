## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will be able to preform symbol manipulation within the complex field
- Students will understand the notion of analyticity and apply it to solve a wide range of problem
- Students will be able to construct Taylor and Laurent series of well known functions
- Students will be able to compute residues and use them to evaluate certain integrals
- Students will be able to use conformal mapping to solve problems arising from electrostatics, fluid flows, and thermodynamics

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

10 problem sets, 3 exams, and optional final

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these.
