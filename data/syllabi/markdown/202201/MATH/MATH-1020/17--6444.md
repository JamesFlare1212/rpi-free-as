## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**Course Description:** Techniques and applications of integration, polar coordinates, parametric equations, infinite sequences and series, vector functions and curves in space, functions of several variables, and partial derivatives. Prerequisite: MATH 1010 (from the [Rensselaer Catalog](http://catalog.rpi.edu/))

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

**Textbook and Achieve:** [Calculus: Early Transcendentals](https://store.macmillanlearning.com/us/product/Calculus-Early-Transcendentals/p/1319050743), Fourth ed., by Jon Rogawski, Colin Adams, and Robert Franzosa, Macmillan Publishers, 2019. We will cover material from chapters 7, 8, and 10–14. Access to the textbook—physical or electronic—is **required**.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Basic symbol manipulation skills pertaining to integration.
- Ability to state and explain basic Calculus definitions and theorems and their applications.
- Ability to describe curves parametrically and apply Calculus concepts and techniques to curves described in this way.
- Ability to express curves via polar equations and to solve Calculus problems in the polar coordinate system.
- Ability to apply Calculus to selected problems in Science, Engineering and Mathematics.
- Ability to use convergence tests to analyze the behavior of infinite series.
- Ability to represent functions using power series and solve problems using these representations.
- Ability to describe lines and planes in space in equation form and solve problems using these representations.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Homework:** Homework will be assigned usually once or twice weekly on Achieve and will be graded automatically by the system. Achieve homeworks are typically due on Tuesday nights on the previous Friday’s lecture material and on Friday nights on the previous Tuesday's lecture material. You are responsible for checking Achieve to make sure you are aware of when homework sections are due on Achieve. There is a grace period where you can request a two-day extension for a missed or incomplete homework section on Achieve. A penalty of 10% per day will be applied to the score and a record of requests will be retained. When assigning your Achieve homework grade for the course, 2-3 homework sections will be dropped and each homework section will be weighted equally.

Additional homework problems will be assigned as necessary from the textbook. These textbook problems will not be collected nor graded, but you are encouraged to do all assigned exercises so that you can determine whether or not you understand the material that is covered in class. Additionally, you will find working these problems beneficial for your quizzes as most problems will be identical or nearly identical to these textbook problems.

**Recitation quizzes:** Quizzes will be given weekly during recitation. Material to be covered on each quiz will be posted on LMS. Students must take quizzes in their assigned sections.

No make-up quizzes will be given for any reason. To compensate for this, the lowest quiz score for each student will be dropped at the end of the semester. Only students who miss a quiz due to an [excused absence documented by the Student Success office](http://studentlife.rpi.edu/student-experience/excused-absence) may be excused from a quiz.

**Exams:** The material covered on each in-class exam will be announced on LMS when the date of the exam is near. Exams will be given during lecture (see **Meeting Times** above).

**All make-up exams will be given during the final exam period** and will be held on a date and time determined by the [Office of the Registrar](https://info.rpi.edu/registrar/grades/#FinalExamScheduleandConflicts). Students will be able to take a make-up exam without penalty only if they provide an [excused absence documented by the Student Success office](https://success.studentlife.rpi.edu/student-success/excused-absence). **Missing an exam should not be taken lightly.** Thus, students missing an exam due to an excused absence should notify the instructor **before the exam begins**. Students who have an excused absence who do not make timely arrangements may be subject to a penalty applied to their score. If a student misses an exam due to an unexcused absence (e.g., did not know there was an exam, overslept, etc.), then the student can still take a make-up exam during the final exam period and a **30% penalty** will be applied to the score.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

**Attendance:** Students are responsible for all material covered in lectures and recitations whether they are present or not. Attendance is expected, but will not be checked.

### Other Course Policies:

**Remote students:** Students who have been approved by RPI to attend classes remotely must notify the instructors as soon as possible so alternate arrangements can be made.

**Extra credit:** No extra credit requests will be considered for any reason. Any requests for extra credit will be ignored.

**Calculator policy:** Calculators will not be allowed on exams or quizzes. Problems will be designed so that this does not create an issue. Occasionally, a calculator may be required to complete a homework problem, but students should not rely on calculators for simple arithmetic calculations.

**Grade appeals:** If upon reviewing an exam or quiz a student feels that his or her work was graded incorrectly, then the student may submit a grading appeal on Gradescope. To request a regrade, the student must type an explanation of the grading issue. For multiple grading issues, the student should summit regrade requests separately for each problem. Students have one week after the exam or quiz was returned on Gradescope to submit an appeal.

**Classroom decorum:** Students must be on time for class and should refrain from leaving and reentering the classroom during class. If a student has a legitimate reason for arriving late to class or being excused early from class, then he or she should discuss this with the instructor before class. Phones may not be used during class and should be silent. Computers and tablets may not be used for anything other than taking notes. Students who are unable to courteously comply with these policies may be asked to leave the room.

**Webex Decorum:** Please adhere to the following during Webex meetings for this course.

- Join Webex meetings promptly at scheduled times.
- Turn your camera on, if at all possible. In case of connectivity issues, upload a photo in your Webex profile, which will be displayed when your camera is off.
- Use good microphone etiquette. Mute your microphone unless you are speaking. If you are having microphone issues, you may participate by using the chat feature.
- Whenever possible, set yourself up to attend meetings in a space where you are not distracted and your surroundings are not distracting to others in the meeting. This is a class and meetings require your attention.

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. ### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Academic integrity:** Students should familiarize themselves with the section on academic integrity in the [Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities](https://info.rpi.edu/sites/default/files/Rensselaer%20Handbook%20of%20Student%20Rights%20&%20Responsibilities%20%C2%BB.pdf) (pp. 16–18). All work submitted on exams and quizzes must be the student’s own. A student may not change an answer on an exam or quiz before submitting it for an appeal. In addition, no calculators, computers, tablets, open books, open notes, phones, headphones, or smartwatches may be used during exams or quizzes. Talking or communicating with other students in any way during exams or quizzes is also prohibited. The minimum academic sanction applied to a student found in violation of the above policies will be a grade of 0 on the assignment. A stronger sanction may be applied if warranted. A report regarding the violation will also be submitted to the Dean of Students Office and will remain on file until graduation. Repeat offenders will fail the course and will be subject to judicial action.
