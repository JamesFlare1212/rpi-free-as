## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Jon Kabat-Zinn said that “Mindfulness means paying attention in a particular way: on purpose, in the present moment, and nonjudgmentally.” Throughout the day we tend to spend much of our time thinking about the past and the future. Mindfulness involves giving particular attention to our moment-to-moment experiences in a way that emphasizes observing, rather than judging or evaluating. Everyday activities such as driving a car or eating a meal provide us the opportunity to practice this focused, moment-to-moment attention. In addition, we can practice mindfulness through formal meditation. There is empirical evidence of the many health benefits of mindfulness meditation, including the potential to produce fundamental changes in brain structure and epigenetic change. In this class, we will discuss the historical and psychological foundations of mindfulness and empirical findings supporting its positive effects. Students will participate in structured exercises. This course is a very hands-on approach to mindfulness…you will be required to practice mindfulness and meditation and develop your mindful skills. It is also more of a discussion course.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

1. **Maya Angelou’s *Letter to My Daughter***

**15 points** Participate in class discussion (and write a 3-4 page paper) highlighting how her book affected you and how it changed (or reinforced, or didn’t change) how you think about life. Submit on LMS

2. **Review of Holly Roger’s *The Mindful Twenty Something* book**

**20 points** Participate in class discussion (and write 1-2 page reflection of the 5 sections as specified below) highlighting how her book affected you and how it changed (or reinforced, or didn’t change) how you think about life. Reflection is due before class begins. Clarification: You will have a total of 4 papers, each worth 5 points. Submit on LMS. Note: this is **NOT** a summary of the readings, but a self-reflection.

3. **Koru App Daily Log**

**10 points** There are daily log opportunities in the app. Make sure you complete 20 days of reflections for 4 weeks during the time we will be discussing this book. Complete 2-3 things you are grateful for each day, and then reflect on the meditation you did that day. Questions you could answer: Which meditation did you do? How did you feel before and after the meditation? How did the meditation impact you?

This assignment is completed in the Koru app.

4. ***Leadership and Self-Deception***

**15 points** Participate in class discussion and share 1 demonstration of a situations where you were in the box and the outcomes of that, and 1 demonstration of a **DIFFERENT** situation where you were out of the box and the outcomes of that. Be prepared to speak for about 5 minutes. Your grade is your class participation of your examples/demonstrations.

5. **Attendance**

**20 Points** This is a communication intensive course. You are encouraged to contribute to class discussions. Attendance will be taken each class. You can miss 3 class meetings without an Institute accepted reason. Beyond 3 missed classed meetings, you must provide documented absence excuse to the Institute. Even on lecture days, we will often break off into group meetings so you do have to be physically present and attending the lecture.

If you are sick, please do not come to class. Let me know you will be absent and I will set up a WebEx meeting so you can still be “present” for class.

If you are a student athlete and you have an away game, please email me the day of class (or the day before) and remind me so that I mark in the attendance book excused absence.

6. **Presentation and Paper**

**20 points** (5 points presentation, 15 points paper) Choose a topic related to mindfulness and write a 3-4 page paper, with 12pt Times New Roman font, double spaced. Submit all parts of this assignment on LMS.

Note: Only the final paper is graded. If drafts are omitted, then 5 point deduction for each missing draft will occur on final paper grade.

Submit all assignments on LMS

- Plagiarizing will result in a grade of 0 on the paper. If you copy material from a source, you must use quotations and a citation.
- Use PsycInfo
- Use APA style citations
- Minimum of 5 citations from empirical research studies
- The Writing Center https://rpi.mywconline.com/
- Presentation is 8-10 min summary of your paper (highlight what you learned)
  - o Have a PowerPoint presentation prepared
  - o Turn on your camera (if we are remote)
  - o Dress appropriately
