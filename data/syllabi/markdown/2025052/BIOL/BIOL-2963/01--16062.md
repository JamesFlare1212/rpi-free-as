## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is an undergraduate level introductory course. It explores fundamental concepts in ornithology, such as bird evolution from dinosaurs, finch evolution on Galapagos Islands, and focuses on the diverse species of birds found in New York habitats such as on our RPI Campus. The goal is to provide active learning in a collaborative environment via interactive discussions. Field Trips to Museums and Parks, such as to the NYS Museum at Albany, Peebles Island State Park, Albany Rural Cemetery are planned. Out-of-town Field Trips are also planned.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Course Content: The course content is divided into the topics/activities per week but not in this particular order:

- Birds - in class and field (weather permitting)
- Dinosaurs - in class (weather permitting)
- Simulations - in class
- Field Trips - NYS Birds, RPI Campus Birds
- Projects - in class

For this course on Birds and in your independent research projects focus on learning:

- Phylogeny - Avian Diversity and Evolution from Dinosaurs
- Adaptation - Feathers and Flight
- Adaptation - Anatomy and Physiology
- Ecology - Food and Foraging
- Ecology - Mating and Migration
- Solutions - Bird Conservation
