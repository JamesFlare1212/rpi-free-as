## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is an undergraduate level introductory course. It explores fundamental concepts in neurobiology such as, the human body’s chemical and electrical signals, how neurons communicate, the action potential, ion channels, neurotoxins, muscle contraction, neurological diseases, as well as animal movement, behavior, and intelligence. The goal is to provide active learning in a collaborative environment via interactive discussions.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

The course content is divided into the following topics:

- Introduction to Neurobiology
- The Action Potential
- Animal Senses
- Human Senses
- Drugs and Medications
- Conclusions

Textbook Readings are recommended to learn the following introductory concepts:

1. Introductory Neurobiology - Hormones, the Endocrine System, and Endocrine Disruptors. The Nervous System, Action Potentials, and Ion Channels.
2. Animal Senses - bones and muscle. Animal Behavior and Intelligence.
3. Human Senses - the human ear, eye, smell and taste receptors. Living Supercomputer - the Human Brain.
