## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is an introductory course in Biology. It is intended for undergraduates, both non-majors and majors. It serves as the first course for life science majors and for students interested in the health professions.

Students generate hypotheses via in silico simulations, and share their ideas in interactive in-person mini-groups. Hands-on in vitro labs impart basic laboratory skills. Field data using AI tools enhance student understanding of RPI campus Biodiversity. Laboratory activities include scientific computing.

The goal is for students to gain biological literacy by applying the scientific method. Students will use quantitative tools, develop data dexterity, and analytical thinking skills and learn basic laboratory skills. This is a D1 Data Intensive Course.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

1. Students will gain basic biological literacy using the Scientific Method.
2. Students will gain introductory data dexterity (D1 level) using R Statistical Language for Biostatistics.
3. Students will learn basic laboratory skills, such as microscopy.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

The laboratory course content is divided into different types of laboratory experiments:

1. In vitro Labs
2. In vivo Labs
3. In silico Labs

Each laboratory draws from fundamental principles from one, two or all of the units, at once, that taught are in the corequisite BIOL 1010 coursework, such as those listed below, but also additional research work from the real world:

1. Ecology
2. Evolution
3. Genetics and Medicine

Thus, each experiment encourages students to find connections between disparate biological concepts and fundamental principles in the textbook, classroom and the real world.

The Scientific Method is applied to understand fundamental concepts in Biology.
