## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Jon Kabat-Zinn said that “Mindfulness means paying attention in a particular way: on purpose, in the present moment, and nonjudgmentally.” Throughout the day we tend to spend much of our time thinking about the past and the future. Mindfulness involves giving particular attention to our moment-to-moment experiences in a way that emphasizes observing, rather than judging or evaluating. Everyday activities such as driving a car or eating a meal provide us the opportunity to practice this focused, moment-to-moment attention. In addition, we can practice mindfulness through formal meditation. There is empirical evidence of the many health benefits of mindfulness meditation, including the potential to produce fundamental changes in brain structure and epigenetic change. In this class, we will discuss the historical and psychological foundations of mindfulness and empirical findings supporting its positive effects. Students will participate in structured exercises. This course is a very hands-on approach to mindfulness…you will be required to practice mindfulness and meditation and develop your mindful skills. It is also more of a discussion course.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

1. **Maya Angelou’s *Letter to My Daughter* (10 pts) June 2nd**

- Paper due prior to class.
- Prepare an (informal) oral overview of 5 different essays from the book (probably the same as what you wrote about in your paper). You will present on one of them in class. Class discussion.
- Write a 2 page paper highlighting how her book affected you and how it changed (or reinforced, or didn’t change) how you think about life.

2. **Review of Holly Roger’s *The Mindful Twenty Something* book (20 pts)**

Write 4 papers that are 1-2 page double-spaced self-reflection and also participate in class discussion. Highlight in your essay how her book affected you and how it changed (or reinforced, or didn’t change) how you think about life. ***Reflection is due before class begins***. Clarification: You will have a total of 4 papers, each worth 5 points. Submit on LMS. Note: this is **NOT** a summary of the readings, but a self-reflection.

3. **Daily Gratitude Journal, 15 points**

Each day in class we will have time to write 3 things you are grateful for and WHY. These are handwritten please.

4. **Big Panda and Tiny Dragon, 15 points (written paper and discuss in class)**

*The Journey: Big Panda and Tiny Dragon.*

Write a 2-page double-spaced paper. Choose a few passages that resonate with you in some way. Explain how the passages impacted you and how they made you think about your own life journey.

We will have “attentive” partner discussions about the quotes and how they resonated with you.

For example: on p. 62 “*I’m worried,”* said Tiny Dragon. “*I don’t know what to do next.” “For just a moment*,” said Big Panda, “*stop, breathe, and listen to the wind in the bamboo.”* This is such an inspiring way to remind me to be in the moment, especially when things are so uncertain. Yesterday I was asked a challenging question and I wasn’t really sure how to answer at first, so I stopped and took a breath to collect my thoughts, and then when I had my answer, I spoke. Listening to the bamboo is a way to clear our minds of the thing we are worried about so we can think of a way to move forward.

5. **Thich Nhat Hanh Presentation, 10 points (May 22nd )**

Class time: research quotes/meditation poems by Thich Nhat Hanh (or someone similar) and choose 2 quotes that resonate with you. Present a short 3-5 minute presentation of what those quotes mean to you, or why they resonate with you. Create a PowerPoint slide with Introduction (your name), and a slide for each quote. You can be creative and add graphics, pictures, and aesthetic creativity to your slides.

6. **Mindfulness Applications, 10 pts (Present Tues June 3rd)**

Choose a partner to work with. Class time will be spent researching an example of a mindfulness study implemented in either an academic, business, military, or medical area. You will prepare a 3-5min presentation of a summary of what was implemented and why, and the outcomes of the research (i.e., the mindfulness intervention impacted…)

You and your partner must both speak in the presentation.
