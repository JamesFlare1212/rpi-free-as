## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course utilizes MMORPGs (Massively Multiplayer Online Role-Playing Games) and other interactive technologies to teach practical Chinese conversations and modern Chinese cultural practices. Immersed in a virtual world of China with simulated real-life scenarios, students are engaged in the learning process and react to various communicative situations as a guest invited to participate in a Championship in Beijing. They learn practical Chinese expressions and cultural practices by interacting with players and NPC (non-character players) when encountering new friends and experiencing Beijing. The automatic speech recognition technologies, problem-solving communicative tasks, and fun-filled game quests enable the students to get a sense of how well his/her pronunciation is and to what extent he/she can survive in the real world of Chinese speaking community. By being exposed virtually to authentic language and cultural content in a modern Chinese environment, students gain interpersonal communication skills and develop intercultural communication awareness.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Through self-guided learning and weekly one-on-one oral practice with the instructor, the main goal of the course is to enhance the student's oral fluency and autonomy of interpersonal speaking in Mandarin Chinese. The performance-based, communicative skill-focused curriculum intends to (1) immerse students in real-life situations in China; (2) expose students to authentic speaking situations and the cultural and linguistic norms across China; (3) allow students to explore virtual and realistic immersive locations and scenarios in China, contextualizing their language use, and providing ownership of knowledge; and (4) connect students with each other and with other learners across the world.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students who successfully complete this course will acquire the following competencies and be able to perform communicative tasks pertinent to Novice Mid and Intermediate High levels of language proficiency in ACTFL scales (depending on the level of entering the course). Specifically, they will be able to: 1. Communicate and exchange information about familiar topics (e.g., foreign currency exchange, hotel check-ins, transportation & directions, food ordering, shopping & price negotiation, etc.). 2. Present information on familiar topics listed above. 3. Understand words, phrases, and sentences appropriate to their level of language proficiency in the above topics 4. Compare the cultural and linguistic differences between their own lives and that of others living in China.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

*Attendance*: The learning of the course materials is student self-paced online. There will be no synchronous sessions for the whole class. However, each student is required to sign-up for a weekly individual meeting with the instructor to practice speaking. The students are also required to practice speaking with the assigned group members after finishing one or two Units throughout the semester.

*Experience Points (EXP)*: EXP indicates how many and how much a student has learned in Fun China World over the week. See details of EXP scale on LMS. The minimum EXP is 60 points each week. More engagement and learning (that is, higher levels of engagement and more EXP earn extra credits).

*Assignments*: In addition to interactive learning in Fun China World, students are required to follow guidelines to complete other assignments posted each Monday on LMS, in “Week by Week”. It is ***the student’s responsibility*** to check out the weekly assignments and carefully spread out the time to complete each assignment before the due date. If an assignment is submitted late, 10% of the possible points will be deducted per 24-hour period after the due time.  
*Project*: There will be a couple of projects in this course. Details on due dates, directions, and rubrics will be posted in the “Course Documents” on LMS.

*Exam*: There will be two exams in this course. One in the week of June 28; one in the week of Aug. 16.

**P****olicy on collaboration and cheating/plagiarism in this course**

It is VERY EASY to spot cheating in a foreign language course. Students are not permitted to turn in work done by someone else. Getting assistance from a native speaker on assignments is OK, but your final product must be your own work as it is easy to identify the excessive structures beyond your level of proficiency. In addition, the use of a WEB TRANSLATE (e.g., Google Translate) for translation other than vocabulary or phrase is not permitted.

The first time I suspect cheating I warn the student and tell why I suspect (e.g. use of non-course grammar or vocabulary) and I take off 50% points of the grade. If it's very obvious I do not give a warning. I will just give a zero. If there is a subsequent infraction the student will receive a grade of F for the course or be reported to the Student's Office.
