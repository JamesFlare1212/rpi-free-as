### Additional Information for Course Information Section:

A selection of modern physics topics with emphasis on concepts and applications of quantum physics and quantum statistics. Topics will include: consequences of exchange symmetry; single and multi-electron atoms, molecules, quantum statistics, periodic potentials (solids), special relativity for particle and nuclear physics, and elementary particles and fields.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Identify when symmetry can be usefully applied to solve intermediate level problems in quantum physics.
- Apply concepts in quantum statistics to solve intermediate problems in electronic or thermal properties of matter.
- Demonstrate understanding of special relativity by showing when and how to use Lorentz transformations in basic relativistic motion and energy problems.
- Demonstrate a deep understanding of how to apply quantum physics and quantum statistics by solving original problems in nuclear, solid state, or particle physics.
- Use computational tools including numerical integration to solve appropriate quantum physics problems.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Homework:** Solving problems and explaining your solutions are significant learning tools in Physics. You should plan on spending at least four hours per week on self-paced assigned homework, practice, and material review. Written assignments will be due on Fridays unless otherwise stated in the assignment. When feasible, I will also supply practice problems that will not be graded.

Late homework is accepted, but with a deduction of 20% for homework submitted after but within 24 hours of the due time, and an additional 10% for each weekday late thereafter up to a maximum deduction of 50%.

**Quizzes:** There will be a weekly quiz, mostly given at the beginning of class on Tuesdays. Quizzes will usually cover the topics and reading from the previous week’s homework and class activities, but may include earlier material if it is relevant. Most quizzes will be timed for 30 minutes, so it is worthwhile to review and make summary notes on the material for yourself. It will be possible to retake a quiz with similar content for a maximum of 75% of the original credit, the purpose being to encourage you to master the material. The quiz retake will be in a window of about 8 hours a few days after the original quiz to give you time to review the material.  
**Major Exams:** There will be a mid-term and a final examination in this course. These are intended to be more comprehensive than topical quizzes.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Students are encouraged to discuss solutions to homework problems with one another before the class due date, but all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. Do not copy someone else’s homework and do not cheat on exams. If submission of any assignment is in violation of this policy, then you will be charged with academic dishonesty, and given a formal letter of reprimand which will be copied to the Dean of Science, Dean of Students, your advisor and the Learning Center. You will receive a non-droppable zero for that work. If submission of any exam is in violation of this policy, then you will receive an ´F´ for the course and you will receive a written warning to be copied to the Dean of Science, Dean of Students, your advisor and the Learning Center and it will become part of your permanent record.

Some examples of cheating:

- Copying another student’s exam, quiz, homework, or software.
- Giving or receiving help on exams or quizzes to or from anyone other than the instructor or proctor.

Some examples of “not cheating”:

- Discussing solution approaches and collaborating on homework and computational problems. (I strongly encourage you to collaborate and may even steer you into groups.)
- Collaborating on in-class activities.

If you have any questions concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask the instructor for clarification.
