## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course aims to deliver fundamentals to understand the colloid and interface phenomena, which primarily originate from the small scales of objects of interests. Qualitative understanding and quantitative assessments on the phenomena will be discussed. Representative topics to be discussed in the course are van der Waals forces, surface phenomena, colloids including association kinds, light scattering, and electrical double layers.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate an understanding of physical models of colloid and interface phenomena.
- Demonstrate an understanding of measurement techniques related to colloids and interfaces.
- Demonstrate an understanding of associative colloids and related interfacial phenomena.
- Demonstrate an understanding of fundamental forces related to colloids and interfaces.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Assessment Measures: Homework and Exams. For the details, see Grading Criteria below.

**Grade Appeal**

A student may appeal a grade by attaching a memo to the specific exam or homework assignment that is being appealed. This memo must state the specific reason(s) for the regrade. The student has one week after the grades have been assigned to contest the grade. The regrading process will be based on proper physical and chemical implication(s) of the answer to the given question. Be professional in the appeal process.
