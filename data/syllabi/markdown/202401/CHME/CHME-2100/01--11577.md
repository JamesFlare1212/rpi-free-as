**CHME 2100 Chemical Process Safety**

**Fall Semester 2023**

**Course Description**

This is a course designed to supplement your lab safety courses and deals with safety protocols and practices associated with chemical process plants. It is designed to provide information necessary for your ABET accredited degree and to assist you in finding an internship and a job**.** The course is offered on-line by the American Institute of Chemical Engineers. It is part of the Safety and Chemical Engineering (SAChE) Certificate program offered by the society. The link is:

https://www.aiche.org/ccps/education/safety-and-chemical-engineering-education-sache-certificate-program

To get “credit” for this course, you should complete the Level I Basic Curriculum. Later in the senior year, you will be able to complete Level II if you wish.

To be eligible for the course, you will have to become a member of AIChE. Membership is free for undergraduate students, but you should also join RPI's Student Chapter as well. The link to sign up for membership is:

https://www.aiche.org/ccps/membership

Once you become a member, you can sign up for the course. The course is free for members.

Upon completing the course and obtaining your certificate, please send a copy to the instructor. This will be used to assign a P/F grade for the course so you can receive credit.
