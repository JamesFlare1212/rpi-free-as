## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course covers materials processing in the area of metals, polymers, ceramics, glasses and electronic materials. Some processes covered include solidification and casting, deformation processing, particulate processing, heat treatment, semiconductor processing and additive processing. Includes laboratories, through which design and analysis of experiments and process modeling are emphasized.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Demonstrate knowledge in the types of available processing methods for metals, polymers, ceramics, glass and electronic materials.
- 2\. Demonstrate the ability to model materials processes and demonstrate knowledge of the changes in materials structure and properties as related to processing parameters.
- 3\. Design, conduct, and analyze experiments related to materials processing.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

There will be 4 lab reports, a group lab report and presentation related to experiments that the students design, in-class problems, two in-semester exams and a final exam to assess student performance in this class. Lab reports will be evaluated using the University of Minnesota Student Writing Guide for lab reports.
