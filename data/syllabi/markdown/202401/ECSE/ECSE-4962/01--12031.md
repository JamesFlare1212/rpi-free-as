### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

1. Demonstrate understanding of the fundamentals of wave confinement and propagation in passive photonic devices such as optical cavities, optical filters, waveguides, optical fibers, cavity theory and waveguide equations and an ability to calculate optical cavity Q-factor, free-spectrum range, single mode condition of waveguides and optical fibers.
2. Demonstrate an ability to explain the working principles of light emitting diodes, laser diodes, photodetectors, photovoltaic devices and optical modulators.
3. Demonstrate understanding of the physics of optical polarization, ordinary and extraordinary waves in birefringent materials, phase retardation plates, liquid crystal display, Pocket effect and Kerr effect, intensity and phase modulators, and an ability to perform simple calculation.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Homework:** There will be 6-8 homework problems assigned in accordance with the quizzes. These problems can be done individually or in a group, but each individual must submit their own homework. Please note that homework grading is based on effort only. In general, **no** late assignments will be accepted. However, extensions may be requested **in writing** **prior to the assignment due date** and evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

**Term paper:** Course term paper will be performed individually and involve choosing a research topic to investigate in depth. Students may choose from a list of recommended topics or propose own topics, subject to instructor approval. Term paper should involve a detailed analysis of the chosen topic, computation validation, survey of relevant technology and state-of-the-art report.

**In-class quiz:** There will be 8 in-class quizzes with each of 20-25 minutes in length. The quiz is open book and open lecture notes. No computer, cell phone, internet access, smart watches are allowed in these quizzes. Students must work independently on these quizzes.
