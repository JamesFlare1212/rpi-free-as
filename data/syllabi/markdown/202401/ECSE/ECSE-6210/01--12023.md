Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Mandatory statement on academic dishonesty:**

- Copying from a neighbor in an examination or turning in someone else's homework/reports/term papers as one's own constitutes academic dishonesty.
- The compilation, ad verbatim reproduction, or paraphrased reproduction of someone else's work in a written or oral report without citation of original source constitutes academic dishonesty.
- What is plagiarism? This is the use of someone else's work without crediting the source.
- What is an “ad verbatim” reproduction? This is an exact word-by-word reproduction.
- What is paraphrased reproduction? This is a reproduction using very similar words and phrases.
- What is a phrase? This is a sequence of two or more words.
- What is the penalty for academic dishonesty? The penalty may result in failing the course.
- Note: Teamwork during class exercises and homework and the use of other resources (calculators, books, etc.) are allowed.
