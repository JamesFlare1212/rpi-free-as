## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Advanced power electronics techniques and systems, as well as their modeling and applications. Topics include advanced power electronics converters with emerging wide bandgap power semiconductors, modeling and analysis, design and control, spectral and dynamic characteristics of pulse-width modulation, ac-dc converters with active power factor correction, soft-switching converters, and EMI filter design, as well as its applications on electric vehicles, more electric aircraft, and grids. Course assignments include projects involving simulation, analysis, and measurement.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

1. Component design
   - Power semiconductors
   - Capacitor
   - Magnetics and inductor
2. Subsystem design
   - Topology and modulation
   - Power stage design
   - Thermal management
   - Filter design, including EMI filter
   - Control and auxiliary hardware, including gate drive, protection, dead-time effect
3. Application considerations
   - Electrified transportation (Electric Vehicles, More Electric Aircrafts)
   - Grids (Energy Storage, Grid-tied Inverters)
4. Converter design and optimization

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- CLO1 - Demonstrate an ability to model power semiconductors based on understanding of their characteristics and to analyze the behavior of power semiconductors in power electronics circuits, in particular, wide bandgap (WBG) power semiconductors
- CLO2 - Demonstrate an ability to apply method, models, and tools to design and analyze key components and subsystems in power electronics converters, including passives, topologies, thermal management, gate driver and protection
- CLO3 - Demonstrate an ability to identify and solve the important source and load interface issues, including harmonics, EMI noise, dv/dt noise
- CLO4 - Discuss and compare the development of power electronics converters for different compelling applications, such as electric vehicles, more electric aircrafts, and grids

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Four homework, two projects (one take-home mid-term exam and one final project), and one student presentation are designed to measure the CLOs defined above.

- Homework 1- Wide bandgap switching analysis to measure CLO1
- Homework 2 - Magnetics and inductor design to measure CLO2
- Homework 3 - Gate driver design and analysis to measure CLO2
- Homework 4 - EMI filter design to measure CLO3
- Project 1 (take-home mid-term exam) - Thermal management and loss analysis with topologies and modulation to measure CLO2 and CLO4
- Project 2 (final project) - Filter design and weight breakdown and analysis dependence on applications to measure all CLOs
