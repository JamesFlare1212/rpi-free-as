## Course Goals

### Goals:

Part II of a two-semester sequence focusing on the chemistry, structure, and function of biological molecules, macromolecules, and systems. Topics include lipids and lipid metabolism, amino acid metabolism and the coenzymes involved in this metabolism, nucleic acid synthesis and chemistry, protein synthesis and degradation, integration of metabolism, photobiology, and photosynthesis.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will be able to explain the biochemical drivers of photosynthesis and how ATP is generated.
- Students will be able to explain describe the mechanisms involved in lipid and amino acid synthesis and metabolism.
- Students will be able to explain describe how cellular metabolic pathways are integrated.
- Students will be able to explain describe the structure function relationship and mechanism of action of key enzymes involved in the metabolism of biological molecules.
- Students will be able to explain describe how proteins and nucleic acids are synthesized.
