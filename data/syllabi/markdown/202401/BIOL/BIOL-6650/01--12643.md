### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

## At the end of this course, a successful 6000-level student will be able to:

## · Demonstrate the knowledge of the meaning of Systems biology and have a basic comprehension of the components that are necessary to investigate biological questions on the systems scale.

## · Demonstrate familiarity with modern -omics scale experimentation and comprehend the strengths and weaknesses of each technique.

## · Demonstrate a basic comprehension of the computational tools involved in systems biology.

## · Demonstrate the ability to read and critically review primary scientific literature in the field of Systems biology.

## · Develop the ability to formulate a mini proposal for next-step research in Systems Biology.

## · Demonstrate the ability to synthesize information on a systems biology topic into a cohesive presentation.
