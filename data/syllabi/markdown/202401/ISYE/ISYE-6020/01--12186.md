## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course deals with the design of experiments concepts; how to design single and multiple factors experiments; statistical analysis of the data from experiments including the application of analysis of variance (ANOVA), full factorial design, fractional factorial design, factor screening, and related statistical methods. The goals are to learn how to plan, design, and conduct experiments efficiently and effectively and learn how to analyze the resulting data to obtain objective conclusions. Experimental design and analysis and different DOE applications in the industry including cases and examples are investigated. This course is intended for students who have completed the first course in statistical methods. This background course should include at least some techniques of descriptive statistics, standard sampling distributions, and an introduction to basic concepts of the confidence interval and hypothesis testing for means and variances.
