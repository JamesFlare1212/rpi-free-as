## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

**Lab Grading Rubric**

***Participation, completion and data collection: These criteria examine whether the student is actively involved in the required lab activities and gathered all the required data***

| **Points** | **Criteria** |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Student makes little to no effort to fully complete lab. Data collection blank or missing most of the critical information; if drawings are needed, they are missing |
| 3-1* | Student shows moderate effort to organize lab information; some missing and incomplete answers |
| 6-4* | Some data present, but many of the critical values are missing or clearly incorrect; some drawings are missing or incomprehensible |
| 9-7* | All data present and generally correct, but poorly organized or unclear; drawings are messy and/or poorly labeled |
| 10 | All data present and correct; clearly organized; drawings are neat and well labeled. Answers are complete |

***Content: These criteria focus on analyses based on the data collected and questions asked related to the results.***

| **Points** | **Criteria** |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Missing or highly inaccurate interpretations of the data; answers to all or most questions missing; misunderstandings of terms and concepts |
| 3-1* | Poor job discussing the results; major flaws in reasoning or explanations; not all questions answered |
| 6-4* | Ok job discussing the results; flaws in reasoning or explanations; answers not fully developed |
| 9-7* | Good job answering questions and interpreting the data, but too general, cursory, or incomplete **or** with someinaccuracies or flaws in reasoning |
| 10 | Excellent answering questions and interpreting the data; questions answered fully with no inaccuracies |

**note about Rubric---the points assigned is a range. You may often see an adjustment to your grade to distinguish 7 from 9 points in the good category (for example)*

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

**Attendance:** Geology is an observational science, and as such, laboratory sessions provide an excellent opportunity to learn geologic concepts and methods though applied activities. As such, attendance is required! There are **NO**excused absences for lab. ***The lowest lab grade will be dropped***, in order to allow every student the opportunity to miss one lab due to illness, a schedule conflict with a game, etc., without penalty. Make-up labs are very difficult to arrange and are impossible in the case of field outings, so if you know you will be missing more than one lab, you need to work with Dr. Cadieux in advance. If you have a conflict but are able to attend a different lab section, please let Dr. Cadieux know in advance.

### Other Course Policies:

**Tardiness:** Being on time for lab is critical in order to have enough time to complete the assignment! When we go on a field trip we will leave promptly at the start of lab; being late could lead to missing the trip.

**Late Policy**: Labs will generally be due at the end of the lab period. If you need additional time, you can turn in at the beginning of the next lab session. Labs turned in after this will receive 10% deduction for each day late. Late labs will not be accepted after 1 week.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Working together and plagiarism:** Science is a collaborative subject! You are strongly encouraged to discuss course material with your classmates. Working together allows students to ask each other questions and brainstorm ideas together, but the resulting work should reflect each student’s own understanding of the topic. On labs where you are allowed to share results with your peers, specific instructions will be provided. However, unless specifically told otherwise, you should not be sharing your written or drawn work for submission with classmates. ***Submission of any work that is not your own will result in a failing grade for the course.***
