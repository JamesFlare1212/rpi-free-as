## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

The course will follow ‘Computational Physics: Problem Solving With Python’ Rubin H. Landau, Manuel Jos Pez Meja, Cristian C. Bordeianu Third revised edition. This textbook is available from the RPI library as an electronic resource. Lecture videos will be made available on LMS. Lecture notes will be made available on LMS. LMS will be the main means for information sharing (including all homework submission)  
For Python beginners the following resource may be useful but is not required: Lin, J. W.-B., Aizenman, H.,  
Manette Cartas Espinel, E., Gunnerson, K., & Liu, J. (2022). An Introduction to Python Programming for Scientists  
and Engineers. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Students will learn when to apply computational tools to solve physics problems. Students will learn computational methods that will enable the exploration of the physical world.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

1\. Computing Basics, Error and number representation  
2\. Numerical Integration and Differentiation  
3\. Root Finding: Trials and Error  
4\. Introduction to Random Numbers

5\. Monte Carlo Applications  
6\. Metropolis Algorithm and Ising Model  
7\. Data Fitting  
8\. Ordinary Differential Equations  
9\. ODE and 1D quantum mechanics

10\. Partial Differential Equations (PDE)  
11\. Heat Equation and PDEs  
12\. String, Membranes and PDEs  
13\. Matrix Algebra Part 1: Matrix Inversion  
14\. Eigenvalue problem

15\. Machine learning  
16\. Neural networks and GPUs  
17\. Fourier Analysis  
18\. Neural Networks applied to Image processing  
19\. Quantum computing

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will be able understand concepts in computing basics such as: error and number representation
- Students will learn to apply numerical computing so solve physics problems. They will learn when best to apply computational tools and will learn about the limitations of applying these tools.
- Students will be able to calculate integrals and derivates numerically.
- Students will be able to learn computational methods, such as Monte Carlo to tackle problems in physics
- Students will be able to learn how to solve differential equations using numerical techniques and will apply this knowledge to various problems in physics.
- Students will be able to perform matrix algebra numerically and apply these tools to solve physics problems
- Students will learn how to leverage artificial intelligence to solve problems in physics
- Students will learn about quantum computing and use a quantum computer to perform simulations
