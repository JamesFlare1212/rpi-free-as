## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Catalog: This course surveys 5000 years of game history, from ancient Sumer/Sumerian to the latest next-generation consoles and MMOGs. In parallel with this historical tour, several major theories will be examined about the nature of play and the nature of games. Along the way, it will also look at how games and play influence the cultures they are found in, and how culture in turn influences how people structure their leisure time will also be considered.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

- Develop Research skills
  - o Evaluate the reliability of sources
  - o Utilize and discuss limits of AI research tools
  - o Use APA format
- Research topic
  - o Build a cohesive understanding of their topic using various media forms
  - o Relate multiple aspects of game history and culture to their chosen topic
  - o Write annotated bibliography of 10 or more sources
  - o Succinctly summarize research for classmates to easily understand
  - o Analyze classmates’ research to see how it fits into one’s own topic of inquiry
  - o Create a presentation that gives classmates an in-depth exploration of their topic
  - o Give constructive written feedback to peers
- Play games that demonstrate time periods/genres
  - o Analyze games from a chosen perspective
  - o Justify the chosen perspective
