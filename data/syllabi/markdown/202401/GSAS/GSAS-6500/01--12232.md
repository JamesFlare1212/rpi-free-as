## Course Goals

### Goals:

We will be exploring game design, evaluation and testing through the lens of cognitive science, including cognitive load theory, cognitive psychology user oriented design principles and the evolving practices of the field under the auspices of "User Experience".

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Advanced Game Design and UX Methods is a studio course that combines theory and practice. Students will learn about experimental design and methods for User Oriented Design and HCI, as well as the principles of user interface design. Students will also practice reading and writing research papers, as well as designing a game or conducting user studies.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):
