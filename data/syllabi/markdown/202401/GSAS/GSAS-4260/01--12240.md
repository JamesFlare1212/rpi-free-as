## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Game Narrative Design moves the focus more squarely onto the design elements of game writing – most especially integration with gameplay design structure – and away from line-by-line writing. This course is for undergraduates.

This course focuses on design elements in video games, specifically the ones impacting on a title’s storyline, character development, plot, and player’s interaction with the narrative.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

- Learn core tenets of game narrative design techniques, principles and approaches. Including:
  - o Storytelling through systems
  - o The ubiquity of storytelling avenues within a game
  - o The delineation of game real estate for storytelling
  - o Analyzing and designing interactive structures for storytelling
  - o Analyzing and designing other interactive elements for storytelling
  - o Mechanics Matching
  - o Narrative design with game levels
  - o Systematizing storytelling
- Learn how to generate creative responses to case study examples to become proficient in a variety of narrative design approaches
