## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Practice articulating their ideas in written and oral formats
- Learn concepts including technological determinism, the social construction of technology, human-centered design and standpoint theory through: Regular assigned readings Class discussion Questions to the instructors in writing or in class discussion
- Demonstrate their understanding of course concepts by: Stating what they’ve learned in writing and in class discussion Applying the concepts they’ve learned in new contexts through writing a final analytic paper

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Class Discussion  
Regular, short-form writing  
Final Analytic Paper

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Class / Small Group Participation – 30%  
Journal Entries – 30%  
Final Paper – 40%  
Assignment Submission  
All assignments are to be submitted through the LMS unless other arrangements are made with the instructor. Files must be of type .doc, .docx, .pdf, or .txt unless otherwise instructed. Google Docs are NOT acceptable.  
Should you encounter technical problems, contact the RPI helpdesk at https://support.rpi.edu/.  
Late Submissions  
All assignments are due at 11:59 PM the night before the assignment date unless otherwise stated. This rule is not flexible. I encourage you to complete and submit the assignment early to avoid encountering any problems with this deadline.  
Late journal entries are not accepted. All other late assignments will be docked 15% per class meeting. If the assignment is more than three class meetings late, it will not be accepted.  
No work whatsoever will be accepted after April 24th, 2024 at 11:59 PM.  
Extensions and Excused Assignments  
If you need a deadline extended or to be excused from an assignment, you have 4 “passes” in this class. Each pass may be used to skip an assignment entirely. For example, if you are out of school sick for a week, rather than contact your doctor to obtain documentation of your illness, all you need to do is use your passes to be excused from that week’s assignments altogether. You do not have to tell me why you are using your passes. Any reason or no reason at all is sufficient. You may use them at any time, which means you can wait until the end of the semester to decide which assignments you wish to skip.  
There will be no other extensions or excused assignments, so you must use these wisely. If you use them up and then later feel that you need additional assignments extended, that will not be an option.  
The purpose of this policy is: to decrease the amount of work both on your part and mine when you have to miss school, to remove any need for you to convince either me or the anyone else here that your reasons for requesting an extension are valid, and to give you the responsibility for making decisions about how urgent your need for an extension is.  
In order to use a pass, just type “pass” in the comments of the assignment on the LMS. (You may have to submit a blank file in order to post a comment.)  
This policy does not apply to the final paper. You may not apply a pass to an assignment you have already submitted (because this is not a tool for improving a grade you were

## Policies

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).
