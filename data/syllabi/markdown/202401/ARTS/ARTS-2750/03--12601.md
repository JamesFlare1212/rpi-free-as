## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Private cello lessons for undergraduate students: Students will receive 50-minute weekly cello lessons designed to help the students achieve higher levels of musical and technical skills. Topics and activities may vary depending on the students personal goals and level of performance.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Suggested Materials:  
Galamian Scale System for Violoncello, Arr. Hans Jorgen Jensen

## Course Goals

### Goals:

To improve the students ability to play and perform on their instrument, and expand the student’s knowledge of their craft and its repertoire. Specific goals will vary from student to student.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Development of tone,  
Accuracy of intonation,  
Meaningful, musical and natural physical motions  
Interpretation of notated music  
Practicing techniques  
Stylistic understanding and historical context

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

**Grading:** Individual Lessons = % 80 Studio Performance = %20

Grading for each individual lesson will be determined on the following:

1\) The student’s progress made from the previous lesson.

2\) The student’s ability to incorporate the skill learned.

3\) The student’s level of participation during each lesson.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

**Attendance Policy:** Be on time to all of your lessons. The instructor will only wait for you for 10 minutes and then is free to leave. **Failure to attend a lesson, without notification, will result in and F for that lesson.** If a student needs to cancel a lesson he/she must do so at least 24 hours in advance. The instructor is not required to make up lessons cancelled with less than 24 hours notice.
