### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Learning Outcomes:**

Students who successfully complete this course:

- \- will be able to communicate effectively through visual design using design elements to create engaging and well conceived images.
- \- will demonstrate the ability to discuss articulately their own and other students’ work through group discussions and critiques of student work.
- \- will be able to discuss and critically analyze works of art from the history of art and contemporary art and to apply concepts and strategies of pictorial design that they have learned to their own work.
- \- will demonstrate their understanding of design elements by the production of a well-designed, high quality, coherent portfolio to be presented at the end of the semester.
