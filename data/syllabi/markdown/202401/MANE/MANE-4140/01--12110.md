## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course provides an understanding of computational methods used in fluid dynamics, and hands-on experience in solving complex flow problems. Students will learn about computational fluid dynamics methods, write code, solve benchmark problems, and analyze the stability and convergence of algorithms. They will use CFD package(s) to solve problems motivated by complex engineering applications, learn how to represent geometry, design and generate mesh, select turbulence models, pick solution procedures, set numerical parameters and post-process data.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

1. Present the basic principles of computational methods applied to solve fluid flow problems.
2. Develop and implement simple CFD algorithms.
3. Solve complex fluid flow problems using a software package.
4. Select appropriate turbulence model along with numerical discretization and parameters (mesh size, time-step, nonlinear tolerance, etc.) when solving fluid dynamics problems.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

1. Introduction to advection-diffusion equation
2. Introduction to the Navier-Stokes equations
3. Introduction to computational methods (finite volume, and possibly finite difference and/or finite element)

4. Flux approximation methods: central differences, upwind methods, TVD schemes, etc.
5. Time discretization methods: explicit and implicit
6. Different types of boundary conditions
7. Convergence of a numerical method: accuracy and stability
8. Turbulence modeling: popular RANS, DES and LES approaches
9. Meshing for boundary layers and shocks
10. Computation for several problems of interest: pipe flows, flow over wings, turbulence, etc.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Students should be able to formulate and correctly use the basic principles of computational fluid dynamics: numerical methods, procedures, discretization, convergence, accuracy and stability.
- 2\. Students should understand and apply computational concepts such as upwind methods, higher-order methods, methods for discontinuities or under resolved solution features.
- 3\. Students should able to independently use a CFD software package to solve relevant engineering problems, i.e., in aeronautical and mechanical engineering.
- 4\. Students should understand and apply concepts such as mesh convergence, time integration/stepping, turbulence models and accounting for boundary layers and shocks.
