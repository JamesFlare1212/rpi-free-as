## Course Goals

### Goals:

The objective of this course is to introduce the concepts involving nonlinearities in the analysis of solids and structures using the finite element method in a practice-oriented platform. In addition to covering the theory behind the nonlinear behavior of engineering structures in response to loads and constraints, the course will dwell in simulating such behavior in various materials and applications. Students will be exposed to utilizing commercial finite elements software for a well-rounded experience.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

- Review of linear finite element concepts
- Nonlinear continuum mechanics of solids
  - o finite strain and stress measures,
  - o total and updated Lagrangian approaches,
  - o variational methods – virtual work principle.
- Non-linear finite element procedures
  - o incremental solution of nonlinear equations,
  - o Newton-Raphson method.
- Finite elements for elasto-plasticity
- Finite elements for hyper-elasticity
- Contact of solids using finite elements
