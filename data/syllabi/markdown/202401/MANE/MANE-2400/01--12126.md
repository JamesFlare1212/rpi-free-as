## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- \- Explain basic concepts of on nuclear reactor systems and types
- \- Describe fundamental physical phenomena in nuclear reactor
- \- Explain major interactions of neutron for its moderation
- \- Describe the relationship between binding energy of nucleons and fission
- \- Explain interactions of gamma radiation and charged particles with matters
- \- Calculate criticality of nuclear reaction using neutron diffusion equation
- \- Describe nuclear fuel cycles
- \- Calculate reactor kinetics using six precursor group model
- \- Describe reactor safety factors
- \- Analyze nuclear plant economics

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Nine homework sets and three exams will be used to access students learning outcomes: nuclear reactor systems and types; basic reactor physics, criticality calculations using neutron diffusion equation; nuclear cycles analysis; reactor kinetics calculations using six precursor group model; reactor safety and economics analysis
