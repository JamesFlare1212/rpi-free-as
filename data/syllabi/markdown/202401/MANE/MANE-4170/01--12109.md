## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**Catalog Description:** The principles of dynamics as applied to the analysis of the accelerations and dynamic forces in machines and machine components such as linkages, cams, and gears. The effect these dynamic forces have on the dynamic balance and operation of the machines and the attending stresses in the individual components of the machines.

**Course Overview**: General spatial treatment of kinematics, mass distribution and kinetics; Intermediate dynamics using vector and energy methods; Analysis of motion governed by differential equations formulated from either vector or energy methods.

**Course Objective:** To develop an understanding of the interrelationships of motion, kinematic constraints, forces, and moments as they apply to the general 3-dimensional dynamic behavior of mechanisms and machine elements.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

**Course Topics**: (tentative)

- Kinematics: Review of basic vector algebra. Body-attached coordinate systems and coordinate transformation. Angular velocity, and differentiation of vectors. Position vectors, and velocity and acceleration. Closed loop mechanisms and constraints.
- Mass distribution: system of particles – Motion of center of mass, Moment of momentum and rotational motion about the center of mass; Rigid bodies – Inertia matrix, Principal axes of a rigid body
- Kinetics: Newton-Euler Methods. Gyroscopic effects.
- Advanced Topics: Lagrange’s Equations: Kinetic and potential energies. Introduction to Lagrange’s equations. Generalized forces.
- Applications to Machine Dynamics: Equilibrium and stability. Linearizing equations of motion. Free and forced response. Vibration, absorption and measurement. Introduction to rotor dynamics.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Identify coordinates and reference frames and basis transformations suitable for kinematic analysis
- Identify and formulate appropriate expressions for position, velocity and acceleration for unconstrained and constrained problems
- Decompose systems into simple rotational and translational elements for analysis
- Understand and utilize center of mass and inertia properties
- Derive and utilize Newton-Euler equations to formulate equations of motion
- Learn methods to solve equations of motion and extract forces
- Introduce Lagrange’s equations
