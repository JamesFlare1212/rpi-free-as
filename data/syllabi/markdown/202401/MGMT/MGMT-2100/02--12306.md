## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is an introductory course in business statistics. This course will provide participants the quantitative foundations for statistical methods, which will be used extensively in future management courses. In this course, students will learn the basic concepts and tools in statistics, and then apply the techniques on phenomena of interest. In particular, students learn to present and describe data, analyze probability distributions, make statistical inferences based on data samples, and develop models for prediction and forecasting.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

No Course Learning Outcomes are available for this course.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Students are expected to achieve the following outcomes after successfully completing this course:

1. Demonstrate and understand the basic concepts and tools in business statistics.
2. Distinguish between different statistical tests and identify the usage of different tests
3. Conduct statistical tests of hypothesis and infer conclusions.
4. Use software for statistical analysis and apply statistical methods in a real business environment.
5. Discuss the ethical considerations of data analysis.
