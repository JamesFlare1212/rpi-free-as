## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

## COURSE DESCRIPTION:

Overview of risk management, concepts and measurement of risk; types of risks (market, credit, liquidity, operational, business, strategic). Clustering, classification and optimization tools applied to risk management. Frameworks and instruments available for risk management. Specific issues related with managing specific risk types — credit, liquidity and operational risk, with emphasis on current challenges. Securitization, asset-liability management, and role of insurance for risk management.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

## COURSE GOALS/OBJECTIVES

1. Overview of Risk Management; Types of Risks (Market, Credit, Liquidity, Operational, Business, Strategic).
2. Concepts, tools and instruments available for risk assessment, analytics, and management.
3. Managing: Market Risk; Credit Risk; Interest-rate and Liquidity Risk; Operational Risk.
4. Role of insurance for corporate and personal risk management.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Can differentiate between different Types of Risks (Market, Credit, Liquidity, Operational, Business, and Strategic) and measures for Risk.
- Apply the terminology, concepts, and tools available for risk analytics and management.
- Implement risk management framework for specific issues and specific risk types, Market, Credit, Interest-rate, Liquidity Risk, and Operational Risk.
- Apply the concepts underlying insurance and strategy for risk management using insurance.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

## COURSE ASSESSMENT MEASURES

Projects: Two (2) projects will be done in groups of 2 or 3. Additional details on team formation and project description will be provided before projects begin.

Homework: Reading and research homework assigned weekly. Quantitative homework assigned roughly every other week. Discussions with instructor are strongly encouraged, but eventual implementation, analysis and writing must be student’s own work.

The homework assignments will be due through course’s LMS space and must be readily legible. No late homework or e-mail submissions will be accepted. Although submission of code/Excel work is necessary, the actual homework should be reported in a word processor such as MS Word.

All project or homework assignments done in groups will have a mandatory requirement of submitting a peer contribution and review report submitted individually by each group member. The expectation in this course is to develop strong team coordination and collaboration, and all course participants to strive for that goal throughout the term.

Exams: First exam will be roughly half-way into the course, the second exam will be towards the end. The second exam will be non-cumulative and based only on the second half of the course. There is no final exam.

Software: Microsoft Excel, R, Matlab or Python.
