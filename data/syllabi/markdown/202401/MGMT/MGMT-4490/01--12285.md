## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Every day, you are exposed to hundreds of advertisements and promotions encouraging you to behave in a certain way; to buy something, to vote for someone, to volunteer for a cause. This cross-listed course focuses on the theories, frameworks, and tactics that underpin this highly visible aspect of marketing under the umbrella of “Integrated marketing communication” (IMC). IMC is a cross-functional process for creating profitable relationships with customers and publics by strategically controlling all messages sent to groups in a consistent, distinctive, and memorable way. Students learn to integrate marketing communication tools (e.g., advertising, public relations, sales promotion, event marketing, direct and digital marketing, and personal selling) to advance an organization’s success. This is an interactive course wherein students will develop skills in creating, planning, implementing, and evaluating client-oriented projects by developing an IMC campaign. Students are responsible for applying the content in Marketing Principles (MGMT 2430) to the project in this course. Students need to be especially familiar with the material pertaining to market segmentation and audience targeting, promotion, consumer decision making, segmenting, targeting, differentiation, and value positioning.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The goal of the course is to help you understand how to develop an innovative and effective integrated marketing communications plan with potential to make a positive, measurable impact for an organization. The best way to learn about creative communications is to dive in and, as such, students should expect interactive exercises and regular explorations of what’s happening in the “real world.” There will be varied learning opportunities in formats such as traditional lecture, in-class exercises, assignments, guest lectures, and an experiential group project.

The objectives of this course are:

1. To help students gain an understanding of target customer needs and how IMC is being used by marketers to develop brand identities that leverage those needs
2. To help students identify and explain the major components of the IMC process
3. To acquaint students with analyzing IMC situations and problems
4. To provide students with the opportunity to develop skills in creating, implementing and evaluating a variety of theory-grounded media for today’s omnichannel landscape
5. To challenge students to consider ethics, diversity and global issues relevant to marketing communication
