## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

*I do not seek, I find. To seek is to depart from known facts wiling to discover something known within the new. To find, that is what is totally new, even in movement. All paths are open, and what is found is something completely unknown. It is a risk, a sacred adventure. The uncertainty of such risk can only be assumed by those who feel at ease and protected when in a state of absolute exposure, those who feel steered while being confused, those who in obscurity can abandon themselves to an invisible star and feel attracted by their destiny, instead of fixing it through human limitations. That aperture to new knowledge, to all new interior and exterior experience, that is the essence of the modern human being, who, despite the fear of being carried away, experiences the grace of being protected by the manifestation of new possibilities.* Pablo Picasso

Suddenly, artificial intelligence has become public matter, as if fell from the sky, generating a commotion that we still do not know what form it will take, probably that of an altogether new naturalization of technology, of a new state of things rapidly absorbed and turned into a common resource, made available at every device or application, yet another form of *whateverness*. Both generic and specific, potent and trivial, tailored and depersonalized, opaque and transparent, amicable and repugnant, fascinating and terrifying, it has become theme and *métier* of everybody in equal measure. My mother uses it without knowing what it is about, my father constantly speaks of it, children play with it unconsciously, its ubiquity entertains everybody inside its hazy black box. Unintelligible overflow that makes system with the idea that nothing can be truly new, artificial intelligence is capable to produce simultaneously everything, and nothing meaningful. Prolific and inscrutable, it is the ultimate machine of proliferation of nonsense.

Yet rather than confronting this nonsensicality with skepticism, the challenge of this design-research seminar will be to embrace the renewed promise of its broader specter of sense. We shall investigate the capabilities of Midjourney to produce an instantaneous architecture culture through the passionate consumption and the incommensurable proliferation of images. Using as a data base the most manifest canonical work of the 100 most influential of the past five centuries as a common platform (from Leo Battista Alberti to Rem Koolhaas), we shall extemporaneously navigate the historical sea of architecture, thinking-by-design, attentively but openly, its potential becoming process, without pleading, celebrating, or honoring its orthodoxy, and rather seriously playing through meanings, senses, and implications. Classes will work as short brainstorming sessions of intuitive thinking (intuition as a method to produce hyperculture), learning to see architecture, identifying genius, formulating ideas, and building up new forms of sense.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

01 To study and conceptualize an architectural precedent,  
02 To denaturalize historical materials and explore their variation,  
03 To turn the organization of a project into a computational instruction,  
04 To develop rigorous intuitive sensitivity out of a methodical approach to AI,  
05 To synthetize a culturally relevant architectural proposal.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

The procedure will produce a mosaic of elemental projects out of a chosen historical architectural material progressively mixed with 90 other built or unbuilt projects, which will be selected from an extensive list of canonical projects of the past five centuries. Through the use of blending tools, divergent directions of the chosen project will be recognized, sharpened, singularized, and synthesized conceptually, thus expanding the spectrum of architecture culture by means of versions of a historical canon. The course will work in four stages: 01 investigation and construction of an archive of images, 02 description of the material and formulation of a general hypothesis, 03 development and specification of blends, 04 definition of ideas, rewriting of hypothesis, and final presentation. These stages will mutually redefine each other as they unfold along the duration of the seminar.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

The work will be developed individually. Classes will consist of presentations and critiques. The course is organized in four stages, which will be evaluated both autonomously and as a whole through the submission of a final portfolio:

Stage 01 investigation and construction of an archive of images, which will be evaluated according to its extensiveness and the quality of its images  
Stage 02 description of the material and formulation of hypothesis, which will be evaluated according to its conceptual clarity and its material rigor  
Stage 03 development of blends and successive specification of architectural ideas, which will be evaluated according to the specificity of its attention  
Stage 04 description of new projects and conceptualization of a new spectrum of sensibility, which will be evaluated according to its creativity and its formal rigor
