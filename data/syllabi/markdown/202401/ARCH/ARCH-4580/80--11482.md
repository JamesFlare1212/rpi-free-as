## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Outcome 1: Situate Materials Culturally and Historically: (BArch + Graduate) Explain the first principles of material families and be able to make reasoned material selections within the design process using Granta Edupack Software and research (Graduate) Situate materials culturally within the framework of Materials, Energy & Information for the Masters Thesis
- Outcome 2: Use Material Principles for their Selection: (BArch + Graduate) Explain the first principles of material families and be able to make reasoned material selections within the design process using Granta Edupack Software and research (Graduate) Situate mechanical and thermal performance of materials within the framework of Materials, Energy & Information for the Masters Thesis
- Outcome 3: Design and Analyze Material Assemblies: (BArch + Graduate) Propose emerging material systems and assemblies based on performance criteria. Develop testing and analysis methodologies of material based investigations (Graduate) Situate material assemblies within the framework of Materials, Energy & Information for the Masters Thesis
- Outcome 4: Design Circular Material Manufacturing and Supply Chains: (BArch + Graduate) Integrate principles of economies involved in material systems and production methods and propose appropriate systems of manufacturing and supply chains that promote circular economies of materials (Graduate) Situate material manufacturing methods, supply chains and economies within the framework of Materials, Energy & Information for the Masters Thesis
- Outcome 5: Develop a Multi-modal Material Proposal: (BArch + Graduate) Calculate and project the implications - structural, energetic, perceptive, social etc. - of material selections and their effect on the built environment within local and global ecologies through Life Cycle Analysis and research (Graduate) Situate the integrated material proposal within the framework of Materials, Energy & Information for the Masters Thesis

## Policies

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Students are expected to attend all classes. In the event of sickness, a certificate from a medical practitioner indicating the period of absence must be presented to the Program Manager within seven days of the absence. An unsatisfactory record of attendance may result in a failing grade, and/or dismissal from the program. Students are expected to participate actively in all class discussions, studio and laboratory, and project reviews. They are expected to come to class with all required reading and assignments completed. On time attendance at all scheduled meeting is mandatory. Late assignments will not be accepted.
