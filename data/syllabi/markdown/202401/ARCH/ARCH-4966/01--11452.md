## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The Built Environment is undergoing a significant shift from one that is configured from material chosen for its very inertness (strength, hardness, durability), for its unchanging character to one that is chosen for its capacity for dynamic change in response to shifts in conditions, program, or needs. This can be seen in ‘smart cities’, in buildings that are capable of autonomous regulation and response, and at the scale of the room in environments like the CRAIVE lab or the CIR at EMPAC. Environments are becoming essentially robotic with sensing, actuation, and communication capabilities. In this case, however, we inhabit the belly of the machine.

Responsive environments would seem to be fundamentally a technological project, but perhaps architecture too is fundamentally technological. It is, of course, also and simultaneously a profoundly cultural one. Our investigation of responsive environments will engage this dual nature but will emphasize its cultural aspects.

The seminar will explore the role of a technologized environment to support cultural productions, in this case, an opera for performers who do not have biological voices but instead speak and perform with synthetic voices.

This project will be a collaboration with Robert Whalen of HASS, who is developing the opera, and his students in Choir together with external artists to produce the ‘sketch’ for an Opera for Synthetic Voice. We will concentrate on developing methods of connecting events within the opera to visual and auditory content displayed in the performing environment, the CRAIVE lab. We will work towards crafting an environment responsive to individual differences. The ‘sketch’ will be a public performance, a prototype which in broad outlines suggests a potential future development of the performance while also being an independent artistic work.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The seminar will be a project-based interdisciplinary collaboration that looks at cultural ramifications of the technological substrates that both enable and constrain individuals of varying abilities. Its a loaded topic one that will require sensitivity and creativity, but promises a rewarding experience, both emotionally and creatively. The production of the sketch for an opera will require the acquisition of skills related to audio and visual production in technologically sophisticated venure such as EMPAC or CRAIVE-lab.
