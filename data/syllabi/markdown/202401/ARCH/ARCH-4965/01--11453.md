## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The *Lighting Workshop* is an upper-level undergraduate and graduate research and design studio course, integrating scholarship, design, entrepreneurship, and communication in a project-specific context. Illumination is one of the most critical aspects of any built environment that enables the aesthetics of the space to be appreciated while serving the visual and physiological needs of the occupants. One of the industry trends today is 3D printing of custom lighting products. 3D printing enables the making of custom architectural elements and lighting systems on demand. This course will introduce students to state-of-the-art advanced lighting technologies and application trends with an eye toward product and service innovation. Students will learn how to use 3D printing for making lighting products and understand lighting trends and needs to provide suitable solutions. Students will gain skills, in-depth knowledge, and hands-on experience while working on group and individual projects. The aim of this course is to prepare students to take on leadership roles and face real-world challenges.

At the conclusion of the course, students will be able to:

## Course Content

### Content Details:

- Introduction and overview of the lighting industry
- Fundamentals of light sources and lighting technologies
- Individual and group work in and out of class on class project 1 (lighting design of a local museum)
- Individual work in and out of class on class project 2 (design competition)
- Group work in and out of class on class project 3 (3D light fixture)
- *Individual work on advanced class project topic for 6000-level students (project 4)
- Weekly project reviews, presentations, pin-ups, and/or desk critiques

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

- For class project 1 (Design Competition), students will be assessed based on their interim in-class presentations and their registration and final submittal to a design competition.
- For class project 2 (Lighting Design), students will be assessed on the quality of the information collected during the site visit and prior in-class discussion; the improvement in their design process; in-class presentations; the quality of the materials produced for a final presentation with the customer (drawings, renderings, schedules, budgets, details), and their participation in a final presentation to the customer.
- For class project 3 (3D-printed Light Fixture), students will be assessed along three phases of the project, including a) Background and initial lighting solution concept, b) Detailed design development, gather customer feedback, design for 3D printing, and prototyping; and c) Integration of components, laboratory testing, verbal and written case study presentation.
- For class project 4 (Light & Arch applications, market analysis), students will be assessed on their summaries of relevant literature and case studies on the assigned topic.
