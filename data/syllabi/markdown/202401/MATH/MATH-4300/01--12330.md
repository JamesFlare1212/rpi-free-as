## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An introduction to the theory and applications of complex variables. Topics include  
complex numbers, complex functions and analyticity, elementary functions, complex integration, series  
representation, residue theory, conformal mapping, transforms in applied mathematics.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Reference Texts:  
1\. Introduction to Complex Analysis and Its Applications, by Donald Trim (recommended)  
2\. Complex Variables and Applications, by James W. Brown and Ruel V. Churchill  
3\. Fundamentals of Complex Analysis with Applications to Engineering, Science, and Mathematics, by  
Edward B. Sa and Arthur D. Snider

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- A student successfully completing the course will be able to 1) use complex variable techniques to solve certain kinds of problems in the applied sciences,

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Though you can discuss homework problems with other students or work in groups, the work you submit  
must be written by yourself and represent your own independent understanding on the subjects. If your  
work involves signi cant help from other people, online sources, reference books or papers, it is required  
that you explicitly state this in your report to avoid penalty in grade.  
It is strictly forbidden to copy any part of the homework solutions from others (including classmates,  
members in your study group, books, online solutions etc). Such behavior will be considered the highest  
breach of academic integrity. Any violation will result in no grade for the entire homework set  
(of all involved individuals if there is any), and can land you in the Dean of Students Office.
