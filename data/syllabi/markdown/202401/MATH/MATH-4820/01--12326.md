## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Outline:  
Part I: Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs)  
(1) Introduction, ODEs, well-posedness, analytic stability concepts, applications.  
(2) Numerical methods for initial value problems (IVPs)  
Single-step methods, multi-step methods, Runge-Kutta methods,  
numerical stability, accuracy and convergence.  
(3) Numerical methods for boundary value problems (BVPs)  
Linear and non-linear BVPs, discrete approximation methods, shooting methods, direct solution  
methods.  
Part II: Partial Differential Equations (PDEs)  
(1) Partial differential equations, classification, well-posedness, initial conditions  
and boundary conditions  
(2) Numerical methods of parabolic PDEs  
Discretization methods, method of lines, approximation to boundary conditions.  
(3) Numerical methods of elliptic PDEs  
Finite difference and finite element methods, solution techniques.  
(4) Numerical methods of hyperbolic PDEs  
Finite difference methods, time-stepping methods, numerical stability.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Differential equations are mathematical models that can be used to describe a wide class of important problems. Ordinary and partial differential equations arise in many fields of science including engineering, physics, chemistry and biology as well as economics, computer graphics and computer games. Most differential equations cannot be solved analytically but must be solved numerically. Successful completion of the course should enable you to \item understand the process of solving a differential equation on a computer. (1) be familiar with the common and widely used algorithms. (2) implement, in matlab, basic algorithms for solving differential equations. (3) understand the strengths and weaknesses of different methods. (4) understand the notions of accuracy, stability and convergence. This knowledge will be highly beneficial when using existing scientific and commercial software for solving differential equations.
