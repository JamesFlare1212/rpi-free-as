## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Main Contents: The focus of the course in on the mathematical and computational fundamentals along  
with the applications of finite element methods and discontinuous Galerkin methods. The tentative topics  
include  
⋄ Finite element methods for elliptic (i.e. diffusion) equations, and possibly also for parabolic equations  
⋄ Discontinuous Galerkin methods for elliptic equations, convection-diffusion equations, and advection /  
kinetic transport equations

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- To understand the mathematics behinds the classical finite element methods and discontinuous Galerkin methods, as well as some related methodologies and concepts: formulation, matrices assembly for implementation, discrete spaces, approximation theory, stability, error estimates
- To carry out standard mathematical/numerical analysis and derivations
- To implement and to evaluate numerical methods for one- and two-dimensional problems; To extract mathematically meaningful information from numerical results and observations

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Homework assignments (required) and self-proposed projects (optional)
