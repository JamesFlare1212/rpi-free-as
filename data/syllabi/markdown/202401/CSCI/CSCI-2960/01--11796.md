## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course is intended for undergraduate students in their second year (Sophomore students) in the Computer Science Department. In this class, students will delve into the essential building blocks of machine learning, focusing on topics in linear algebra, continuous probability and stochastic, and optimization. These mathematical foundations are the bedrock upon which machine learning is constructed. By mastering these fundamental concepts, students will gain a powerful understanding of machine learning algorithms and predictive modeling. Moreover, this course is on track to becoming a prerequisite for all machine learning classes in the Computer Science department.  
The topics that will be covered in this course are: Vectors, matrices, matrix operations and decomposition, eigenvalues, eigenvectors, vector calculus, calculating gradients of functions of vectors and matrices, probability theory, and optimization methods.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

- Provide students with a solid foundation in essential mathematical concepts relevant to machine learning, including linear algebra, probability and stochastic, vector calculus, and optimization.
- Establish a clear connection between mathematical principles and their applications in basic machine learning models, emphasizing how these mathematical foundations form the basis of various algorithms and predictive modeling techniques.
- Ensure students comprehend the core components of machine learning - data, models, and learning algorithms - and understand their interactions and significance in designing and evaluating predictive models.
- Foster critical thinking skills by encouraging students to evaluate and compare different machine learning algorithms based on their mathematical underpinnings.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will gain practical experience in collaborative software development on real-world projects of their choosing
- Students will learn practical aspects of community driven software development
- Students will learn and apply the principles of open source on real life projects in an open environment
- Students will be given the opportunity to learn new technologies of their choosing and will need to develop capabilities to "self learn" new topics areas.
- Understand and apply fundamental linear algebra concepts, including vectors, matrices, matrix operations and decomposition, eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and their relevance to machine learning algorithms.
- Introduction of vector calculus concepts, including gradients of functions of vectors and matrices, highlighting their relevance in optimization techniques used in basic machine learning models.
- Understand basic concepts of probability theory with a focus on continuous probabilities and apply them to elementary machine learning models.
- Learning basic optimization methods, including gradient descent, emphasizing their applications in simpler machine learning models, with an introduction to gradients of vector functions.
- Connect foundational mathematical concepts to the components of machine learning - data, models, and learning algorithms - at an introductory level, emphasizing their basic functionalities and relationships.
