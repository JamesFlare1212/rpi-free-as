### Additional Information for Course Information Section:

A time series is a sequence (xt) of data where the subscript t indicates the time at which the data xt was observed. Time series (TS) are found in wide range of applications ranging from economics and finance (e.g., stock values, oil prices, unemployment numbers) to biomedical domains (e.g., EEG, EKG signals). The course provides an introduction to the theory and practice of statistical time series analysis. We are interested in the nature of TS data i.e., how it is sampled and how to resample it which is also related to missing data problem. We are also interested in analysis TS data to identify its deterministic and nondeterministic component. The next step is to fit a model to such data that describes it well and use it for prediction.  
Topics covered include stationary and non-stationary stochastic processes, autoregressive and moving average (ARMA) models, model selection and estimation, state-space models and the Kalman filter, forecasting and forecast evaluation, an outline of spectral techniques.

While these fundamental techniques are remarkably successful availability of large time series data sets motivates application of AI/ML methods. Thus we will cover various deep neural networks that are relevant to time series data including RNN, LSTM.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

**Tentative Topics:**  
•**Preprocessing the data**  
•Sampling, window size, smoothing, lag and difference operations, transformations  
**•Mathematics of TSA**  
•Probability theory •Linear algebra •Stochastic process •Statistics  
**•Modeling of TSA**  
•Temporal dependency based models: MA, AR, ARMA, ARIMA..  
•State Space models: Kalman filters  
•DNN (iid assumption: bag of time windows): RNN, LSTM, Transformer  
**•Multivariate TSA**
