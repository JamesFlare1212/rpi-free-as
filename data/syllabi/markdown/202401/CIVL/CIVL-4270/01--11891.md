## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will be made aware of the Construction Industries numerous contract delivery methods and contract types available to owners. Cost estimating and bidding will be discussed in detail as well as business ownership types and company organization policies. Project management and project scheduling will be explained in detail including various schedule formats most common to the industry. A final project of creating a Project Safety Manual for your fictitious construction company will be required.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

The course assessment will be based on the students' knowledge of material presented in lecture, tested and quizzed on.  
There will be one quiz & four-unit tests on various chapters, and a final safety project. In additional there will be various homework assignments.  
Class participation will add to a student's grade.  
Reading assignments are expected to be completed prior to each lecture.

### Attendance Policy:

Attendance at each lecture is expected and will allow the student the maximum opportunity to learn the material presented.

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work.
