## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course will center on applications of basic data analysis techniques such as data visualization, classification, clustering, and ridge regression. A case study approach will be used to provide immediate immersion into the problem of understanding high-dimensional data. The course will develop the necessary mathematics to understand and execute the techniques. Each student will execute a mini-data analysis project on a real-world data problem such as from engineering, medicine, and business.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The course is designed to start from the basics and immediately immerse students in high-dimensional mathematics and data analytics. The course will center on the applications of basic data analysis techniques such as data visualization, clustering, and classification. A case study approach will be used to provide immediate immersion in the problem of understanding high-dimensional data and to enhance learning. Students will work in groups to conduct analysis of real-world data problems such as from engineering, medicine and business. Each student will execute a mini-data analysis project within the course. The course will provide a hands-on experience and a window into the higher-level mathematical issues that need to be understood when modeling and analyzing data. The course will expose a mathematical scaffold, i.e. the outlines and foundations of the techniques that let us express and find structure in high-dimensional data. The scaffold will provide preview and concreteness to basic mathematical ideas that are studied later in the curriculum such as linear algebra, vectors, matrices, eigenvalues, multivariate functions, systems of equations, and quantifying uncertainty. In addition, the scaffold includes basic computer skills such as R that allow one to take advantage of powerful mathematical algorithms without necessarily first learning the details of how they work. Students will emerge with very concrete and practical skills for extracting knowledge from data.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

On successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

*extract structure from large scale data using techniques such as principal component analysis and linear discriminant analysis

*derive information from data by interpreting and visualizing this extracted structure

*present the derived information in a professional way understandable by others

*demonstrate knowledge of the mathematical scaffolding needed for expressing and analyzing data problems

*demonstrate knowledge of basic linear algebra, and  
*pursue interests in high-dimensional mathematics and data analysis techniques applied to problems in science, engineering, and business using cyberinfrastructure.
