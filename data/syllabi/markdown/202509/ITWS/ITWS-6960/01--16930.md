## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**Course Description:**

A Platform Business differs from a tradition Product Business in the generation and capture of data that is valuable to multiple new market opportunities. The intent of this course is to bring together disciplines to develop new value creating outcomes. In this course, students will integrate topics of database systems, networking, software design, web science and business strategies, along with traditional product concepts, to develop digital solutions for new value creating opportunities. Students will work together on project opportunities of their own creation, develop a digital solution prototype and write a short business plan on these innovations. In some cases, the teams will be asked to present their solution to members of the investment community for evaluation and possible future funding.

Utilizing the skills and expertise developed in earlier courses, the students will be guided on an entrepreneurial adventure to prototype their solution to a marketplace challenge, evaluate the economics of their solution and prepare an entry level business plan to champion that solution.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Student Learning Outcome: Students will apply and integrate the concepts of business strategy, systems engineering, database systems, networking, software engineering and human computer interaction along with concepts of ITWS to create valued solutions to marketplace challenges. Students will develop a business plan around their solution, demonstrating the unique attributes and economics of the goal. Students may be asked to deliver a multi-media presentation of their project and their business plan, to a professional audience for evaluation.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Students will be graded on creative and active in-class participation, where they can demonstrate their understanding of new concepts, as well as sharing ideas for peer review.  
The class demands participation on a team project to create and demonstrate "value creation" utilizing the concepts of Platform Business Models.

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust.
