## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The study of the link between engineering, and management. Students will gain an in-depth understanding of the foundations of engineering management. Topics covered include the concept of engineering management, its relationship to engineering, and its historical underpinnings, the traditional roles of management with particular emphasis on leadership skills to manage projects and processes, as well as the organizational and administrative abilities required to oversee the day-to-day operational performance of complex engineering. The ethical dimensions of the organization and the engineering community are also explored.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

To meet the ABET goal for the ISE department by studying the link between engineering, and management.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Explain and describe knowledge of the historical development, theoretical aspects, and practice application of managerial process.
- Identify and use the fundamental concepts & principles of engineering management, including the roles, skills, and the functions of a manager.
- Assess the importance of interaction between the external environment as well as internal functions to achieve high organizational performance.
- Evaluate how to achieve effectiveness as an engineer within the globalization and millennium challenges.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Chapter Assignments, self assessment, exercises, reflections, Interviewing engineering managers and presentations to the class, and assignments (such as leadership philosophy statement)

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Assignments 20%, Exercises 10%; chapter assignments 30%; self-assessment 10%; assignments (presentation - Presenting on interviews with Engineering managers, leadership philosophy statement) 30%
