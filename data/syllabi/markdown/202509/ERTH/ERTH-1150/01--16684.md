## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Analyze mineral and rock properties
- Apply plate tectonic theory to predict the distribution of energy resources and geological hazards
- Evaluate geological hazard risks (earthquakes, volcanoes, landslides) using scientific data and monitoring techniques

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Labs:** Each lab is worth 20 points. Generally, these 20 points will come from a combination of effort/completion in class (10 points) and post-class quiz (10 points). For the field labs, a short lab report will be written and will be due at the start of the next lab period. These are marked with a * on the schedule.

- *Effort/Competition + Quiz:*
  - o 10 points (less for sloppy or incomplete work-includes not labeling numbers in math work) will be earned simply by completing the lab in a satisfactory manner and correcting your lab during the lab time. When you correct your labs, make the corrections in a different color. Do not destroy your original answer! When you review this material for exams, then, you can see your mistakes and not repeat them. Labs are an opportunity to make mistakes and learn! When you have completed the lab, have your TA check your lab. They will let you know what samples/questions to revise.
    - When you are finished with your lab, you may leave only after you have corrected your answers and you have “checked out” with your TA. Do you not leave without watching your TA write your points down!
  - o 10 points will be available through a quiz on the lab material at the start of lab the following week. You will use your self-graded lab as a study guide to prepare.

**Lab exam:** A cumulative laboratory exam will be held during the normal lab during the last week the semester

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

**Attendance:** Geology is an observational science, and as such, laboratory sessions provide an excellent opportunity to learn geologic concepts and methods though applied activities. As such, attendance is required! There are **NO** excused absences for lab. **There are a total of 290 points you can earn, but a 100% is 270 points, allowing everyone the opportunity to miss a lab without penalty.**

**Make-up Labs**: Make-up labs are very difficult to arrange and are impossible in the case of field outings, so if you know you will be missing more than one lab, you need to work with Dr. Cadieux in advance. If you have a conflict but can attend a different lab section, please let Dr. Cadieux know in advance. Lab make-ups can only occur during TA office hours and only one lab quiz can be made up.

### Other Course Policies:

**Tardiness:** Lab quizzes will start promptly at the beginning of class. If you are more than 5 minutes late, you will not be able to take the quiz.

**Late Policy**: Labs that are turned in will receive 10% deduction for each day late. Late labs will not be accepted after 1 week.

**AI Policy:** AI cannot be used for laboratory assignments. If it is suspected AI has been used for a lab report, you will receive a zero for the assignment.

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these.
