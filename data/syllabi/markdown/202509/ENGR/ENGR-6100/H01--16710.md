## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course investigates business-related considerations in successfully commercializing new technology in a new venture or within an existing enterprise: market and customer analysis, beating the competition, planning and managing for profitability, high-tech marketing and sales, and business partnerships and acquisitions. Not a general management course; focuses explicitly on what is relevant for engineers and scientists working in a commercial environment. For junior/senior undergraduate or graduate students.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Evaluate the organization’s position in the market, relative to competitors, within a market “ecosystem”.
- Evaluate the competitive and strategic position of the organization.
- Interpret financial statements and filings.
- Calculate and interpret financial metrics of the organization.
- Propose strategies for releasing new products and services to fulfill a market need.
- Evaluate current events and articulate how demand and supply may be impacted.
- Articulate how specific initiatives of the organization may influence financial and strategic performance.
- Recommend specific strategies to improve the organization’s performance.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

### Evaluation Criteria

This course comprises three overall projects. The first project explores the organization and the market in which it operates – the “system” within and around the organization. The second evaluates the performance of the organization relative to its goals and objectives. The third draws on the first two to evaluate a new market opportunity the organization may consider.

You will complete each project by reaching multiple milestones. The framework, scope, and deliverable of the project are detailed up front, as are the criteria (rubric) that will be used to evaluate your work. You then interact with learning resources and materials that will help you walk through completion of the project.

Many of the learning resources have ungraded quizzes that you can take to serve as knowledge checks – if you do well on the quiz, you then know that you have successfully grasped the related topics. The time expectation associated with each project is defined up front as well so you can pace yourself accordingly.

As you complete each project, you receive feedback according to the associated rubric for the project. You can use that feedback to revise your project, or if you have “met the standard” for the project, you can move to the next project. When you have completed all three projects in a successful manner, you have completed the requirements of the course.

You are strongly encouraged to collaborate openly with each other – the work you submit must be your own original work but learning with others to explore ideas in strongly encouraged.

- **Project 1:** External and Internal Evaluation of the Organization – Worth 30 % of overall course grade.
- **Project 2:** Financial Performance of the Organization – Worth 35 % of overall course grade.
- **Project 3:** Launching a New Product – Worth 35 % of overall course grade.
