## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course provides skills to apply econometrics to diverse economic topics. Econometric methods are developed for panel data analysis and for tools such as instrumental variables, regression discontinuity, difference-in-differences method, synthetic control method, quantile regression, and spatial regressions. Methods to identify causal effects are emphasized. Students do hands-on data work that puts to use these econometric tools.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

This is a tentative schedule and subject to change depending upon the progress of the class:

- Panel Data: Fixed/Random Effects Models, Dynamic Panel Data Models
- Linear and Non-Linear Hypothesis Testing
- Statistical Inference Using the Bootstrap
- Instrumental Variable Models and GMM
- Treatment Effects and Policy Evaluation (RD, DID, SCM)
- An Introduction to Spatial Analysis
- Quantile Regression and Its Applications
- Shrinkage Methods with Many Regressors

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate modern econometric methods and explain key mathematical concepts behind these methods.
- Implement statistical inference and draw meaningful interpretation from the analysis.
- Apply appropriate econometric techniques to data analysis and report the research findings.
- Gain competence in the statistical software R.
- Evaluate the validity of econometric analysis in empirical studies.
- Conduct independent (individual) research to develop expertise in selected topics of interests in applied econometrics.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

I will randomly check attendance for 6 times. First absence will be waived. After that, any absence will lose 20% of the credits for this category. If you have a legitimate reason to miss the class, please let me know **in advance**.

### Other Course Policies:

- **Assignments:** Unless otherwise noted, one assignment will be given at the end of each two or three lectures. You can work in groups, but you must turn in your own version of the assignment. All assignments will be graded on a 0-100 scale (plus bonus points if applicable). Some problem sets will ask you to estimate econometric models and interpret the results. Your grade will depend on both whether or not you apply appropriate econometric methods, and on how well you explain your models and discuss your results.
- **More about grades:** Problem sets may simply be checked for the percentage completed, or may be graded based on content. Late assignments will have their grades multiplied by 0.8 if handed in no more than one week late, or by 0.5 if handed in up to two weeks late, but cannot be credited if more than two weeks late or if after 4:00 p.m. on the last day of classes in the term. If answers to assignments are provided, please check them to identify any problems you are having and learn how to deal with these problems.
- **Midterm:** The take-home midterm exam will cover all materials before spatial analysis. The midterm date will be announced in class. All exams must be taken completely alone. **Late submission will not be accepted. The only exceptions include: 1. Illness (must provide a doctor’s note) 2. RPI-approved official activities (must provide documentation in advance).**
