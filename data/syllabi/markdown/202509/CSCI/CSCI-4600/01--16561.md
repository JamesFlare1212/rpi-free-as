## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

## Course Goals

### Goals:

- Introduce students to relevant methods to recognize and apply quantitative algorithms, techniques, and interpretation
- To develop students' strategic thinking skills, combined with a solid technical foundation in data and model-driven decision-making.
- Develop the ability to apply critical and analytical methods to formulate and solve science, engineering, medical, and business problems
- Students will examine real-world examples using modern cyberinfrastructure to place statistical and data-mining techniques in context, to develop data-analytic thinking, and to illustrate that proper application is as much an art as it is a science.
- By the end of the course, students can effectively communicate analytic findings to non-specialists

## Course Content

### Content Details:

**Group 1 - Intro / Setup**

- Week 1 (Aug. 29 / Sep. 02): Introduction to Course, Case Studies, and Preview of Course Material/Refresher on basic statistics.
- Week 2 (Sep. 09 / Sep. 12): Introduction/ refresher on basic statistics continue / Distributions, Probabilities, Role of Hypothesis - R/ RStudio introduction and Intro to Labs. (lab 1)
- Week 3 (Sep. 16 / Sep. 19): Introduction to Analytic Methods, Types of Data Mining for Analytics, Regression Analysis, Supervised & Unsupervised Machine Learning.

**Group 2 - Patterns, relations, descriptive, and predictive analytics**

- Week 4 (Sep. 23/ Sep. 26): Linear models, kNN, Decision Trees, Naive Bayes (lab 2 / Assignment 2)
- Week 5 (Sep. 30/ Oct. 03): k-Means, PAM, DBSCAN, Hierarchical Clustering (lab 3)
- Week 6 (Oct. 07/ Oct. 10): Evaluating Regression Models / Evaluation Metrics for Classification & Clustering Models (lab 4 / Assignment 3 / Assignment 4)
- Week 7 (Oct. 14/ Oct. 17): Project Proposal presentations (Assignment 5)

**Group 3 - Model evaluation, dimensionality reduction, more supervised, and unsupervised learning methods**

- Week 8 (Oct. 21/ Oct. 24): Dimensionality Reduction, PCA, SVD (lab 5)
- Week 9 (Oct. 28/ Oct. 31): Support Vector Machines (lab 5 / Assignment 6)
- Week 10 (Nov. 04/ Nov. 07): Generalization, Validation, Optimization (lab 6)
- Week 11 (Nov. 11/ Nov. 14): More Supervised and Unsupervised learning Methods (lab 7)

**Group 4 - Reviewing methods, project advising, and peripheral topics**

- Week 12 (Nov. 18/ Nov. 21): Reviewing kNN, RF, PCA, SVM (lab 8)
- Week 13 (Nov. 25/ Nov. 28): Thanksgiving - no classes
- Week 14 (Dec. 02/ Dec. 05): Guest Lecture
- Week 15 (Dec. 09/ Dec. 12:) Last Day of Data Analytics Classes: final Project presentations, written reports due.

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own.
