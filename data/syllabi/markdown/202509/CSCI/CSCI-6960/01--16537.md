## Description

During the Fall 2025 term, this class will explore the emerging and quickly changing field of Hybrid AI, which combines the structured, rich representation of knowledge inherent in knowledge graphs(KGs) with the revolutionary power of large language models (LLMs) to tackle complex knowledge representation and reasoning tasks. Through reading and discussions students will delve into the theoretical foundations and practical applications of hybrid AI system architectures, including the design and deployment of domain-authoritative and (hopefully) provenance-aware KGs, the integration of common LLMs with KGs, utilizing hybrid AI architectures to achieve explainable AI, and more. Through readings, paper discussions and potential projects, students will develop the skills to design and implement and utilize hybrid AI systems.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Prepare students for research in the emerging field of hybrid AI, including and especially the application of semantic technologies and KGs to create new AI architectures

Teach students how to:

- Discover, read and explain relevant research papers
- Identify gaps in existing research
- Present research ideas
- Synthesize material
- Critically review published work (e.g. for a publication venue)
- Develop a literature corpus for use in research

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understand the concept of hybrid AI and its significance
- Learn to critically review semantic technology papers
- Learn to create & deliver technical presentations on research
- Learn to prepare AND evaluate a research paper review
- Learn to prepare research surveys about a specific hybrid AI research question
- Learn to find and evaluate related work
- Learn to create a related work corpus and annotated bibliography
- Learn to synthesize a written research proposal from current research

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

- Via written assignments
- Via oral (individual presentations)
- Via participation in class
- Late submission policy: first time with valid reason – no penalty, otherwise 20% of score deducted each late day

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

If you have any questions concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification.
