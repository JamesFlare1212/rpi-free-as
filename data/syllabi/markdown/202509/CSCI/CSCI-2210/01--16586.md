## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course covers the essential building blocks of machine learning,  
focusing on topics in linear algebra, continuous probability and stochastic, and optimization.  
This provides students with foundational mathematical concepts to the components of  
machine learning - data, models, and learning algorithms - at an introductory level,  
emphasizing their basic functionalities and relationships. These mathematical foundations are  
the bedrock upon which machine learning is constructed. The topics that will be covered in  
this course are: Vectors, matrices, matrix operations and decomposition, eigenvalues,  
eigenvectors, vector calculus, calculating gradients of functions of vectors and matrices,  
probability theory, and linear regression. Prerequisites: CSCI 2200 and MATH 2010 (4 credit  
hours)

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

No required textbook. *Mathematics for Machine Learning*, by Deisenroth et al. will be a primary resource.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Students will enhance their facility with the tools and notation of linear algebra, vector calculus, and probability (especially continuous probability).

Students will gain an appreciation for how these tools are used to solve the foundational problems in machine learning.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will become proficient with the tools and notation of linear algebra that are relevant to machine learning.
- Students will become proficient with the tools and notation of vector calculus that are relevant to machine learning.
- Students will become proficient with the tools and notation of probability theory that are relevant to machine learning.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

12 homework assignments, two exams, plus attendance & participation in lectures
