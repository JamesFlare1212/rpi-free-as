## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

*This graduate level course prepares students to plan, design, execute, and present results of a research topic in lighting, including, technology, human-factors, and lighting applications related research. This course is intended for new graduate students in lighting and related fields preparing for MS degree projects or dissertation. It prepares students to identify research questions and develop hypotheses after conducting a thorough literature review and identifying knowledge gaps that require better understanding to answer the identified research question. To do so the students will design experimental or simulation studies, collect data, analyze them and verify hypothesis. Following this student will write a project report highlighting the findings, discus the findings without overly generalizing the results. Include the limitations of the study so that future researchers can address those limitations. Finally, prepare a presentation and an outline of a research paper to disseminate the findings*

*The course includes lectures, discussions with topic mentors, homework, literature surveys, software analyses, and laboratory experiments*

*This course fulfills the academic requirements of MS degree in Lighting and MS Architectural Sciences, with Lighting Concentration.*

### Goals:

The primary goal of this course is to provide students in the graduate programs in lighting with an understanding of the process of research so that they are prepared to undertake a Master’s Project.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Be able to identify significant research problems and develop well defined research questions
- Conduct a comprehensive literature search and identify knowledge gaps, and develop hypothesis that can be verified by designing and conducting good experimental or theoretical studies that can fill the knowledge gap.
- Design a research project setup, collect data, analyze and discuss results, and conclude findings within the limits of the study
- Able to use graphical and statistical analysis to analyze data and develop results
- Able to disseminate results by preparing presentations and written research paper.
