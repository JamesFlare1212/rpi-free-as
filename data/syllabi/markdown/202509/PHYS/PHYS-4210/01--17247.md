## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course covers the fundamentals of electrodynamics including Maxwell’s Equations and common applications: Field theory of electricity and magnetism with emphasis on solving boundary value problems; Dielectric and magnetic materials; Maxwell’s equations and wave propagation with applications to optics; Relativistic electrodynamics.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

No cheating of any kind will be tolerated. Copying homework solutions is cheating. Looking at someone else’s exam is cheating. You are allowed (and encouraged) to work with others on homework problems, but all work that you turn in must be your own. Cheating of any kind will result in an F in the course, no exceptions! For flagrant cases, an academic sanction will also be filed with the university!

Using artificial intelligence (AI) to generate text, problem solutions, or some part thereof, is restricted. You should treat AI as you would a classmate or a solution manual. Consulting said resources for help or to get ideas is encouraged, but you MUST create all work that you submit independently. Copying a solution, even in part, is cheating. Submission of any work generated by AI will be considered a violation of academic ethics. If you find yourself in a position where you think that the only way to pass the course is to do so dishonestly, please reach out to me for support.

The expectation is that everyone, meaning you as the student, your classmates, your TA, and me as your instructor, treats and is treated by everyone appropriately.

## Other Course-Specific Information

### Additional Course Information:

Work-life balance:

I strive to encourage a work-life balance for everyone in this course. That means I respect your right spend time doing activities not related to this course, but that means you must also respect the time demands of this course. It is a **balance** between the two. Exam dates and homework due dates are already posted, so plan your personal activities to ensure you can comply with these due dates. Any reasonable exception must be communicated with me well in advance. You should also not expect that short notice for help on homework, exam prep, or other last-minute requests will always be accommodated. The TA and I also have responsibilities beyond this course, so expect up to a **one business day response time** on any request. Anything faster than this is a luxury and not to be expected.
