## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Mentoring counts for 5% of the course grade in PHYS 1100. Mentoring is a mandatory part of class and it is not possible to get a 100% in the class without adequate effort, attendance, and participation in the sessions. In the event of an absence or conflict, email ahead of time to make appropriate arrangements.

Grading per session is done on a trinary scale and is awarded as follows:

- 0 – The student is not present at the session (unexcused absence)
- 1 – The student is present but does not actively participate (e.g. the student is preoccupied with electronic devices and/or does not collaborate with other students during group activities.)
- 2 – The student is present and actively participates during the session (e.g. - The student is not distracted by electronics, collaborates with other students during group activities, answers questions or does work on the board when asked).
- **Note**: There may be in class assignments given out during the semester. While these are not graded for credit, they do count towards overall participation in that session.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Students are expected to be on time and prepared for weekly mentoring sessions. This will allow students to make the best use of the time. Students are also expected to be engaged in weekly sessions and participate in activities such that they are an active part of the learning group (this is 5% of the class grade)

### Other Course Policies:

Be respectful to the mentor and fellow mentees. Listen to others and do not speak when others have the floor.  
Please do not use technology for non-mentoring activities during the 50 minutes sessions.  
Be receptive to new ideas and processes, because other mentees may have ideas that you hadn't thought of. This is one of the best ways to learn, especially with a difficult subject like physics.

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. ### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Grades in the course are based on attendance and participation in weekly mentoring sessions. Unexcused absences will result in a score of 0 for that session. For illness or conflicts students are to make arrangements with mentors. Lying about an illness or conflict to get out of mentoring is a violation of the academic integrity policy and will result in a 0 for the session. Repeated violation of the academic integrity policy in this way will result in a grade of 0 for mentoring (5% of the overall PHYS 1100 grade) and a report to the Dean of Students.
