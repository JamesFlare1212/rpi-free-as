## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course, designed for first-year graduate students, starts with the core principles of states, operators, and eigenvalues. You'll learn to see the Schrödinger representation not as the only way, but as one choice among many to describe quantum systems. We'll explore how classical physics arises from this quantum foundation and delve into fascinating phenomena with no classical analogue, such as the electron's intrinsic spin and magnetic moment.  
\\\  
I will assume prior mastery of core physics concepts—including introductory quantum mechanics, classical mechanics, and electromagnetism—as well as the necessary mathematical tools of multivariable calculus and differential equations (both ordinary and partial).

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:\&nbsp; Formulate and solve quantum mechanical problems in the abstract Dirac (bra-ket) notation, independent of a specific basis like position or momentum.\&nbsp;
- Explain the postulates of quantum mechanics (state space, operators, measurement, time evolution) rigorously in terms of Hilbert space geometry.
- Analyze the properties of quantum angular momentum using operator methods (commutation relations, ladder operators) and derive the eigenvalues for general spin systems.
