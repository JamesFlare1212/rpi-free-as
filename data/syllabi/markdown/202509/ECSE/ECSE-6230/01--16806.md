## Course Goals

### Goals:

We plan to teach students how to analyze and understand basic semiconductor physics and 2 and 3-terminal devices (pn junction diodes, MOS capacitors and FETs). The ability to perform both hand calculations and PISCES-based device simulations is stressed. Particular emphasis is placed on first and second order effects of device operation physics and physics-based SPICE models. We also present advanced MOSFET scaling, structures and development trends. We expect, of note, that students will become familiar with the meaning of common terms and acronyms, and critical issues associated with modern semiconductor and MOSFET technologies.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

1. Reciprocal Lattice, Brillouin Zones
2. Energy Bands.
3. Principle of Detailed Balance, Mass-Action Law.
4. Donors and Acceptors.
5. Carrier Transport.
6. Carrier Lifetimes.
7. PN Junctions.
8. MOS Capacitors.
9. MOSFETs.
10. Device Models.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Familiarize with the basic semiconductor phenomena involved in the semiconductor device operation.
- Learn the physics of operation of basic semiconductor devices.
- Be able to utilize the equations of first- and second- order terminal I-V characteristics to estimate the performance of these basic devices.
- Develop an understanding of device models, with the associated parameters, usually used in circuit simulations.
