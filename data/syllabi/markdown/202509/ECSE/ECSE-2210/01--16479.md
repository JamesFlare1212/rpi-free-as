## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An introductory survey of microelectronics technology emphasizing physical properties of semiconductors, physical principles of device operation, equivalent circuits useful in circuit design and fundamental limitations of semiconductor devices and principles of integrated circuit fabrication. Particular emphasis is placed on semiconductor fundamentals, p-n junctions, and MOSFETs.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

R. F. Pierret “Semiconductor Device Fundamentals” Addison–Wesley

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- (i) Determine the hole and electron concentration of semiconductor materials, both under thermal equilibrium and under steady state
- (ii) Calculate the I-V characteristics of PN junction devices given material parameters
- (iii) Capable to evaluate C-V characteristics of MOS capacitors
- (iv) Compute the I-V response of MOSFETs
- (v) Develop an ability to use semiconductor devices in different application areas
