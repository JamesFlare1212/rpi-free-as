## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**Course Description:**  
Economics of the operation of power systems. Control of hydro and thermal generating units. Aspects of interconnected operation. Transmission losses and techniques for optimum economic generation. Hydrothermal coordination problems. Modern power markets. State estimation

## Course Goals

### Goals:

**Course Objectives for ECSE 4966:** A student will be able to ***classify*** the characteristics and constraints of various types of power generation, and ***analyze*** the operation of a power grid, in a vertically integrated electric utility company and in a restructured electricity market. A student will also be able to ***apply*** the mathematical models and ***perform*** appropriate calculations to solve economic dispatch and unit commitment problems.

**Course Objectives for ECSE 6140:** Students will ***classify*** the characteristics and constraints of diverse power generation types and ***analyze*** the operation of power grids in both vertically integrated utilities and restructured markets. They will ***apply*** mathematical models to calculate solutions for economic dispatch and unit commitment problems. This knowledge will prepare them to ***evaluate*** and ***design*** solutions for power system planning and operation, enabling them to pursue advanced studies or industrial careers

**Research and Lecture Topics**

**On**

**Modern Power Markets**

Please schedule your topics (first come, first serve) and tentative dates no later than TBD. Please give me a one-page outline one week before your presentation.

Possible topics include:

1. Tutorial on the Cornell MATPOWER optimal power flow program. (Independent of modern power markets)
2. Report on the restructured power markets in England, Argentina, Chile, Brazil, China, Korea, Philippine, Australia, etc. (*one country per student*). Describe how the markets are set up and their success and failure. Look for IEEE and non-IEEE sources.
3. FERC Order 888 and 2000 (*one order per student*). What were the FERC’s objectives? What changes did the FERC make to meet those objectives? Your challenge will be to find out what ghastly documents nearly 1,000 pages long say, without reading every page. Download the order from the FERC web site.
4. What is the current state of electric energy market in CAISO, Midwest ISO, ISO New England, PJM, New York ISO, and SPP (*one region per student*)? What is working and what isn’t?
