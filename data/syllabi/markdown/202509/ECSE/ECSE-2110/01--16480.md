## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course introduces basic concepts of power systems. Core topics covered in the course include electromechanical and electromagnetic energy conversion, solid-state power conversion, power electronics, and AC circuits. This course also provides an overview of power generation from conventional and renewable sources, AC and DC power transmission and distribution, as well as the use of electricity in various forms, which are expanded upon in other courses in the area.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

1. Electrical Machinery and Power System Fundamentals by Stephen J. Chapman, 2002

2. Renewable and Efficient Electric Power Systems by Gilbert M. Masters, 2004 (Electronic version available through RPI library)

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The course aims to equip students with an understanding of the fundamental principles of power system components and the operation of major energy and power systems, so that the students have a basic understanding of how electrical energy and emerging energy systems impact their lives and the broader world.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Basic concepts, Introduction to AC Systems. 6 Lectures

Transformers. 6 Lectures

Electromechanical Energy Conversion and Synchronous Machines. 7 Lectures

Transmission lines and Power System Operation. 4 Lectures

Wind and Solar Energy. 5 Lectures

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Perform computations and carry out basic analysis of AC circuits in steady state, including single-phase and three-phase.
- 2\. Describe, derive and calculate phasors, phase angle, active and reactive power, power factor, harmonics and other metrics for AC circuit analysis.
- 3\. Interpret fundamental principles and applications of electromechanical and electromagnetic energy conversion and solid-state power conversion.
- 4\. Identify past and emerging trends in the development of energy systems.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Homework 20%. (For each of you, the lowest one will be dropped, and others have equal percentage).

Homework will be assigned every 2-4 classes (about 8 homeworks total) and posted on Piazza. You are allowed to discuss problems with other students, but you must prepare your solution independently. No copying!

Homework is due on Gradescope. Late homework will NOT be accepted. Grade appeals should be submitted on Gradescope within 48 hours of its availability on Gradescope. Please take the time to carefully compare your solution to the posted homework solutions before appealing.

Midterm 1 25%

Midterm 2 25%

Final Exam 30%

All are cheat-sheet exams. (Closed book with one page of cheat sheet)

In the rare scenario that a Midterm has to be missed (at the consent of instructor), a Missed Midterm will have the grade proportion added to the other exams (for example: if you miss Exam 1, then your Exam 2 will count 37.5% and Final Exam will count 42.5%. If you miss both Midterms, Final Exam will count 80%). Final Exam MUST be taken. Any conflicts with the exam dates must be brought to the attention of the instructor as soon as they arise.
