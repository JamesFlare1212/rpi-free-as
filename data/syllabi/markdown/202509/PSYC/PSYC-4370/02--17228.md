## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

## Course Description

What is the mind? How does it work? These are not easy questions! Cognitive psychology is the scientific discipline that attempts to answer these questions through behavioral, computational, and biological experimentation and investigation. Cognitive psychology seeks to uncover the processes and mechanisms that underlie how sensory information, and the knowledge we gain through learning and experience, are used to guide our actions and overcome problems and obstacles.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Demonstrate understanding of foundational theories, methods, and findings in cognitive psychology
- 2\. Apply cognitive psychology concepts by designing and carrying out an experiment that tests a hypothesis, analyzing results, and evaluating methodological strengths and limitations
- 3\. Analyze and synthesize research evidence to situate a significant question in cognitive psychology within its historical background and current state of knowledge, integrating peer-reviewed sources
- 4\. Formulate and communicate original insights into unanswered questions in cognitive psychology, presenting arguments and evidence clearly in oral and written formats.

### Other Course Policies:

## Course Policies

Laptops / computers:

Students are required to bring a tablet or laptop computer to every class that is able to connect to LMS in order to take in-class quizzes. During lectures and discussions, use of laptops should be limited to course-related activities (note-taking, exercises, or instructor-directed tasks). Non-academic use is discouraged, as it distracts both the user and peers.

Attendance:

Attendance is expected at all class meetings. If you must miss class due to illness, travel, or other special circumstances, notify the instructor in advance when possible. Attendance is indirectly factored into grades through in-class quizzes.

Make-up policy for missed quizzes:

The two lowest quiz grades will be dropped. Students who miss more than two quizzes may request a make-up. Make-ups will be granted at the instructor’s discretion, considering the reason for absence and the number of classes missed. Points may also be deducted for late quizzes at the instructor’s discretion.

Penalty for late/missed assignments:

Extensions will not normally be granted except in exceptional circumstances (e.g., illness, family emergency). Students should contact the instructor as soon as possible if they are unable to meet a deadline. Late assignments will incur a grade penalty, typically a deduction of one letter grade per day late, unless otherwise specified by the instructor.

Exam policy:

Exams must be taken at the scheduled date and time. Make-ups are only considered in cases of serious illness, family emergency, or other serious circumstances. Make-up exams may be in a different format than the original (e.g., essay-based rather than multiple choice). Students with prolonged emergencies should contact the instructor and academic advising for guidance.
