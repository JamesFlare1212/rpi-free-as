## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Laboratory introduction to the design and interpretation of key components of an intelligent physical system: sensing, communication, computation, simulation, programming, and control. Modeling and simulation of physical phenomena. Design of control algorithms (e.g., PID) applied to a physical system. Analysis of data, algorithm development, and implementation in hardware, including nonlinear and hardware effects of discretization, delays, saturation, sampling, and friction. The course will make extensive use of MATLAB and Simulink.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Model various physical phenomena through simplified ODE models and use these models for prediction, analysis, and design.
- Simulate ODEs in Simulink and include nonlinear and hardware effects of discretization, delays, saturation, and sampling, etc.
- Identify different types of experimental data (e.g., frequency response, transient response, step response) discuss issues of data quality (noise, uncertainty), and methods to interpret such data (filtering, least squares fitting, etc.)
- Design control algorithms (such as PID and state feedback) for a physical system
- Identify components of an intelligent control system: sensing, communication, computation, simulation, programming, and control.
