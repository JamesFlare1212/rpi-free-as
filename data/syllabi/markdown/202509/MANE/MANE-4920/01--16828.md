## Course Goals

### Goals:

To develop fundamental understanding of the static and dynamic response of flexible, light-weight structures used in aerospace applications.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

LIST OF EXPERIMENTS

1. Tensile Testing (Materials Selection)
2. Column Buckling (Structural Stability Investigation)
3. Forced Vibration (Natural Frequencies and Mode Shapes)
4. Free Vibration (Structural Damping Measurement)
5. Impact Testing (Crashworthiness and Safety)

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- To understand the static and dynamic response of flexible lightweight structures used in aerospace vehicle design. An ability to conduct experiments, analyze and interpret data, and use engineering judgment to draw conclusions.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Students will perform experiments in groups of 6 to 8 students. Groups will be assigned by the instructor. Each student will submit an **individual report** after the completion of each experiment. There is **no final exam**. Each Lab is 20% of the grade.
