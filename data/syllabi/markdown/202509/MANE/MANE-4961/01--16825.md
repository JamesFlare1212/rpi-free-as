### Additional Information for Course Information Section:

**Course Description:**

The course will focus on applications and implementation of machine learning methods. Mathematical and theoretical basis of ML models will be introduced. Concepts in machine learning will be used for classification and regression towards the prediction of performance characteristics of engineering systems.

The following topics will be covered:

1\) Basics: a) learning b) features c) ML challenges d) model building e) ML terms: hyperparameters, loss functions, train-test-validation, regularization f) error measures g) metrics h) end-to-end project design, i) Python packages , j) Multi-variate regression, k) Multi-class vs multi-label classifications.

2\) Supervised learning: a) kNN b) MLP/ANN c) LRC d) SVM e) CNNs, f) CART and ensemble methods

3\) Unsupervised learning: a) k-means b) SVD c) PCA d) dimensionality reduction e) data visualization

4\) Contemporary special topics of interest in AI/ML research as needed.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

**Attendance:**

Each student is responsible for all materials presented and all announcements made in class. There is a strong correlation of student performance with attending classes and doing the homework. Attendance will be kept from time to time comply with the excused absence policy to ensure sufficient presence.

According to the updated instructions regarding the excused absence policy, specifically, “Students missing more than a week of classes due to illness will be required to provide a doctor’s note or other supporting documentation to the Student Success Office. The Class Dean will communicate the receipt of the note (with expected end date) to the relevant faculty.”

Since there is a strong correlation of student performance with attending classes and doing the homework, absence from several classes may also trigger the SIS emergency warning system (EWS) so the student can address the challenges affecting their learning and seek help to mitigate such challenges. Absence from project presentations and discussions, and delayed progress submissions or report submission may also trigger the SIS EWS system so the student can address the challenges preventing them from completing these learning tasks and an official record can be created so other relevant offices at the institute can provide necessary help.

Lastly, the updated instructions regarding the excused absence policy also state that “Students are responsible for completing any work that they might miss – including assignments, quizzes, labs, exams, etc. – due to illness, quarantine/isolation, or other circumstances.” Therefore, the ultimate responsibility to complete the missing work within time lies with the student.
