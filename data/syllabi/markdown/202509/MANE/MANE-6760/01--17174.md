## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

## This course focuses on the finite element methods for problems encountered in fluid mechanics. Computational approaches are presented to solve advection-dominated transport problems starting with scalar advection-diffusion equations and building to compressible and incompressible Navier-Stokes equations. Emphasis is placed on developing and analyzing formulations that are stable and higher-order accurate such as Galerkin/least-squares, SUPG and VMS methods. Unsteady formulations are considered using the semi-discrete and/or space-time methods. Issues concerning implementation of finite element methods (FEM) are also discussed. These issues include: the discrete formulation, time integration, boundary conditions, root finding for nonlinear equations, and linear equation formation and solution.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

1\. Present the basic principles of finite element methods applied to solve fluid flow problems.

2\. Develop and implement CFD algorithms.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students should be able to formulate and correctly use the basic principles of finite element methods for fluid flows.
- Students should understand and apply computational concepts such as stabilization methods, time-integration techniques and non-linear procedures.
