## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

A course on identifying and solving marketing problems through the systematic gathering and analysis of market information and data. This undergraduate course focuses on a careful definition of marketing problems and objectives, specification of information needs, completion of the different stages in the process of marketing research including deciding upon a research design, sampling theory, data collection and analysis and using the information so obtained to identify implications for marketing management.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Identify the various steps in the process of marketing research and the different types of marketing research.
- Compare and critique the various sources of marketing information and the means methods of gathering information.
- Design and execute a marketing research project, including preparation of a questionnaire.
- Analyze and interpret research data.
- Present the research results in a manner useful to the marketing decision-making process, both orally and in the form of a written report.
- Discuss the ethical considerations of marketing and marketing research methods.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

1. **Exams:** There will be two exams that are equally important. The exam dates and times are in the course schedule section of this document. Both of the exams will require application of the theories and principles covered in the class. The format of the exams will consist of objective and subjective questions. There are NO makeup exam without a written proof of a university approved conflict.

1. **In Class Assignments:** There will be in-class assignments in some of the classes. The assignments will be based on the concepts taught in the last class and/or the present class. There are no make-up assignment and if you miss a class, you will lose the points. Please note that you will work in teams for these assignments. You need to work remotely with your team for completing these assignments.

1. **Individual Assignments:** Final Assignment: The details of the final assignment will be given after exam 1. It is due on the first day of the exam week. It has to be submitted electronically on LMS/BB. Late submissions will not be accepted. The assignments will be different for the students in registered for MGMT 4470 and MGMT 6550. Students registered for MGMT 6550 will have questions extra questions.

1. **Class Participation:** Class participation includes attendance, punctuality, and active participation in class discussions. Please come to class having read the material assigned for the week and provide comments and examples that demonstrate knowledge and integration of course material, including respectful responses to and meaningful elaboration on the comments of others. Asking related questions is also a form of class participation. Your performance will be adversely affected by your absence from the class. If you have more than two unexcused absences in this class, you will start losing class participation points. Sleeping in class, engaging in other non-class related activities during class time (texting, chat, email, web browsing, working on assignments for other classes, etc.) are not allowed and will significantly affect your class participation grade.

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).
