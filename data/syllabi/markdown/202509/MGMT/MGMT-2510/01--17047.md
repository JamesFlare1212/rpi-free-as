## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is an introductory course in data analytics that covers topics that you will use in future management courses. You will learn how data are generated in organizations, how data move through the various departments, how data are stored, and how data can be converted to information and used for strategic, tactical, and operational decision making. You will also become proficient in spreadsheets and relational databases, and practice using specialized software to model business processes, conduct business intelligence, and visualize data. This course is highly interactive and you will need to come to class prepared to do the hands-on class activities individually and with a group.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

1. Business Processing Modeling
2. Entity-Relationship (ER) Diagrams
3. Relational Databases and SQL Queries
4. Spreadsheets: Data Cleaning and Manipulation, Functions, Conditional Formatting, Pivot Tables, Business Intelligence, What-If Analysis, and Basic Modeling
5. Data Visualization, Data Preparation, Charts and Graphs, Interactive Dashboards, Storytelling

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Be able to describe entire business processes visually.
