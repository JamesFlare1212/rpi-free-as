## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is a project-based course where MGMT6840 students work directly in a consultant role for a company, nonprofit, or startup. Students may also pursue their own startup idea with instructor permission. The course begins with lectures and workshops designed to teach the fundamentals of project planning, client engagement, and modern consulting practices, which will then be applied to semester-long team projects.

Project proposals with defined milestones and deliverables will be developed during the first two weeks of class and must be approved by the instructor. Throughout the semester, students will learn to scope consulting engagements, manage client relationships, and adapt to changing circumstances while maintaining a professional standard of communication and delivery.

By the end of the course, students will not only gain practical experience in client-facing consulting projects but will also develop the skills and confidence to operate effectively in real-world consulting engagements, preparing them for careers in consulting or in leadership roles within organizations.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Practicum in Management is designed and developed for graduate students from all educational backgrounds who want to learn the fundamentals of business as it applies to satisfying a “customer’s need”. Students will work on a team or individually on projects and communicate to the class frequently to facilitate student learning from one another. We begin with some basic tenets of business and an overview of how to develop and oversee a project plan. The course will help you begin to understand the process by which an idea can be developed into a business or to recognize that there is no business case, quickly. Alternatively, if you are working for a “customer” it will give you first-hand experience understanding their needs, articulating a plan and executing the plan to fill the need of your client.

This project-based course explores business processes and illuminates how business leaders develop ideas with a customer-driven approach, often in the face of scarce resources and great uncertainty.

Additionally, students will gain experience in the following skills associated with the practice of management consulting:

- Establish an understanding with the client on the need for a consulting engagement on a specific topic, and the appropriate approach and resources to be deployed
- Concretely define the scope of a consulting engagement
- Break up a complicated problem into logical pieces that can be individually addressed
- Efficiently gather the right data to build a relevant fact base
- Effectively extract information from internal and external experts
- Develop and present recommendations and conclusions in a persuasive manner to gain buy-in
- Influence others effectively using a range of ethical tactics

It has a number of educational objectives:

You will understand (and hopefully develop) the skills, attitudes and judgment that enable successful business professionals to recognize and pursue high potential opportunities;

- You will learn to identify, develop and execute a successful project plan, and/or learn how to use customer imperatives to drive your work effort;

- You will gain experience in talking to global business leaders related to your project and others in the value chain;

- You will understand the issues faced by businesses today in multiple stages of development;

- Learn how to effectively work with a diverse team;

- Learn how to develop a project plan and to manage the work flow;

- Determine your own potential to be a business leader.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Students will be asked to work individually and in teams. Individual accountability is assessed through milestone completion, participation, and professional communication, while team performance is assessed through project management, deliverables, and presentations. Project teams will be formed early in the semester based on submission and approval of project plans.

Assessment is a combination of **participation, report writing, presentations, and project management**, and applies to **two distinct types of projects**:

- **Technology Translation Project** – Teams will work on translating a technology, research concept, or innovation into a business or market application. Deliverables will be customized to reflect the needs of the project, but may include market analysis, customer discovery, business model development, and feasibility assessments.
- **External Client Project** – Teams will engage with an external organization, startup, or nonprofit to address a real client problem. Deliverables will be defined in consultation with the client and instructor, and may include strategy documents, process recommendations, financial analyses, or other actionable insights aligned with client needs.

In both projects, students are expected to **develop customized deliverables tailored to client requirements** and demonstrate the ability to adapt frameworks and methods to unique, real-world contexts.

**Grading will therefore reflect not only the quality of the final outputs, but also the professionalism, adaptability**

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Active participation is essential in a project-based consulting course, both for your learning and for the success of your team and client engagement. Attendance will be taken at all class sessions, workshops, and presentations.

- **Absences:** More than **two unexcused absences** will result in a **5% reduction of your final grade for each additional unexcused absence.**
- **Notification:** If you must miss class, you are expected to notify both the instructor and your teammates in advance whenever possible.
- **Making Up Missed Work:** Students missing class should (a) provide an asynchronous update on project progress in Webex related to their group tasks, and (b) coordinate with teammates to confirm additional plans for the following week.
- **Excused Absences:** Excused absences (e.g., illness, religious observance, professional obligation) must be communicated to the instructor in a timely manner and will not impact your grade.

Because client deliverables and team collaboration are central to the course, **poor participation, lack of communication, or repeated absences may result in a more significant reduction to your grade** regardless of overall class performance.
