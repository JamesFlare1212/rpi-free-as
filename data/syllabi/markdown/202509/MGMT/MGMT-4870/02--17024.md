## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

In this course you will integrate learning from various functional fields of management to develop your skills in the following areas:

- analyzing business situations and formulating strategy
- implementing strategy and making decisions that enhance a firm’s competitive advantage
- communicating strategic concepts and ideas persuasively to internal and external audiences

At the end of this course you will develop an appreciation of how various elements of an organization fit together to result in superior performance and sustained competitive advantage. In addition, you will also be able to identify effective business and corporate strategies, and differentiate them from those that are weak and uncompetitive.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

**Some student learning outcomes:**

- Learn how to conduct an in depth industry analysis
- Apply frameworks to understand firm strengths, weaknesses, sources of competitive advantage
- Learn the costs and benefits of different business level strategies
- Analyze the costs and benefits of various firm scope decisions- vertical integration, product diversification, geographic expansion
- Identify the appropriate entry mode for expansion- e.g. alliances, acquisitions
- Analyze different organizational structures and their benefits and disadvantages

**Strategy Analysis Report/ Presentation (Group)**

The strategy analysis report requires you to select a company with multiple business segments and analyze its performance. Details of the assignment are provided below.

**Case Assignment Questions**

**Note: These questions are meant as guidelines. Please consider any other issues that you think are relevant to support your analysis.**

Wal-Mart USA Searching For Growth

1. What does the retailing industry look like from a PESTLE/ Porter’s 5 forces standpoint?
2. What are Wal-Mart’s strengths/weaknesses and VRIN competencies?
3. Wal-Mart has always had a lot of success with new store formats. Between 2014-2018, Wal-Mart doubled the number of neighborhood stores, but growth is saturating. Do you think it is time to revive the ambitious plan to capture a larger share of urban markets? Would you support the proposal to grow the Neighborhood format from 800 to 1200 stores over the next four years? Why or why not? Give a detailed analysis and reasons.
4. Do you think Wal-Mart is successful because it is exploiting workers? Suppliers?
5. Looking at Wal-Mart’s growth options and its sources of competitive advantage, which of these strategic initiatives would you prioritize: (1) a build out of the company’s e-commerce initiative, or (2) an upscaling initiative
6. What steps would you take to implement your recommendation in (3) above? Any other recommendations for Wal-Mart overall?

Mc Donalds

1. What strategic issues is McDonald’s facing at the time of the case?
2. What are Mc Donald’s’ VRIN competencies?
3. How does McDonald’s capture value and make money?
4. What is Mc Donald’s strategy? Has it changed over the last few years and if so how?
5. What trends in McDonald’s environment are likely to impact the company’s ability to derive sustained competitive advantage?
6. McDonald’s thrived during the 2020-2022 pandemic by emphasizing digital, drive-through and delivery. How can they build on that momentum going forward? Any other recommendations for McDonald’s to pursue?

Disney

1. Why has Disney been successful for so long? What are the sources of its inimitable advantage?
2. What did Bob Iger do to rejuvenate Disney? What are some distinctive steps he took to turn Disney around? Do an in depth analysis of his turn around.
3. What issues is Disney facing at the time of the case? What should Disney do to address these issues?
4. Is Disney too diversified given current technological, globalization, and shareholder activist trends? Should Disney increase its involvement in distribution, notably through direct to consumer online streaming? How important is the latter for Disney’s long run performance?

**Guidelines for the Strategy Analysis Report**

The strategy analysis comprises of the following eight components:

1. Corporate Overview II. External Analysis III. Internal Analysis

1. Analysis of a key acquisition/alliance V. Governance analysis
2. Emerging Issues VII. Recommendations

The first deliverable is a **midterm** **report and presentation** covering sections I-III. The second deliverable is a **presentation** covering sections IV-VII. The two presentations in class will be for a maximum of 25 minutes. The mid term report will be a maximum 17 pages. Appendices and tables may be used to present additional material.

**What types of companies are appropriate for this project?**

For the project the goal is to pick a company that has **at least 3-4** lines of business or divisions (ideally one per group member). Here are some specific guidelines:

- (1) The company should be a multi-division company.
- (2) Each segment/line of business/division should report individually.
- (3) The company should be a public company to ensure enough data is available.
- (4) Fortune 500/Russell 1000 companies are good candidates, but there could be other choices as well.

Further guidelines on selecting companies and searching for data will be discussed in class, but you should start thinking of names and options that satisfy the above criteria as soon as possible. Note that the **deadline for selecting your company is September 19th**.

**Corporate Overview- Assignment I**

The assignment will cover the following:

1\) Analyze your company’s mission, objectives, and values. What is the corporate purpose behind your company and how does it intend to serve its shareholders and stakeholders? Is your corporation’s mission and purpose appropriate, or do you think it should be changed in any way? Is your company adequately responsive to ESG goals? How has it been performing along ESG dimensions?

2\) Discuss the products of your company's lines of business. Is their coherence in the various products/ lines of business and are they consistent with each other? Does each segment/ line of business serve the mission and vision of the company? Does any division standout as unrelated and not fitting in?

3\) What is the sales of your company? What is the sales of each line of business? How has your company grown over the last five years? Does growth of your company appear to be strong or is it stagnating? Are all divisions growing, or are some falling behind compared to the others?

4\) Identify to what extent your company operates overseas. What percentage of sales comes from abroad? Which are its primary markets overseas? How much production happens overseas/domestically?

5\) Conduct a financial analysis and financial benchmarking of competitors to assess your firm's performance- is your company generating above normal returns on invested capital? Returns greater than competitors and industry average?

6\) How has the stock of your company been performing? For the last 1, 3, and 5 years? Has it been outperforming the Dow Jones Industrials Average over these periods? What is the market value of your company and how has it been changing? What is the current opinion of analysts covering your company?

7\) Calculate key financial ratios of your company, ideally for the last 3 years. E.g. profitability ratios, leverage ratios. How have these ratios been changing over the last three years? Also calculate R&D/Sales, Advertising/Sales, and Tobin’s Q.

8\) What are some key products and brands of your company? How are they distributed and through what channels? What promotional strategies does your company adopt?

9\) Who are the key leaders of your company? The top management? How big is the board and what is the proportion of insiders/outsiders?

10\) Outline two or three key strategic issues that your firm is currently facing. Discuss why these are core issues

**External Analysis - Assignment II**

Assignment 2 and assignment 3 require you to separate out the divisions within your company and analyze them individually. Assignment 2 focuses on external drivers, while assignment 3 focuses on internal drivers. In assignment 2 you will conduct a Porter’s 5 forces analysis of the industry that the division is based in. In addition you will also be required to analyze key macro trends that are likely to impact the industry over time (using frameworks such as PESTLE).

**Internal Analysis - Assignment III**

In this assignment you will perform an internal analysis of each division within your company. As analytical tools, you may use VRIN and other relevant internal analysis tools discussed in class such as generic strategies (cost leadership, product differentiation). The purpose of this module should be to develop a division/business specific analysis of strengths/weaknesses and competitive position. Essentially you should answer the questions: how strong is the business in its industry, and where does it stand relative to other businesses within the company?

**Analysis of Key Acquisition and Key Alliance- Assignment IV**

Choose one major alliance and one major acquisition that your company recently entered into (e.g. in the last five years).

1. What was the strategic intent behind the alliance/acquisition? What goal is it achieving and what skills/capabilities is it bringing?
2. Was the choice of partner/target firm appropriate? Is there cultural fit? Was the premium paid justified (in the case of an acquisition)?
3. How did the stock market react to the announcement in the case of the acquisition? Did the price of your company go up/go down? What was the opinion of analysts? Did they think positively/negatively of the acquisition? What was the opinion of analysts, industry experts etc. on the alliance?
4. Why did the company choose to do the deal as an acquisition and not an alliance? Conversely, why did it choose to do an alliance, and why did it not choose to buy out the partner firm? What would have been the inefficiencies if some other means were chosen such as licensing?
5. Provide an update on the alliance and acquisition- has it been performing well? Are the objectives being achieved? Did the integration go smoothly (in the case of the acquisition)? Did the partners behave cooperatively and foster the alliance relationship?
6. Provide a brief overview of the overall alliance and acquisition activity of your company- for instance how many alliances/ acquisitions it has conducted in the past five years, how it has been leveraging alliances/ acquistions, in the US, abroad, in building technological capabilities etc. How active is it in Corporate Venture Capital?

**Analysis of Governance- Assignment V**

The assignment requires you to assess whether your company has sound corporate governance.

1. What percentage of your company is owned by insiders? Who are the major institutional investors in your company? What percentages do they hold?
2. How does the compensation of your CEO compare with competition? Is your CEO overpaid?
3. Is your company generally friendly to shareholders? Or does it have any factors that are unfavorable, such as poison pills, concentrated voting rights etc.? Have there been any recent governance scandals that have rocked your company? Any ethical scandals?
4. How has the composition of the board changed over the years? For this question, retrieve the board composition from 10 years back (e.g. using the form DEF14A from SEC’s Edgar database) and compare it with the current composition. What changes do you see? Has the board increased in size? Has the number of insiders increased/decreased? Has diversity in terms of race/gender increased? How do the changes in the board reflect changes in strategy?

**Emerging Issues -VI**

1. What is the approach your company is taking toward doing business abroad- multi domestic, global, international, transnational? Or is it primarily exports driven? To what extent is it affected by tariffs etc.? What steps is it taking in response- is manufacturing etc. being restructured?
2. Briefly describe your company’s digitalization efforts to date.
3. How has your company been leveraging AI, predictive analytics, and LLMs in its different businesses? Describe some key initiatives and impacts of AI on your company. Have these efforts been centralized or decentralized?
4. What about others emerging areas- e.g. iOT, Blockchain, Robotics?

**Corporate Recommendation - VII**

In this assignment, you will bring everything together to provide recommendations for the company, i.e., suggest specific strategic actions the company should undertake to enhance competitive advantage. Your recommendations should be concrete and specific keeping in view the overall strength/weaknesses of the company.

If two or more group members rate your contribution as unsatisfactory, you will be awarded a substantially lower grade (e.g 0%) on that assignment. Additionally, at my discretion, you may be awarded a lower grade on other assignments as well. It is therefore essential that you do your fair share of group work.

Note- If any group member does not contribute satisfactorily to group activities, please let me know asap. Additionally I may award a particular group member a lower grade if I have reason to believe that group member has not contributed adequately to a project (for instance if the group member is unable to answer questions on a group project satisfactorily).
