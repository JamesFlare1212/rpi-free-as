### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

## STUDENT LEARNING OUTCOMES

Students are expected to achieve the following outcomes after successfully completing this course:

1. Demonstrate and understand the basic concepts and tools in business statistics.
2. Distinguish between different statistical tests and identify the usage of different tests.
3. Conduct statistical tests of hypothesis and infer conclusions.
4. Use software for statistical analysis and apply statistical methods in a real business environment.
5. Discuss the ethical considerations of data analysis.
