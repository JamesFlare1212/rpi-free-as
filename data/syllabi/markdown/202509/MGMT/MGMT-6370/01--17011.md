## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course is designed to introduce Finance students to the theoretical and practical aspects of financial futures, options, and other derivatives. In recent years, there has been considerable growth in markets for futures and options; and there has been phenomenal growth in the markets for futures contracts on financial assets, as well as options on these assets. Derivatives have become one of the most important tools of modern finance, from both the academic and the practical standpoint. The purpose of the course is to provide the student with the necessary skills to value and to employ options, option-like-instruments, and futures. In order to provide a useful treatment of these topics, it is necessary to stress fundamentals and to explore topics at a technical level.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Expose the students to knowledge about derivative products, including forwards, futures, options, and swaps, to foster an understanding of their impact in financial markets.
- Develop insights into pricing and trading strategies for call and put options to aid analysis in their use for investment risk management.
- Understand the concept of swaps and credit derivative products to utilize them for risk mitigation under various financial scenarios.
- Evaluate the appropriateness of derivatives in a variety of corporate and investment scenarios to develop skills in reporting financial analysis.
