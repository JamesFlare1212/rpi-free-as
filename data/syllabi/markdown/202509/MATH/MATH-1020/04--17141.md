## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Basic symbol manipulation skills pertaining to integration.
- Ability to state and explain basic Calculus definitions and theorems and their applications.
- Ability to describe curves parametrically and apply Calculus concepts and techniques to curves described in this way.
- Ability to express curves via polar equations and to solve Calculus problems in the polar coordinate system.
- Ability to apply Calculus to selected problems in Science, Engineering and Mathematics.
- Ability to use convergence tests to analyze the behavior of infinite series.
- Ability to represent functions using power series and solve problems using these representations.
- Ability to describe lines and planes in space in equation form and solve problems using these representations.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Exams:** The material covered on each exam will be announced on [LMS](https://lms.rpi.edu/) when the date of the exam is near. Exams will be given in person during the Wednesday test block in ACADMY AUD (sections 01–04) or DARRIN 308 (sections 09–16). **Students who receive testing accommodations from the** [**Disability Student Services (DSS)**](https://studenthealth.rpi.edu/list-services/disability-student-services) **office will take their exams in the DSS testing center (DARRIN 345), preferably at the same date and time. Instructions for scheduling an exam time in the testing center will be provided to students who submit their DSS documentation to the instructor.**

If a student has a pre-scheduled absence (e.g. an athletic competition, official excused absence to attend a conference) on the day of an exam, then they must notify the instructor via email as soon as they learn of the absence and **no later than one week before the regularly scheduled exam**. Documentation excusing the student from class at the time of the regularly scheduled exam must be received by the instructor before the day of the exam. This documentation must be either an excused absence documented by the Student Success office or, if the absence is due to an athletic competition, documentation from a coach. Provided the above conditions are met, the student will then take a conflict exam at a different time.

If a student misses an exam for any other reason (e.g. overslept, was ill on the day of the exam, did not know there was an exam, did not know where the exam was), then the student will receive a zero on the exam. This score will be dropped as the lowest score at the end of the semester. The remaining 2 exam scores plus the final exam score times two will count toward the student’s course grade. Thus, **the final exam serves as a make-up exam for the missed exam**. If a student misses more than one exam, then they will need to email the instructor within 24 hours of missing the additional exam(s) to make arrangements to discuss options, if any.

Note the policies in this section do not apply to the final exam. See relevant policy under **Final Exam** below.

**Final Exam:** The final exam will be cumulative and will be held on a date and time determined by the [Office of the Registrar](https://registrar.rpi.edu/services/class-scheduling/final-exam-schedule). All students must take the final exam at the time scheduled by the Office of the Registrar. Exceptions will only be made if there is a scheduling conflict as defined [here](https://registrar.rpi.edu/services/class-scheduling/final-exam-schedule-conflicts), or if a student is excused by the Student Success office. Please note that the Student Success office will only issue an excuse for final exams for extreme circumstances, such as hospitalization, and not for travel. (Read more [here](https://success.studentlife.rpi.edu/current-students/academic-and-personal-support/missing-final-exam).) Thus, students should not make end-of-semester travel arrangements until the final exam schedule is posted, or should only schedule travel for the time period after the last day of final exams, which is Monday, December 22.

**Homework:** Achieve homework assignments will be due once or twice weekly on lecture days (Mondays and Thursdays or Tuesdays and Fridays) at 11:59 PM and will be graded automatically by the system. Students may submit these assignments late up to two days after the original deadline, but a penalty will be applied to the score. A 10% penalty will apply to assignments submitted the day after the deadline and a 20% penalty will apply to assignments submitted two days after the deadline. These extensions are granted by Achieve automatically to all students and do not need to be requested. **No other due date extensions will be granted for any reason.** To further compensate for this, five homework assignment scores will be dropped at the end of the semester.

Additional problems will be assigned from the textbook and will be posted on [LMS](http://lms.rpi.edu/). Though these problems will not be collected nor graded, they should not be viewed as optional. Success in the course depends on successful completion of all homework, both Achieve homework and textbook homework. Exam and quiz problems are frequently chosen from homework assignments and lecture examples. Full solutions to odd-numbered textbook problems can be found on Achieve. When logged in to our Achieve course, view content by assignments and scroll to the bottom of the page. The student solutions manual for our textbook may be found below No Due Dates. Tip: download and save these files for use in future courses!

**Quizzes:** Quizzes will be given most weeks during lecture. Material to be covered on each quiz will be posted on [LMS](http://lms.rpi.edu/). Students must take quizzes in their assigned lectures in order to receive credit for the quiz.

**No make-up quizzes will be given for any reason.** To compensate for this, the lowest two quiz scores for each student will be dropped at the end of the semester. If a student misses more than two quizzes for valid reasons, then they will need to email the instructor within 24 hours of missing the additional quiz(zes) to make arrangements to discuss options, if any.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Academic Integrity:** Students should familiarize themselves with the section on academic integrity in the [Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities](https://rpi.app.box.com/s/p1b39vybm914b5cao4csrgl84bfrrh2l) (pp. 17–19). All work submitted on exams and quizzes must be the student’s own. In addition, no calculators, computers, tablets, open books, open notes, formula sheets, phones, headphones, or watches may be worn or used during exams or quizzes. Talking or communicating with other students in any way during exams or quizzes is also prohibited. Students may discuss homework with other students, tutors, the TAs, or instructor, but the work submitted must be the student’s own.

The minimum academic sanction applied to a student found in violation of the above policies will be a grade of zero on the assignment. This zero will not be able to be dropped at the end of the semester. A stronger sanction may be applied if warranted. A report regarding the violation will also be submitted to the Dean of Students Office and will remain on file until graduation. Repeat offenders will fail the course and will be subject to judicial action.

**Disability Student Services (DSS):** Students with a memorandum from the [DSS](http://studenthealth.rpi.edu/disabilityservices) office identifying accommodations for exams must email the memorandum to the instructor **by Friday, September 5** since providing the accommodations requires advance planning. In your email, you must state the following:

- full name,
- course and section number (e.g. MATH 1020-01),
- which accommodations on your DSS memo you are requesting to use in the course, and
- which assignments (exams and/or quizzes) you are requesting to use your accommodations on.

Students will not be granted accommodations unless a memorandum from DSS has been received.

Students who receive testing accommodations from the DSS office will take their exams in the DSS testing center (DARRIN 345), preferably at the same date and time. Instructions for scheduling an exam time in the testing center will be provided to students who submit their DSS documentation to the instructor. DSS students opting to also use their accommodations on quizzes will also take their quizzes in the DSS testing center (DARRIN 345), preferably at the same date and time.
