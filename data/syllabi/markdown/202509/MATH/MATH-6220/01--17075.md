## Course Goals

### Goals:

The main objectives of this course is for the students to grow their analytical skills, their team work skill, and the ability to apply the concepts and tools to understand mathematical problems.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Contents: Principles and applications of (linear) functional analysis, with tentative topics on

- Normed spaces: Banach and Hilbert spaces
- Functionals and operators (e.g. linear, bounded/continuous, compact); spectral theory
- Hahn-Banach theorem, Baire Category theorem: reflexivity, weak convergence, uniform boundedness theorem
- Applications to differential and integral equations, approximation theory, optimization, numerical methods, data analysis

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- To learn and to translate concrete problems into the language of functional analysis
- To be able to apply concepts, properties, and tools in functional analysis to establish theoretical understanding for mathematical models and applications, with coherent, concise, and well organized arguments and justification
- To contribute to group work and discussions in a team learning setting

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

1\) Homework assignments  
2\) In-class group work and discussions  
3\) In-class midterm exam

## Grading Criteria

### Other Course Policies:

**Policy on Collaboration and on Use of External Sources (e.g. papers, books, AI products):** You  
are encouraged to discuss homework problems with others, or to refer to online or library references.  
However, the work you submit for grading must be written by yourself and represents  
your own understanding on the subjects. If your work involves significant help from other people,  
reference books or papers, explicitly state this in your report to avoid penalty in grade.  
Using artificial intelligence (AI) tools (including AI writing tools) to complete your homework  
assignments for this course is not permitted and will be considered academically dishonest and a  
violation of the Academic Integrity Policy.  
Students may be called for interviews with the instructor to verify their homework represents  
their own thinking and understanding.  
**Policy on Late Submission:** Each homework is due by a specified time & date. Homework  
reports must be submitted as single documents via LMS/gradescope. Late submission is strongly  
discouraged, and it will result in reduction in grade of  
5%: on the same day of deadline, at most up to 1/3 of a day (i.e. 8 hours); 10%: up to one day; 20%: up to two days  
No late homework is accepted after two days. Any request for extension without penalty needs  
to be made at least 24 hours before the deadline.  
**Questions regarding the grading of assignments and exam** must be discussed with the instructor  
within one week from your reports being available (an email notification will be sent to the class) and  
no later than December 12, 2025 (final class).

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).
