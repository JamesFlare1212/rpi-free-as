## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Before there was any print or recorded media available to humanity, there was one effective way to store information in human memory and transmit it in an easily received and memorable fashion: the song. Virtually all human societies have histories of song—defined here as lyrics/words with music—that often define the origins and aspects of their cultures, especially those societies with oral traditions. The mnemonic power of song has been used to pass historical information through generations, and in the present day, a new song can unite and even define a generation through the power of global media. Songs have the power to profoundly affect people emotionally at the most personal and intimate level, while financially helping create and sustain large multinational corporations. Songwriting is not an insignificant act and though it requires some skill, it can be done successfully by almost anyone who tries, even without any musical training.

This course examines the wide variety of elements present in the creation of a successfully written song, while honing a higher sense of critique when analyzing the work of songwriters, both successful and obscure. Song recordings, videos, and lively class discussion frame a closer look at multiple creative techniques for this art form, and students are encouraged to experiment on a grand scale, without fear, when writing ten complete songs over the course of the semester.

Students get positive and useful creative feedback and song analysis along with opportunities for collaboration, while learning how to make their own effective demonstration recordings. Though it focuses primarily on aspects of songwriting as a popular and commercial art form, this course can also be seen as an effective study in structure building for short-duration creative design forms, analogous to similar forms in architecture, mathematics, chemistry, and computer sciences, where creative collaboration using sound precepts can yield innovative results. Or it can just help you finish that surefire hit you’ve been working on.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Develop an improved understanding of the art of songwriting, and selected works of great songwriters.
- Accumulate key recording techniques to effectively capture and affix their works.
- Collaborate effectively with writers and musicians, learning how to create alongside others successfully.
- Improve general writing, presentation, and communication skills.
- Generously and insightfully offer high-quality critique to other creative people, and receive it in kind.
- Create a personal portfolio of song demos, recorded at as high a quality as possible.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

10 SONGS/SONGWRITER PORTFOLIO (60%)- Students will meet the song deadlines as presented, delivering their demo files and lyric sheets to Webex prior to class on the due dates. Songs presented on time with a minimum of 1:30 in length (Song 1 & 2) or 2:00 (all other songs) receive 100 points. Songs presented after class on due dates receive a 5-point reduction, and a 10-point reduction for each day late. This grade is determined by an average of ten scores, one for each song.

SONGWRITER PAPER (15%)- Students will write a 1000-word paper (APA style, 3 citations min.) due in November, providing information and analysis of a selected songwriter, focusing on a single representative example of their work.

PRESENCE AND PARTICIPATION (25%)- The success of this class depends on the presence and participation of each student when listening to and critiquing everyone’s work. With this class, we will strive to make a community where quality creative feedback can be freely given and taken. Any student in danger of not receiving these points (aka not participating or responding) will be notified discreetly weeks in advance of assessment, and given every chance to reclaim them.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

The first unexcused absence I’ll let go. Any work due on that date is still expected on time, any missed quizzes score a zero. The second time will make me look askance at you, and wonder why you need to miss the class without an official excuse. Third time gets a probationary warning, the fourth will result in a half-grade drop off your final grade, with any further absences resulting in a further half-grade drop. Really, don’t let it get to this point for a songwriting class.

Truthfully, if you are repeatedly absent for whatever reason, I will become very concerned about you, and will do whatever I can to help you rather than just slash away at your grade. Please communicate with me if things are going so badly that this class has become an additional stress, and we can work it out.

An absence can become an excused one by informing me of your very good reason beforehand or, if it’s an emergency, as soon as possible after class with an appropriate reason. It is best for both of us for you to have documentation for the reason of your absence obtained through the Student Success Office.

### Other Course Policies:

USING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE FOR SONGWRITING: If you want to experiment with AI and songwriting, go ahead, have fun with it. **I, however, am not interested in listening to AI generated songs represented as your work in this class, so please do not rely on this technology to write your songs for you**. This course is an opportunity for you to develop a personal artistic discipline in a safe and nurturing creative environment and community, and I will personally do everything in my power to assist you in improving your craft if you want to take it seriously. Playing AI-generated material for me and the class and presenting it as your artistic statement will have no positive consequences, so please make time to create your own material, or seek a different, less demanding course to take this semester. If I suspect repeated use of AI from you, I will run the product through AI detection technology and let you know the response. You have the rest of your life to have AI write songs for you based on your queries…please use this moment in time to hone a blossoming creative skill that you might find fulfilling in the future.
