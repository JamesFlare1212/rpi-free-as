## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will improve their understanding of and practice their pronunciation, spoken grammar, fluency, and listening skills in English.
- Students will be able to develop presentation styles that meet the needs of different types of audiences.
- Students will be able to explain concepts in their field to listeners without expertise.
- Students will be able to gauge audience comprehension and respond to questions effectively.
- Students will understand cultural differences and how they impact teaching and learning.
- Students will be able to detect elements of speech that make their speech unintelligible to listeners and set goals for ongoing improvement.

The purpose of the teaching demos is to practice your public speaking in a lecture type class, apply the skills we are working on in the course, and build confidence and expertise discussing the content of your subject matter knowledge.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Attendance Policy

Students are expected to attend on time and actively participate in all classes. As students cannot participate well in class without being present, students will be expected to be physically in class and participate actively to receive full points for participation.

5 points for each 50 minutes of class. Students may miss 30 points (300 minutes of class) and receive an A grade in participation (full marks). These classes can be missed for any reason without any documentation.

If you have a prolonged illness or other circumstance that would cause you to miss more classes, please see the instructor and special arrangements may be made.

Students arriving after attendance has been called will lose 1 point for the day (if they fully participate while in the class). Students arriving more than10 minutes late – will lose 5 points for the day (if they fully participate while in the class), and students arriving more than 1 hour 10 minutes late will not receive any points that day.

### Other Course Policies:

Rules for Homework and Writing Assignments:

LATE POLICY: Late submission of assignments will be penalized 10% for each day after the due date (i.e., 1-24 hours late is a 10% deduction; 25-48 hours late is a 20% deduction). An absence is not an excuse for not completing homework. However, PLEASE always email me BEFORE you know you will be absent or if you cannot complete your work by the deadline.

ABSENCE POLICY FOR IN-CLASS ASSIGNMENTS: If you are absent on the day of an in-class assignment, such as a quiz or a peer review and your absence is included within the allowed absences, you may be allowed to make up the assignment or complete a similar assignment. If you have missed more than the allowed amount, you will no longer be allowed to make up in-class assignments

ABSENCE POLICY FOR IN-CLASS PRESENTATIONS: You must be present in class on the day of your presentation to receive full points on the presentation unless you have talked to me in advance and I have approved your request to present later (requests will usually only be approved in the case of medical or family emergencies). If the request is not approved or you do not talk to me in advance, you will be allowed to present outside of class for a significantly reduced score.

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

For plagiarism that is deemed unintentional (poor/lack of citation), the first violation will received a reduced score on the assignment. Further violations will result in a 0 grade for that assignment.

For plagiarism that is deemed intentional (copying someone else’s work), the first violation will result in a 0 grade for that assignment. The second violation results in failure of the course.
