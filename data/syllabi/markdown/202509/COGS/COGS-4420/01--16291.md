## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course introduces students to basic concepts and methods of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and their applications in computer games. The topics we will discuss in this class include decision making, movement, path finding, formation, learning, and AI for humanlike characters. This course will take the form of a combination of lectures, presentations by students, class discussions, and self-directed projects.
