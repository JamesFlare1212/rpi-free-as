## Course Goals

### Goals:

The intent for this course is to establish an understanding and a sense of urgency around the impact of building design on climate change, and to prepare researchers and advanced professionals to work with energy and operational carbon in buildings, using various tools to analyze and improve their designs.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Climate and Buildings, Thermal Comfort, Psychrometrics, Ventilation, Heating and Cooling Loads, Building Envelope, Solar Radiation, Operational Carbon

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Student will learn how to analyze the impact of buildings on climate change via calculating operational carbon emissions
- Student will learn how to evaluate the factors that influence thermal comfort.
- Student will learn how to analyze the climate data using .epw files
- Student will learn how to demonstrate an understanding of the three-tiered approach to designing energy efficient buildings

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Attendance is mandatory and will be recorded during each day of class, unless explicit permission is given based on unique individual circumstances. Two unexcused absences will result in reduction of a letter grade. Further absences may result in failing the class.

### Other Course Policies:

Late arrivals to class impact the entire session and is not tolerated unless prior explanation is provided by the student.  
Missed assignments due to an excused absence can be made up the following week, uploaded prior to the lecture.  
Final project submission and presentation date is not flexible. Only in extraordinary circumstances (such as severe illness), it can be made up via an online session.
