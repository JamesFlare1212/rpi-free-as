## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course will introduce basic material principles and construction concepts and explore their creative and technical application to the architectural design process. In the course, the students will develop intuition relating to structural and material functions, behavior, aesthetic qualities, and form/assembly of materials through a series of **hands-on** exercises. Students will learn through digital simulations, building prototypes, trial and error, and documenting and reflecting on their processes. Throughout the semester, students will study and analyze structural and material strategies in a project of their own design, communicating their proposal through language, images, drawings and diagrams.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Outcome #1: STRUCTURAL KNOWLEDGE; VERTICAL LOADS: Ability to demonstrate and communicate structural knowledge through the active testing and experimentation with both physical and digital structural models under vertical loads.
- Outcome #2: STRUCTURAL KNOWLEDGE; MULTI-DIRECTIONAL LOADS: Ability to demonstrate, analyze, and communicate structural knowledge through the active testing and experimentation with both physical and digital structural models under multi-directional loads.
- Outcome #3: MATERIAL KNOWLEDGE: Ability to demonstrate, communicate material knowledge through drawings, models and direct engagement with various physical materials.
- Outcome #4: MATERIAL ANALYSIS: Ability to demonstrate, analyze and communicate material knowledge through research and the production of writing and diagrams.
