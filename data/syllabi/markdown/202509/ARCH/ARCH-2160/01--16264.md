## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course introduces students to the breadth of concerns within the history, theory, and critical practices of architecture and especially the generation of architecture. In a series of lectures, discussion sessions, workshop assignments and a final project, we will examine the way several key historical and contemporary figures in architecture have used different mediums to generate, explore, and convey architectural ideas. We will focus specifically upon the mediums of photography, drawings, publications, constructs, and new media.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Outcome #1 Ability to demonstrate how various mediums such as sketching, diagraming, drawing, and photography are used to in the design and production of architecture.
- Outcome #2 Ability to critically assess the historical and theoretical makeup of architectural media, using visualization, and writing.
- Outcome #3 Ability to propose an original architectural idea using various mediums such as sketching, diagraming, drawing, and photography.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

In addition to weekly lectures, students will be given a series of assignments, which may take the form of sketching, photography, drawing and writing. These assignments are intended to supplement and enhance the content being delivered in the lectures. These assignments are distributed periodically and

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

40% of your final grade will be determined by the completion and quality of the responses to the periodical assignments of the course. All assignments must be formatted as per the instructions and submitted in a timely manner.

50% of your final grade will be determined by the completion and quality of your final project. This assignment will challenge you to think critically and comprehensively. The final product should reflect your understanding of several the key themes and topics covered in the course.

10% of your final grade will be determined by your level and participation and engagement during the lecture and discussion sessions.
