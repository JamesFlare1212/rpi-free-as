## Course Goals

### Goals:

This is really a physics course. You will learn the wave physics of Xray diffraction, and the spin physics of nuclear magnetic resonance. Both methods are applied tp the structure of proteins, but the course is about the physics, not about proteins. Students will pick up some new mathematical concepts, such as space group symmetry, and spin operators. This course will prepare you for a career in molecular biophysics.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Students will be able to critically assess the quality of a protein structure as demonstrated by a poster or oral presentation.
- 2\. Students will be able to demonstrate a thorough understanding of biophysical methods for protein structure determination by answering questions on an exam.
- 3\. Students will be able to recall and explain the reasons behind steps in the experimental process of protein structure determination by answering questions on an exam.
- 4\. Students will be able to interpret raw data from biophysical methods for protein structure determination by completing homework assignments.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

| SLO | Assessment method |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Understanding quality of protein sstructure | Oral presentation, Exam questions |
| 2. Understanding the methods | Exam questions, Homework assignments |
| 3. Underpinnings of the methods | Exam questions, Homework assignments |
