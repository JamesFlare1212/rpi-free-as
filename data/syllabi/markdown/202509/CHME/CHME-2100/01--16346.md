## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is a course designed to supplement your lab safety courses and deals with safety protocols and practices associated with chemical process plants. It is designed to provide information necessary for your ABET accredited degree and to assist you in finding an internship and a job**.** The course is offered on-line by the American Institute of Chemical Engineers. It is part of the Safety and Chemical Engineering (SAChE) Certificate program offered by the society. The link for an overview is:

https://www.aiche.org/ccps/education/safety-and-chemical-engineering-education-sache-certificate-program

This second link will take you to a list of courses. You will need to click on the courses you need to take and Checkout from the site.

https://www.aiche.org/sites/default/files/docs/pages/sache_recommendations_fro_che_curriculum_r5_jan2025.pdf

To get “credit” for this course, you will complete the Level I Basic Curriculum. **There are 5 certificates in this module and they are listed below.** You are responsible for the first 5 (ELA 950 – ELA 954), but you can complete all 6 if you wish. Later in the senior year, you will be able to complete Level II if you wish.

**Courses**

**Level 1: Process Safety Basics**

1. ELA 950 Introduction to Process Safety
2. ELA 951 Hazard Recognition
3. ELA 952 Identifying & Minimizing Process Safety Hazards
4. ELA 953 An Introduction to Managing Process Safety Hazards
5. ELA 954 Introduction to Lab Safety
6. ELA 975 Process Safety Ethics – A Brief Introduction (Level 3)

**To be eligible for the on-line courses, you will have to become a member of AIChE**. Membership is **free** for undergraduate students, but you should also join RPI's Student Chapter as well. If you have issues, there is a link for FAQs that should help. https://www.aiche.org/ili/academy/about/faqs

**The link to sign up for AIChE membership is:**

https://www.aiche.org/membership/student

Once you become a member, you can sign up for the course. The courses should be free for student members.

Upon completing the course and obtaining your certificates, please email a copy to the instructor. The certificate file should have the following file name: “Last, First – RIN.pdf” or “Last, First – RIN.jpeg”. For example, “Smith, John – 1234567.pdf”

This will be used to assign a P/F grade for the course so you can receive credit.
