## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

A continuation of CHME 4010. Course includes topics on multi-dimensional transport processes, potential, boundary layer and turbulent fluid flows, convective heat and mass transfer processes, friction factors and drag in and around solid objects, heat and mass exchangers, and radiation heat transfer. The course extends the use of numerical methods to apply to multidimensional problems, convective heat and mass transfer problems, and the simulation of more complicated fluid flows including turbulence approximations. Credit not allowed for both this course and ENGR 2250.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Simplify models based on geometry and values of dimensionless groups
- 2\. Construct and solve microscopic balance equations
- 3\. Construct and solve macroscopic balance equations
- 4\. Solve coupled momentum/energy/species transfer problems

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Homework**  
Students are encouraged to discuss the underlying concepts and approaches with their classmates and collaborate on homework assignments in teams or groups. Each student team will be required to turn in their own individual assignment. Note that the work turned in should reflect only the team’s efforts (no copying or duplicate assignments should be turned in). Homework will be administered through the course LMS. A stapled, collocated, hard copy of the homework must be submitted to the instructor mailbox before the due time. Late homework will not be accepted.  
**Quizzes**  
Quizzes will be administered during lectures to review concepts discussed and reflect the most recent homework assignments. The frequency of quizzes will vary depending on the class progression but will generally be offered every other week. The lowest quiz grade will be dropped. This is done to provide for sickness, interviews, emergencies, personal problems, and other such exigencies that inevitably occur during the semester. No official excuses are necessary.  
**Exams**  
Four equally weighted exams (15% each) will be given throughout the semester. The exam dates may be found in the included course schedule. The final exam is TBD. An announcement will be made on LMS.

### Other Course Policies:

**Electronics are prohibited during lecture time**. Students observed using electronic devices such as cell phones, tablets, smart watches, etc. will receive a zero on the forthcoming exam.  
**Note About Showing Work**:  
In this class, the process of getting to an answer is as important (if not more) than the actual answer, and you need to be able to clearly communicate that process. Therefore, you must show, explain, and justify all your work to receive credit. If you do not show and annotate your work, you will receive zero credit (even if the answer is correct). For example, a series of equations without explanation will receive minimal credit even if all equations are correct. If you are algebraically manipulating equations to simplify, you should state what steps are being taken.  
Unless specifically requested in the problem statement, it is not acceptable to use packages such as MATLAB, Maple, Mathematica, etc. to perform manipulations, integrals, etc. If software is used to arrive at a solution the program name and the capacity in which it was used should be stated.
