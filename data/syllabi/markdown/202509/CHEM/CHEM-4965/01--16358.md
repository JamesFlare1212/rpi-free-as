## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

*Protein Structure Prediction from Deep Learning* is a seminar course of readings and student presentations from the recent literature. It is targeted to upper-level undergraduate and graduate students in chemistry and/or biology (co-listed); computer science students are also welcome. Students will participate in reading and presenting papers from the recent scientific literature in the areas of protein and nucleic acid structure prediction, structural virology (including structural biology of corona viruses), cancer biology, de novo protein design, generative AI, structural bioinformatics, protein dynamics, protein interaction networks, drug design, deep learning, protein folding, and protein evolution. Special emphasis will be placed on protein structure prediction with new AI-based methods, including *AlphaFold2*, *RosettaFold*, *ESMFold (Evolutionary Scale Modeling*), and generative AI methods for *de novo* protein design.

Some papers that students will present and the class will discuss include:

## Course Goals

### Goals:

- To understand the basic concepts by which protein sequence encodes protein structure and function.
- To understand fundamental biophysical principles defining protein’s conformation and its conformational dynamics.
- To understand and use evolving methods of contact prediction from evolutionary sequence co-variance data.
- To be aware recent advances in machine learning that can be applied to structural biology problems.
- To appreciate strengths and weaknesses of recent advances in DL/ML in protein structure modeling
- To appreciate recent advances in DL/ML methods for de novo protein design
- To appreciate the roles of protein dynamics in determining protein function.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

- Conformational properties of proteins
- Sequence-based bioinformatics
- Structural Bioinformatics
- Machine Learning
- Critical Assessment of Protein Structure Prediction (CASP)
- Evolutionary co-variance
- AlphaFold, RosettaFold, OpenFold, EMS Fold AIs
- De novo protein design using methods of deep learning Artificial Intelligence in structural biology

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will learn basic principles of protein structure, contact prediction, evolutionary co-variance analysis, machine learning, and generative AI. Students will have hands on experience modeling protein structures using modern ML/DL methods. All students will also gain experience in presenting scientific lectures.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Assessement is based on in class presentations.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

This is a 1 cr seminar course. Students may take the course for a grade or S / U. The grade is based on four criteria: Attendance, in person or on line, and participation in discussions (25%). Short in class ppt presentations based on assigned proteins (25%). Presentation of a paper from literature (40%). Short final paper (10%). Graduate students will be assigned more complex assigned proteins for their short in-class ppt presentations. Grades appeals may be initiated by contacting the Instructor.
