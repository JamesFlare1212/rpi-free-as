## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course introduces the principles and practices of high-performance concrete in modern civil engineering. Topics include the fundamentals of high-performance concrete, specialty molding and release agents, aggregate types and properties, mix design methodologies, and experimental testing. The course also covers the role of supplementary cementitious materials, chemical admixtures, and fiber reinforcement in enhancing concrete performance. Emphasis is placed on designing with specialty concrete mixes, optimization-based approaches, and sustainability considerations. Students will work on a real-world project focusing on the design of an ultra-lightweight concrete canoe from conception through construction. Teamwork, project planning, and management are emphasized, providing students with valuable hands-on experience in structural engineering and professional collaboration.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Explain the effects of constituent materials on concrete performance.
- Design specialty concrete mixes to meet specific performance objectives.
- Determine the mechanical properties of high-performance concrete through experimental testing.
- Apply optimization-based strategies to design high-performance concrete for multi-objective problems.
- Develop a design proposal and manufacturing plan for a concrete canoe that meets the constraints of the Concrete Canoe Competition that is organized by the American Society of Civil Engineers.

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own.
