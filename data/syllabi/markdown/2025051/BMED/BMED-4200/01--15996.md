## Course Assessment Measures

**Midterm Exams: Counts 50% towards the final grade calculation**

***There will be a total of two midterm examinations.*** The midterm exams are scheduled for ***Monday 06/09*** (midterm examination 1) and ***Monday 07/14*** (midterm examination 2). The first midterm exam covers material related to algebraic equations as well as ***all relevant material covered in class*** up to this point. The second midterm exam covers material related to algebraic and ordinary differential equations as well as ***all relevant material covered in class*** up to this point. Both midterm examinations have equal weight.

***There are no make-up for any of the midterm examinations. In case of a verifiable excuse, the percentage of the final examination will be used to substitute the percentage of the (one) missing midterm examination.***

**Final Exam: Counts 30% towards the final grade calculation**

This is a comprehensive 2-hour final examination and scheduled for ***Thursday, 08/14***. The final examination covers algebraic, ordinary and partial differential equations, controller design as well as ***all relevant material covered in class***.

**Quizzes: Counts 20% towards the final grade calculation**

There will be a total of 10 quizzes during the summer semester. Each quiz will be on a Thursday after class between 9:30 and 9:45 am (except weeks 1, 7, and 13). A particular quiz will typically cover material that was covered over the past two lectures, i.e., the Monday of the same week and the Thursday before.

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these.
