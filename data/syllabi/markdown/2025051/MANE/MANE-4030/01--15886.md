## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course is designed to introduce the student to the design process through a  
thorough study of mechanical design. Problem definition and solution requiring both analysis and  
optimization are emphasized, while studying both the fundamentals of mechanical design and specific  
mechanical components.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate an ability to apply knowledge of mathematics, science and engineering to analyze the stress and deflection in a variety of machine elements; Predict failure in machine elements subjected to static, cyclic, and impact loads; Design and analyze a variety of commonly used machine elements.
- Demonstrate an ability to identify, formulate, and solve engineering problems involving stress and deflections in machine elements, identification of potential failure modes, and performing the appropriate analysis.
- Demonstrate an ability to use the techniques, skills, and modern engineering tools necessary for engineering practice such as drawing free-body-diagrams, relating loading conditions to stresses, identifying potential failure modes and appropriate analysis techniques.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

LMS. You are expected to spend 8 hours outside of class each week doing work associated with this class,  
and the size of the homework and reading assignments reflects this. Most weeks, homework will be  
collected and graded. Submission of homework assignments is via Gradescope only. Homework  
assignment submissions are due by 9 PM on the day listed. Late homework will be accepted with a penalty  
of 10% off of that assignment for each 8 hours late. The graded homework, after dropping the lowest score,  
will count for 15% of the overall grade. The homework grading is not detailed, so you should always check  
your solutions against the posted solutions. It is essential that students do the problems and check their  
work against posted solutions to ensure that they understand the material.  
If a student cannot turn in a homework on time due to a valid excuse, late submissions may be allowed  
without penalty at the discretion of the instructor. See attendance policy below for details about valid  
excuses.
