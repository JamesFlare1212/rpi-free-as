### Additional Information for Course Information Section:

### How to Approach This Course

This course is going to be the first half of one project where you will be submitting the first three phases of the DFSS framework, DMA. The remaining three phases (DOV) will be completed in the next course, ISYE6970 Professional Project, thus here is the approach:

We will have four synchronous sessions, in which we discuss and scope the approach. They are:

1. **Project Kick Off:** conducted during the first week of class to discuss project selection and course layout.
2. **Cultural Change Management and Team Effectiveness**: where you will be going through various tools to help you get your stakeholders ready for your new design.
3. **TRIZ:** begin the conceptual designs by looking at problem-solving techniques for more complex problems.
4. **DOE and Dashboards**: continue the conceptual designs learning how to conduct a "design of Experiments"
5. **Dashboards & KPIs**: learn how to create a Communication Cell identifying your key metrics for your project.
6. **Final Package:** goes through Lessons Learned, Minitab comparison, and Do’s / Don’ts for the Final.

The digital classroom will walk you through a series of steps – the approach – and provide you with the learning resources you will need right when you need them. In addition, you will be able to access the LSS Library and Minitab Instructions throughout the semester.

Whenever you have an issue or get stuck, send an email to your mentor to schedule a one-on-one Zoom session to talk through the problem. Your Mentor is available to assist you at any point where and when you need. Just reach out and we will schedule something mutually convenient.

As you progress through, you will prepare a series of “deliverables” – artifacts that show your work to that point. You will submit these deliverables in the digital classroom.

Your Mentor will evaluate your deliverables and give you feedback – feedback you can incorporate for your final package.

When you have completed your phase, your Mentor will evaluate it according to the pre-established rubric (which you can see in the classroom before you submit). If your project meets the criteria, you can move to the next phase.

When you are done with all the phases and you have received feedback that your work has met the rubric criteria, you will consolidate all the phases into one cohesive package which will be your Final.

My role in the course is to serve as your Mentor as you work through the project. Rensselaer hires us to be Mentors based on our academic background as well as our years of being a practitioner in the field – and I still continue to actively practice in the profession as a full-time endeavor.  
Therefore my role is to be your Mentor – to be your guide, to give you feedback, to be there wherever you have questions or need assistance. We can schedule individual times to meet and discuss, we can do it during a HW session, we can do whatever is needed so that you have what you need, and where you need it, to successfully master the course.  
Let me address “grades” for a moment – If you are to receive a zero for your deliverable because it failed to meet the rubric criteria and thus, you require to resubmit, you will lose 2 points. For lateness in submitting your deliverables, I will deduct 1 point per day. If you contact me prior to the due date, informing me of the possible lateness, you will not receive any deductions in your grade. The reason for the grading deducts is to ensure you are progressing in your journey within the allotted timeline of this course. It is vital you complete the work sequentially and as per the instructions so to avoid “waste” in rework. I want you to always be submitting your best work – work that you feel strongly not only meets the criteria but also is a deliverable you would proudly submit to your manager at work. When this occurs, the focus is no longer on the “grade” but on your mastery.  
There will be supplemental deliverables that are going to be optional for you to complete. If you successfully complete them as per the outlined criteria, then you can be rewarded with up to 5 additional bonus points going towards your overall deliverables’ percentage.  
Finally, I know that you are a working professional with many draws to your time and energy. I also know how big of a commitment it is to work on a graduate degree while employed – I did it myself. So, please, the key to success is communication. If you need additional time for your deliverable, please let me know. We can work on a workaround together. If you are struggling, do not wait until you are at the end of the phase, reach out – we can schedule a mutual time to discuss your issue and get back on track.  
The syllabus is posted – please take a look at it and plan ahead for the synchronous sessions and the project milestones as laid out. Remember, I am here to mentor and guide you through this journey, so take advantage in developing your expertise in DFSS projects. I hope you have an enlightened and outstanding semester. And most of all, I hope you improve your workplace!

### Additional Information for the Instructor Section:

### Instructor Note

Welcome to the start of your Design for Six Sigma (DFSS) Lean Six Sigma journey! This course will introduce you to the DFSS philosophy. The goal is to use the “Show by Doing” mentality making you AMBASSADORS for change. It is having you grasp the paradigm shift from “Increasing Quality at work means higher costs” to “Increasing Quality means reducing costs”.  
This course is designed with a logic flow where every week you will be completing a piece of the final deliverable which is the DMA Project piece of DMADOV framework. Since Design for Six Sigma deals with creating a new process or redesigning an existing one within your workplace it requires additional tools and methods than producing a DMAIC project. Thus, this course is designed to handle the first three stages of the DFSS framework and the ISYE6970 Professional Project course will comprise the remaining three stages. It is important during project selection that you work with your manager, your local BB / MBB and the Mentor to choose a viable project for these two courses which will make up 6 credits for your Degree.  
Before you get started, review the information in this document to better familiarize yourself with the course, its structure, expectations, outcomes, and flow of work. If you have any questions, please post your question in the "Ask the Mentor" discussion board so it becomes a learning for your fellow classmates as well.
