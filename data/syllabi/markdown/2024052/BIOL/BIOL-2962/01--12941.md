## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is an undergraduate level introductory course. It explores fundamental concepts in neurobiology such as, the human body’s chemical and electrical signals, how neurons communicate, the action potential, ion channels, neurotoxins, muscle contraction, neurological diseases, as well as animal movement, behavior, and intelligence. The goal is to provide active learning in a collaborative environment via interactive discussions.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

The course content is divided into the following topics:

1. Hormones, the Endocrine System, and Endocrine Disruptors.
2. The Nervous System, Action Potentials, and Ion Channels.
3. The Senses - the human ear, eye, smell and taste receptors.
4. Animal Movement - bones and muscle.
5. Living Supercomputer - the Human Brain.
6. Animal Behavior and Intelligence - Final Project.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate mastery of the foundational knowledge and core concepts of Human Body Systems by answering graded multiple choice questions and/or short answer questions in the weekly Assessments.
- Apply foundational knowledge and core concepts of Human Body Systems by answering graded multiple choice questions and/or short answer questions in the weekly Assessments.
- Present a Final Project by gathering information from research articles, texts, the web, and other relevant established sources, and by using the RPI Library, assessment is by oral presentations to peers in class.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

- There are 5 Assessments.
- Assessments are each worth 20 points, and consist of short answer questions, or multiple-choice type questions.
- The Final Project is a presentation on any relevant topic newly learned this term.

The total course maximum possible points is 100 points
