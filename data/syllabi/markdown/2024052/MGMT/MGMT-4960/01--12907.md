## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Sustainability (as defined by the triple bottom line concept of pursuing economic, social, and environmental positive outcomes) has become the norm in many places of our society and businesses must respond. This course looks at the ways the early environmental movement, which more recently has given way to the sustainability movement, has shaped, and will continue to shape, business decision-making and behavior through the law, markets, and stakeholder pressure. Dominant issues around business sustainability will be explored such as pollution, natural resource depletion, and climate change, as will the ways that businesses are responding to these big picture issues.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will demonstrate the ability to research and analyze companies and industries (as it applies to sustainability).
- Students will demonstrate the ability to integrate and apply their knowledge behind each of the core functional areas in business (as it applies to sustainability)
- Students will demonstrate the ability to analyze complex business problems and formulate recommendations that could be used by managers.
- Students will demonstrate an ability to communicate using modern business methods.

Weekly Homework (20%)  
We will have homework assignments throughout the six weeks. They will be short and targeted towards reinforcing key points throughout our readings and discussions. They may include brief quizzes on the material to be covered that day or short reflective essays to be written before or at the beginning of class.  
Individual Assignments Company Research Project (30%)  
This is a research project in which you will analyze one company (pre-approved) in depth over the course of the class. You will be asked to learn about the company you select (in general) and apply the topics we’re covering in class on a daily and weekly basis to the company you’ve selected. This will include the development of a comprehensive slide deck (i.e., PPT, Google Slides, Prezi) over the course of the class—with students presenting their work-in-progress research as it relates to the topic/reading each class. Your final grades on this project will be broken down between ongoing presentations of research (15%) and the final presentation itself (15%). The goal is to have a comprehensive picture of the “Sustainability Strategy and Implementation Details” of the company you select. While there is no final paper required to accompany these slides, the slides themselves should include details (i.e., facts, data, quotes, information) and should be sourced properly within the slide (i.e., with data referenced in the slides and an accompanying set of Reference slides). The recommended style will be APA—however, that’s less important as long as the referencing is consistent. More information on this project will be provided in class and on the LMS.  
Group Project & Presentation – Sustainable Event (30%)  
The class will be developing information and plans for RPI’s Athletic Department to hold a “sustainable event” in the fall—likely a home football game. That will include the creation of sub-groups to work on portions of this event plan as well as coordination amongst the entire class to develop one comprehensive presentation/plan.
