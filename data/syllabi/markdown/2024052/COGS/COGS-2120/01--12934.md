## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This undergraduate course is an introduction to the new and quickly growing field of cognitive science. Cognitive Science is a highly interdisciplinary field of study of mind at the intersection of philosophy, psychology, computer science, neuroscience, linguistics, and anthropology. Since it is so highly interdisciplinary, we will spend a bit of time on a number of different topics; this broad approach will serve to introduce the students to the wide range of subfields within cognitive science.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Develop an understanding of the many subfields and approaches within cognitive science and an ability to think about cognitive science by focusing on the connections between them
- Assess and critique various models of cognition
- Connect and apply principles of cognitive science to experiences outside of the classroom

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

This course uses an additive grading system. Your grade will be determined by the number of points you accumulate by the end of the semester. No assignment is, strictly speaking, “required,” though missing out on more points means fewer points left to still gain. You can mix and match these point opportunities however you like throughout the semester within the additive grading system.

**500 points – Exam I** – **Friday, June 7th** – open-note, taken online via LMS

**500 points – Exam II** – **Friday, June 28th** – open-note, taken online via LMS

**300 points – Attendance/Participation** – semester-long grade

**25 points – Intro Video** – easy early points and let me learn your names!

**200 points – Concept Map Assignment** – due by **Sunday, May 26th** – in this assignment, you will organize a visualization of the concepts of cognitive science and their connections

**100 points – Chinese Room Assignment** – due by **Thursday, May 23rd** – in this assignment, you will read one of the most important papers in Cognitive Science and answer a few questions about your interpretations of the paper

**100 points – Article Summary Assignment** – due by **Tuesday, June 4th** – in this assignment, you will read an academic journal article within Cognitive Psychology and provide a concise summary of the research questions, methods, and results presented in the article

**100 points – Cognition & Aging Assignment** – due by **Friday, June 14th** – in this assignment, you will read a paper presenting an interesting perspective on cognition and aging and provide a response summarizing and following up on this paper

**100 points – Concept Map Part II** – due by **Tuesday, June 25th** – in this assignment, you will reflect on your Concept Map from the first week of class.

**50 points – Deep Dive** – due by **Friday, June 28th** – explore a class-related topic in more detail and report on your findings (**max 2**)

**50 points – Cognitive Science and Media** – due by **Friday, June 28th** – consume media (tv show, movie, book, videogame, etc.) that relates to cognitive science and reflect on the connections (**max 2**)

**50 points – Cognitive Science and Current Events** – due by **Friday, June 28th** – connect ideas from class to ongoing current events (**max 2**)

**50 points – Cross-topic Reflections** – due by **Friday, June 28th** – connect concepts from two different topics from class and reflect on their relationship (**max 2**)

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work.

Above all else, this means that content must be student-generated, not AI-generated. Use of generative AI writing tools such as Chat GPT for work that you are claiming is your own is prohibited (spelling and grammar-checking tools are fine).

Submission of any assignment that is in violation of this policy will result in a penalty of a grade of zero and formal disciplinary action might be undertaken.

You may not reproduce, verbally convey, or otherwise reveal the content of an exam to any individual at any time.

If you have any question concerning these policies, please ask for clarification.
