## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is an introductory course – an introduction to the fundamentals of microcomputer technology and its application in management and information systems. Through handson exercises students will develop competencies in working with data through spreadsheets applications and relational databases. Topics include foundational concepts in information systems, spreadsheets and basic data modeling and database design.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- (1) Create Business Process Models
- (2) Create Operational Models from Business Process Models
- (3) Create Dimensional Models from the Operational Models
- (4) Create Reporting Models from the Dimensional and Operational Models
- (5) Create Analysis Models from the Dimensional and Operational Models

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Test 1: Business Process Model Fundamentals

Percent of course grade: 20%

Students will be given a business process and will be required to build a Business Process diagram and an Entity-Relationship diagram.

Test 2: Operational Model Fundamentals

Percent of course grade: 20%

Students will be given an Entity-Relationship diagram and will be required to write SQL statements to answer questions **without using gui or inbuilt software**.

Test 3: Dimensional Model Fundamentals

Percent of course grade: 20%

Students will use MS Access to write queries against a pre-built Data Warehouse.

Test 4: Excel Fundamentals

Percent of course grade: 20%

Students will be tested on their mastery of Excel fundamentals through a series of problems.

Test 5: Analytical Model Fundamentals:

Percent of course grade: 20%

Students will use what they have learned during the semester to analyze a data set, create analytical reports, and display them in a functional dashboard.

Final (optional, if you have already achieved >90 points):

Percent of course grade: 20% (if taken, the best 5 tests including the final will be considered towards the final grade)

Final exam includes everything learnt during the semester.
