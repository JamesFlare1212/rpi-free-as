## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is an introductory course in business statistics. This course will provide participants with the quantitative foundations for statistical methods, which will be used extensively in future management courses. In this course, students will learn the basic concepts and tools in statistics, and then apply the techniques on phenomena of interest (Prerequisites: MATH 1500 and MATH 1520).

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate and understand the basic concepts and tools in business statistics.
- Distinguish between different statistical tests and identify the usage of different tests
- Conduct statistical tests of hypotheses and infer conclusions.
- Use software for statistical analysis and apply statistical methods in a real business environment.
- Discuss the ethical considerations of data analysis.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Attendance, Preparation, and Participation: Students are expected to attend all lectures, come prepared by having done the assigned readings, and participate during lectures.

One-minute memos: As part of the class participation grade, students will be asked to write a one-minute memo to be submitted online at the end of some of the lectures. The purpose of the one-minute memo is to establish a direct line of communication between the students and the instructor. The memo will help the instructor assess the class’ understanding of the main concepts discussed during the lectures, identify unclear points, and address any other concerns. I want students to feel that they can be open and honest in these memos, so your grade is not affected by the content of these memos. I will respond to the major questions raised in the memos during the following lecture. Missing a class or failing to submit a memo will result in an absence being recorded.  
Assignments: There will be a number of self-assessment assignments posted online to help you with the material. Each assignment will be graded based on general effort, and completion. Students are responsible for turning in completed assignments by the due date. Unless the student has made prior arrangements with the instructor, no late assignments will be accepted. Solutions will be provided immediately after the due dates.  
Quizzes: There will be several quizzes after some of the lectures. Students are responsible for completing the quizzes outside of class. These quizzes will be unannounced and will cover material from the previous lectures. The quizzes will be based on the concepts taught in the class. These quizzes are to ensure that you are not falling behind. The lowest two quiz grades will be dropped. Make-up quizzes will NOT be offered. If you miss a quiz, it will be counted as one of your lowest grades to be dropped.  
Exams: There will be two exams. Each exam will cover material discussed in class up to the exam date. The second exam will focus mainly on material covered after the first exam. Tests are individual work; students cannot collaborate in any way. Copying, communicating or using any materials during an exam is cheating unless it is an open-book exam that will be decided by the instructor.  
Final Project (Optional): Students will be divided into groups of 3-4 to work on final project. More details will be given after the first exam. The written report will include the following: 5 pages max of text and a max of 8 pages total of text, figures, tables, and references. Sections: an abstract, introduction, a short literature review, methodology, results and implications, conclusion, and list of reference. Clean and well-documented data files (codes and data)
