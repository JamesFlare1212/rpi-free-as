## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Marketing is the most visible business function to consumers and presents the framework around which all other business functions center. It is a unique blend of both science and art. This course provides students with an understanding of marketing principles and the role of the marketing discipline. The course is intended to help students learn the basic concepts and practices of marketing and to familiarize them with the terminology and techniques for properly framing and analyzing marketing problems. Issues such as global marketing, diversity, and ethics and social consequences of marketing are also discussed.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The objectives of this course are:

1. To help students gain an understanding of key marketing concepts and principles
2. To provide insight into the role of marketing within an organization
3. To provide students with the opportunity to evaluate, develop and implement marketing strategies
4. To acquaint students with the marketing research process and provide the opportunities to participate in research studies
5. To challenge students to consider ethics, diversity and global issues relevant to contemporary marketing
6. To encourage students to consider marketing as a career

7. Identify potential issues in marketing that present ethical challenges and articulate the consequences associated with unethical behavior

**GROUP PROJECT**

**What is the group project about?** The goal of this group-based project is to help students learn the marketing planning process with which the companies communicate marketing offerings (e.g., product, service, person, organization, idea, etc.) to the marketplace and gain real-world marketing experience. The project involves students developing, organizing, and managing a student-run marketing agency. The agency (group) can choose a brand or a company from an industry recommended by the instructor, then conduct a detailed industry analysis and develop a marketing program for its marketing offering.

**How to find your group members?** Each group should have about 5 members and strive for heterogeneity in composition. You will be allowed to form your own groups during the first two weeks, and I reserve the right to place latecomers into appropriate homes and assign students using different learning mode (in-class vs. remote) to different groups. Please submit your group decision on LMS before the due date listed on Course Calendar.

**How to measure effort?** Your project grades rely on your group’s performance and your individual performance. Group communication and joint effort is critical to your success. I will set up Webex team space for each group and keep monitoring your group communications there. Throughout the semester, you will also rate one another in terms of their relative contribution in group work. Students who constantly contribute less or little may get significantly lower score than their group members. As such, shirking group responsibilities is by far the surest route to oblivion.

**What are the required deliverables**? Project deliverables include status reports, midterm presentation, final report, final presentation, and any document showcasing your implementations. Each group will give a 10-15 minute midterm group presentation in the middle of the semester and a 25-minute group presentation at the end of the semester. The entire class is expected to participate and vote for the best presentation and presenters. Please see *Project Description* for more details.

**Expectation:** Each student is expected to spend about **45 hours** on the project, including the time spent on group meetings, research, report writing, and implementations of marketing plans (e.g., videos, logo design, online campaign, etc.). You are expected to present your work in a manner that is consistent with a business professional. Work should be well-organized and flow in a manner that allows the reader to understand the purpose without having to try to figure out what you are saying. Write the paper from the third person point of view (grammar). Make sure that you are diplomatic to the client and that you are recommending these actions. Proofread for spelling and grammatical errors. Use headings, subheadings, and bullets as needed. This is expected for all homework assignments as well as the project. If a section does not make sense, multiple points may be deducted. These points will be taken off the final grade.

**ASSIGNMENTS**

**Advertisement Reflection:** There is one advertisement reflection assignment during the semester. You need to individually select an advertisement, identify the target market, discuss key features, and assess the quality of the advertisement using course concepts. The advertisement should be a **video advertisement** that is viewable online and newly published (< 1 year). Please see *Ad Reflection Descriptions* for more details.

**Marketing “In Real Life”**: This practice is designed to help you professionally think about and evaluate real-life marketing examples using the concepts and terminologies we learned in class. Early in the semester, each student can pick one lecture date to give a 5-min presentation on a recent (< 1 year) marketing news relating to the marketing concepts we are discussing that day. The news can be found from newspapers, magazines, TV, radio broadcast, or social media websites. It should be an illustration or extension of the marketing topics/concepts that we are discussing that day. For example, you can discuss “how design features of the latest iPhone attract customers” on the day when we introduce Chapter 8 – Developing New Product.

In the presentation, you should have a 1-2 minute overview, explain how it relates to the topics and concepts we are discussing that day, provide your own evaluation or judgment based on data/facts, and answer any questions or comments from the class or instructor.

**You are required to present once during the semester.** There is a maximum of 2 students presenting in each class, so please make sure you pick the date early and be prepared in advance. I will share a Google file for you to pick the date of presentation early in the semester.
