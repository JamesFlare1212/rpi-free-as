## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course is an overview of the nature and scope of sport management. This course is designed to expand the students’ understanding of basic principles and issues in sport management and provide information about career opportunities in the sport management industry.

## Course Text(s)

## Course Goals

### Goals:

A student demonstrates the relevant disciplinary knowledge and competencies related to sport management industry.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

No Course Learning Outcomes are available for this course.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

1. Students will understand terminology and theory in the discipline of sport management.
2. Student will create compelling, well-supported verbal and written arguments.
3. Student will apply understanding of culture and subculture when making business decisions.
4. Student will effectively use information technology and research resources.
