### Additional Information for Course Information Section:

The chief premise of this course is the following: in order to be an effective contributor to any business, you must understand how business systems support business transactions and business analysis. Specifically, in order to influence the improvement of business processes, you must understand how business systems are designed to model these transactions and provide the information for the analysis.

This course is designed to challenge you to understand business requirements and translate them into a design which can be developed as a business system through a number of simulated business problems. In addition, the course will address development methodologies and system analysis tools which are commonly used by today’s organizations.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

1. Have a working knowledge of the steps in the system design process
2. Document a business process or transaction
3. Create a design document for a system to support business requirements
4. Understand of common systems analysis tools and methodologies, including the following:
   1. The software development lifecycle
   2. Activity diagrams
   3. Use case diagrams and use cases
   4. Data requirements and preliminary database design
   5. Designing forms, reports, and user interfaces

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

## COURSE ASSESSMENT MEASURES

The course will use the following assessments:

1. **Exercises (65% of total grade):**

A significant amount of the course will use exercises to challenge the student to think about the problem and work through various solutions. Exercises are designed so that the “learning is in the doing.”

In some cases, exercises will be done in-class, in other cases, exercises will be given as work outside of class.

A penalty will be given for late exercises (including in-class exercises). However, a student can ask for that penalty to be waived *in advance* by communicating with me by e-mail or during class.

2. **Assignments (15% of total grade):**

Assignments are graded as you would expect, with credit given for providing the right answers to problems. The assignments will be posted on LMS, with due dates specified. Please note that I \*will not\* send you any reminders about when an assignment is due. The specifics of each assignment will be provided.

Unless otherwise announced, all case write-ups or presentation slides are due to Blackboard by 10 PM the night before the assignment is discussed in class. There is a 10% penalty for late assignments. No assignments are accepted after the start of class.

3. **Final Project (Design Document) (10% of total grade):**

The final will be a complete system design problem where you will work in teams. It will be due on 12/11. More details will be provided as we progress in the semester.

4. **Class Participation (10% of your grade):**

A proactive and positive attitude towards class participation is anticipated and will be a factor in grading. I expect everyone to come to class well prepared and to participate well. A significant part of this course is learning by doing and thus an important component of class participation. I also encourage online participation (through LMS and WebEx chat, if need be). If you cannot make it to a class that involves an exercise that you should have done, please post it to the discussion board I will provide to partially make up for your absence.

Keep in mind that the current COVID related situation would necessitate our online participation even more.
