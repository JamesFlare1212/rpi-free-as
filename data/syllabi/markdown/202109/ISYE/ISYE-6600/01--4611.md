## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**Course Description for ISYE 6600: Design of Manufacturing System Supply Chains, (***Credit Hours:* *3)*

Dynamics of manufacturing systems and supply chains, lean manufacturing, lead time reduction in manufacturing and service operations, advanced pull systems, concurrent design of products and supply chains, rapid new product introduction, remanufacturing and reverse supply chains, and integration of information technology in supply chain operations. Analysis of models and their application to design and planning problems in manufacturing as well as service systems is emphasized.

**Exams:** There will be three exams. The format for the exams will be discussed before the exam. Exams are an individual effort.

## **Homework Assignments:** Throughout the semester exploratory assessment activities will be assigned to reinforce or expand on material that is covered in the lecture. Four homework sets will be assigned and these are to be worked on outside of class. Students may collaborate on the assignments, meaning students can discuss the assignments, but *every student must complete and turn in his/her own written solutions in his/her own words (unless otherwise stated in the assignment instructions)*. The homework assignments are due at the beginning of class. Homework is meant to be a learning tool. If you are having difficulty, find help right away – don’t wait until you fall even further behind!

**Activity Assignments:** These assessments, which are meant to provide opportunities for students to test their knowledge throughout the course, will take multiple forms, including case studies, in-class activities, in-class quizzes and assignments, in-class responses, online activities and online quizzes. Assignment weights will vary depending on the effort required of students.

## **Journal Articles:** Students enrolled in the ISYE 6600 section of the course are required to read and analyze a number of journal articles in the field of supply chain. The purpose of these readings is to get students familiar with the type of research conducted in this area, as well to understand why articles are published, and the variety of journals that publish supply chain research. Each student is responsible for turning in a written Reading Assignment. One type of reading assignment is a Level 3 Assignment, which is an approach to efficiently read journal articles and will be discussed further in class, along with other reading assignment formats.

**Course Project:** Each student will complete a course project that is research based. In addition, a peer-review process will be administered, allowing students to provide feedback and to respond and incorporate the feedback into their final reports. Additional guidance, as well as grading rubrics, will be provided during the course.
