## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course prepares students to take and pass the (ISC)2 Systems Security Certified Practitioner (SSCP) certification exam. It covers the seven domains that comprise the exam’s common body of knowledge: 1. Access controls; 2. Security operations and administration; 3. Risk identification, monitoring, and analysis; 4. Incident response and recovery; 5. Cryptography; 6. Network and communications security; 7. Systems and application security. Suitable for advanced students interested in pursuing a career in information security, this course is an extension of other security-related courses and is designed to impart the necessary skills to lead hands-on operational security tasks and teams. An additional research component will allow students to extend their knowledge in one domain beyond the requirements of the exam and begin to think about long-term specialization within information security.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course prepares students to enter the information security community by providing the theoretical knowledge necessary to take and pass an ANSI/ISO/IEC Standard 17024 compliant exam, the Systems Security Certified Practitioner (SSCP) exam from (ISC)2. Students will extend previous information security knowledge with insights from industry leading best practices across the domains of knowledge needed to ensure leadership in a hands-on IT security environment. As students progress through their careers, specialization is a common future endeavor. This course contains a term-length project in which students engage in further research within a knowledge domain of their choice to begin that specialization process.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will demonstrate knowledge of the theoretical foundations of industry-standard best practices for information security.
- Students will demonstrate emerging specialization in one information security knowledge domain of their choosing.
- Students will demonstrate mastery of the seven knowledge domains comprising the common knowledge base of the SSCP exam.
- Students will be able to employ their knowledge under pressure through the taking of practice SSCP exams leading to the taking of the real SSCP exam.
- Students will be able to investigate and present topics in information system security at a level so that other students obtain an understanding of the key issues.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Weekly homeworks and lab assignments. There are 10 total, each worth 3% (30%)

Practice exams. There are 2 proctored practice exams worth 15% each (30%)

Final research paper. There will be a term-length research paper in which students research specific topics with a knowledge domain of their choice, to demonstrate specialization (15%)

Presentation. Students will present their research finding in a manner that teaches these topics to other information security professionals (your peers) who may not specialize in your particular topic (15%)

Participation. Students will engage in lectures and in-class learning activities (10%)

## Other Course-Specific Information

### Additional Course Information:

Some of the techniques learned in this course can be used for malicious purposes. These techniques are taught because they are necessary for a complete understanding of information security. Using such techniques for malicious purposes may be a crime, depending on context. Using such techniques against any RPI machines, including but not limited to other students’ machines or any RPI computers, are grounds for immediate failure of the course.
