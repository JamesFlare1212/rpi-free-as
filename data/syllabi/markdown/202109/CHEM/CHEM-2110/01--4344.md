### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Learning Outcomes****:**

Upon completion of the course, the student will be able to:

1. Demonstrate a proficient knowledge of chemical reaction equilibrium.
2. Distinguish between different measures of performance in analytical measurements such as sensitivity, detectability, accuracy, and precision.
3. Explain the basic principles of and approach to performing different spectroscopic and electrochemical measurements.
4. Effectively analyze and process data with various statistical methods.

**Course Assessment Measures****:**

- Homework (35%)
- Pre-class activities (10%)
- In-class Worksheets (5%)
  - Will NOT be graded for correctness, only assessed for completeness
- Three in-class exams (30%)
- Final exam (15%)
- Class participation (5%)
