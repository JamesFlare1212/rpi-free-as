### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Student Learning Outcome** (1) Students will be able to explain electronic structure and covalent bonding in organic molecules. Students will be able to explain why certain molecules are more acidic or more basic and how their structures affect acidity strength. Students will demonstrate knowledge of nomenclature and physical properties and how to represent molecules’ structures. (Ch 1-3  
(2) Students will demonstrate knowledge of arrangement of atoms of organic molecules in three dimensions. Students will demonstrate knowledge of structures, nomenclature, and reactivity of alkenes. Students will demonstrate knowledge of strereochemical outcomes of addition reactions of alkenes. (Ch 4-6)

(3) Students will demonstrate knowledge of reactions of alkynes and how to synthesize more complex molecules by multistep synthetic routes. Students will demonstrate knowledge of delocalization of electrons and their effect on stability and reactivity. Students will demon- strate knowledge about substitution reactions of alkyl halides. Students will demonstrate knowledge about elimination reactions of alkyl halides. (Ch 7-9)

(4) Students will demonstrate knowledge of the reactions of alcohols, ethers, epoxides, amines, and thiols. Students will demonstrate knowledge of the oxidation of alcohols. Students will demonstrate knowledge of concepts and reactions of organometallic compounds. Students will demonstrate knowledge of radical reactions of alkanes. (Ch 10-12)

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Penalties for Academic Integrity Violations**

A first instance of academic dishonesty will result in a grade of 0% on the assignment, quiz, or exam during which the dishonesty took place and the incident will be reported to the Dean of Students Office. A second instance of academic dishonesty will result in automatic failure of the course without option to withdraw, and again the incident will be reported to the Dean of Students Office. Procedures for appealing an academic integrity decision can be found at http://doso.rpi.edu/update.do?artcenterkey=676.
