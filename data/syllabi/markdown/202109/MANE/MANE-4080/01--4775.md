## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Fundamental concepts from thermodynamics and fluid mechanics will be used to predict the general performance characteristics of propulsion systems used in aircraft and spacecraft. The following topics will be covered:

- 1\) Basics: a) fluids b) thermodynamics
- 2\) Compressible flow: a) stagnation properties, b) 1-D compressible flow, c) quasi 1-D compressible flow with area change, d) Rayleigh flow, e) oblique shock waves, f) Prandtl-Meyer expansions
- 3\) Rocket propulsion: a) performance/requirements, b) multistage rockets, c) solids and hybrids
- 4\) Air-breathing propulsion systems: a) ramjets, b) turbojets, c) afterburning, d) turbofans, e) turboprops, f) cycle analysis, g) non-ideal component and cycle analysis
