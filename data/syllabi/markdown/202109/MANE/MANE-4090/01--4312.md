## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Performance, stability, and control of airplanes. General equations of motion for rigid aircrafts, aerodynamic forces and moments, quasi-steady and non-steady flight paths. Generalized performance methods and flight envelopes. Small disturbance theory, stability derivatives, longitudinal and lateral static stability. Basic airplane motion, response to control actions and to atmosphere disturbances. Simulation of aircraft performance and dynamics.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The objective is to illustrate how the basic concepts of fluid mechanics, dynamics and mathematics can be applied to determine the motion, performance, stability, and control of flying vehicles.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

**Topics:**

1. **Introduction to vehicle dynamics and control:** The concepts of equilibrium, static stability, dynamic stability, and control. (Week 1, **MC:** Chapter 1 and **N:** Chapter 1).
2. **Lift:** Wing geometry; Airfoil’s theory; Stall; Lifting-line model; Lifting-surface model; Maximum lift; Delta Wings and vortex breakdown (Week 2, **MC:** Chapter 3).
3. **Drag:** Skin drag; Form drag; Induced drag; Interference drag; Total Drag (Week 3, **MC:** Chapter 4).
4. **Lift and drag at High Mach numbers.** Transonic airfoils; Supersonic airfoils; Supersonic wings. (Week 4, **MC**: Chapter 5).

5. **Longitudinal static stability and its control:** Contribution of aircraft components, stick

fixed and control by elevator/stick forces. (Weeks 5, 6, **N:** Chapter 2).

6. **Lateral static stability and its control:** Directional Stability, roll Stability control by a rudder and ailerons. (Weeks 7, 8, **N:** Chapter 2).
7. **Aircraft performance:** Takeoff; Rate of climb, Time to climb, and ceiling, Range; Endurance, turning maneuvers (Weeks 9, 10, **MC**: Chapter 7).
8. **Equations of motion:** Inertial frame and body-fixed frame, small disturbance theory, aircraft longitudinal and lateral transfer functions, stability derivatives. (Week 11, **N:** Chapter 3).

9. **Longitudinal motion:** Pure pitching, Phugoid and Short-period motions and its approximations, flying qualities. (Weeks 12, 13, **N**: Chapter 4).
10. **Lateral motion:** Inertial coupling, pure roiling and yawing motions, approximations of the spiral mode, the roll mode and the dutch-roll oscillations flying qualities, (Week 14, **N**: Chapter 5).
