## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course covers the basic concepts, techniques, and tools related to optimal control for dynamical systems. Major topics include calculus of variation, the maximum principle, the Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman Equation, and Linear Quadratic Regulator problems. This course will also include topics from reinforcement learning.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

- Static Optimization: Constrained and Unconstrained
- Discrete-time Optimization
- Riccati Equations
- Calculus of Variation
- Pontryagin’s Maximum Principle
- Dynamic Programming
- Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman Equation
- Reinforcement Learning
- Differential Games and Dynamic Games (subject to time availability)

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students are able to formulate first order optimality condition for calculus of variations and optimal control problems.
- Students are able to find optimal control by solving for two-point boundary value problem.
- Students are able to find optimal control for linear time invariant systems by solving the corresponding Riccati equations.
