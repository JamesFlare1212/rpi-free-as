## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Applied Mechanics of Solids, by Allan F. Bower, CRC Press. Available at http://solidmechanics.org/

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate an ability to perform vector and tensor analysis using the basic mathematical tools and principles of continuum mechanics.
- Demonstrate an ability to mathematically represent and analyze the deformation of materials.
- Demonstrate an ability to perform stress analysis in linear elastic solids.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Homework and 3 exams.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these.
