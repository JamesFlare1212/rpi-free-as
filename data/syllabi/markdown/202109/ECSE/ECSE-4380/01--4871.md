## Course Goals

### Goals:

Students will be taught the basics of LED design and fabrication, LED packaging and thermal management techniques, LED drivers, basics of lighting measurement techniques and lighting control systems, color science, and applications of LEDs in displays, lighting, horticulture and UV germicidal systems

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understand the basic principles of LED system design, including III/V materials, LED datasheet understanding, packaging and the role of phosphors in making white LEDs
- The student will understand the basics of an LED module, including electronic drivers and thermal management systems needed for solid state lighting systems
- The student will understand fundamental optical systems and color science related to the operation of LED based lighting systems

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Student learning outcomes will be assessed by quizes given at regular intervals (4 during the semester) an a final exam.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Quiz grades will represent 60% of the course final grade, and the final exam will represent the remaining 40%. All tests will be "open note"
