## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**Course Description:** Techniques and applications of integration, polar coordinates, parametric equations, infinite sequences and series, vector functions and curves in space, functions of several variables, and partial derivatives. Prerequisite: MATH 1010 (from the [Rensselaer Catalog](http://catalog.rpi.edu/))

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Basic symbol manipulation skills pertaining to integration.
- Ability to state and explain basic Calculus definitions and theorems and their applications.
- Ability to describe curves parametrically and apply Calculus concepts and techniques to curves described in this way.
- Ability to express curves via polar equations and to solve Calculus problems in the polar coordinate system.
- Ability to apply Calculus to selected problems in Science, Engineering and Mathematics.
- Ability to use convergence tests to analyze the behavior of infinite series.
- Ability to represent functions using power series and solve problems using these representations.
- Ability to describe lines and planes in space in equation form and solve problems using these representations.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Exams:** Exams will be given in person when the Institute allows it during the test block on Wednesdays beginning at 8:00 PM. Details about each exam, including the material to be covered on the exam, room assignments, and time limit, will be announced on LMS when the date of the exam is near.

The dates for Exams 1–3 are listed above in section Grading. The date of Exam 4 will be announced at a later time.  
The make-up exam policy above applies to Exams 1–3. The policy will be adjusted for Exam 4 once the date of Exam 4 has been decided.  
**Missing an exam should not be taken lightly.** Thus, students who miss an exam must make every effort to notify the four instructors **as soon as possible** to avoid grade penalties.

- If a student misses an exam and fails to notify the instructors before noon on the Friday after the exam, then the student may not take the make-up exam and receives a score of 0 on the exam, regardless of whether the absence was excused.

- If a student misses an exam and notifies the instructors before noon on the Friday after the exam, then the student may take a make-up exam on the Wednesday following the exam at 8:00 PM and a 30% penalty will be applied to the make-up exam score. The 30% penalty will be removed if both of the following conditions are satisfied:

  1\. the student notifies the instructors before the exam begins, and  
  2\. an [excused absence notice from the Student Success office](https://success.studentlife.rpi.edu/student-success/excused-absence) is received by the instructor before noon on the Friday after the exam or, if the absence is due to an athletic competition, documentation from a coach is received by the instructor before noon on the Friday after the exam.

**Homework:** Achieve homework assignments will be due once or twice weekly on Mondays and Thursdays at 11:59 PM and will be graded automatically by the system. Students may submit these assignments late up to three days after the original deadline, but a penalty will be applied to the score. A penalty of 10% per day will apply to assignments submitted after the deadline. No other due date extensions will be granted for any reason. To further compensate for this, several homework assignment scores will be dropped at the end of the semester.  
Additional problems will be assigned from the textbook and will be posted on LMS. Though these problems will not be collected nor graded, they should not be viewed as optional. Success in the course depends on successful completion of all homework, both Achieve homework and textbook homework. Exam problems are frequently chosen from homework assignments and lecture examples. Full solutions to odd-numbered textbook problems can be found on Achieve. When logged in to our Achieve course, view content by assignments and scroll to the bottom of the page. The student solutions manual for the chapters covered in the course may be found below No Due Dates.  
**Quizzes:** A short quiz will be given weekly at the end of one of the lectures, starting on the second week of classes, except during the weeks we have an exam. Dates and topics covered on each quiz will be announced in advance.  
**Recitations:** A Teaching Assistant (TA) will conduct weekly recitations. During these 50-minute sessions, the TA will solve problems and answer questions about the material, including homework problems. At the end of most recitations you will have to submit a problem to be graded.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

**Attendance:** Students are responsible for all material covered in lectures and recitations whether they are present or not. Quizzes will take place in lectures, and work will be collected during recitations.

### Other Course Policies:

**Disability Services for Students (DSS):** Students with a memorandum from the [Disability Services for Students (DSS)](http://studenthealth.rpi.edu/disabilityservices) office identifying accommodations for exams must present the memorandum to the instructors by Friday, September 10. Providing the accommodations requires advance planning. Students will not be granted accommodations unless a memorandum from DSS has been submitted to the instructors.  
**Remote Students:** Students who have been approved by RPI to attend classes remotely must notify the instructors as soon as possible so alternate arrangements can be made.

**Grade Appeals:** If upon reviewing the answer key and solutions for an exam or quiz a student feels that their work was graded incorrectly, then the student may submit a grading appeal to the instructor. Students have one week after the scores of exams or quizzes are released to submit an appeal.

**Calculator Policy:** Calculators will not be allowed on exams. Problems will be designed so that this does not create an issue. Occasionally, a calculator may be required to complete a homework problem, but students should not rely on calculators for simple arithmetic or trigonometric calculations.

**Health and Safety Protocols:** Students are required to follow the health and safety protocols of RPI, which are subject to change throughout the semester. Students who are not cleared for campus access are prohibited from attending class. While masks are required in classrooms, eating and drinking in class is prohibited. Failure to comply with health and safety protocols will be met with a request to comply, a request to leave the class, or a dismissal of the entire class and referral of the non-compliant student to the Dean of Students.

Please note that the instructor and TA will practice social distancing whenever possible and thus will not be available to speak with students in the classroom at the beginning or end of class.

**Visitors:** Visitors are prohibited during all class meetings. Only students who are registered for the class should be present in the classroom. Students may only attend class meetings at the time and place listed on their schedule on SIS.

**Classroom Decorum:** Students must be on time for class and should refrain from leaving and reentering the classroom during class. If a student has a legitimate reason for arriving late to class or being excused early from class, then they should discuss this with the instructor before class. Phones may not be used during class and should be silent. Computers and tablets may not be used for anything other than taking notes. Students who are unable to courteously comply with these policies may be asked to leave the room.

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. ### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Academic Integrity:** Students should familiarize themselves with the section on academic integrity in the [Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities](https://rpi.app.box.com/s/d4ndpn3f7qdwxh9jaz5n1hkkr0mnd25e) (pp. 16–18). All work submitted on exams must be the student’s own. No calculators, computers, tablets, open books, open notes, phones, headphones, or smartwatches may be used during exams. Talking or communicating with other students in any way during exams is also prohibited. Students may discuss homework with other students, TA, and the instructor, but the work submitted must be the student’s own.

The minimum academic sanction applied to a student found in violation of the above policies will be a grade of 0 on the assignment. A stronger sanction may be applied if warranted. A report regarding the violation will also be submitted to the Dean of Students Office and will remain on file until graduation. Repeat offenders will fail the course and will be subject to judicial action.
