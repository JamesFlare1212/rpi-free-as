## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

DCIII will focus on polygonal modeling, advanced nurbs modeling, and representation through a variety of techniques. These  
objectives will be addressed through a semester-long design project that is divided into three sections. First, students will  
study polygonal modeling and develop sound technique, initially by producing digital models of existing objects and then by  
combining and altering the objects. During the class’ second section, we will look at advanced nurbs modeling by adding detail  
to the projects coming out of maya. Additions will include seaming, interiority, and material divisions, with special interest paid to  
curvature modeling. In the final section of the course we will focus on representational techniques. This section will give students  
the ability to produce standard representations as well as develop their own aesthetic and begin to become comfortable working  
with a personal style.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Develop a high proficiency of polygon modeling strategies and techniques, and exploit their advantages to leverage the strengths of polygon modeling
- 2\. Produce renderings of polygon based projects, including materiality and lighting that reinforce key project concepts
- 3\. Become proficient in creating digital models suitable for 3d printing and approved by instructor - models must be watertight and have no non-manifold edges
- 4\. Develop a high proficiency with nurbs modeling, including curvature modeling
- 5\. Apply nurbs modeling techniques to existing models, adding refinement while maintaining a well-made digital model
- 6\. Demonstrate an ability to produce representations of digital design work through a variety of media
- 7\. Demonstrate an ability to produce and present multiple presentation drawings in a coherent way on a single board

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

\- students will receive assessments of their weekly assignments, either from an instructor or in collaboration with other students. Group critique between students will help encourage a self-critical approach to design.  
\- projects will be assessed by both the instructor and outside experts that will offer unique perspective from their own biases and points of view. These reviews will be analogous to a studio midreview and final review.
