## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Drawing is the language of architecture – architects generate and communicate ideas through drawing. The primary goal of this course, as the first semester of the four-semester Digital Constructs sequence, is to introduce fundamentals of architectural drawing. Skills acquired in this course will be built on, and added to, in subsequent semesters. At the conclusion of the Digital Constructs sequence, students will be able to generate and communicate sophisticated architectural concepts through drawing and will be able to use drawing creatively and effectively in design and technical courses across the curriculum.

As drawing in contemporary architectural practice is done primarily with digital tools, this course will focus on gaining proficiency in two and three-dimensional digital environments. Students will acquire expertise in Rhinoceros, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop, and VRay.

Class meetings will incorporate critique, tutorials and working sessions. The semester is organized into three distinct phases that will build on each other:

- \- **Phase 1** – Two-dimensional drawing with Rhino and Illustrator.
- \- **Phase 2** – Three-dimensional modeling with Rhino and graphics in Illustrator.
- \- **Phase 3** – Visualization with Rhino VRay, Illustrator, and Photoshop.

The first two phases will consist of weekly exercises focusing on a range of technical drawing techniques in the digital environment. The third phase will consist of a more complex, longer-term project that uses skills learned in the first half of the semester to produce more speculative drawings. Handouts outlining specific techniques and deliverables for each week will supplement the course syllabus.

The final deliverables for the course will be a series of beautiful, complex, and rigorously produced drawings that demonstrate the student’s mastery of all drawing tools and techniques introduced during the semester. These drawings must stand alone as a creatively authored work.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- \- Demonstrate the ability to use drawing as a communicative tool within architecture
- \- Develop projective drawings in two and three dimensions
- \- Differentiate between digital and real environments, leverage the advantages of both in order to communicate effectively, and work between both environments in an efficient manner.
- \- Become proficient and disciplined working in a digital modeling environment.
- \- Develop a sound methodological basis for the creation of drawings, digital models, and renderings of digital models.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

- \- Students will receive, on a weekly basis, critical feedback and direction in the form of collective or individual critiques. Students are expected to respond to feedback by proactively reworking various aspects of their projects.
- \- Students will receive the opportunity to present ideas to various external experts, who will provide critical feedback to students.
- \- Students will be introduced to comparative theories and concepts related to the content of the course.
- \- Students will receive two major reviews at the midterm and end of the term. For both reviews, students will receive feedback on their progress and accomplishments.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Grades will be based upon the quality of drawings and models produced as well as attendance and participation in class.

- \- Mid Review: 30%
- \- Final Project: 40%
- \- Attendance and Participation: 30% (This course will be taught entirely online; attendance will be recorded by attending scheduled WebEx meetings AND by handing in weekly assignments on time).

Mid-semester evaluations for the course will be provided to each student.

Grades will be established according to the following policy:

- \- A (EXCELLENT) Exceptional performance strongly exceeding the requirements of the course, showing strong academic initiative and independent resourcefulness.
- \- B (GOOD) Performance above the norm; accurate and complete; beyond the minimum requirements of the course; work demonstrates marked progress and initiative.
- \- C (AVERAGE) Satisfactory work that adequately meets minimum requirements and demonstrates satisfactory comprehension, communication skills, and effort; demonstrates little initiative to investigate the problem without substantial input from the instructor; work shows little improvement.
- \- D (INFERIOR) Unsatisfactorily meets minimum requirements; demonstrates minimum comprehension, communication skills, and effort at an inferior level; initiative lacking; improvement not noticeable.
  - \- F (FAIL) Does not meet minimum requirements; fails to adequately demonstrate comprehension, communication skills, and effort.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Attendance is required at all class meetings during the semester. Two (2) unexcused absences from class will result in the lowering of the grade by one (1) letter. Four (4) unexcused absences from class during the term result in an automatic F. Whether an absence (health, immediate family death, officially approved field / sport trip, etc.) is excused or unexcused is determined by the Students Experience Office (4th Floor Academy Hall, x8022, se@rpi.edu), upon presentation of required documentation, according to established policies and procedures (see http:// http://se.rpi.edu/policies/ea/).

Students are required to work in class during class hours and have the required materials and basic tools to develop the assignments, including a working computer running the required software. Mac users MUST have Bootcamp, Windows, and the PC version of Rhino installed. Failure to work in class with undivided attention and disruptive behavior (tardiness, leaving early, lack of participation, general socializing, etc.), as well working on assignments from other classes during class, will count as absences. Cell phone use during class is prohibited, students using cell phones during class will be asked to leave them at the front of the room for the duration of the class.
