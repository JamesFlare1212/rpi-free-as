## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

In the Chamber Music Ensemble, students will rehearse and perform music in small groups. Each student will have a primary group assigned that matches their musical experience and background, but the ensemble will also maintain a flexible format throughout the semester in order to allow spontaneous collaborations and projects to emerge out of musical interaction. In addition to regular coachings provided by the primary instructor, guest instructors and performers will be brought in when possible to provide the a variety of perspectives.

**Course Materials**

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will develop their skills for playing music in a variety of styles and ensemble configurations.
- Students will gain hands-on musical experience through rehearsals, coachings, and performances.
- Students will expand their technical and collaborative prowess as musicians, both individually and as participants in a group.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Rehearsal Participation  
Performance Participation/Professionalism  
Preparation

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Rehearsal Participation [40%]  
Performance Participation/Professionalism [30%]  
Preparation [30%]

**Rehearsal Participation:** The most important part of membership in this ensemble is focused, active participation in scheduled rehearsals. As the collective thriving of the group is the ultimate goal, students are encouraged to contribute their energies and talents in pursuit of that goal.

**Performance Participation/Professionalism**: The work throughout the term will culminate in one or more public performances. Because everyone in a chamber ensemble depends on everyone else, it is vital for participants to be absolutely professional and responsible.

**Preparation**: In addition to the time spent in weekly rehearsals, musicians will be expected to spend time outside of class learning and practicing their parts.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Attendance is mandatory. A student is permitted to miss two classes over the course of the semester without penalty. Beyond this, any unexcused absence will result in lowering the overall grade by 1/3 of a letter grade (A to A-, etc.). If a student misses a class, they are responsible for catching up on the material covered. Two latenesses are treated as equivalent to an absence. Contact your classmates or schedule an appointment with your instructor to make sure you are up-to-date. Excused absences must be documented by an official form processed through the RPI Student Experience office (http://studentlife.rpi.edu/student-success/excused-absence). Whenever possible, please inform the instructor of a planned absence by email at least a week beforehand.  
Attendance at scheduled performances is absolutely necessary. Failure to attend a performance could result in a failing grade in the course.

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own.
