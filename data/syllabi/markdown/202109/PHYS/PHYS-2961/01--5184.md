## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is an undergraduate course designed to give an introductory overview of the field of quantum information science. Students should learn the basic concepts of quantum computation and information, including entanglement and quantum communication, quantum circuit models, quantum algorithms/simulation, as well as gain an understanding of physical implementations of qubits and practical concerns (e.g, decoherence and error-correction).

## Course Text(s)

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. ### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Working together on homework and activities is encouraged (asking each other questions on approach, looking for problems in each others work, etc), however, each student is expected do their own work. Identical documents submitted for multiple students are not allowed, and the first offense will result in a zero for the assignment. Work on quizzes and exams are expected to be done entirely on your own and the use of any disallowed external material or the participation in online collaboration on a quiz or test will result in an F for the course.
