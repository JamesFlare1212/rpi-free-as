## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is the laboratory component of BIOL-2120. Here, you will gain practical experience with the concepts and techniques covered in the BIOL-2120 lecture. The lab and lecture courses are linked; the curriculum covered in lab and lecture is intended to give you an integrated experience. This is an introductory course in cell structure and function. It is intended for Biology and Biomedical Engineering majors, and covers such topics as chemical composition of cells, molecular structure function of organelles, membrane transport, and the basic biochemistry underlying cellular behavior including cancer cells.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

None – laboratory manuals will be proved as PDFs on BIOL-2125 Lab LMS. A physical lab notebook will **not** be needed, but you **MUST bring your laptop to lab** to record that day's work in your digital notebook.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

1. The student will learn to successfully perform common techniques currently used in prokaryotic and eukaryotic cell biology research.
2. The student will generate, analyze, and interpret data from experimental techniques.
3. The student will demonstrate proper documentation of laboratory work by keeping a laboratory notebook.
4. The student will demonstrate effective communication through the oral presentation of their work.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Microscopy I: Prokaryotes, Microscopy II: Eukaryotes, Pipetting and Standard Curves, Enzyme Kinetics, SDS-PAGE, Bacterial Transformation, Western Blot Part I, Western Blot Part II, PCR and Motility Assay, and Fluorescence Microscopy.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Pre-lab Questions**

- ­ Pre-lab questions can be found on BIOL-2125 Lab LMS. These questions will also appear in your lab manual, but they **must be submitted to LMS for a grade**. These assignments must be submitted **before you meet for that week's lab**.

**Quizzes**

- ­ There will be 3 lab quizzes held during the semester. All of these will be held on BIOL-2125 Lab LMS on predetermined dates and will be open for a period of time that should accommodate all students. These quizzes will not be held in person. Each quiz is timed**. Group work is NOT allowed** on quizzes, and plagiarism will result in a **0 on the first offence** and an **F for the course** on the second offence.

**Notebook Checks**

- ­ All students will keep a lab notebook. Your lab notebook will be proved to you as a Google Docs file that you can edit; physical notebooks will not be used. You **MUST bring your laptop to all in-person labs** for the purposes of recording that day's work. Notebook checks will be conducted on the dates listed in the *Lab Seclude*; on these dates you are expected to export your Google Docs notebook as a PDF file and submit it to BIOL-2125 Lab LMS **before the start of lab**. More detailed instructions are given on LMS.

**Student Presentation**

- ­ Students will work as pairs during the semester; you will give a presentation at the end of the semester with your lab partner. The presentation will be based on the data that you collected during one of our lab sessions. A PDF version of your presentation slides must be submitted on LMS, and you will give your presentation in lab or on WebEx depending on COVID-19 restrictions at the end of the semester.

**Lab Citizenship**

- ­ Good lab citizenship includes being on time, not eating or drinking in lab, proper sharing of instruments, maintaining a clean work space, following instructions, and social distancing. Violation of these rules or any rule listed under the *COVID-19 Precautions & Restrictions* and *Lab Safety* sections will result in loss of points.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

- Attendance is **REQUIRED** for all labs. **Unexcused absences are NOT allowed** - missing a lab will result in a deduction of **10 %** from your lab grade.
- If you miss a lab, you must obtain a Dean’s excuse. Students who need to report an illness should contact the Student Health Center via email or call 518-276-6287. Then, you must **submit this documentation to BIOL-2125 Lab LMS under the "Excused Absences" section**. For example, if you receive a Dean's excuse or doctor’s note via email, "print" this email as a PDF and submit the PDF to LMS; if you receive physical documentation, you must scan this document and then submit the scanned image to LMS under the "Excused Absences" section. **Your Lab TA tracks attendance, and this is the only way in which your lab TA will know about the excused absence.**
- For any excused absence from lab, your lab work must still be made up. If you foresee yourself missing a lab, contact your lab TA and instructor well ahead of time. For any absence, it is your responsibility to coordinate a make-up time. Failure to make up lab work will result in the same penalty as missing the lab. When a make-up lab is not feasible (e.g., when quarantining), an online make-up lab will be offered.

### Other Course Policies:

**COVID-19 PRECAUTIONS & RESTRICTIONS**

- **Masks**
  - ­ **Masks are to be worn by all students and faculty in all classrooms, labs, and any other institute buildings**. Masks must be worn properly, covering both the mouth and nose. Students who violate this policy by not wearing masks correctly will be reported to the Dean of Students and will be requested to leave the lab and return to their living quarters. The Dean of Students will provide the appropriate sanctions for the students per the code of conduct signed by the students.
- **Cleaning of Spaces**
  - ­ Students are required to disinfect their work surfaces in the lab before they begin work and after they have finished for the day. 70% ethanol will be provided for disinfection. Disinfecting lab chairs and stools is also encouraged.
- **Social Distancing**
  - ­ Students and faculty will respect the need for social distancing to the degree possible by the setting.
- **Student Health**
  - ­ Students who are ill, under quarantine for COVID-19, or suspect they are ill must report this to Student Life. Once faculty notification is made, your instructors will make every reasonable effort to accommodate the student’s absence and will communicate that accommodation directly to the student. However, to maintain accurate attendance tracking, **your TA must be informed through BIOL-2125 lab LMS** (see *Attendance Policies*).
- Refusal to comply with any appropriate request to limit the spread of COVID-19 will be treated as a classroom disruption and disciplinary actions and sanctions will be taken through the judicial process in the Student Handbook (request to change the behavior; request to leave the lab; dismissal of the lab and referral to Student Life and the Dean of Students.)

**LAB SAFETY**

- Follow all rules that your lab TA and instructor issue.
- Do not engage in any activities that will endanger yourself or those around you.
- **Wear gloves** when interacting with any laboratory equipment and **change gloves often**. You will be provided gloves.
- **You must wear safety glasses or goggles** at all times during lab except when using microscopes - disinfect eye pieces of microscopes with ethanol before and after use.
- Lab coats are optional but encouraged; lab coats are not provided. Wear clothing that entirely covers the legs and torso.
- You must wear shoes that **enclose the entire foot**; footwear like sandals and crocs are not allowed.
- Report any spills or accidents to your TA or instructor immediately.
- When finished, you must clean your work area and return it to the condition you found it in; **disinfect your work areas with 70% ethanol**.
- TAs, instructors, and other students will use 70% ethanol to disinfect working areas and equipment before, during, and after lab exercises.
- Social distancing will be observed in accordance with RPI policies.
- Follow traffic flow guides in lab to help maintain social distancing; do not crowd other students while they are working at a shared location or on shared equipment.
- Always **wear a mask correctly** while in lab or any institute building.
- **Use of cell phones is not allowed in lab**. Laptops are required; however, unlike laptops, cell phones are placed on or near the face, creating a serious COVID-19 transmission risk and increases the risk of contamination by other hazardous reagents used in the lab.
- Book bags, backpacks, and other personal items must be stored under your lab bench, away from other student's belongings. Be sure to avoid making your personal belongings a tripping hazard.
- Violation of any rules stated here, in your lab manual, or verbally by your lab TA or instructor will impact your **Lab Citizenship grade**, and major violations can have you removed from the lab.
