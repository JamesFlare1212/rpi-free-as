## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course is an introduction to the role of physiological mechanisms in behavioral processes. There will be detailed examination and discussion of the involvement of biological systems in feeding and drinking, sexual behavior, sleep and arousal, learning and memory, stress/emotions, psychopathology and psychopharmacology.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students who successfully complete this course will be able to: 1. describe the relationship between the mind and the brain.
- Students who successfully complete this course will be able to: 2. describe the basic functions of nerve cells, that neurons communicate by electrical and chemical means, and general psychopharmacology principles
- Students who successfully complete this course will be able to: 3. explain how the mammalian nervous system develops and changes throughout the lifespan
- Students who successfully complete this course will be able to: 4. identify brain targets and mechanisms for sensation/perception, movement, and other complex behaviors (ingestion, reproduction, memory, emotion)
- Students who successfully complete this course will be able to: 5. describe the current understanding of neurobiological underpinnings of brain health and well-being as well as neurodevelopmental, neurological, and neuropsychiatric disorders.
- Students who successfully complete this course will be able to: 6. apply their understanding of Behavioral Neuroscience to analyze and interpret data collected in class or described in scholarly literature, and communicate their perspectives.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Participation and attendance (15%):** Attendance and active engagement in class is mandatory. Students will receive an average grade for attendance and participation. Participation includes working in groups or alone on activities during class time and contributing to class discussions.

**Graded assignment (15%):** Students will complete one short paper this semester: “Methods assignment.” Papers should be 2-3 pages in length, double-spaced, 12-point font.

**Journal Article Critiques (10%):** We will read 4 journal article readings throughout the semester; articles will be chosen by students by a vote on professor-provided options. Students are expected to read and write critiques of 4 primary literature articles related to the topics discussed in class. Each critique is due on the day that the article will be discussed by 11:59 pm (see schedule). For class sessions indicated, students must write a critique or reflection on the journal article listed. These should be brief (1 page, single-spaced), explicitly include student’s thoughts about the work, and submitted on LMS.

**Group project (20%):** Students will work in groups to analyze data and present their findings at the end of the semester. For this presentation, student groups will be given data on emotional or cognitive measures of human subjects to analyze and interpret and present to class.

**\*\* Any assignment that is worked on outside of class needs to be submitted via the LMS link by 11:59 pm EST on its due date to be considered on time. See lateness policy below for more details.\*\***

**Quizzes (10%):** Quizzes will be given most weeks on the dates indicated in schedule (most Mondays) and will be available to take on LMS for the entire day. Quizzes will be multiple-choice, true/false or fill-in-the-blank questions. Students will be expected to answer questions associated with assigned readings and the lecture materials from the previous lecture(s). No make-up quizzes will be permitted. I will drop the lowest quiz grade for each student when calculating the average quiz grade.

**Exams 1 and 2 (30%; 15% each):** There will be two exams during the semester consisting of multiple-choice, short answer, and true or false questions. Questions will be related to materials (e.g. lectures, readings, exercises) covered in the class up until when the exam is given. Exams will be on LMS.
