### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Analyze and evaluate uses of music across different audio-visual genres and formats using diverse perspectives from popular music, world music, and western classical music studies.

Interpret and apply theories of music and audiovisual media to individual examples from diverse genres (e.g. documentary film, music video, video games, video memes, television, film, VR).

Apply these theories, terms and techniques to their personal engagement with music/sound and screen media, whether via interactive play or non-interactive viewing and listening.

Acquire auditory skills to differentiate between and evaluate the breadth of music and media traditions experienced in the contemporary world.

Academic Integrity

Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration. Submission of any assignment that is in violation of this policy will result in a penalty. In the case of major acts of plagiarism (e.g., text copied verbatim from an unidentified source) the student will receive a grade of F for the course. In the case of minor acts of plagiarism (e.g., referencing the findings of others without appropriate citations) the student will fail the assignment and receive a reduction of final course grade by one letter grade. If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. The use of ChatGPT or any other similar program is prohibited from being used on any assignment for this course.
