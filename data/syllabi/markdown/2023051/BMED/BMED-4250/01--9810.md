## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Quantitative analysis of transport phenomena in physiological systems, including fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, and mass transfer. Fluid statics, mass and energy conservation, laminar and turbulent flow, microscale and macroscale analytical methods, mass transport with biochemical reactions, applications to transport in tissue and organs.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Basic Transport Phenomena in Biomedical Engineering, 4th Ed. Ronald L. Fournier.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Lectures 01-06: Introduction to thermodynamics, including the first and second law and various thermodynamic properties.  
Lectures 07-12: Physical properties of body fluids and the cell membrane. Energy and mass conservation. The Bernoulli equation. The physical and flow properties of blood and other fluids.  
Lectures 13-24: Mass transfer fundamentals in homogenous systems and mass transfer fundamental in heterogeneous systems.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Develop an understanding of the fundamentals of thermodynamics and mass transport in biomedical systems.
- Learn to simplify realistic systems by applying appropriate assumptions.
- Apply course material in the solution of short, well-defined problems.
- Apply computational tools in the analysis of various biomedical systems.
