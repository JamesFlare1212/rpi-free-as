## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

In physics we encounter group theory in many areas. May it be in the symmetries of space-time, quantum mechanics, the Standard Model, crystalline structures, general relativity, or grand unifying theories. This course will introduce group theory to the student with application to the aforementioned topics. This course is not focused on be mathematical rigor, but will rely more on physical intuition and will be aimed at practicality and concreteness.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Required Texts: “Group Theory in a Nutshell for Physicists”, A. Zee, ISBN: 9780691162690

## Course Goals

### Goals:

By the end of the course, and upon satisfactory completion of all HW assignments, class activi- ties and exams, the students will have an elementary understanding of Group Theory and, more importantly, its applications in physics.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understand what a group is.
- To be able to identify irreducible representations of groups.
- Calculate character tables.
- Understand the significance of special orthogonal groups in physics.
- Understand the significance of special unitary groups in physics.
- Identify how SO(N) and SU(N) are related.
- Understand Lie groups and their algebras.
- Understand the connection between particle physics and group theory
- Understand the invariance group of all physical laws, the Poincar ́e group.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Learning will be assessed using a combination of homework, reading assignments, and exams, as described elsewhere in this syllabus.
