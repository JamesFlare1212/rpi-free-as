## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Appreciation and understanding of uncertainties and the conditions under which they occur, within the context of the engineering problem-solving pedagogy of measurements, models, validation, and analysis. Problems and concerns in obtaining measurements; tabular and graphical organization of data to minimize misinformation and maximize information; and development and evaluation of models. Concepts will be supported with computer demonstration. Applications to problems in engineering are emphasized.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course teaches students

- An appreciation for uncertainty in the application of engineering models;
- Discrete and continuous distribution functions;
- Marginal and conditional distributions with multiple variables;
- Descriptive statistics, difference between probability and statistics;
- Sample distributions and point estimates;
- Central Limit Theorem;
- Confidence intervals and Hypothesis testing.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Students will be able to:

- Understand the concept of probability and solve word problems related to probabilities;
- Obtain and graph information about random variables and variable sets;
- Understand and apply various discrete and continuous distribution functions;
- Formulate hypotheses tests for one-sample and two-samples, and conduct the tests using critical region, p-value, and confidence interval method.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

## Grading Criteria

- **Tests and final exam (80%):** There are three 1.5-hour long tests and a 2-hour long final exam. All tests are cumulative but with emphasis on the material since the last test. The lowest test grade can be replaced by the final exam grade if you have at least two tests taken before the final exam. The three tests count for 15%, 20%, and 20% respectively. The final exam includes the entire semester and counts for 25%. They are all close-book and in-person. Relevant formulas and statistical tables will be provided.
- **Projects (10%):** There are two projects based on R, each counting for 5% of overall grade. Late submissions receive a reduction of 25% per day from points earned.
- **Quizzes (10%):** There are quizzes taken remotely via LINUS LMS (https://mau.linuslearning.net/) on Thursday evenings. If you prefer taking the quizzes in person, they will be during the last 30 minutes of lecture hours (12 – 12:30 pm) on Thursdays. The lowest quiz grade is dropped.
