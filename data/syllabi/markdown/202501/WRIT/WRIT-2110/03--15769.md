## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Analyze academic texts in order to cultivate an ability to recognize and adapt to the features of different genres and purposes in writing
- Understand and use written and spoken academic structures and conventions to produce texts for global academic audiences
- Engage in the processes of academic writing and presentations including reading, outlining, drafting, peer review and editing
- Find information from appropriate sources, both primary and secondary, understand these sources and integrate them into writing and original ideas through the use of summary, paraphrase, quotation, and reflection
- Practice academic citation conventions and referencing

1. Diagnostic Essay Revision- 5%

During the first week of class students will be asked to complete a 5 paragraph essay within the class period on the topic of an article that will be read together in class. The purpose of this essay is for the instructor to determine the students’ incoming skill level. During the first part of the class, students will review some of the basic structures and rules for writing an academic essay and edit their original essay to comply with these rules.

2. Short Essays- 10%

Two short essays will be assigned as response and reflection papers on the readings in class.

3. Paper Proposal- 10%

Students will write a short proposal for their academic research paper.

4. Paper Proposal Presentation- 5%

Students will give a very short presentation of their paper proposal. Through the paper proposal presentation, students will share their ideas with their classmates and have a chance to practice presenting in front of the class before their final presentation.

5. Research Paper Preparation - 10%

Students will create be given assignments in preparation for the research paper such as topic selection, outlining, and an annotated bibliography.

6. Academic Research Paper- 30%

Students will write an academic sourced research paper on a topic approved by the instructor. The students will write 3 drafts of the paper. The first draft will be graded for completion as a homework assignment and will be used for peer review in class. The second and third draft will both be graded and will both contribute to the final score of the paper.

7. Presentation-10%

Students will give an academic presentation of their final research paper.

8. Homework- 10%

Frequent homework and in-class assignments will be given throughout the semester. Most homework assignments will be worth 10 points. Students may miss 30 points on homework and still receive a full score, or students may complete all assignments and receive 30 points of extra credit in the homework category.

9. Participation- 10%

Students will be expected to attend and actively participate in class throughout the semester. Students may lose points in this category if they do not attend class or attend class but are unengaged and do not participate.

Rules for Assignments

LATE POLICY: Late submission of assignments will be penalized 10% for each day after the due date (i.e., 1-24 hours late is a 10% deduction; 25-48 hours late is a 20% deduction). An absence is not an excuse for not completing homework. However, PLEASE always email me BEFORE you know you will be absent or if you cannot complete your work by the deadline. In cases of emergencies I may give you an extension, but you must email me for this. I might not give you an extension unless it is truly an emergency.

ABSENCE POLICY FOR IN-CLASS ASSIGNMENTS: If you are absent on the day of an in-class assignment, such as a quiz, a presentation, or a peer review and your absence is excused, you may be allowed to make up the assignment or complete a similar assignment. If your absence is not excused, you will not be allowed to make up the assignment and will receive a zero.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Students are expected to attend on time and actively participate in all classes. As students cannot participate well in class without being present, students will be expected to be physically in class and participate actively to receive full points for participation.

5 points will be given for each 50 minutes of class. Students may miss 40 points (400 minutes of class) and receive an A grade in participation (full marks). These classes can be missed for any reason without any documentation; these points include both excused and unexcused absences.

If you have a prolonged illness or other circumstance that would cause you to miss more classes, please see the instructor and special arrangements may be made.

Students arriving after attendance has been called will lose 1 point for the day (if they fully participate while in the class). Students arriving more than 15 minutes late – will lose 5 points for the day (if they fully participate while in the class), and students arriving more than 1 hour 15 minutes late will not receive any points that day.

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

For plagiarism that is deemed unintentional (poor/lack of citation), the first violation will received a reduced score on the assignment. Further violations will result in a 0 grade for that assignment.

For plagiarism that is deemed intentional (copying someone else’s work), the first violation will result in a 0 grade for that assignment. The second violation results in failure of the course. If you have any questions concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification.

Use of AI

AI programs such as Chat GPT may be used as an aid to your writing in this class for things such as but not limited to the following: help with choosing a topic, creating outlines, finding sources, making reference lists, and editing papers. If you use an AI tool, you should cite that tool and state at the end of your paper how you have used that tool. AI should never be used to write or translate your entire paper or large portions of your paper for you. This will result in a 0 grade for the assignment.
