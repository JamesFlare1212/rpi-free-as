## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An intermediate hands-on studio course in 3D computer animation. Students will complete several small assignments that are designed to encourage creativity, develop a further skill with the tools and techniques of animation.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The goal of this course is to develop a solid foundation of the principles and practice of 3D animation and gain fluency with industry-standard 3D animation software.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- By the completion of the course students will be able to: - Demonstrate proficiency in animation techniques, vocabularies and relevant technologies - Demonstrate effective use of animation principles such as timing, spacing, overlap, arcs, weight, force - Collaborate successfully in a studio setting. - Critique peer artworks in a creative community and and evaluate their earlier creative work. - Adopt multiple perspectives on contemporary complex, global issues concerning artistic practice in animation. - Analyze animation practices that engage social, cultural, environmental, and economic issues.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

3D animation is a combination of artistry, technical methods, animation craft, and - most importantly - *practice!* Becoming a good animator is about practice, and being self-reflective in your practice. Animation is also about finding inspiration: from other animations, from other media, and from the world around you; and about participating in a creative community.

Work in this course will be in 5 categories. You can receive a badge for receiving a set number or points in each category:

- **Habits**: daily or weekly practice, from keeping a sketchbook to doing short practice animations, and coming to class and participating
- **Inspiration**: watching and analyzing animations, studying shots frame-by-frame, finding inspiration in games, films, visual art, music, books, and motion everywhere
- **Community**: sharing, critiquing in class, discord or on-line forums
- **Skills**: essential techniques with Maya and other software
- **Quests**: are where you apply your understanding of animation principles to complete small shots and pieces. These are the things that will go into your portfolio. While they are the most visible output, all of the other categories are essential to success in animation.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Grading in this course will use a point accumulation system. Assignments in Habits, Inspiration, Community, Skills, and Quests all provide points, and your final grade is based on the number of points you have accumulated at the end of the semester. Completing enough points in a category earns a Badge. You must complete all five Badges to receive an A in the course.

The number of points that an assignment is worth is determined by how advanced it is and how much time it typically takes. There are multiple ways to achieve a Badge in each category, and to achieve the grade you want, by choosing the assignments you want to do.

The Quests will represent in total roughly 60% of the total points, and each of the other categories represents 10%.

Each week you will submit the work you’ve done that week. Some assignments will be short and apply to that week, such as a weekly sketchbook. Longer assignments will often have two or more stages (such as the “planning stage”, “blocking pass” and “splining pass”), and a typical rhythm is to complete one stage per week.

**Weekly Participation: Habits, Inspiration, Community, Skills Point:** max 8,000 points per week

**Quest Points:** 10,000-50,000 points each base on the complexity of quest

**Quest Revision/bonus Points:** 0-20,000 points. You can unlock these points by participating in a round of peer review/critique and revision. Very good, responsibly revised quest will receive 5,000 bonus points, once-in-a-lifetime completely reimagined work will receive 20,000, top revision work might get 10,000.

**Assignments by choice:** various, from 500 points to 120,000 points
