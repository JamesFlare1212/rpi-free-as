## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course in an introductory studio course in drawing techniques and concepts. Students will develop perceptual and conceptual drawing skills through exercises, critiques, slide lectures, and strong drawing practice. This class stresses building hand-eye coordination and understanding of spatial relationships, perspective, and proportion through direct observation. Assignment subjects include still-life, architecture, live model and landscape. No prior experience is assumed or expected.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

No required texts for this course. However the following materials are required:

• Container to hold tools and supplies. Anything that will fit all of your supplies. Tool box, tote bag, whatever.

• 1 Drawing Board, with clips, big enough to accommodate 18x24 drawing paper.

• 1 Drawing Paper Pad, 18x24 for finished drawings

• Graphite pencils (1 of the following): 4H, 2H, H, HB, B, 2B, 4B, 6B, 8B. Best if purchased in a box set.

• 1 Box vine charcoal (soft)

• 1 Box compressed charcoal (soft)

• Charcoal pencils (soft) *optional*

• Blending stumps/ fabric squares *optional*

• 1 Pencil sharpener

• 1 Hard white Staedler eraser

• 1 Kneaded Eraser

• 1 Box Knife, preferably the kind with snap-off blades for sharpening pencils and erasers, and cutting paper

## Course Goals

### Goals:

**COURSE GOALS:**

The emphasis of the work in this class will be on the development of skills in perception, communication, and design as they relate to the medium of drawing. Students will be able to demonstrate skills in observational looking, basic handling of various drawing media and processes of image translation through skilled mark-making with an understanding of composition.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Portfolio 90% (Main Projects: 55%, exercises 35%)

Class Participation: 10% (contributing to discussions / critiques, class preparedness)  
*(see below for definitions of both class participation and class preparedness)*

Each assignment will be graded on both your effort (how much time and care given to project) and process (following instruction, demonstrating understanding of concepts, showing investigation)

Each assignment will be graded with the following criteria: (\_\_\_/100)

Effort toward accuracy (proportion, measuring): 40 points

Effort toward craftsmanship (quality of marks (or) value/sense of light): 30 points

Effort toward composition (balance/arrangement of subjects/ drawing elements): 25 points

Presentation of work (perferation removed, no tears or folds in paper): 5 points

If your project is not hanging at the beginning of class on Crit day then it will be considered late. Unfinished work will not be critiqued. You will only receive a grade for finished work. Late work will suffer in grade by 10 points. A no show/no word on crit days will result in an additonal 10 points off projects. (20 points off total)

**Class Preparedness** means you are prepared and ready to work as soon as class begins with the assigned materials at hand. It also means that you have all assignments/homework hung at the beginning of crit with your name clearly written on the front of each drawing.

**Class Participation** means you are actively contributing to discussions and class critiques by showing interest in what others make, asking questions about the assignment goals and outcomes, and using learned vocabulary. It also applies for the explanation and presentation of your own work. Critiques begin by describing observations and interpretations grow from there. Do your best to speak up at each critique.
