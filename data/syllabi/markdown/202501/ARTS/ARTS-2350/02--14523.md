## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An instrumental class that will be coached, rehearsed and will perform regularly. It focuses on the collaborative experience of music in small ensembles such as string quartets, woodwind quintets, trios, etc., as well as into more unusual combinations that might be required to prepare 20th century repertoire. One can participate as an individual or as part of a pre-formed ensemble.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Repertoire will be assigned according to students' musical interests and instrumental skills.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course is an interpretive context and practice of all musical concepts taught in other musical classrooms and private lessons, such as structure, dynamics, expressive techniques, etc. The aim is an analytical and performative engagement with musical texts, their historically grounded expressive codes for the different repertoires and their specificities for different instruments while learning the collaborative techniques and strategies that allow for their renditions.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Traditional and contemporary chamber music repertoire of any instrumentation including technological supports, transcriptions as well as original compositions.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Analytical engagement with musical texts.
- Develop collaborative strategies and techniques in group music playing.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Students who successfully complete this course will demonstrate:

an understanding of musical notation, theory and musical expression

a methodology for their individual and ensemble practice

strategies for synchronization with other players

Weekly practice and participation in class are essential and bi-weekly progress assessments will be guiding the course.  
A final in class presentation either as a recording or a final concert participation.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Final grade is a weighting of 60% weekly attendance and practice, 20% participation and engagement in class, and 20% final presentation.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Since much of the class is focused on performing, listening to and discussing musical works, instrument techniques and ensemble strategies in class, attendance is essential.

1. More then two unexcused absences will affect your grade, detracting 1/2 grade each additional 2 unexcused absences.
2. Absences can only be excused by a letter from a medical doctor or from the Dean of Students' office.
