## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will have a basic understanding of the arc of jazz history and influence.
- • Students will be able to identify major historical figures in the jazz canon and other forms of improvised music.
- • Students will have an understanding of the role jazz has played in the revitalization of improvisation in American and European music.
- • Student will be able to identify by ear different historical periods of Jazz and its precursors.
- • Students will have an understanding of several important current trends of improvised music.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

35% Research Essay of 2500 words (in MLA format) based on a topic of your own choice.

Students have the option of doing a creative project instead of a written essay for this assignment. Creative projects can include a recording of a personal instrumental or vocal performance, or original composition.

For both essay and creative project options, a proposal is due four weeks before the submission deadline of **April 21.**

20% Group annotation assignments using Perusall. For each assigned article, your written contribution will require critical reading of the article and will consist of a mix of thoughtful comments, questions, links to shared resources, and replies to your classmates’ annotations. Annotations are due by midnight the day before the class when the article is being discussed. Perusall link:

https://app.perusall.com/courses/history-of-jazz-and-improvised-music-796085612

Course code: **CENTURY-7LPZ3**

15% Jazz in Film review, 750 words. A list of feature films with a prominent role for jazz is provided. Due **March 17**

15% Two live performance reviews, each 500 words, following concert rubric provided. Performance opportunities will be listed on LMS, but other performances may be reviewed, so long as they are identified in advance and approved by the instructor. Due **February 10, March 31**

15% Participation. Includes in-class discussion and presentations, plus attendance.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Course Policy for Use of Generative AI writing tools.** This is a communication intensive course emphasizing original writing for course assessments. Academic dishonesty sanctions will apply to the uncredited use of material generated from AI apps, like ChatGPT, Microsoft Co-pilot, etc. No use of AI is permitted in writing the short assignments, i.e the two concert reviews and the music in film review.

Limited use of AI is permitted for the final project, essay option, under the specific condition that the use is fully documented as defined in detail on LMS. In brief, this policy requires you to critically analyze GenAI writing support technologies as a component of your creative writing process. You may not use AI to generate the text of your essay, only to support the crafting and refinement of the essay outline. This process must be fully documented in a submission that is due in the final project proposal section on LMS
