## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Private applied music lessons are offered to provide a fine arts experience for credit and are focused on vocal performance and assessment in both private and group settings.

This course creates an explorative environment for students to develop their voice and vocal technique with the student’s vocal identity at the center. Introductory ideas to voice science/vocal pedagogy, breathing and singing technique, languages/diction, and phonation, with the aim of making a healthy, efficient acoustic sound across a variety of vocal genres in an energizing, encouraging experience. This is an exploration of student’s unique vocal identity, guided by technique and the instructor’s insight as tools for individualized progress.

In this course, students will gain the skills to give and receive technical feedback, gain performance experience, access information on voice resources, workshops, and vocal connections across campus, and engage in periodic activities throughout the semester, including shared assessment in other singers’ lessons. Certain dates are TBD.

**Note: Innovative pedagogy techniques (flip the classroom, group activities, new events, attendance of others’ lessons) will supplement this year’s course offering**.

Repertoire across the vocal spectrum and students new to singing are welcome. Previous singing experience is not a requirement, though the pedagogy in this course interfaces with voice ensemble participation in Concert Choir ARTS 2310.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Learning Outcomes**

- Students in the private lesson program will study to improve their sense of vocal singing identity and to musically interpret a variety of musical works and styles/genres.
- Students will commit to their own daily technical singing work to create space for greater individual expression and vocal exploration, in pedagogic solo, chamber, choral and various musical contexts.
- Students will explore application of learnings in group voice / performance settings.
- Students will improve listening skills, as well as mature in their ability to work at once independently on appropriate warm-ups, ideas, and music. as assigned and discussed in class, as well as guided self-direction.
- Students will be encouraged to question the musical canon, and structures and systems of organization in vocal/musical history, and to explore elements of social justice in their repertoire and application of knowledge.
- Students track progress with note-taking and weekly updates via a shared Google Doc.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Grades and assessment of student progress is made based on individual pace, effort, and improvement over the course of the semester.

- In-class singing/feedback application 50%
- Attendance at group events/lessons 30%
- Self-assessment /journal 20%

Regular in-person assessments and feedback will be provided during workshops, along with a warmup routine and repertoire selections. **Students should attend at least one lesson per semester of another singer and provide a brief post-lesson reflection in their journal**.

The final reflection will address students’ response to special group events:

## Policies

### Other Course Policies:

**Course Expectations**

- Regular attendance at all scheduled lessons (50 min. x 15 times/semester).
- 15-20 min. daily practice using the warm-up routine and techniques from class.
- Satisfactory progress on repertoire studied, as determined by private instructor.
- Music should be printed, notated, and provided to the instructor as requested. Please provide a copy to the professor of any new music, double sided and hole-punched with breaths marked in pencil. Bring a pencil and scores to each lesson.
- Students should keep a vocal journal (Google doc) to record lesson notes and progress.

Note regarding cancellations, illness and COVID safety:

- Absences and make-ups: Any instructor absence will be rescheduled to a make-up and must occur before April 28, 2025. **Make ups will not be available** in Spring 2025 for missed course time. Excused absences fall under the categories listed here: studentlife.rpi.edu/current-students/academic-and-personal-support/requesting-excused-absence.

We can continue to do valuable vocal work remotely that may be non-singing work in the event of illness. Either email me or request a link by the start of your lesson time to pivot to remote class. 24 to 48 hours’ notice is encouraged.
- For a Lesson Swap: **Email our voice studio group email or contact individual singers** to request a lesson time swap. Please do so in advance to allow response time and please email me to confirm that a change is agreed upon.
