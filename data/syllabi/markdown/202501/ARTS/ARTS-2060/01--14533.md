### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

In this course the student will develop their creative ability and depth of expression using digital imaging tools.

Students will gain knowledge of animation history and its role in the art world, including experimental and underground animation.

Students will apply technical/aesthetic knowledge in the completion of a series of animation projects.

Students will gain experience in critique process by presenting their work in class.

Students will learn the fundamental vocabulary of animation, such as spacing and timing, anticipation/reaction, squash and stretch, motion cycles, exaggeration, and staging.

Students will develop technical skills in animation and video production techniques using appropriate software.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Academic Dishonesty

*The Rensselaer Handbook* defines various forms of academic dishonesty and procedures for responding to them. Students are responsible for familiarizing themselves with these policies. Students should note that the penalty for plagiarism and other forms of cheating is a grade of “F” for the course.

In this class “cheating” consists of plagiarism or representing the work of others as your own. This can include the deliberate or unintentional omission of acknowledgments or references. Cheating also consists of copying graphics, created by others and submitting that work as your own.
