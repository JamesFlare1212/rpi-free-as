### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Learning Outcomes:**

Students who successfully complete this course:

- \- will be able to communicate effectively through visual design using design elements to create engaging and well-conceived images.
- \- will demonstrate the ability to discuss articulately their own and other students’ work through group discussions and critiques of student work.
- \- will be able to discuss and critically analyze works of art from the history of art and contemporary art and to apply concepts and strategies of pictorial design that they have learned to their own work.
- \- will demonstrate their understanding of design elements by the production of a well-designed, high quality, coherent portfolio to be presented at the end of the semester.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

**Grading**

Studio projects are graded by both **concept** and **execution**.

Students are expected to solve the weekly design problems in novel, expressive, and meaningful ways. Their execution should contain a level of craft necessary to communicate the design components.

**Late Work:**

If you report an illness 48 hours before a project is due, I can certainly grant an extension on work and push back a due date. However, if you report an illness with 24 hours of a deadline for an assignment, I am requiring that you send in the assignment by the due date, even if you do not come to class. For physical/paper projects, this means sending me a quick cell phone image showing work completed.

*The Rensselaer Handbook* defines various forms of academic dishonesty and procedures for responding to them. Students are responsible for familiarizing themselves with these policies. Students should note that the penalty for plagiarism and other forms of cheating is a grade of “F” for the course.

In this class “cheating” consists of plagiarism or representing the work of others as your own. This can include the deliberate or unintentional omission of acknowledgments or references. Cheating also consists of copying graphics, created by others and submitting that work as your own.

The use of AI generated work is prohibited in this course, and will constitute as cheating, resulting in an “F” for the course.
