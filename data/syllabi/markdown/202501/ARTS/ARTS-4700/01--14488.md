## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

For those who already have a fundamental understanding of audio recording and production principles and techniques, this course is designed to enhance and develop further pathways of knowledge in the field. From using microphones and direct injection (DI) for recording audio signals into digital formats, to mixing and mastering sound files for multiple loudspeaker and binaural playback systems, we will cover a wide range of options presently available to sound recordists and producers. Captured audio can be modified in unlimited ways utilizing recent improvements in digital signal processing (DSP) and, through MIDI and VST’s, virtual worlds of sound can be created within a multitude of sequencing programs and digital audio workstations (DAWs). This class will explore many techniques and applications for creative and commercial recording and production endeavors.

Students will be required to have a current version of Avid ProTools—student discount subscriptions are available—on their school or personal computer, plus audio interface (MBox, Apollo, Scarlett, etc.) and decent monitoring capabilities (i.e. loudspeakers, good headphones) for lab assignments. Lectures will be provided weekly on the class Webex space, and the class will meet in the HASS Media Studio weekly in one of two groups (A and B) for in-person instruction and demonstration. Lab time will be provided on Mondays and Thursdays from 6-12 PM and Fridays 12-12 PM by appointment. Homework and quizzes are based on the reading and the video lectures.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Audio continues to be a critical element in almost all forms of entertainment and artistic expression, and is becoming a more immersive format on a daily basis. Students completing this course will possess an enhanced understanding and appreciation of the technologies available for sonic manipulation at a high level, and have a foundational knowledge of how to achieve desired results using Avid ProTools and the Avid S6 control surface. From game audio structures to recording/mixing/mastering a range of musical genres, students will find their knowledge and skill set well developed upon completion of this course.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Recording with multiple microphone arrays, advanced ProTools application for recording and mixing, use of compression, distortion, equalization, reverberation VSTs in mixing, advanced mixing techniques for stereo, 5.1, 7.1, etc., mixing for video, ambisonics, Dolby Atmos and surround, remote collaboration with Jacktrip, Max DSP/game audio middleware, mastering for present-day formats.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

LEVEL 3 CERTIFICATION QUIZ

Early in the semester, all students who have not already qualified for Level 3 certification must pass a brief proficiency and quiz on studio operation procedures in the HASS Media Lab in order to use the studio without supervision.

Five (5) HOMEWORKS

Each homework will be a set of questions derived from key concepts covered in the text (*Sound FX*). Late homework will have ten points deducted for every class day it is late.

Four (4) LABS

LAB #1: MAKING A SAMPLE FILE: 20 samples.

LAB #2: MIXING and WORKING in PROTOOLS

LAB #3: VOCAL SESSION.  
LAB #4: ADD SOUND TO PICTURE/GAME

MIDTERM TEST

Covers salient points of readings and lectures in first 12 weeks of class, plus important studio techniques learned.

FINAL LAB

Each student will be involved with two projects, working in pairs as engineer and producer respectively. Session must be consolidated with a minimum of eight (8) recorded tracks and 2:30 length, and use equalization, compression, reverb, MIDI/Native Instruments, and automation. Each session should also use at least one advanced technique: ambisonics, Dolby Atmos, 5.1/7.1., Jacktrip, convolution, Unity/Fmod/Wwise, etc. Mix must conform to reasonable LUFS levels of modern playback media, and specifications for Apple Digital Masters.

### Attendance Policy:

**It is best for both of us for you to have documentation for the reason of your absence obtained through the Student Success Office.** However, an absence can (on occasion) become an excused one by informing me of your extremely good reason beforehand or, if it’s an emergency, as soon as possible after class. If you have a sports-related absence, you must notify me with a list of all classes you expect to miss in advance, and have a plan worked out with me for any make-up work.

I will allow up to three (3) unexcused absences in this class, though it will be on you to keep up with any missed class content, and after the 3rd you will be notified that you have reached the limit and any unexcused absences thereafter with result in a half-grade drop in your final grade. **Bottom line: if you must miss the class for a good reason, please make sure to get an official excuse as I’m sure you would for any other class.**
