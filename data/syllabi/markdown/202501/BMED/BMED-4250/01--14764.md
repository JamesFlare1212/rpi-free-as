## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Course Description: Quantitative analysis of transport phenomena in physiological systems, including fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, and mass transfer. Fluid statics, mass and energy conservation, laminar and turbulent flow, microscale and macroscale analytical methods, mass transport with biochemical reactions, applications to transport in tissue and organs.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Basic Transport Phenomena in Biomedical Engineering, 4th Ed. Ronald L. Fournier

## Course Goals

### Goals:

1\. Develop an understanding of the fundamentals of thermodynamics and mass transport in biomedical systems  
2\. Learn to simplify realistic systems by applying appropriate assumptions.  
3\. Apply course material in the solution of short, well-defined problems.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Week 1-3: Introduction to thermodynamics, including the first and second law and various thermodynamic properties  
Weeks 4: Physical properties of body fluids and the cell membrane.  
Weeks 5-6: Energy and mass conservation. The Bernoulli equation. The physical and flow properties of blood and other fluids  
Weeks 7-11: Mass transfer fundamentals in homogenous systems  
Weeks 12-14: Mass transfer fundamental in heterogeneous systems

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students should be able to successfully simply and solve realistic problems for open and closed systems in thermodynamics
- Students should be able to simplify and solve a realistic problem in BioFluids (Momentum Transport)
- Students should be able to successfully simplify and solve realistic problems for homogenous and heterogenous mass transfer problem
