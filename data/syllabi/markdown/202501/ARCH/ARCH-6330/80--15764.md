## Course Goals

### Goals:

The goal of Built Ecologies 2 is to advance the initial understanding developed as part of Built Ecologies 1 into a combined design and systems thinking approach informed by environmental and carbon analyses.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Explore various methods to reduce embodied carbon during building design.
- Calculate the impact of building materials on climate change via environmental product declarations (EPDs).
- Simulate passive conditioning strategies e.g. natural ventilation.
- Simulate active conditioning strategies i.e. mechanical systems.
- Analyze total (whole life) carbon of building designs to evaluate the carbon benefit of passive conditioning strategies.
