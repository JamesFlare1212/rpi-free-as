### Prerequisites or Other Requirements:

MANE-4480 Physics of Nuclear Reactors or familiar with the following topics: Introductory physics and technology of nuclear reactors; reactor statics-neutron diffusion and moderation; the fission process; criticality of bare homogeneous and heterogeneous systems; reactor point kinetics model based on diffusion theory. Specifically, familiarity desirable with the following:

- Background material relating to the basic atomic and nuclear physics, fluid dynamics and thermal heat transfer relevant to nuclear engineering.
- Ordinary and partial differential equations.
- Systems of linear equations, eigenvalues and eigenvectors.
- Nuclear cross sections data and evaluated nuclear data files.
- Nuclear reactor statics: nuclear fission systematics, neutron diffusion theory, slowing down theory and criticality conditions as applied to bare, homogeneous nuclear reactors.
- Nuclear reactor dynamics: dynamic behavior of nuclear fission system, reactor point kinetic model with prompt and delayed neutrons, reactor transient with temperature feedback

## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The broad goal of this course is to prepare students with basic understanding of nuclear reactor analysis principles and state-of-the-art techniques/methodologies used in modern nuclear reactor analysis codes.

Topics covered in this course include: multigroup diffusion equation for full core analysis, few-group cross section generation, fine group spectrum calculation, neutron slowing down and thermalization analysis, resonance absorption treatment, spectral and spatial homogenization techniques, solution methods of transport and diffusion equations, reactor kinetics models and analysis, thermal-hydraulic analysis, coupled neutronic and thermal-hydraulic analysis with temperature feedback

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Prepare students with basic understanding of nuclear reactor analysis principles and state-of-the-art techniques/methodologies used in modern nuclear reactor analysis codes. Enable the student to gain insight of the basic material and techniques relating to the physics and analytical/numerical treatment of neutron fission chain reacting systems involving both steady-state and time-dependent behavior. Develop the ability to computationally analyze system operational behavior and performance of realistic nuclear reactors with specified characteristics and parameters.
