## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Undergraduate course. This course is designed to introduce the concepts and methods used in the analysis of primary aircraft/aerospace structure, including principal components of the wing and fuselage. Students will become comfortable with the basic aircraft structural elements loads, and materials. Building from the concepts of structural mechanics and elementary elastic continuum mechanics, students will be able to determine the stresses, strains, and deflections of simple thin-walled monocoque and semi-monocoque aircraft/aerospace structures subjected to torsional, bending, and shear loadings. A link is made between this analysis and the failure of aerospace structures and materials by yielding, fatigue, fracture, and buckling. In the latter portion of the course, the students are presented with a brief introduction in the mechanics and design of composite materials for aerospace structures.

This aerospace structures and materials course focuses on formulation, analysis and solution of aerospace structural engineering problems. The course facilitates graduate and professional work in advanced aerospace structures and aerospace materials (metallic and composites).

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Beam structures under combined shear, bending and torsional loads. Semi-monocoque structures: idealizations involving wings, ribs, and fuselage bulkheads. Effects of taper and cutouts in stiffened shell structures, shear deformations and warping, location of elastic axis in open and closed sections, torsion of multicell sections. Stability of beam and membrane elements. Introduction to materials used in aerospace vehicles including metals and composites with special emphasis on fiber-reinforced composite materials. Methods for material analysis and selection for various aerospace components. Numerical methods in structural analysis - introduction to finite difference and finite element method.
