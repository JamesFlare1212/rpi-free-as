### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**STUDENT LEARNING OUTCOMES**

- ) Students will learn to read historical scientific texts, understand their contribution to our understanding of Nature, identify their limitations, and be able to discern the quality of the information recorded in the chemical literature.

- ) Students will learn the contributions of key figures in the development of chemistry and of the scientific method.

- ) Students will learn how to access and use online resources to locate relevant information regarding the topics of interest.

- ) Students will present their work as a paper AND a final presentation, which will focus on a particular chemist and his or her contribution to our understanding of Nature.
