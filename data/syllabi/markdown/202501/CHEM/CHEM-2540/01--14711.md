## Course Goals

### Goals:

1\. To understand and explore concepts in modern geochemistry. This includes hard rock geochemistry, economic geochemistry and organic geochemistry (OIL!), and cosmochemistry, as well as aqueous geochemistry, biogeochemistry, and environmental geochemistry. We shall develop a common toolbox of techniques to understand geochemical problems, using introductory chemistry and other techniques. We may go on short field trips.

2\. To apply geochemical techniques to understand problems in the immediate environment. This will include some studies of samples from around campus as well as from other locations. To accomplish this, throughout the course it will be important to consider the topics discussed, such as the abundance of elements in samples and what that implies about the nature of the sample (and if there are associated health risks!), from descriptive, graphical, and quantitative points of view. For example, you should consider how geologic materials are described chemically- in terms of composition, isotopes, age, and chemical speciation.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Week 1– Introduction to geochemistry, overview of scientific concepts, intro to the syllabus  
– The water cycle, solutions, compositions of water, Introduction to geochemistry, the periodic table, and classification of elements

Week 2– pH and acids/bases  
– Properties of water, acids and bases

Week 3 –– Thermodynamics 1

Week 4– Thermodynamics 2  
– Redox reactions

Week 5– Understanding redox chemistry through time, Eh pH diagrams  
– Phase diagrams for minerals

Week 6– Rates of chemical reactions  
– Segue into Radioactivity

Week 7– EXAM 1 (Material through week 6)  
–Radioactive Dating

Week 8– Isotope Geochemistry

Week 9– Phase diagrams and high temperature thermodynamics

Week 10- Aqueous alteration- terrestrial and extraterrestrial  
\- Formation of clays and other reactions

Week 11- Exam 2  
– Water/mineral exchange reactions

Week 12– Introduction to biogeochemistry  
– Carbon, Nitrogen, Phosphorus cycling, Biogeochemical cycles through deep time

Week 13– Diagenesis, Intro to Economic Geology  
– Organic Geochemistry, Oil and other carbon compound maturation and changes

Week 14– Fluid processes on other planets  
– Titan! And other planets/moons/objects and their composition

Week 15- Experimentation and Analysis Techniques  
\- Catch up and Review  
Week END- Final Exam
