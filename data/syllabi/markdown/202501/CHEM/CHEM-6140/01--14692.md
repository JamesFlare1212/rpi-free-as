## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course provides a qualitative treatment of modern techniques in multi-dimensional NMR spectroscopy, including the history of magnetic resonance, principles of NMR spectroscopy, 13C and 1H NMR, exchange and dipolar couplings, multinuclear NMR, 2D homonuclear and heteronuclear methods, nuclear Overhauser effect, relaxation, solid-state NMR and the nuts and bolts of NMR spectrometers. It is intended for graduate and upper-level undergraduate students in the School of Science and Engineering and is structured so as to facilitate routine NMR applications for research.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

## Course Content

### Content Details:

History of magnetic resonance

Introduction

What is a spin?

Pulse and FT NMR

Basic single pulse experiment

Chemical shifts

Spin relaxation

Spin-spin couplings

Instrumental aspects

Spin echo experiment

Magnetic interactions

Two-dimensional NMR

COSY, DQF-COSY, TOCSY, HSQC, HMQC,

HMBC, NOESY and ROESY experiments

Solid-state magic angle spinning (MAS) NMR

Sensitivity enhancement by polarization transfer (DNP)
