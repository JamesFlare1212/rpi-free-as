## Course Goals

### Goals:

This is a course in global human ecology and modeling. Students will learn how to develop and encode systems dynamics models. Students will development an understanding of human interactions with the environment and with each other, and ways to capture those causal relationships in causal loop diagrams. Using debates, students will learn to develop, present and analyze logical support using logic support diagrams. Students will engage in open discussion of topics of the day in a judgement-free envoronment.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

1\. Systems modeling. 1/7-1/24  
2\. Moral psychology and group behavior. 1/28 - 2/25  
3\. Global ecology and human systems 3/11 - 4/11

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will be able to present and critique a logical argument for a forward prediction of variables relevant to human ecology.
- Students will be able to build and run systems dynamics models for human ecology.
- Students will be able to draw from biology, ecology, geochemistry, psychology and math in discussing human population dynamics.
- Students will understand and practice objectivity and ordered process in science writing.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

7-minute Science : 5% -- Present an essay on a topic of the student's choosing.  
Homework : 30% -- develop and test systems dynamics models  
Debates : 20% -- work as teams to test a hypothesis in a debate setting, evaluate other teams using logic support diagrams  
Reading/Participation: 10% -- read and discuss the two textbooks.  
Final Project: 15% -- Spot-check of course concepts and terms.  
Quizzes/Tests : 20% -- testing of specific topis, including moral psychology, ecological footprint, philosophy of science.

## Grading Criteria
