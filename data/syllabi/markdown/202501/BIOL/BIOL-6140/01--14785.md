## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Students will be assessed by participation in class discussion and their presentation. For their presentation, students will choose papers in consultation with the instructor. After each presentation students will meet with the instructor and receive feedback on their performance. Presentations will be graded by the instructor.

•Participation – 70%  
•Presentation – 30%  
•Total – 100%

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Attendance is expected at each class. Each missed class after the first two will result in the loss of half a letter grade. All students are expected to read the assigned papers before class and be prepared to discuss them in detail. A short writeup describing two questions about the discussion paper is due to the instructor the night before class.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. Submission of any assignment that is in violation of this policy will result in a penalty. If found in violation of the academic honesty policy, students may be subject to two types of penalty. The first violation results in a 0 grade for that assignment. The second violation results in failure of the course.
