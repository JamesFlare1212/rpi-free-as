## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- At the end of the course a successful student will be able to: • Demonstrate the ability to distinguish between prokaryotes and eukaryotes
- Demonstrate the ability to distinguish different microbes
- Demonstrate the ability to summarize the main properties of each group of microorganisms: fungi, protozoa, algae, bacteria and viruses
- Demonstrate the ability to summarize current topics in Microbiology
- Demonstrate the ability to summarize current problems with antibiotic resistance
- Demonstrate the ability to summarize the current topics on viral infections, including: Ebola, H1N1, H1N5 and swine flu.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Partial exams and a presentation.

If you miss a class, it is your responsibility to make up for any missed material.

**-EXAMS:**

There will be **4 partial exams:**

-**3 written partial exams**: The **1st and 2nd exams** will be scheduled during class periods (see below). The first exam will cover the material presented from the beginning of the class. Each subsequent exam will focus on the material covered since the previous exam, but questions can cover any material since the beginning of the class. The format of the exams will vary.

**3th** **exam** will be in the form of a presentation that WILL COUNT as a partial exam (see below.)

**4th exam** will be scheduled at the time of the final exam. This is a partial exam. THERE IS NO FINAL EXAM.

ALL EXAMS ARE MANDATORY.

**An exam missed without a legitimate excuse will receive a grade of zero.**

Make–up exams will be scheduled for students who miss the original time of the exam with a valid reason.

If you miss an exam, you must let me know as soon as you can. If you fail to let me know in a timely manner, I reserve the right to refuse to give a make-up exam.

Again, There will not be a final exam.

**-Exam 3. Presentations:**

A 30-minute oral presentation on a microbiology topic.

Topics needs to be submitted for approval (see below).

This is important so we will not have repeat topics.

**Basic principles:**

NO CHEATING! Every professor tells you this. I mean it! For complete details in academy honesty, review the Academic Dishonesty document attached.

All forms are violations of the trust between students and teachers. Students should familiarize themselves with this portion of the Rensselaer Handbook and should note that the penalties for plagiarism and other forms of cheating can be quite harsh. Reading the syllabus is important.

Anyone cheating on an exam will be given a zero for that exam. **Two cheating episodes will result in an F in the course and may be reported for academic discipline.** NO BOOKS, notes, crib sheets or other written materials are allowed during exams.
