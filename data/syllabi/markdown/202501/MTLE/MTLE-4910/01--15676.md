## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This class covers basic materials selection concepts and the underlying structure-property-process-performance interaction. Engineering materials, structures and properties, principles and process of materials selection, generation of materials performance indices, assessment and optimization of performance, processing routes and manufacturing issues, role of reverse engineering and failure analysis in design are covered. Materials selection against yielding, fracture, flexure, buckling, fatigue, creep, corrosion, and wear are addressed. Decomposition of engineering problems into functional, geometric and materials constraints are emphasized. Materials selection based on simple and complex or conflicting constraints will be developed. Students will perform written assignments and oral presentations to develop communication skills. Enrollment for MS&E majors is restricted to juniors, seniors or graduates.

Other parts of the class involve measurement analysis, design of experiments, and other selected topics that are not covered in the textbook. All course materials will be shared on Box and LMS.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Use the materials design and selection process to solve engineering problems relating to materials and shape selection to meet engineering and societal needs (3.4);
- • Use different communication methods (such as customer interviews, functional decomposition, writing, oral presentation) to collect and disseminate data relating to materials selection (3.3);
- • Analyze a materials selection problem in terms of functional, geometric, and materials constraints (3.1);
- • Perform materials selection in multi-objective and conflicting constraint scenarios (3.2);
- • Perform a measurement systems analysis (3.6). Matching ABET criteria are listed in parenthesis after each learning outcome. Estimated category content in Math and Basic Sciences: 1 credit hour. Estimated category content in Engineering Topics: 2 credit hours.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Throughout the semester, you will work on small projects of increasing depth and complexity, either by assignment, or of your own choosing in some cases. Where applicable, you will follow the design process and in-part use Ashby's approach in development of a performance equation and decomposition of that performance equation into functional, geometric and material-specific parts in the design of components. You will select a material system that meets the objectives and obeys the constraints of the subsystem and deliver a written and/or oral report on both engineering and societal considerations.
