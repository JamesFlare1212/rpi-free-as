## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Theory and practice of IC fabrication in a research laboratory environment. Test chips are fabricated and the resulting devices and circuits evaluated. Processes and fabrication equipment studied and used include oxidation/diffusion, CVD reactors, photolithography, plasma etching, vacuum evaporator, ion implantation, etc. Instruments used in process monitoring and final testing include thin film profilometer, ellipsometer, resistivity probe, scanning electron microscope, capacitance-voltage system, etc. The fundamentals of hazardous material handling and clean room procedures are studied.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Lectures and laboratory sessions are integrated parts of the course.  
1\. Introduction & Lab Safety  
2\. Diffusion in Si  
3\. Thermal Oxidation & TSUPREM  
4\. Photolithography  
5\. Dielectrics and Poly-Si Deposition  
6\. Ion Implantation  
7\. Plasma-Assisted Etching  
8\. Metallization  
9\. MOSFET  
10\. Device Characterization

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Collaborative group work is required for this course, except individual homework assignments and online quizzes. The course laboratory report must be from the students. While some fundamental materials may be found from textbooks or other publications, students should not directly copy the materials without integrating with the actual laboratory work. A grade of zero will be given on the first assignment where a violation is detected. If there is a subsequent infraction the student will receive a grade of F for the course.
