## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Employs strategic analysis to evaluate the position of the organization in technical markets relative to its competitors, given customer needs and wants. Utilizes market analysis to gauge the present state of the market. Analyzes customer needs to evaluate the organization’s competitive advantage in the market, and articulate goals and tactics to ensure the organization achieves its strategy. You will be challenged to develop highly effective teams to implement strategies, including product development to closely align with market needs and opportunities.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):
