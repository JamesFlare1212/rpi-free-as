## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This topics course will present an overview of ethics and policy and explore their relationships within the world of data and informatics. In conjunction with the concept of data dexterity, the ethical use of data to address societal issues requires an awareness of both the implications and consequences inherent is any solution. Through readings, lectures, and group projects, students will learn the basics of ethics and of policy approaches as they apply to current issues. Examples are taken from such fields as AI, health, and finance, with the goal of developing reasonable policy responses to those issues. This course also covers information security and ethics in accordance with NSA certification.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Course topics include:

- Broad overview of common ethical frameworks
- Discussion of ethics and data
- Examination of ethics and policy
- Practical evaluation of a data-intensive app
- Cybersecurity and ethics

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Describe the development of ethical frameworks in the United States
- Explain the four basic concepts of research ethics
- Analyze current events or case studies for ethical implications
- Evaluate commonalities among instances of ethical lapses with respect to data
- Create and deliver a cogent presentation that evaluates the ethical implications of a given data-related application
- Understand and explain NSA cybersecurity and ethics issues and requirements

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

students will be assessed via class discussion, participation, and papers. rubrics are posted for each assignment.
