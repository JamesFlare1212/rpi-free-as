## Course Goals

### Goals:

This is a required first course for management majors and minors. It emphasizes broad, basic principles of managerial functions and processes using an interdisciplinary approach to goal-oriented situations of private and public organizations.

The course is designed to introduce students to the key managerial functions and processes used in businesses. We will cover finance, accounting, marketing, operations, entrepreneurship, business analytics, and other general management concepts. For those of you that are business majors, the class will serve as an introduction to these topics, such that you can be familiar with them as you work through your curriculum at Lally. For those of you that are not business majors, the course will give you familiarity with these concepts so that you can use them, or at least have a basic understanding of them, as you go to work in organizations as engineers, scientists, architects, etc.

We will use readings and lectures to provide content, but then will use cases and/or exercises extensively in order to help you *apply* the ideas. In some deliverables you’ll apply concepts to specific questions but in others you’ll be tasked with making decisions and recommendations that would impact an entire business. That is one of the main objectives of this class: to get you experience in the complex, multi-faceted decision making that is done by organizational leaders. This culminates in your final project, wherein you’ll analyze a business and present your findings and recommendations in a professional presentation format.

You will also build team, interpersonal, and communication skills.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

## COURSE ASSESSMENT MEASURES/GRADING CRITERIA

***Class Participation: ~10%***

You’ll find that complex business decisions often don’t have a ‘correct’ or ‘best’ answer. In some areas, such as accounting, there is a correct way to do things, of course. However, much of what we discuss will require informed judgment, points of view, and differing opinions. This makes participation very important – the class is not just about me telling you what to think. Therefore, your contribution to discussions is necessary. Your participation grade is based on your “presence,” as opposed to mere attendance, and your contribution to discussions, both in the larger class and in your groups.

Note that I will ask you to complete confidential peer reviews of your team members at the end of term. Thus, **your team members** will be assessing **you** as well. **Your grade for participation and on your group assignments *may* be impacted by your teammate’s evaluation of your group participation and contributions to group deliverables.**

If you are going to miss class, please notify me in advance by email. An excused absence does not exempt you from the assignment due for that day. If you are sick and need to take the class asynchronously, you must notify me via email and let me know your situation. **Unexcused absences and excessive excused absences will reduce your class participation grade.** Three unexcused absences will reduce your class grade one-half a full letter per each absence after (ex. B to a B-). Life happens, though, so I’m also extremely sensitive to what has happened the last few years. We will discuss mental health early in the semester. If you are struggling –with anything –please approach me after class, via email, or in any other matter and ask if we can meet, get coffee, etc. We will then do so and talk. I am extremely flexible and will always find time for any student.

If you find yourself struggling with your mental or physical health this semester, please feel free to approach me. I try to be flexible and accommodating. You may also contact the Troy Student Health Center (518-276-6287) which provides a variety of services for full-time/degree-seeking undergraduate and graduate students. The Counseling Center is also available to help students maximize their sense of well being as well as their academic, personal and social growth. Appointments are free and confidential, and in-person at the Counseling Center, located 4th Floor of Academy Hall. Appointments can be made by calling 518-276-6479 or email counseling@rpi.edu.

***Homework: (~30%)***

The assignment will be given orally in the class prior to the due date and are also posted on the syllabus and LMS. Homework comprehension questions are posted in the Homework tab of LMS, or in Pearson Revel.

**LATE SUBMISSIONS OF HOMEWORK ASSIGNMENTS.** Understandably, there are circumstances (e.g., job interviews, illness, family matters, etc.) that may cause you to miss class. However, all assignments are to be submitted electronically using the LMS Drop Box and are **due by the start of class**. Requests for an extension of time will be handled on a case-by-case basis and are granted only when compelling reasons are given. **Late homework will be accepted at a 50% penalty up to the date of the associated quiz on that material. It is not possible to make up homework at the end of the semester.**

There are Mini simulations within Pearson’s Revel that are also part of your homework. Decisions made within the simulation earn students’ points. The number of points possible depends on which path the student chooses throughout the simulation. The percentage score they earn from the simulation equals the percentage of assignment points that will be recorded in the grade book.

***Quizzes: (~45%)***

There will be three quizzes throughout the semester, and will primarily cover the topical content, i.e., strategy, finance, accounting, marketing, operations, etc. It may reference the cases, but I’m not testing you on detailed case knowledge. We’ll talk about this more in class ~1 class session before each quiz. Each may consist of multiple-choice questions, True/False, fill in the blanks, and some calculations.

**All tests will open at the beginning of the class session. If you are using the testing center, your test will be set to start/open at the time of your scheduled appointment. If you are late, you will not be given extra time.**

***Team In class Activities and Assignments: (~15%)***

There will be multiple occasions throughout the semester where you will be working in teams on an in-class assignment, building off previous lecture content, and applying those concepts covered in class towards a Consulting Activity. Each team will be assigned a company and provided instructions in class as to what analysis is required. At the end of the class session, each group will submit their response on LMS, and present their results in front of the class. The class will provide feedback, and each team submission will be graded. **Only the team members in attendance will receive credit for the submission, and there will be no opportunity for makeup. If any student has an excused absence from a coach or their class dean, these assignments will be exempt from the student’s final grade.**
