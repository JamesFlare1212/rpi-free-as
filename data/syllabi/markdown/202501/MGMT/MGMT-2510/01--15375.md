## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

**Attendance (5 points)**

Attendance is mandatory. You begin the semester with 5 points and are allowed up to two unexcused absences. After that, you will lose 1 point for each unexcused absence.

If by the end of the semester you have missed more than 25% of the classes, you will not be permitted to complete the team project.

Note: To be counted as present, you must **arrive on time and stay for the duration of the class**.

**Homework (5 points)**

There will also be multiple homework assignments. **I will drop your lowest two (2) scores**. You cannot makeup/retake a homework assignment.

**Pop Quizzes (10 points)**

There will also be ~10 in-class pop quizzes. Each quiz will focus on the topic from the previous class. **I will drop your lowest two (2) quiz scores**. If you miss a quiz, there is no “make up” pop quiz unless you can convince the Student Experience Office and me of the *extreme circumstances*.

**Tests (60 points)**

There are 3 tests, each worth 20 points. **All 3 tests will count toward your final grade**. The tests will afford you an opportunity to demonstrate your mastery of the skills you learned in the preceding course component. Active class participation will help improve your chances of doing better on the tests and quizzes. **There is no end-of-term test**.

**Team Project (20 points)**

Details will be provided in class and on LMS.

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.
