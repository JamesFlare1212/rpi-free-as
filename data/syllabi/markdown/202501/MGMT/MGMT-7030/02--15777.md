## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The purpose of this course is to help you formulate and implement effective strategies. The course has three main goals:

- To equip you with theoretical tools and frameworks that will guide you when dealing with strategic situations.
- To help you develop and practice rigorous analytical skills relevant to strategic problems.
- To help you effectively communicate your logic and reasoning to others in various strategic contexts.

The objective of any strategy is to achieve superior performance. Hence throughout this course our concern will be with the central question: how do firms create and sustain competitive advantage? In dealing with this question we will touch upon a number of inter related themes including the attractiveness of industries, the optimal size and scope of the firm, game theory, mergers and alliances, and corporate governance.

The course is integrative in nature. Hence it will draw on multiple functional disciplines, such as Economics, Finance, Organizational Behavior, Marketing, and Technology Management. Throughout the course you will be required to step beyond your functional background and develop a general management perspective, while analyzing problems that affect the organization as a whole.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Attendance and Class Participation**

You will be required to attend classes regularly, and to read the materials assigned for each class (Note-from time to time, ***additional readings will be assigned*** depending upon the subjects we cover). Based on your reading and in class discussions, you are then expected to make thoughtful and constructive contributions. Students may be called upon randomly to answer questions in class to ensure uniform participation. In addition I may also give you brief polls or in class exercises and assignments (e.g. the Crown Cork and Seal case) which you are expected to complete, and which will count towards your participation grade.

An attendance sheet will be circulated at the beginning of every class. It is your responsibility to make sure that you record your attendance. You are allowed a maximum of **two absences** (unexcused without documentation). Any additional absences will require clear and supporting documentation (e.g. a doctor’s note) giving reasons for why you should be excused from class. In general, unless circumstances are exigent, I would expect your total absences to be minimal. **Unexcused absences as well as excessive excused absences will reduce your class participation grade.** Three unexcused absences will reduce your class grade one-half a full letter (ex. B to a B-), after which two percentage points will be deducted for every additional absence.

Note: Attendance alone will not count toward your class participation grade, and the two will be graded separately.

**Main Case Analysis- Report and Presentation (Group)**

Students will be divided into groups and each team will be assigned a case to analyze. For this assignment the group will be responsible for preparing a written report and making a formal presentation of their analysis and recommendations in class. Broadly speaking, your presentation will cover the background of the case, the issues identified (see the discussion questions for each case outlined below in the syllabus), recommendations for the future, and any other points that you think are relevant.

Each team will prepare a report that is no longer than *12 pages* (please see the guidelines for written reports below). The in class presentation will be for a maximum of *20-25 minutes* (guidelines are also provided below for presentations). The written report is due the day the case is scheduled to be presented. Late submissions will not be accepted.

Note- The cases discussed in class are designed to illustrate specific concepts and decision scenarios. Please therefore do not look up any online material or ‘solutions’ for the case, including ChatGPT or other LLMs. Any attempts to do so will be treated as a serious ethical violation, and in the minimum equivalent to a first offense (see below, and also see LLM policy).

**Brief Report (Group)**

In this report (maximum of 3 pages, excluding the cover page), you are required to identify the central issues in the case, develop a brief analysis, and provide your main recommendations. The brief report is due the day the case is scheduled to be presented, and should be emailed to me ahead of class time. Late submissions will not be accepted, and failure to turn in a report on the due date will lead to a lower grade.

**Workshop Assignment (Group)**

The workshop assignment requires you to develop an insightful presentation on an assigned topic (e.g. the role of organizational culture, why acquisitions succeed/ fail). You will be given brief guidelines ahead of the assignment, and your task is to develop a presentation of about 20-25 mins as a group. The purpose is to not only get you thinking about the topic, but also to inform the rest of the class and enhance their understanding on the subject. It is essential that you develop an in depth analysis and come well prepared to present on the day that the workshop assignment is scheduled.

**Strategy Analysis (2 presentations, one report; Group)**

The strategy analysis report requires you to select a company with multiple business segments and analyze its performance. Details of the assignment are provided below. Please make note of deadlines associated with this assignment, listed in the schedule.

**Exams (Individual)**

Two exams will held on the dates indicated in the schedule. The exams will cover all the material discussed, including the cases. The emphasis will be on testing your understanding of the material, rather than on how well you remember various concepts.

**LLM Policy**

You are encouraged to use AI tools such as LLMs to supplement your learning in class. LLMs can be a powerful tool for researching firms and industries. However they can also be wrong, and in many instances they can be superficial and not in depth enough. Hence you should critically examine and question whatever information they produce.

In this class, you may use LLMs to conduct independent research as a first step. Beyond that, all submitted assignment material, such as class slides and written reports, should be your own intellectual contribution, and should be developed by you. If you use LLMs for this purpose (e.g. cutting and pasting LLM outputs etc. in a presentation or report without substantial input and modification), it will constitute a violation of academic integrity policies, and will count in the minimum as one strike .

Note also that you are *explicitly prohibited* from using Chat GPT or any other LLM to solve case questions, or obtain information pertaining to the cases assigned in class. Doing so, again, constitutes a violation of academy integrity policies, and would count in the minimum as a strike.

The report should be double spaced (excluding the Strategy Analysis mid term report), and the font size should be 12. Your report should use headings/ sub headings to reflect what you are discussing in each section, and to guide the reader throughout.

**Group Assignments and Peer Review**

A significant part of your grade is determined by group case assignments. At the end of the semester, your group members will be asked for input on whether your contribution to group activities was satisfactory or unsatisfactory. This system is designed to identify any ‘free riders’ in the group. If two or more group members rate your contribution as unsatisfactory on a particular assignment, you will be awarded a substantially lower grade (75% or lower) on that assignment, or on all group work if deemed necessary by me. It is therefore essential that you do your fair share of group work.

Note- If any group member does not contribute satisfactorily to group activities, please let me know asap, preferably before the 1st deliverable for the Strategy Analysis project. Additionally I may award a particular group member a lower grade if I have reason to believe that group member has not contributed adequately to a project (for instance if the group member is unable to answer questions on a group project satisfactorily).

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Academic Integrity**

Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process.

The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and the Graduate Student Supplement (For 6000 level and above courses) define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these.

In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In instances where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration. Submission of any assignment that is in violation of this policy will result in (1) an academic (grade) penalty and (2) reporting to Lally’s Associate Dean of Academic Affairs and either the Dean of Students (for Undergraduates) or the Dean of Graduate Education (for Graduate students).

**In this course, the academic penalty for a first offense on any assignment as assessed by the instructor is 30% of the overall course grade*.* A second offense will result in failure of the course as noted in Lally’s Three Strikes Policy.** If you have any questions concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification.

Note also that during exams etc., no unauthorized of resources is permitted that can aid in your taking the exam (e.g. using LMS to determine correct answers). Doing so will result in a failing grade on the exam, and you may be awarded zero points. If you have any doubt about this policy, please check, as the final decision on whether you have used appropriate resources rests with me.

**LLM Policy**

You are encouraged to use AI tools such as LLMs to supplement your learning in class. LLMs can be a powerful tool for researching firms and industries. However they can also be wrong, and in many instances they can be superficial and not in depth enough. Hence you should critically examine and question whatever information they produce.

In this class, you may use LLMs to conduct independent research as a first step. Beyond that, all submitted assignment material, such as class slides and written reports, should be your own intellectual contribution, and should be developed by you. If you use LLMs for this purpose (e.g. cutting and pasting LLM outputs etc. in a presentation or report without substantial input and modification), it will constitute a violation of academic integrity policies, and will count in the minimum as one strike .

Note also that you are *explicitly prohibited* from using Chat GPT or any other LLM to solve case questions, or obtain information pertaining to the cases assigned in class. Doing so, again, constitutes a violation of academy integrity policies, and would count in the minimum as a strike.
