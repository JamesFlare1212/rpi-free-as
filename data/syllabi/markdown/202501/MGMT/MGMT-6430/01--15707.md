## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course (i) provides you with a framework for business analysis and valuation using financial statement data; and (ii) shows you how to apply this framework to a variety of business decisions. The course integrates key elements from accounting, finance, economics, and business strategy and applies them to financial decision-making. The course is case-oriented. Students are responsible for preparing case discussions in advance of the class sessions, including familiarizing yourself with the relevant issues across accounting, finance, and business strategy.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

*The objective of the course is to provide students with hands-on experience in financial statement analysis. Students will be exposed to general tools of financial analysis, theoretical concepts, and practical valuation issues. By the end of the course, students should be comfortable with using firms' financial statements to develop an understanding of firm performance and to establish a basis for making reasonable valuation estimates.*

The classes will be a combination of PowerPoint slides, in-class discussions, exercises, computation-oriented spreadsheets, and examinations.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

- **Class Attendance & Participation 10%** of the overall course grade. Attendance will be recorded at each class. If you have more than two unexcused absences, your grade will be negatively impacted. Participation will be evaluated and you must be prepared in each class to participate in class discussions.

### Other Course Policies:

- **Students must be prepared at all times to contribute** informed input to the class discussion. Do not come to class unprepared and expect to follow the material. Preparation is essential to comprehension. If you do not participate actively in class, your grade will be negatively impacted. Do not sit on the sidelines and complain. Get involved and help get the most out of class time. Your input is welcome, and it is also expected. The remaining 100 of 200 points for Class Participation / Attendance will reflect the quantity, as well as quality, of your participation in class. Both are expected - quantity alone will not garner a good grade. That means asking and answering questions intelligently, volunteering for discussion, submitting articles for consideration, as well as not being disruptive to others. Students who consistently add to the course via their contributions may be awarded a boost to their final course grade at the instructor’s discretion. My courses are intended to be an active discussion.

- **No incomplete** (grades of “I”) will be given, so it is important that all students remain continuously up to date with their work.
- **Late work** is not accepted. Late is defined as any time after the start of class on the date the assignment is due. In the business world excuses are not tolerated - so please prepare in advance, use anti-virus software, and back up your work accordingly. If you have a personal emergency, please contact the instructor before the assignment deadline and an extension may be granted.
