## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This graduate course focuses on designing and evaluating financial portfolios that help organizations manage risk-return trade-offs. The course covers portfolio management, risk management, asset pricing and hedging, and provides the necessary quantitative background to students in the growing field of asset management. The course is primarily concerned with the “buy-side” firms consisting of asset managers, hedge funds, and other firms that buy or sell securities on behalf of their clients. To be successful, students need to develop research and analytical skills, the ability to identify investment opportunities, an understanding of global market trends, the ability to create timely and high-quality reports for investment decisions, financial modeling and Excel skills, to name a few.  
Asset management is a broad subject, and the students are expected to have a basic finance background as indicated in the above course-prerequisites. In order to provide a useful treatment of the relevant topics, it is necessary to stress fundamentals and to explore topics at a technical level. The course comprises four major sections: the first section covers introduction to investments, cash flows, discount rates, fixed income assets, and term structure of interest rates. In the second, topics such as modern portfolio theory, mean-variance efficient portfolio selection, the capital asset pricing model, and alternative risk measures and their properties are discussed. The third covers markets for options and derivatives, option valuations, trading strategies, and their extensions. In the final section, advanced asset management strategies are discussed, covering fund alpha creation via various portfolio strategies, firm efficiency analysis and asset management under changing macroeconomic conditions.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Expose students to the knowledge and skills required to select and employ basic tools for financial analysis of varying assets for investment purposes.
- 2\. Foster an understanding of different types of assets, including stocks and bonds, as well as options and derivatives, and their risk characteristics to aid analysis in investment asset selection.
- 3\. Develop insights on risk management and portfolio diversification, leverage control, and develop an understanding of risk control measures to be able to evaluate and support varying objectives of asset management to suit applications.
- 4\. Understand and practice basic concepts of reporting that require portfolio stress testing, risk adjustment, and out-of-sample analysis to organize documents and presentations of quantitative fund management.
