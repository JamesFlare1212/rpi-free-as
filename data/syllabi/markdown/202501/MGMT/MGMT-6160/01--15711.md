## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Business analytics enables organizations to leverage large volumes of data in order to make more informed decisions. It encompasses a range of approaches to integrating, organizing, and applying insights obtained from the data in various settings. This course develops an understanding of concepts in business analytics, data manipulation and more importantly data visualization to share the insights. Through hands-on experience with a range of techniques students will learn to work with large data sets that could be multimodal in some instances, analyze trends and segmentations and develop models for prediction and forecasting. This course is part of the MS program in Business Analytics and builds on the introduction to ML applications course offered in the Fall semester.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- (1) Demonstrate an understanding of analytics-based problem solving and analytics thinking in the context of providing robust solutions for real world scenarios.
- (2) Be able to extract, match, transform, and clean data from a variety of sources and data types
- (3) Develop efficient predictions for business applications and beyond.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Exam** (20% total): in-class and individual test, covering the material studied up to this date.

**Project*** (20%): a hands-on project (individual task) will ensure you are able to apply what we have covered throughout the course. More information will follow.

**Assignments** (45%): there will be three case studies (or assignments – which are individual tasks), shown in the schedule on the next page.

**Active class participation** (10%) This class is heavily discussion oriented. So, class participation is very important.

**Data Representation Exercise** (5%) – For graduate level students only (individual task)

The student will choose one visualization and explain the dataset used to generate that visualization, problem being solved, why the problem is important, the findings, technical aspects that you appreciate, the business implications in non-technical language suitable for managers, and what would you like to change? Please follow the instructions given by the instructor during the appropriate lecture about this exercise.

Missing an assignment or a test without prior approval from the instructor will result in a grade of zero (0). Tasks including case studies or assignments and project (which will together add to 65% of your grade) in this class are all team tasks. Only the exams are individual tasks. There will be no opportunities for extra credits or make-up assignments. Please reach out to the instructor for more information if you have any questions.

For any unexpected situations (with a proper documentation submitted), late submissions of case studies (only) will be considered and graded towards 50% of the original score.
