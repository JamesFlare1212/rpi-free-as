## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course will present the quantitative tools and models of risk management used by banks, traders and financial institutions. The student will gain the necessary technical foundation to assess, quantify and manage financial risks via a thorough understanding of the concepts and measurement of various types of risks and the frameworks and instruments available for risk management.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:
