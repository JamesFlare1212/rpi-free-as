## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Students will analyze and learn the role of marketing in a sports organization and the interrelationship between marketing, communications, product development, sales and revenue.
- 2\. Students will hear from industry practitioners on how they have implemented marketing and communications strategies to meet goals and objectives of sports organizations.
- 3\. Students will study human behavior and “fandom” to understand what motivates fans and connects them to sports and how that connection impacts fans support of an organization.
- 4\. Students will apply sport marketing strategies and techniques in developing marketing proposals and in completing assignments.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

## GRADING CRITERIA / COURSE ASSESSMENT MEASURES

**Assignments (% of Grade)—These assignments may be adjusted. If so, students will be given appropriate notice**

1. Participation/Attendance (10%); regular class attendance and participation will contribute to your grade.

1. Quizzes (20%); Periodic in-class activities and/or quizzes. Quizzes will be true or false and multiple choice. Activities may require small teams of students to brain storm and present their ideas to the class.

1. Individual Assignments (30%)

1. Career report (2 pages single spaced 15%). Interview a person who works in sports marketing. They can be associated with a college or university, professional sports, private company, or other organization that manages a sports marketing portfolio. Investigate and write about the person’s education, career path, current job responsibilities, their impression on where the future opportunities are for a career in sports marketing.

2. Emerging trend report (2 pages, single spaced, 15%). Identify an emerging trend in sports marketing (AI, Web3, NFT, VR) and review at least three articles related to the trend and report on how the trend is used as a marketing tool by a sports property (e.g. team, league, event, product): How is it used to identify the properties market? How does it engage the customer? How does it contribute to revenue?

1. Marketing Plan (40%)—Group Project
2. Develop a marketing plan for an agreed upon entity. You can select a real entity, or you can make on up. Your marketing plan should;
   1. Describe the product you are marketing
   2. Detail the demographic of your Intended market segment
   3. Articulate the vision you have for your entity
   4. Stated goal of your plan (e.g. sell tickets, elevate brand awareness, introduce a product to market)
   5. Include three strategies
   6. List three objectives in support of the strategy
   7. Include action times in support of each objective
   8. Plan should include the use of at least one emerging trend
   9. The plan should draw upon class discussions
3. Work product:
   1. 20 minute classroom presentation using power point or another presentation application.
