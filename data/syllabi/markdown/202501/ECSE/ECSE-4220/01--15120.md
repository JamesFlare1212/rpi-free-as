## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

No Course Learning Outcomes are available for this course.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

1. an ability to design complimentary static and dynamic combinational circuits structures and determine the optimum transistor sizing
2. an ability to draw a compact layout for CMOS circuits
3. an ability to use commercial CAD tool Cadence for simulation and parasitic extraction for accurate modeling
4. an ability to analyze performance & timing and compare different circuit structures for combinational logic, latches and flip-flops in both static and dynamic logic

## Policies

### Other Course Policies:

**Course Policies:**

- There is no make-up for the midterm exam. Students who have officially documented evidence that they missed the exam for legitimate reasons will have the grade of final exam substituted for the missed midterm.

Students who miss the midterm without officially documented evidence that they were under a doctor’s care or had another legitimate reason will have a score of seventy percent of the grade of final exam substituted for the missed exam.

- If you feel a mistake has been made in grading your midterm or assignments, resubmit the exam or assignments within 2 class sessions of the time the exam or assignments was returned to you. Otherwise, NO re-grading will be performed.
- Homework submitted more than 7 days late receives no credit. Homework missing the 11:59pm submission deadline but less than 7 days late will be penalized -50% (half credit). Late Project reports will have 10 points deducted for each day late. It may be possible to pass the course without turning in Project 2, but it is highly unlikely.
