## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course introduces basic concepts of power systems and prepares students for other courses in the power area. Topics covered in the course include electromechanical and electromagnetic energy conversion; solid-state power conversion and power electronics; power generation from conventional and renewable sources; AC and DC power transmission and distribution, as well as the use of electricity in various forms. Students also learn AC circuit theory and use it to analyze power system operation in a steady state.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The course aims to equip students with an understanding of major components, principle of operation, and emerging trends of modern power systems, as well as the ability to analyze power system operation in steady state

## Course Content

### Content Details:

- Basic concepts, introduction to AC systems
- Electromagnetic energy conversion and transformers
- Electromechanical energy conversion and machines
- Solid-state power conversion and power electronics
- Power transmission and distribution
- Wind and solar energy and energy storage systems

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- CLO1 - Perform computations and carry out basic analysis of AC circuits in steady state, including single-phase and three-phase.
- CLO2 - Describe, derive and calculate phasors, phase angle, active and reactive power, power factor, harmonics and other metrics for AC circuit analysis.
- CLO3 - Interpret fundamental principles and applications of electromagnetic energy conversion and solid-state power conversion.
- CLO4 - Identify past and emerging trends in the development of energy systems.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Homeworks to measure CLO1, CLO2, and CLO3;  
Report (survey paper) to measure CLO4;  
Midterm exam 1 to measure CLO1 and CLO2;  
Midterm exam 2 to measure CLO3;  
Final exam to measure all CLOs.
