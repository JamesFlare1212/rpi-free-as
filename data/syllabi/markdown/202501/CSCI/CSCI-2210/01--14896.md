## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course is intended for undergraduate students in their second year (Sophomore students) in the Computer Science Department. In this class, students will delve into the essential building blocks of machine learning, focusing on topics in linear algebra, vector and matrix analysis, vector calculus, and probability and stochastic. These mathematical foundations are the bedrock upon which machine learning is constructed. By mastering these fundamental concepts, students will gain a powerful understanding of machine learning algorithms and predictive modeling. Moreover, this course is on track to becoming a prerequisite for all machine learning classes in the Computer Science department.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

- Provide students with a solid foundation in essential mathematical concepts relevant to machine learning, including linear algebra, vector calculus, and probability and stochastic.
- Establish a clear connection between mathematical principles and their applications in basic machine learning models, emphasizing how these mathematical foundations form the basis of various algorithms and predictive modeling techniques.
- Ensure students comprehend the core components of machine learning - data, models, and learning algorithms - and understand their interactions and significance in designing and evaluating predictive models.
- Foster critical thinking skills by encouraging students to evaluate and compare different machine learning algorithms based on their mathematical underpinnings.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understand and apply fundamental linear algebra concepts, including vectors, matrices, matrix operations and decomposition, eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and their relevance to machine learning algorithms.
- Introduction of vector calculus concepts, including gradients of functions of vectors and matrices, highlighting their relevance in optimization techniques used in basic machine learning models.
- Understand basic concepts of probability theory with a focus on continuous probabilities and apply them to elementary machine learning models.
- Connect foundational mathematical concepts to the components of machine learning - data, models, and learning algorithms - at an introductory level, emphasizing their basic functionalities and relationships.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

- Pop-up quiz: A short assessment in the class with 4-5 questions of multiple-choice
- Homework: Each homework due date will be listed when published

- Final exam
