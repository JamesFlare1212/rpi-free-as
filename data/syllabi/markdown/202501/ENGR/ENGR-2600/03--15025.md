## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Appreciation and understanding of uncertainties and the conditions under which they occur, within the context of the engineering problem-solving pedagogy of measurements, models, validation, and analysis. Problems and concerns in obtaining measurements; tabular and graphical organization of data to minimize misinformation and maximize information; and development and evaluation of models. Concepts will be supported with computer demonstration. Applications to problems in engineering are emphasized.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Uwe Kruger & Xun Wang. Modeling and Analysis of Uncertainty - A Theoretical Foundation and Practical Guide to Engineering Probability, Statistics and Regression Analysis. ISBN: 978-1-60797-993-7. Linus Learning, 2023.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course teaches students

- An appreciation for uncertainty in the application of engineering models;
- The difference between probability and statistics;
- Discrete and continuous distribution functions;
- Marginal and conditional distributions;
- Descriptive statistics;
- Sample distributions and point estimates;
- Confidence intervals;
- Hypothesis testing.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Students will be able to:

- Solve for the probability and conditional probability of events in various scenarios;
- Obtain and graph information about random variables and variable sets;
- Identify variable distributions correctly and apply various discrete and continuous distribution functions;
- Construct and interpret one- sample and two- sample confidence intervals;
- Formulate hypotheses tests and perform the tests using the method of critical region, p-value, and confidence interval.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

- **Tests and final exam (80%):** There are four tests on Wednesdays (10 – 11:30 am) and a final exam. Each of the four tests is 1.5 hours long and counts for 15%. All tests are cumulative but with emphasis on the material since the last test. The final exam includes the entire semester and counts for 20%. They are all close-book and in-person. Relevant formulas and statistical tables will be provided. The lowest test grade can be replaced by the final exam grade if higher, given you have **at least three tests** taken in the semester.

One of the essential skills for an engineer is being able to clearly communicate their ideas and process in problem solving.
