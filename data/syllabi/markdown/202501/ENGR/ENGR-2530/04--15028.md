## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Concept of stress and strain, generalized Hooke’s Law, axial load, torsion, pure bending, transverse loading, transformation of stress and strain components in 2D, design of beams and shafts for strength, deflection of beams, work and energy, columns.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\) Evaluate normal and shear stress, strain, principal stresses and directions for simple problems.
- 2\) Apply basic mechanics of materials concepts and properties, such as yield strength, ultimate strength, and failure criteria.
- 3\) Predict the expected magnitude and location of maximum stresses in beam structures subjected to simple loads (axial, torsion, bending).
- 4\) Calculate simple beam deflections.
- 5\) Apply course material in the solution of short, well-defined problems in a professional manner.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Homework:** Twelve homework sets will be assigned per the Course Schedule in LMS at least one week ahead of their due date. Solutions must be hand written (using a tablet is allowed) and follow the Problem Solving Format (see document). Homework is due by the end of the deadline day, and must be submitted in Gradescope. Homework submitted within 24 hours after the deadline will receive a 10% penalty and within 48 hours of the deadline, a 20% penalty, and so on (10% per day) until one week following the deadline when the homework solutions will be posted, and late homeworks will no longer be accepted. Only contact your instructor with a valid excuse (illness, family emergency, etc.) that prevents you from completing your homework on time and appropriate allowances will be arranged.

**Exams:** The two midterm exams on the Course Schedule and one final exam (scheduled by the Registrar) are mandatory. Make-up exams are only provided for absences with valid excuses (illness, family emergency, etc.). Accommodation will be provided according to the memos received from Disability Services. Grade challenges will be considered upon receipt of a written description of grading issue, due one week following return of graded exams. Missing a midterm exam and its subsequent make-up will result in an assigned score for that midterm exam based on your performance on the two other exams. Missing both midterm exams and their make-up is not allowed and will result in failing the course.

**Final Exam:** The comprehensive final examination will be given at a time scheduled by the Registrar, during the finals period. The final is ***mandatory***, and students are advised not to make travel plans until the Registrar releases the preliminary final exam schedule.

The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these.
