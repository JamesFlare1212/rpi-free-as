## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Overview: In this topic course, we will consider model order reduction (MOR) techniques for large or complex systems. Though the data involved/measured in such systems, e.g., images or physical quantities, often have high dimensionality, they can exhibit dominant patterns which can be characterized by low-dimensional structures. With MOR techniques, one aims to extract low dimensional representations of the data, or to design reduced-order models to efficiently simulate complex systems in a many-query or real-time setting (e.g. optimal design and inverse problems).

## Course Content

### Content Details:

0. Contents: Starting from a review for concepts in linear algebra, especially the Singular Value Decomposition (SVD), the course will mainly focus on the fundamentals of the mathematical and algorithmic aspects of data-driven or computation-based MOR strategies, e.g., Proper Orthogonal Decomposition (POD), Reduced Basis Methods (RBMs), and (adaptive) low-rank algorithms. Other techniques may be discussed to address nonlinearity or learning-based nonlinear reduction strategies
