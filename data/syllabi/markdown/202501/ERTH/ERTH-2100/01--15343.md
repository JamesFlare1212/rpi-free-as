## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

No Course Learning Outcomes are available for this course.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Having successfully completed this course, students will be able to:

- Discuss Earth’s internal structure and summarize observations that constrain Earth’s structure and evolution;
- Apply geophysical methods to study the structure of the Earth;
- Review processes that shape the Earth and apply geophysical methods to study those processes;
- Discuss the theory of plate tectonics and summarize the observations that inform our understanding of the processes that drive plate motions.
