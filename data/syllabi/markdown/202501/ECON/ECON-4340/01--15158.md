## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course describes how individuals make financial decisions, and how those decisions might deviate from those predicted by traditional financial or economic theory. Throughout the semester we will examine how the insights of behavioral economics complement the traditional finance paradigm by introducing some of the main psychological biases in financial decision-making and examining the impacts of these biases in financial and other economic decisions. This course will also introduce students to behavioral and experimental methodologies used in finance, economics and other disciplines.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- By the end of this course, students, in their written and verbal assessments, will demonstrate proficiency in identifying some of the main behavioral financial economic concepts (i.e. psychological biases) in economic decision-making.
- By the end of this course, students, in their written and verbal assessments, will demonstrate proficiency in assessing data and methods, and current research on how these biases impact financial and economic decisions.
- By the end of this course, students, in their written and verbal assessments, will demonstrate proficiency in knowledge of how individuals make financial and economic decisions and how these differ from rational behavior.
- By the end of this course, students, in their written and verbal assessments, will demonstrate proficiency in policies and other ways to improve individuals’ financial decision-making.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

***General Participation:***

- In general, you are expected to join all lectures and be an active participant. If you cannot join lectures for any reason, you are expected to communicate this to me as soon as possible. I expect you to participate in in-class activities such as MobLab, participate in and contribute to in-class discussions (by asking questions, providing answers or comments etc.), and to have read the materials that will be assigned.
- I will keep track of each student’s active engagement weekly. I will determine your final grade based on the quality of in-class participation and general classroom citizenship.
- Please bring your laptops. However, you are expected to have all your electronics put away until they are needed for class. If you need your electronic device to take notes etc., you must obtain my prior approval.

***Leading Paper Discussion:***

- During each of the paper discussion days, we will discuss two papers in class. Each paper discussion will be led by 1-3 students (i.e. leaders) (depending on the class size). These papers will be chosen from a list of papers (provided by me) by discussion leaders.
- Discussion leaders will read their assigned paper very carefully and lead a discussion of the paper in class. They will come to class prepared with slides and a list of questions that you want to ask to class that will guide the discussion of the paper.
- In addition to their assigned paper, leaders are responsible to read the introduction of the other paper to be discussed and participate in the discussion.

***Grades:*** Your grade will be determined based on i) how well you understood the paper, and ii) your ability to foster an active and engaging discussion environment.

***Discussion Participation:***

- During each of the paper presentation days, we will discuss two papers in class. Half of the class will be assigned to one paper and the other half will be assigned to the other one. You will be responsible for reading your assigned paper very carefully. Additionally, you are also expected to read the introduction section of the other paper.
- Whether it is your assigned paper or not, all students (including leaders) are expected to participate in the discussion of both papers. Students are expected take an active role by asking leaders questions, sharing their thoughts, and making comments.

***Grades:*** Your grade will be determined by the quality of your participation during the paper discussions. I will give you a grade at the end of each discussion day using the following table. Your final grade will be the average of these grades.

| **95-100** | You participated substantially in the discussion of both papers and asked thoughtful questions in each discussion |
| --- | --- |
| **90-94** | You participated substantially in one of the discussions and participated some in the other one |
| **80-89** | You participated in one of the discussions, but you did not provide any meaningful contributions to the other one, **OR, y**ou participated some in both discussions |
| **75-80** | Your participation was minimal |
| **70** | You were present in class but did not contribute anything to the discussions |
| **0** | You did not come to class without an approved excuse |

***Quizzes:***

- At the end of each paper presentation, we will randomly determine whether there will be a quiz (with a 50% chance). Each quiz will be about 5 min long and contain one or two questions about the paper presented and its in-class discussion.
