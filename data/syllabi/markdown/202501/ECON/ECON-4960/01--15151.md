### Additional Information for Course Information Section:

This course applies economic analysis to explore the intersection of energy systems and societal values, investigating global energy consumption, climate change impacts, energy poverty and inequality, and strategies for decarbonization. We will explore the dynamics of nonrenewable and renewable energy markets, the implications of market failures, the role of policy interventions, and the evaluation of energy policies through cost-benefit analyses.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Participate in meaningful and respectful discussions on topics in energy policy.
- Recognize and explain market failures that warrant energy policy interventions.
- Describe the economic and societal impacts of using markets to supply and allocate energy.
- Develop the skills to evaluate the benefits and costs of different energy policies.

### **Grade Component Descriptions**

#### **Participation (20%)**

Participation is a significant part of the final grade. Students must attend class regularly and come **fully prepared** to discuss the assigned readings, which will be available as PDFs on the LMS website. Participation points are earned by:

1. Completing in-class reading questions each week (10%)
2. Actively participating in reading discussions (5%)
3. Submitting in-class worksheets on analytical problems (5%)

If a student cannot attend class, they should email in advance.

#### **Homework Assignments (15%)**

There will be **four** homework assignments throughout the course, each worth **3.75%** of the total grade.

#### **Open-Notes Exams (30%)**

The course includes **two non-cumulative exams** consisting of short and long response questions. Students may bring their own class notes but **cannot** reference lecture slides, textbooks, assigned readings, or use the Internet. Well-organized class notes will be helpful for success on the exams.

The class immediately before each exam will be reserved for review. **There will be no make-up exams**—missing an exam results in a grade of zero.

#### **Policy Paper (25%)**

Each student will write a **1500-word paper** on an energy policy issue of their choice. The paper must define the policy, analyze its background, objectives, and impact, and provide a critical evaluation of its strengths and weaknesses. The following deadlines apply:

- **March 14, 2024 (12:00 PM):** Submit a one-paragraph summary of the chosen topic
- **March 28, 2024 (12:00 PM):** Submit a first draft or a detailed outline
- **April 15, 2024 (12:00 PM):** Submit the final draft

#### **Policy Paper Presentation (10%)**

Towards the end of the course, students will deliver a **20-minute presentation** based on their policy paper. Visual aids are encouraged to enhance engagement. **Slides must be submitted by 10:00 AM on the presentation day.**

The presentation will be followed by a **Q&A session**, where students must answer questions and discuss their topic with classmates. Attending all presentations and participating in discussions will contribute to the participation grade.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Regular attendance is expected. In-class lecture notes will cover detailed course materials that are closely related to homework and exams. Office hours are not for reproduction of the lectures.  
Please be aware that if you need to miss a class due to an extracurricular or athletic event, it is your responsibility to inform me in advance and submit any assignments early if needed. Please let me know as soon as possible if you anticipate missing class due to a university-related commitment.

### Other Course Policies:

**Electronics**: You are expected to attend all classes and be an active participant in class discussions. As such, please put away your electronic devices until they are needed for class. All your devices must be put on silent mode.  
**Late Work**:

Late homework submissions will be penalized according to the following formula:

**Grade = max{0, (Assignment Grade) × (1 - 0.2 × Days Late)}**

**Missed Exams**: Students are required to take all exams. Any student without a University approved excuse will receive a zero on the missed exam.

- If there is a University approved absence for the midterm exam, final exam will carry the total weight of both exams.
- If there is a University approved absence for the final exam, there will be a make-up final exam.
- No other excuses besides University approved absences qualify for this policy.

**Grade Appeals:** If you believe a grade is incorrect, you have 2 days to appeal. Email me from your RPI address and specify  
(a) the assignment,  
(b) the grade you received,  
(c) why the appeal is justified.
