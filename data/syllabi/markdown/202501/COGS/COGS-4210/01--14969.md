## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Computational models allow us to instantiate assumptions about cognitive mechanisms and information processing as randomized computer programs. This course will take a simulation-based approach to cognitive modeling and introduce participants to formal workflows for model development, estimation, and criticism. It will cover a broad range of existing model families and encourage participants to build their own models. Along the way, participants will learn to write their own Python modules for Monte Carlo simulation and fully Bayesian analysis.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

The course is fully self-contained, featuring a series of research and opinion articles on the topics of cognitive modeling, statistical inference, and neuroscience.
