## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course is an advanced linguistics class focusing specifically on the topic of Psycholinguistics. Psycholinguistics rests uniquely at the conjunction of cognitive psychology and linguistics, making it a specifically-great fit for a cognitive science approach. The course will approach many topics within psycholinguistics, including but not limited to sentence comprehension, language production, speech perception, and lexical access. The course will cover both classic approaches and modern trends in psycholinguistics

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Develop an understanding of the field of psycholinguistics, including how it relates to both linguistics and cognitive science
- Build skills reading, analyzing, and discussing academic journal articles
- Deliver a presentation to the rest of the class effectively communicating information about a topic within psycholinguistics
- Conduct a psycholinguistic experiment, including designing the experiment, collecting and analyzing data, and writing up the results in the form of a paper.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Presentation – 20% Project – 60% Participation – 20%

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Every student will lead the instruction of one topic in the form of a ~20 minute presentation. The project will be due at the end of the semester but there will be assignment-like stages throughout the semester; the project will be to conduct a psycholinguistic experiment – this will include setting up the experiment, collecting and analyzing data, and writing up the results in the form of a paper. Participation will come from in-class discussion and pre-discussion posts on LMS.
