## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course is an introduction to passenger transportation systems, specifically urban transit systems. The topics covered will be design, operation and evaluation of transit systems using US and foreign examples. The course topics will also include policy and regulatory assessment of urban transit systems.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

No required textbooks. The instructor will make available several free publications from The United States Department of Transportation, The Transportation Research Board and the World Bank.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students should be able to critically assess the capacity and quality of passenger transportation systems
- Students should be able to determine the appropriate types of transit to serve a specific market
- Students should be able to design a transportation system of a specific quality and capacity
- Students should be able to understand the economic issues associated with transit prices
- Students should understand the relationship between transit service, transit demand and land use
- Students should understand and appreciate the differences in transit services throughout the world.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Exams:**

There will be two examinations in this class. There will NOT be a final exam during finals week. The exams will all be IN-CLASS, CLOSED-BOOK, CLOSED-NOTES. Make-up exams will not be offered except under extraordinary circumstances.

**Papers and Projects:**

There will be a writing and analysis project during the semester.

Paper or Project or analysis **of the student’s choice**. Students are to write a paper of their choice in the field of passenger transportation. It can be a research paper, an assessment of data or some analysis of a transportation project or policy. I expect this to be written by a single student.

This assignment will be due on the last day of class. **Students should have a one-page outline illustrating that they have the resources to accomplish the project by February 15.** Students will keep the instructor advised of progress at least every two weeks by personal meeting.

The paper should relate to course content and include and your personal analysis. A narrow but deep paper is preferred to a broad and shallow paper.
