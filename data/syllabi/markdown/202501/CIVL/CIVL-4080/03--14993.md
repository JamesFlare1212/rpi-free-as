## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Analysis and design of reinforced concrete structures using ultimate strength methods. Design of beams, columns, slabs, and footings. Development and anchorage of reinforcing bars. Laboratory testing of hardened concrete mechanical properties.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Text: “Design of Concrete Structures”, 16th Edition, D. Darwin, C.W. Dolan  
Design Standard: “Building Code Requirements for Structural Concrete” ACI 318-19

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Students are expected to attend all classes. If a student does not attend class, it is the responsibility of the student to notify the instructor in advance and to provide justification. Every student is allowed a maximum of two automatically-excused absences for medical reasons, as long as the instructor was notified in advance and justification was provided. Students missing more than two classes due to illness are required to provide a doctor’s note or other supporting documentation to the Student Success Office. Their Class Dean will communicate the receipt of the note (with expected end date) to the instructor. For all absences that are not due to medical reasons, students can request an excused absence from the instructor by providing a valid reason and supporting documentation to the instructor. In such cases, and based on the provided information, the instructor will determine whether the student will be excused from class.

### Other Course Policies:

**Late/Excused Assignment Submission Policy:**

All students are expected to submit all assignments (i.e., in-class problems, homework, lab reports, and prelabs) by the specified due date. If a student was excused from class, the corresponding in-class problem will not count toward their final score. There are no make-ups for in-class problems.

Excusal from class does not automatically excuse students from submitting homework/lab reports/prelabs. If a student cannot submit a particular assignment by the associated due date, it is the responsibility of the student to notify the instructor in advance and to request an extension. Based on the provided information, the instructor will determine if such an extension will be allowed.

For each type of assignment other than in-class problems (e.g., homework), students are allowed one late submission without a grade penalty. For any subsequent late submissions of assignments of the same type, the maximum points that can be earned in each late assignment is reduced by 10% of its originally possible points for every day past the due date that the assignment has not been submitted. As an example, if an assignment was submitted 8 days past its due date, the maximum points that can be earned in that assignment are now 20% of its originally possible points, even if all parts of the late assignment are correct.

**Missed Exam Policy:**

Midterm exams are scheduled by the instructor and all students are expected to take such exams. A student who misses a midterm exam could take a make-up exam only if they notify the instructor and provide justification along with supporting documentation. Based on the provided information, the instructor will determine if the student will be allowed to take a make-up midterm exam.

The final exam is scheduled by the Registrar and all students are expected to take the final exam. If a valid reason exists that prevents a student from taking the final exam, it is the responsibility of the student to submit a request to make up the final exam to their Class Dean. If their Class Dean approves their request, the grade of the student for the course would then become NE (Not Examined) and they would have to take a make-up final exam next semester by a specified date.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work, although students may compare solutions before submitting individual assignments. Submission of any assignment that is in violation of this policy (e.g., one student copying work done by another student) will result in a grade of zero for that particular assignment for both students. Also, cheating on an exam will result in a grade of zero for that exam and, in accordance with the *Handbook*, notification to the Dean of Students Office of the incident of academic dishonesty.
