## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Design of systems to consider foundations, structures and constructability; foundation  
alternatives; structural design to simplify erection; prefabrication, modulation of structures;  
material handling on a construction site; crane selection and placement; temporary works.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Temporary Structures in Construction (2012) by Ratay, R. Third Edition.  
McGraw-Hill Press. ISBN: 978-0071753074

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Estimate gravity and environmental loads on temporary and permanent structures
- 2\. Design temporary works such as bracing, shoring, formwork, etc.
- 3\. Design earth retaining structures for temporary work
- 4\. Determine crane placement, selection, and lifting capacity for mobile & tower crane systems
- 5\. Perform lifting & rigging analyses for the placement of structures
- 6\. Read contract and shop drawings
- 7\. Communicate constructability concepts as they apply to building and civil construction

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

It is expected that there will be approximately 6 assignments during the semester. Students will

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work.
