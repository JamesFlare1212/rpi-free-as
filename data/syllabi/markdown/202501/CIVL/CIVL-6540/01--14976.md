## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Basics of dynamic response of soil and soil-foundation systems, including applications to earthquake engineering and machine foundations. Systems  
studies include shallow and deep foundations, buried structures, earth structures, slopes, and earthquake site response.  
This is a graduate course addressed especially to geotechnical and structural engineering students.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Book: R. Dobry M. Vucetic, and M. Zeghal, Dynamics of Soil and Soil Foundations Systems (in preparation and not commercially available).

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The course covers:  
Cyclic Soil Properties  
Dynamic Response of One- and Two-Degree-of-Freedom Systems  
1D, 2D and 3D Wave Propagation  
Dynamic Response of Shallow Foundations  
Dynamic Response of Deep Foundations

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

- Quantify the dynamic properties of soil systems.
- Analyze and solve problems of the dynamic response of one- and multi-degree-of-freedom systems.
- Analyze and solve problems of wave propagation in soil media.
- Analyze and solve problems of the dynamic response of foundations.
