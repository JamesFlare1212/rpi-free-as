### Prerequisites or Other Requirements:

CIVL 4070 Steel Design (or equivalent) using AISC LRFD design. You are expected to be familiar with the following:

- Tension Members (capacity, net effective section, etc.)
- Compression Members (local buckling, effective length factor, etc.)
- Flexural Members (compact section, unbraced length of compression flange, shear, deflection, etc.)
- Compression / Flexural Members (i.e., “beam-columns”)
- Bolts (direct and eccentric shear loading)
- Welds (direct and eccentric shear loading)

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Students will gain an understanding of and will perform structural calculations relating to the following topics:

- Column grid layout
- Bar joists, metal deck, and concrete slabs on metal deck
- Composite beam design
- Floor vibrations due to human activities
- Bolting and welding
- Simple connections
- Fixed connections
- Cover plates on beams
- Plate girders
- Torsion
- Openings in Webs of Beams
- Base plates (including shear lugs) and anchor rods
- Lateral-load-resisting systems
- Archaic and historic construction materials and techniques

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Perform structural calculations related to the design of structural steel framed building.
- Communicate to colleagues the technical merits of a topic clearly using professional terminology.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

homeworks, exams, presentations related to trends and current topics and structural engineering
