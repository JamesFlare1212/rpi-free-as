## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is a 4-unit junior-level statistical analysis course. The primary objective of this course is to introduce various statistical topics and learn to solve statistical problems using R. These topics include basic concepts such as sampling distribution, point estimation, confidence intervals, and hypothesis testing; regression methods such as simple and multiple regression, selection procedures, regression diagnostics, residual analysis, stepwise regression, and analysis of variance. This course will make extensive use of statistical software, R. (4.0 credits)

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Attendance is necessary for success in this course, which will take 5% of the end-of-semester grade. There are three times of no excused absences granted for each student. It is mainly designed to accommodate those emergent personal issues that do not leave enough time to receive official approval. Beyond the no excused absences, missing classes are only allowed if there is an official excuse. Any absence beyond the three times of no excused absences and without an official excuse will cause a 0.5 penalty on the end-of-semester grade.

### Other Course Policies:

**Excusal from Course Assignments and Examinations:** If you know ahead of time that you will miss an exam/assignment deadline, then you must make arrangements before the assessment is due. Failure to do so will result in 25% off for each 24-hour period the assessment is late.

If an emergency arises and a student cannot submit assigned work on or before the scheduled due date or cannot take an exam on the scheduled date, the student (or the Student Experience Office) MUST give notification to the instructor NO MORE THAN 48 HOURS AFTER the scheduled date. If you have an emergency, the campus is here to help, please coordinate with the Student Experience Office – 4th floor of Academy Hall, x8022, se@rpi.edu

**Re-Grading Policy:** Document in writing which part you want re-graded and why, and turn in this document with your original assignment. You will be given one chance (per assignment) to request a re-grade. No re-grade will be accepted more than one week after an exam or an assignment has been returned to the student. Re-grades made after this period has elapsed will NOT be considered.

**End-of-semester grades:** End-of-semester grades are not given. Instead, end-of-semester grades are calculated according to a student’s performance on the individual assessment requirements and the weights in this syllabus. Final grades are final. Therefore, final grades will not be changed unless a numerical error is made in computing your final grade. After final grades have been calculated, I will not respond to emails received requesting a grade change unless it is in response to a numerical calculation error. Please do not ask for extra credit or extra work after final exams have been administered.
