## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course deals with the design of experiments concepts; how to design single and multiple factors experiments; statistical analysis of the data from experiments including the application of analysis of variance (ANOVA), full factorial design, fractional factorial design, factor screening, and related statistical methods. The goals are to learn how to plan, design, and conduct experiments efficiently and effectively and learn how to analyze the resulting data to obtain objective conclusions.  
Experimental design and analysis and different DOE applications in the industry including cases and examples are investigated. This course is intended for students who have completed the first course in statistical methods. This background course should include at least some techniques of descriptive statistics, standard sampling distributions, and an introduction to basic concepts of the confidence interval and hypothesis testing for means and variances.

**c. Grading scheme**

**c.0** **In-class presentation (Group activity):** You are required to deliver a 10-minute in-class presentation during the semester on a topic related to the course content.

**c.1 Participation points (Individual activity)**

- Evaluation will be based on active participation and the caliber of responses provided.

**c.2 Assignments (Individual activity) + DOE Labs (Group activity)**

- Throughout the semester exploratory assessment activities will be assigned to reinforce or expand on material that is covered in the lecture.
- Four to six homework sets will be assigned.
- There will be two labs during the semester, and attendance is mandatory in order to submit the corresponding lab reports.

- The project can be done individually or in a group of two undergraduate students. **Graduate students should work individually on their projects.**

**c.4 Article Assignments (individual activity for graduate students only)**

- You are required to write a reflection paper on one or more articles. Three to four articles will be assigned, and data will be provided for analysis based on the assigned articles.
