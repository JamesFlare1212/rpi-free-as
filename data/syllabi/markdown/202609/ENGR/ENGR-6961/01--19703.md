## Course Goals

### Goals:

This pilot, introductory course, co-taught with a team of leading industry experts, will provide insight into key fabrication technologies that enable the semiconductor manufacturing to produce integrated circuits with more functionality, less power consumption at a similar cost as before.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Semiconductor manufacturing is a highly interdisciplinary endeavor, drawing on many disciplines including Chemical, Electrical, Material, Mechanical, and Industrial Engineering, plus Chemistry, Physics, and Computer Science. This pilot, introductory course, co-taught with a team of leading industry experts from multiple semiconductor companies, will introduce, and provide deep insight into, the technological advances in Materials, Metrology, Equipment, Automation, Data Analytics and Infrastructures that enable the semiconductor manufacturing to produce computer chips with astonishing increase in performance and functionality and reduction in energy, size and cost. The course topics were selected together with a group of technical leaders as the semiconductor industry has high demand for engineers in these topic areas. The course is targeted towards advanced undergraduates and early graduate students who are interested in exploring career opportunities in the semiconductor industry. Students will have the opportunities to engage with semiconductor experts representing the full industry ecosystem.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Explain the fabrication processes and technologies for state-of-the-art computer chips, as well as the issues and solutions related to the fabrication yield/failures and the reliability of the chips.
- Demonstrate an ability to understand several aspects of computer chip design for manufacturing, including those related to memory, high power, radio frequency and silicon photonics
- Carry out research on selected topics, and gain experience on technical presentation and report.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

- Pre-lecture questionnaire (PLQ) for students to get prepared for the lecture.
- Homework assignments in the form of multiple choices and short answers to help students review and learn the lecture material.
- Team project reports/presentations; one submission per team. Students will present their projects during the class hours, so they will learn from peers.
  1. Undergraduate project may be based on lecture topics and/or related literature to summarize the key learning, provide feedback or questions, identify the critical innovations, and figure out the future challenges, as well as the potential solutions.
  2. Extra requirement for graduate project is that it should be at a level of graduate research with in-depth analysis on chosen research papers published in top conferences/journals.

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).
