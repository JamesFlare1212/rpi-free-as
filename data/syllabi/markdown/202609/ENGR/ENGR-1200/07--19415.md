## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is an undergraduate introductory course covering techniques for creating solid models of engineering designs. Topics include three-dimensional parametric modeling of parts, assemblies, visualization of three-dimensional space, orthographic and isometric hand-sketching and computer-generated design documentation.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

No Textbook Required

## Course Goals

### Goals:

1. Students should gain introductory skills necessary to create solid model designs using a parametric modeler.
2. Students should be able to translate conceptual designs to virtual models by understanding 2-D & 3-D spatial relationships of basic geometry.
3. Students will be able to document their engineering designs using a 2-D drafting application.
4. Students will demonstrate orthographic and axonometric view hand-sketching techniques to aid in 3-D visualization.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Understanding 3-D and 2-D geometry creation.
- 2\. Standard documentation practices. Translating 3-D models to 2-D drawings.
- 3\. Creation and documentation of a basic assembly with motion.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

1. Attendance in sessions is mandatory to receive staff assistance and an in-class evaluation/grade for assignments.
2. **IN-LAB assignments require live attendance unless an official excuse is given.**
3. Students absent from the IN-LAB **with** an **official excuse** will need to e-mail the coordinator for approval to make up the in-lab assignment via Office Hours (see Due Date & Extension policy below).

### Other Course Policies:

In-Lab Assignments (ILA):

1. The ILA is a timed assignment that will be shown on the classroom display for a limited duration of the class period. The assignment will focus on knowledge of the most recent three sessions of material (including the current session) but may also require any prior session’s material.
2. **Attendance is mandatory for ILA sessions**.
3. The ILA start/end time is at the discretion of the Lead TA.
4. The ILA is due to be uploaded to the student’s BOX folder by the time specified by the Lead TA.Students with **Extra Time Accommodations** should notify the Coordinator within a week of semester start, or registration, whichever comes later.
5. **ILA Files MUST BE NAMED** IN THE FOLLOWING FORMAT**:** “ILA_XX” and “ILA_XX_dwg” (where “XX” is the Session number, e.g., 03, 06, 09, 12)
6. Students are permitted to view content on LMS during an ILA.
7. Students **are prohibited** from using **any recording devices** to take video, photograph, or otherwise digitally capture the ILA shown on the screen.
8. Students seen doing this will forfeit the ILA and receive no grade. There is no chance of make-up.
9. Students **are prohibited** from giving/receiving help to/from any other student. A class warning will be issued first. Second offense will result in a zero grade.
10. Students **with** an **official excuse** will need to contact the coordinator for approval to make up the assignment and have that excuse emailed to the coordinator from an official entity (see Excusal policy).
11. Students without an official excuse will forfeit the in-lab assignment. No make-up.
12. Students **are prohibited** from **STARTING** an ILA outside of their course section time, except when permitted by the coordinator. Any violation will result in a non-grade.
