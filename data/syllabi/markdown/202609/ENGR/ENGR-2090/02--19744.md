## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Engineering Mechanics: Dynamics – Meriam, Kraige, and Bolton, 9th Edition, John Wiley and Sons.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

***Student Learning Outcomes***

Students who successfully complete this course shall be able to:

1. Master the basic principles of particle and rigid body dynamics
2. Systematically apply these principles to mechanical systems rising in diverse aspects of technology.
3. Solve the mathematical equations that govern these systems, and physically interpret the results.
4. Master vector algebra at a high level as a result of applying previous outcomes.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Kinematics of Particles and Rigid Bodies; Kinetics of Particles and Rigid-Bodies using Newton’s Second Law, Work-energy for Particles and Rigid Bodies; Impulse-momentum for Particles and Rigid bodies, Introduction to Three-Dimensional Kinetics of Rigid Bodies, Principal Moments

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Lecture Notes:**

Lecture notes will be posted in the form of slides in .pdf format prior to the scheduled start of lecture. These notes will be provided through LMS.

**Homework:**

Ten Homework sets will be assigned, which will usually consist of approximately 3-4 problems. The schedule of the homework due dates is included in the schedule at the end of this document, and LMS will also inform students of the due dates of homework sets. Homework assignments are to be scanned to the portable document format (.pdf) and must be submitted at the **beginning of class** on the dates given in the syllabus. **No late homework assignments will be accepted**.

You are encouraged to collaborate with other students on homework assignments, however, each student is expected to submit their own homework.

**In Class Assignments**

At the end of selected lecture days, an “in-class assignment” will be collected and graded. These problems will be given in class and a scheduled amount of time depending on the problem difficulty will be provided. The solutions will be collected (physical copies only!) at the end of the lecture period and graded. The lowest grade will be dropped at the end of the course.

**Project:**

There will be a project to complete during the semester. This project will be the creation of a PowerPoint Presentation demonstrating how to solve a Engineering Dynamics Problem. Detailed instructions on how to create this project will be provided on LMS. Note : No Oral presentations will be conducted. **Failure to submit an acceptable attempt of this project will result in, at a minimum, a reduction of one letter grade at the end of the term. You must attempt this project.**

**Exams:**

Three exams (closed book, closed notes), *including the Finals*, are scheduled on the course calendar. **Exams must be completed**. If you are in need of a makeup examination, you must contact the instructor for a makeup exam. If for *any reason, including excused absences*, more than one exam must be missed, an incomplete will be given for this class, and you will need to retake the course.

The final exam must be taken to receive a grade for this course. This final exam will be administered during the final scheduled class time. This final examination will be comprehensive, in that the material of this course will continually build upon itself during the semester.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

***Academic Integrity***

This statement is intended to cover most, but not all, forms of academic dishonesty. (You should refer to *The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities* for detailed information on Institute policy in this regard.)

**Homework:**

Collaboration among the students in the class is permitted and, in fact, encouraged, as described above. Seeking help from other sources is also encouraged. However, the work you submit should be your own. Evidence of you merely copying another student’s homework (or from the homework solutions) will be considered academic dishonesty and will result in **a zero grade for homework for the semester for all parties involved.**

**Exams:**

Collaboration during an exam (exchanging verbal or written information or copying) is, of course, prohibited. In addition, use of email or the internet during exams for any purpose is strictly prohibited. The use of Chegg, Yahoo! Answers, Quora, Discord, Twitter, Facebook, messaging programs of any kind, or other materials that are not assigned through the course are prohibited. You will be required to take and complete an integrity quiz on LMS prior to the start of exams, indicating that you accept the terms under which the exams are given.

**Unauthorized collaboration or compromise of any examination will result in investigations. All parties involved will receive an F on the course.**

**Other:**

Outrageous offenses such as arranging for a substitute to take an examination, attempting to obtain examination questions or solutions in advance, or attempting to change a grade record will result in a failing grade for the course and a recommendation to the Dean of Students that the student(s) in question be suspended.

If you have any questions concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification.
