## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Students will demonstrate familiarity with a variety of creative writing genres.
- • Students will demonstrate habitual engagement in creativity as a process, including analysis of models, brainstorming, trial and error, seeking feedback, and reflection/revision.
- • Students will explore, develop, and improve craft skills in the art of creative writing.
- • Students will demonstrate a consistent writing practice.
- • Students will practice close reading peer texts and engaging in productive self-reflection.
- • Students will demonstrate the intra and interpersonal skills necessary for positive participation in a creative community.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Attendance and Participation.** I expect that each of you contribute to discussions, in large and/or small groups, every class.

**Mission Statement & Final Reflection.** In weeks one and fifteen you will be writing a letter to help you reflect on your creativity, intentions, & learning

**Creative Practice Portfolios (3):** The project portfolio consists of 1) A draft of a creative writing project 2) A non-evaluative response to peer project(s) 3) A project reflection.

**Craft Checks:** There are 6 applied close reading activities to provide accountability for each unit’s reading. These cannot be made up. Lowest score is dropped  
**Revision Portfolio.** Rather than producing a major work at the end of the session, you will review your 3 creative projects and implement a revision to one of them based on your course learning and feedback sessions.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

This is a small seminar class, the success of which is entirely dependent on active student participation. Therefore, I expect all students to attend every class, and participate to the extent of their abilities. Every student can miss one class for whatever reason, no questions asked. Each subsequent absence will deduct ten points each from the student’s final grade (1%)**. 3 tardies/early departures = one absence. A student who exceeds four absences will automatically fail the course even if they are otherwise passing.**

### Other Course Policies:

## Late Work Policy

Late creative drafts are eligible for up to half credit (50%). Late peer response comments are eligible for up to 75% credit. Craft Checks cannot be made up.

## Final Grading Policy

Students are expected to monitor their grades throughout the semester via Blackboard’s grading feature, and I am happy to help you navigate that. If you have any questions about your grade at any time, please do not hesitate to respectfully inquire. All inquiries should be made as soon as possible and no later than the last day of class. Please review the late work policy; students may not submit late work to better a grade at the end of the semester. All grades on SIS are final.

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).
