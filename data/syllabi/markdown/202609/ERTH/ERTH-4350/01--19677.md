## Course Goals

### Goals:

• Instruct future scientists how to sustainably generate / collect and use data for their research as well as for others.  
• Instruct future technologists how to understand and support essential data and information needs of a wide variety of producers and consumers.  
• To develop students’ knowledge of the full life-cycle of data and relevant frameworks, technologies, standards, and best practices.  
• To train students in the methods, applications, and tools necessary to properly handle data and information.  
• To introduce students to methods for data analysis, data visualization, and the development and evaluation of predictive models.
