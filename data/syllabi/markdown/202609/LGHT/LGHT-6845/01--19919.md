## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course provides an overview of the influence of lighting conditions on people's visual capabilities, perceptions, moods, and alertness, with understanding derived from the structure and operating characteristics of the human visual system. The primary objective is to increase students’ understanding of how lighting affect people’s visual performance, comfort, and behavior and how this understanding can be utilized in lighting applications. A review of human factors topics related to architectural applications such as office and classroom lighting, retail and exhibition lighting, emergency lighting, and outdoor lighting is included.  
The course will consist of lectures, demonstrations, and lab workshops related to the course topic. The class schedule outlines topics and dates.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- At the conclusion of the course LGHT-4840, students will be able to explain how the visual system works.
- At the conclusion of the course LGHT-4840, students will be able to analyze the visual requirements of various visual tasks, and make and critique lighting recommendations
- At the conclusion of the course LGHT-4840, students will be able to recognize and predict lighting conditions likely to cause discomfort, generate specific impressions, and/or modify behavior.
- At the conclusion of the course LGHT-4840, students will be able to explain the basics of the human circadian system.
- In addition, at the conclusion of the course LGHT-6845, graduate students will be able to specify a stimulus presented to the visual system under various conditions
- In addition, at the conclusion of the course LGHT-6845, graduate students will be able to estimate the effects of different lighting conditions on people with different viewing capabilities
- In addition, at the conclusion of the course LGHT-6845, graduate students will be able to explain how light intensity, light spectrum, duration, timing and light exposure history affects the human circadian system

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

For the students in LGHT-4840, student assessment will be based upon a review of field assignments, critique assignments, laboratory assignments, quizzes and exam, and the degree to which the student participates in class discussions.  
In the critique assignment, students will review and critique a published recommendation for lighting in a specific application and suggest improvements. In the field assignment, teams will measure and assess lighting conditions in an office, classroom, home or other space for visual performance and comfort and prepare individually written reports on the findings. In the laboratory assignment, teams will carry out a simple human factors experiment and report on the results and implications for lighting practice. In the examinations, students will demonstrate their knowledge of course concepts by providing short answers to a broad range of questions. Class participation includes attending all class sessions and active involvement in team assignments.  
In addition to these, for the students in LGHT-6845 student assessment will include a written and oral report for a final class project. The objective of the final class project for students in LGHT-6845 is to provide practical experience in analyzing the relevant human factors aspects of a realistic lighting application. The final class project will require students to review and synthesize information from class and the literature to analyze a set of conditions in a lighting application and propose alternatives to improve visual and/or non-visual aspects of the application to the benefit of the end user in the space. The final class project will be assigned during the first half of the semester with a due date on or near the last day of classes.
