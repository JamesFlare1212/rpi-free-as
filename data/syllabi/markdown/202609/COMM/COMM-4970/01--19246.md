## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate an understanding of the basic principles of 2D animation and compositing as evidenced in class assignments.
- Develop an informed and discriminating eye for understanding sequence, pacing, timing and syncingin a time-based piece.
- Create a narrative with moving text, graphics, illustrations, photos, videos, and sound.
- Use research and data to inform and/ or support a position using animation.
- Develop a strong working knowledge of Adobe After Effects to create motion graphics.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Course projects:**

1. Kinetic Type
2. Animated Logo
3. Title Sequence
4. Data In Motion
5. Narrative

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

**Grading:**  
**60%:** Projects and assignments  
**20%:** Participation (engagement both in and out of class, during critiques and working hours)  
**20%:** Final project  
**Project Assessments:**  
**Aesthetics 25%:** Does the project have strong aesthetic qualities?  
**Conceptual Framework 25%:** Are the concepts or ideas behind the project strong, original, thoughtful, creative. How well did you use animation to enhance and express your concept.  
**Technical Skill 25%:** Does the project use techniques and skills that demonstrate a mastery of the skills and techniques addressed and/or go beyond the technical expectation? Is the production value precise.  
**Comp Work 25%:** Did you complete the progress work on the due date? How far did you take the ideas from one due date to the next. Did you push your explorations and make variations on the theme or explore multiple ideas?
