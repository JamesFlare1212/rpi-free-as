## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course introduces students to the practice of graphic design with a variety of hands-on projects. Studies include the principles and theories of two-dimensional design and the integration of form, color, type, image and content. Students will develop a heightened visual awareness and an understanding of how design influences meaning. The course emphasizes strategic and critical thinking in the solving of graphic design problems for the purposes of professional communication across a range of media platforms. In addition students will study the basic functions and integral properties in the following applications–Adobe Illustrator, InDesign, Photoshop and After Effects to create solutions for various graphic design projects.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understand and explain visual communication and graphic design concepts and terms
- Use design thinking, techniques and theories through guided projects and readings to visually solve communication problems
- Understand how design thinking and the utilization of graphic design principles can be applied to solving marketing, engineering and other design application problems
- Learn the basics of the following design software while creating documents for all class projects: Adobe Illustrator, Adobe InDesign, Adobe PhotoShop, Adobe After Effects
- Develop and demonstrate team working skills through group interaction, critique, problem solving and verbal expression

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

PROJECTS ASSIGNMENTS (75%- 5 Projects each worth 15% )  
– Aesthetics 25% Does the project have strong aesthetic and formal qualities?  
– Conceptual Framework 25% Are the concepts or ideas behind the project original, thoughtful?  
– Comp Work 25% How far did you take the work and explore the concept? How many comps or variations did you make? – Technical Skill 25% Does the project demonstrate a mastery of the skills and techniques addressed with good production?

Formal Focus + READINGS (20%)  
Formal Focus Paper (5%)

**Projects:**  
– Point Compositions (5%) A series of design studies to get students familiar with the theories behind using the point as a metaphor for graphic communication.

– Line Compositions (5%) A series of design studies using line to express emotion.

– Typography Compositions (5%) A series of studies introducing students to fundamentals of, and the materiality of typography.

– Texture, Form & Image (15%) Explores using the property of texture, other elements of form,& relationship between type & image.

– Music Event Poster (15%) Uses shape, line, point and typography to conceptually represent a musical genre.

– Logo Design (15%) Students explore the concept of identity and symbol making in a personal logo design.

– Motion Graphics (15%) Introduces students to the fundamental concepts of motion graphics and animating using After Effects.
