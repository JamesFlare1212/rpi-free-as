## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is a graduate level course. Given below are 1) Catalog description and 2) Additional description that is shared in class (not given in RPI catalog).  
***Lean Six Sigma I (catalog description)***  
Learn the fundamentals of lean and six sigma philosophies and gain experience with a toolkit to improve existing processes by applying them to relevant workplace process. The Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve and Control (DMAIC) improvement cycle is used, in which students learn and gain experience using multiple tools for each step of the improvement cycle, including (VOC), quality functional deployment (QFD), 5s, Kaizens, plan-do-check-act (PDCA).  
***Additional description shared in class: (not in RPI catalog)***  
Lean DFSS tools such as failure modes & effects analysis (FMEA), process mapping, value stream mapping (VSM), pugh matrix, 5 whys, fishbone diagram, design of experiments, ANOVA tools, Weibull statistics and its applications etc. are covered.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Lean Six Sigma i is the first of 3 courses which leads to a NYSED approved certificate "Lean Six Sigma Graduate Certificate". The other 2 courses are Lean Six Sigma II and Graduate Professional Project. Lean Six Sigma I and II cover the theory part of Lean Six Sigma, and Graduate professional project covers the practice of Lean Six Sigma tools and processes in an real life company or lab setting etc.  
Lean Six Sigma I covers lean and six sigma tools needed to learn and practice how to get an existing process to meet lean six sigma standards. DMAIC (Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve & Implement, and Control) is the process that students will learn and practice by working on a real project in their respective workplace.  
At the end of the semester, student will become proficient in using DMAIC process.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\) Students will learn and practice lean six sigma tools needed to use DMAIC (Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve & Implement and Control) process to bring existing real life processes such as manufacturing, technology services, other services etc., to lean six sigma standards.
- Students learn and practice using statistical packages used in industry such as Minitab or JMP or equivalent
- Students learn to use and practice software packages used in industry such as MS Visio and / or Pro Model and / or Matlab or equivalent etc.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Home work, Studio work and Class participation due dates may vary depending on when the tools and topics are covered, which as mentioned earlier may vary with cohort experience, familiarity of topics etc.  
1\) Home work assignments (anywhere from 15 to 20) to practice and learn lean six sigma tools - 30%  
2\) Present first iteration of Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve & Implement and Control (DMAIC) for individual / group project from their workplace - 25%  
3\) Final DMAIC presentation of project - 30%  
4\) Studio work and class participation - 10%  
5\) Attendance - 5%
