## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Coverage of several areas of interest in optimization, with proofs of some major results. Convex sets and functions. Optimality conditions in nonlinear optimization. Lagrangian duality. Linear, quadratic, and conic optimization. Convex relaxations of integer optimization problems. Stochastic optimization.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course is to introduce you to optimization theory, methods, and applications. The focus is on nonlinear optimization, with some discussion of linear and conic optimization as well as integer optimization.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

1. Convex sets and functions: (4 weeks)  
   Including: separation theorems, optimality conditions. Convex approximations to nonconvex functions.
2. Linear and conic optimization: (2 weeks)  
   Including: Duality and complementary slackness, and semidefinite programming. Considered in more detail in MATP 6640 in alternating spring semesters.
3. Optimality conditions in nonlinear programming: (2 weeks)  
   Including: Fritz John conditions, Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions, constraint qualifications.
4. Integer optimization: (1 week)  
   Brief discussion of modeling and solution techniques.  
   Considered in more detail in MATP 6620 in alternating spring semesters
5. Duality in nonlinear programming: (2 weeks) Including: Lagrangian duality, saddle points.
6. Nonlinear programming algorithms: (2 weeks)  
   Including: Classic optimization algorithms. Time permitting: some discussion of recently popular algorithms.
