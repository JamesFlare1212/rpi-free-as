## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

- ISYE 4230 / 6960 – Quality Control / Statistical Machine Learning for Quality Control. Fall term annually. 3 credit hours. This course explores modern statistical and machine learning approaches for quality control and process monitoring in industrial and systems engineering. The 4000-level component emphasizes fundamental statistical quality control methods, including the construction and interpretation of various control charts (e.g., Shewhart, CUSUM, EWMA), graphical methods, process capability indices, acceptance sampling, and experimental design principles such as Taguchi methods.
- The 6000-level component extends these concepts by integrating advanced machine learning techniques for data-driven quality improvement. Topics include active machine learning for real-time monitoring, data cleaning techniques for quality assurance, unsupervised learning for anomaly detection, and supervised learning for predictive quality analytics. Students will explore the intersection of statistical methods and modern computational techniques to enhance decision-making in complex manufacturing and service systems.
- Students in the graduate section will complete additional coursework focusing on theoretical foundations, algorithmic implementation, and case studies involving large-scale, high-dimensional quality control datasets. Prerequisites include a working knowledge of probability, statistics, linear algebra, and algorithms.
