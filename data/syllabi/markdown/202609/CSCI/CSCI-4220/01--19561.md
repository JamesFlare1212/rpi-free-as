## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understand why the OSI reference model and layered protocols are critical to the success of modern large-scale computer networks
- Implement specific network programming protocols and sockets-based client/server applications using C on a Linux platform
- Understand the design tradeoffs of scalable application-layer protocols
- Design and implement robust multi-process and multi-threaded servers able to run concurrently without error or interruption

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

1. There will be 3 individual homework assignments (15%)
   1. Homeworks will primarily be in C and will be auto-graded via Submitty to the extent possible
   2. Due dates are typically Thursdays (by 10:00PM)
   3. Assignments will be made available at least one week before the due date
2. There will be 6 lecture lab assignments (various Tuesdays) (12%)
   1. Labs will be coding and networking problems in C and some written problems
   2. You can work in a team with at most two other students
   3. Labs will be due in Submitty by 10:00PM Wednesdays (if not finished in lecture)
   4. Labs will be made available at latest by Sunday evenings
3. There will be 1 project split into 2 separate deliverables (22%)
   1. For the project, you are required to work in a team with one other student
   2. The project will primarily be in C and will be auto-graded via Submitty to the extent possible
   3. Due dates are typically Thursdays (by 10:00PM)
   4. Each deliverable will be made available at least two weeks before the due date
4. There will be 3 in-class quizzes (21%)
   1. Quizzes will be during lecture and cover more recent material
   2. No cribsheets or notes or laptops or etc. are allowed
   3. Quizzes have a narrow make-up window; an official excused absence is required
5. There will be 2 in-class exams (30%)
   1. Exams will be during lecture — each exam covers roughly half of the course
   2. Cribsheets are allowed on each exam
   3. A make-up exam will only be given with an official excused absence
6. There will be no final exam

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Individual assignments in this course must be the sole work of each individual student; for team-based work, such work must be the sole work of the team members  
Students must write their own code and do their own work; use of AI and external online resources must be extremely rare (except where such use is permissible as noted on an assignment)  
If found in violation of the academic integrity policy:

- You will receive a grade of 0 on the given assignment
- For a second offense, you will receive a non-droppable F in the course
- Each incident will be reported to the Dean of Students or Graduate Dean, as applicable, as well as your Department Head
- Cheating may cause you to be ineligible to mentor for the department, participate in various departmental organizations and honor societies, etc.
