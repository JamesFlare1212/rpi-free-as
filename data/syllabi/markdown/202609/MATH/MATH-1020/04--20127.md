## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Basic symbol manipulation skills pertaining to integration.
- Ability to state and explain basic Calculus definitions and theorems and their applications.
- Ability to describe curves parametrically and apply Calculus concepts and techniques to curves described in this way.
- Ability to express curves via polar equations and to solve Calculus problems in the polar coordinate system.
- Ability to apply Calculus to selected problems in Science, Engineering and Mathematics.
- Ability to use convergence tests to analyze the behavior of infinite series.
- Ability to represent functions using power series and solve problems using these representations.
- Ability to describe lines and planes in space in equation form and solve problems using these representations.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Exams:** Exams will be given during test block with the possible exception of students with accommodations who have sent me their memo from ANSS and excused absences as outline below. Attendance will be taken at all exams using sign in sheets. Details about each exam, including the material to be covered on the exam and time limit, will be announced on [LMS](http://lms.rpi.edu/) when the date of the exam is near. The dates for Exams 1–3 are listed above in the section **Grading.**

Students with an ANSS memo will have the option to take their exams at the testing center. ANSS students are responsible for booking their own testing appointments with the testing center following the guidelines that the instructor provides you. Note that these appointments **must be made well in advance** of scheduled exam dates. If an ANSS student fails to schedule an testing appointment with the testing center more that 5 business days prior to the scheduled exam date, it is possible that part of their accommodation can not be met. In this case, the exam will be taken as scheduled with their classmates and an email from the instructor will outline how other accommodations will be handled.

A conflict exam will be given to students who have **made prior arrangements** with the instructor. Students eligible to take a conflict exam are students with a pre-scheduled absence (e.g. official excused absence documented by Student Success to attend a conference, athletic competition) on the day of the exam who have notified the instructor of said absence as soon as they learn of it and no later than one class period prior to the regularly scheduled exam. Documentation excusing the student from class at the time of the regularly scheduled exam ([**excused absence documented by the Student Success office**](https://success.studentlife.rpi.edu/current-students/academic-and-personal-support/requesting-excused-absence)**, similar documentation directly to instructor or documentation from a coach with game departure time noted)** must be received by the instructor before the day of the regularly scheduled exam. Note that if you know ahead of time that you are going to miss an exam, for any reason, you must discuss this with the instructor no later than one class period prior to the exam.

If a student misses an exam for any other reason (because they overslept, was ill on the day of the exam, didn’t know where the exam was, or didn’t know there was an exam, to give a few examples), the student will be allowed to take the conflict exam under the following conditions

- A student who misses the exam due to an unexcused absence (e.g. did not know there was an exam, feeling ill, overslept, etc) **and informs the instructor via email no later than 5 PM** on the day of the exam of their absence and request to take the conflict exam. *(1st missed exam given without penalty; any future missed exams will have a penalty applied)*
- A student who misses the exam due to an unforeseen reason and fails to email the instructor by 5 PM on the day of the exam but **does email the instructor within 24 hours of the exam** and requests to take the make up exam, may take a make up exam ***with penalty.***

**Missing an exam should not be taken lightly.** Thus, if a student fails to inform (via email) the instructor within 24 hours of the exam of their absence (excused or unexcused), it is at the discretion of the instructor to give the student a 0 on the exam or allow the student to take a make-up exam with application of a penalty.

Note that the policy outlined here is for in-semester exams only. The policy for the final exam is outlined below.

**Final Exam:** The Final Exam will be cumulative and will be administered during the finals period December 16 - 22. **All students must take the final exam at the time scheduled by the Office of the Registrar. Exceptions will only be made if there is a scheduling conflict as defined** [**here**](https://info.rpi.edu/registrar/grades/#FinalExamScheduleandConflicts)**, or if a student is excused by the Student Success office.** Please note that the Student Success office will only issue an excuse for final exams for extreme circumstances, such as hospitalization, and not for travel. (Read more here.) ***Do not make arrangements for travel at the end of the semester until the Final Exam Schedule from the Registrar’s Office is published or schedule your travel after the last day of final exams - December 22.***

**Homework:** Achieve homework assignments will be due once or twice weekly on Mondays and Thursdays at 11:59 PM and will be graded automatically by the system. Students may submit these assignments late up to two days after the original deadline, but a penalty will be applied to the score. A 10% penalty will apply to questions completed the day after the deadline and a 20% penalty will apply to questions completed two days after the deadline. Any questions completed before the deadline receive full credit. No other due date extensions will be granted for any reason. To further compensate for this, several homework assignment scores will be dropped at the end of the semester.

Additional problems will be assigned from the textbook and will be posted on [LMS](http://lms.rpi.edu/) in the **Lecture and Homework** content area for each exam. Though the textbook problems will not be collected nor graded, they should not be viewed as optional. Success in the course depends on successful completion of all homework, both Achieve homework and textbook homework. Exam problems are frequently chosen from homework assignments and lecture examples. Full solutions to odd-numbered textbook problems can be found on Achieve. When logged in to our Achieve course, view content by assignments and scroll to the bottom of the page. The student solutions manual for chapters 5-7 and 10-14 may be found below No Due Dates.

**Quizzes:** Quizzes will be given in-person during Thursday lecture most weeks of the semester. Thursday quizzes will be on sections covered the week before the quiz. Students must take quizzes during the lecture time that is listed on their schedule on SIS. No make-up quizzes will be given for any reason. To compensate for this, three(3) in-person quiz scores will be dropped at the end of the semester. Students who miss a quiz due to pre-scheduled absence (e.g. official excused absence documented by Student Success to attend a conference, athletic competition) may be excused from a quiz if they provide documentation to the instructor prior to the missed quiz. A student with three(3) or more excused absences must discuss with the instructor how their quiz average will be calculated for the course.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

**Attendance:** Students are responsible for all material covered in lectures whether they are present or not. Attendance is expected in lecture, but will not be checked. Students are required to attend lectures when exams are given and the recitation time/location they have on SIS when recitation quizzes are given, unless alternative arrangements have been made with the instructor before the time of the assessment.

### Other Course Policies:

**Access and Neurodiverse Students:** Students with a memorandum from the [Access and Neurodiverse Student Success (ANSS)](http://studenthealth.rpi.edu/disabilityservices) office identifying accommodations for exams and quizzes must present the memorandum to the instructor in order to receive their accommodations. Providing the accommodations will require advance planning and students must monitor their RPI email for communication from the instructor on how accommodations will be handled. Students will not be granted accommodations unless a memorandum from ANSS has been received.

Details about exams for ANSS accommodations can be found above. Students will have the option to take Quizzes in lecture without their accommodations, or to arrange an alternate time to take quizzes. . Information on arranging this will be sent via email, thus ANSS students are advised to monitor their emails regularly.

**Grade Appeals:** If ***upon reviewing the answer key and solutions for an exam*** a student feels that the work on their exam was graded incorrectly, then the student may submit a grading appeal to the instructor on Gradescope. Regrade requests will only be accepted during the grade appeal window specified on LMS once the grades are released. Regrade requests made outside of Gradescope will be ignored.

**Calculator Policy:** Calculators will **not** be allowed on exams. Problems will be designed so that this does not create an issue. Due to the online nature of Achieve homework, a calculator may be required to complete a homework problem, but students should not rely on calculators for simple arithmetic or trigonometric calculations.

**Health and Safety:** Students are required to follow the health and safety protocols of RPI, which are subject to change throughout the semester.

**Visitors:** Visitors are prohibited during all class meetings. Only students who are registered for the class should be present in the classroom. Students may only attend class meetings at the time and place listed on their schedule on SIS.

**Classroom Decorum:** Students must be on time for class and should refrain from leaving and reentering the classroom during class. If a student has a legitimate reason for arriving late to class or being excused early from class, then they should discuss this with the instructor before class. Phones may not be used during class and should be silent. Computers and tablets may not be used for anything other than taking notes. Students who are unable to courteously comply with these policies may be asked to leave the room.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Academic Integrity:** Students should familiarize themselves with the section on academic integrity in the [Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities](https://rpi.app.box.com/s/d4ndpn3f7qdwxh9jaz5n1hkkr0mnd25e) (pp. 16–18). All work submitted on exams and quizzes must be the student’s own. In addition, no calculators, computers, tablets, open books, open notes, phones, headphones, or smartwatches may be used during exams or quizzes. Talking or communicating with other students in any way during exams or quizzes is also prohibited. Students may discuss homework with other students, mentors, TAs, and instructors, but the work submitted must be the student’s own.

The minimum academic sanction applied to a student found in violation of the above policies will be a grade of 0 on the assignment. A stronger sanction may be applied if warranted. A report regarding the violation will also be submitted to the Dean of Students Office and will remain on file until graduation. Repeat offenders will fail the course and will be subject to judicial action.
