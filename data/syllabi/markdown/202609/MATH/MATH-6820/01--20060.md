### Text Details:

Required textbook: Computer Methods for Ordinary Differential Equations and Differential-Algebraic Equations, by Uri. M. Ascher and Linda R. Petzold (SIAM)

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Topics

- – Formulation, analysis and implementation of Runge-Kutta methods, linear multi-step methods, and some related methods, for non-stiff and stiff initial-value problems; symplectic methods for Hamiltonian systems
- – Other possible topics, led by students via team-projects: spectral deferred correction (SDC), parareal algorithm, time integrators with mixed precision, time integrators for stochastic differential equations etc

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will demonstrate an understanding of algorithms for the numerical solution of systems of ODEs
- Demonstrate an understanding of fundamental concepts of order of accuracy (consistency), stability and convergence,
- Demonstrate an understanding of the behavior of algorithms, and their strengths and limitations.
- Implement numerical methods using clear and correct computer code.
- Present written solutions to problems in a clear, concise and coherent fashion.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Course assessment is based primarily on in-class exams. Homework will also be assigned and collected, but will have a minor contribution to the course assessment.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Course grades will be based primarily on three exams. Homework will be assigned regularly and collected. A small portion of the course grade will be based on homework completion. The weights for these items are 90% for exams (30% for each exam) and 10% for homework.
