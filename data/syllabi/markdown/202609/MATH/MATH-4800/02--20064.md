## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Successful completion of the course should enable you to (1) be familiar with some common and widely used numerical algorithms used in the solution of equations and the approximations of functions, derivatives, and integrals. (2) implement, in Matlab, basic numerical algorithms. (3) understand the strengths and weaknesses of different methods. (4) understand the notions of accuracy, conditioning and convergence.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

The course grade will be based on two midterm exams, a final exam and approximately 9 homeworks.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities defines  
various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself  
familiar with these. In the case of problem sets, collaboration  
between students is encouraged. However, each student must write  
his/her own solutions and submit his/her own problem sets. Copying  
someone else’s solution or Matlab code is not allowed.  
Using artificial intelligence (AI) for problem solutions or code is prohibited. Submission  
of work generated by AI will be considered a violation of academic ethics.  
In the case of exams, there is no collaboration of any kind permitted. If cheating is  
suspected, then an explanation will be requested. If the explanation  
is not satisfactory, then an exam grade of zero will be given and a  
report will be made to the Dean of Students.
