## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Directional derivatives, maxima and minima, double integrals, line integrals, div and curl, and Green’s Theorem; matrix algebra and systems of linear equations, vectors and linear transformations in R^n, eigenvectors and eigenvalues, applications in engineering and science.

Students cannot get credit for this course and MATH 2011 or MATH 2012. Students cannot get credit for this course and MATH 2100. (from the [Rensselaer Catalog](http://catalog.rpi.edu/))

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Students are responsible for all material covered in lecture whether they are present or not. Attendance is expected, but will only be checked when there is an exam or quiz.

### Other Course Policies:

**Exams:** The material covered on each exam will be announced on [LMS](https://lms.rpi.edu/) when the date of the exam is near. Exams will be given in person during the Wednesday test block at 2:00–3:50 PM in RCKTTS 203. **Students who receive testing accommodations from the** [**Access and Neurodiverse Student Success**](https://studenthealth.rpi.edu/list-services/access-and-neurodiverse-student-success) **(formerly Disability Student Services (DSS)) office who submit their accommodations memorandum to the instructor will be notified via email regarding the specific arrangements for their exams. This may include taking the exam in a small classroom at their regularly scheduled exam time or taking the exam in the testing center in DARRIN 345.**

If a student has a pre-scheduled absence (e.g. an athletic competition, official excused absence to attend a conference) on the day of an exam, then they must notify the instructor via email as soon as they learn of the absence and **no later than one week before the regularly scheduled exam**. Documentation excusing the student from class at the time of the regularly scheduled exam must be received by the instructor before the day of the exam. This documentation must be either an [excused absence documented by the Dean of Students Office](https://doso.rpi.edu/student-support/excused-absence) or, if the absence is due to an athletic competition, documentation from a coach. Provided the above conditions are met, the student will then take a conflict exam at a different time.

If a student misses an exam due to an unforeseen absence (e.g. illness on the day of the exam), then they may take a make-up exam provided they notify the instructor via email **before the exam begins**. The make-up exam may differ in format from the regularly scheduled exam (e.g. oral exam) and must be taken within one week of the regularly scheduled exam. It is the sole responsibility of the student to contact the instructor to make arrangements for a make-up exam when an exam is missed.

If a student misses an exam and fails to notify the instructor via email before the exam begins, then the student may not take the make-up exam and receives a score of 0 on the exam.

**Final Exam:** There is no final exam in the course.

**Quizzes:** Quizzes will be given most weeks during lecture. A quiz schedule for the semester will be posted on [LMS](http://lms.rpi.edu/).

**No make-up quizzes will be given for any reason.** To compensate for this, the lowest two quiz scores for each student will be dropped at the end of the semester. If a student misses more than two quizzes for valid reasons, then they will need to email the instructor within 24 hours of missing the additional quiz(zes) to make arrangements to discuss options, if any.

**Homework:** Homework will be assigned regularly from the textbooks. Though these problems will not be collected nor graded, they should not be viewed as optional. Success in the course depends on successful completion of all homework. Exam and quiz problems are frequently chosen from homework assignments and lecture examples.

**Access and Neurodiverse Student Success (formerly Disability Student Services (DSS)):** Students with a memorandum from the [Access and Neurodiverse Student Success (ANSS)](https://studenthealth.rpi.edu/list-services/access-and-neurodiverse-student-success) office identifying accommodations for exams must email the memorandum to the instructor **by Friday, September 4** since providing the accommodations requires advance planning. In your email, you must state the following:

Students will not be granted accommodations unless a memorandum from the Access and Neurodiverse Student Success office has been received.

Students who receive testing accommodations from the Access and Neurodiverse Student Success office will be notified via email regarding the specific arrangements for their exams. This may include taking the exam in a small classroom at their regularly scheduled exam time or taking the exam in the testing center in DARRIN 345.

Students opting to also use their accommodations on quizzes will take their quizzes in the testing center in DARRIN 345, preferably at the same date and time.

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Students should familiarize themselves with the section on academic integrity in the [Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities](https://doso.rpi.edu/policies/academic/academic-integrity). All work submitted on exams and quizzes must be the student’s own. In addition, no calculators, computers, tablets, open books, open notes, formula sheets, phones, headphones, or watches may be worn or used during exams or quizzes. Talking or communicating with other students in any way during exams or quizzes is also prohibited.

The minimum academic sanction applied to a student found in violation of the above policies will be a grade of zero on the assignment. This zero will not be able to be dropped at the end of the semester. A stronger sanction may be applied if warranted. A report regarding the violation will also be submitted to the Dean of Students Office and will remain on file until graduation. Repeat offenders will fail the course and will be subject to judicial action.
