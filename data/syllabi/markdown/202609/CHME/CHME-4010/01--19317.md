## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An introductory course in transport phenomena covering fluid statics, and one-dimensional  
diffusive processes including laminar flow, heat conduction, and mass diffusion. Course focuses  
on developing the equations of change, introducing sum-of-resistance concepts and couple fluid  
flow, heat transfer, and mass transfer problems. The concept of extended surfaces as a means  
of enhancing transport process is included. The course introduces numerical simulation  
concepts for solving simple, one-dimensional transport problems. Credit not allowed for both this  
course and ENGR 2250.

## Course Text(s)

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The course goals are to teach the students how to develop an engineering approach to solve transport problems and to introduce fundamental concepts of momentum, mass, and energy transport.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- solve problems involving fluid statics and fluids in rigid body motion.
- solve steady state fluid flow, heat transfer, and mass transfer problems without any generation terms.
- solve steady state transport problems involving the generation of momentum, energy, or chemical species.
- solve transient transport problems in finite and semi-infinite systems.
