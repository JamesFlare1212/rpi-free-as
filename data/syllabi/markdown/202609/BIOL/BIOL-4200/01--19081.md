## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An introduction to the concepts and techniques of modern statistics. Computer-based, in-class sessions emphasize hands-on application of statistical techniques using data and examples from biological and medical sciences. Pre-class online exercises prepare students for deeper classroom exploration.

**Topics include:** Sampling, regression, ANOVA, factor analysis, MANOVA, maximum likelihood methods, discriminant analysis, time series analysis, forecasting, and more. The final project requires analysis of a major research dataset and a scientific paper-style write-up.
