## Course Goals

### Goals:

The goal of this course is to familiarize students with the knowledge necessary to analyze a topic in evolutionary cell biology and critically evaluate primary literature.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate knowledge of cells and the modifications of underlying molecular, structural, and physical mechanisms that have contributed to their diversification over time.
- Demonstrate knowledge of experimental tools and approaches used in modern evolutionary cell biological research.
- Read and critically review primary scientific literature in evolutionary cell biology.
- Analyze a subfield of evolutionary cell biology and design a set of next-step experiments, and package these in a persuasive research proposal.
- Review grant proposals, critically evaluate proposed experiments, and provide constructive feedback.
- Review a broad area of evolutionary cell biological research, identify the important concepts, and develop and present a lecture on the topic.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Examinations and evaluations:** The exams will be short answer questions and will be used to assess knowledge in both BIOL 4050 and 6050. Makeup exams may be scheduled if arranged prior to the test date and approved by the instructor. The format of these exams may differ significantly from the standard exams. If you miss the exam due to illness or some other emergency, a note needs to be produced from Student Health, the Dean’s Office, ALAC, etc. before a makeup exam will be scheduled.

**Paper presentation:** Students will be assigned current scientific papers and present the findings to the class. Details on the presentation format will be described in class.

**Homework:** For each paper discussion students will answer questions about the paper. This will be due on the google drive before the discussion. Details and structure of the homework will be announced in class. Presenting students are not required to submit a homework response.

**Proposal:** Each student will choose a topic to write a grant proposal. Proposals will be graded by the instructor.

**Lectures:** BIOL 6050 students will prepare and deliver one course lecture on a topic related to evolutionary cell biology (45 min in length).

For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own.
