### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Course objectives: This course focuses on conveying fundamental physiological processes and their mechanisms of action in human and higher mammalian organisms. It emphasizes the control and interaction of physiological systems such as the muscle, nervous, circulatory, renal, respiratory, digestive, reproductive and hormonal systems.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities defines various forms of academic dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, collaborative work is encouraged within the following guidelines:

- Ø Something of Interest: Each student must make an individual presentation but may consult with anybody during the preparation for that presentation.
- Ø Significance, Hypothesis, and Specific Aims presentation: Each student must make an individual presentation, but each student is encouraged to consult and collaborate closely with their research mentor prior to that presentation.
- Ø Writing component: Each student must submit an individual effort but may interview or otherwise collect information from any responsible source.
- Ø Research Proposal: Each student must submit their own proposal, but each student is encouraged to work closely with the research mentor (or designee) during the preparation of the proposal. The proposal may contain preliminary data (or other short components) obtained from other workers in the mentor’s laboratory, but such information should be identified clearly in the proposal.

Any assignment in violation of this policy may result in a grade of 0 (zero) for that component of the course.
