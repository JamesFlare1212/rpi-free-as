## Course Goals

### Goals:

This is an introductory course in cell structure and function. It is intended for Biology and Biomedical Engineering majors, and covers such topics as chemical composition of cells, molecular structure and function of proteins, transport of solutes across the membrane, and the basic biochemistry underlying cellular behavior including cancer cells**.**

## Course Content

### Content Details:

- structure of cellular building blocks
- How the cell extracts energy from food
- how proteins fold and work
- control of gene expression
- how solutes are transported across the cell membrane
- mitosis
- signal transduction pathways
- cytoskeleton
- extracellular matrix and cancer

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Demonstrate a good understanding of prokaryotic and eukaryotic cell structure, function, replication and regulation of gene expression by answering multiple choice and short answer/drawing questions on in-class exams and homework problems.
- • Interpret the results of online databases regarding protein alignments and analysis
