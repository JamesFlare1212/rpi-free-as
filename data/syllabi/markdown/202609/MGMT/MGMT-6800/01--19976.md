## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course introduces the motivations and related factors that shape consumers’ attitudes, judgments, and, behavior. Theories from the fields of psychology, economics, and, marketing relevant to Consumer Behavior and Product Design will be covered.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course is designed to introduce the students to the theories and applications of Consumer Behavior from the relevant fields of psychology, economics, and, marketing. Students will be trained to apply the concepts and theories to new product development as well as various aspects of marketing

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Identify the fundamental theories of consumer behavior
- Understand why consumer behavior is important in marketing
- Understand how and why the consumers are not rational
- Understand heuristics and biases
- Demonstrate how to apply the concepts in designing a new product
- Discuss the ethical considerations of marketing and consumer behavior
- Demonstrate how they can apply concepts of consumer behavior in pro-social and pro-environmental behavior.
- Demonstrate their understanding of sensory cues in the environment can impact consumer choices and judgments.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

1. **Exams:** There will be two exams that are equally important. The exam dates and times are in the course schedule section of this document. Both of the exams will require application of the theories and principles covered in the class. The format of the exams will consist of objective and subjective questions. There are NO makeup exam without a written proof of a university approved conflict.

1. **In Class Assignments:** There will be in-class assignments in some of the classes. The assignments will be based on the concepts taught in the last class and/or the present class. There are no make-up assignment and if you miss a class, you will lose the points. Please note that you will work in teams for these assignments. You need to work remotely with your team for completing these assignments.

1. **Individual Assignments:** Final Assignment: The details of the final assignment will be given after exam 1. It is due on the first day of the exam week. It has to be submitted electronically on LMS/BB. Late submissions will not be accepted. The assignments will be different for the students in registered for MGMT 4460 and MGMT 6800. Students registered for MGMT 6800 will have questions related to consumer behavior for sustainable marketing practices as well.

1. **Class Participation:** Class participation includes attendance, punctuality, and active participation in class discussions. Please come to class having read the material assigned for the week and provide comments and examples that demonstrate knowledge and integration of course material, including respectful responses to and meaningful elaboration on the comments of others. Asking related questions is also a form of class participation. Your performance will be adversely affected by your absence from the class. If you have more than two unexcused absences in this class, you will start losing class participation points. Sleeping in class, engaging in other non-class related activities during class time (texting, chat, email, web browsing, working on assignments for other classes, etc.) are not allowed and will significantly affect your class participation grade.
