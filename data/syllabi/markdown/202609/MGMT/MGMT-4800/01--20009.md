## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is a required course for management majors. It is designed to introduce students to the key operational characteristics of a real company. Students are expected to secure an internship and successfully complete it before registering for this class. Students will submit a written report documenting what they did, what they learned, etc. It is a 0-credit hour class used to document the fact that Lally students have completed an internship. This is a requirement for graduation for all Lally students.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

A set of short readings will be posted on the course LMS (Blackboard) and serve as required readings for the course.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Students will demonstrate an understanding of the core functional areas in business based on their experience during their internship.
- 2\. Students will demonstrate the ability to secure an internship and perform on the job.
- 3\. Students will demonstrate the ability to apply what they have learned in school to help with their internship experience.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

The course requirements resulting in the final grade are as follows:

**Written Assignments – Internship Survey & Percipio Case (50%)**

Students will be required to complete a survey form that provides information on your internship experience, what you learned from it, and how it relates to your coursework at RPI and your professional goals. Only complete responses to this online survey will be accepted to complete this portion of the course. The link to the survey will be available on the course LMS. Students are also asked to complete a brief case study assignment provided through RPI’s Percipio system.

**Video Reflection (50%)**

After the written assignment has been completed, students will also be required to participate in the creation of a brief (1-2 minute), video interview of their internship experience. These videos are completed in person during the second half of the semester. More will be provided about this assignment early in the semester and via our Blackboard space (LMS).

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Written Reports - 50%

**Grading Scale**

Since this is a 0-credit course, but required for graduation, there are two possible grading outcomes:

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:
