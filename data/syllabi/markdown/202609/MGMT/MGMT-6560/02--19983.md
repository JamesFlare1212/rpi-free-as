## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) are reshaping the foundations of business and management, at large. From enhancing customer experiences and optimizing supply chains to informing strategic decisions and enabling new business models, AI/ML technologies are now integral to how organizations operate. This introductory course provides a hands-on foundation in machine learning -- starting with preprocessing the data, then designing, training, and evaluating models; and applying them to diverse business and management contexts. Students will gain experience in diverse machine learning methods including contemporary innovations related to generative AI, large language models, and AI agents. While we will examine contemporary AI applications driving innovation, our emphasis will be on mastering the fundamentals to equip students with the technical skills needed to understand, implement, critically evaluate, and responsibly use AI/ML systems in practice.

### Goals:

The goal of this course will be to provide a strong technical foundation that enables students to become not just data scientists but also machine learning experts who can ask the right set of questions given a dataset, work with challenging multimodal datasets and confidently model them to perform prediction tasks.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- (1) Demonstrate an understanding of analytics-based problem solving and analytics thinking in the context of machine learning models, deep learning, and big data.
- (2) Be able to extract, match, transform, and clean data from a variety of sources.
- (3) Develop machine learning predictions for business applications.
- (4) Apply unbiased and robust frameworks to the creation of intelligent models.
- (5) Translate research on state-of-the-art deep learning to real-world applications in business especially with the surge of generative AI applications in the current society

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Assignments**

You will have assignments associated with many classes. Be sure to check piazza and submit assignments via blackboard (or LMS). Preparing questions on different topics in advance is expected that helps you participate and contribute to class discussion. You can use Piazza (link above) for discussions on course-related questions with the instructor, TA, or your classmates. Engagement on Piazza threads is highly recommended.

**Quizzes**

I use quizzes to not only test your knowledge about the topics we are learning in the class but also to assess whether you are attending the lectures or not. There will be a few surprise quizzes throughout the semester to incentivize you to review the readings/concepts prior to class and arrive on time. The door will be locked at the start of class on a quiz day, and you will receive a 0 if you arrive late.

**Exams**

There are two exams during the semester. Students will be provided with further instructions on the specifics of these exams by the instructor.

**Project**

There will be an individual project to be completed as part of the class. The overall goal is to undergo all stages of the data understanding, data pre-processing, visualization, modelling, and evaluating the models you propose.

**Research Translation Exercise**

This will be a separate exercise where the 6000-level students were given a specific ML task and use in-class learnings to research and report on that task. Only 6000-level students can receive a grade for this. More details will be announced later.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

**PLEASE DO NOT BE LATE TO CLASS.** Absences will result in a 0 score for quiz grades if a quiz is given on that day of absence. Please email the instructor in case you miss any class.

In-Class exercises are designed to be worked on during class time in a collaborative environment in which you each help one another. We have very different levels of technical expertise in the class, and it is important to work together on the exercises to help one another in a classroom environment. While you may be able to complete the work at home by yourself, in doing so you will be robbing your classmates of your expertise.

Therefore, the imperative clearly stated: each participant attends class fully prepared, willing, and be able to offer constructive criticism, provide goal-oriented analytics and synthetic insights, and encourage investigative dialectic. You earn your grade on participation through consistent, daily contribution. Merely "coming to class” is not sufficient but is necessary.

Simply put: Do not miss class hours! Understandably, there are circumstances (e.g., job interviews, family matters, extracurricular activity, etc.) that may cause you to miss class; nevertheless, excessive absences will reduce your class participation grade (quiz). Notify the instructor in advance of any planned absences (especially students who participate in extracurricular activities as representatives of RPI). Overall integrity is expected and appreciated.
