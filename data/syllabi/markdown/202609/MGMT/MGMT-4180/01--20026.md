## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course examines how analytical tools can be used to generate business insights from data to address marketing problems. It focuses on applying these tools to areas such as segmentation, targeting, positioning, forecasting, customer choice, new product design, pricing, and other marketing challenges. Techniques covered include logit models, cluster analysis, discriminant analysis, conjoint analysis, prediction models, and more. Rather than focusing on mathematical derivations, the course emphasizes understanding the intuition behind these methods, recognizing which method(s) to apply to a given business problem, knowing how and why to use them, interpreting the results, and understanding how to generate insights that can inform marketing decisions.

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work.
