## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The course is primarily aimed at first-semester quantitative finance graduate students to provide sufficient background for various financial decision applications, as well as basic analytical tools that are required for analysis for improved decision-making. Prescriptive decision tools such as optimization modeling and descriptive stochastic models of parameter uncertainty are studied in the context of financial problems such as portfolio selection, derivative pricing, and risk management. The emphasis of the course is on mathematical modeling of such problems and the necessary tools for analysis. The course is also intended to help prepare the student for more advanced graduate work in quantitative finance and risk analytics, including financial optimization. Students will implement financial models on Excel spreadsheets as part of homework.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Expose students to knowledge and skills in financial modeling and basic statistical and optimization analysis tools required for analysis of uncertain financial asset returns.
- Foster an understanding of different types of assets, such as stocks, bonds, options, and derivatives, to aid analysis in investment asset selection.
- Develop insights on portfolio diversification and risk modeling, to evaluate and support varying objectives of portfolio optimization.
- Develop and practice basic skills in preparing reports and presentations that require portfolio modeling and risk optimization.
