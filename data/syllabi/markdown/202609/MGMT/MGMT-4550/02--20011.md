## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This seminar is intended to provide engineering and science sophomore and juniors participating in the Accelerated preMBA program with a compelling look into our Master’s program, gaining a better sense of how an MBA can empower engineers and scientists to become well-rounded professionals capable of driving innovation, leading teams, and making strategic contributions to their organizations' success. Through this seminar, pre-Eng/Sci MBA students will gain a better understanding of what topics they will be learning in the MBA program, what career paths will be open for them, how an MBA can complement their undergraduate degree, and what outcomes they can expect.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

The Accelerated Engineering & Science 5-Year MBA Pre-Program Seminar is designed to support undergraduate students who are exploring or preparing for the accelerated MBA pathway. The seminar convenes selectively throughout the semester to offer high-impact, value-added experiences that enhance students’ academic, professional, and career readiness. Sessions may include networking events with alumni and industry professionals, site visits to alumni recruiters in New York City, and targeted professional development workshops such as building a professional portfolio and preparing for graduate-level study. Participation in seminar activities is strongly encouraged, as each session is intentionally curated to provide practical insights, connections, and skills that support students’ long-term success in the MBA program and beyond.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Demonstrate increased professional readiness through the development of materials such as a professional portfolio, résumé, and/or LinkedIn profile appropriate for MBA-level recruiting.
- • Apply effective networking strategies when interacting with alumni, recruiters, and industry professionals in both on-campus and off-campus settings.
- • Engage professionally in site visits and employer interactions, including preparation, conduct, and follow-up communication.
- • Identify resources, opportunities, and expectations that support a successful transition from undergraduate study to an accelerated MBA program.
- • Describe key business and management competencies that complement technical training and are critical for success in graduate business education and early career roles.
- • Evaluate the fit and expectations of the Accelerated 5-Year MBA program in relation to their academic background, career goals, and professional interests.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Satisfactory (S): A satisfactory grade is earned through professional conduct and meaningful engagement in seminar activities. This includes appropriate preparation for and participation in selected events (e.g., professional development sessions, networking events, or alumni site visits), timely communication when registering for or attending activities, and behavior that reflects positively on the student and the program. Satisfactory performance is based on the quality and professionalism of engagement, not the number of events attended.

Unsatisfactory (U): An unsatisfactory grade reflects a lack of professional engagement or conduct, such as failure to communicate regarding attendance after committing to an event, unprofessional behavior during activities, or repeated non-participation after indicating intent to engage. Unsatisfactory performance indicates that seminar expectations for professionalism and responsibility were not met.

*No exams, homework, or formal assessments are associated with this seminar; grading is based solely on professional engagement and conduct.*

This seminar offers a flexible and dynamic curriculum that varies each semester to reflect contemporary topics and professional development opportunities. ## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

**PARTICIPATION AND ATTENDANCE POLICY:** The Pre-MBA Program Seminar does not meet on a regular schedule; instead, sessions are offered selectively throughout the semester when value-added professional development opportunities are available. Participation in individual events is **optional**, and students with academic, research, work, or other scheduling constraints are **exempt from attending** specific events. However, all students are strongly encouraged to make a **good-faith effort to participate** whenever possible, as seminar activities are intentionally designed to strengthen readiness for the Accelerated MBA program and to support students’ professional development.

Students who choose to participate in seminar events are expected to engage **professionally and responsibly**, including arriving on time, being prepared, and adhering to professional standards of conduct. Because many activities involve alumni, recruiters, or off-campus site visits, advance registration and commitment are expected. Students who register for an event but are unable to attend should notify the program coordinator in advance whenever possible. Failure to communicate regarding attendance or repeated last-minute cancellations may be considered unprofessional and may affect the student’s seminar standing.

*Professional behavior in participation and attendance is an essential component of the seminar and reflects expectations students will encounter in the accelerated MBA program and in professional settings.*

- ### Academic Integrity
