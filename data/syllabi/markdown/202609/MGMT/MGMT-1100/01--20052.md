## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Welcome to your first step into the world of business! This course introduces the big-picture view of how organizations work, and the decisions leaders make every day to keep them moving forward. Whether you are a future manager, engineer, designer, or entrepreneur, you will walk away with a practical understanding of how businesses operate. We will cover the major functions of business, including strategy, finance, accounting, marketing, operations, entrepreneurship, business analytics, and other relevant management concepts, through an interdisciplinary lens. If you are a business major, this course sets the foundation for everything to come. If you are in another field, it will provide you with the tools and language to collaborate across industries and roles.

Our work together will go beyond textbook reading. Through real cases, simulations, and team activities, you will practice solving business problems, making strategic decisions, and presenting your ideas effectively. Along the way, you will also sharpen your communication, collaboration, and critical thinking skills - the ones employers value most, regardless of your major.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will demonstrate an understanding of the core functional areas of business: accounting, marketing, finance, operations, entrepreneurship, strategy, business analytics, and general management.
- Students will demonstrate the ability to research and analyze companies and industries.
- Students will demonstrate the ability to integrate and apply their knowledge across the core functional areas of business.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

***Class Participation: 10%***

In this course, you will quickly discover that complex business decisions often don’t have a single “correct” or “best” answer. While some areas such as accounting, follow strict rules, many of our discussions will involve informed judgment, differing perspectives, and real-world ambiguity. That’s why your active participation is essential.

Your participation grade is based not just on attendance, but on your meaningful engagement in both full-class discussions and group work. I encourage you to contribute thoughtfully, ask questions, share perspectives, and help foster a collaborative learning environment.

Note that at the end of the term, you will complete **confidential peer evaluations** of your team members. These evaluations will contribute to both your **participation grade** and your **group assignment grades**, as they reflect your contribution to team efforts throughout the course.

If you need to miss a class, please notify me in advance by email. An excused absence does not exempt you from submitting any assignments due that day. If you are sick and need to attend asynchronously, email me ahead of time so we can make arrangements. Please note:

- **Three unexcused absences** will lower your overall course grade by **half a letter** (*e.g.,* B to B-), and each additional unexcused absence will incur further penalties.
- Excessive excused absences may also impact your participation grade.

That said, I understand that life can be unpredictable, especially in recent years. We’ll have an open discussion about mental health early in the semester, and I want you to know that I’m always available. If you are struggling with anything, please don’t hesitate to reach out to me - anytime, and in whatever way feels most comfortable to you.

If you’re experiencing challenges with your mental or physical health, I encourage you to connect with campus resources:

- **Troy Student Health Center**: (518) 276-6287
- **Counseling Center** (4th Floor, Academy Hall): (518) 276-6479 or counseling@rpi.edu Counseling appointments are free, confidential, and designed to support your personal, academic, and emotional well-being.

***Homework: 30%***

The Homework assignments will be announced in class and posted on both the syllabus and the LMS. The comprehension questions for Homework #9 can be found under the “Assignment” tab on LMS.

**Late Submissions.** I understand that situations such as illness, family obligations, or job interviews may occasionally interfere with your ability to complete assignments on time. However, all homework must be submitted electronically **by the start of class on the due date**.

- **Late submissions** will be accepted with a **50% penalty**, but only **up until the date of the associated quiz** for that topic.
- **Extensions** may be granted on a case-by-case basis for compelling reasons and must be requested in advance.
- Homework cannot be made up at the end of the semester under any circumstances.

**Pearson MyLab Mini Simulations.** Some homework assignments will include interactive mini simulations in Pearson MyLab. These simulations allow you to apply your decision-making skills in realistic business scenarios. Your grade for each simulation is based on the decisions you make throughout the activity.

Each decision point is scored based on the quality of the choice:

- **5 points** – Best choice
- **3 points** – Acceptable choice
- **1 point** – Weak choice
- **0 points** – Incorrect or ethically flawed choice

- The simulation score you earn (expressed as a percentage) will directly determine the points you receive in the gradebook. For example, if your simulation score is 90%, you will receive 0.9 out of 1 for the assignment.

***Quizzes: (45%)***

There will be **three quizzes** throughout the semester, each covering major course topics such as strategy, finance, accounting, marketing, operations, and other core business functions. Quizzes may reference key concepts from assigned cases, but you will **not** be tested on detailed case facts.

Each quiz will be discussed in class approximately one session in advance, so you’ll know what to expect. Question formats may include multiple choice, true/false, and basic calculations.

All quizzes will open at the **beginning of the scheduled class session**. If you are using the testing center, your quiz will begin at the time of your scheduled appointment. **Late arrivals will not receive extra time**, so please plan accordingly.

***Team In-class Activities and Assignments: (15%)***

There will be multiple occasions throughout the semester where you will be working in teams on an in-class assignment, building off previous lecture content, and applying those concepts covered in class towards a Consulting Activity. Each team (3-4 students) will be assigned a company and provided instructions in class as to what analysis is required. At the end of the class session, each group will submit their response on LMS, and present their results in front of the class. The class will provide feedback, and each team submission will be graded. **Only the team members in attendance will receive credit for the submission, and there will be no opportunity for makeup. If any student has an excused absence from a coach or their class dean, these assignments will be exempt from the student’s final grade.**

### Attendance Policy:

Attendance is expected at all class sessions. Live meetings are designed to apply the skills and concepts introduced in the readings through in-class exercises, simulations, and group work. As such, both your attendance and active participation are essential to your success in this course.

Attendance is **mandatory for all exams**. If you must miss an exam, you are required to provide a written excuse from your class dean or coach in order to be considered for a makeup opportunity.  
**Three unexcused absences** will lower your overall course grade by **half a letter** (*e.g.,* B to B-), and each additional unexcused absence will incur further penalties.
