## Course Content

### Content Details:

The behavior of soil is extremely variable because it is affected by various factors such as compositions, stress state and environmental conditions. In other words, no soils behave the same way. To account for such behavior, it is important to understand how soil behavior alters, how to determine its properties, how to model its behavior and how to provide reliable predictions. This course aims to achieve the aforementioned four points through acquisition of the fundamental knowledge of soil behavior and its application to geotechnical engineering problems.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Explain the fundamentals of soils that govern their behavior.
- • Identify the conditions that alter soil behavior.
- • Determine soil properties and model parameters.
- • Solve simple problems analytically.
- • Apply geotechnical engineering in the framework of Civil Engineering design.
- • Organize and deliver effective written and graphical communications.
