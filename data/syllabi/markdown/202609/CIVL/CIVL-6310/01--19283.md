## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Students are expected to attend all classes. If a student does not attend class, it is the responsibility of the student to notify the instructor in advance and to provide justification. Every student is allowed a maximum of two automatically-excused absences for medical reasons, as long as the instructor was notified in advance and justification was provided. Students missing more than two classes due to illness are required to provide a doctor’s note or other supporting documentation to the Student Success Office. Their Class Dean will communicate the receipt of the note (with expected end date) to the instructor. For all absences that are not due to medical reasons, students can request an excused absence from the instructor by providing a valid reason and supporting documentation to the instructor. In such cases, and based on the provided information, the instructor will determine whether the student will be excused from class.

### Other Course Policies:

**Late/Excused Assignment Submission Policy:** All students are expected to submit all assignments by the specified due date. Excusal from class does not automatically excuse students from submitting homework. If a student cannot submit a particular assignment by the associated due date, it is the responsibility of the student to notify the instructor in advance and to request an extension. Based on the provided information, the instructor will determine if such an extension will be allowed. For each type of assignment, students are allowed one late submission without a grade penalty. For any subsequent late submissions of assignments of the same type, the maximum points that can be earned in each late assignment are reduced by 10% of its originally possible points for every day past the due date that the assignment has not been submitted. As an example, if an assignment was submitted 8 days past its due date, the maximum points that can be earned in that assignment are now 20% of its originally possible points, even if all parts of the late assignment are correct.  
**Missed Exam Policy:**  
Midterm exams are scheduled by the instructor and all students are expected to take such exams. A student who misses a midterm exam could take a make-up exam only if they notify the instructor and provide justification along with supporting documentation. Based on the provided information, the instructor will determine if the student will be allowed to take a make-up midterm exam.  
The final exam is scheduled by the Registrar and all students are expected to take the final exam. If a valid reason exists that prevents a student from taking the final exam, it is the responsibility of the student to submit a request to make up the final exam to the Dean of the Graduate Student Experience. If the Dean approves their request, the grade of the student for the course would then become NE (Not Examined) and they would have to take a make-up final exam next semester by a specified date.

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).
