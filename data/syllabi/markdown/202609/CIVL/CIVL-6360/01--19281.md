## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course offers an in-depth study of timber design, focusing on the behavior and design of wood structures in accordance with the latest National Design Specification (NDS) for Wood Construction. Students will gain a comprehensive understanding of timber as a building material, the design of various wood structural components, and the integration of these components into a complete structure. The course includes a hands-on group project where students design a four-story wood-framed building over a podium slab.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

- **Design of Wood Structures – ASD/LRFD** **8th Edition** by Breyer, K.F., Fridley, K.J., Pollock, D.G., and Cobeen, K.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Understand the properties and behavior of wood as a construction material.
- 2\. Apply NDS standards to the design of wood structures.
- 3\. Design key timber components such as beams, columns, and shear walls.
- 4\. Integrate individual wood components into a complete structural system.
- 5\. Collaborate in a team to produce a comprehensive timber design for a multi-story building.
- 6\. Acquire familiarity with Timber design software used in practice.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

- **Class Participation and Attendance**
- **Homework Assignments**
- **Group Project**
- **Exams**

**Group Project:**

Students will work in teams to design a four-story wood-framed structure over a podium slab. The project will be broken into weekly tasks corresponding to lecture topics. Final deliverables include a complete structural design, calculations, drawings, and a group presentation. The project will mimic professional practice, emphasizing teamwork, communication, and the application of NDS and other building code standards.

## Course Calendar

### Calendar Details:

**Week 1 (Monday, 8/31/2026): Introduction to Timber Design**

- Overview of wood as a construction material
- Wood species, grading, and properties
- Framing practices; history, current, and future.
- Introduction to the NDS and other design resources
- Introduction to the group project: Design of a four-story wood-framed building over a podium slab

**Week 2 (Thursday, 9/10/2026 – rescheduled from Monday 9/7, Labor Day): Wood Properties and Material Behavior**

- Load Path and Continuity
- Design considerations for wood
- Reference Design Values and Adjustments
- Assignment of project teams and initial planning

**Week 3 (Monday, 9/14/2026): Design of Beams and Bending Members**

- Design of sawn lumber and glulam beams
- Considerations for deflection, shear, and vibration
- Application to group project: Design of floor framing

**Week 4 (Monday, 9/21/2026): Design of Columns and Compression Members**

- Buckling, slenderness, and effective length factors
- Design of wood columns under axial loads
- Application to group project: Design of vertical load-carrying elements

**Week 5 (Monday, 9/28/2026): Combined Loading + Introduction to Connections**

- Members under combined bending and axial loading
- Introduction to connection design: fasteners, nails, bolts, and metal connectors
- Application to group project: Preliminary gravity system check

**Week 6 (Monday, 10/5/2026): Design of Wood Connections**

- Detailed design of various wood connections
- Yield limit equations and adjustment factors for connections
- Application to group project: Connection design for the structure

**Week 7 (Friday, 10/16/2026 – rescheduled from Monday 10/12, holiday): Wood Connections (Continued) and Manufactured Connectors**

- Manufacturer connections and proprietary hardware
- Detailing of hold-downs, hangers, and shear transfer connections
- Midterm exam review; group project progress check

**Week 8 (Monday, 10/19/2026): MIDTERM EXAM + Introduction to Lateral Systems**

- Midterm exam covering Weeks 1-7, held during class time
- Introduction to lateral load resisting systems and load path

**Week 9 (Monday, 10/26/2026): Lateral Load Resisting Systems (Shear Walls)**

- Design principles for shear walls
- Overturning, hold-downs, and chord forces
- Application to group project: Design of lateral load-resisting elements

**Week 10 (Monday, 11/2/2026): Lateral Load Resisting Systems (Diaphragms)**

- Detailed analysis and design of horizontal and vertical diaphragms
- Diaphragm chords, collectors, and drag struts
- Group project progress check

**Week 11 (Monday, 11/9/2026): Design for Durability and Serviceability**

- Considerations for long-term performance and durability of wood structures
- Shrinkage, temperature effects, and load duration
- Detailing for serviceability and protection from moisture

**Week 12 (Monday, 11/16/2026): Detailing for Wood Construction + Advanced Topics**

- Final integration of structural components
- Detailing considerations for constructability and performance
- Sustainability, mass timber, and fire resistance in wood design

**Week 13 (Monday, 11/23/2026): Group Project Work Session**

- Group project finalization
- In-class time to work on project
- Instructor consultation with each team

**Week 14 (Monday, 11/30/2026): Group Project Presentations + Course Review**

- Group presentations of the final design
- Peer and instructor feedback
- Comprehensive review of course material and final exam preparation

**Week 15 (Monday, 12/7/2026): FINAL EXAM**

- Comprehensive final exam, held during class time in MRC 136
- Final project deliverables due
