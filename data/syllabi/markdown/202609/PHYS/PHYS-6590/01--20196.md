## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Learning fundamental concepts, frameworks, and developing problem-solving skills in statistical mechanics at the graduate level.

**Course topics:**

review of thermodynamics, review of probability and statistics, statistical basis of thermodynamics, various ensembles, quantum statistics, ideal Fermi and Bose gases, interacting systems, phase transitions and critical phenomena.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Collaboration and discussion is allowed and encouraged in homeworks. **However, you must carry out, write up, and turn in your own solutions.** **On exams, you’re on your own and not allowed to discuss anything with your classmates.** Thus, don’t copy someone else’s solutions, and don’t cheat on exams. If I suspect you of either, I’ll ask for an explanation. If your explanation is unsatisfactory, you’ll be given a grade of zero and reported to the Dean of Students. If this happens more than once, you will be given an *F* for the course.

Using **artificial intelligence (AI)** to aid problem solutions **for HW problems** is allowed but you **must write-up and turn in your own “synthesized” solutions** (i.e., you cannot just blindly copy&paste the output of AI). Use of AI to solve exam problems (just like using any online or human assistance) is strictly prohibited. Submission of work generated by AI for exam problems will be considered a violation of academic integrity.
