## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course introduces fundamental principles and engineering applications of heat transfer by conduction, convection, and thermal radiation. Topics include steady and transient conduction using thermal-resistance networks and solutions of heat diffusion equations. Forced and natural convection are covered through boundary-layer concepts and empirical correlations for heat-transfer coefficients. Radiative exchange between surfaces and combined-mode heat transfer are addressed. The course also introduces heat exchangers and extended surfaces and features a design project.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Introduction to heat transfer including conduction, external and internal convection, and radiation. Emphasis is on applications of heat transfer to a wide variety of engineering systems including HVAC, automotive design, materials processing etc.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- On successful completion of this course students will be able to: • to select simplifying assumptions in analyzing a complex thermo-fluids system. • to analyze thermo-fluids systems by applying the conservation of mass and energy. • to analyze heat transfer in thermo-fluid systems by identifying heat transfer modes–convection (forced/natural, internal/external), conduction (transient and steady-state) and radiation using the appropriate equations to evaluate them. • to analyze the performance of heat exchangers and design heat exchangers given a set of specifications.
