## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The course will introduce basic nuclear reactor theory. It will focus on neutron transport methods, neutron diffusion and slowing down, criticality analyses for homogeneous and heterogeneous systems, reactor kinetics and control, reactivity coefficients, reactor systems and types, reactor design and reactor safety.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Learn the fundamentals of neutronic and physics as it pertains to nuclear reactors.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

The course will concentrate on the neutronics of thermal nuclear reactors. The topics will cover neutron reactions and cross sections, the transport equation, the diffusion equation, solutions of the diffusion equation, criticality, multigroup methods, fast and thermal spectrum calculations, reactor kinetics, and heterogeneous lattice calculations.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Be able to calculate neutron interaction probabilities.
- Demonstrate setup and solution of the diffusion equation in different geometries.
- Demonstrate calculations of the multiplication factor in one and two groups.
- Demonstrate calculations for numerical solution of the diffusion equation.
- Be able to solve the neutron slowing down equation.
- Demonstrate calculations of resonance integrals and resonance escape probabilities.
- Be able to solve the time dependent neutron equation.
- Be able to write and use solutions of the point kinetic equations.
- Demonstrate understanding of basic concepts in heterogeneous systems
