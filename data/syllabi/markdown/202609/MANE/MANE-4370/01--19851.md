## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An undergraduate-level course  
A laboratory course covering topics in instrumentation, radiation detection associated with X-ray, gamma-ray, applied physics, fluid dynamics, and spectroscopy. Statistics of random events, error propagation, and error analyses are emphasized. Lab attendance is required along with formal written lab reports, which include data error analysis.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

- To ensure basic skills in data acquisition and analysis in a laboratory setting.
- To get practical experience in nuclear spectroscopy, fluid flow, radiation interaction, detection and measurement
- To enhance skills in technical writing and communication

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Proportional Counter; Gamma Spectroscopy using NaI(Tl) and HPGe Detectors; Air Sampling for Radon;

Fluid flow in circulating loop; alpha spectroscopy; X-ray fluorescence, Compton scattering; and Radiation Survey Meter

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

**Grading:**

70% Lab reports (70 points)

20% Group project (10 points) and associated presentation (10 points)

10% Team efforts (10 points)

**Rubrics for conducting experiments and lab report grading (100 points in total)**

1. Summary/Abstract (10 points)
2. Introduction and Theoretical Background (10 points)
3. Experiment and Procedure (15 points)
4. Data Analysis (15 points)
5. Error Analysis (15 points)
6. Results and Discussion (25 points)
7. Conclusions (10 points)
8. References

**Students in will take turns to lead on different sessions for each group report.**

**For 4-member groups,** the group report can be divided into 4 parts: Summary/Abstract + Theoretical Background; experimental procedure + Data analysis; Error Analysis; Results & Discussion + Conclusion + References.

In each group report, a table documenting the specific sections students work should be included into the cover page of the group report. The grade of each section will be recorded separately, and the grade for individual group member for the group report will be calculated based on following equation:

**Grade of individual members = 60% of grade of the group report + 40% individual sections.**

For example, if the group report is 90/100; individual sections: 20/25, then,

the individual grade = 90x60% + 20/25x100x40%= 86

**Team and leadership (10 points)**:

For functioning effectively on a team setting, each team member is expected to provide leadership in coordinating the effects of data acquisition, discussion and generating the lab report. The group leader is responsible to create a collaborative and inclusive environment, establish goals, plan tasks/assignment, and meet timeline for the delivery and completion of the group report.

***Rubrics for evaluating team efforts and leadership***

- (1) Lab attendant and actively engaged in data acquisition; Lab attendant is mandatory unless pre-arranged with the TA/course instructors for legitimate reasons (e.g., medical, conference).
- (2) Efforts as group leaders in planning tasks/assignment, establishing goals and delivery, and assuring the group members delivering.
- (3) Efforts as group members in completing the assignment/meeting the timeline.

Asynchronous attendance on a case-by-case basis given the usual documented one-off excused (illness, family emergency or extreme time zone difference) can be approved.
