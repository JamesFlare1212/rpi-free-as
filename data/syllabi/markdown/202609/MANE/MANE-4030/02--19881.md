## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Introduction to the design of mechanical components and integrated assemblies. Loads, stresses, and strains. Failure phenomena and material selection. Mechanical components including shafts, couplings, bearings, gears, springs, clutches, brakes, screws and fasteners, and bonded joints. (4 credit hours)

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

**Recommended Textbook:** *Mechanical Design of Machine Elements and Machines, Second Edition*, by Jack A. Collins, Henry Busby, and George Staab, John Wiley & Sons, 2010. ISBN: 9780470413036.

**Reference Textbook**: *Shigley’s Mechanical Engineering Design, Eleventh Edition*, by Richard G. Budynas and J. Keith Nisbet, McGraw-Hill Education, 2020. ISBN: 9780073398211.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course is designed to introduce the student to the design process through a thorough study of mechanical design. Problem definition and solution requiring both analysis and optimization are emphasized, while studying both the fundamentals of mechanical design and specific mechanical components.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Demonstrate an ability to apply knowledge of mathematics, science and engineering to analyze the stress and deflection in a variety of machine elements; Predict failure in machine elements subjected to static, cyclic, and impact loads; Design and analyze a variety of commonly used machine elements.
- • Demonstrate an ability to identify, formulate, and solve engineering problems involving stress and deflections in machine elements, identification of potential failure modes, and performing the appropriate analysis.
- • Demonstrate an ability to use the techniques, skills, and modern engineering tools necessary for engineering practice such as drawing free-body-diagrams, relating loading conditions to stresses, identifying potential failure modes and appropriate analysis techniques.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Exams:** There will be three in-class exams, all of which will count towards the final grade. There will also be an optional cumulative final exam during final exam week. During exams, you are permitted printouts of posted class notes, a physical copy of the textbook (Mechanical Design of Machine Elements and Machines, Second Edition, by Collins, Busby, and Staab) or printouts of pages from the textbook, your notes including homework solutions, a pocket calculator, and a straight edge. You are not permitted to use any other electronic devices such as laptops, tablets, phones, smartwatches, etc. You are also not permitted to bring printouts of solutions to any previous exams. Students are encouraged to create summary crib-sheets. The exams will emphasize problem solving and conceptual understanding required for problem solving.

The Final Exam will serve as a make-up exam for any missed exams. If a student misses any of the three in-class exams for any reason, the student must take the Final Exam, and the scores will be distributed as shown in the table above.
