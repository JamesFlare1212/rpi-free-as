### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

STUDENT LEARNING OUTCOMES

Upon successful completion of this course, students should be able to:

• Demonstrate an ability to perform vector and tensor analysis using the basic mathematical tools and

principles of continuum mechanics.

• Demonstrate an ability to mathematically represent and analyze the deformation of materials.

• Demonstrate an ability to perform stress analysis in linear elastic solids

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

GRADING AND EVALUATION

• Homework – 40%

Homework assignments will be regularly given, collected, and graded. Students are encouraged to

discuss the homework, but the actual solutions should be worked individually. If identical solutions

are turned in, all students involved will receive a zero for the assignment.

• Exams – 60%

There will be three 1-hour and 20-min exams. The first two will be in-class and the third will be

during final exam week. Each exam will count as 20% of your overall grade. The exams will be

open-book and open-note.

- ### Academic Integrity

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

ACADEMIC INTEGRITY

In the RPI Academic Integrity statement published by the Dean of Students Oﬀice, several forms of Aca-

demic Dishonesty are defined and the possible consequences are outlined. In this course, students are

encouraged to learn from one another and discuss the approach to solve homework problems; however,

the solution you submit must be your own. Students are furthermore not permitted to solicit external

help (e.g., online resources such as, but not limited to, Chegg or ChatGPT). Students are not allowed

to receive or give help on the exams. If found to be in violation with the Academic Integrity policy or

the policies of this course, all students involved will receive a zero on the affected assignment(s) or exam(s).
