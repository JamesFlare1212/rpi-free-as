## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The course covers tools and processes combining, Engineering design process, human centered design process, Lean design for six sigma process, Disney's Imagineering process, and a few home grown tools and processes to **ideate, innovate, design and develop new and innovative** product, technology etc. in **Biodesign** space.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

No text book. Slides contain information on tools and processes that will be used in this course. LMS has folders with additional reference material for this course.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Goals are:  
1\) to have students learn and practice tools and processes to ideate, innovate, design and develop new and innovative product, technology etc. that helps society.  
2\) Additionally, students learn how to protect their Intellectual property (IP) by learning and practicing how to determine patentability of their idea, and write disclosures for provisional patents, or utility patents etc.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

1)Select “Tools" for innovation, and how to choose the appropriate tool for an idea or problem  
2)Systematic “Process" for innovation to develop an innovative idea from concept to a minimum workable prototype  
3)MVP-Minimum Viable Product - iterate on fine tuning innovative idea, learning from each "iteration" using a ideate-build-learn cycle. Individual innovation concept project (Paper Design) & 3-D printing of sub-system or component.  
4)MVP-Minimum Viable Product - iterate on fine tuning innovative idea, learning from each "iteration" using a ideate-build-learn cycle. Teams of 1-4 members for Innovation final project (Prototype of system or sub-system)  
5)Demonstrate use of computational tool for innovation, design and analysis.  
6)Internal and External speakers to talk about Innovation, Intrapreneurship and Entrepreneurship.  
7)Engineering Innovation for Society Blitz – – 3 to 4 member team, societal problem, prototype, poster and paper in journal

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students would learn to ideate, innovate, design, and develop a new innovative product, technology, etc, in Biodesign space.
- Students will learn how to protect their intellectual property (IP) and apply for a provisional patent before they graduate.
- Student would learn how to write a proposal (research or SBIR / STTR etc.) to either a federal agency, or state agency etc. such, as NSF, NIH etc. before they graduate.
