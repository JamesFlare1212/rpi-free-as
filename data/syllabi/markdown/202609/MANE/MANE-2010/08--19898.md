## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The course introduces the fundamentals of the mechanics of deformable bodies, including basic concepts such as traction, stress and strain, as well as the formulation of linear solid mechanics, including equilibrium and constitutive equations. Analytic and numerical solutions for axial, bending, torsion, and combined loading of beams and beam structures are presented in tandem. Numerical solutions are obtained with a finite element computational tool and are compared with analytic solutions. Other problems discussed include pressure vessels, deflection and stability of beam models, and thermal stress.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

- Balance equations in solid mechanics
- Material behavior and failure theories
- Definition of boundary value problems and use of computational tools
- Torsion of circular shafts: analytic and numerical solutions
- Bending of beams: stress distribution solved analytically and computationally
- Eccentric axial loading: analytic and computational solutions
- Shear in beams – loading with transverse loads
- Combined loading: analytic and computational solutions
- Power transmission shafts
- Beam deflection and statically indeterminate systems
- Stability
- Stress concentration
- Factor of safety
- Pressure vessels
- Thermal stresses

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understand stress and strain in tensorial form, deformation, displacement, equilibrium and constitutive equations.
- Understand how to define boundary conditions in solid mechanics and perform stress and deformation analysis using a finite element-based computational tool.
- Perform stress analysis for beams and structures of beams subjected to various loads using analytic and numerical methods.
- Use classical Euler beam theory to calculate deflection of structures modeled by beam model and perform stability analysis.
- Evaluate what problems can be modeled by classical beam models and compare the results of this classical analysis with the computational solution.
