## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course provides an overview of emerging contaminants in soil and water, focusing on occurrence, adsorption, degradation, and fate and transport.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\) Define and explain key terminology, techniques, and recent advances in the field of environmental chemistry, environmental engineering, and contaminant fate and transport.
- 2\) Evaluate the effectiveness of adsorption and degradation technologies in the removal of emerging contaminates in the environment
- 3\) Define and explain key processes and governing mechanisms of the fate and transport of emerging contaminants.
- 4\) Apply knowledge and skills in emerging contaminants to identify, analyze, and solve environmental pollution problems.
- 5\) Use adsorption and degradation techniques necessary for the practice in water treatment and soil remediation field.
- 6\) Integrate principles of adsorption and degradation technologies to analyze, evaluate, and design solutions for monitoring and treating emerging contaminants in the environment.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

1\) Students are expected to arrive on time and attend all classes.  
2\) Students are expected to participate in discussions and have read assigned research articles.  
3\) Students are expected to conduct an independent literature review and complete a 4-8 pages final report.
