## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This undergraduate intermediate-level macroeconomics course examines the determination of national income, economic growth, inflation, unemployment, interest rates, and exchange rates within modern economies. Using *Macroeconomics* by N. Gregory Mankiw as the primary text, students develop analytical tools to understand both short-run economic fluctuations and long-run economic performance. Topics include economic growth theory, savings and investment, monetary and fiscal policy, aggregate demand and aggregate supply, business cycles, and the role of government in stabilizing the economy. Emphasis is placed on applying macroeconomic models to real-world economic issues, interpreting economic data, and evaluating policy responses to domestic and global economic challenges.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will demonstrate proficiency working with concepts in aggregate economic measures such as GDP, unemployment rates, etc.
- Students will demonstrate proficiency in working with concepts related to aggregate supply and aggregate demand modeling
- Students will demonstrate proficiency working with concepts related to the business cycle
- Students will demonstrate proficiency working with concepts related to monetary policy
