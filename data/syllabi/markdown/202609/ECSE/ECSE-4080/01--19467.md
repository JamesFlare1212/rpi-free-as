## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Power electronics is the application of power semiconductor devices to the control and conversion of electric power. It is the key enabler for various applications, such as data center power supply, renewable energy and energy storage, grid modernization, electrified transportation, space power, and so on. The course is an introduction to switched-mode power converters using power semiconductors. It provides a basic knowledge of power semiconductors and circuitries for the control and conversion of electrical power with high efficiency. These converters can change and regulate the voltage, current, or power; dc-dc converters, ac-dc rectifiers, and dc-ac inverters. The course targets for senior undergraduate and graduate students who are interested in power electronics.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Principle of energy conversion using power electronics

- DC conversion
- AC conversion
- Power semiconductors

DC-DC converters

- Basic converter circuits and operation
- Steady state analysis and equivalent circuit
- Ripple calculation and filter elements selection
- Case study - energy storage converter with simulation and analysis

Inverters

- Basic Inverter topology and operation
- Steady state analysis and equivalent circuit
- Modulation and harmonics analysis
- Case study - grid-tied inverter with simulation and analysis

Rectifiers

- Half-wave rectifiers
- Bridge rectifiers
- Phase-controlled converters
- Case study - charger based on boost power factor correction (PFC)

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- CLO1 - Understand the principles of DC and AC energy conversion
- CLO2 - Understand the principles and selection criteria of power semiconductors in power converters
- CLO3 - Understand steady state analysis and equivalent circuit of power electronics converters
- CLO4 - Understand basic circuits and operation principles of DC-DC conversion, inversion, and rectification
- CLO5 - Perform simulation and conduct stress, loss, and efficiency analysis using Plexim/PLECS

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Six homework, two projects, one mid-term exam, and one final exam are designed to measure the CLOs defined above.

- Homework 1 - Principles of energy conversion using power converters to measure CLO1
- Homework 2 - DC converter steady state analysis to measure CLO3
- Homework 3 - Power semiconductors realization to measure CLO2
- Homework 4 - DC converter steady state equivalent circuit to measure CLO-3
- Mid-term exam to measure CLO1, CLO2, and CLO3
- Project 1 - energy storage converter analysis to measure CLO4 and CLO5
- Homework 5 - AC energy conversion analysis to measure CLO3 and CLO4
- Project 2 - Inverter analysis to measure CLO4 and CLO5
- Homework 6 - Passive rectifier analysis to measure CLO4 and CLO5
- Final exam to measure all CLOs
