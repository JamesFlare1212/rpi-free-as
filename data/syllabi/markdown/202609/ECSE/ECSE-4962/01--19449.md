## Course Goals

### Goals:

This class introduces the fundamentals of integrated photonics, including guided wave theory, planar optical waveguides on various material platforms, and coupled-mode theory. Students will explore the operation principles of common photonic components such as optical beam splitters, couplers, wavelength division multiplexing (WDM) components, and optical switches. These building blocks are essential for applications ranging from fiber communication and optical interconnects in AI chiplets to data centers, photonic computing, and quantum optics

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Understand the physics of mode, polarization in waveguide 2. Analyze the guided wave propagation 3. Gain insight into the operational principles of passive integrated photonic components including optical power splitters, direction coupler, mode converter, micro-ring resonators (MRRs), and multimode interference (MMI) devices 4. Using integrated photonic simulation tools to design relatively simple photonic components

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

1\. Mini projects: each individual student will need to submit their own project report. Students will be assessed on mini-project reports.  
2\. Group projects: a small group consisting of 2 students per team will be formed. The student will submit a joint report on device design and performance analysis  
3\. Term project: a group of 2-3 students per team is formed. Students will be assessed on design and modeling of photonic components for system applications
