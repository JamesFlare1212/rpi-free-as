## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Students learn how government programs get proposed and planned. Techniques are used to identify, propose, build, and plan how to forecast the program using earned value management. Students learn how projects roll up in a program - integrating the DoD framework- by developing an Integrated Master Plan and Integrated Master Schedule. Students also employ financial, contractual, and systems thinking skills to build sound program plans, processes, and strategies.

**Credits Allocated:** 3 credit hours  
**Synchronous Sessions**  
As part of the RPI at Work model, this course offers synchronous sessions to provide you with the opportunity to connect with your instructor-mentor and classmates in a live setting. Please refer to the RPIStudio to view synchronous session dates and Zoom links.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Lead as a program manager through behaviors and skills that can contribute to superior performance in leading a program to success.
- Deploy organization strategy by linking the strategic vision of the organization to the program and effectively communicating this to all members of the program teams. This strategic vision guides day-to-day decision making.
- Plan programs and evaluate their performance through the creation of program plans which utilize operational concepts and the triple constraint.
- Manage contractual scope, developing the scope of work (SoW) and translating it into the integrated Master Plan which manages the top levels of the work breakdown structure (WBS).
- Critical Path Methodology is created using PERT and Network Diagram methods and seeing how it would incorporate in schedule.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

This course is comprised of three (3) overall projects. You will complete each project by completing multiple deliverables. The scenario, the challenge, the approach, and the deliverables for each project are detailed upfront, as are the criteria (rubric) that will be used to evaluate your work. You then interact with the provided learning resources and materials that will help guide you through the completion of the project. Synchronous sessions via ZOOM will also assist as you complete each project. In these sessions, you will have the opportunity to discuss options and ideas with other students as well as your Mentor.

- **Project 1:**Program Proposal & Contract– **31%**
- **Project 2:** Integrated Master Plan– **28%**
- **Project 3:** Integrated Master Schedule– **41%**

Each project builds on the one(s) prior - the goal is for you to successfully complete all three (3) projects while receiving feedback along the way. Therefore, all projects will be iteratively completed through multiple submissions and through multiple instances of feedback. Guidelines and further information about re-submissions will be covered in the details of your deliverables.

For each project and milestone, you will be given a "rubric" which simply translates to a list of the criteria your Faculty Mentor will use to evaluate your project. You can also use this list to self-evaluate your work before you submit. You will receive feedback from your Faculty Mentor if your submission can be improved.

If there are 10 elements in the rubric for a particular project, you must work toward a final deliverable that shows appropriate mastery of all 10 items - if you are good on 9, but not so good on 1, you must still achieve appropriate mastery on that project - in this program, each project builds on the prior so you want to make sure you are at the appropriate level before you move to the next. Otherwise, you may have problems in the next project.
