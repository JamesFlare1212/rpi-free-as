## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

In this course, students will develop an awareness and understanding of executive leadership from the perspective of the most senior levels of the organization. Students will understand why leaders focus on value creation internally and externally, what this looks like, and what it means to a broad range of stakeholders. They will gain exposure to a variety of strategic analysis techniques that enable leaders to create strategies and learn how these are developed cross-functionally in an organization. Since leaders are often confronted with evaluating information from a wide range of marketing sources, students will use a customer-based survey to do in-depth data analysis and think about the value and effectiveness of such a survey to determine business performance. Along with these business management projects, students will take a deep dive into the topic of leadership and ethics and will be given a unique opportunity to evaluate their own personal leadership vision, values and behaviors using a renowned emotional intelligence assessment. Lastly, pulling these topics and project experiences together, students will finish the course working comprehensively on a business case that requires transformational leadership for a full-scale business turnaround.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Develop and enhance personal leadership capabilities to influence and lead individuals and teams and realize organizational objectives.
- Explore ethics in leadership in a range of decision-making scenarios in various contexts and domains.
- Use the RPI Analytic Framework for decision-making with data, create sound responses to problems, make informed decisions, and develop analytical hypotheses.
- Understand and perform strategic analyses and how these are used to develop a sustainable competitive advantage.
- Diagnose, prescribe, plan, and execute organizational and transformational change.
- Communicate with purpose and clarity to present ideas in written and oral formats.
- Experience unique responsibilities and decision-making requirements at the chief-executive level of an organization.
- Learn the mechanics of executive-level presentations.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

This course uses a series of projects that serve as the context in which students learn and apply this learning. Specifically, this course has five major projects:

1. **Project 1 – Value Creation**: In this project, you will learn value creation for a range of important stakeholders of an organization and assemble an executive presentation using your company or organization as an example. ***Your submission will be a narrated PPTX presentation.***
2. **Project 2 – Strategic Analysis and Planning**: In this project, you will perform strategic analyses using multiple methods to contemplate and understand potential challenges facing your organization as it considers strategic planning, competitiveness, and growth. ***Your submission will be a PPTX document with Explanatory Notes.***
3. **Project 3 – You as a Leader:** As a follow-on project to your experience in the RLE, you will create a personal and strategic leadership development plan using a leading assessment tool. You will spend time evaluating your plans in real time, and with faculty-mentor coaching. In addition, you will spend time taking an outcome-based view of ethics in leadership through study and scenario-based application. ***Your submission will be a Word/Text based document.***
4. **Project 4 – Leaders Making Decisions with Data:** In this project, you will analyze a predetermined dataset and perform an investigation to determine how customers feel about a product in the market, and as an executive make inference on the state of the business. Using data summary/analysis techniques with visualization, you will draw out your conclusions and present deductive reasoning to support actions. ***Your submission will be a PPTX document with Explanatory Notes.***
5. **Project 5 – How the Leader Makes Things Happen:** In the culminating course project, you will be provided with a case study that will enable you to employ all elements of this course in a final project. You will be in the position of CEO who is required to perform a business turnaround, confronting financial, cultural, organizational, commercial, and operational challenges. You will create short and longer-term plans using the data provided, strategic analysis, and your understanding of a variety of organizational issues. ***Your submission will be a narrated PPTX document for a High-Level Board of Directors presentation.***
