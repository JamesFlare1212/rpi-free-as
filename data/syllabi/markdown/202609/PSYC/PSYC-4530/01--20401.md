## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 4000-level: Critically evaluate and synthesize approaches to data analysis and probabilistic modeling across limitations and opportunities. 6000-level: Independently extend and defend end-to-end data analysis workflows using multifaceted quantitative metrics and predictive checks.
- 4000-level: Implement and justify appropriate probabilistic models and visualization techniques to address inference problems using statistical tools such as Stan, PyMC, and brms. 6000-level: Implement and justify more advanced models (e.g., hierarchical/custom when needed) with reproducible diagnostics and validation.
- 4000-level: Explain and compare core principles of modern probabilistic modeling, with particular attention to their role in data analysis broadly and the cognitive sciences. 6000-level: Connect course principles to primary research and present a paper-style synthesis of a modeling approach and its implications.
- 4000-level: Design, execute, and summarize a complete data analysis workflow, including model specification, evaluation of computational faithfulness, model adequacy, and predictive performance. 6000-level: Complete an expanded, independent project with deeper model criticism and predictive evaluation; communicate results clearly and with technical precision in written and oral form.
