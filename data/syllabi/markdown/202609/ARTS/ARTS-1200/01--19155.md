## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course in an introductory studio course in drawing techniques and concepts. Students will develop perceptual and conceptual drawing skills through exercises, critiques, slide lectures, and strong drawing practice. This class stresses building hand-eye coordination and understanding of spatial relationships, perspective, and proportion through direct observation. Assignment subjects include still-life, architecture, live model and landscape. No prior experience is assumed or expected.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The emphasis of the work in this class will be on the development of skills in perception, communication, and design as they relate to the medium of drawing. Students will be able to demonstrate skills in observational looking, basic handling of various drawing media and processes of image translation through skilled mark-making with an understanding of composition.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Measuring techniques
- Line quality
- Proportional accuracy
- Structural systems of various simple forms
- Development of surface and texture
- Translation of light information through the manipulation of value
- Expanding knowledge of Art History

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Assignments and homework will be graded on both your *effort* (how much time and care given to project) and *process* (following instruction, demonstrating understanding of concepts, showing investigation)

Each assignment will be graded with the following criteria: (\_\_\_/100)

Effort toward accuracy (proportion, measuring): 40 points

Effort toward craftsmanship (quality of marks (or) value/sense of light): 30 points

Effort toward composition and project concepts (balance/arrangement of subjects/investigation of ideas): 30 points

If your assignments and homework are not hanging at the beginning of class on crit day then it will be considered late. Unfinished work will not be critiqued. You will only receive a grade for finished work. Late work will suffer in grade by 10 points.

**Assignment check points:** Students will be required to present “progress” check points on major assignments, usually due the class before crit. These are referred to as “organizational drawings.” This is a helpful point in the project to take pause and see how the structure, compostion and other project concepts are progressing, If your organizational drawing is not completed or presented when it’s due, you will recieve five points off of your overall grade.

**Class Participation** means you are actively contributing to discussions and class critiques by showing interest in what others make, asking questions about the assignment goals and outcomes, and using learned vocabulary. It also applies to the explanation and presentation of your own work. At the beginning of each critique, we will have small “peer to peer discussions” before we begin where you will get the opportunity to discuss with a classmate (one on one) their work at close looking. If you miss this segment of the class, your participation grade will suffer.
