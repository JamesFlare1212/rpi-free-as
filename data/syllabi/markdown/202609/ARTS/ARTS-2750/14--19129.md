## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Private applied piano lessons are offered to provide a fine arts experience for credit. Throughout the semester 50 minutes weekly individual lessons, will improve the students' ability to read, discuss, interpret, and perform a plethora of music composed for their individual instrument.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Texts and repertoire are chosen by the instructor according to student's skillset and likings.

All participants in class will have access to the practice rooms on the third floor in West Hall and the first floor in the DCC (Darrin Communications Center) as well as additional rooms in West Hall during the scheduled class times and upon reservation with the instructor and Zoe Evans (107 WH).

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Students in the private piano lesson program will improve their ability to read music notation and interpret a variety of musical works. We will focus on expressive and musical elements of interpretation in a variety of historical styles and genres.

The goal is to further the technical and musical expertise on the instrument and create an independent musician for life.

Students will improve listening skills, as well as mature in their ability to work at once independently.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Music notation
- Historical interpretation of different periods and styles
- Gain technical skills on the piano
- Improve listening skills
- Ability to work independently
- Expressive techniques

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

The class is based on an interpretive context and practice of all musical concepts such as notation, structure, dynamics, expressive techniques, etc. The aim is an analytical and performative engagement with musical texts, their historically grounded expressive codes for the diﬀerent repertoires, and their specificities for the piano.

By the end of the class, you should be able to depict theoretical, technical, expressive, and historical principles within musical scores and learn to apply techniques and strategies to allow for their renditions. These skills will serve you in developing your musical practice and performance skills and will be useful in all your future music practice.  
The instructor will issue 3 progress reports through semester (week 5, week 10, week 15) in the process of determining musical progress and semester grade.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

This is a music-reading-intensive and playing-intensive class. Attendance is 60%, effort and engagement in class 20% and individual practice 20%
