## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Wind Symphony is a laboratory ensemble in which students encounter and perform music from diverse styles, genres, and historical periods written for symphonic winds and wind band. Students in the Wind Symphony will perform in concert at least once per semester and may participate in special activities, events, projects, and collaborations with featured guest artists. The Wind Symphony is open to all RPI students on an auditioned basis.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Student Learning Outcomes Students will meaningfully engage in the production, cultural expression, and study of the creative arts. Students will demonstrate their knowledge through the production of an artistic artifact and/or performance, and through analysis of historical and/or theoretical frameworks. Wind Symphony members will contribute to the ensemble as a whole and progress as individual musicians through study and practice of the following major areas: 1. Rehearsal Etiquette Students will demonstrate their understanding of rehearsal etiquette and protocol by: • Arriving to all rehearsals on time with music, their instrument, and a pencil • Taking instruction from the conductor, section leaders • Playing with appropriate articulations, dynamics, rhythms, and pitches and making changes as necessary throughout the rehearsal process. • Playing with correct posture and form throughout the rehearsal • Asking music-related questions directed towards the appropriate person to clarify understanding • Notating all musical markings given in your music with pencil • Being mindful and aware of what to practice at home for the next rehearsal and taking practice parts home • Waiting respectfully as the director addresses questions throughout the ensemble • Actively listening to the others and adjusting accordingly • Turning off and refraining from using cell phones 2. Demonstration of Musical Skills • Theory • Reading and interpreting musical notation • Playing correct pitches and rhythms • Musical vocabulary • Ensemble skills • Understanding of historical and cultural context • Expression • Performance practice • Articulation • Dynamic contrast Students will demonstrate their informed understanding of these areas through active participation in rehearsals and concerts. 3. Performance Etiquette Students will demonstrate their understanding of appropriate performance etiquette by: • Arriving to all performance call times on time with music, instrument, and proper concert attire • Playing with all the musical elements as rehearsed including articulation, phrasing, intonation, and dynamics • Being engaged with individual sections, ensemble, and the conductor at all times • Observing protocol discussed for bows and acknowledgements following the performance

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Homework**

Individual practice is essential to the success and progress of the orchestra. You are expected to practice at least 4-5 hours per week individually outside of orchestra. Daily practice builds confidence and familiarizes you with your part and your instrument. It is best not to overload on a practice session on just one day; rather, practice every day. Frequency beats duration.

**Starred Passages**

In rehearsal (either tutti or sectional), if after three attempts at a passage it does not substantially improve, the passage will be starred. When a passage is starred, it will be the first thing rehearsed at the next rehearsal. The demonstrating substantial improvement of the starred passage will be integrally tied to the musician’s preparation/improvement grade for that day.

**Principal Positions**

Seating and assignment of principal players will be assigned by the instructor.

**Respectful Communication**

Creating a positive learning environment for all is critical to the ensemble’s growth and learning. Distracting communication of any sort will not be permitted. Talking in class is only condoned so long as it is music/course-related discussion (e.g., resolving a bowing question) that is done quietly and without disrupting rehearsal. If there is disruptive communication that is not course-related and/or significantly distracts from the flow of rehearsal, the individual(s) will be asked to leave class immediately without warning and their grade will be lowered one-half letter grade per dismissal (e.g., from A to A-). Using a cellphone/mobile device in rehearsal is considered disrespectful to the rehearsal and learning environment and the student will immediately be asked to leave.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

***Attendance Policy***

Excellent ensemble performance occurs when individual members are responsible for detailed preparation with the full group. Orchestra rehearsals are meant for putting the pieces together, refining, polishing, getting used to playing as a cohesive ensemble, to hearing and listening to everything around you as well as to reading the conductor, NOT a time to be learning notes and rhythms. Attendance, punctuality, and full participation are mandatory for rehearsals and performances, except in the following excused cases:

1. Emergencies: family emergency or hospitalization.

2. Serious illness: if you display symptoms of and/or test positive for COVID-19, please stay home. Please make sure you notify the instructor and teaching assistant via e-mail in advance of class.

For the first unexcused absence, your final grade will be docked half a letter grade; the second, docked 1.5 letter grades (e.g. A to B-), a third unexcused absence results in a failing grade for the class.

Lateness will not be permitted. Class begins at the class start time, and late comers will be marked late immediately following the start of class unless they otherwise notify the professor and teaching assistant. For every 2 late arrivals, your final grade will be docked half a letter grade.

If you miss a dress rehearsal or concert for unexcused reasons, your final grade will be docked 25%. For every two times that you arrive late or miss part of a rehearsal for unexcused reasons, your final grade will be docked by half a letter grade. Your grade presumes that you’re in rehearsal and participating fully, so any lack of participation (no instrument, not notating rehearsal markings, etc.) will penalize your grade, as will any behavior that conflicts with our rehearsal etiquette standards.
