## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Speculative fiction suffuses our culture, society, and politics. What can account for this? In a word: *worldbuilding*. Speculative fiction is fundamentally a literature of difference. It artfully represents possible, plausible, implausible, and impossible worlds that depart from our own reality and, in so doing, taps into a host of desires for (and fears about) the future. In this course, we will read a selection of classic and new speculative fictions, all of which foreground the genre’s unique capacity for worldbuilding, from *Dune*’s Arakkis to a near-future planet Earth.

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. ### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. You must trust that I am making appropriate decisions about the structure, content, and policies of my course. And I must trust that your work is their own. Violations of this trust undermine the intellectual rigor and integrity of your education. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities defines various forms of academic dishonesty with which you must familiarize yourself.

Plagiarism is the presentation of others’ ideas, words, or any other work as your own—without, or with incomplete, attribution. Anyone who plagiarizes or commits other types of academic dishonesty will be referred to the Dean of Students. Students will also earn 0% on any assignments related to the violation. Visit Student Rights, Responsibilities, and Conduct for more information on RPI’s Academic Integrity Policy.

Generative AI is categorically banned in this class. Assignments in this class are designed to help you cultivate an authorial voice of your own, build confidence in your original ideas, and improve and refine those ideas with others. The use of generative AI undermines these goals, and its use will be treated as a violation of academic integrity.
