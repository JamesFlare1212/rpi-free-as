## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**This course focuses on the kinetics of materials, i.e., the time-dependent behaviors of materials transport and transformation. The mathematical formulations, numerical solutions and atomic mechanisms will be covered for important kinetic processes in various material systems. The application of kinetics in material synthesis, particularly solidification of metallic systems, will be discussed in details.**

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

*Phase Transformations in Metals & Alloys*, by D.A. Porter and K.E. Easterling

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Part I Foundation  
¨ Course overview  
¨ Review of Thermodynamics  
¨ Review of Phase Transformation and Phase Diagrams  
¨ Chemical reaction kinetics  
Part II Transportation  
¨ Atomic Mechanisms of Diffusion  
¨ Mathematical Description of Diffusion (steady, non-steady)  
¨ Representative Diffusion Problems and Solutions  
¨ Experimental Measurement of Diffusion  
Part III Transformation  
¨ Boundaries and Interfaces  
¨ Solidification: Nucleation and Growth  
¨ Solidification: Spinodal decomposition  
¨ Extreme Solidification: Metallic Glasses  
¨ Diffusional Transformations in Solids: Homogeneous vs Heterogeneous Growth  
¨ Diffusional Transformations in Solids: Precipitate Growth  
¨ Diffusional Transformations in Solids: TTT Diagrams  
¨ Diffusionless Transformations in Solids: Martensite Growth  
¨ Reaction Diffusion coupling  
¨ Diffusion in non-ideal systems

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • determine whether kinetics should be considered in a particular process or transformation, in addition to the thermodynamic driving force
- • apply specific diffusion solutions to practical engineering problems
- • understand the atomic-level mechanism of mass transport and transformation
- • apply empirical solidification equations to engineering problems
- • differentiate diffusional transformation and diffusionless transformation
