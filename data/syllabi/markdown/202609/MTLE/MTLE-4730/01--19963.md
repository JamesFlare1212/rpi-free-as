## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- select appropriate data-science methods leveraging statistical and mathematical foundations
- apply data-science techniques to research and engineering design problems in materials science
- assess the uncertainty of conclusions derived from data-driven methods

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

- Weekly quizzes on data/model interpretation and underlying mathematical concepts, 40%
- Project development and code/analysis stand-up meetings with peer feedback, 40%
- Final presentation on term project showcasing a material informatics approach applied to a real-world materials engineering problem, 20%

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

In this class, collaboration is strongly encouraged for the project work, but final submitted work should be written individually. All discussions and references consulted should be properly acknowledged on each submission. In-class quizzes should of course reflect individual work with no communication. A grade of zero will be given on the first assignment where a violation is detected, and a referral to the Dean of Students. If there is a subsequent infraction the student will receive a grade of “U” for the course and referral to the appropriate Dean.

*Additional Policy on use of Generative AI*:

Students are encouraged to use generative AI tools as part of their research and preparation for their project work, but should be prepared to explain every line of code and interpret the resulting output in the project meetings. Consequently, grading of the projects will weigh student understanding over quality of the results. ‘Vibe coding’ without understanding the code is strongly discouraged. AI use should be treated exactly like collaboration with a classmate, and it should be acknowledged and cited properly.

Students are not allowed to use AI in the quizzes. Violations of this policy will be treated as academic misconduct. If you have any questions about this policy or if you are unsure whether a particular use of AI is acceptable, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
