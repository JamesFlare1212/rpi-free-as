## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- STRUCTURAL KNOWLEDGE; VERTICAL LOADS: Ability to demonstrate and communicate structural knowledge through the active testing and experimentation with physical structural models under vertical loads.
- STRUCTURAL KNOWLEDGE; MULTI-DIRECTIONAL LOADS: Ability to demonstrate, analyze, and communicate structural knowledge through the active testing and experimentation with physical structural models under multi-directional load
- MATERIAL KNOWLEDGE: Ability to demonstrate, communicate material knowledge through drawings, models and direct engagement with various physical materials

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Assignments and quizzes will be issued as per the course schedule and count towards the  
final class grade as per the course learning outcome matrix. Below is a summary of the intent  
of each assessment measure:  
Quizzes (individual)  
Short quizzes will be provided at the completion of each in-class discussion. Quizzes will be  
based on the comprehension of the individual readings and will be presented in multiple  
choice, multiple answer and True / False formats.  
Assignment #1: Joint (individual) / Column (group)  
As part of their wood shop section training, each student will be assigned the making of a X  
Cross Lap Joint detail. This assignment is considered as a precursor of Assignment #1.  
Working in your assigned groups, students will design, build and document a scaled column  
considering the fundamental forces and principles described in the class lectures.  
Assignment #2: Cast / Ground (group)  
As part of the class deliverables, each group will be assigned the making of a plaster tile. This  
assignment is considered as a precursor of Assignment #2.

Syllabus 7 of 7 Arch 4320 / 5320  
Working in your assigned groups, students will design, build and document a ground  
condition for their designed column considering the fundamental forces and principles  
described in the class lectures.  
Assignment #3: Full Scale (team)  
Working in your assigned groups, students will analyze a specific detail from their column and  
/or ground exercise, determine how to scale it to a 1:1 scale, design and build the connection  
detail. The process should be documented and presented along with the final detail.

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).
