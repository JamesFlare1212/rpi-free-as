## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Outcome 1: Characterize Research Processes - (BArch + Graduate)Students will have learned to critically characterize a research problem, its state of the art analysis, research hypothesis and methods in written, graphical and oral form through technical report, poster and presentation formats. (Graduate) Students will integrate and apply research processes for the development of the Masters Thesis with a focus on quantitative methods
- Outcome 2: Perform a Literature Review - (BArch + Graduate) Students will be able to perform a literature review for their research problem and state of the art analysis (Graduate) Students will develop a preliminary literature review applied to the Masters Thesis
- Outcome 3: Design Data Graphics for Research - (BArch + Graduate) 3. After taking this course, students will be able to design static and interactive data graphics for research (Graduate) Students will produce data graphics for the development of the Masters Thesis
- Outcome 4: Design Experiments for Post-Occupancy - (BArch + Graduate) After taking this course, students will learn to design experiments measuring building system performance within the post-occupancy phase of buildings (Graduate) Students will design an experiment that applies to development of the Masters Thesis
- Outcome 5: Practice Ethical Methods of Research - (BArch + Graduate) Students will have learned to adhere to ethical methods of research practice as outlined in the Rensselaer Student Handbook and the course textbook requirements. (Graduate) Students will integrate and apply ethical methods for the development of the Masters Thesis

## Policies

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).
