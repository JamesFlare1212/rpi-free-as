### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

## Student Learning Outcomes

1. Using matrix decompositions to condition the solution of complex and large-scale algebraic equations and increase the accuracy of the solution.
2. Solving large-scale multivariate nonlinear optimization problems by using gradient-based optimization methods.
3. Setting up data-driven multiple linear regression problems and estimate unknown model parameters.
4. Estimating unknown model parameters of mechanistic models based on experimental data.

## Course Assessment Measures

**Projects:**

There will be a total of 3 project assignments, which contain understanding type problems and problems that are comparable to what we cover in class, based on the methods covered in class. Project 1 covers material discussed during the first four weeks and Project 2 is based on the material presented during the first 10. Project 3 covers topics covered in week twelve to fourteen. Each project submission is to be *submitted on LMS* on the due date up until 23:59pm. ***Late submissions will not be accepted and receive a grade of zero, as there are at least two weeks to work on each assignment***.

**Midterm Examination (October 3rd):**

There is one midterm examination. It covers the lectures in weeks one to five on fundamentals of numerical linear algebra as outlined in the course calendar. Midterm examination is scheduled during class for a duration of 80 minutes. Midterm exams contain understanding type questions about the methods and solving related problems.

**Final Examination:**

There is one final examination that comprehensively assesses the course material. The exam will include understanding type questions about the methods covered in this course and also present plots of modeling results that are to be interpreted. The final exam is during final’s week.
