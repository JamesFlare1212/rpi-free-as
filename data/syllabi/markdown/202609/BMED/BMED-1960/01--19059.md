## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course is an introduction to the field of biomedical engineering, the biomedical engineering specializations available at RPI, and the biomedical engineering faculty members at RPI. Students will learn more about unmet needs in BME, get an introduction to basic professional writing and presentation, understand the resources they have as BME students at RPI, and the opportunities they have after graduation.

- Students will learn more about the different fields and specializations within biomedical engineering
- Students will learn about unmet needs in biomedical engineering
- Students will learn how to find and research unmet needs
- Students will learn how to summarize technical information concisely in written form

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

This course is designed to aid students in critical thinking skills, growing analytical prowess, and expanding creativity. Therefore, reliance on AI tools to perform critical thinking, analysis, and/or creative exploration will adversely affect your learning process and education. However, used in appropriate ways AI tools can greatly expand the course related content that a student is exposed to, can aid the student in organizing their notes on course materials (with associated additional online content), and be used as a study companion (suggesting connections and interpretations of course related notes). However, generative AI is known to output false statements, even though its tone implies confidence and correctness. Thus, all AI output should be used as a suggestion and verified for correctness through other scholarly sources.

Use of AI in this class is limited and should not be used:

- 1\) During classroom instruction sessions (unless otherwise advised to do so).
- 2\) To directly write (based on AI output) text or create images for any paper, assignment, or examination response (unless otherwise advised to do so).
- 3\) On other class projects or activities that you are explicitly asked by the class instructor during the course to refrain from using AI on

Use of AI in this class is permitted for:

- 1\) Identification of other non-classroom materials and content relevant to your classwork and/or project/assignments (but these additional sources should be verified outside of the AI tool and adequately referenced)
- 2\) Use in translating a non-English source so that you are able to read it (again, making sure to reference the source)
- 3\) Analysis and organizing personal notes on class materials and content. However, please note that directly uploading course materials (either from textbooks and courseware/slides) may violate copyright laws and/or codes of conduct.

If you are unsure of the proper use of AI in this class, please consult the instructor. When using AI in a permitted fashion please state the specific tool used and its specific use at the bottom of your assignment. AI-produced text is prohibited in assignments and exam responses.

If AI tools are used as part of a learning accommodation, in consultation with student services, please speak with the instructor about your needs.

“AI tool” refers to generative AI service such as those operated by OpenAI (ChatGPT), Google (Gemini), Anthropic (Claude), and other companies; any other service that generates text, code, images, audio, or other output; any service that summarizes material submitted to it; and any service that edits or corrects material that you submit to it (such as Grammarly or CoPilot), other than the standard spellcheck features.
