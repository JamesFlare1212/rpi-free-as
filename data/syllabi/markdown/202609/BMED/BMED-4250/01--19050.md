## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Quantitative analysis of transport phenomena in physiological systems, including fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, and mass transfer. Fluid statics, mass and energy conservation, laminar and turbulent flow, microscale and macroscale analytical methods, mass transport with biochemical reactions, applications to transport in tissue and organs.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Basic Transport Phenomena in Biomedical Engineering, 4th Ed. Ronald L. Fournier

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

## Course Calendar

### Calendar Details:

**Course Calendar:**

Week 1-2: Basic concepts and principles including mass conservation. First law of thermodynamics; Readings: Chapter 1.1-1.4; Chapter 2.1-2.2

**Quiz 1**

Weeks 3-4: Second law of thermodynamics and fugacity; Chapter 2.3-2.4, 2.6

***Exam 1**

**Potential Places Where Academic Dishonesty May Take Place In This Course:**

1. Signing an attendance sheet for a friend who has missed class.
2. Changing exam answers after the exam has been graded to receive additional points in a dishonest manner.
3. Lying to a professor or TA concerning reasons for missing an exam/assignment.
4. Since the course requires homework assignments, any instance of plagiarism or copying other’s work word for word without proper citation is academic dishonesty.
5. Cheating on an exam/quiz.
