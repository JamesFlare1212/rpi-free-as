## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

### **This is a hybrid course where students enrolled in BMED 4510 (undergraduate) and BMED 6510 (graduate) meet simultaneously with different level of difficulty at undergraduate & graduate levels for assignments, quiz and examination Course description:**

Mechanical regulation of biological systems will be discussed. Topics include Principles and concepts of mechanobiology; Embryogenesis and histogenesis of tissues with a focus on the role of epigenetics/mechanical forces on development; Physical forces at cellular, tissue, and organ level; Mechanical regulation of cellular behavior, tissue growth and organ development in multiple systems including skeletal, neural, and renal/cardiovascular systems; Limits of mechanical regulation: Biochemical influences; Application of mechanobiology to tissue regeneration.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

**Objective:**

To introduce the principles and concepts of mechanical regulation of biological systems applied to tissue regeneration with special emphasis on skeletal, neural, and other systems such as renal/cardiovascular systems.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

**COURSE OUTLINE**

# Principle and concepts of mechanobiology

Definition

Theories on relationship between form &function

Evolution of Mammalian Skeleton from a Mechanobiological perspective

# Embryogenesis and Histogenesis of tissues

Embryogenesis

Skeletal Pattern formation

# Physical forces at cellular, tissue and organ level

Stress and Strain Analysis

Cellular level forces and deformation

Tissue level forces and deformation

Organ level forces and Strain histories

**Mechanobiology of Brain**

Mechanical Properties of Brain Tissue

Cellular Mechanotransduction in Neural Cells

Neurodevelopment and Mechanics, TBI

Neurodegeneration and Mechanobiology

**Mechanobiology of Articular Cartilage, IVD and Fibrocartilage**

Formation, Growth and Development

Mechanical Regulation ECM remodeling (Tissue Level)

Mechanical Regulation cellular behavior and function (Cell Level)

Mechanical loading & Osteoarthritis

# Mechanobiology of Bone

# Formation, Growth and Development of Long Bones

Mechanical regulation of Ossification & Organogenesis of bone (Organ-Level)

Mechanical regulation & adaptation of lamellar arrangement in bone (Tissue Level)

Mechanical regulation of bone cell(s) biology & function (Cell Level)

Mechanical regulation of bone modeling & remodeling (Homeostatis)

**Concept and Application of Strain Energy Regulation in Biology**

Mathematical model for growth and adaptation of Cortical bone

Continuum model approach to mechanically regulated remodeling of cancellous bone

**Application of mechanobiology to tissue engineering:**

Cell cultures and mechanical perturbation

Mechanobiology of Muscle Regeneration

Cyclic compression vs Distraction osteogenesis

Skeletal tissue regeneration at bone-implant interfaces  
**Limits of mechanical regulation: Biochemical influences, Cell and Gene Therapy applications**
