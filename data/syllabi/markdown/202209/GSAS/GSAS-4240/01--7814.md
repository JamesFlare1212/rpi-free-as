## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An intermediate level games writing course, building off the core principles and introductory elements of Introduction to Game Storytelling. This course delves deeper into the working practices of games writers, exploring adaptation, in-game dialogue, character creation, story beats, game industry writing tests, and writing software.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

No set texts.  
All relevant materials will be cited during the course.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The course is designed to provoke analytical consideration of narrative elements in games, and to expose students to the practice of creating narrative elements within a framework of guidance and industry experience.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Topics Taught

- \- Adaptation
- \- Character Creation
- \- In-game dialogue and editing
- \- Writing a premise and synopsis
- \- Creating a beat sheet
- \- Writing screenplays
- \- How to take industry tests
- \- Working with writing software
