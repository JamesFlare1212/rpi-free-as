### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Course Description:**

Comprehensive treatment of conduction, convection, including boiling and condensation, and radiation heat transfer. Thermal system design and performance, including heat exchangers. Emphasis is on physical and mathematical modeling of engineering systems for application of modern analytical and computational solution methods.

**Course Objectives:**

- q *To impart an understanding of conduction, convection and radiation heat transfer for application to several engineering fields.*
- q *To familiarize students with physical and mathematical modeling for engineering applications**.*
- q *To provide an understanding of concepts and applications of different modes of heat transfer.*

**Learning Outcomes:**

*By the end of the course, students will be able to:*

- q *Understand and solve relevant engineering application problems through use of* *physical and mathematical modeling**.*
- q *Be able to select series solutions methodologies to address and evaluate various sets of engineering problems.*
- q *Be able to apply heat transfer models to solve problems in several engineering disciplines*

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Academic Integrity and Rules of Behavior**

Any form of cheating will result in an F. Violations will be reported immediately to the Dean of Students Office.

I do not expect that we will incur any issues with academic integrity. With that being said, if such issues do arise, the proper measures, laid out in the student handbook and discussed in Section I, will be taken.

Student-teacher relationships are built on trust, and this trust is especially critical for blended classes. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments which students turn in are their own effort. Acts which violate this trust, undermine the educational process. An extensive and general discussion of this subject is contained in the Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and Graduate Student supplement to the handbook.

Evidence of copying another person’s work is considered a serious violation of professional and academic ethics. Violations will be reported immediately to the Dean of Students Office.

**A special note regarding the use of solution manuals:** The use of solution manuals to solve HW, or test problems is prohibited. Using solutions manuals is considered plagiarism and/or copying and is in direct violation of the Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities, as well as the Graduate Student supplement to the handbook. All assignments showing evidence of the use of solution manuals will receive a zero and, under my discretion, may also be reported to the Dean of Students Office.

**Buckley Amendment**

The Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (Buckley Amendment) guarantees privacy to students, particularly in the areas of grades and performance.
