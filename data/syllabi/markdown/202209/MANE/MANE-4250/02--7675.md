## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Space Vehicle Design introduces the major elements of the spacecraft system design process from proposal preparation through detailed specification and test design. Students are organized into design teams associated with different subsystems and tasks, to develop a solution to a Space Vehicle system’s problem of practical interest, by drawing on their background in aerospace engineering science, machine design, and manufacturing methods. Topics include problem definition and requirement analysis, design specifications, concept development, reliability, consideration of alternative solutions, risk, materials considerations, use of appropriate standards, mission analysis, project management and presentation skills, consideration of health, safety, environments, social, economic, etc. issues as appropriate. This is a communication-intensive and writing-intensive course.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

-To foster design strategies for space vehicles that encourage innovation.

Introduce the students to scientific space missions as well as complex space system design.

\- Give the students the opportunity to work on a project that is of real interest in the aerospace engineering and scientific community.

\- Provide the students with the work environment they would find in their future careers as aerospace engineers.

\- Expose the students to the different realities of federal agencies sponsoring various classes of spacecraft.

Practice critical thinking and acting as an engineer

- – Acquiring skill in working with others as a member of a team
- – Developing skills in expressing oneself orally and in writing
- – Development of basic project management skills
- – Production of viable ethical designs often requiring substantial consideration of non-technical issues (health, environmental, social, cultural, economic, etc.).

## Other Course-Specific Information

### Additional Course Information:

**STK**

***Systems Tool Kit*** **(STK)** produced by AGI [ http://www.agi.com ] is a highly capable, free modeling environment which currently enjoys wide usage by major companies within the aerospace sector. These users include engineers, mission analysts, software developers, etc. to **model** complex aerospace systems—such as aircraft, missiles, **satellites** and their sensors—**analyze** mission simulations and **visualize** dynamic datasets in 4D (X,Y,Z,Time). This tool (particularly the “*Astrogator*” module of STK) will be very helpful in aiding you in major aspects of the flight mechanics necessary to your design effort. Thus, it is really essential that at least one member of each design team become proficient in the use of STK Astrogator. Due to the wide usage of STK within the aerospace sector, being able to put “STK Certified” or “proficient with STK” on your résumé is a very good thing. As such it is in your best interest to become proficient in the use of STK. To promote this, you can receive 5% extra credit added to your individual final course score by becoming STK certified.

Directions for downloading a student version of STK from AGI, and receiving your license key will be provided in a subsequent e-mail.
