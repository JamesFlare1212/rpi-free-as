## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An undergraduate-level course  
A laboratory course covering topics in instrumentation, radiation detection and dosimetry associated with X-ray, gamma-ray, applied physics, fluid dynamics, and spectroscopy. Statistics of random events, error propagation, and error analyses are emphasized. Lab attendance is required along with formal written lab reports, which include data error analysis.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

**Referene:**

Class handouts, Lab manual.

Reference textbook: “Radiation Detection and Measurement” by Glenn F. Knoll, Third Edition, Wiley 1999.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

- To ensure basic skills in data acquisition and analysis in a laboratory setting.
- To get practical experience in nuclear spectroscopy, fluid flow, radiation interaction, detection and measurement
- To enhance skills in technical writing and communication

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Proportional Counter; Gamma Spectroscopy using NaI(Tl) and HPGe Detectors; Air Sampling for Radon;

Fluid flow in circulating loop; alpha spectroscopy; X-ray fluorescence, Compton scattering; and Calibration of Gamma Source and Radiation Survey Meter

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Students will be able to conduct experiments using radiation sources and fluid flow (3.6)
- • Demonstrate an understanding of radiation detection, spectroscopy and measurement techniques (3.6, 3.7)
- • Students will learn, document, analyze and report the experimental results and their associated errors (3.6, 3.7)

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

As previously noted, since students will be conducting experiments in rotated teams, similarity in results among different groups is expected. Discussing the experiment results during and after class is also encouraged. However, each group lab report and associated data analysis must be done on an individual basis. Evidence merely transcribing other groups or previous lab reports will be ground for failure of that report.
