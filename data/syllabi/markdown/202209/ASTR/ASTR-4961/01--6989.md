## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Cosmology is the study of the Universe as a whole. In this course we will try to answer the following questions (among others): What are the contents of the Universe? How has the history of the Universe looked until now? How will the Universe evolve from here? How come one location in the Universe is not more special than another? And how come one direction is not more special than another?

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Upon successful completion of the course, the student will have gained an insight into the founda- tions of cosmology.
- The student will be able to detail the evidence supporting the Hot Big Bang theory and will have an understanding of inflationary cosmology.
- The student will also be able to make predictions from cosmological models which can be compared against observations.
- Furthermore, the student will be able to use modern observational data to constrain the cosmological parameters and understand the physical basis behind their determination.
