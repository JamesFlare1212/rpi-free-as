## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Build a foundation of knowledge in the design principles of enclosure systems including monolithic systems, cavity systems, and openings.
- • Use this foundation to explore new enclosure systems and find opportunities for innovation.
- • Develop documentation and representational techniques appropriate to the communication of the materials, assembly, and performance of enclosure systems.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Attendance and participation**

Attendance for lectures and pinups will be taken for each class and accounts for 20% of your total grade. If a course cannot be attended live or in-person, all lecture slides and recordings will be posted to the course website however you must notify the instructor to account for attendance.

**Group Labs and Design Charette**

Throughout the semester, students in groups of 3-4 will work together to complete the schematic design of a building wall assembly. By the end of the semester, students will apply concepts learned in the class and test them using energy modeling tools. Lab time will be used to apply the concepts and calculations represented in the lecture in an iterative schematic design process. All members of the group are expected to participate equally and the work should demonstrate that.

**Midterm and Final Exam**

Both Midterm and Final exam will be administered through LMS on the date noted on the syllabus. The exams will be based on content from the lecture presentations as well as calculations and methods exercised in the labs. They will consist of true/false, multiple choice and multiple answer.
