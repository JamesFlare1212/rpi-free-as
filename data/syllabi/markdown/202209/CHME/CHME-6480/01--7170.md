## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The goal of this course is to explore the connections between the behavior of single molecules and their interactions and macroscopic non-Newtonian behavior. We will discuss microscopic models of these systems and the impact on macroscopic behavior. The first half of the course will focus on the methods and techniques for modeling and connecting the micro and macro behavior. The second half of the course will discuss the application of these methods and techniques to a wide range of systems.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

“Understanding Rheology” by Faith A. Morrison, Oxford University Press, 2001.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- compare and contrast the response of Newtonian and non-Newtonian fluids
- demonstrate knowledge of constitutive relations for non-Newtonian fluids
- demonstrate knowledge of the microstructure of non-Newtonian fluids
- identify an example of non-Newtonian behavior and apply the course methods to quantify how microstructure gives rise to the non-Newtonian response

## Other Course-Specific Information

### Additional Course Information:

**Final Course Project**

As part of the course, you need to complete a final course project. This project consists of a written document describing original work in which you apply the course concepts to a complex fluids problem that interests you. There will also be an oral presentation of the work.

**Important Due Dates:**

Thursday, October 6th – By this date, you must meet with me outside of class to discuss the topic you have chosen for your project.

Thursday, November 3rd – A one-page update of the written project is due.

Thursday, December 1st – Final written project is due.

December 5th and 8th – These class periods are reserved for oral presentations. The day and time for each person’s presentation will be chosen at random.

Update due on November 3 (12.5% of the project grade)

To help with the progression of the project, a one page update is due November 3rd. In this update you will discuss the project, progress that has been made, problems that have been encountered, and how those problems will be addressed.

Written project requirements (50% of the project grade)

This will consist of a 1 page abstract, the main document with maximum length 15 pages, and references. The 15 page limit does not include the 1 page abstract and references, but it does include figures. You should use 1-inch margins and single-spaced 11 point font.

Oral presentations requirements (37.5% of the project grade)

The oral presentation will be a maximum of 15 minutes and will consist of a description of the topic you chose and a discussion of your original contributions.
