## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

What is the mind? How does it work? These are not easy questions! Cognitive psychology is the scientific discipline that attempts to answer these questions through behavioral, computational, and biological experimentation and investigation. Cognitive psychology seeks to uncover the processes and mechanisms that underlie how sensory information, and the knowledge we gain through learning and experience, are used to guide our actions and overcome problems and obstacles.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students who complete this course will be able to: 1. Define Cognition and Cognitive Psychology.
- 2\. Identify techniques and methods used to study cognition.
- 3\. Understand the basic mechanisms of cognition.
- 4\. Understand some of the higher-order processes of cognition.

## Policies

### Other Course Policies:

Laptops / computers:

Students are required to bring a tablet or laptop computer that is able to connect to LMS in order to take in-class quizzes.

Attendance:

Students are required to attend classes. It is understood that there may be circumstances where a student is unable to attend a particular class (due to travel, illness, quarantine, or other special circumstances). In such cases, the student should notify the instructor as far as possible in advance of the absence.

Make-up policy for missed quizzes:

If a student misses an in-class quiz, he or she may request the opportunity to complete the quiz at a later date. At the discretion of the instructor, points may be deducted for late quizzes, depending on the reason for missing the class, and/or the number of classes missed by the student.

Penalty for late/missed assignments:

Extensions to the due date for the take-home assignments will not be given apart from exceptional circumstances. If a student is unable to meet a deadline, he or she should contact the instructor to discuss their individual circumstances. The student’s grade on the assignment may be lowered at the discretion of the instructor.
