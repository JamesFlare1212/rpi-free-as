## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course will cover the theory, design, and implementation of digital signal processing algorithms. The first half of the course will emphasize frequency domain and Z-transform analysis. The second half will cover some advanced topics such as filter design and adaptive filtering. This course should provide a theoretical foundation which is applicable to a range of areas such as communications, control, and image processing.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

- Discrete time signals and their properties
- Discrete time systems and their properites
- LTI systems and impulse response
- Review of Fourier series and Fourier transforms
- Discrete-Time Fourier Transform (DTFT)
- Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT)
- Z-transform, its properties, and its inverse
- Fast Fourier Transforms (FFT)
- Sampling theory
- Up/down sampling
- FIR filter design
- IIR filter design
- Adaptive filters
- Wiener filters

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- An understanding of the use and application of Z-transform and Discrete-Time Fourier Transform techniques for the analysis of discrete time, linear time invariant systems.
- An understanding of discrete time sampling theory and amplitude quantization.
- An understanding of the design and implementation of FIR and IIR digital filters.
- An understanding of adaptive digital filtering, signal estimation and prediction.
