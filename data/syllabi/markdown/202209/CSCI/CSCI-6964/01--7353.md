## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course will explore the synergistic combination of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Blockchain technologies. A primary focus of the course is to identify the disruptors in the two technologies from a research lens. Specifically, we intend to discuss how leveraging blockchain technology can benefit AI and how applying AI can enhance the capabilities of blockchain technology. Furthermore, students are encouraged to think about the impact of these two technologies on society. Topics discussed in the course include blockchain fundamentals, smart contracts, web3, federated machine learning, and decentralized applications that combine AI and blockchain technologies.

By the end of this course, students would have a working knowledge of several tools and techniques at the intersection of AI & Blockchain. Students will know how to (1) perform smart contract development on several blockchains (Ethereum, Algorand, etc.), (2) deploy and run decentralized machine learning code on federated learning frameworks, and (3) critically assess the state-of-the-art research in AI and blockchain. Furthermore, students will develop a project that leverages techniques at the intersection of AI & Blockchain.

**Students may not receive credit for both the 4000 level and 6000 level versions of this course.**

## Course Goals

### Goals:

- Introduce students to relevant tools and techniques in Blockchain and AI.
- Develop students' critical thinking skills and a solid technical foundation in blockchain and model-driven decision-making.
- Develop the ability to apply decentralized methods to formulate and solve real-world problems.
- Come up with novel solutions using blockchain and AI to some real-world problems.
- [6000 level]: Critique the existing research at the intersection of AI and blockchain.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students will recognize and apply appropriate blockchain technologies in relevant scenarios.
- Students will use their solid technical foundation in AI and blockchain to demonstrate strategic thinking in analyzing/solving [some relevant] problems.
- Students will use AI and blockchain techniques and analytic methods to formulate and solve science, engineering, medical, and business problems.
- Students must effectively communicate the state-of-the-art Blockchain and AI research discussed in the class.
- Students must develop and demonstrate a novel use case that demonstrates the utility of AI and blockchain.
- [6000 level]: Demonstrate in-depth knowledge of synergistic blockchain and AI applications, confidently evaluate the failings and successes of those, and think about new research directions in this space.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

- Students will be assessed on three written assignments, three programming assignments, and the final group project.
- The written assignments will provide the basis for synergies between AI and Blockchain.
- The programming assignments will equip the students with the foundation to build robust applications that leverage AI and Blockchain technologies.
- The final group project will leverage techniques at the intersection of AI & Blockchain to solve a real-world problem and advance the state-of-the-art.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Every student will be doing different work in this class. Teams can and should work together. Students will be asked to present their unique contributions in their project notebooks and team breakouts. Students should fairly represent their own work, and misrepresenting others’ work as your own could be a violation of academic integrity. Team members found to not be contributing their fair share of the workload will be counseled, and if the problem persists, given a low or failing grade for the work segment or segments involved.

Submission of any assignment that is in violation of this policy will result in (1) an academic (grade) penalty and (2) reporting to the Associate Dean of Academic Affairs and either the Dean of Students (for Undergraduates) or the Dean of Graduate Education (for Graduate students).

**In this course, the academic penalty for a first offense is zero grade for the relevant portion of the grade. A second offense will result in failure of the course.**

If you have any questions concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification.
