## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- • Analyze your audience: Identify specific knowledge, needs, values, and experiences that are characteristic of your audience. • Engage your audience: Show your audience how your topic matters to them, especially by showing how your topic relates to what the audience knows, cares about, and wonders about. • Explain your ideas in ways that reflect a thorough understanding of the subject and your audience’s knowledge, needs, etc., especially by anticipating and answering the audience’s likely questions and elaborating appropriately. • Organize texts effectively, especially by getting directly to the point in answering audience questions, creating and fulfilling expectations, and moving from given to new. • Create an appropriate voice not only in the words you choose but also in the kinds of questions you answer or avoid, and the kinds of information you include/exclude. • Incorporate visual information effectively.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Writing Assignment:**

- You will complete a number of short writing assignments throughout the semester, which include negative message, letter of request, and visual analysis.
- You will write an annotated bibliography (at least 5 sources) and a final research paper.

You will select some innovation /technology/advancement in your field of interest (mathematics, computer science, engineering, business, communication, etc.), and try to argue a position and persuade your target audience (college students) to take your recommendation. You will conduct a secondary research, synthesize the literature, argue a position and make your recommendation. For this project, you need to prepare an annotated bibliography and write a final paper.

- You will create a final writing portfolio by revising and compiling all the writing assignments (negative message, letter of request, visual analysis, annotated bibliography, and final research paper) at the end of the semester. All the writing assignments should be carefully revised based on the instructor’s feedback.

- Formatting requirements are as follows:

\- Typed, double-spaced

\- 12-point Times New Roman font; 1-inch margins on all sides (including top and bottom)

\- References and in-text citations must follow APA guidelines

- **Speaking Assignment:** You will deliver an oral presentation to persuade audience to take your recommendation. The presentation is based on your final research paper.
- **Module Exercises:** Module exercises will be used to help you understand principles and strategies in writing. These will include, but may not be limited to, in-class writing assignments, paired assignments, group assignments, and short oral presentations. Please be prepared to participate in these activities as well as class discussions. The assignments missed due to absence can't be made up, unless in case of emergency that can be proved by official documents. Please do not ask for an excused absence unless there are exceptional circumstances.
- **Attendance/Participation:** Attendance/participation grade is not “free” points automatically given just for showing up. It must be earned. This grade is calculated based on the quality and quantity of your responses to discussion questions, your attendance to the online classes requested by the instructor, and submission of the final presentation peer evaluations.

## Policies

### Academic Policies:

- ### Academic Integrity

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust. For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. Acts that violate this trust undermine the educational process. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. In this class, all assignments that are turned in for a grade must represent the student’s own work. In cases where help was received, or teamwork was allowed, a notation on the assignment should indicate your collaboration.

  Violations of academic integrity may also be reported to the appropriate Dean (Dean of Students for undergraduate students or the Dean of Graduate Education for graduate students, respectively).

  If you have any question concerning this policy before submitting an assignment, please ask for clarification. In addition, you can visit the following site for more information on our Academic Integrity Policy: [Students Rights, Responsibilities, and Judicial Affairs](https://info.rpi.edu/dean-students/student-rights-responsibilities-and-judicial-affairs).
