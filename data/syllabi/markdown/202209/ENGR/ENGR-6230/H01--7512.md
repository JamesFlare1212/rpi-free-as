### Instructor Note

Welcome to the start of your Lean Six Sigma journey! This course will introduce you to Lean Six Sigma philosophy. The goal is to use the “Show by Doing” mentality making you AMBASSADORS for change. It’s having you grasp the paradigm shift from “Increasing Quality at work means higher costs” to “Increasing Quality means reducing costs”.  
This course will be hands-on where you will be selecting the problem you want to solve at your workplace. The objective is to apply the Lean Six Sigma methodologies at your workplace to learn how you can improve your environment.  
This course is designed with a logic flow where every week you will be completing a piece of the final deliverable which is the DMAIC project. Thus, it is important you complete the assigned milestones in each of the Phases sequentially.  
Before you get started, review the information in this document to better familiarize yourself with the course, its structure, expectations, outcomes, and flow of work. If you have any questions, please post your question in the "***Ask the Mentor***" discussion board in Canvas so it becomes a learning for your fellow classmates as well.

The bottom section of the “Syllabus” page in Canvas provides a weekly list of important course deliverables, including project deadlines and synchronous sessions. It also provides suggested weekly dates for starting projects, to help you stay on track throughout the semester. The dates appear in the Canvas calendar (grayed out), as well as the “To Do” list that appears on the course home page.

## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Builds the main perspectives of Lean Six Sigma including quality improvements, waste reduction, and best practices that ultimately lead to fewer defects and increased efficiency. Topics covered include the principles, qualitative methods, process improvements, and problem-solving techniques for improving existing business processes. The emphasis is on the Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, and Control (DMAIC) phases of Lean Six Sigma.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Develop and maintain a DMAIC project charter
- Execute on DMAIC projects
- Perform FMEA Risk management methodology
- Get the “eye” for identifying Waste through Gemba
- Utilize MINITAB for statistical analysis
- Learn how to utilize simple root-cause methods
- Perform Kaizens for quick hit solutions (low hanging fruit)
- Improve their problem-solving techniques
- Perform PDCA approach
- Tie statistical thinking to real world benefits
