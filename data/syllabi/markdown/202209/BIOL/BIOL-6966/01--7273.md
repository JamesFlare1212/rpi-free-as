### Other LMS Information:

The Perspectives course is lecture-based and designed to expose trainees and other graduate students to overviews of the fundamental science and engineering principles inherent in research conducted by the NIGMS/NIA T32 training faculty. Each training faculty member as well as invited individuals from industry partners describe their work as a case-study within the context of the broader field of biotechnology; however, the focus on the key fundamental underpinnings of their specific research and its impact on biotechnology. The lecturers describe the critical need for interdisciplinary research both in their own area and in related areas, ideally through personal experience.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

To expose trainees and other graduate students to overviews of the fundamental science and engineering principles inherent in research conducted by the NIGMS/NIA T32 training faculty and highlight the critical need for interdisciplinary research.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- To expose students to the contemporary research areas in bimolecular science and engineering
