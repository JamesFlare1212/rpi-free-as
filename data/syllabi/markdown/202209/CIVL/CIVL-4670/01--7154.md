## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course teaches students the fundamentals of highway design and engineering. It focuses on the highway design process including the design and selection of horizontal and vertical alignments; design of interchanges, pedestrian facilities, bicycle facilities, intersections, environmental factors, highway drainage, signs, signals, roadside design, embankments and pavements. The emphasis on design is followed by an introduction to asset management systems, construction, maintenance and operations.

The students will complete a proposal for a Design-Build Highway and Bridge Project. This will be a comprehensive project that includes a written report describing the Project. The students will present their proposals to their peers and instructors who will assess and critique their work.

Students will prepare a written report describing their observations from two field trips. Students must have access to a PC and know how to use word processing, Computer Aided Design and Drafting (CADD) and spreadsheet software. All reports must be typed.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

The students will apply the fundamentals of highway design and engineering to develop a proposal for a Design-Build Project. Students shall conduct in-depth analysis and assessment of alternative designs and strategies then apply these methods to ensure that the outcome is a safe, efficient, balanced and environmentally sound highway system.

The students will demonstrate their abilities to prepare reports, develop high-level engineering plans, and conduct professional presentations.
