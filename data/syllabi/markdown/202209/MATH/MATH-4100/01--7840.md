## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The theory underlying vector spaces, algebra of subspaces, bases; linear transformations, dual spaces; eigenvectors, eigenvalues, minimal polynomials, canonical forms of linear transformations; inner products, adjoints, orthogonal projections, and complements.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Introduction to Linear Algebra, Fifth ed., by Gilbert Strang, Wellesley-Cambridge Press, 2016, ISBN: 978-09802327-7-6.

Other sources may be used for some of the topics. Notes or references will be provided.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the basic ideas of Linear Algebra, including concepts of linear systems, independence, theory of matrices, linear transformations, determinants, vector spaces and subspaces, bases and dimension, eigenvalues, eigenvectors and diagonalization, inner products, etc.
- write clear and accurate proofs using the concepts of this course,
- apply Linear Algebra concepts of this course to solve problems.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Students are responsible for all material covered in lectures whether they are present or not. Attendance is expected, but will not be checked. All students are expected to participate and contribute to discussions during lectures. All students are expected to read the text to fill in the material not covered, or partially covered, in lecture.

### Other Course Policies:

**Exams:** Exams will be given in person, during lecture, as long as the Institute allows it. Details about each exam will be announced on LMS when the date of the exam is near. The dates for Exams 1–3 are listed above in the section Grading. There will be **no Final Exam** for this course.

**Make-up exams will only be given to students with an** [excused absence documented by the Student Success office](https://success.studentlife.rpi.edu/current-students/academic-and-personal-support/requesting-excused-absence) **or, if the absence is due to an athletic competition, documentation from a coach.** Thus, if a student misses an exam because they overslept, didn’t know where the exam was, or didn’t know there was an exam, to give a few examples, the student may not take the make-up exam and will receive a score of 0 on the exam.

**Missing an exam should not be taken lightly.** Thus, students who miss an exam due to an excused absence must make every effort to notify the instructor **before the exam begins** to avoid grade penalties.

- If a student misses an exam due to an excused absence and notifies the instructor before the exam begins, then the student may take a make-up exam without penalty.
- If a student misses an exam due to an excused absence and notifies the instructor after the exam begins but before 11:59 PM the day after their scheduled exam, then the student may take a make-up exam and a 30% penalty will be applied to the make-up exam score.
- If a student misses an exam due to an excused absence and fails to notify the instructor before 11:59 PM on the day after their scheduled exam, then the student may not take the make-up exam and receives a score of 0 on the exam.
- If a student misses an exam and does not have an excused absence, then the student may not take the make-up exam and receives a score of 0 on the exam.

It is the sole responsibility of the student to notify the instructor to make arrangements for a make-up exam when an exam is missed due to an excused absence.

**Homework:** All homework assignments will be of equal weight and are typically assigned weekly. Homework will be submitted via Gradescope and will be due on Wednesdays at 11:59 PM. Late submissions will be subject to penalties as follows: up to one day late 10% up to two days late 30%. Late submissions will not be accepted after two days late (Fridays at 11:59 PM). The lowest homework assignment score will be dropped at the end of the semester. Successful completion of all homework problems is essential in learning Linear Algebra. Clarity in writing and presentation will be taken into account in grading.

**Lectures:** Students should review any posted lecture material on LMS before the respective class. The lectures and/or practice problems will complement and reinforce the material posted on LMS.

**Classroom decorum:** Students must be on time for class and should refrain from leaving and reentering the classroom during class. If a student has a legitimate reason for arriving late to class or being excused early from class, then he or she should discuss this with the instructor before class. Phones may not be used during class and should be silent. Computers and tablets may not be used for anything other than taking notes. Students who are unable to courteously comply with these policies may be asked to leave the room.

**Disability Services for Students (DSS):** Students with a memorandum from the [Disability Services for Students (DSS)](http://studenthealth.rpi.edu/disabilityservices) office identifying accommodations for exams must email the memorandum to the instructor by **Friday, September 16**. Students will not be granted accommodations unless a memorandum from DSS has been received. Providing the accommodations requires advance planning. Students may not be granted accommodations if a memorandum is received on the day of an exam.

**Calculator Policy:** Calculators will not be allowed on exams.  
**Health and Safety Protocols:** Students are required to follow the health and safety protocols of RPI, which are subject to change throughout the semester. Students who are not cleared for campus access are prohibited from attending class. When masks are required in classrooms, eating and drinking in class is prohibited. Failure to comply with health and safety protocols will be met with a request to comply, a request to leave the class, or a dismissal of the entire class and referral of the non-compliant student to the Dean of Students.

**Visitors:** Visitors are prohibited during all class meetings. Only students who are registered for the class should be present in the classroom. Students may only attend class meetings at the time and place listed on their schedule on SIS.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Academic Integrity:** Students should familiarize themselves with the section on academic integrity in the [Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities](https://info.rpi.edu/sites/default/files/Rensselaer%20Handbook%20of%20Student%20Rights%20&%20Responsibilities%20%C2%BB.pdf) (pp. 16–18). All work submitted on exams and quizzes must be the student’s own. A student may not change an answer on an exam or quiz before submitting it for an appeal. In addition, no calculators, computers, tablets, open books, open notes, phones, headphones, or smartwatches may be used during exams or quizzes. Talking or communicating with other students in any way during exams or quizzes is also prohibited. Students may discuss homework with other students, the TA, and the instructor, but the work submitted must be the student’s own. Submissions with glaring similarities will violate academic integrity polices.

The minimum academic sanction applied to a student found in violation of the above policies will be a grade of 0 on the assignment. A stronger sanction may be applied if warranted. A report regarding the violation will also be submitted to the Dean of Students Office and will remain on file until graduation. Repeat offenders will fail the course and will be subject to judicial action.
