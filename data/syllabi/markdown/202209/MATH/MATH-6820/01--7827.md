## Course Content

### Content Details:

Topics:

- Formulation, analysis and implementation of Runge-Kutta methods, linear multi-step methods, and some related methods, for non-stiff and stiff initial-value problems; symplectic methods for Hamiltonian systems
- Other possible topics: numerical methods for two-point boundary value problems and differential-algebraic equations

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- The students will be able to perform numerical analysis for consistency, stability, convergence and other properties of the methods; \<br>
- to be able to implement numerical methods efficiently and robustly, and more importantly, to be able to interpret /analyze the observations and results, and to extract useful information and draw meaningful conclusions;
- to be able to evaluate/compare various methods, such as regarding their accuracy, efficiency, reliability, either numerically or mathematically;
- to be able to choose or to design numerical methods to solve a given problem.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

In-class exam and homework assignments.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Collaboration: You are encouraged to discuss the problems with others or to refer to other references. However, the work you turn in must be written by yourself and represents your own understanding on the subjects. It is absolutely not acceptable to share or exchange computer files, or to copy any part of the homework solutions from someone else. Any violation will result in a grade of zero for the assignment (of all involved individuals if there is any). If your work involves a teammate, or significant help from other people, reference books or papers, explicitly state this in your report to avoid penalty in grade.
