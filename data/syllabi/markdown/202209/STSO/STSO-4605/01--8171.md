## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

"On Monuments and Memorials" explores monuments and memorials in Troy and beyond. The course will bridge the relationship between student’s individual studio practices with the public at large. In addition to taking field trips to visit monuments and memorials, students will learn how to create proposals for a monument or memorial. These proposals will consist of project descriptions, research, models (both digital and analog) and digital presentations. Through presentations, readings, group discussions and site visits students will explore the many ways in which the arts engage the public. Topics covered in group discussions and through readings will be: monuments and memorials, public art controversies, site specificity and temporary vs. permanent projects.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- To express an idea through art in the public places where the audience is not limited to gallery goers and art enthusiasts.
- To gain the experience of making proposals for public art calls for entries including conceptual development, artist statements, project descriptions, model making, and photography.
- To analyze and understand public art.
- To gain experience in conceptualizing art works that fit a specific site - visually, physically and contextually.
