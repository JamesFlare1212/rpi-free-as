## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

We will study the nature and extent of disparities in economic outcomes relating to discriminatory behavior. We will focus on differences that occur in the labor market, in educational settings, in the housing market, and in health and health care.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

## Course Goals

### Goals:

We will study the nature and extent of disparities in economic outcomes relating to discriminatory behavior. We will focus on differences that occur in the labor market and supplement those with studies of discrimination in educational settings, the housing market, and in health and health care.

**Method of Instruction:** Class time will be used for both lectures and discussion. Any “core” theoretical or empirical material will be presented in class. I will also post class notes/slides to LMS. For each topic we cover, I will assign corresponding readings and post them to the LMS. Please note that reviewing posted notes/slides will not be an effective substitute for attending class.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

1\. The economic theory of discrimination  
2\. The economic theory of the labor market, with emphasis on job match, hiring decisions, and wage determination  
3\. Experimental and econometric tools used to identify discrimination in real-world data  
4\. A survey of estimates of discrimination regarding age, gender, health status, race, national origin, and sexual orientation

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Engage in productive and respectful discussion relating to divisive issues in economics.
- Define and explain discrimination to others, including how discrimination differs from prejudice and the differences between statistical and taste-based discrimination.
- Use economic theory to describe how prejudicial attitudes are incorporated into economic decision making and to predict how discrimination manifests and then persists.
- Describe the extent of disparities in outcomes in the United States to others, including disparities by gender, race, sexual orientation, national origin, age, health behaviors, and disability status.
- Explain the key methods used to identify evidence of discrimination in market/economic settings, including being able to explain their benefits and drawbacks.
- Use statistical and experimental methods, and appropriate data, to quantify discrimination.
- Explain to others the effects or expected effects of policies designed to alleviate discrimination.

Note that my late policy is not applicable to cases of academic integrity violations.

2. What your response was (include as an attachment, photo, etc.)

**How to be successful in this class:**

- Come to class. The material builds continuously so missing a class can and probably will lead to confusion later. It will take several reviews of the material before it begins to sink in.
- Ask questions. **Your questions are not an imposition**; they are welcome and make class more interesting. If you are confused, chances are other people are too. If you feel uncomfortable asking questions in class, please ask during office hours or by email.
- **The time to be concerned about your grade is in the first 15 weeks of the semester**, not just in the final week. Contact me if you feel you are falling behind.
- The key to mastering economics is, like anything, **practice**. The internet is full of economics practice problems and video explanations (YouTube is your friend!). If you are struggling with the material, you just need more practice.
