## Course Goals

### Goals:

Course Objective:  
To provide the student the relevant disciplinary knowledge and competencies related to sport management industry.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Students will understand terminology and theory in the discipline of sport management.
- 2\. Student will create compelling, well-supported verbal and written arguments.
- 3\. Student will apply understanding of culture and subculture when making business decisions.
- 4\. Student will effectively use information technology and research resources

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Assignments (point value)—These assignments may be adjusted. If so, students will be given appropriate notice**

1. Participation (10 points); regular class attendance and participation will contribute to your grade.

1. Issue Report and Presentation (10 points);

The assignment is designed to explore current issues in the sports industry. The students are expected to present the issue and lead the classroom discussion on an assigned date. The students’ issue report should be uploaded to Blackboard as assigned.

Select a sport business/management related topic from a journal like *Sports Illustrated, SportsBusiness Journal, Fortune Magazine, or Forbes.* The article should have been published *after* January 2020. The issue report should summarize the article, present fact based questions generated by the student along with answers for class room, present key takeaways from the case (practical implications); and (d) address discussion topics for the class. Your issue report should be submitted to the Discussion Board ***by Sunday before your presentation week***.

1. Exams (2 at 15 points each = 30 points); Exams will be designed to assess the students comprehension of the topics covered during class by lecture, assigned readings, class discussion and / or guest speakers. Exams may consist of various questions like true/false, multi choice, short answer or essay

1. Group Project (30 points). Students will be divided into two groups with each group being assigned a different unit within a college athletic department to research and then design. Students will research defined colleges to determine things like: where in the organization does the unit report? What is the purpose of the unit (e.g. mission statement)? How many people work within the unit? Each group will present a 20 minute review of their findings using PowerPoint or similar presentation deck along with a 10 page, double spaced written report.

1. In class assignments (2 at 10 points each for a total of 20 points); these in class assignments will be designed to test the student’s knowledge of information covered that day. Will be administered randomly during the semester

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

**ATTENDANCE POLICY**

Students are expected to attend and participate in all class sessions. Students should review weekly course announcements and materials on the Blackboard modules and complete the required class assignments and/or activities by the announced deadline. Grades for class participation will be based on your participation (Both quantity and quality will be counted.) on the assignments, activities, and discussions. For the class attendance, 25% or more absences/non-participation from the scheduled sessions will result in a significant grade reduction or grade of “FA” (Failure due to attendance) based on the instructor’s discretion.

Project/assignments that are handed in late will not receive full credits. Students can receive partial credits for late project/assignments, so do not forget to submit your works for evaluation.

  Student-teacher relationships are built on trust.
