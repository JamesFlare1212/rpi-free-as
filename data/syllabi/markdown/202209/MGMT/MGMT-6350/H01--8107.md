## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course is an introductory graduate course in advanced heat transfer. The course content provides a comprehensive treatment of conduction and convection, including boiling and condensation, and radiation heat transfer. Also covered is thermal system design and performance, including heat exchangers. Emphasis is placed on physical and mathematical modeling of engineering systems for application of modern analytical and computational solution methods.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Apply the analytic approach to develop descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive analytic models to supply chain and logistics management scenarios.
- Use software tools to discover relationships and deliver insights to evaluate and respond, in context, to a supply chain problem, decision, or question.
- Use supply chain data to produce meaningful and representative results summarized and presented in an understandable format.
