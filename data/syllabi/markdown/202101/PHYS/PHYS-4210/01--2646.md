## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**Course contents:**

PHYS 4210 Electromagnetic theory is a course on electricity and magnetism building on students’ learning outcomes defined for PHYS 1200 Physics II and PHYS 1250 Honors Physics II. The course covers classical field theory of electricity and magnetism with emphasis on solving boundary value problems, including dielectric and magnetic materials. Maxwell’s equations and its application to the description of electromagnetic wave propagation with applications to optics is discussed. The electric and magnetic fields of electrical charges moving close to the speed of light are analyzed using Lorentz transformation for electric and magnetic fields. This course is required for students studying towards a Bachelor of Science in Physics.
