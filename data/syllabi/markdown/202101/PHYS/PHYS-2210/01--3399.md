## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Introduction to quantum physics.

Wave nature of matter, de Broglie wave, Schrodinger equation, wave packets, expectation value, particle in a box, eigenvalue problems, finite well solutions, simple harmonic oscillator, scattering from potentials, barrier tunneling, Hermitian operators, commutators, angular momentum, spherical harmonics, hydrogen problem, Zeeman effect, intrinsic spin.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

‘Quantum Physics: A Fundamental Approach to Modern Physics’ by John S. Townsend, First Edition. University Science books.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

To understand the quantum nature of the world around us.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Student will understand about wave-particle duality and be able to apply those concepts to explain wave phenomena of light and matter.
- Students will understand how to use Schrodinger's equation to describe quantum systems. These include particles in a box, free particles, quantum tunneling and scattering.
- Students will be able to understand a quantum mechanical description of the hydrogen atom.
- Student will be able to apply their knowledge of quantum physics to describe spin phenomena.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Ten homework assignments will be given. Twenty six in-class assignments will be given.
