### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Develop a functional understanding of:**

1. The concepts fundamental to the synthesis (design) and analysis of mechanisms, viz., Degree of freedom, links, joints, kinematic chains, mobility, grashof condition.
2. Position analysis techniques for four bar linkage system: Graphical and analytical Methods
3. Velocity analysis techniques for four bar linkage system: Graphical and analytical Methods
4. Acceleration analysis techniques for four bar linkage system: Analytical Methods
5. Kinematic synthesis (design) techniques for four bar linkage system: Graphical and analytical methods
6. Cam design principles

**Enable the student to:**

7. Apply design synthesis and analysis techniques to open-ended real-world problems.
8. Write technical reports that clearly communicate the iterative design process, design choices and final results, as required in professional settings.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Homework 15%

Exam 1 15% - Take home, Open book – Limited Time (24 hrs)

Exam 2 15% - Take home, Open book – Limited Time (24 hrs)

Exam 3 15% - Take home, Open book – Extended time (2.5 days)

Exam 4 15% - Take home, Open book – Extended time (2.5 days)

Project 25% - Detailed hand out to follow

**Homework:** Homework will be assigned, graded and account for 15% of the total course grade. There will be approximately 7 homework assignments. The problems are best done individually in a professional manner (**neatness counts**!). Each problem will be graded on a 10 point scale and will be returned in class approximately one week after they were due. **If your homework solutions are not legible, a penalty ranging from 10-15% percent will be applied to your final homework score**. Collaboration in the solution of the homework problems is permitted and is strongly encouraged if it enhances the learning process, but mere copying of the solution is not acceptable. In case of collaborative work, **the front page of the turned-in homework should clearly list all the names of the students who have collaborated**. In general, **no** late assignments will be accepted. However, extensions may be granted if a situation arises for which it is warranted.

**Exams:** There will be four exams during the course. Each exam will count for 15% of the course grade. Exams will only be allowed to be made up if a written excuse is granted by a doctor or the Dean of Students.

**Term Project:** There will be a required term project associated with this course. The subject of the project will be selected by the student teams and will be approved by the instructor. **The projects should ideally be done by a team of 2 students and may be very broad in scope** (These projects may involve analysis of existing mechanism, analysis of proposed mechanism, synthesis of a proposed mechanism, construction of a mechanism (working model), use of available computer software for analysis and visualization (i.e. whatever the student is into and feels he/she would gain the most from!). **This project will account for 25% of the total course grade**.
