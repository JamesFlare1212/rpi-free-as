## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course focuses on heat transfer and fluid flow applications in nuclear power reactor and other energy systems, including heat transfer in LWR fuel rods, coolant, and reactor internals.

Course goals are:

- To provide a systematic treatment of reactor thermal hydraulics for LWRs, with a focus on one-dimensional analysis
- To understand the fundamentals of single-phase fluid flow and heat transfer
- To understand the fundamentals of two-phase fluid flow and heat transfer, including two-phase pressure drop, boiling heat transfer, and condensation
- To be aware of various two-phase flow models and understand their application domains and limitations
