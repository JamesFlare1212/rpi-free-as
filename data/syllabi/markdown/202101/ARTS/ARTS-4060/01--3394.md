## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

3D Visual Effects prepares students to competently plan and execute a variety of technical effects using digital content creation software, primarily Side Effects Houdini. These will include including particle, fluid, liquid, gas, pyrotechnic and rigid-body destruction effects with emphasis upon a general understanding of the details of the algorithmic principles behind the visuals. To support this, the course provides an introduction and an ongoing elucidation of the workflow and contexts (including modeling, shading, rendering, animation and simulation) that are specific to the Houdini software, as well as general principles that are applicable to other DCC applications.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Upon satisfactory completion of the course, students will be able to:  
Understand and be able to use Houdini and its procedural workflow  
Design and execute visual effects with Houdini  
Understand the concepts underlying the simulations used for VFX such as particle systems, rigid body systems, constraints, position-based dynamics, gaussian and laplacian simulations, voxel fluids, and FLIP fluids  
Gain an intuitive understanding of the strategies and trade-offs involved with running simulations “good enough” for visual effects
