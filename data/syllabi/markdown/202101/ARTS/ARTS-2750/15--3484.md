### Prerequisites or Other Requirements:

REQUIRED MATERIALS:

\- Student-owned harp; or if not possible, student will need to rent a harp (either lever of pedal, depending on preference and budget)

\- Suitable cover(s) for the harp (harp should be kept covered when not in use)

\- Harp cart/dolly (required for pedal harp unless one is provided by the college or instructor; optional for lever harp)

\- Electronic digital chromatic tuner (or suitable phone/latptop/tablet app)

\- Tuning pickup wire (optional but can be helpful)

\- Harp tuning key (will be provided if it’s a rental harp)

\- Metronome (or suitable phone/laptop/tablet app)

\- Spiral notebook for keeping personal lesson notes (if lessons are online, the instructor’s notes will also be emailed after each lesson). Instructor usually provides new students with a spiral notebook at the first lesson when in-person.

\- Good pencil and eraser for marking music and taking notes

## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Private applied music lessons are offered to provide a fine arts experience for credit.

Open to undergraduates and graduate students. No experience necessary, although some musical background and the ability to read music (grand staff preferred) are very helpful.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students in the private lesson program are expected to improve their ability to read, discuss, interpret, and perform a plethora of music composed for their individual instrument.
- Students are expected to be able to expressively and musically interpret a variety of musical works/styles/genres.
- Students are expected to further mastery of their chosen instrument.
- Students are expected to improve listening skills, as well as mature in their ability to work at once independently.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Because these are private music lessons, what is most important it showing up prepared and with a willing-to-learn attitude, practicing as consistently as possible, and making some kind of tangible progress throughout the semester. Grading is roughly determined by the following critiera: 25% attendance, 25% having the correct materials ready at the beginning of the lesson; 25% good attitude, attentiveness and earnestness in learning, and 25% evident progress over the course of the semester.

## Policies

### Attendance Policy:

Because we have to meet a goal of a certain amount of minutes and therefore a certain number of lessons per semester, attendance is critical or we can fall too far behind to meet our goal. Lateness can become an issue due to the instructor’s schedule. Please always communicate ahead of time if you need to reschedule a lesson. It’s always best if we work together on this and not let things fall behind.
