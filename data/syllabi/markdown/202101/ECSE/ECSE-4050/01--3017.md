## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Linear and non-linear applications of operational amplifiers, with an emphasis on circuit design. Non-ideal operational amplifier behavior, including both static and dynamic characteristics. Amplifier stability and frequency compensation techniques. Operational amplifier-based oscillators. Circuit noise.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Design with Operational Amplifiers and Analog Integrated Circuits, 4th Edition by Sergio Franco

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Course Objective: The objective of this course is to study the principles of analog circuit analysis and design with an emphasis on practical applications. The focus is primarily on the use of operational amplifiers and a strong emphasis is placed on the non-ideal aspects of this device and its impact on circuit performance.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Course Topics: Linear and non-linear op amp applications (chapters 1, 2, 9)

Non-ideal op amp behavior: static and dynamic (chapters 5, 6)

Noise (chapter 7)

Stability and frequency compensation (chapter 8)

Signal Generators (chapter 10)

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

3 Quizzes @ 25% each 75%

Homework (lowest grade dropped) 10%

ADI Design Contest 15%

- The quizzes and homeworks are the same for ECSE-4050 and ECSE-6050. Quizzes are open book/open notes. Students are able to use their textbooks, their own notes, and any of the material posted on the LMS site for the course. Calculators are needed for the quizzes.
- Students are encouraged to work together on the homework assignments but each student must submit their own work, i.e. do not submit copies of the same solution.
- The ADI Design Contest will include extra requirements for ECSE-6050.
- Each course has its own grading curve and the performance required for a given grade will be higher for ECSE-6050.
