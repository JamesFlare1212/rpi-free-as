## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is an Undergraduate course.

**Course description:** This course introduces major components of today’s electric power system starting with review of single and three phase electric circuits, fundamental laws (Ampere’s law, Faraday’s law, Maxwell’s equations, Lenz’s law, Lorentz Force Principle, etc.), magnetic circuits, transformers, synchronous machines and transmission lines. Renewable energy sources such as wind and solar are briefly introduced.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Textbooks: Chapters 1 to 5 & Chapter 9 from “Electric Machinery and Power System Fundamentals” by Stephen J. Chapman, 2002 Edition, McGraw-Hill Publishers,  
Chapters 6, 8 & 9 from “Renewable and Efficient Electric Power Systems” by Gilbert M. Masters, 2004 Edition, John Wiley & Sons, Publishers.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The goals of this course is to enable students understand the fundamentals of power/energy conversion from various forms (mechanical, chemical, solar, etc.) to the electrical form employing magnetic field or photo-voltaic effect as the media and familiarize them with key components is electric power/energy systems.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

**Course details:** Electromagnetic fundamentals, Review of Single & Three phase circuits, phasor notation use, concept of real & reactive power, transformers, AC machinery fundamentals, synchronous machines (generators & motors) and their operation with varying loads & excitation levels, transmission line representation by short, medium & long line models using the two-port concept employing the ABCD constants. Fundamentals of wind power, Betz’s efficiency, wind speed probability density functions, wind turbine capabilities including cut-in, rated and cut-out speeds with wind power shedding, etc. Solar power basics, physics of solar cell/module shading and it impact on the level of power, by-pass & blocking diode usage.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Learn and master the understanding of the fundamental/natural laws of electromagnetism.
- Learn about various electric power/energy system components employed in converting various forms of energy to the electrical form.
- Learn about electric transmission lines system level representation. Get familiar with the wind and solar power systems basics.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Mandatory statement on academic dishonesty:**

- Copying from a neighbor in an examination or turning in someone else’s homework / reports /term papers as one’s own constitutes academic dishonesty.
- The compilation, ad verbatim reproduction, or paraphrased reproduction of someone else’s work in a written or oral report without citation of original source constitutes academic dishonesty.
- What is plagiarism? This is the use of someone else’s work without crediting the source.
- What is an “ad verbatim” reproduction? This is an exact word-by-word reproduction.
- What is paraphrased reproduction? This is a reproduction using very similar words and phrases.
- What is a phrase? This is a sequence of two or more words.
- What is the penalty for academic dishonesty? The penalty may result in failing the course.
- Note: Teamwork during class exercises and homework and use of other resources (calculators, books, etc.) are allowed.

**Mandatory Statement on Mobile Policy**

All mobile devices (cell/smart phones, computers, pagers, etc.) must be stored securely away during lecture and are not be used unless specifically directed otherwise by the instructor. Use of (or ANY interaction with) a mobile device during an exam without explicit permission of the instructor will be interpreted as the illicit transfer of exam data, will be considered an act of cheating and will be treated as such.
