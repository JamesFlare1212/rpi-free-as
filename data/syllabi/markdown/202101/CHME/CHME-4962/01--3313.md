## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course discusses the basic theory and experimental methods to understand and characterize the colloid and interface phenomena. In particular, this course aims to encourage students to develop a cohesive understanding of colloid and interface in the context of structure and dynamics.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

At the conclusion of this course, students should be able to:

- Demonstrate an understanding of the basic knowledge of colloids and interface phenomena and related topics.
- Demonstrate an understanding of the physical models of colloid and interface phenomena.
- Demonstrate an understanding of the light scatting measurements.
- Demonstrate an understanding of the physical phenomena of colloids and interface phenomena related to ions.
- Demonstrate an understanding of the importance of the interface phenomena related to the phase transitions.

Students who successfully acquired these learning outcomes from this course will be capable of understanding the fundamental physical chemistry related to colloid and interface phenomena and solving related problems by applying the principles and knowledge gained from this course.

**HOMEWORK ASSIGNMENTS**

Homework will be uploaded on LMS. Homework is due at the beginning of class. Late homework, including homework turned in during or after class, will be accepted, with a penalty of 20% for each day late (even 1 minute late). Graduate students may have additional questions in homework assignments. *Although an assignment may have several problems, often a small number of questions will be selected at random for grading.*

**Note about Showing Work:** In this class, the process of getting to an answer is as important (if not more) than the actual answer. Therefore, you *must show and justify* all of your work to receive credit. If you do not show your work, you will receive zero credit (even if the answer is correct). *If your homework shows any sign of non-your-work, it will be considered as an offense. The first offense will result in zero score for the homework, and the second offense will fail the course.*
