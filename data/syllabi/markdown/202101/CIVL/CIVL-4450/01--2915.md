## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Understand the behavior of different structural systems
- Determine applicable loading conditions
- Perform a variety of approximate analyses
- Demonstrate quantitatively horizontal and vertical building irregularities
- Develop proposed structural systems for potential building types
- Understand the integration of a structural system for specific building types

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

There will be homework problems and in class problems which will review the material presented in class. Class problems will not be graded.  
There will be two exams plus the final exam.  
THere is one project which is usually a group project but may have to be modified for remote learning depending on student locations and time zones.

- ### Academic Integrity

For example, students must trust that teachers have made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of the courses they teach, and teachers must trust that the assignments that students turn in are their own. The Rensselaer Handbook of Student Rights and Responsibilities and The Graduate Student Supplement define various forms of Academic Dishonesty and you should make yourself familiar with these. ### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Attendance at lectures is highly recommended as understanding is crucial. Lectures will be recorded for remote students with attendance issues. If a student misses a lecture, they are responsible for all the material. All assignments must be completed on time to receive full credit. Much of the project work is collaborative in this course. However, exams which are to be completed individually and found to be shared in any way, will result in a drop of a full letter grade in the student's final grade for the course.
