## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

An introduction to various aspects of the study of the physics of the Earth. Stress and strain, deformation, isostasy, seismic waves, earthquakes, Earth structure, resource exploration, Earth dynamics, plate tectonics, mountain building, gravity and geodesy, magnetic field, and heat flow.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

No text is required. However, the following is highly recommended:

Fowler, C.M.R., The Solid Earth: An Introduction to Global Geophysics, Cambridge University Press.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The purpose of this course is to introduce the basic concepts involved in applying physics to understand the Earth. We will also discuss fundamental techniques of geophysical data collection and analysis.  
Specifically, we will cover the fundamentals of Gravity, Seismology, Geochronology, Heat Flow, Electromagnetism, and Geodynamics as they apply to the Earth. If time allows, we will discuss GPS and InSAR geodetic techniques near the end of term.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate competency in the design of geophysical data collection exercises in the major fields (gravity, seismology, MT, resistivity, and magnetics)
- Demonstrate competency in carrying out of geophysical data analysis in the major fields (gravity, seismology, MT, resistivity, and magnetics)
- Demonstrate competency in interpreting the results of geophysical data analysis in the major fields (gravity, seismology, MT, resistivity, and magnetics)

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Assessments will be based primarily on performance on exams and homeworks, which will include problems related to the CLOs described above. There will also be some consideration of feedback in class or during office hours where an understanding of the material may be demonstrated.
