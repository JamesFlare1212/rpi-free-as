## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

There is no text required, but a strongly recommended text is Modern Global Seismology by Lay and Wallace

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The goal of the course in Spring 2021 is to provide graduate students in Geophysics the background necessary to perform research in seismology

## Course Content

### Content Details:

The main topics to be covered are: stress and strain, body waves, surface waves, normal modes, earthquake source mechanisms, and subsurface imaging

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate competency in applications of fundamental seismology to wave propagation
- Demonstrate competency in a basic subsurface imaging application (e.g., body wave tomography or attenuation tomography)

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

As this is an interactive course, assessment will be made primarily on student presentations of the material, along with the completion of an imaging project using actual seismic data.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Student presentation of material 80%  
Independent project 20%

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

It is expected that each student do his or her own work on any assignment. I encourage students to confer with each other for the purpose of gaining increased understanding of concepts presented in the class. However, I expect that assignments will reflect the student's own thoughts and efforts; anything else will be considered cheating.

A first offense will normally result in a warning, unless the offense is especially egregious. A second offense will result in a failing grade for the course.
