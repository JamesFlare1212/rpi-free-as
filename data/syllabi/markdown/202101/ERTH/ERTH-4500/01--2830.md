## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Overview of physical components of Earth’s climate system; builds on this foundation by examining the roles of both natural Earth system processes and anthropogenic influences in determining Earth’s climate and climate changes in the past, present, and future. Initial focus is on recent and future climate change. Remainder of course will emphasize the study and reconstruction of climates and climate change through geologic history.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

*Earth’s Climate: Past and Future,* Ruddiman, 2014 (3rd edition)

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students who successfully complete this course will be able to: 1) explain the components of Earth’s climate system and how they interact 2) explain and give examples of recent and future climate changes, including anthropogenic forcings and climate responses 3) explain the study and reconstruction of climates and climate change through geologic history 4) explain basic techniques and proxies that scientists use to study and reconstruct past climates 5) lead a class discussion based on an article and topic of the student’s choice 6) use the knowledge they gain in

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

As a student, you must have confidence that the instructors made appropriate decisions about the structure and content of this course, and that we will make a strong effort to accommodate the varying needs of a diverse group of students. We, in turn, must have confidence that the report, assignments, and exams that you complete are your own work.

The Rensselaer Handbook defines various forms of academic dishonesty and procedures of responding to these. All forms are violations of student-teacher trust. Students should familiarize themselves with the appropriate portion of the Rensselaer Handbook and note that the penalties for plagiarism and other forms of cheating are quite harsh.

The evaluation of student performance is a service provided by Rensselaer. Attempts to undermine this service lower Rensselaer's reputation. Therefore, it is essential that academic honesty be preserved. Unless otherwise indicated, you should cooperate with one another in and outside of class on the solution of problems. You may collaborate with partners on reports with instructor permission. However, you may not collaborate on term papers or misrepresent another person's work as your own on term papers. Students who violate the spirit or letter of these rules are subject to penalties as stipulated in the Rensselaer Handbook.

Penalties for violating Honesty and Academic Integrity Policy

For exercises that are specified to be worked on as individuals, copying another student’s report will result in 0 points given to the student for that report.

Attendance is required. Unexcused absences will result in 0 points given to the student for related assignments (see above for details).
