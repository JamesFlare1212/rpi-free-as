## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course provides a general overview of the entrepreneurial finance market. We will cover, among many things, how to break down the valuation problem of early-stage ideas, the sources of financing for young businesses and which sources are more vs. less appropriate for different types of young businesses, investment strategy for fundraising, and how contract terms change over a business's life-cycle, potential exit strategies, the inner-workings of the VC industry and the incentives of the different parties involved in the process.  
As such the primary objective of the course is to provide an understanding of the concepts and institutions involved in entrepreneurial finance and private equity markets, with a focus on VCs. Entrepreneurial finance and VC investing are intrinsically linked, and the course is designed to be broad and comprehensive, with the emphasis being on entrepreneurial finance. In order for an entrepreneur to make the appropriate decisions from a financing perspective and to obtain the most suitable financial contracts, they have to understand the motivations of the various investors that invest in entrepreneurial ventures. Conversely, investors need to understand things from an entrepreneur’s perspective and what incentivizes them in order to evaluate the various investment opportunities with which they are presented. Consequently, the course is designed for students who are interested in either pursuing entrepreneurial activity or financing entrepreneurial ventures or both.  
Success in this 4-credit (3-credit) course is based on the expectation that students will spend a minimum of 10 hours of study time per week in preparation for class (readings, cases/assignments, group projects, preparation for exams, etc.)

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- 1\. Understand business valuation in the entrepreneurial context
- 2\. Understand financial contracting in addressing information and incentive problems
- 3\. Understand core elements of financial planning for an entrepreneurial venture
- 4\. Understand the sources of internal and external finance for entrepreneurial ventures
- 5\. Understand the relevant elements of a deal structure
- 6\. Understand venture finance in a portfolio context
- 7\. Understand the economics behinds the venture capital market and deal structure

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

The grade for the course will be based on performance in five activities: a mid-term exam, a final exam, case studies, homework assignments, and class participation. Class participation will be reflected in your final grade as a +/-5% adjustment to your final grade.  
**Homework/In-Class Assignments:**  
Assigned during lectures – reading, research, and problems. Homework assignments must be done individually, unless explicitly stated otherwise (by the instructor). Discussions with classmates and the instructor and strongly encouraged, but eventual implementation, analysis, and writing must be individual work. Homework is due at the start of the lecture the day it is due. In-class assignments will be assigned during lectures. **No late homework will be accepted.**  
**Case Studies:** There will be several case studies assigned during the semester. Students are required to work in groups (group size depends on enrollment) and each group must have access to all case material. The solutions are to be written and submitted via LMS. ***Students are responsible for all material in the case.***  
**Presentation(s):** Throughout the semester, each group will be required to present at least one case (depending on the number of groups we may have time for multiple presentations for each group). ***Each group member is responsible for all material being presented.*** There is no dress code for presenting.  
**Grading:** Cases are graded at the group level. The exam for students enrolled in MGMT 6260 will be somewhat different in that they will be required to answer additional questions.  
**Quizzes:**  
Quizzes are done through LMS. There will be between 6- 8 quizzes. They will be multiple-choice questions and you are allowed 10 minutes to complete it. Each quiz is weighted evenly. It is open-book and open notes.
