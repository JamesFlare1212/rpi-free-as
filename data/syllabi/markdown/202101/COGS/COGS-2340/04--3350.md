## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course will provide an overview of the field of linguistics, the study of human language. As linguistics is a relatively diverse and interdisciplinary field (as we will see), we will cover many different topics and sub-fields in an attempt to discover and discuss the various avenues linguistics has to offer. This broad approach will introduce students to a range of language-related topics.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Language Files: 12th Edition (The Ohio State University Press, 2016)

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Describe and differentiate the different subfields of linguistics
- Apply linguistic concepts in analysis of new language data
- Discuss and think critically about the core concepts of linguistics, including language-focused, people-focused, and computation-focused topics

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Three exams will assess knowledge and understanding of main concepts. First exam will feature problem set style questions, second and third exams will be more short answer-oriented.  
Various assignments throughout the semester will reinforce main concepts and help practice skills for the exam.  
Discussions throughout the semester will allow students to reflect on their own language experience and understanding.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

Student grades will be comprised of five components: three exams, participation, and various assignments. The exams will be non-cumulative; later exams will necessarily contain concepts included in earlier ones, but these concepts will not be tested directly. These exams will target big-picture concepts and application of skills rather than memorization. Paying attention during class is the best way to prepare for the exams! The “assignments” category consists of an assortment of activities to be turned in throughout the semester. Participation refers to participation in class discussions, either through live chat or discussion board.  
Exam I – 20% Exam II – 20% Exam III – 20% Participation – 10% Assignments – 30%
