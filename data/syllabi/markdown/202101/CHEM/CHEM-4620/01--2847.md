## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

## Course Description

This course is designed to provide those understandings for upper-level undergraduate students in chemistry, chemical engineering, biomedical engineering, material science and engineering or related disciplines. This course will focus on the structure, synthesis, and reaction of chemistry associated with both natural and synthetic polymers. Associated topics such as polymer properties, characterization and processing will be discussed.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

## Course Goals / Objectives

1. Students will understand the basic principles that apply to the synthesis, structure, properties, characterization and applications of polymers.
2. Students will gain fundamental knowledge on polymer synthesis methods in conjunction with development of new properties of polymeric materials.
3. Students will show competence in the fundamental concepts in the structure-property-relationships of polymeric materials that are a key to the application of polymers.
4. Students will learn the elemental concepts in the molecular heterogeneity of polymers as materials.
5. Students will learn the analysis and characterization of polymers.

## Course Calendar

### Calendar Details:

**Lecture topics**

(Polymer Synthesis Section) by Professor Chulsung Bae

1. Introduction, Definitions and History & Polymer Classification and Structure
2. Step Growth Polymerization
3. Chain Growth Polymerization
4. Free Radical Polymerization and Transition Metal Coordination Polymerization
5. Controlled Polymerization: Anionic, Cationic, and Radical, Ring-Opening Polymerizations
6. Miscellaneous Polymers
7. Reactions of Synthetic Polymers

**Special Topics**

- Polymers for Energy Conversion and Storage: solar cell, fuel cell, electrolysis, flow battery
- Polymer for Membrane Separation: gas separation, water purification, desalination, biomolecule separation

(Polymer Properties and Characterization Section) by Professor Chang Y. Ryu

1. Polymer Dispersity (Chapter 1 & lecture notes)
2. Polymer Shape. Gaussian Coil, statistical segment length and Random Walk Model (Chapter 10, Young & Lovell 3rd Ed)
3. Polymer Solution and Characterization
   1. Osmotic Pressure (Chapter 11,) - Mn
   2. Light Scattering (Chapter 12) - Mw
   3. Intrinsic Viscosity & Mark-Houwink equation (Chapter 13) - Mv
   4. Gel Permeation Chromatography (Chapter 14), Mn, (Mv), Mw, & Mz
4. The Glass Transition & Polymer Crystals
   1. Amorphous vs. Semicrystalline State (Chapters 16 & 17)
   2. Thermal Analysis of Polymers (DSC, TGA, DMA and TMA) (Chapters 16 & 17)
   3. Mechanical Properties of Polymers (Chapter 22)
5. Special Topics
   1. Polymer Recycling
   2. Additive Manufacturing of Polymers
