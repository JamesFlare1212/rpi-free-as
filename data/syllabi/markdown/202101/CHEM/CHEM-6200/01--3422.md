## Course Goals

### Goals:

**COURSE GOALS**

- ) Students will learn to interpret MS, IR and NMR spectra and determine molecular structures from these and other information.

- ) Students will learn the fundamental principles of MS, IR and NMR and how spectra are collected for each technique.

- ) Students will learn how to access and use online resources such as spectroscopic databanks and simulators to predict chemical structures.

- ) Students will learn how to piece together spectroscopic information to determine the chemical formula and structure of previously unknown compounds of varying degrees of complexity.
