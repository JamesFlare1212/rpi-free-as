### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

*Participation & Performance*: Language learning involves using the vocabulary and grammar you learned to interact with others for all purposes of life in a target language society. You are required to participate and perform different language learning tasks/activities in class. You receive full credit of participation for excused class absence and can schedule a make-up work for the absence. No make-ups for any unexcused absences and lateness. ***Your class participation will be checked by random QR code. If you do not respond to the QR code check-in, you will lose one-half of the participation points of that period***. ***If you are in a different time zone, talk to the instructor and make arrangements for make-up.***

*Homework*: Homework is an important integral for successful Chinese learning. **For the best effect of proficiency-based language learning, assignments of this course are spread out over the week** so that you practice 20-30 minutes daily. Each new week’s homework is posted on Monday of the previous week on LMS and there are THREE (3) due times for the routine weekly homework: (1) Tuesday before class; (2)Friday before class, and (3) Sunday Midnight.

It is **your responsibility** to check the weekly homework and due time carefully when posted on the LMS. If homework is submitted late, 10% of the possible points will be deducted per 24-hour period after the due time. Extension of homework submission should be requested in writing.

*Unit Dictation*: Dictation is a form of testing the listening and writing of vocabulary and sentences. You write down what you hear. ***Unit Dictations usually will be taken place after we finish each Unit (two chapters)***. You are not allowed to make up dictations for **unexcused** tardiness and/or absence.

*Quizlet*: Quizlet is an online flashcards website allowing you to study each chapter’s vocabulary with different modes of study. Much of the vocabulary learning and assessment will be through this Web 2.0 platform. An invite to join will be sent to you in week one.

*Project*: There will be a couple of projects in this course. Details on due dates, directions, and assessments will be explained in class. You can also find them under “Course Documents” on LMS.

*Exam*: There will be two exams in this course. Each exam consists of two parts: written and oral. The two exams test your knowledge of vocabulary, grammar, cultural knowledge as well as your competency in listening, speaking, reading, and writing of the Chinese language.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**P****olicy on collaboration and cheating/plagiarism in this course**

It is VERY EASY to spot cheating in a foreign language course. Students are not permitted to turn in work done by someone else. Getting assistance from a native speaker on assignments is OK, but your final product must be your own work as it is easy to identify the excessive structures beyond your level of proficiency. Besides, the use of a WEB TRANSLATE (e.g., Google Translate) for translation other than vocabulary or phrase is not permitted.

The first time I suspect cheating I warn the student and tell why I suspect (e.g. use of non-course grammar or vocabulary) and I take off 50% points of the grade. If it's very obvious I do not give a warning. I will just give a zero. If there is a subsequent infraction the student will receive a grade of F for the course or be reported to the Student's Office.
