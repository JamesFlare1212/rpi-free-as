## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course is designed to help students build a solid ground in data analytics and policy evaluation.

Particular attention is given to the analysis of ‘panel’ or ‘longitudinal’ data sets. Topics to be studied

include fixed effects and random effects models and their estimation and inference in the linear panel

data framework. With a range of applications from the literature, we will also discuss some recently

developed econometric techniques for policy evaluation. In particular, quantile regression will be

introduced to investigate the heterogeneous treatment effects. Some other recent advances in data

analytics including LASSO and adaptive LASSO will be briefly introduced.
