## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- to gain a factual knowledge of money, banking, and the financial system
- to practice interpreting data in the monetary context
- to understand monetary policy and its implications for national and world economies.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Tests: There will be three tests administered online via MyLab. The basis for tests will be all assigned textbook readings, all MyLab assignments, and all lecture material. Each test will be made available for a period of seven days. Test format is multiple choice and numerical answer. Each student will receive a unique test. Tests must be completed in one session and will be timed. *Makeup tests will only be allowed for absences having an official Rensselaer excuse issued by the Health Center or the Dean of Students, and submitted to the TA by two days after the test. The makeup test is scheduled during the week after the test.*

MyLab Assignments: Assignments using the Pearson MyLab online system will be due on three dates. These assignments include: 1) three sets of homeworks and quizzes which pertain to the assigned readings, and 2) one set of monetary data analysis problems, called the Fed Project. All assignment sets, except for the last one due, have a 7 day period of time following the due date during which assignments may be completed late for a 10% penalty on all questions unanswered by the due date. After the 7 day late period, you must contact the TA for extra time with a 25% late penalty. The last assignments due allow a late submission period of only 3 days.

FOMC Essay: Students will submit a written analysis of the most recent meeting of the Federal Open Market Committee, to be submitted in LMS by the due date specified, instructions to be posted in LMS. The policy for academic integrity with regards to plagiarism from any source including from former students is in full effect for this and all work completed. Any student who is not confident of their writing ability is urged to contact the campus center for student writing development, called “Comm+D Center”, located in the lower level Folsom Library. Any essay which contains poor spelling and grammatical errors or appears to be the awkward result of translation software will be given zero credit; there is no revision opportunity.

Optional Final Exam: An optional final exam will be offered during the last seven days of class as a way for students to attempt to improve their grade. The final exam will cover all course material. If you take the final exam, the score you obtain on the final exam will be compared with the average of your mid-semester tests. You will be awarded the higher of these scores as your grade for the test portion of the course. *If you miss any of the three mid-semester* test(s) and do not have a proper excuse (see policy on makeup tests above)*, then plan on taking the final exam to improve your grade.*

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

By enrolling in this course, all students are committed to absolute agreement to and understanding of the course policy on academic integrity.

- \- MyLab assignments allow teamwork. However, students are expected to put forth their own effort and not simply take answers from other students. By doing one’s own work on assignments, one gains deeper understanding and is better prepared to do well on tests.
- \- It is expected that students will do their own work to complete tests. Tests are open book/open note. Teamwork on tests is counterproductive because each student receives a unique and timed test.
- \- Plagiarism involves the intentional copy of sentences or paragraphs from published or online sources without citation or from work completed by a current or former student. Written work submitted will be monitored for plagiarism from any source, including present and former students. Plagiarism, either in entirety or in part, is a violation of the policy on academic integrity.

In general and to a certain extent, there is a degree of “you get out of the class what you put into it.” Professors cannot possibly monitor all dishonest activity, nor can they force students to learn. It is your responsibility to get your money’s value out of this and any college class. Any student found to engage in dishonest activity in any aspect of this course will be penalized with failure of the course.
