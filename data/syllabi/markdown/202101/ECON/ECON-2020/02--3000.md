## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

In this course, you will build on and apply what you have learned in ECON1200: Introductory Economics. You will use the concepts of various measures of economic activity such as aggregate output and consumption, interest rates, employment, inflation and investment to study the dynamics of an economy at a more advanced level. For example, now that you understand the relationship between supply and demand in general terms, you will be asked to examine the effects that short-run and long-run price changes have on full employment and output. As the course progresses, you will gain a better appreciation for how policy shifts and changes in one sector impact the rest of the macroeconomy. You will also examine the causes of inflation and depression, why some economies grow more rapidly than others and discuss various approaches to responding to them.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The goal of this course is to provide you with a formal tool for macroeconomic analysis. By the end of this course, you should be able to think critically about the economy and develop your own unique perspective on various issues. More specially, you should demonstrate an understanding of intermediate level theory and empirical evidence in the following areas: (i) Classical macroeconomic theory (ii) Economic growth (iii)Aggregate supply and demand (iv)Macroeconomic policy.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- After completing this course, students will be able to (1) Explain how to compute the main macroeconomics aggregates and understand their main limitations. (2) Explain how national income, inflation and unemployment behave in the the long run using the classical model. (3) Explain the determinants of economic growth.in the Solow growth model, (4) Explain the golden rule level of capital and economic growth across countries. (5) Explain the endogenous growth theory. (6) Use growth accounting to determine the sources of economic growth. (7) Describe how the economy behaves in the short-run as in in the IS-LM model. . (8) Explain the role of macroeconomic policies in the IS-LM model. (9) Explain the aggregate demand (AD) and aggregate supply model (AS). (10) Describe the main theories of consumption and Investment (11) Explain the current debate on macroeconomic policies.
