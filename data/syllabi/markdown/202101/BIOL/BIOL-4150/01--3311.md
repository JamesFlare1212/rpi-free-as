## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Neurons are uniquely organized to facilitate signal transmission and information processing in the brain. Examine the mechanisms that develop and maintain neurons, the unusual cell-biological problems these cells solve, and the experimental tools that drive modern neuroscience research. You will read current scientific literature, learn to evaluate it critically, and develop a research project.

### Goals:

The goal of this course is to familiarize students with the knowledge necessary to analyze a topic in cellular neuroscience and critically evaluate experiments.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

Demonstrate knowledge of neurons and the underlying molecular, structural, and cellular mechanisms that enable them to maintain their unique structure and fulfill their function in the nervous system.

Demonstrate knowledge of experimental tools used in modern cellular neuroscience research.

Read and critically review primary scientific literature in cellular neuroscience.

Analyze a field of neuroscience research and design a set of next-step experiments, and package these in a persuasive grant proposal.

Review grant proposals, critically evaluate proposed experiments, and provide constructive feedback.
