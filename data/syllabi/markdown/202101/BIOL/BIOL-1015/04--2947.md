## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

### Course Description

This is an introductory course in biological systems focusing on major topics in evolution, human genetics & medicine, and ecology. It is an undergraduate level course. It is intended for non-majors and majors and covers a variety of topics that impact our daily lives and the grand challenges of this decade and your generation. The goal is to provide an understanding of fundamental principles, to develop analytical thinking skills in the context of modern biology, and to develop data dexterity in the life sciences. This is a D1 Data Intensive Course.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

### Course Goals / Objectives

1. Principle Concepts: Students will be able to grasp and master introductory biological principles. Students at a technological university will gain basic biological literacy in laboratory techniques.
2. Predict and Propose: Students will be able to connect and link core concepts, and apply them to the bases of protocols used in the laboratory course. Students will gain experience in formulating and testing hypotheses, using appropriate materials and methods, and analyzing data sets quantitatively and/or qualitatively to arrive at conclusions, and predict and propose possible solutions.
3. Pre-requisites: Students will be able to satisfy prerequisites, in order to take advanced courses in the biological sciences. Students will be prepared to take the second introductory biology course. We do not have separate major/non-major introductory courses. BIOL 1010 serves as the first course for life science majors and for students interested in the health professions.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate mastery of the foundational knowledge and core concepts of evolution, genetics & medicine, and ecology by answering multiple choice questions in graded Simulations Modules and Online Lab Tests.
- Apply foundational knowledge and core concepts in these areas to solve novel problems, analyze realistic scenarios, and make predictions. Evaluation by answering multiple choice questions in graded Simulations Modules and Online Lab Tests.
- Apply quantitative reasoning and methods to the analysis and solution of biological problems. Evaluation by answering multiple choice questions in graded Simulations Modules and Online Lab Tests.
- Utilize information and data from a wide range of sources to construct knowledge and communicate that knowledge effectively. Specifically, given a current topic in biology, gather information from research articles, texts, the web, and other relevant sources. Analyze, organize, distill, and synthesize this information and present as a Final paper or Final Project.
