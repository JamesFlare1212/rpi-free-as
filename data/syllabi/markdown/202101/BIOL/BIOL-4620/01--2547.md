## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

The Molecular Biology I course will focus on the organization of prokaryotic and eukaryotic genomes and the molecular processes for storing, maintaining, and expressing genomic information in cells and organisms. The emphasis of this course will be on the regulation and gene expression from the transcription of chromosomal DNA through the processing and translation of the gene transcript. The molecular tools and techniques for studying genomes and the applications of genome sequences to basic scientific research, medical and forensic studies will be discussed.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

The goal is to discuss the molecular interactions necessary for a cell's continual existence such as cell division, DNA repair, regulation of gene expression and translation. Students will discuss data and techniques in journal articles to apply their knowledge and to gain experience in interpreting scientific data.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

The protein - DNA interactions that are necessary for cell division, DNA repair, and gene expression including the process of translation.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

-  Use proper scientific vocabulary to communicate information regarding molecular biology of a cell.
-  Demonstrate knowledge of structures, properties, and biological interactions between the major molecules in cells.
-  Compare and contrast major features of transcription, translation and replication in prokaryotic and eukaryotic organisms.
- Demonstrate a thorough understanding of events involved in initiation, elongation, termination and processing of RNA and protein and the regulation of these events.
