## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Appreciation and understanding of uncertainties and the conditions under which they occur, within the context of the engineering problem-solving pedagogy of measurements, models, validation, and analysis. Problems and concerns in obtaining measurements; tabular and graphical organization of data to minimize misinformation and maximize information; and development and evaluation of models. Concepts will be supported with computer demonstration. Applications to problems in engineering are emphasized.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course teaches students

- An appreciation for uncertainty in the application of engineering models;
- Defines the difference between probability and statistics;
- What are discrete and continuous distribution functions;
- Marginal and conditional distributions;
- Descriptive statistics;
- Sample distributions and point estimates;
- Confidence intervals;
- Hypothesis testing;
- Univariate and multivariate regression analysis; and
- An introduction into machine learning

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

- **Quizzes (25%):** A total of 9 quizzes will be assigned.
- **Projects (25%):** There are two projects. One serves as an introduction to R **(10%)** and is designed to experimentally verify the central limit theorem, the other is more comprehensive **(15%)** and involves the use of R to determine regression models based on real data. Late submissions receive a reduction of 10% per day from points earned. Submission later than a week will not be accepted. An excellent introduction and source for R is available on https://moderndive.com/index.html.
- **Tests 1 and 2 (20% for Test 1, 30% for Test 2):** Test 2 is cumulative, i.e. it also includes material covered before Test 1.
