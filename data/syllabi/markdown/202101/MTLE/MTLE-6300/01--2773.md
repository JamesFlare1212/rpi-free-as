## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Theory and practice of IC fabrication in a research laboratory environment. Test chips are fabricated and the resulting devices and circuits evaluated. Processes and fabrication equipment studied and used include oxidation/diffusion, CVD reactors, photolithography, plasma etching, vacuum evaporator, ion implantation, etc. Instruments used in process monitoring and final testing include thin film profilometer, ellipsometer, resistivity probe, scanning electron microscope, capacitance-voltage system, etc. The fundamentals of hazardous material handling and clean room procedures are studied.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

\* Review the critical theory and fundamentals of IC fabrication processes and design.  
\* Learn the essential micro-processing technologies, key processes, and fabrication equipment and instruments in a research laboratory environment.  
\* Learn the basic process monitoring and testing techniques and instruments.  
\* Learn and practice the hazardous material handling and clean room procedures.  
\* Develop the skills to design, fabricate and test micro-structures and devices in a research cleanroom environment.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

Lecture topics:  
1\. IC Fabrication Overview and Cleanroom Safety  
2\. Thermal Oxidation / SUPREM processing simulation  
3\. Photolithography  
4\. Dielectrics and Poly-Si Deposition  
5\. Ion Implantation  
6\. Diffusion in Si  
7\. Plasma-Assisted Etching  
8\. Metallization & Interconnects  
9\. MOSFET Device Physics and Critical Processing Parameters  
10\. Testing of Processing Monitor Structures & Device Characterization.  
Laboratory Processes:  
1\. Field Oxidation  
2\. M1 Active Area Definition: Photolithography & Oxide Wet Etch  
3\. Gate Oxidation, Poly-Si Deposition and Doping  
4\. M2 Poly-Si Gate Patterning: Poly-Si/Oxide Etch, S/D Ion Implantation (@ Vendor)  
5\. ILD Deposition and Densification & Implant Activation  
6\. Contacts Patterning: Photolithography & ILD RIE Etch  
7\. Metallization: Al/Si Contact - Sputtering Deposition  
8\. Metallization: Al/Si Wet Etch - Frontside Metal patterning  
9\. Metallization: Backside Metal Contact: Etch Backside Oxide, Deposit Al/Si on Back, Sintering in Forming Gas  
10\. Testing of Processing Monitor Structures & Device
