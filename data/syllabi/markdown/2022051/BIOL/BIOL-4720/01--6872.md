## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Proficiency in content knowledge concerning molecular biology protocols including primer design and DNA cloning steps (e.g. calculations for volumes of enzymes, bacterial transformations and DNA quantitation)
- The ability to develop and maintain a laboratory notebook and reporting results using well-labeled graphs and pictures and conclusions (success or failure) at each step in the procedure.
- Interpret and paraphrase genomic and gene expression data from online databases and illustrate proficiency in using the molecular biology software program, Benchling.
- The ability to produce laboratory reports in a primary journal article format through independent learning and writing about familiar subjects at a high level of competence.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Lab reports A presentation 2 Tests 2 notebook checks Lab assessment**

**Final Exam (in recitation)** **12.5 %** (cumulative)

**Lab Notebook checks** **25 %** (week 4 – **10 %;** End of the semester **15 %)**

**Lab Assessment** **10 %** (e.g. exhibits ***professional*** behavior at all times i.e., maintains a clean lab bench, persistent, attention to details, fluent in using all the equipment and the Benchling computer program, submits well labeled primer sequences on time and in the correct format).

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

**Penalty for Late Assignments** Assignments turned in late will be penalized half a letter grade for each day or fraction thereof that the papers are tardy. If a paper is more than 3 (3) days late I will send an EWS. A late penalty of half a letter grade is applied for each day late. Papers will not be accepted (7) seven days after the due date or when the papers are returned the students. If you cannot hand in the final paper by the end of the semester, it is up to the student to get the forms for an Incomplete (I) for the course. Once a new due date has been agreed upon the paper must be handed in before that time or I will give a zero (0) for the assignment and your final grade will be determined as such.

**Grading Appeals** Appeals of grades must be made to the instructor within one week of the return of graded material to the student. Since grades are assigned both in consideration of the instructor's absolute standards and relative to the performance of the rest of the class, it is unlikely that grades will be changed unless a clear error on the part of the instructor/TAs can be demonstrated.

**Attendance**

Timely attendance at both laboratory and recitation sessions is mandatory. If you must miss a class due to an extenuating circumstance, you must contact the professor as soon as possible. In most cases, a missed laboratory session cannot be made up. Missing one lab will result in a grade reduction. You must email me a Dean’s excuse explaining why you missed lab and additional work will be provided to you to make up the time lost. I go over protocols, data from that week’s lab, and the use of Benchling during recitation.
