## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This is an undergraduate level introductory course in Human Evolution. It explores the current knowledge of our own ancestry and evolution. The goal is to provide active learning in a collaborative environment via experiential laboratory activities, interactive discussions, simulations and computational labs. Students will analyze the supporting evidence while tracing the evolutionary relatedness amongst primate species with other vertebrate species via phenotypic and genotypic datasets, and study the impact of disease pathogens and environment on human populations.

## Course Content

### Content Details:

The course content is divided into the following laboratory & discussion type meetings:

1. Introduction - INTRO - Evolution from mammals to primates.
2. Introduction - INTRO - Evolution primates to hominins.
3. Evidence - BONES - Evidence in primate fossils, such as skulls, teeth & bones.
4. Experiential Lab- BONES - Evidence in hominin fossils, such as skulls, teeth & bones.
5. Simulations Lab - EVOEVI - Evolutionary Evidence to construct Phylogenies.
6. Simulations Lab - HIV - Human Epidemics, HIV.
7. Computational Lab - ACE2 - Human Genetics, ACE2 receptor in SarsCoV2.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Demonstrate mastery of the foundational knowledge and core concepts of Human Evolution by answering graded multiple choice questions and/or short answer questions in the Final Exam, and/or by delivering oral presentations to peers in class.
- Apply foundational knowledge and core concepts to make predictions by answering graded multiple choice questions and/or short answer questions in the Final Exam, and/or by delivering oral presentations to peers in class.
- Propose, predict and present a model on the Future of Human Evolution by gathering information from research articles, texts, the web, and other relevant established sources, and by using the RPI Library, assessment is by oral presentations to peers in class.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

- Your Interactive Assessments are each worth 10 points each. There are a total of 3 Interactives. These are your reflections on any aspect of Human Evolution discussed in labs. This is worth a total of **30 Points.**
- Your Simulations are each worth 10 points, for a total of **20 points** this semester.
- Your Application Lab is worth **10 points**.
- Your Final Project is worth **10 points**, and is on any relevant topic of your choosing - human paleoclimate, language, tools, art, culture, technology, AI or any other topic. It is to be orally presented by you to your peers in person in class. 10 points are for your presentation which must consist of your Model for the Future of Human Evolution.
- Your Peer Reviews are worth 10 points each. 10 points are for each of 4 Peer Reviews. These are due in each class where you are not presenting. This is worth a total of **40 Points.**
- The Final Exam is worth **10 points**. All topics will be tested.
- Drop 2 lowest scoring above Assessments.

The total course maximum possible points is **100 points.**
