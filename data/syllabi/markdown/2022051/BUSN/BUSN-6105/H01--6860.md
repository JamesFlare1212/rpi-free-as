### **Instructor Note**

Welcome to Agile Planning and Execution! My name is David Stec and, as your instructor, I would like to welcome you to BUSN 6105. My role will be to assist and guide you in completing the project-based learning activities for this course. In this course, you experience change from a new perspective by applying contemporary change management and project management principles to plan and execute a sustainable change.

You will leave the course with a better understanding of types of change, change models, and agile project management practices to best implement these changes within your own organization. Typically, the study of project management focuses on the tasks needed to accomplish a project (e.g., what, when, who, etc.). It provides project structure and addresses the actions and deliverables. Change management, however, focuses on the people impacted by the change, ways to reduce resistance to change, and to elicit behaviors to support change. Although change management is critical to the success of any change initiative, it is often forgotten and not integrated into project management theory. Before you get started, review this document to familiarize yourself with the course, the course structure, expectations, outcomes, and the flow of work. I will be here throughout the course to help provide guidance and mentorship.

## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

**Course Description**

This course focuses on the integration and application of Agile project management principles with change management models and processes to plan and execute a change initiative within an organization. An array of change management models, processes and their respective applicability are presented facilitating the appropriate selection of the model for the given change initiative. Integrating Agile and change management allows for a more robust understanding of the types of change and how to effectively plan and implement the change. Students will:

- Identify a proposed change by evaluating the organization’s current state, proposing specific improvements that better meet organizational objectives.
- Develop a fully integrated change and project management plan using the Agile methodology to plan and execute a change management initiative.
- Use leadership best practices to engage and involve stakeholders to ensure the project is fully vetted.
