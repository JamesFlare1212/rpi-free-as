## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course presents solutions to problems in transport phenomena using the finite element method. The fundamentals of heat, mass, and momentum transport are presented. Finite difference methods in 1D and 2D are presented. Simulations are performed in COMSOL Multiphysics software for 1D, 2D and 3D systems. The course covers writing user-defined functions and variables, results processing, and data extraction from simulations.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

- Develop an understanding of problems in heat, mass, and momentum transport.
- Develop an understanding of solutions to complex problems using finite difference methods.
- Demonstrate basic knowledge of the solution of fundamental problems in transport phenomena and the underlying physical interpretation.
- Understand and be able to use tools to solve complex problems in transport phenomena.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

No Course Learning Outcomes are available for this course.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

1. Students should understand basic conservation laws of fluid mechanics, heat transfer, and mass conversion.
2. Students should be familiar with control volume analysis and applications.
3. Students should be able to formulate and solve simple fluid and heat flow problems.
4. Students should be familiar with finite difference methods
5. Students should be familiar with simulation tools and methods.
