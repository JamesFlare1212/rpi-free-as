### Instructor Note

Welcome to the start of your Design for Six Sigma (DFSS) Lean Six Sigma journey! This course will introduce you to the DFSS philosophy. The goal is to use the “Show by Doing” mentality making you AMBASSADORS for change. It is having you grasp the paradigm shift from “Increasing Quality at work means higher costs” to “Increasing Quality means reducing costs.”

This course is designed with a logic flow where every week you will be completing a piece of the final deliverable which is the DMA Project piece of DMADOV framework. Since Design for Six Sigma deals with creating a new process or redesigning an existing one within your workplace it requires additional tools and methods than producing a DMAIC project. Thus, this course is designed to handle the first three stages of the DFSS framework and the ISYE6970 Professional Project course will comprise of the remaining three stages. It is important during project selection that you work with your manager, your local BB / MBB and the Mentor to choose a viable project for these two courses which will make up 6 credits for your degree.

## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course is meant to provide you with an introduction to Design for Six Sigma approach. Unlike other on-line classes, this course is designed with a logic flow where every week you will be completing a piece of the final deliverable. Since Design for Six Sigma deals with creating a process within your workplace it requires additional tools and methods than producing a DMAIC project. Thus, this course is designed to handle the first three stages of the DFSS and the ISYE 6970 Professional Project course will comprise of the remaining three stages. It is important during project selection that you work with your local BB / MBB and the Mentor to choose a viable project for these two courses which will make up six credits for your degree. Both classes will follow a module structure and thus, each module needs to be completed before starting the next and any uncertainties on the materials is addressed in the “Ask the Mentor.”

The approach to this course will be hands-on where you will be required to select a project feasible to applying the DMA concept of Lean Six Sigma DFSS:

- **Define**: Shaping a vision with elevator speech, establishing integration strategy along with cost-benefit analysis preparing for your business case, using the Conjoint analysis and Kano model for identifying the VOC, and learning cultural change management techniques i.e., Kotter’s steps, GRPI, In/Out Frame and stakeholder analysis with RACI.
- **Measure**: Introduction to TRIZ knowledge model, data collection and statistical calculations utilizing MINITAB for Design of Experiment.
- **Analysis**: Statistical calculations utilizing MINITAB to drill down to the root cause of the problem and yield its baseline sigma value, learn how to create KPI scorecards and dashboards, develop conceptual design using benchmarking and tradeoff analysis.

As mentioned earlier, the second part of DFSS will be covered in the ISYE 6970 Professional Project course. This has been done so you have more time to absorb and apply the concepts to your project. As a rule of thumb, DFSS projects take longer than DMAIC projects simply because you are creating something new instead of improving something existing. The most important task for these two courses will be selecting the project you wish to complete.

All projects are to be approved by the Mentor, your manager and/or your company’s BB/MBB by the end of the third week. NOTE: When selecting a project, ensure it complies with the all the following:

- The process does not exist and is a need (preferable at your workplace)
- Problem statement is identifiable and can be clearly stated
- Any funding required is already appropriated before embarking on this course
- Relates to the Customer (which can be external end user or internal departmental)
- Presumed solution does not exist
- Data is readily available and can be used for statistical analysis
- Resources other than yourself are not dependent for project success

When making your project selection, keep in mind you are going to be managing this project for two semesters, so here are example project ideas which can help you in your selection:

- Development new recruitment program
- Process design project
- Manufacturing machine automation or acquisition
- Process Redesign
- Rebranding a product or service
- Establish niche within a department for customer CTQs
- New SME program with training
- Establishing new KPIs or redesign metric system
- Robust and alignment of metric system at the department level
- Measurement Redesign (Business or Manufacturing)
- New product development
- New manufacturing machinery development
- Development of software / IT requirements
- Establishing new department
- Creating new services or aftermarket group

In addition to completing this course as part of your degree, you will be on the path to receive the Lean Six Sigma certification.

**The Mentor’s objectives**: My goal is to teach you the DFSS methodology and how it gets applied in your workplace. I designed this course to be more interactive and less theoretical. It’s important to me that you learn the tools that you can easily use in your day-to-day life to improve your way of working. There is no quiz, test, or exam in this course. I do not intend you to memorize anything. What I intend you to do is put in practice the methodologies taught and find some of the templates indispensable in your life. Thus, my objective is to have this not only be a 13-week course, but to have you continue using the DFSS approach whenever you see it fit to put something in place at work. This will be the course’s ultimate success.
