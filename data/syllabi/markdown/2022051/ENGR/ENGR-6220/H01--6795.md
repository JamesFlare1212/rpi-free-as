### Additional Information for Course Information Section:

This course is designed to be delivered in a blended format where approximately 90% of the course is delivered via the learning management system, and synchronous components make up approximately 10% of the course. Students are strongly encouraged to be present during synchronous sessions. To access the course, log in at: https://rpi.instructure.com to see your courses. This course is comprised of three parts. The first part explores data and information, as well as fundamental approaches in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence and how they contrast or complement techniques from Business Analytics. The second evaluates how data can yield powerful insights and the strategies to build a solution to classify observations. The third project includes extracting information from large datasets to make a prediction of a continuous variable. You will complete each project by reaching multiple milestones. The framework, scope, and deliverable of the project are detailed up front, as are the criteria (rubric) that will be used to evaluate your work. You then interact with learning resources and materials that will help you walk through completion of the project. The time expectation associated with each project is defined up front as well so you can pace yourself accordingly. As you complete each project, you receive feedback according to the associated rubric for the project. You can use that feedback to revise your project, or if you have “met the standard” for the project, you can move to the next project. When you have completed all projects in a successful manner, you have completed the requirements of the course. You are strongly encouraged to collaborate openly with each other – the work you submit must be your own original work but learning with others to explore ideas in strongly encouraged.

- Take an active role in the course, pursuing intellectual development, asking questions and engaging fully with the material and projects. I will be a mentor, give you clear and detailed feedback so that you have the opportunity to learn even after you’ve submitted a deliverable.

- Give me and your colleagues dignity and respect in all interactions. I will do exactly the same.
- Make adjustments to your busy personal and professional lives to make this course fit – and give this course the priority it deserves given your career aspirations. I will use your time wisely and effectively.

## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Design and deploy systems that serve as the basis for the machine learning process. Choose appropriate learning models, using decision tree, Bayesian, neural network and vector machine approaches. Use multiple statistical approaches to evaluate output that leads to best results.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Evaluate the opportunity for using data in combination with machine learning and artificial intelligence to add value across a broad range of use cases
- Develop machine learning solutions for data-driven problem solving
- Interpret predictions and classifications from ML/MI systems
- Calculate the accuracy of machine-generated predictions and classifications
- Evaluate the competitive and strategic position of the organization with respect to the use of data
- Recommend specific strategies to improve the organization’s performance using data
- Propose strategies for new opportunities, products and services using data and machine learning strategies to fulfill a market or corporate need.
- Evaluate data, machine learning products and solutions that may have potential for driving new value to their organizations
- Articulate how specific initiatives of the organization using data with machine learning or machine intelligence techniques may influence financial and strategic performance.
