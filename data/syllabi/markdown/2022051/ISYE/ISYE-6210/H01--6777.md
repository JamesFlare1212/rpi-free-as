Welcome to ISYE 6210 Theory of Production Scheduling! My role will be to assist and guide you in completing the project-based learning activities for this course. This course is designed to provide a systems-based approach to analyzing, managing, and improving production and supply chain systems.

## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

In this class, you will understand and address the inherent variability and waste present in production systems and supply chain systems. Fundamental principles governing the behavior of production and supply chain systems focused on the objective of throughput time reduction will be covered including extended value stream mapping, push and pull systems, production smoothing, and logistics and transportation management.  
**Credits Allocated:** 3 semester hours

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students successfully completing this course are able to:
- • Apply the value stream mapping tool to a production system and supply chain system to identify system level waste & inefficiency.
- • Quantify production system and supply chain performance measures.
- • Identify critical “levers” within the production system and the supply chain to formulate an appropriate improvement plan to improve system performance.
- • Apply “level pull” scheduling and control systems to manage material and information flow for the production system or supply chain.
