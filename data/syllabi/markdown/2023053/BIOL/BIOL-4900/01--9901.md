## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

Students will learn and add to the microbiological protocol for bioelectronic sensor research.

## Course Text(s)

### Text Details:

Please email Prof. Shayla Sawyer for the latest version of the Sawyer Group Manual.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

Students will learn how to work as a team to practice, maintain, and establish regular microbiological lab protocols.  
Students will learn the early steps of bioelectronic research by shadowing and starting projects under Sawyer Lab staff and students.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- The student(s) in this experience will be able to 1) follow lab protocol for microbial experiments 2) communicate to a team of researchers about progress with experiments 3) articulate needs or suggestions for new or existing protocol 4) write a report on experiences.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

The student will write a final report based on their summer project.

## Grading Criteria

### Criteria Details:

50% of their grade will be based on the quality of their final report.  
50% of their grade will be on their effort and progress as evaluated by mentors in the lab.
