## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course introduces the significance and techniques of Chinese calligraphy, a highly regarded art form in Chinese culture. Through hands-on practice with brush and ink, students will gain fundamental skills and knowledge of calligraphy and explore its aesthetic and intellectual values. They will learn how calligraphy has been used as a visual and narrative art form and create projects based on their artistic interpretation. They will also learn about the wellness benefits of calligraphic practice, including improved mental health, concentration, posture, and spiritual calmness. Taught in English, this course requires no prior knowledge of Chinese.

## Course Goals

### Goals:

This course is designed for students who want to gain comprehensive knowledge of Chinese calligraphy, from theory to practice. Through lectures, hands-on practices, presentations, and in-depth discussions, students will be introduced to the history and aesthetic principles of Chinese calligraphy. The course will also explore the evolution of Chinese calligraphy and its development across cultures, including contemporary and non-traditional formats.

Daily step-by-step practices and projects will guide students through the process of holding a brush and interpreting and creating their own artwork. By actively engaging in these activities, students will develop a deeper interest in and understanding of the Chinese language and its rich cultural heritage.

Please note that prior knowledge of Chinese calligraphy is not required for this course. All levels of expertise are welcome to join and explore the fascinating world of Chinese calligraphy.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- By the end of this course, students will be able to: 1. Demonstrate fundamental skills: Acquire basic techniques and skills in Chinese calligraphy, including brush handling, stroke order, and ink application. 2. Explore aesthetic and intellectual values: Analyze and appreciate the aesthetic and intellectual values embodied in Chinese calligraphy, including the relationship between form and meaning, the expressive power of strokes, and the balance of composition. 3. Create projects based on artistic interpretation: Apply learned calligraphy techniques and concepts to create original projects that reflect their artistic interpretation and understanding of the art form. 4. Recognize the wellness benefits of calligraphy: Understand the wellness benefits associated with calligraphic practice, such as improved mental health, concentration, posture, and spiritual calmness. 5. Develop cultural awareness by exploring Chinese calligraphy's historical and cultural context. 6. Cultivate openness to diverse art forms: Develop an open and receptive attitude towards different art forms and artistic expressions.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Academic dishonesty encompasses various actions, including cheating, fabricating, or falsifying information or sources, improper collaboration, submitting the same paper for multiple classes without permission, and plagiarism. Plagiarism occurs when writers intentionally or unintentionally use someone else’s language, ideas, or materials without giving proper credit and citation. Familiarizing yourself with RPI’s Student Rights and Responsibilities regarding academic integrity is essential.

Plagiarism is defined as the act of taking credit for someone else’s work, whether done in deliberately or unintentionally. Examples of plagiarism include submitting an essay, in whole or in part, written by someone else (such as a friend or an internet source) and claiming it as your own, as well as incorporating information or ideas from research materials without properly citing the sources.

In this class, plagiarism is unacceptable. If any instances of accidental or deliberate use of someone else’s words, ideas, or work without attribution are identified, you will first receive a warning. Failure to rectify the situation may result in penalties, such as receiving a failing grade on the paper, a failing grade in the course, and/or being referred to the Dean of Students.

## **Course Policy Regarding Usage of AI and ChatGPT**

The use of AI and specifically ChatGPT for completing coursework is strictly prohibited. All assignments, projects, and exams must be completed independently, without relying on AI-generated content or responses. This includes but is not limited to using ChatGPT to generate essays, provide solutions, or obtain answers. Any violation of this policy will be considered a breach of academic integrity and may result in severe consequences, including receiving a failing grade on the assignment, disciplinary action, and potential academic penalties.

Although you can use AI or ChatGPT ***as a tool*** to assist your thinking, the purpose of coursework is to assess your individual understanding, critical thinking skills, and creativity. Using AI or ChatGPT undermines the integrity of the assessment process and deprives you of the opportunity to develop and demonstrate your own knowledge and abilities. It is essential to engage in honest and independent work to uphold the principles of academic integrity and ensure fair evaluation.

If you have any questions about the use of AI or ChatGPT in relation to your coursework, please consult with the instructor for clarification. I will provide guidance and support to ensure compliance with the academic policies and uphold the integrity of the learning experience.
