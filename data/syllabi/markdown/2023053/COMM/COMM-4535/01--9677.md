## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- Students who successfully complete this course will be able to articulate, access, and analyze social media uses in the East (e.g., China) and the West. Especially, they will: 1. Understand cultural diversity in mediated messages: Recognize and appreciate the cultural diversity inherent in mediated messages, developing an understanding of how different cultures share and influence media content. 2. Enhance cross-cultural communication: Improve intellectual skills and intercultural communication abilities, enabling students to engage more effectively with counterparts from different cultural backgrounds. 3. Analyze Eastern and Wester social media: Critically analyze the diverse uses of Eastern and Western social media platforms in the context of socializing, entertainment, publicity, and marketing. 4. Evaluate the impact of media and mediated communication: Analyze the differences between American and Chinese culture from a comparative and cross-cultural perspective. 5. Apply critical thinking skills: Engage in meaningful discussions with peers about important social media issues in the Eastern and Western societies 6. Foster appreciation for cultural differences: Develop an appreciation for cultural differences and the role of media in shaping and representing diverse cultures, fostering a more inclusive and culturally sensitive perspective.

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

*Attendance & Participation*: Attendance and active participation in class discussions, activities, and group exercises are essential for a meaningful learning experience.

*Readings & Research*: Engage with assigned readings and conduct independent research to deepen understanding and contribute to class discussions.

*Discussion Board and Reflections*: LMS “Weekly Assignments” tab contains weekly discussions and reflective topics. Post your original thoughts and respond to peers’ insights on social media practices and cultural differences. It is **the student’s responsibility** to check the new week’s assignments and due time when posted on the LMS on the previous Friday morning. If an assignment is submitted late, 10% of the possible points will be deducted per 24-hour period after the due time. * if you have special circumstances that prevent you from performing group work, discuss it with the instructor to arrange an alternative.

*Case Studies*: Analyze and present case studies that compare and contrast social media platforms and practices in Eastern and Western cultures. These case studies should explore specific examples, cultural influences, and societal implications.

*Term Paper*: The term paper will critically examine one or more specific aspects of social media in the Eastern and Western-mediated communication phenomena addressed in this course. Check instruction and grading criteria in the Course Documents folder on LMS. One-on-one meetings with the instructor and Comm+D writing tutors for term paper drafts are required.

### Additional Academic Integrity Course Policy and Penalty Information:

Academic dishonesty encompasses various actions, including cheating, fabricating, or falsifying information or sources, improper collaboration, submitting the same paper for multiple classes without permission, and plagiarism. Plagiarism occurs when writers intentionally or unintentionally use someone else’s language, ideas, or materials without giving proper credit and citation. Familiarizing yourself with RPI’s Student Rights and Responsibilities regarding academic integrity is essential.

Plagiarism is defined as the act of taking credit for someone else’s work, whether done in deliberately or unintentionally. Examples of plagiarism include submitting an essay, in whole or in part, written by someone else (such as a friend or an internet source) and claiming it as your own, as well as incorporating information or ideas from research materials without properly citing the sources.

In this class, plagiarism is unacceptable. If any instances of accidental or deliberate use of someone else’s words, ideas, or work without attribution are identified, you will first receive a warning. Failure to rectify the situation may result in penalties, such as receiving a failing grade on the paper, a failing grade in the course, and/or being referred to the Dean of Students.

## Course Policy Regarding Usage of AI and ChatGPT

The use of AI and specifically ChatGPT for completing coursework is strictly prohibited. All assignments, projects, and exams must be completed independently, without relying on AI-generated content or responses. This include but is not limited using ChatGPT to generate essays, provide solutions, or obtain answers. Any violation of this policy will be considered a breach of academic integrity and may result in severe consequences, including receiving a failing grade on the assignment, disciplinary action, and potential academic penalties.

Although you can use AI or ChatGPT ***as a tool*** to assist your thinking, the purpose of coursework is to assess your individual understanding, critical thinking skills, and creativity. Using AI or ChatGPT undermines the integrity of the assessment process and deprives you of the opportunity to develop and demonstrate your own knowledge and abilities. It is essential to engage in honest and independent work to uphold the principles of academic integrity and ensure fair evaluation.

If you have any questions about the use of AI or ChatGPT in relation to your coursework, please consult with the instructor for clarification. I will provide guidance and support to ensure compliance with the academic policies and uphold the integrity of the learning experience.
