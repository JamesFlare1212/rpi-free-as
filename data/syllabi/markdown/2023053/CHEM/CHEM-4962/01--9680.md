## Course Description

### Additional Course Description :

This course will focus on understanding the chemical aspects of food composition with emphasis to the functional properties and chemical reactions of the major components of foods: Water, carbohydrates, proteins and fats/oils (lipids). Several food-related concepts such as Gelation, Browning and the Maillard reactions, Meat glue, Food texture and Mouth feeling, Emulsions, Sous-vide cooking, Thickening, etc. will be introduced through videos of a lecture series by renowned chefs from The United States and abroad.

## Learning Outcomes

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes (CLOs):

- At the end of this course, the student will gain a better understanding of: a. The functional properties and chemical reactions of major food constituents b. The relationship between Science and Cooking c. Food Processing and Microbiology

### Course (Student) Learning Outcomes Assessment Measures:

**Quizzes (75%)** A quiz will be given at the end of each discussion session. Each quiz will be made up of several questions to test your understanding of the concepts connected with the materials covered during the previous discussion/lecture. The source material for the questions will be the lecture slides, and the previously discussed video. Students must show up to class or they will forfeit their opportunity to take the quiz. Make-ups without valid excuse will not be given.  
**Discussion (20%)** Each student or group of two students will be assigned a video on science and cooking. Students will summarize the content of their assigned video and present it during the discussion session. They will have to establish a correlation between the video content and the topics being covered in this course.  
**Attendance** **(5%)** Attendance is mandatory and will account for 5% of the final grade.
